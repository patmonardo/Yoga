# VAK_1.01 Bhāṣya

## Homage to the Teacher According to Reality

This file records the first Bhāṣya-pass study for `VAK_1.01.md`.

The Kārikā file remains unchanged in this commit. Where the Bhāṣya requires a correction to the first-pass translation, that correction is recorded here as a translation decision for later review.

---

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

### IAST

> yaḥ sarvathāsarvahatāndhakāraḥ saṃsārapaṅkājjagadujjahāra /
>
> tasmai namaskṛtya yathārthaśāstre śāstraṃ pravakṣyāmy abhidharmakośam // VAkK_1.01 //

### Bhāṣya-informed translation

> Having bowed to him who has destroyed darkness in every way and concerning everything, who raised the world from the mire of saṃsāra—to that teacher according to reality, I shall proclaim the treatise, the *Abhidharmakośa*.

The Bhāṣya explicitly parses:

> *yathārtham aviparītaṃ śāsti iti yathārthaśāstā*

That is:

> He teaches reality correctly, without inversion; therefore he is the teacher according to reality.

Accordingly:

```text
tasmai yathārthaśāstre
    to him, the teacher according to reality
```

This differs from the first-pass interpretation of `yathārthaśāstre` as an expression modifying how the treatise is composed. The correction is recorded here and should be applied to `VAK_1.01.md` only during a deliberate Kārikā revision pass.

## 2. Natural Bhāṣya Unit

The natural opening unit extends from:

> *śāstraṃ praṇetukāmaḥ...*

through:

> *katamac chāstram ity āha—abhidharmakośam.*

It forms one continuous argument:

```text
intention to compose the treatise
    ↓
homage through declaration of the Teacher’s qualities
    ↓
the Teacher’s own benefit
    ↓
the Teacher’s benefit of others
    ↓
the means of benefiting others
    ↓
announcement of the treatise
    ↓
identification of that treatise as the Abhidharmakośa
```

The opening commentary therefore should not be segmented mechanically by individual phrases of the Kārikā.

## 3. Bhāṣya Analysis

### A. Why Vasubandhu begins with homage

> *śāstraṃ praṇetukāmaḥ svasya śāsturmāhātmyajñāpanārthaṃ guṇākhyānapūrvakaṃ tasmai namaskāram ārabhate ya iti.*

### Translation

> Desiring to compose the treatise, he begins the homage expressed by “he who,” preceded by a declaration of qualities, in order to make known the greatness of his own Teacher.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The homage has a determinate literary and doctrinal function:

```text
guṇākhyāna
    declaration of qualities

        precedes

namaskāra
    homage
```

Vasubandhu first establishes the qualities that make the Buddha worthy of homage and authoritative for the śāstra.

---

### B. The meaning of “destroyer of darkness”

> *buddhaṃ bhagavantam adhikṛtyāha—hatam asyāndhakāram anena veti hatāndhakāraḥ.*

### Translation

> Speaking with reference to the Blessed Buddha, he says: “His darkness has been destroyed,” or “darkness has been destroyed by him”; hence, “one whose darkness is destroyed.”

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The compound permits two closely related determinations:

```text
hatam asya andhakāram
    his darkness has been destroyed

hatam anena andhakāram
    darkness has been destroyed by him
```

The first stresses the Buddha as completely purified. The second stresses the Buddha as the active conqueror of darkness.

---

### C. The scope and manner of its destruction

> *sarveṇa prakāreṇa sarvasmin hatāndhakāraḥ sarvathāsarvahatāndhakāraḥ.*

### Translation

> He has destroyed darkness in every manner and with respect to everything; therefore he is one whose darkness is destroyed in every way and concerning all things.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The expression contains two universals:

| Expression | Function |
|---|---|
| **sarvathā** | in every way; complete with respect to mode |
| **sarvasmin** | concerning everything; complete with respect to field |

The Buddha’s knowledge is unrestricted both intensively and extensively.

---

### D. Darkness as ignorance

> *ajñānaṃ hi bhūtārthadarśanapratibandhāndhakāram.*

### Translation

> For ignorance is the darkness that obstructs the seeing of things as they really are.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The definition is:

```text
andhakāra
    =
ajñāna
    =
bhūtārtha-darśana-pratibandha
    obstruction of seeing real meaning / reality as it is
```

Darkness is therefore a cognitive obstruction, not merely a rhetorical image.

---

### E. Why the Buddha’s destruction is absolute

> *tac ca bhagavato buddhasya pratipakṣalābhenātyantaṃ sarvathā sarvatra jñeye punar anutpattidharmatvād dhatam.*

### Translation

> And in the Blessed Buddha it has been destroyed absolutely, in every way and regarding every knowable, through the attainment of its counteragent, because it has the determination of never arising again.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The Bhāṣya supplies the ground of the Kārikā’s completed determination:

```text
pratipakṣa-lābha
    attainment of the counteragent
        ↓
atyanta-hāna
    absolute destruction
        ↓
punar-anutpatti-dharmatva
    the determination of never arising again
```

The absence of ignorance is not an empty negation. It is a mediated result grounded in the attainment of its counteragent.

---

### F. Contrast with pratyekabuddhas and śrāvakas

> *pratyekabuddhaśrāvakā api kāmaṃ sarvatra hatāndhakārāḥ kliṣṭasaṃmohātyantavigamāt.*
>
> *na tu sarvathā.*

### Translation

> Pratyekabuddhas and śrāvakas indeed have darkness destroyed everywhere, because afflicted delusion has been completely eliminated—but not in every way.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The distinction is:

```text
sarvatra hatāndhakārāḥ
    free from darkness throughout the field relevant to liberation

not

sarvathā hatāndhakārāḥ
    free from every possible mode of ignorance
```

Their afflicted ignorance has been eliminated, yet non-afflicted absence of knowledge can remain.

---

### G. Non-afflicted ignorance

> *tathā hy eṣāṃ buddhadharmeṣv ativiprakṛṣṭadeśakāleṣv artheṣu cānantaprabhedeṣu bhavaty evākliṣṭam ajñānam.*

### Translation

> For they still possess non-afflicted ignorance concerning the qualities unique to Buddhas, concerning things at extremely remote places and times, and concerning meanings divided into limitless varieties.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The Bhāṣya sharply distinguishes:

```text
kliṣṭa ajñāna
    afflicted ignorance
    bondage-producing obscuration

akliṣṭa ajñāna
    non-afflicted ignorance
    absence of exhaustive knowledge without bondage
```

The śrāvaka or pratyekabuddha may be liberated without possessing the Buddha’s unrestricted knowledge.

---

## 4. The Two Accomplishments

### A. Accomplishment directed toward his own benefit

> *ity ātmahitapratipattisaṃpadā saṃstutya...*

### Translation

> Having praised him through the accomplishment of the practice directed toward his own benefit...

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The complete destruction of ignorance constitutes `ātmahita-pratipatti-saṃpad`: the perfection attained in the Buddha himself.

### B. Accomplishment directed toward the benefit of others

> *punas tam eva bhagavantaṃ parahitapratipattisaṃpadā saṃstauti—saṃsārapaṅkājjagadujjahāreti.*

### Translation

> He then praises that same Blessed One through the accomplishment of the practice directed toward the benefit of others, with the words: “He raised the world from the mire of saṃsāra.”

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The two descriptions of the Buddha form a precise pair:

```text
sarvathā-sarva-hatāndhakāraḥ
    accomplishment of one’s own benefit

saṃsāra-paṅkāt jagad ujjahāra
    accomplishment of the benefit of others
```

---

## 5. Saṃsāra as Mire

> *saṃsāro hi jagadāsaṅgasthānatvād duruttaratvāc ca paṅkabhūtaḥ.*

### Translation

> Saṃsāra is like mire because it is a place in which the world becomes stuck and because it is difficult to cross out of.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The metaphor is defined by two marks:

| Feature of mire | Determination of saṃsāra |
|---|---|
| one becomes stuck in it | `jagad-āsaṅga-sthāna` |
| it is difficult to escape | `duruttaratva` |

---

## 6. How the Buddha Raises the World

> *tatrāvamagnaṃ jagad atrāṇam anukampamāno bhagavān saddharmadeśanāhastapradānair yathābhavyam abhyuddhṛtavān.*

### Translation

> Moved by compassion for the world submerged there and without protection, the Blessed One raised it up according to its capacities by extending the hands of teaching the True Dharma.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The movement is:

```text
world submerged in mire
    ↓
without protection
    ↓
Buddha moved by compassion
    ↓
teaching of the True Dharma as an extended hand
    ↓
raising beings according to their capacities
```

The qualification `yathābhavyam` indicates that the Buddha teaches according to the capacity or disposition of those taught.

---

## 7. Homage

> *iti ya evam ātmaparahitapratipattisaṃpadā yuktas tasmai namaskṛtyeti śirasā praṇipatya.*

### Translation

> Thus, “having bowed to him” means: having prostrated with the head before the one endowed in this way with the accomplishments of practice for both his own benefit and the benefit of others.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

Homage follows from the unity of perfected knowing and compassionate instruction.

---

## 8. The Teacher According to Reality

> *yathārtham aviparītaṃ śāstīti yathārthaśāstā.*

### Translation

> He teaches reality correctly, without inversion; therefore he is the teacher according to reality.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

```text
yathārtham
    according to real meaning / reality

aviparītam
    without inversion or error

śāsti
    he teaches, instructs, disciplines
```

The Buddha is the teacher according to reality because his instruction is non-inverted.

---

## 9. The Means of Benefiting Others

> *anena parahitapratipattyupāyam asyāviṣkaroti.*

### Translation

> By this expression, the means of his practice for the benefit of others is revealed.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The distinction is:

```text
benefit of others
    = raising the world from saṃsāra

means of that benefit
    = teaching reality without inversion
```

### Not through miraculous power

> *yathābhūtaśāsanāc chāstā bhavan asau saṃsārapaṅkājjagad ujjahāra, na tv ṛddhivarapradānaprabhāveṇa.*

### Translation

> Being a Teacher through instruction according to things as they are, he raised the world from the mire of saṃsāra—not through the power of granting miraculous favors.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

This is the strongest philosophical determination in the opening unit:

```text
yathābhūta-śāsana
    instruction according to reality
```

rather than:

```text
ṛddhi
    supernatural power

vara-pradāna
    bestowal of favors or boons
```

Liberation is mediated by truthful teaching and knowing. It is not externally conferred.

---

## 10. Announcement of the Treatise

> *tasmai namaskṛtya kiṃ kariṣyāmīty āha—śāstraṃ pravakṣyāmi.*

### Translation

> What will he do after bowing to him? He says: “I shall proclaim the treatise.”

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

> *śiṣyaśāsanāc chāstram.*

### Translation

> It is a śāstra because it instructs disciples.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The verbal relation is explicit:

```text
śās
    to teach, discipline, instruct

śāstṛ
    teacher

śāstra
    that which instructs
```

> *katamac chāstram ity āha—abhidharmakośam.*

### Translation

> Which treatise? He says: “The Abhidharmakośa.”

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

This closes the natural opening unit and leads directly into the question opening VAK 1.02: What is this thing called Abhidharma?

---

## 11. Argument Summary

```text
Ignorance obstructs seeing real meaning.
        ↓
The Buddha attains its counteragent.
        ↓
Ignorance becomes incapable of arising again.
        ↓
He possesses unrestricted knowledge.
        ↓
This perfects his own benefit.
        ↓
Out of compassion, he teaches beings according to their capacities.
        ↓
Truthful, non-inverted teaching is the means of benefiting others.
        ↓
He raises the world through Dharma instruction,
not through miraculous donation of liberation.
        ↓
Vasubandhu bows to this Teacher.
        ↓
The Abhidharmakośa continues the activity of instruction.
```

The unity of the opening is:

```text
true knowing
    → truthful teaching
    → liberation through instruction
```

## 12. New Technical Terms

### **guṇākhyāna**

- **provisional meaning:** declaration or narration of qualities
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** explains why homage is preceded by an account of the Buddha’s excellence

### **bhūtārthadarśanapratibandha**

- **provisional meaning:** obstruction of seeing real meaning or reality as it is
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** definition of ignorance as darkness

### **pratipakṣalābha**

- **provisional meaning:** attainment of the counteragent
- **status:** absent from VAK 1.01; the language of counteragents becomes important throughout the Kośa
- **function:** supplies the ground through which ignorance is absolutely destroyed

### **punar-anutpatti-dharmatva**

- **provisional meaning:** possessing the determination of never arising again
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** distinguishes absolute destruction from temporary absence

### **kliṣṭasaṃmoha**

- **provisional meaning:** afflicted delusion
- **status:** absent from VAK 1.01
- **function:** identifies the ignorance completely abandoned by śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas

### **akliṣṭājñāna**

- **provisional meaning:** non-afflicted ignorance
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** distinguishes liberation from the Buddha’s exhaustive knowledge

### **ātmahitapratipattisaṃpad**

- **provisional meaning:** accomplishment of practice directed toward one’s own benefit
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** classifies the complete destruction of darkness

### **parahitapratipattisaṃpad**

- **provisional meaning:** accomplishment of practice directed toward the benefit of others
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** classifies the Buddha’s raising of the world through instruction

### **yathābhavyam**

- **provisional meaning:** according to the capacity or spiritual potential of the recipient
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** qualifies how the Buddha teaches and raises beings

### **saddharmadeśanā**

- **provisional meaning:** teaching or exposition of the True Dharma
- **status:** absent from VAK 1.01
- **function:** identifies the effective means by which the Buddha raises the world

### **yathābhūtaśāsana**

- **provisional meaning:** instruction according to things as they really are
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** identifies the Buddha’s proper means of liberation

### **ṛddhivarapradānaprabhāva**

- **provisional meaning:** the power of granting miraculous favors
- **status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **function:** rejected alternative account of how the Buddha liberates beings

## 14. Kārikā Translation Decision

The Bhāṣya requires revising the first-pass treatment of `yathārthaśāstre`.

### First-pass interpretation

```text
yathārthaśāstre
    in accordance with the teaching of reality
```

### Bhāṣya-supported interpretation

```text
tasmai yathārthaśāstre
    to that teacher according to reality
```

The philosophical translation should also preserve the Bhāṣya’s determination that:

```text
The Buddha raises the world through non-inverted Dharma instruction,
not by miraculously granting liberation.
```
