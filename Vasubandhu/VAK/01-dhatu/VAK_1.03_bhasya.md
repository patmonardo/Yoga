# VAK_1.03 Bhāṣya

## Why Abhidharma Must Be Taught

This file records the Bhāṣya-pass study for `VAK_1.03.md`.

The Kārikā file remains unchanged in this commit. Where the Bhāṣya sharpens or qualifies the first-pass analysis, the result is recorded here for later Kārikā revision.

---

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

### IAST

> dharmāṇāṃ pravicayam antareṇa nāsti kleśānāṃ yata upaśāntaye 'bhyupāyaḥ /
>
> kleśaiś ca bhramati bhavārṇave 'tra lokas taddhetor ata uditaḥ kilaiṣa śāstraḥ // VAkK_1.03 //

### Bhāṣya-informed translation

> Since there is no means for the pacification of the afflictions without discriminative examination of dharmas, and since the world wanders in this great ocean of saṃsāra through those afflictions, therefore—on account of that cause—this śāstra is said to have been taught.

The Bhāṣya makes clear that the verse answers two connected questions left open by VAK 1.02:

```text
Why is Abhidharma taught?
    because Dharma-discrimination is required for pacifying kleśa

Why is Dharma-discrimination required?
    because kleśas cause the world to wander in saṃsāra
```

The verse therefore gives not merely the usefulness but the necessity of Abhidharma instruction.

---

## 2. Natural Bhāṣya Unit

The natural unit extends from:

> *yato na vinā dharmapravicayenāsti kleśopaśamābhyupāyaḥ...*

through:

> *...ity āhur vaibhāṣikāḥ.*

It closes when Vasubandhu asks which dharmas are to be discriminated and thereby introduces VAK 1.04.

The argumentative movement is:

```text
no pacification of kleśa without Dharma-discrimination
    ↓
kleśas cause the world to wander in saṃsāra
    ↓
Abhidharma is taught for the sake of Dharma-discrimination
    ↓
the disciple cannot discriminate dharmas without instruction
    ↓
Vaibhāṣika account of how the dispersed teaching was collected
    ↓
question: which dharmas are to be discriminated?
```

## 3. Bhāṣya Analysis

### A. No pacification without Dharma-discrimination

> *yato na vinā dharmapravicayenāsti kleśopaśamābhyupāyaḥ.*

### Translation

> Because without discriminative examination of dharmas there is no means for the pacification of the afflictions.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The Bhāṣya restates the first half of the Kārikā as a strict dependence:

```text
kleśa-upaśama
    pacification of affliction

requires

dharma-pravicaya
    discriminative examination of dharmas
```

`pravicaya` is not presented as one optional intellectual exercise among others. It is the indispensable cognitive operation by which the afflictions can be pacified.

The relation is practical as well as theoretical:

```text
not knowing the dharmas distinctly
    sustains affliction

knowing them through pravicaya
    makes pacification possible
```

---

### B. Kleśas cause wandering in saṃsāra

> *kleśāś ca lokaṃ bhramayanti saṃsāramahārṇave 'smin.*

### Translation

> And the afflictions cause the world to wander in this great ocean of saṃsāra.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The Bhāṣya expands `bhavārṇava`, the ocean of becoming, as:

> *saṃsāra-mahārṇava* — the great ocean of saṃsāra.

The causal structure is explicit:

```text
kleśas
    ↓
cause wandering
    ↓
world caught in the great ocean of saṃsāra
```

The world does not wander merely because conditioned existence is spatially or cosmologically vast. It wanders because kleśas actively propel and disorient it.

The Bhāṣya thus identifies the practical problem that gives Dharma-discrimination its necessity:

```text
pravicaya is required
    because
kleśa is causally effective
```

---

### C. The purpose for which Abhidharma was taught

> *atas tad-dhetos tasya dharmapravicayasyārthe śāstraṃ kila buddhenābhidharma uktaḥ.*

### Translation

> Therefore, for that reason, Abhidharma is said to have been taught as śāstra by the Buddha for the sake of that discriminative examination of dharmas.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The chain of purpose is:

```text
world wanders through kleśa
    ↓
kleśa must be pacified
    ↓
pacification requires Dharma-discrimination
    ↓
Abhidharma is taught for the sake of Dharma-discrimination
```

The śāstra is therefore not first justified by its completeness, authority, antiquity, or taxonomic scope. It is justified by the practical necessity of `dharmapravicaya`.

The particle `kila` should remain visible. It may carry the sense “as is said” or “according to the received account.” The Bhāṣya soon makes the received Vaibhāṣika account explicit.

---

### D. The disciple requires Abhidharma instruction

> *na hi vinābhidharmopadeśena śiṣyaḥ śakto dharmān pravicetum iti.*

### Translation

> For without instruction in Abhidharma, a disciple is not capable of discriminatively examining the dharmas.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

This sentence adds a mediation absent from the compressed Kārikā:

```text
dharmas
    are to be discriminated

but

the disciple cannot yet discriminate them unaided

therefore

Abhidharma instruction is necessary
```

The relation between śāstra and prajñā established in VAK 1.02 now becomes pedagogically concrete.

```text
śāstra / upadeśa
    instruction
        ↓
capacity for dharmapravicaya
        ↓
kleśopaśama
```

The śāstra is not itself the final stainless prajñā. It is the instructional mediation through which the disciple becomes capable of the discriminative activity leading toward it.

---

### E. The Vaibhāṣika account of collection

> *sa tu prakīrṇa ukto bhagavatā bhadantakātyāyanīputraprabhṛtibhiḥ piṇḍīkṛtya sthāpito bhadantadharmatrāto dānavargīyakaraṇavad ity āhur vaibhāṣikāḥ.*

### Translation

> The Vaibhāṣikas say that it was spoken in dispersed form by the Blessed One and was collected and established by the venerable Kātyāyanīputra and others, just as the venerable Dharmatrāta formed the Udānavarga.

**Voice:** Vaibhāṣika position

This is the first explicitly marked school attribution in the opening chapter.

The account distinguishes:

```text
Buddha
    speaks Abhidharma in dispersed form

Kātyāyanīputra and others
    collect and establish it systematically

Dharmatrāta / Udānavarga
    supplies the analogy for compilation
```

The phrase `ity āhur vaibhāṣikāḥ` must govern the entire historical explanation. It should not be silently presented as Vasubandhu's own unqualified historical claim.

The Bhāṣya thereby distinguishes two moments:

```text
dispersed teaching
    prakīrṇa

systematic collection
    piṇḍīkṛtya sthāpita
```

This is important for the status of śāstra. The systematic treatise is not said to be a verbatim single discourse delivered in its present arrangement. It is presented, according to the Vaibhāṣikas, as the collection and establishment of teachings dispersed through the Buddha's instruction.

---

### F. Transition to the field of dharmas

> *katame punas te dharmā yeṣāṃ pravicayārtham abhidharmopadeśa ity āha...*

### Translation

> But which are those dharmas for whose discriminative examination the instruction of Abhidharma is given? He states...

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

This question closes the introductory justification and opens the systematic field itself.

The sequence of the first three Kārikās is now complete:

```text
VAK 1.01
    the truthful Teacher and the act of instruction

VAK 1.02
    what Abhidharma is

VAK 1.03
    why Abhidharma instruction is necessary

VAK 1.04
    which dharmas are to be discriminated
```

VAK 1.03 therefore functions as the transition from the definition of Abhidharma to its determinate field.

---

## 4. Argument Summary

```text
Kleśas cause the world to wander in saṃsāra.
        ↓
Their pacification requires discriminative examination of dharmas.
        ↓
A disciple cannot perform that examination without instruction.
        ↓
Abhidharma is taught as the indispensable instructional mediation.
        ↓
According to the Vaibhāṣikas, the Buddha's dispersed teachings
were collected and systematically established by Kātyāyanīputra and others.
        ↓
The inquiry can now ask which dharmas are to be discriminated.
```

The central relation is:

```text
Abhidharma instruction
    → capacity for Dharma-discrimination
    → pacification of kleśa
    → release from wandering
```

## 5. New Technical Terms

### **kleśopaśama**

- **Provisional meaning:** pacification of the afflictions
- **Status:** the Kārikā contains the verbal idea `kleśānām upaśāntaye`; the compact nominal form appears in the Bhāṣya
- **Function:** states the practical end requiring Dharma-discrimination

### **dharmapravicaya**

- **Provisional meaning:** discriminative examination of dharmas
- **Status:** present analytically in the Kārikā; repeated as a compact technical expression in the Bhāṣya
- **Function:** indispensable method for the pacification of kleśa

### **saṃsāramahārṇava**

- **Provisional meaning:** the great ocean of saṃsāra
- **Status:** expands the Kārikā's `bhavārṇava`
- **Function:** intensifies and interprets the field in which kleśas cause wandering

### **abhidharmopadeśa**

- **Provisional meaning:** instruction or doctrinal teaching in Abhidharma
- **Status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **Function:** pedagogical mediation enabling the disciple to discriminate dharmas

### **śiṣya**

- **Provisional meaning:** disciple, one who is to be instructed
- **Status:** absent from the Kārikā; anticipated by the definition `śiṣyaśāsanāc chāstram` in the commentary on VAK 1.01
- **Function:** identifies the recipient whose capacity must be generated by instruction

### **prakīrṇa**

- **Provisional meaning:** dispersed, scattered through separate teachings
- **Status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **Function:** characterizes the original mode in which Abhidharma was taught by the Buddha according to the Vaibhāṣikas

### **piṇḍīkṛtya**

- **Provisional meaning:** having collected or gathered into a whole
- **Status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **Function:** describes the compilation of dispersed teachings

### **sthāpita**

- **Provisional meaning:** established, set into systematic form
- **Status:** absent from the Kārikā
- **Function:** describes the doctrinal organization following collection

### **Vaibhāṣika**

- **Provisional meaning:** representative of the Vaibhāṣika doctrinal tradition
- **Status:** absent from the Kārikā; first explicit school attribution in this Bhāṣya sequence
- **Function:** marks the historical compilation account as a school position

## 7. Kārikā Translation Decision

The first-pass translation is broadly sound, but the Bhāṣya suggests several refinements.

### A. Preserve the necessity claim

`nāsti ... abhyupāyaḥ` should be rendered strongly:

```text
there is no means
```

rather than merely:

```text
Dharma-analysis is useful
```

The verse claims necessity, not preference.

### B. Make the causal role of kleśa explicit

The instrumental `kleśaiḥ` is interpreted causally by the Bhāṣya:

```text
kleśas cause the world to wander
```

### C. Retain the received-doctrine marker `kila`

A later revision should avoid suppressing `kila`. A provisional rendering is:

> “this śāstra is said to have been taught.”

This becomes especially significant because the following prose explicitly attributes the compilation account to the Vaibhāṣikas.

### D. Distinguish verse and Bhāṣya terminology

The Kārikā says `bhavārṇava`, while the Bhāṣya glosses it as `saṃsāramahārṇava`. The Kārikā translation should preserve “ocean of becoming,” with the Bhāṣya explaining it as the great ocean of saṃsāra.

No change is made to `VAK_1.03.md` in this commit.

## 8. Provisional Organon Contact Point

The Bhāṣya provides a more determinate account of the relation between principle and instruction:

```text
prajñā
    is the discriminative power

śāstra / upadeśa
    generates the disciple's capacity to exercise that power

pravicaya
    applies it to the field of dharmas

upaśama
    is its practical result against kleśa
```

The decisive point is not that taxonomy somehow produces liberation externally. Rather, truthful instruction mediates the disciple's own capacity for discrimination.

```text
teaching
    is not liberation bestowed

teaching
    is the generation of the capacity by which the field can be discriminated
```

This preserves the principle established in VAK 1.01: the Buddha raises beings through instruction according to reality, not by miraculous donation. VAK 1.03 now specifies the inner operation of that instruction as `dharmapravicaya`.
