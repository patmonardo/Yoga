# VAK_1.31 Bhāṣya Study

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ārūpyāptā manodharmamanovijñānadhātavaḥ /
>
> sāsravānāsravā ete trayaḥ śeṣās tu sāsravāḥ // VAkK_1.31 //

### Translation

> The mind-domain, the dharma-domain, and the mental-consciousness-domain belong to the formless realm. These three may be either with outflows or without outflows; the remaining domains are only with outflows.

## 2. Natural Bhāṣya Unit

> ārūpyāptā manodharmamanovijñānadhātavaḥ /
>
> rūpavītarāgāṇāṃ tatropapattir ato 'tra daśa rūpasvabhāvā dhātavas tadāśrayālambanāś ca pañca vijñānadhātavo na saṃbhavanti //
>
> kati sāsravāḥ katy anāsravāḥ / ya ete manodharmamanovijñānadhātava uktāḥ
>
> sāsravānāsravā ete trayaḥ
>
> ye mārgasatyāsaṃskṛtasaṃgṛhītās te 'nāsravā anye sāsravāḥ /
>
> śeṣās tu sāsravāḥ // VAkK_1.31 //
>
> pañcadaśa dhātavaḥ śeṣās tv ekāntasāsravāḥ //

### Translation

> The mind-domain, the dharma-domain, and the mental-consciousness-domain belong to the formless realm.
>
> Beings who are detached from form are reborn there. Therefore the ten domains whose intrinsic nature is material, together with the five consciousness-domains that depend upon and take those material domains as objects, do not occur there.
>
> How many domains are with outflows, and how many are without outflows? Concerning the mind-domain, dharma-domain, and mental-consciousness-domain just mentioned:
>
> These three may be either with outflows or without outflows.
>
> Those included within the truth of the path and within the unconditioned are without outflows; the others are with outflows.
>
> But the remaining domains are with outflows.
>
> The remaining fifteen domains are exclusively with outflows.

## 3. Bhāṣya Analysis

### 3.1 The formless realm contains only three dhātus

The Bhāṣya grounds the formless realm in detachment from form:

```text
rūpavītarāga
    → rebirth in ārūpya
```

From this, two exclusions follow.

First, the ten material dhātus do not occur there:

```text
five material faculties
+ five material objects
= ten rūpasvabhāva dhātus
```

Second, the five sensory consciousness-domains do not occur there because their supports and objects are absent:

```text
material support absent
+ material object absent
    → corresponding sensory vijñāna absent
```

Thus only these remain:

```text
manodhātu
dharmadhātu
manovijñānadhātu
```

The realm distinction is therefore structural and relational, not merely spatial.

### 3.2 The last three dhātus have mixed outflow-status

The three non-material domains can be either:

```text
sāsrava
or
anāsrava
```

The Bhāṣya gives the criterion:

```text
included in mārgasatya
or
included among the asaṃskṛtas
    → anāsrava

otherwise
    → sāsrava
```

This means that neither “mental” nor “non-material” by itself implies purity. The decisive issue is doctrinal inclusion and function.

### 3.3 The remaining fifteen are necessarily sāsrava

The other fifteen domains are `ekāntasāsrava`: exclusively with outflows.

These are:

```text
ten material dhātus
+ five sensory consciousness-dhātus
```

They belong wholly to conditioned, appropriated experience and cannot themselves be counted as outflow-free.

### 3.4 The scope of anāsrava dharmadhātu

The Bhāṣya’s formula includes:

```text
mārgasatya
+ asaṃskṛta
```

within the outflow-free side of dharmadhātu.

This preserves the distinction established earlier:

```text
skandhas
    include conditioned dharmas only

āyatanas and dhātus
    include both conditioned and unconditioned dharmas
```

## 4. Argument Summary

```text
Detachment from form
    → no material dhātus
    → no sensory vijñānas dependent on them
    → only mind, dharma, and mental-consciousness remain

Among these three:
    Path-truth and unconditioned content
        → anāsrava
    other content
        → sāsrava

Remaining fifteen dhātus
    → necessarily sāsrava
```

The verse therefore performs two classifications at once:

1. realm-membership;
2. outflow-status.

## 5. New Technical Terms

### ārūpyāpta

- **Provisional meaning:** belonging to the formless realm
- **Status:** Bhāṣya technical classification
- **Function:** marks the three dhātus that remain when material support and object are absent

### rūpavītarāga

- **Provisional meaning:** detached from form
- **Status:** causal condition for rebirth in the formless realm
- **Function:** explains why material dhātus do not occur there

### sāsrava

- **Provisional meaning:** with outflows
- **Status:** doctrinal classification
- **Function:** marks dharmas implicated in appropriation, defilement, and saṃsāric continuation

### anāsrava

- **Provisional meaning:** without outflows
- **Status:** doctrinal classification
- **Function:** applies here to what is included in the truth of the path and in the unconditioned

### ekāntasāsrava

- **Provisional meaning:** exclusively with outflows
- **Status:** strict classification
- **Function:** characterizes the remaining fifteen dhātus without exception

## 6. Voice Register

- **Kārikā voice:** states the realm and outflow classifications.
- **Vasubandhu’s expository voice:** explains the absence of the ten material domains and five sensory consciousness-domains in the formless realm.
- **Questioning voice:** asks how many domains are with or without outflows.
- **Doctrinal reply:** identifies Path-truth and the unconditioned as anāsrava.
- **No explicit rival-school attribution:** the passage is presented as the operative Abhidharma classification.

## 7. Kārikā Translation Decision

The existing Kārikā translation should be refined in two ways.

First, `ārūpyāptāḥ` should be translated explicitly as “belong to the formless realm,” not merely “are found in the formless.” The Bhāṣya makes this a structural membership claim grounded in rebirth after detachment from form.

Second, `śeṣās tu sāsravāḥ` should be rendered strongly:

> the remaining fifteen domains are exclusively with outflows.

The word `ekānta` appears in the Bhāṣya and removes ambiguity.

## 8. Provisional Organon Contact Point

The Bhāṣya distinguishes a field by what supports, objects, and corresponding cognitions can occur within it:

```text
realm
    = lawful configuration of possible domains
```

The formless realm is not simply “less sensory.” It is a field in which the material support-object-consciousness triads are absent, leaving only the mental triad.

A provisional Organon contact point is therefore:

```text
field-structure
    determines
possible modes of manifestation and cognition
```

This remains our reconstruction. Vasubandhu’s explicit concern is the Abhidharma classification of dhātus by realm and outflow-status.
