# VAK_1.16 — Bhāṣya Study

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vijñānaṃ prativijñaptiḥ mana āyatanaṃ ca tat /
>
> dhātavaḥ sapta ca matāḥ ṣaḍvijñānāny atho manaḥ // VAkK_1.16 //

### Working translation

> Vijñāna is apprehension with respect to each particular object. That same vijñānaskandha is the mind-sphere. As domains it is accepted as seven: the six vijñānas and mind.

The verse completes the initial coordination of the three classificatory systems:

```text
skandha
āyatana
dhātu
```

Its subject is the **vijñānaskandha**, now redescribed first as one āyatana and then as seven dhātus.

---

## 2. Natural Bhāṣya Unit

> vijñānaṃ prativijñaptiḥ
>
> viṣayaṃ viṣayaṃ prati vijñaptir upalabdhir vijñānaskandha ity ucyate /
>
> sa punaḥ ṣaḍ vijñānakāyāḥ — cakṣurvijñānaṃ yāvan manovijñānam iti /
>
> ya eṣa vijñānaskandha ukta āyatanavyavasthāyāṃ
>
> mana āyatanaṃ ca tat /
>
> dhātuvyavasthāyāṃ sa eva
>
> dhātavaḥ sapta ca matāḥ /
>
> katame sapta /
>
> ṣaḍ vijñānāny atho manaḥ // VAkK_1.16 //
>
> cakṣurvijñānadhātur yāvan manovijñānadhātur manodhātuś ca /
>
> evam atra pañca skandhā dvādaśāyatanāny aṣṭādaśa dhātavo nirdiṣṭā bhavanti /
>
> avijñaptivarjyo rūpaskandho daśāyatanāni daśa dhātavaḥ /
>
> vedanādayaḥ skandhās trayo 'vijñaptir asaṃskṛtāni ca dharmāyatanaṃ dharmadhātuś ca /
>
> vijñānaskandho mana āyatanaṃ ṣaḍ vijñānadhātavo manodhātuś ceti //

### Translation

> “Vijñāna is prativijñapti.”
>
> Apprehension—*upalabdhi*—with respect to each particular object is called the aggregate of vijñāna.
>
> This, in turn, consists of the six bodies of vijñāna: eye-vijñāna through mind-vijñāna.
>
> That which has been stated as the aggregate of vijñāna is, in the arrangement of the spheres, the mind-sphere.
>
> In the arrangement of the domains, that same aggregate is accepted as seven domains.
>
> Which seven?
>
> The six vijñānas and mind: the eye-vijñāna-domain through the mind-vijñāna-domain, together with the mind-domain.
>
> Thus five aggregates, twelve spheres, and eighteen domains have now been stated.
>
> The form aggregate, excluding avijñapti, constitutes ten spheres and ten domains.
>
> The three aggregates beginning with feeling, together with avijñapti and the unconditioned dharmas, constitute the dharma-sphere and dharma-domain.
>
> The aggregate of vijñāna constitutes the mind-sphere, the six vijñāna-domains, and the mind-domain.

## 3. Bhāṣya Analysis

### 3.1 `prativijñapti` is glossed by `upalabdhi`

**Voice:** Vasubandhu's explanatory voice.

The Bhāṣya does not leave `prativijñapti` as an abstract verbal formation. It supplies the direct gloss:

```text
viṣayaṃ viṣayaṃ prati vijñaptiḥ
    = upalabdhiḥ
```

That is:

```text
with respect to each particular object-domain
    there is a corresponding apprehension
```

The distributive repetition `viṣayaṃ viṣayam` matters. Vijñāna is not introduced as an undifferentiated field of awareness. It is always articulated according to a determinate object:

```text
visible form  → eye-vijñāna
sound         → ear-vijñāna
smell         → nose-vijñāna
taste         → tongue-vijñāna
tangible      → body-vijñāna
dharma        → mind-vijñāna
```

The definition is therefore relational and domain-specific.

This strongly supports the project rendering **knowledge**, provided that “knowledge” is understood here in its minimal Abhidharma sense of object-specific apprehension, not necessarily true judgment or wisdom.

### 3.2 The vijñānaskandha is sixfold

The aggregate is one classificatory totality, but its members are six bodies or series of vijñāna:

```text
vijñānaskandha
    = six vijñānakāyas
```

The word `kāya` prevents us from imagining only six isolated acts. Each type forms a body, class, or stream of events governed by a corresponding support and object-domain.

Thus unity and plurality coexist:

```text
one aggregate
    six classes of apprehension
```

### 3.3 The same material is reorganized under three systems

Vasubandhu is not adding new entities whenever he moves from skandha to āyatana to dhātu. He is reorganizing the same dharmas according to different analytic purposes.

For vijñāna:

```text
as aggregate:
    vijñānaskandha

as sphere:
    mana-āyatana

as domains:
    six vijñānadhātus + manodhātu
```

This is one of the clearest textual grounds for reading the dhātus as **knowledge domains**. Six of the seven dhātus stated here are explicitly object-specific vijñāna-domains.

The seventh, manodhātu, will require the next verse's explanation. It must not yet be treated as a separate substantial mind.

### 3.4 The completed classificatory map

The Bhāṣya pauses to summarize the whole system established so far.

#### Five aggregates

```text
rūpa
vedanā
saṃjñā
saṃskāra
vijñāna
```

#### Twelve spheres

```text
five sensory faculties
five sensory objects
mana-āyatana
dharma-āyatana
```

#### Eighteen domains

```text
six supports
six objects
six vijñānas
```

The present wording temporarily counts `manodhātu` alongside the six vijñāna-domains. VAK 1.17 will explain why the total is eighteen even though manodhātu is not a seventh substance additional to the six vijñānas.

### 3.5 Exact distribution of the aggregates

The summary gives a precise conversion table.

#### Rūpaskandha

```text
rūpaskandha minus avijñapti
    = ten āyatanas
    = ten dhātus
```

These are the five faculties and five objects.

Avijñapti is excluded here because it belongs to the dharma-sphere and dharma-domain rather than to one of the ten sensory divisions.

#### Three middle aggregates

```text
vedanā
saṃjñā
saṃskāra
+ avijñapti
+ three unconditioned dharmas
    = dharmāyatana
    = dharmadhātu
```

#### Vijñānaskandha

```text
vijñānaskandha
    = mana-āyatana
    = six vijñānadhātus + manodhātu
```

The classificatory systems therefore overlap without confusion because each answers a different question.

### 3.6 Dhātu as a relational knowledge architecture

The passage supports a cautious formulation of the user's insight:

```text
five object-domains
    are correlated with
five sensory vijñāna-domains

 dharmadhātu
    is correlated with
manovijñānadhātu
```

A dhātu is not merely an inert region containing things. Within the eighteenfold structure, every cognitive series is defined through a triad:

```text
support-domain
object-domain
vijñāna-domain
```

This makes “Knowledge Domain” a strong project rendering, especially for the six vijñānadhātus and their correlated fields.

However, the Bhāṣya does **not** yet say that all dhātus ontologically belong to Vijñānaskandha, nor does it identify Vijñānaskandha here with paramārthika Vidyā. Those are project-level interpretations that must remain provisional and be tested against the coming analysis.

## 4. Argument Summary

```text
1. Vijñāna is apprehension with respect to a particular object.

2. The vijñānaskandha consists of six classes of vijñāna.

3. In the āyatana system, that same aggregate is mana-āyatana.

4. In the dhātu system, it is distributed as:
       six vijñānadhātus
       + manodhātu

5. The classifications now total:
       five skandhas
       twelve āyatanas
       eighteen dhātus

6. These are not competing inventories.
   They are distinct organizations of the same dharmas.
```

The principal doctrinal result is:

```text
vijñāna
    is intrinsically object-indexed apprehension
```

It is not first a self-enclosed subject that later encounters an object.

## 5. New Technical Terms

### `prativijñapti`

- **Provisional meaning:** apprehension with respect to a particular object
- **Status:** explicit Kārikā definition, clarified by the Bhāṣya
- **Function:** defines vijñāna as object-specific disclosure or apprehension

### `upalabdhi`

- **Provisional meaning:** apprehension; cognitive taking-up
- **Status:** direct Bhāṣya gloss of `prativijñapti`
- **Function:** supplies the operative meaning of vijñāna in this passage

### `vijñānakāya`

- **Provisional meaning:** body, class, or series of vijñāna
- **Status:** Bhāṣya classification
- **Function:** distinguishes the six systematic types within the one vijñānaskandha

### `cakṣurvijñānadhātu` through `manovijñānadhātu`

- **Provisional meaning:** the six domains of object-specific knowledge
- **Status:** members of the eighteen-dhātu system
- **Function:** articulate cognition according to faculty and object-domain

### `manodhātu`

- **Provisional meaning:** mind-domain
- **Status:** seventh domain counted from the vijñānaskandha; exact identity explained in VAK 1.17
- **Function:** establishes the support of mind-vijñāna and cognitive succession

## 6. Voice Register

| Passage | Voice |
|---|---|
| `viṣayaṃ viṣayaṃ prati ... upalabdhiḥ` | Vasubandhu's explanatory definition |
| Enumeration of six vijñānakāyas | Standard Abhidharma exposition |
| Conversion among skandha, āyatana, and dhātu | Vasubandhu's systematic summary |
| No explicit `ity āhur vaibhāṣikāḥ` | No marked Vaibhāṣika attribution in this natural unit |

The unit is predominantly expository. The dialectical problem concerning the apparently additional manodhātu begins immediately afterward and belongs naturally to VAK 1.17.

## 7. Kārikā Translation Decision

The current Kārikā file renders:

> “Knowledge is prativijñapti”

and develops `prativijñapti` as “counter-disclosure / discriminative disclosure.”

The Bhāṣya requires a more controlled translation:

> **Vijñāna is apprehension with respect to each particular object.**

or, retaining the Sanskrit:

> **Vijñāna is object-specific prativijñapti, that is, apprehension.**

Recommended later revision:

```text
prativijñapti
    not primarily: counter-disclosure
    not yet: full discriminative knowledge

prativijñapti
    = apprehension corresponding to a particular object-domain
```

A second correction is needed in the Organon note. The Bhāṣya does not state that manas “coordinates” the six vijñānas. Its technical identity and function are established in VAK 1.17 through the immediately preceding vijñāna.

No change is made to the Kārikā file during the present Bhāṣya pass.

## 8. Provisional Organon Contact Point

The strongest provisional contact is a domain-structured model of knowledge:

```text
knowledge does not begin as an empty universal capacity

knowledge is always:
    this mode of apprehension
    with respect to this object-domain
    under this support-condition
```

The eighteen-dhātu system can therefore be read as a relational architecture:

```text
support
    ↕
object
    ↕
knowledge
```

This supports the project thesis:

```text
dhātus
    = articulated Knowledge Domains
```

But the text presently supports this most directly for the six vijñānadhātus and for the correlation of supports, objects, and vijñānas. The stronger metaphysical identification:

```text
all dhātus belong to Vijñānaskandha
Vijñānaskandha = supersensible paramārthika Vidyā
```

remains a provisional Organon hypothesis rather than an explicit claim of the Bhāṣya.

The next verse is decisive because it explains how **manodhātu** emerges from the succession of vijñāna without becoming an additional substance.
