## Prose Segmentation and Translation

### 1. The classificatory question

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

> Among the eighteen domains, which cuts, which is cut, which burns, which is burned, which weighs, and which is weighed?

The Bhāṣya expands the compact verse into six possible material predicates:

```text
cuts
is cut
burns
is burned
weighs
is weighed
```

The Kārikā directly settles only part of this field and preserves a dispute concerning the remainder.

### 2. The external tetrad

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

> The external tetrad of domains both cuts and is cut.

The tetrad is explicitly identified as:

```text
rūpa
    visible form

gandha
    odor

rasa
    taste

spraṣṭavya
    the tangible
```

These are designated through such ordinary complexes as an axe, wood, and similar material things.

Sound is excluded, as are the five material faculties.

### 3. What cutting is

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

> What kind of determination is cutting? It is the production of divided occurrence in a stream of aggregates whose members had arisen in connection.

The crucial Bhāṣya definition is:

```text
cheda
    = vibhakta-utpādana
      of a saṃghāta-srotas
      previously arising in sambandha
```

Cutting is therefore not the destruction of a single permanent thing. It is a reconfiguration of an aggregate-series:

```text
connected aggregate-series
    ↓ cutting
separately arising aggregate-series
```

The operation is explained genetically through the production of a divided continuation.

### 4. Why the faculties are not cut

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

> The body-faculty and the other faculties are not cut. Even when a limb is cut away completely, the faculty itself is not thereby divided into two. Faculties do not become twofold, for the severed limb is without the faculty.

This is the decisive refinement of the Kārikā.

The visible bodily organ and the **indriya** must not be treated as simply identical. When a bodily part is divided, the faculty does not become two faculties distributed across the two pieces. The removed part is described as `nirindriya`, without the faculty.

Thus:

```text
visible bodily member
    can be divided

indriya
    is not divided with it
```

The Bhāṣya therefore preserves the faculty as irreducible to the merely seen bodily aggregate.

### 5. Why the faculties do not cut

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

> Nor do the faculties themselves cut, because of their clarity, like the radiance of a jewel.

The transmitted wording is textually unstable, but the comparison is clear enough: the faculty is treated as subtle and lucid rather than as a gross obstructive mass capable of incision.

This is especially important for the project’s interpretation of the faculty:

```text
indriya
    is not merely a visible body-part
    is not itself an external cutting mass
    is not divided when the body-part is divided
```

The Bhāṣya leaves open precisely the distinction the user identified: the faculty can remain aligned with the power of seeing rather than being reduced to something merely seen.

### 6. Burning and weighing

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

> Just as the external tetrad cuts and is cut, that same tetrad is burned and weighs.

The faculties are again excluded because of their subtle clarity, compared with jewel-radiance. Sound is excluded because it is interrupted and does not persist as a material aggregate capable of these operations.

The verse therefore assigns burnability and weight-operation to the same four external domains.

### 7. The dispute

**Voice: Vasubandhu reporting divergent positions**

> There is dispute concerning what burns and what is weighed.

Two positions are reported:

**Voice: first position**

> The same tetrad of domains is itself the burner and the weighed/weighing material.

**Voice: second position**

> The fire-domain alone is the burner, and heaviness alone is what is weighed.

The first position attributes the operations to the concrete external material complex. The second isolates the specific operative determination:

```text
burning
    → fire-domain

being weighed
    → heaviness
```

The Bhāṣya records the disagreement without forcing a final resolution in this unit.

## Translation Consequences

The Bhāṣya confirms the external tetrad identified in the Kārikā analysis:

```text
rūpa

gandha

rasa

spraṣṭavya
```

It also significantly sharpens three terms.

### 1. *Chinatti / chidyate*

The project translation should not suggest substantial destruction. Cutting is:

> the production of a divided aggregate-continuation from a previously connected aggregate-continuation.

### 2. *Indriya*

The faculty cannot be translated as though it were simply the gross bodily organ. The Bhāṣya explicitly distinguishes the faculty from the limb that can be severed.

A stronger working determination is:

```text
indriya
    = faculty or governing power
    dependent upon a bodily basis
    but not identical with the visible bodily member
```

### 3. *Vivāda*

The disagreement concerns the subject of the operation:

```text
concrete material complex
    versus
specific operative determination
```

### Revised Philosophical Translation

> The four external material domains both divide connected material aggregates and undergo such division. They are also burnable and enter relations of weight. The faculties themselves are neither divided with bodily members nor gross cutting agents. A dispute remains whether burning and weighing belong to the concrete external tetrad as such, or specifically to the fire-domain and heaviness.

---

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya establishes a crucial distinction between:

```text
adhiṣṭhāna-like bodily member
    visible, gross, divisible

indriya
    subtle faculty
    not duplicated by bodily division
    absent from the severed member
```

This is not yet a declaration that the faculty is an immaterial seer. It remains a material faculty within the Kośa’s analysis. But Vasubandhu does refuse the naïve identity:

```text
faculty
    = visible anatomical part
```

The indriya is materially grounded without being exhausted by the seen gross member.

The passage also gives a process ontology of cutting. What is divided is not a persisting substance but a connected stream of aggregates. Division is a new mode of production within continuity.

---

## Logical Determination

VAK_1.35 established the structure of material constitution:

```text
great elements
    ↓
derivative materiality
    ↓
atomic aggregation
```

VAK_1.36 now introduces operation and internal differentiation:

```text
connected aggregate-stream
    ↓ division
separately produced aggregate-streams
```

The faculty introduces a second distinction:

```text
gross bodily basis
    ≠
subtle governing faculty
```

The faculty is present through the bodily organization but is not simply one more gross piece within that organization. Its relation to the bodily member is therefore neither external separation nor static identity.

This is a genuine reflective mediation:

```text
the faculty is embodied
but embodiment does not exhaust the faculty
```

---

## Organon Contact Point

The most important result is the refusal to collapse the power of apprehension into the apprehended bodily object.

The passage does not yet prove:

```text
indriya = Seer
```

But it preserves the logical space required for that determination:

```text
what is seen and divided
    = gross bodily aggregate

what governs seeing
    = indriya
    not duplicated or divided with the gross member
```

This is a new development for the Bhāṣya study: **the material status of an indriya does not force its reduction to visible objecthood**. Vasubandhu’s distinction allows material faculty to function as an operative power of apprehension rather than merely as one more item apprehended.

The cutting definition also exemplifies the living Copula of the project method. The connected aggregate and divided aggregate are not two unrelated states. Division is the mediation through which the former continues as the latter in altered production:

```text
connected
    → division
    → separately arising
```
