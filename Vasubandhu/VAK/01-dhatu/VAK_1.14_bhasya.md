# VAK_1.14 Bhāṣya

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> indriyārthās ta eveṣṭā daśāyatanadhātavaḥ /
>
> vedanānubhavaḥ saṃjñā nimittodgrahaṇātmikā // VAkK_1.14 //

> These very faculties and objects are accepted as the ten Relations and the ten Principles. Feeling is undergoing. Saṃjñā is the taking up of a mark.

Kārikā: `VAK_1.14.md`. Previous: 1.13. Next: 1.15.

## 2. Decisions

1. `te eva`: no new items. Five faculties and five objects, already counted in rūpa, sit in two arrangements. Not one hybrid.
2. The ten are faculties plus objects. Avijñapti is not among them. It stays for the dharma-Relation and the dharma-Principle.
3. Vedanā is anubhava: pleasant, painful, or neither. Sixfold, born of eye-contact through mind-contact. Undergoing, not citta.
4. Saṃjñā is mark-taking: blue, yellow, long, short, woman, man, friend, enemy, pleasant, painful. Sixfold on the same pattern. A mark, not a finished thing.
5. No order between vedanā and saṃjñā is stated.

## 3. Terms

Relation is pure composite chained access to appearance. Faculty to object. The chain is the access. Nothing is added when the count changes.

Principle is the second placement of the same factor. Logogenesis before Logic.

Saṃjñā is not Idea.

Prior note calling dhātu Territory, or saṃjñā Conceiving, is withdrawn.
