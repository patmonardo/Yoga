# VAK_7.42 — The Six Direct Knowledges

## Sanskrit

### Devanāgarī

```text
ऋद्धिश्रोत्रमनःपूर्वजन्मच्युत्युदयक्षये ।
ज्ञातसाक्षीक्रियाभिज्ञाः षड्विधा मुक्तिमार्गधीः ॥ ७.४२ ॥
```

### IAST

```text
ṛddhiśrotramanaḥpūrvajanmacyutyudayakṣaye /
jñāta-sākṣīkriyābhijñāḥ ṣaḍvidhā muktimārgadhīḥ // VAK_7.42 //
```

## Padaccheda

```text
ṛddhi
śrotra
manaḥ
pūrva-janma
cyuti-udaya
kṣaye

jñāta-sākṣī-kriyā
abhijñāḥ
ṣaṭ-vidhāḥ
mukti-mārga-dhīḥ
```

The first line names six fields:

```text
1. ṛddhi
2. śrotra
3. manaḥ
4. pūrva-janma
5. cyuti-udaya
6. kṣaya
```

These correspond to the six higher knowledges:

```text
ṛddhi-abhijñā
    extraordinary efficacy

divya-śrotra
    purified auditory cognition

para-citta-jñāna
    direct knowledge of another mind

pūrva-nivāsānusmṛti
    recollection of former lives

cyutyupapāda-jñāna
    knowledge of death and reappearance

āsrava-kṣaya-jñāna
    knowledge of the exhaustion of the influxes
```

## Literal Translation

The direct knowledges concerning extraordinary power, hearing, mind, former birth, death and arising, and exhaustion are sixfold. They are cognitions belonging to the path of liberation by which these fields are made directly known.

## Philosophical Translation

The six higher knowledges directly realize six increasingly comprehensive domains: effective command of embodied manifestation, cognition beyond ordinary sensory range, immediate knowledge of another’s mind, recollection of prior lives, vision of beings dying and reappearing according to their conditions, and finally direct knowledge that the afflictive influxes have been exhausted. They are liberative cognitions, not merely extraordinary experiences.

## Abhijñā — Direct Superior Knowledge

```text
abhi
    toward
    over
    in an intensified manner

jñā
    to know
```

A working translation is:

```text
abhijñā
    direct superior knowledge
```

The common principle of the six is not spectacle. It is the removal of a specific cognitive obstruction.

```text
ordinary cognition
    knows through limited instruments
    and indirect conditions

abhijñā
    makes a normally hidden field
    directly cognitively available
```

## 1. Ṛddhi — Effective Mastery of Manifestation

```text
ṛddhi
    accomplishment
    extraordinary efficacy
    developed power
```

The essential point is not marvel but operative capacity:

```text
ordinary embodiment
    appears as fixed limitation

ṛddhi
    demonstrates developed command
    over modes of embodied manifestation
```

This is cognition becoming effective power. Yet it remains only the first of the six and therefore cannot be identified with liberation.

## 2. Śrotra — Purified Auditory Cognition

```text
śrotra
    hearing
    auditory faculty
```

The divine ear extends auditory cognition beyond ordinary distance and coarseness.

```text
a sensory domain remains
    but its habitual limitation is removed
```

This is not transcendence of cognition as such, but extension and purification of a particular cognitive channel.

## 3. Manaḥ — Knowledge of Another Mind

```text
manaḥ
    mind
```

Another mind is ordinarily known only through expression and inference. Here its present state becomes directly determined.

```text
universal Path
    +
actual condition of this mind
        ↓
appropriate instruction
```

This is the cognitive basis of ground-sensitive teaching.

## 4. Pūrva-Janma — Former Lives

```text
pūrva
    former

janma
    birth
```

The present stream is shown as genetically conditioned:

```text
present existence
    is not self-grounded

it bears
    prior actions
    prior formations
    prior appropriations
```

What appears as present identity is comprehended through its history of formation.

## 5. Cyuti-Udaya — Death and Reappearance

```text
cyuti
    falling away
    death

udaya
    arising
    emergence
```

This knowledge sees the lawful relation among:

```text
conduct
disposition
karma
death
new arising
```

Becoming is disclosed as structured consequence rather than arbitrary migration.

## 6. Kṣaya — Exhaustion of the Influxes

```text
kṣaya
    exhaustion
    destruction
    termination
```

The sixth differs qualitatively from the first five.

The first five disclose expanded conditioned fields:

```text
powers
sounds
minds
former lives
death and rebirth
```

The sixth knows the ending of the condition of bondage itself.

```text
āsrava-kṣaya-jñāna
    is not merely wider cognition

it is direct knowledge
    of liberation
```

## The Five World-Directed Knowledges and the Sixth

The series has a decisive asymmetry:

```text
1. command of manifestation
2. extended sensory field
3. other minds
4. past continuity
5. conditioned arising
6. exhaustion of bondage
```

The first five expand reach within conditioned existence. The sixth establishes the liberative measure of that reach.

```text
expanded capacity
    ≠
freedom
```

Only when the powers are governed by knowledge of cessation do they belong fully to the liberation path.

## Jñāta-Sākṣī-Kriyā — Direct Cognitive Presence

The compressed expression indicates more than discursive information:

```text
jñāta
    made known

sākṣī
    directly witnessed

kriyā
    actualization or making
```

A cautious rendering is:

```text
abhijñā
    cognition that makes its object
    directly evident
```

The object is not merely inferred from a sign. The obstruction preventing its direct cognition has been overcome.

## Mukti-Mārga-Dhī — Cognition of the Liberation Path

The verse classifies the six as cognitions belonging to the path of liberation.

This supplies:

```text
their discipline
their direction
their ethical status
their ultimate criterion
```

A power may be extraordinary without being liberative. The sixth knowledge gives the measure of the first five:

```text
Can this capacity serve
    the cognition and ending of suffering?
```

## Pure Practical Reason

The six direct knowledges are a powerful presentation of practical reason in its raw operative form.

They are not only representations of what is. They are cultivated capacities that make determinate fields available for action.

```text
knowledge of manifestation
    becomes effective operation

knowledge of another mind
    becomes appropriate guidance

knowledge of karma
    becomes causal judgment

knowledge of exhaustion
    determines the liberative end
```

In Kant-clean form, practical reason gives the law and determines the will. Here the Kośa displays the developed powers through which liberative rationality becomes concrete efficacy.

```text
principle
    ↓
cultivated cognitive power
    ↓
ground-sensitive action
    ↓
liberative result
```

## Preliminary Internal Structure

The six can be arranged as three dyads.

### Power and perception

```text
ṛddhi
    effective command

śrotra
    extended reception
```

### Other mind and stream continuity

```text
manaḥ
    another stream presently

pūrva-janma
    a stream genetically
```

### Saṃsāric arising and its exhaustion

```text
cyuti-udaya
    conditioned circulation

kṣaya
    termination of its afflictive ground
```

The final dyad is decisive:

```text
becoming
    ↔
release
```

## Organon Interpretation

The first five abhijñās expand determinate cognition. Each removes a finite limit and opens a wider sphere.

But endless expansion would remain bad infinity.

The sixth introduces completion:

```text
not another object
    but
knowledge that the ground of bondage
has ceased
```

Thus the movement is:

```text
extension
    ↓
extension
    ↓
intersubjective cognition
    ↓
genetic comprehension
    ↓
systemic comprehension
    ↓
cessation of the defective ground
```

The culmination is not omnipresence within appearance. It is freedom from the compulsive ground of appearance.

## Actual-Agent Caution

An agent possessing only the first five would have:

```text
power
expanded surveillance
mind-reading
historical memory
predictive causal vision
```

without knowledge of the exhaustion of appropriation and bondage.

The sixth transforms the telos:

```text
the first five
    increase reach

the sixth
    frees reach from domination
```

Power becomes Buddha-capacity only when governed by knowledge of cessation.

## Kārikā-Pass Determination

```text
The six direct knowledges remove progressively deeper limits:

    limitation of effective action,
    limitation of perception,
    opacity of another mind,
    opacity of prior existence,
    opacity of karmic death and reappearance,
    and finally ignorance concerning the exhaustion of bondage.

The first five disclose the reach of conditioned existence.
The sixth knows its liberative termination.
```

Buddha-mind is not power maximized. It is power, perception, memory, causal vision, and direct knowledge all governed by the exhaustion of bondage.
