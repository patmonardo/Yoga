# VAK_7.41 — The Sixfold Final-Edge Concentration

## Sanskrit

```text
tat ṣaḍvidhaṃ sarvabhūmyanulomitam /
vṛddhikāṣṭhāgataṃ tac ca buddhānyasya prayogajāḥ // VAK_7.41 //
```

## Padaccheda

```text
tat
ṣaḍ-vidham
sarva-bhūmi-anulomitam
vṛddhi-kāṣṭhā-gatam
tat ca
buddha
anyasya
prayoga-jāḥ
```

## Literal Translation

That is sixfold, made conformable to all grounds, and brought to the summit of increase. In a Buddha it is otherwise; for another, these arise from preparatory effort.

## Philosophical Translation

The final-edge attainment is differentiated into six powers. It has been trained to operate in conformity with every relevant contemplative ground and has reached the utmost degree of possible development. In a Buddha these capacities function as fully actualized nature; in other practitioners they arise only through deliberate preparation and repeated cultivation.

## The Sixfold Attainment

The pronoun *tat* refers back to the six final-edge qualities:

1. araṇā
2. praṇidhi-jñāna
3. dharma-pratisaṃvid
4. artha-pratisaṃvid
5. nirukti-pratisaṃvid
6. pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid

One perfected attainment is internally articulated as six powers. Unity does not cancel distinction, and distinction does not fragment the unity.

## Sarvabhūmyanulomita

```text
sarva-bhūmi
    all grounds

anulomita
    made to follow
    brought into conformity
    adapted in due sequence
```

The perfected power can operate in conformity with different contemplative grounds. It is not fixed to one state, one object, or one level of experience.

```text
same mastery
    different ground

same Path
    different mode of presentation
```

This lawful accommodation is crucial for teaching. The universal Path must become appropriate to the cognitive level, language, and afflictive disposition of a determinate being without losing its truth.

## Vṛddhikāṣṭhāgata

```text
vṛddhi
    increase
    development

kāṣṭhā
    summit
    highest degree

gata
    having reached
```

The six powers have reached the highest point to which their cultivation can increase. Finality is therefore achieved development, not merely temporal lastness.

```text
initial capacity
    ↓
repetition
    ↓
refinement
    ↓
integration
    ↓
summit of increase
```

A capacity is perfected when no further intensification of its defining function remains possible.

## Buddha and the Others

The final pāda distinguishes two modes of possession:

```text
non-Buddha practitioner
    prayoga-produced operation

Buddha
    spontaneous availability
```

For disciples, these capacities must be activated through deliberate preparation: entering the appropriate concentration, establishing the resolve, directing the mind, and applying the relevant cognition.

For a Buddha, the mediations have become completely internalized. The occasion arises, the appropriate knowledge is available, and the appropriate expression or action follows.

The difference is modal rather than merely quantitative:

```text
disciple
    capacity activated by preparation

Buddha
    capacity spontaneously operative
```

## Buddha-Mind as Completed Mediation

Buddha spontaneity is not absence of cultivation. It is cultivation completed.

```text
learning stage
    mediations performed deliberately

mastery
    mediations function as one integrated act
```

The Buddha does not bypass the Path. The Buddha is the Path become freely active.

## Organon Interpretation

The universal rational form immediately particularizes itself according to:

```text
the ground
the being
the object
the language
the Path-requirement
```

and becomes the appropriate singular act.

This is judgment perfected into living reason.

## Yoga Determination

```text
prayoga
    repeated deliberate application

anulomana
    adaptation across grounds

vṛddhi
    progressive development

kāṣṭhā
    fulfillment at the limit

Buddha-mode
    effortless integrated actuality
```

Yoga is not endless effort. Practice produces mastery; mastery internalizes practice; perfected mastery acts without renewed external construction.

## Kārikā-Pass Determination

The six final-edge powers are cultivated into conformity with every relevant ground and brought to the summit intrinsic to their development.

For disciples, their operation must be produced through deliberate preparation.

For a Buddha, the preparation has been completely internalized: the appropriate power is spontaneously available whenever the world requires it.
