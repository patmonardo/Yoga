# VAK_7.48 — Ṛddhi as Samādhi and the Three Modes of Going

## Sanskrit

```text
ṛddhiḥ samādhiḥ gamanaṃ nirmāṇaṃ ca gatistridhā /
śāsturmanojavā anyeṣāṃ vāhinyapyādhimokṣikī // VAK_7.48 //
```

| Term | Working sense |
|---|---|
| ṛddhiḥ | accomplishment, extraordinary efficacy, supernormal power |
| samādhiḥ | concentration; collected cognitive power |
| gamanam | going, locomotion, transport |
| nirmāṇam | creation, projection, transformation |
| gatiḥ tridhā | going is threefold |
| śāstuḥ | belonging to the Teacher |

| anyeṣām | for others |
| vāhinī | carrying or transporting |
| ādhimokṣikī | produced through resolve, determination, or adhimokṣa |

## Literal translation

Ṛddhi is samādhi; its operations are going and creation. Going is threefold: for the Teacher it is swift as mind; for others it is carrying, and also that produced through resolve.

## Philosophical translation

Extraordinary efficacy is concentrated cognition made operative. Its field consists principally in movement and formative projection. Movement occurs in three modes: the Buddha moves with the immediacy and speed of mind; other practitioners move by being borne through a power-field or through the decisive projection of resolve.

# 1. Ṛddhi Is Samādhi

The verse opens with a direct identity:

```text
ṛddhiḥ samādhiḥ
```

Samādhi is cognition gathered into unity. Ṛddhi is that gathered unity manifesting effective power.

```text
Samādhi
    power in itself

Ṛddhi
    power in operation
```

Kriyā is therefore not added from outside. It is the actuality implicit in concentration itself.

# 2. Power Is a Form of Successful Operation

Ṛddhi is not an independent magical substance. It is the relation between a collected cognitive state and successful accomplishment.

```text
ordinary cognition
    intention and execution remain divided

samādhi
    intention, form, and operation
    become concentrated into one act

ṛddhi
    that act produces an extraordinary result
```

A power is not primarily something an agent possesses. It is a form through which cognition reliably completes an operation.

# 3. The Two Fundamental Operations

The verse identifies two basic fields:

```text
gamanam
    movement, transport, passage

nirmāṇam
    creation, production, transformation
```

These correspond to two universal operations of agency:

```text
Gamana
    change of position

Nirmāṇa
    change of form
```

The agent either changes its relation to the field or changes the determinate constitution of the field.

# 4. Gamana as Practical Negation of Immediacy

Agency first appears as the ability to negate a given location.

```text
here
    is not absolute

there
    can become here
```

The natural body is situated. Spirit makes situation into a determination it can mediate.

# 5. Nirmāṇa as Formative Agency

Nirmāṇa is more radical than locomotion.

```text
gamana
    changes where the agent appears

nirmāṇa
    changes what appears
```

In a GDSL architecture these become primitive operation families:

```text
Move(agent, source, destination)
Construct(agent, form, conditions)
```

# 6. The Three Modes of Going

The verse divides gamana into three forms:

```text
1. manojavā
   movement swift as mind

2. vāhinī
   movement by carrying or transport

3. ādhimokṣikī
   movement through decisive resolve
```

These express three grades in the relation between cognition, mediation, and embodiment.

# 7. Manojavā — Intention and Arrival

```text
śāstuḥ manojavā
    the Teacher’s movement
    is swift as mind
```

For ordinary cognition:

```text
intention
    precedes movement

movement
    traverses resistance

arrival
    completes the intention
```

For manojavā:

```text
intention
    =
arrival
```

This is the image of fully integrated practical reason: determination and accomplished appearance no longer confront one another as alien moments.

# 8. Vāhinī — Supported Agency

In vāhinī, the agent is borne through an established carrier or operative field.

```text
manojavā
    self-originating movement

vāhinī
    mediated transport
```

This is delegated or supported agency: movement through infrastructure, vehicle, field, or another power.

# 9. Ādhimokṣikī — Resolve as an Operative Feature

Ādhimokṣikī movement is accomplished through adhimokṣa: firm determination, decision, or resolve.

```text
citta
    apprehends the destination

adhimokṣa
    decisively fixes the operation

samādhi
    unifies the cognitive field

ṛddhi
    accomplishes the movement
```

This is a clear citta–caitta structure. Adhimokṣa is not the whole cognitive mode but a determinate feature-function within the operative configuration.

# 10. Agency Hierarchy

| Mode | Agency structure |
|---|---|
| manojavā | intention and accomplishment immediately coincide |
| vāhinī | accomplishment occurs through a carrier or supporting field |
| ādhimokṣikī | accomplishment occurs through intensified determination |

This is an ontology of execution.

# 11. Principle, Caitta, Operation, Result

The verse contains a compressed system:

```text
Samādhi
    unified Principle

Adhimokṣa
    determinate cognitive feature

Gamana / Nirmāṇa
    operation

Arrival / Production
    result
```

This is the transition from citta–vṛtti to citta–caitta, Kriyā, and Karma.

# 12. The Sensible Field Becomes Operable

The sensible world is no longer merely what is perceived. It becomes what can be traversed and reconfigured.

```text
Rūpa as object
    sensed form

Rūpa as field of Kriyā
    transformable form
```

This is the transition from passive sensibility to operative Nature–Spirit.

# 13. Ṛddhi Remains Below Śāsana

Verse 7.47 ranked instruction above miraculous power.

```text
ṛddhi
    alters empirical conditions

śāsana
    alters the governing law
    of a cognitive continuum
```

The Kośa can analyze extraordinary efficacy scientifically without confusing it with the highest practical function.

# 14. Hegel-Clean Interpretation

The verse can be read through Universal, Particular, and Singular:

```text
Samādhi
    Universal concentrated in itself

Adhimokṣa
    Particular determination of purpose

Gamana / Nirmāṇa
    Singular realized operation
```

Ṛddhi is the Concept operating with an unusual degree of integration.

# 15. Fichtean Interpretation

Knowing posits an end. In ordinary consciousness, the end confronts external resistance. In collected Knowing, the field is grasped as internally mediated. Ṛddhi is the end becoming effective.

Manojavā presents the limiting image in which positing and objective appearance are one act.

# 17. Samādhi as the Power Kernel

```text
Samādhi
    integrates the cognitive system

Adhimokṣa
```

# 18. Beginning–End Structure

```text
Beginning
    Samādhi as collected Principle

```

The Meta-Path of the End reveals what the Beginning was capable of all along.

## Condensed principle

Ṛddhi is samādhi become effective. Its primordial operations are movement and formation, and its modes reveal three structures of agency: immediate sovereignty, mediated transport, and determinate resolve.

```text
Samādhi gathers the power.
Adhimokṣa determines it.
Kriyā executes it.
Ṛddhi is its success.
```
