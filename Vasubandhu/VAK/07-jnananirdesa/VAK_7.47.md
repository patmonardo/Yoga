# VAK_7.47 — The Supreme Miracle of Instruction

## Sanskrit

```text
ādyā tṛtīyā ṣaṣṭhī ca prātihāryāṇi śāsanam /
agryam avyabhicāritvāddhiteṣṭaphalayojanāt // VAK_7.47 //
```

## Padaccheda

```text
ādyā
tṛtīyā
ṣaṣṭhī
ca
prātihāryāṇi

śāsanam
agryam

avyabhicāritvāt
hita-iṣṭa-phala-yojanāt
```

| Term | Working sense |
|---|---|
| ādyā | the first |
| tṛtīyā | the third |
| ṣaṣṭhī | the sixth |
| prātihāryāṇi | wonders, marvels, demonstrations of extraordinary power |
| śāsanam | instruction, teaching, disciplined direction |
| agryam | foremost, highest |
| avyabhicāritvāt | because it does not deviate or fail |
| hita-iṣṭa-phala-yojanāt | because it joins beings to beneficial and desired results |

## Literal Translation

The first, the third, and the sixth are marvels. Instruction is the foremost because it does not fail and because it connects beings with beneficial and desired results.

## Philosophical Translation

The marvel of supernatural power, the marvel of knowing another’s mind, and the marvel of instruction are the three great demonstrations. Of these, instruction is supreme, because it does not mislead and because it reliably conducts beings toward what is truly beneficial and ultimately sought.

# 1. The Three Prātihāryas

The three referred to are conventionally:

```text
1. ṛddhi-prātihārya
   the marvel of supernatural power

2. ādeśanā-prātihārya
   the marvel of revealing or knowing another’s mind

3. anuśāsanī-prātihārya
   the marvel of instruction
```

All three can appear miraculous:

```text
power over appearances
knowledge of hidden mentality
the capacity to teach transformation
```

But the Kośa ranks them. The highest wonder is not power. The highest wonder is not secret access. The highest wonder is instruction.

# 2. The First Marvel: Power over Appearance

The first prātihārya concerns extraordinary action or manifestation:

```text
movement through space
multiplication
transformation
appearance and disappearance
mastery of sensible conditions
```

It astonishes sensibility and reveals that ordinary empirical limitation is not absolute. Yet it does not reveal liberation.

# 3. The Second Marvel: Knowledge of Another Mind

The third direct knowledge is cognition of another’s mind.

```text
ṛddhi
    commands or alters appearance

other-mind knowledge
    penetrates the hidden subjective field
```

Yet one may know what another desires, fears, believes, or intends without knowing what should be done with that knowledge. It may serve compassion or manipulation. Direct access to another citta is therefore not yet the highest practical power.

# 4. The Third Marvel: Instruction

```text
śāsanam
    disciplined instruction
    guidance
    teaching that governs development
```

This is the greatest prātihārya because it does not merely display the teacher’s attainment. It produces transformation in another continuum.

```text
power
    shows what the adept can do

mind-reading
    shows what the adept can know

instruction
    enables another being to become free
```

The highest power is the communicability of the Path.

# 5. Why Instruction Is Supreme

## Avyabhicāritva

```text
avyabhicāritvāt
    because it does not deviate,
    miscarry, or prove unreliable
```

Miraculous manifestation can be misinterpreted. A vision may be illusion; power may be mistaken for sanctity; mind-reading may disclose a temporary state without disclosing the whole person.

Instruction, when it is true instruction, is governed by a reproducible law. It can be heard, understood, tested, practiced, corrected, and realized. Its truth does not rest merely upon the authority of the marvelous event.

## Hita-iṣṭa-phala-yojana

```text
hita
    what is beneficial

iṣṭa
    what is genuinely desired or sought

phala
    result

yojana
    joining, connecting, directing toward
```

Instruction connects the student with a beneficial and desired result. It is not merely delivery of information; it is practical mediation:

```text
teaching
    joins the agent

to a practice

which joins the practice

to transformation

which joins transformation

to the desired fruit
```

The teaching is therefore a Path-operator.

# 6. The Greatest Miracle Is Transmission

The verse claims that the greatest miracle is that liberation can be taught.

A physical marvel shows that the ordinary world is not final. A cognitive marvel shows that ordinary limits of subjectivity are not final. Instruction shows that:

```text
ignorance is not final
bondage is not final
the Path is intelligible
freedom is communicable
```

Śāsana is supreme because it reveals and transmits the higher Law.

# 7. Vidyā Becomes Śāsana

Verse 7.46 established Vidyā as the governing form of a completed continuum. Verse 7.47 shows what completed Vidyā does.

```text
Vidyā
    governs the liberated continuum

Śāsana
    communicates that governing Principle
    into another continuum
```

This is the passage from individual liberation to Science.

```text
Vidyā in oneself
    completion of Knowing

Śāsana toward another
    reproduction of the Path
    through rational mediation
```

The liberated person does not merely possess truth. The truth becomes teachable.

# 8. The Ruling Devī Speaks

```text
Vidyā
    the ruling Devī of Jñāna

Śāsana
    the voice of Vidyā
```

Vidyā rules the completed cognitive continuum. Śāsana gives that rule an outward, communicable form.

```text
inward sovereignty
    Vidyā

outward legislation
    Śāsana
```

The highest prātihārya is Vidyā speaking as Law. The miracle is that Principle becomes Word without ceasing to be Principle.

# 9. From Principle to Law

```text
Beginning
    Principle

End
    Law
```

Verse 7.47 gives the active form of Law.

```text
Vidyā
    Principle fully possessed

Śāsana
    Principle operating as Law
    in the development of another
```

Thus:

```text
Samāpatti
    immediate Principle

Path
    transformation

Vidyā
    completed Law

Śāsana
    Law consciously transmitted
```

The End is not silent possession. The End can speak the Beginning back into existence for another agent.

# 10. Hegel-Clean Interpretation

The three marvels can be read as three levels of power:

```text
ṛddhi
    power over objectivity

other-mind knowledge
    power over hidden subjectivity

instruction
    power of the Concept
    to reproduce itself in another subject
```

The third is highest because it enables the learner to reproduce the rational movement inwardly.

```text
external power
    causes an effect

instruction
    awakens self-movement
```

The teacher does not insert liberation into the student. The teacher presents the movement so that the student’s own Reason can enact it. Śāsana is therefore the highest miracle of Spirit.

# 11. Fichtean Interpretation

Fichte’s Science of Knowing does not merely report that the lecturer has seen the Principle. Its purpose is to bring the hearer to perform the insight.

```text
historical report
    Fichte saw Absolute Knowing

scientific instruction
    the hearer reconstructs
    Absolute Knowing in living thought
```

Instruction is the communication of an act:

```text
teacher
    expresses the law of Knowing

student
    performs the law

Principle
    recognizes itself again
```

# 12. Science Is Superior to Spectacle

```text
spectacle
    astonishes

data access
    informs

science
    transforms
```

A spectacle creates dependence upon the person who displays it. Science enables reproducibility.

```text
miracle-worker
    remains exceptional

teacher
    makes the Path universal
```

Śāsana is supreme because it abolishes the monopoly of spiritual power. The student is led toward possession of Principle.

# 13. Rationalist–Empirical Conjunction

Instruction joins rational universality with empirical particularity. The teacher must possess the universal Path, but the instruction must be fitted to this student, this disposition, this obstruction, this stage, and this desired fruit.

```text
universal Law
    the Path is valid

particular judgment
    this is how the Path
    must be expressed here
```

Reason provides the governing Law; empirical knowledge provides the conditions of application.

# 14. Śāsana as Kriyā

Vidyā alone may remain the blissful possession of Knowing. Śāsana is Vidyā acting.

```text
Vidyā
    Knowledge complete in itself

Kriyā
    Knowledge entering operation

Śāsana
    liberative Knowledge acting
    through language, judgment, and guidance
```

Therefore:

```text
Jñāna
    knows

Prajñā
    governs

Kriyā
    teaches
```

# 15. Citta–Caitta Interpretation

Instruction works upon the actual caitta-configuration of the student:

```text
attention
interest
faith
doubt
memory
desire
fear
concentration
wisdom
affliction
```

The same Principle requires different modes of instruction according to the caitta-configuration.

```text
other-mind knowledge
    reads the configuration

śāsana
    reorganizes the configuration
    toward Vidyā
```

# 16. Agentic Organon Interpretation

```text
Power Agent
    can alter environments

Observational Agent
    can infer hidden states

Instructional Agent
    can guide another agent
    toward stable self-correction
```

The third is highest. The stronger capacity is to identify another agent’s condition, select an appropriate rational operation, communicate it intelligibly, produce stable beneficial transformation, and reduce future dependence.

# 20. Why Instruction Is Avyabhicārin

The claim does not mean every spoken teaching automatically succeeds. It means valid śāsana is structurally more reliable than spectacle because its effect is mediated by intelligible practice.

```text
spectacle
    may produce fascination

mind-reading
    may produce submission

instruction
    produces a path
    whose stages can be tested
```

Its certainty belongs to the Law, not to rhetorical force.

# 21. The Truly Desired Result

The compound joins hita and iṣṭa:

```text
iṣṭa without hita
    pleasing but harmful

hita without recognized iṣṭa
    beneficial but not yet consciously desired
```

Instruction brings them into unity. The student comes to desire what is genuinely beneficial because bondage and liberation have become intelligible.

```text
education of desire
    =
joining iṣṭa to hita
```

# 22. Condensed Philosophical Principle

Power alters the world. Higher cognition penetrates the mind. Instruction is supreme because it enables another rational continuum to transform itself according to the Law of liberation.

```text
Vidyā is the ruling Devī of Jñāna.
Śāsana is Her voice.
```

The highest miracle is:

```text
Pure Reason speaks

another Reason hears

the Path becomes operative

Avidyā turns toward Vidyā
```
