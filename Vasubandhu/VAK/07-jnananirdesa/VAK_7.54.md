# VAK_7.54 — Divine Perception as Refined Rūpa-Capacity

## Sanskrit

```text
divyaśrotrākṣiṇī rūpaprasādau dhyānabhūmikau /
sabhāgāvikale nityaṃ dūrasūkṣmādigocare // VAK_7.54 //
```

## Padaccheda

```text
divya-śrotra-akṣiṇī
rūpa-prasādau
dhyāna-bhūmikau

sabhāga-avikale
nityam

dūra-sūkṣma-ādi-gocare
```

## Literal Translation

The divine ear and eye are two refined material faculties grounded in dhyāna. They are invariably corresponding and unimpaired, and their ranges include distant, subtle, and related objects.

## Philosophical Translation

Higher seeing and hearing are not disembodied acts of an abstract mind. They are refined objective capacities of rūpa, generated upon a rūpa-dhyānic ground. They preserve the functional forms of sight and hearing, but without ordinary impairment, and extend those faculties into distant, subtle, concealed, and otherwise inaccessible domains.

# 1. Rūpa-Dhyāna, Not Arūpya

The verse classifies the divine eye and ear as rūpaprasāda and dhyānabhūmika. These are therefore powers of rūpa-dhyāna: refined embodiment rather than bodiless cognition.

```text
rūpa-dhyāna
    refines embodied receptivity

arūpya
    withdraws from rūpa-support
```

The distinction is essential. Divine sight and hearing remain determinate sensory powers. Entering the bodiless field of citta or hṛdaya belongs to the arūpya direction, not to these faculties.

# 2. Prasāda as Refined Receptivity

Prasāda indicates clarity, transparency, sensitivity, and unobstructed receptivity. The faculty is material, but matter has been organized into a more transparent vehicle of cognition.

```text
coarse rūpa
    limits reception

prasāda-rūpa
    transmits the object
    with reduced obstruction
```

Thus Nature is not abandoned. Nature becomes adequate to cognition.

# 3. Dhyāna Generates a New Sensory Ground

Dhyāna reorganizes the cognitive-sensible field. It does not merely alter an inward state.

```text
dhyāna
    establishes a new ground

that ground
    supports refined rūpa

refined rūpa
    extends perception
```

# 4. Functional Specificity Is Preserved

Sabhāga indicates functional correspondence. Divine sight remains sight; divine hearing remains hearing.

```text
eye
    visible form

ear
    sound
```

The higher faculty expands range without erasing type.

# 5. Avikala — Unimpaired Function

The divine faculties are avikala: complete or unimpaired within their proper domain. Ordinary defects of weakness, distance, obstruction, damage, and limited resolution are removed.

```text
ordinary faculty
    function + contingent defect

divine faculty
    function released
    from ordinary defect
```

# 6. Gocara as Operational Domain

Their gocara includes distant, subtle, and related classes of objects. Gocara is not merely object but the lawful range within which a faculty can operate.

```text
DivineEye {
    objectType: visibleForm
    range: distant | subtle | extended
}

DivineEar {
    objectType: sound
    range: distant | subtle | extended
}
```

# 7. Refined Empiricism

The Kośa asks:

```text
which faculty?
of what ontological type?
from which ground?
complete in what respect?
directed toward which range?
```

This is an analytic of enhanced perception, not mystical vagueness.

# 8. Citta Does Not See by Itself

Higher cognition requires the conjunction of citta, caitta, refined faculty, object, and ground.

```text
citta
+
caitta configuration
+
rūpaprasāda
+
gocara
```

The verse therefore expands the architecture beyond citta-vṛtti into a full System of cognition.

# 9. No Automatic Vidyā

Extended range does not imply reversal of avidyā.

```text
refined faculty
    expands empirical access

Vidyā
    transforms the governing continuum
```

The divine eye and ear remain powers, not guarantees of liberation.

# 10. Nature–Spirit Interpretation

Spirit does not flee Nature. Dhyāna reorganizes Nature so that the sensible instrument becomes more adequate to cognition.

```text
Spirit
    requires sensible manifestation

Nature
    provides the faculty

Dhyāna
    refines the instrument
```

# 11. Organon Kernel Interpretation

```text
PerceptualCapability {
    modality
    substrate
    generatingGround
    completeness
    objectRange
    ethicalStatus
}
```

The verse gives a typed faculty registry rather than mystical metadata.

# 12. Scientific Principle

Higher cognition need not abolish the empirical faculty. It may perfect the faculty by reorganizing its substrate, removing impairment, and extending its lawful object-range.

```text
Dhyāna establishes the ground.
Rūpa becomes transparent.
The faculty preserves its type.
Its object-range expands.
```
