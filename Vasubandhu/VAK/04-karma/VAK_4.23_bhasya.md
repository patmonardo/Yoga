# VAK_4.23_bhasya — Temporal Possession of Vijñapti

## Kārikā Anchor

**VAK 4.23**

```iast
vijñaptyā tu punaḥ sarve kurvanto madhyayānvitāḥ |
atītayā kṣaṇād ūrdhvam atyāgān nāsty ajātayā ||
```

Working translation after the Bhāṣya pass:

> But all who are producing a manifest action are endowed with that present vijñapti for as long as they produce it. After the first moment, provided it has not been abandoned, they are also endowed with the past vijñapti. No one is endowed with a future vijñapti.

Kārikā analysis: `VAK_4.23.md`.

## Source Cautions

The source at [210|21] reads `yāvadijñaptiṃ`, where the intended form is evidently connected with `vijñaptiṃ`; the exact e-text reading is preserved here rather than silently normalized.

The preceding sentence `avijñaptyadhikāraḥ samāptaḥ` explicitly closes the avijñapti discussion. VAK 4.23 therefore begins a distinct analysis of `vijñapti`.

## Natural Argument

Vasubandhu first marks the transition: the discussion devoted to avijñapti is complete.

He then states the rule for manifest action. `Sarve` is glossed explicitly as all three practical classes previously distinguished:

```text
saṃvara-stha
asaṃvara-stha
madhyastha
```

Thus the rule is not restricted by standing ethical status. Whoever is actually producing a `vijñapti` is, for exactly as long as that production continues, endowed with that vijñapti as present (`vartamānā`).

Once the first moment has passed, the agent is also endowed with the corresponding past vijñapti, provided it has not been abandoned (`atyāgāt`).

Finally, Vasubandhu states categorically that no one is endowed with a future (`anāgata`) vijñapti. A manifest action that has not yet arisen is not something the agent presently possesses as vijñapti.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu** — closure of the avijñapti section and the complete temporal rule for possession of vijñapti.

No objection-response sequence appears in this unit.

## New Technical Terms

- **avijñaptyadhikāraḥ samāptaḥ** — “the section concerning avijñapti is complete”; **status:** structural marker; **function:** explicitly terminates the preceding topic and opens the vijñapti analysis.
- **vartamānā vijñapti** — present manifest action; **status:** temporal application of an established term; **function:** marks vijñapti possessed while the action is actually being produced.
- **anāgata vijñapti** — future manifest action; **status:** temporal application; **function:** explicitly excluded from present possession.

## Doctrinal Determination

### The rule applies to all practical statuses

```text
saṃvara-stha
asaṃvara-stha
madhyastha
    ↓
if actually producing vijñapti
    ↓
possess present vijñapti
```

Standing ethical status does not change the temporal rule governing manifestation.

### Present possession is coextensive with production

```text
for as long as vijñapti is being produced
    → present vijñapti is possessed
```

The Bhāṣya is precise: `yāvat ... kurvanti tāvat` — as long as they produce it, for that long they are endowed with it as present.

### Past possession begins after the first moment

```text
first moment
    → present vijñapti

after the first moment
    → present vijñapti
    + past vijñapti
      subject to non-abandonment
```

Here again `atyāga`, non-abandonment, governs possession of the past factor.

### No future vijñapti is possessed

```text
future vijñapti
    → no possessor
```

Vasubandhu states this without qualification:

```iast
anāgatayā tu vijñaptyā na kaścit samanvāgataḥ
```

No one is endowed with a future vijñapti.

This is stronger and cleaner than merely saying that future action has not yet been performed. It is a rule of `samanvāgama`, possession.

## Effect on Kārikā Translation

The Kārikā file correctly identified the broad temporal structure, but the Bhāṣya sharpens two points.

First, `sarve` explicitly means all three classes: restraint, non-restraint, and middle.

Second, present possession is exactly coextensive with actual production of vijñapti.

A refined translation is therefore:

> But all—those in restraint, non-restraint, or neither—while producing a manifest action are endowed with that present vijñapti for as long as they produce it. After the first moment, provided it has not been abandoned, they are also endowed with the past vijñapti. No one is endowed with a future vijñapti.

## Logical Determination

The temporal structure is sharply asymmetric:

```text
actual production
    → present possession

completed prior moment
    → past possession

not-yet-arisen manifestation
    → no possession
```

Thus manifestation is indexed to occurrence:

```text
vijñapti as manifest
    requires actual arising
```

This differs from some forms of avijñapti whose possession can extend across a broader three-time structure.

The safest logical result is:

```text
manifestation
    has present actuality
    and past retention
    but no future possession
```

## Organon Contact Point

A restrained contact point is enough:

```text
manifest actuality
    cannot be possessed in advance as manifest
```

The distinction is between an action presently expressed and a determination that may stand beyond its immediate expression. No stronger reconstruction is required here.
