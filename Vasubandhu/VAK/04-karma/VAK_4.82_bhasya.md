# VAK_4.82_bhasya — Karmapathas by Destiny: Endowment and Present Manifestation

## Kārikā

**bhinna-pralāpa-pāruṣya-vyāpādā narake dvidhā |**  
**samanvāgamato ’bhidhyā-mithyā-dṛṣṭī kurau trayaḥ || 4.82 ||**

> In hell, disordered speech, harsh speech, and ill will occur in two ways. Covetousness and false view occur by way of endowment. In Uttarakuru, the three mental courses occur by way of endowment.

See [VAK_4.82.md](./VAK_4.82.md).

## Bhāṣya Source

Vasubandhu introduces the problem explicitly:

> *kasyāṃ gatau kati karmapathā akuśalā kuśalā vā saṃmukhībhāvataḥ samanvāgamato vā* — In which destiny, and how many wholesome or unwholesome karmapathas, occur either by present manifestation or by endowment?

For hell:

> *bhinnapralāpapāruṣyavyāpādā narake dvidhā*  
> *ity ete trayaḥ karmapathā narake dvābhyāṃ saprakārābhyāṃ santi / saṃmukhībhāvataḥ samanvāgamabhāvataś ca /*

The three occur in both modes: present manifestation and endowment.

The Bhāṣya explains their manifestation:

- `saṃbhinnapralāpa` from lamentation (`paridevanā`)
- `pāruṣya` from mutually tormenting one another, because the continuum is harsh (`rukṣa-saṃtānatā`)
- `vyāpāda` from mutual hatred (`paraspara-dveṣa`)

For covetousness and false view:

> *samanvāgamato ’bhidhyāmithyādṛṣṭī / na saṃmukhībhāvataḥ / rañjanīyavastvabhāvāt karmaphalapratyakṣatvācca /*

They occur by endowment, not by present manifestation, because there is no attractive object and because karmic result is directly evident.

The Bhāṣya then explains why the remaining courses do not arise there:

- no killing, because hell beings die only when their karma is exhausted (`karmakṣayeṇa maraṇāt`)
- no theft or sexual misconduct, because there is no possession of property or women (`dravya-strī-parigrahābhāva`)
- no false speech, because there is no purpose in lying (`prayojanābhāva`)
- no divisive speech for the same reason and because beings are already permanently divided (`nityabhinnatva`)

For Uttarakuru:

> *kurau trayaḥ*  
> *samanvāgamata iti vartate / abhidhyāvyāpādamithyādṛṣṭayo na saṃmukhībhāvataḥ /*

The three mental unwholesome karmapathas are present only by endowment, not by manifestation.

Reasons:

- no covetousness because there is no possessiveness or appropriation (`amama-aparigrahatva`)
- no ill will because the continuum is gentle (`snigdha-saṃtānatva`) and there is no object of hostility (`āghāta-vastvabhāva`)
- no false view because there is no corrupt practical disposition (`apāpāśayatva`)

## Natural Segmentation

The Bhāṣya proceeds in four steps:

1. formulate the general problem: distribution by destiny and by two modes of occurrence;
2. identify the three courses that are both possessed and manifest in hell;
3. identify courses possessed but not manifest in hell and explain the absences of the remaining courses;
4. identify the three mental courses possessed but not manifest in Uttarakuru and explain why manifestation is blocked there.

The natural argumentative center is therefore not merely a list of realms. It is a distinction between **continuum-endowment** and **event-level manifestation under domain-specific conditions**.

## Literal Prose Translation

“In which destiny, and how many wholesome or unwholesome karmapathas, occur by present manifestation or by endowment?

In hell, disordered speech, harsh speech, and ill will occur in both ways. These three karmapathas exist there under both modes: through present manifestation and through endowment. Disordered speech occurs from lamentation; harsh speech from mutually tormenting one another, because their continuums are harsh; ill will from mutual hatred.

Covetousness and false view occur there by way of endowment, but not by present manifestation, because there is no attractive object and because the result of karma is directly evident.

There is no killing, because death occurs when karma is exhausted. There is no taking what is not given or sexual misconduct, because there is no possession of property or women. There is no false speech, because there is no purpose for it; for the same reason there is no divisive speech, and also because they are always already divided.

In Uttarakuru the three [mental karmapathas] occur by way of endowment. Covetousness, ill will, and false view do not occur there by present manifestation, because there is no sense of ‘mine’ or possessiveness, because the continuum is gentle and there is no object of hostility, and because there is no corrupt practical disposition.”

## Argument Reconstruction

### 1. Endowment is not manifestation

The opening question explicitly distinguishes:

```text
samanvāgama
    determination belongs to the continuum

saṃmukhībhāva
    determination is presently manifest
```

Thus:

```text
endowed with D
    does not imply
D is presently occurring
```

This distinction is essential for the entire passage.

### 2. Manifestation requires a suitable objective and existential field

In hell, some karmapathas can manifest because the environment and relations provide their necessary supports:

```text
lamentation
    → saṃbhinnapralāpa

mutual torment
    → pāruṣya

mutual hatred
    → vyāpāda
```

But other courses remain merely endowed because their specific objective conditions are absent.

### 3. Absence is explained structurally, not morally in the abstract

Vasubandhu does not say simply that a realm is “too bad” or “too good” for a karmapatha. He asks what conditions the course requires.

Examples:

```text
no attractive object
    → no manifest abhidhyā

directly evident karmic result
    → no manifest mithyādṛṣṭi

no property relation
    → no theft

no useful deceptive purpose
    → no false speech
```

The logic is constitutive rather than merely evaluative.

### 4. Uttarakuru supplies the opposite kind of restriction

The three mental courses are still endowments of the continuum, but present conditions do not support manifestation:

```text
no possessiveness
    blocks covetousness

gentle continuum + no object of hostility
    blocks ill will

no corrupt disposition
    blocks false view
```

Thus favorable conditions suppress manifestation without erasing the underlying endowment relation.

## Voice Identification

**Voice:** Vasubandhu — frames the question and gives the explanatory prose.

**Voice:** Vaibhāṣika doctrinal analysis — the technical distinction between `samanvāgama` and `saṃmukhībhāva`, and the realm-by-realm taxonomy, is presented as the operative Abhidharma framework.

No explicit objector appears in this unit.

## New Technical Terms

### samanvāgama

- **Meaning:** endowment; operative possession of a determination by a causal continuum.
- **Status:** previously encountered, especially in the root-severance discussion.
- **Function:** distinguishes what belongs to the continuum from what is presently manifest.

### saṃmukhībhāva

- **Meaning:** present manifestation; immediate actual occurrence of a determination.
- **Status:** explicit here as the counterpart of `samanvāgama`.
- **Function:** marks event-level actualization.

### rukṣa-saṃtānatā

- **Meaning:** harshness or roughness of the continuum.
- **Status:** Bhāṣya-specific in this passage.
- **Function:** explains why harsh speech can manifest among hell beings.

### snigdha-saṃtānatva

- **Meaning:** gentleness or softness of the continuum.
- **Status:** Bhāṣya-specific in this passage.
- **Function:** explains why ill will does not manifest in Uttarakuru.

### āghāta-vastu

- **Meaning:** object or basis of hostility/injury.
- **Status:** Bhāṣya-specific formulation here.
- **Function:** objective condition required for manifest ill will.

### apāpāśayatva

- **Meaning:** absence of a corrupt or evil practical disposition.
- **Status:** Bhāṣya-specific here.
- **Function:** explains why false view and other unwholesome courses do not manifest in Uttarakuru.

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse establishes a two-axis model:

```text
Axis 1: what the continuum is endowed with
Axis 2: what the present domain permits to manifest
```

These are independent enough that a being may possess a karmapatha without currently producing it.

For hell:

```text
manifest + endowed:
    saṃbhinnapralāpa
    pāruṣya
    vyāpāda

endowed only:
    abhidhyā
    mithyādṛṣṭi
```

For Uttarakuru:

```text
endowed only:
    abhidhyā
    vyāpāda
    mithyādṛṣṭi
```

The crucial doctrinal point is that **existential domain conditions the manifestation of karmic dispositions without necessarily determining their possession**.

## Logical Determination

The Bhāṣya gives a clean conditional structure:

```text
EndowedWith(C, D)
    +
DomainSupports(D)
    +
ObjectiveConditionsPresent(D)
    ↓
D may become manifest
```

Conversely:

```text
EndowedWith(C, D)
    +
ObjectiveConditionAbsent(D)
    ↓
D remains unmanifest
```

This prevents two bad inferences:

```text
not manifest
    ≠
not possessed
```

and

```text
possessed
    ≠
currently active
```

The realm analysis is therefore a modal analysis of practical possibility.

## Translation Consequences

The first-pass Kārikā interpretation correctly identified the key distinction between endowment and manifestation. The Bhāṣya now makes the explanatory grounds explicit.

`dvidhā` should be read strictly as:

> “in both modes: by present manifestation and by endowment.”

`kuru` is specifically Uttarakuru.

`saṃbhinnapralāpa` remains better rendered as “disordered/frivolous afflicted speech” rather than merely “incoherent speech,” since its hell manifestation is explained by lamentation.

## Light Organon Contact

The strongest reusable structure is a distinction between **stored practical determination** and **currently executable manifestation**.

```text
Continuum
    hasEndowment(D)

Environment
    suppliesConditions(D)

PresentEvent
    manifests(D)
```

This is more precise than treating a disposition as either simply present or absent. The system must represent:

```text
possession
possibility of activation
actual activation
```

as three distinct levels.

The Bhāṣya also gives a strong principle for modeling constraints:

> A determination is not ruled out merely because it is morally inconsistent with a domain; it is ruled out because one or more constitutive conditions of manifestation are absent.

That is exactly the kind of genetic explanation the Organon should preserve.

## Commit Note

VAK 4.82 establishes:

- the explicit `samanvāgama / saṃmukhībhāva` distinction;
- three karmapathas manifest and endowed in hell;
- covetousness and false view as endowed but unmanifest there;
- structural explanations for the absence of the remaining unwholesome courses in hell;
- the three mental karmapathas as endowed but unmanifest in Uttarakuru;
- domain-specific manifestation as a function of objective and dispositional conditions rather than mere possession.
