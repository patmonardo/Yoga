# VAK 4.93 Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

**trīṇi catvāri caikaṃ ca dṛg-gheyasya tad-ādayaḥ |**  
**te dve catvāry atha trīṇi bhāvanā-heya-karmaṇaḥ || 4.93 ||**

> For karma abandonable through seeing, the result-dharmas beginning with that class admit respectively three, four, and one fruit-relations. For karma abandonable through cultivation, those same classes admit respectively two, four, and three.

[See the Kārikā analysis](./VAK_4.93.md).

## Bhāṣya Source

```text
darśanaheyasya karmaṇo darśanaheyā dharmāstrīṇi phalāni vipākavisaṃyogaphale hitvā /
bhāvanāheyāścatvāri visaṃyogaphalaṃ hitvā /
apraheyā ekamadhipatiphalam /

te dve catvāryatha trīṇi bhāvanāheyakarmaṇaḥ // VAkK_4.93 //

bhāvanāheyasya karmaṇo darśanaheyā dharmā dve puruṣakārādhipatiphale /
bhāvanāheyāścatvāri visaṃyogaphalaṃ muktvā /
apraheyāstrīṇi vipākaniḥṣyandaphale muktvā /
```

## Literal Prose Translation

For karma to be abandoned through seeing, dharmas to be abandoned through seeing are three fruits, excluding maturation-result and disconnection-result. Dharmas to be abandoned through cultivation are four fruits, excluding disconnection-result. Dharmas not to be abandoned are one fruit: dominant result.

For karma to be abandoned through cultivation, dharmas to be abandoned through seeing are two fruits: activity-produced result and dominant result. Dharmas to be abandoned through cultivation are four fruits, excluding disconnection-result. Dharmas not to be abandoned are three fruits, excluding maturation-result and outflow-result.

## Argument Reconstruction

Vasubandhu now indexes the five-fruit system by a new ordered pair:

```text
source karma:
    darśana-heya
    bhāvanā-heya
    apraheya

result dharma:
    darśana-heya
    bhāvanā-heya
    apraheya
```

VAK 4.93 supplies the first two source rows.

### Karma abandonable through seeing

```text
→ darśana-heya result-dharmas
   3 fruits:
   niḥṣyanda
   puruṣakāra
   adhipati

→ bhāvanā-heya result-dharmas
   4 fruits:
   vipāka
   niḥṣyanda
   puruṣakāra
   adhipati

→ apraheya result-dharmas
   1 fruit:
   adhipati
```

### Karma abandonable through cultivation

```text
→ darśana-heya result-dharmas
   2 fruits:
   puruṣakāra
   adhipati

→ bhāvanā-heya result-dharmas
   4 fruits:
   vipāka
   niḥṣyanda
   puruṣakāra
   adhipati

→ apraheya result-dharmas
   3 fruits:
   visaṃyoga
   puruṣakāra
   adhipati
```

The numerical pattern therefore conceals different memberships. In particular, a count of three may mean either:

```text
niḥṣyanda + puruṣakāra + adhipati
```

or:

```text
visaṃyoga + puruṣakāra + adhipati
```

according to the ordered source-target relation.

## Voice

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The passage is direct explanatory enumeration of the fruit-relations encoded in the Kārikā.

## New Technical Terms

- **darśana-heya / dṛg-heya** — to be abandoned through seeing; **status:** previously encountered doctrinal category; **function:** source/result classification in the fruit matrix.
- **bhāvanā-heya** — to be abandoned through cultivation; **status:** previously encountered; **function:** source/result classification.
- **apraheya** — not to be abandoned; **status:** implicit in the Kārikā, explicit in the Bhāṣya; **function:** third member of the abandonment-status matrix.

## Doctrinal Determination

The first two rows of the matrix are:

| Source karma | darśana-heya result | bhāvanā-heya result | apraheya result |
|---|---|---|---|
| darśana-heya | 3: niḥṣyanda, puruṣakāra, adhipati | 4: all except visaṃyoga | 1: adhipati |
| bhāvanā-heya | 2: puruṣakāra, adhipati | 4: all except visaṃyoga | 3: visaṃyoga, puruṣakāra, adhipati |

The matrix is directed. Reversing source and result changes the permitted fruit-set.

The striking limitation is:

```text
darśana-heya karma
    → apraheya dharma
    = adhipati-phala only
```

while cultivation-abandonable karma can relate to non-abandonable dharmas through disconnection as well as activity-produced and dominant result.

## Logical Determination

The operative relation is no longer merely:

```text
Karma → Fruit
```

but:

```text
SourceAbandonmentStatus
× ResultAbandonmentStatus
× FruitType
```

Equal fruit-count does not imply equal causal membership.

## Translation Consequences

The Kārikā's numbers should not be presented as self-explanatory cardinalities. The Bhāṣya establishes the exact fruit-types in every cell and shows why a later cleaned Kārikā presentation should preserve the ordered target classes and identify the omitted fruit-types where needed.

## Light Organon Contact

This continues the genetic lesson of 4.87–4.92: a causal relation is determined by both relata and by the mode under which each is classified. `darśana-heya`, `bhāvanā-heya`, and `apraheya` are not merely labels attached independently to source and result; their ordered conjunction determines which causal relations are admissible.

VAK 4.94 completes the third row, for `apraheya` karma.
