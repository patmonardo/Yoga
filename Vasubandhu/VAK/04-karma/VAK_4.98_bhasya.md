# VAK 4.98 — Bhāṣya

**Kārikā:** `saṃghabhedas tv asāmagrīsvabhāvo viprayuktakaḥ | akliṣṭāvyākṛto dharmaḥ saṃghas tena samanvitaḥ || 4.98 ||`

**Best current translation:** Saṅgha schism has non-concord as its nature; it is a mind-dissociated, non-afflicted, indeterminate dharma. The Saṅgha is endowed with it.

**Kārikā analysis:** [VAK_4.98.md](./VAK_4.98.md)

## Bhāṣya Source

Pradhan [260|15–260|22].

```text
hetau phalopacārātsaṃghabheda ānantaryamuktam /
bhidyate vā 'neneti kṛtvā /

saṃghabhedastvasāmagrīsvabhāvo viprayuktakaḥ /
akliṣṭāvyākṛto dharmaḥ

asāmagrī nāma cittaviprayuktaḥ saṃskāro 'nivṛtāvyākṛtaḥ saṃghabhedaḥ /
sa kimānantaryaṃ bhaviṣyati naiva ca tena bhettā samanvāgataḥ /
kiṃ tarhi /

saṃghastena samanvitaḥ // VAkK_4.98 //

yo hi bhinnastasya bhedo na bhettuḥ /
```

## Natural Segmentation

The prose makes three determinations:

1. Why `saṃghabheda` can be named among the ānantarya karmas even though the schism itself is not the divider's karma.
2. What the schism itself is: `asāmagrī`, a mind-dissociated conditioned factor, non-afflicted and indeterminate.
3. Who bears that factor: the divided Saṅgha, not the divider.

## Literal Translation

> Saṅgha schism is called an ānantarya by figuratively applying the result to its cause; or because the Saṅgha is split by means of it. But Saṅgha schism itself has non-concord as its nature; it is a mind-dissociated, non-afflicted, indeterminate dharma. “Non-concord” is the name of a conditioned factor dissociated from mind, unobscured and indeterminate: this is Saṅgha schism. Will that itself then be the ānantarya? No. Nor is the divider endowed with it. With what, then? The Saṅgha is endowed with it. For the division belongs to that which is divided, not to the divider.

## Course of the Argument

### 1. Cause and result must be distinguished

The previous discussion counted Saṅgha schism among the five ānantaryas. The Bhāṣya now prevents a category mistake.

```text
schismatic karma
    → produces division

saṃghabheda
    → the produced divided condition
```

`saṃghabheda` is named as ānantarya by `hetau phalopacāra`, applying the result-name to its cause. Thus the expression is causally compressed.

### 2. The intrinsic nature of schism

The schism itself is:

```text
asāmagrī
    = non-concord
```

and is classified as:

```text
citta-viprayukta saṃskāra
akliṣṭa / anivṛta
avyākṛta
```

The divided state is therefore not itself greed, hatred, delusion, intention, or another mental episode. It is a conditioned relational determination of the community.

### 3. Bearer and producer are different

The decisive sentence is:

```text
yo hi bhinnas tasya bhedo na bhettuḥ
```

The division belongs to what has been divided, not to the divider.

Hence:

```text
producer of schism ≠ bearer of schism

divider
    bears the culpable karma

Saṅgha
    bears the divided condition
```

This distinction is required before 4.99 can identify what the divider himself is endowed with.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu / doctrinal exposition** — explains the transferred naming of result as cause and defines the schism.
- **Voice: dialectical question** — asks whether the schism itself is therefore the ānantarya and whether the divider bears it.
- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — rejects both and assigns the schism to the Saṅgha.

No separate school attribution is explicit in this short unit.

## New Technical Terms

- **hetau phalopacāra** — figurative application of the result to its cause.
  - **status:** important Bhāṣya-only explanatory expression here.
  - **function:** resolves the apparent identity of schism and ānantarya karma.

- **asāmagrī** — non-concord, absence of communal unity.
  - **status:** explicit Kārikā term, defined by the Bhāṣya.
  - **function:** intrinsic nature of Saṅgha schism.

- **citta-viprayukta saṃskāra** — conditioned factor dissociated from mind.
  - **status:** established Abhidharma category.
  - **function:** ontological classification of the schism.

- **samanvita** — endowed with / associated by possession.
  - **status:** recurrent.
  - **function:** distinguishes the bearer of the divided state from its producer.

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya distinguishes three levels that must not be collapsed:

```text
1. causal act of the divider
2. resulting communal non-concord
3. bearer of that resultant determination
```

The external historical event “a person splits the Saṅgha” therefore contains two doctrinally distinct dharmic determinations:

```text
agent-side
    culpable verbal karma

collective-side
    asāmagrī / saṃghabheda
```

The first is morally charged; the second, considered in itself, is `akliṣṭa-avyākṛta`.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The existing translation is substantially correct, but “division of the Saṅgha” should be understood strictly as the **condition of non-concord borne by the divided community**, not as the divider's act of producing that condition.

## Logical Determination

Let:

```text
D = divider
S = Saṅgha
K = schismatic karma
B = schism / non-concord
```

Then:

```text
Produces(D, K)
Causes(K, B)
Bears(S, B)
```

but:

```text
Bears(D, B) = false
K = B         = false
```

The Bhāṣya therefore distinguishes causal relation from inherence or possession.

## Organon Contact Point

This is a strong generated relation rather than an external analogy:

```text
activity
    → produces a relational state

relational state
    → inheres in the affected whole
```

A determination may thus be caused by one subject while belonging to another bearer. The division is objective within the collective relation even though its generating karma belongs to the divider.
