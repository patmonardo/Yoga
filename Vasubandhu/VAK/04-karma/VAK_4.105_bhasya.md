# VAK 4.105 — Bhāṣya

**Kārikā:** `saṃghabhede mṛṣāvādo mahāvadyatamo mataḥ | bhavāgracetanā loke mahāphalatamā śubhe || 4.105 ||`

**Best current translation:** False speech that divides the Saṅgha is regarded as maximally culpable. Among wholesome worldly karmas, volition at the summit of existence is maximally fruitful.

**Kārikā analysis:** [VAK_4.105.md](./VAK_4.105.md)

## Bhāṣya Source

```text
eṣāmānantaryāṇāṃ katamat mahāsāvadyam /

saṃghabhede mṛṣāvādo mahāvadyatamo mataḥ /

yaḥ saṃghabhedanimittaṃ mṛṣāvādo dharmādharmajñasya viparyayadyotanāt sa sarveṣāṃ duścaritānāṃ mahāvadyatamaḥ /
kiṃ kāraṇam /
tathāgatadharmaśarīraprahāritatvāt lokānāṃ ca svargāpavargamārgāntarāyakatvāt /
saṃghe hi bhinne lokasya niyāmāvakrantiphalaprāptivairāgyāsravakṣayāḥ pratibadhyante dhyānādhyayanasvādhyāyacintākarmāṇyapi na pravartante sadevanāgamanuṣyaṃ jagaccākulaṃ vimanaskaṃ vartate yāvat punarna pratisaṃdhito bhavati yasmāccāvicau kalpaṃ vipākaḥ /
```

## Natural Segmentation

The Bhāṣya develops two extrema:

1. Which ānantarya karma is maximally culpable?
2. Which wholesome karma is maximally fruitful?

The first is answered through the unique damage caused by Saṅgha-dividing false speech. The second requires a distinction between kinds of fruit: `bhavāgracetanā` is maximal only with respect to maturation-result, whereas volition associated with `vajropamasamādhi` is maximal with respect to disconnection-result.

## Literal Prose Translation

> Of these immediate-consequence karmas, which is the most blameworthy? False speech that causes division of the Saṅgha is regarded as the most blameworthy. The false speech that occasions Saṅgha-division, through presenting Dharma and non-Dharma in inverted form, is the most blameworthy of all bad conduct. Why? Because it strikes the Tathāgata's Dharma-body and obstructs the paths of beings to heaven and liberation.
>
> When the Saṅgha is divided, entry into assurance, attainment of fruits, dispassion, and destruction of the outflows are obstructed. Meditation, study, recitation, reflection, and practice also do not proceed. The world, together with gods, nāgas, and humans, remains disturbed and dejected until the Saṅgha is reunited. Moreover, its maturation is for a kalpa in Avīci.
>
> Among the remaining immediate-consequence karmas, the fifth, third, and first are successively more serious; killing one's father is the lightest of them all.
>
> But the Blessed One has said that among the three punishments the mental punishment is most blameworthy, and elsewhere that wrong view is supreme among faults. [The answer is:] Saṅgha-division is called most blameworthy when the immediate-consequence karmas are being ranked; mental karma is so called when the three karmas are being ranked; and wrong view when views are being ranked. Alternatively, these statements may be understood respectively with reference to breadth of maturation, injury to many beings, and cutting off wholesome roots.
>
> Which wholesome conduct is then most fruitful? Among wholesome karmas, volition at the summit of existence is the most fruitful of all. Its maturation is exceedingly tranquil and lasts eighty thousand kalpas. This statement is made with respect to maturation-result. With respect to disconnection-result, however, the volition associated with the diamond-like concentration is the most fruitful of all, because its result is the exhaustion of all fetters. Hence the Kārikā says 'in the world.'

## Argument Reconstruction

### 1. Why Saṅgha-dividing false speech is worst

The false speech does more than deceive individuals. It inverts the distinction between Dharma and non-Dharma and thereby damages the very institutional and doctrinal medium through which liberation becomes practically available.

The Bhāṣya gives two grounds:

```text
tathāgatadharmaśarīraprahāritatva
    striking the Tathāgata's Dharma-body

svargāpavargamārgāntarāyakatva
    obstructing the paths to heaven and liberation
```

Its seriousness is therefore measured by the field and by the breadth of practical obstruction it produces.

### 2. The obstruction is systematic

While the Saṅgha remains divided, the Bhāṣya lists a whole series of blocked achievements and practices:

```text
niyāmāvakrānti
    entry into assurance

phalaprāpti
    attainment of fruits

vairāgya
    dispassion

āsravakṣaya
    destruction of the outflows

dhyāna
adhyayana
svādhyāya
cintā
karma
    meditation, study, recitation, reflection, practice
```

Thus the offense is maximally culpable because it interrupts not one isolated good but the practical machinery by which beings advance toward liberation.

### 3. Apparent conflict among rankings

The Bhāṣya carefully distinguishes domains of comparison.

- among the five ānantaryas: Saṅgha-dividing false speech is worst;
- among the three karmas of body, speech, and mind: mental karma may be called worst;
- among views: wrong view may be called worst.

So apparently conflicting superlatives are resolved by specifying the comparison class.

### 4. Maximum wholesome fruit requires specifying the fruit

`bhavāgracetanā` is the most fruitful wholesome worldly karma only with respect to `vipākaphala`, maturation-result.

Its result lasts eighty thousand kalpas and is exceptionally tranquil.

But if the relevant fruit is `visaṃyogaphala`, disconnection from the fetters, the maximal volition is instead that associated with `vajropamasamādhi`, the diamond-like concentration immediately preceding arhatship.

Hence `loke` is essential:

```text
within worldly conditioned existence
    → bhavāgracetanā maximal by maturation

with respect to liberation
    → vajropamasamādhi-cetanā maximal by disconnection
```

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu / doctrinal exposition** — identifies Saṅgha-dividing false speech as maximally culpable among ānantaryas and explains why.
- **Voice: Objector** — raises apparently conflicting scriptural rankings of mental karma and wrong view.
- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — resolves the conflict by distinguishing domains of comparison, then gives an alternative interpretation based on breadth of maturation, harm to many, and severance of wholesome roots.
- **Voice: Vasubandhu / doctrinal exposition** — distinguishes maturation-result from disconnection-result in ranking wholesome volitions.

## New Technical Terms

- **tathāgata-dharma-śarīra** — the Tathāgata's Dharma-body; here the doctrinal and practical body of Dharma that is struck by Saṅgha-division.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-specific explanatory expression in this passage.
  - **function:** explains exceptional karmic gravity.

- **svargāpavargamārga** — path to heaven and path to liberation.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory compound.
  - **function:** identifies the broad practical effects obstructed by schism.

- **niyāmāvakrānti** — entry into assurance or the irreversible order connected with noble realization.
  - **status:** previously encountered doctrinal term.
  - **function:** one of the attainments obstructed by Saṅgha-division.

- **vipākaphala** — maturation-result.
  - **status:** previously encountered.
  - **function:** specifies the axis on which `bhavāgracetanā` is maximally fruitful.

- **visaṃyogaphala** — disconnection-result.
  - **status:** previously encountered.
  - **function:** specifies the liberation-axis on which `vajropamasamādhi-cetanā` is maximal.

- **vajropamasamādhi** — diamond-like concentration.
  - **status:** doctrinal term introduced here as the comparator to bhavāgra.
  - **function:** final meditative basis whose volition exhausts all fetters.

## Doctrinal and Logical Determination

The verse does not establish one undifferentiated ranking of karma. It establishes rankings relative to distinct dimensions.

For fault:

```text
RankFault(x, comparison-class)
```

Thus a karma may be supreme within one class without contradicting another superlative uttered within another class.

For wholesome fruit:

```text
MaxByVipāka(BhavāgraVolition)
MaxByVisaṃyoga(VajropamaSamādhiVolition)
```

Therefore:

```text
maximum conditioned result
    ≠
maximum liberative result
```

This is the decisive Bhāṣya refinement.

## Translation Consequences for Kārikā

The first-pass translation remains broadly correct, but `loke` must carry real weight.

A more exact translation is:

> False speech that causes Saṅgha-division is regarded as maximally culpable. Among wholesome karmas within the world, volition at the summit of existence is maximally fruitful with respect to maturation.

The qualification “with respect to maturation” is supplied by the Bhāṣya and prevents the second half from competing with the liberative fruit of `vajropamasamādhi`.

## Light Organon Contact

The deepest structure here is a distinction of **axes of evaluation**.

```text
same object
    → different ranking
      according to different relation
```

Karmic value is therefore not one-dimensional. Saṅgha-dividing false speech is worst because it attacks the institutional medium of the Path; bhavāgra-volition is greatest only as worldly maturation; diamond-like concentration is greatest as release from bonds.

The maximum within conditioned becoming remains a maximum **within that sphere**. Liberation introduces a different relation and therefore a different ordering principle.
