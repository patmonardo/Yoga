# VAK_4.83_bhasya — Distribution of Unwholesome and Wholesome Karmapathas

## Kārikā

**saptamaḥ svayam apy atra kāme ’nyatra daśāśubhāḥ /**  
**śubhās trayas tu sarvatra saṃmukhībhāva-lābhataḥ // 4.83 //**

> Here, the seventh also occurs in present manifestation. Elsewhere in the desire domain, all ten unwholesome courses occur. But the three wholesome courses occur everywhere, both by present manifestation and by acquisition/endowment.

See: [VAK_4.83.md](./VAK_4.83.md)

## Bhāṣya Source

The Bhāṣya continues directly from the Uttarakuru discussion of VAK 4.82:

```text
saptamaḥ svayamapyatra
asaṃbhinnapralāpaḥ saṃmukhībhāvato 'pyasti /
te hi kliṣṭacittāḥ kadācit gāyanti /

apāpāśayatvāt na prāṇatipātādayaḥ /
niyatāyuṣkatvāddravyastrīparigrahābhāvāt prayojanābhāvācca /
...

kāme 'nyatra daśāśubhāḥ /
svayamapīti vartate /
narakottarakurubhyāmanyatra kāmadhātau daśākuśalāḥ karmapathāḥ saṃmukhībhāvato 'pi vidyante /

tiryakpretadeveṣvasaṃvaranirmuktāḥ /
manuṣyeṣvasaṃvarasaṃgṛhītā api /
...

śubhāstrayastu sarvatra saṃmukhībhāva lābhataḥ // VAkK_4.83 //

pañcasu gatiṣu traidhātuke sarvatrānabhidhyā 'vyāpādasamyagdṛṣṭyaḥ saṃmukhībhāvataḥ samanvāgamataśca vidyante /
```

## Natural Segmentation

### 1. Uttarakuru: the seventh course can manifest

```text
saptamaḥ svayam apy atra
```

The seventh karmapatha is `saṃbhinnapralāpa`, disordered/frivolous afflicted speech.

The Bhāṣya explains:

```text
asaṃbhinnapralāpaḥ saṃmukhībhāvato 'pyasti /
te hi kliṣṭacittāḥ kadācit gāyanti /
```

That course exists there not merely by endowment but also by present manifestation, because inhabitants sometimes sing with an afflicted mind.

This is an important qualification to the favorable character of Uttarakuru:

```text
favorable domain
    ≠
complete absence of every unwholesome manifestation
```

### 2. Why the other courses do not manifest there

The Bhāṣya says:

```text
apāpāśayatvāt na prāṇatipātādayaḥ /
```

Because they lack an evil practical disposition, killing and the other major courses do not arise.

Additional reasons include:

```text
niyatāyuṣkatvāt
    fixed lifespan

dravyastrīparigrahābhāvāt
    absence of possessive property-relations and possessive sexual relations

prayojanābhāvāt
    absence of practical motive or occasion
```

The Bhāṣya even explains sexual relations in Uttarakuru through a distinctive social convention: a couple approaches a tree; if the relation is permissible, the tree covers them, and if impermissible, it does not. The point is not the mythology itself but the doctrinal function: the domain lacks the ordinary conditions for `kāmamithyācāra`.

### 3. Elsewhere in the desire domain

```text
kāme 'nyatra daśāśubhāḥ
```

The Bhāṣya makes the scope exact:

```text
narakottarakurubhyām anyatra kāmadhātau
```

Outside hell and Uttarakuru, throughout the desire domain, all ten unwholesome karmapathas can occur by present manifestation.

The prose adds:

```text
tiryakpretadeveṣv asaṃvaranirmuktāḥ /
manuṣyeṣv asaṃvarasaṃgṛhītā api /
```

Among animals, pretas, and gods, these occur apart from formal `asaṃvara`; among humans they may also be included within `asaṃvara`.

The Bhāṣya notes a doctrinal issue concerning gods: although a god may not kill another god, a god can kill a being in another destiny. Some teachers additionally hold that gods may die through severance at the middle of the head.

### 4. The three wholesome mental karmapathas are universal

```text
śubhās trayas tu sarvatra saṃmukhībhāva-lābhataḥ
```

The Bhāṣya names them explicitly:

```text
anabhidhyā
avyāpāda
samyagdṛṣṭi
```

and states:

```text
pañcasu gatiṣu traidhātuke sarvatra
```

They occur in all five destinies and throughout all three domains.

Moreover, they occur in both modes:

```text
saṃmukhībhāvataḥ
    by present manifestation

samanvāgamataḥ
    by endowment
```

So the verse establishes a strong asymmetry:

```text
unwholesome courses
    domain-restricted in manifestation

wholesome mental courses
    universally available across the three domains
```

## Literal Prose Translation

Here in Uttarakuru, the seventh course also exists by present manifestation, for the inhabitants sometimes sing with afflicted minds. Because they lack an evil practical disposition, killing and the other such courses do not occur. This is due also to fixed lifespan, the absence of ordinary property and possessive sexual relations, and the absence of practical occasion.

Elsewhere in the desire domain, apart from hell and Uttarakuru, all ten unwholesome karmapathas also occur by present manifestation. Among animals, pretas, and gods they are not connected with formal non-restraint, while among humans they may also be included within non-restraint.

The three wholesome courses—non-covetousness, non-hostility, and right view—exist everywhere in the five destinies and throughout the three domains, both by present manifestation and by endowment.

## Argument Reconstruction

The argument has three layers.

### A. Domain does not determine moral type by itself

Uttarakuru is highly favorable, yet `saṃbhinnapralāpa` can still manifest.

Therefore:

```text
favorable environment
    does not entail
complete moral impossibility
```

The immediate determinant remains the actual state of citta and the practical conditions of the act.

### B. Manifestation requires supporting conditions

The absence of other courses is explained by the absence of their necessary supports:

```text
no vulnerable lifespan-condition
    → no killing

no ordinary property relation
    → no theft

no ordinary possessive sexual relation
    → no sexual misconduct

no practical purpose
    → certain verbal courses do not arise
```

Thus domain-specific morality is not arbitrary prohibition. It is a modal structure of possible action.

### C. Wholesome mental courses have the broadest scope

The three wholesome mental courses are not confined to a particular destiny or domain.

```text
anabhidhyā
avyāpāda
samyagdṛṣṭi
```

can be both possessed and presently manifest in every destiny throughout the three domains.

This universal distribution does not mean every being continuously manifests them. It means no domain excludes their acquisition or manifestation in principle.

## Voice Identification

**Voice:** Vasubandhu — explanatory framing and distribution of the courses.

**Voice:** Vaibhāṣika position — the systematic domain-by-domain classification.

**Voice:** alternative teachers — the view that gods can die through severance at the middle of the head.

## New Technical Terms

### `svayam api`
- **Meaning:** also by itself / also in present manifestation.
- **Status:** compressed and ambiguous in the Kārikā; clarified by the Bhāṣya.
- **Function:** marks that the seventh karmapatha is not merely endowed in Uttarakuru but can actually occur.

### `apāpāśaya`
- **Meaning:** absence of an evil practical disposition.
- **Status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit.
- **Function:** explains why major unwholesome karmapathas fail to manifest in Uttarakuru.

### `niyatāyuṣkatva`
- **Meaning:** fixed lifespan.
- **Status:** Bhāṣya-only here.
- **Function:** removes the ordinary completion-condition for killing.

### `asaṃvara`
- **Meaning:** non-restraint; a formalized unwholesome discipline or state opposed to restraint.
- **Status:** previously encountered in Chapter 4.
- **Function:** distinguishes the manner in which the ten unwholesome courses occur in humans from their occurrence in animals, pretas, and gods.

### `anabhidhyā`
- **Meaning:** non-covetousness.
- **Status:** previously implied as the wholesome opposite of `abhidhyā`.
- **Function:** wholesome mental karmapatha.

### `avyāpāda`
- **Meaning:** non-hostility / absence of ill will.
- **Status:** previously encountered as wholesome opposite of `vyāpāda`.
- **Function:** wholesome mental karmapatha.

### `samyagdṛṣṭi`
- **Meaning:** right view.
- **Status:** previously encountered.
- **Function:** wholesome mental karmapatha and positive opposite of `mithyādṛṣṭi`.

## Doctrinal Determination

The full distribution now becomes clearer.

### Uttarakuru

```text
mental unwholesome courses:
    endowed only

seventh verbal course:
    may manifest

other major unwholesome courses:
    blocked by lack of required conditions
```

### Elsewhere in the desire domain

```text
all ten unwholesome karmapathas
    may manifest
```

### Everywhere in the three domains

```text
anabhidhyā
avyāpāda
samyagdṛṣṭi

    may be endowed
    and may manifest
```

This reveals a hierarchy of scope:

```text
specific unwholesome acts
    heavily dependent on domain conditions

wholesome mental orientations
    maximally portable across domains
```

## Translation Consequences

The first-pass interpretation of `svayam` as merely “independently” is too abstract. The Bhāṣya establishes that it means the seventh course exists **by present manifestation** in Uttarakuru.

Likewise, `sarvatra` should be translated strongly here: the Bhāṣya explicitly expands it to:

```text
all five destinies
throughout the three domains
```

This is broader and more precise than simply “everywhere relevant.”

## Logical Determination

VAK 4.83 strengthens the modal architecture begun in 4.82.

We now need at least four levels:

```text
course-type
    universal determination

domain-support
    whether a domain permits its manifestation

continuum-endowment
    whether a given continuum possesses it

event-manifestation
    whether it is currently actual
```

The Bhāṣya’s explanations show that domain-support is itself decomposable:

```text
object support
lifespan condition
property relation
social relation
practical motive
mental disposition
```

Thus:

```text
Manifestable(K, D)
```

is not primitive. It is grounded in a structured set of enabling conditions.

## Light Organon Contact

The most valuable structural point is not the cosmographic taxonomy itself but the distinction between **universal rule and local realizability**.

```text
universal determination
    does not guarantee
local instantiability
```

A domain constrains what determinations can become actual without changing the universal concept of the determination.

This gives a clean practical schema:

```text
Universal Course
    ↓
Domain Conditions
    ↓
Continuum Endowment
    ↓
Present Manifestation
```

The wholesome mental courses are notable because their modal scope is widest: no destiny or domain excludes them in principle.

## Commit Note

VAK 4.83 establishes:

- present manifestation of the seventh karmapatha in Uttarakuru;
- the structural reasons why the other unwholesome courses fail to manifest there;
- full tenfold unwholesome manifestation elsewhere in the desire domain;
- distinction between formal `asaṃvara` in humans and unwholesome courses in other destinies;
- universal distribution of `anabhidhyā`, `avyāpāda`, and `samyagdṛṣṭi` across all five destinies and all three domains;
- explicit coexistence of endowment and present manifestation for those three wholesome mental karmapathas.
