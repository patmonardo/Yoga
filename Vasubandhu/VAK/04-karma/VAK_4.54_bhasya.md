# VAK_4.54_bhasya — What Makes Karma Determinate

## Kārikā Anchor

**VAK 4.54**

```iast
tīvrakleśaprasādena sātatyena ca yat kṛtam |
guṇakṣetre ca niyataṃ tat pitror ghātakaṃ ca yat ||
```

> Karma performed through intense affliction or intense confidence, through continuity, or in a field of qualities is determinate; so too is karma that kills either parent.

See: [VAK_4.54.md](./VAK_4.54.md)

## Source Cautions

The e-text has minor orthographic irregularities (`gunakṣetra`, `tīvrakleśaprasādānāṃ`). They are retained in the quotation.

The syntax of the first explanatory sentence is compressed, but the following prose makes the doctrinal structure clear: `guṇakṣetra` has independent force. Karma directed to such a field can become determinate even without intense affliction or confidence and without continuity.

The final sentence should not be generalized into a claim that all acts concerning one's parents are determinate. Vasubandhu specifies the **killing** of mother or father.

## Natural Argument

VAK 4.50–4.53 classified karma as determinate or indeterminate and then examined the temporal and modal forms of that determination. VAK 4.54 asks the genetic question:

```text
kīdṛśaṃ karma niyatam?
What kind of karma becomes determinate?
```

Vasubandhu gives several distinct sources of determinacy.

### 1. Intense affliction

```text
tīvra kleśa
    → karma acquires exceptional unwholesome force
    → niyata
```

### 2. Intense confidence

```text
tīvra prasāda
    → karma acquires exceptional wholesome force
    → niyata
```

The ethical direction differs, but intensity can make either wholesome or unwholesome karma causally decisive.

### 3. Continuity

```text
sātatya
    repeated / sustained performance
    → causal reinforcement
    → niyata
```

A karmic tendency can become fixed through persistence rather than through a single moment of extreme intensity.

### 4. Field of qualities

Vasubandhu defines `guṇakṣetra` concretely:

```text
Three Jewels
    Buddha
    Dharma
    Saṅgha

or

a distinguished person
    who has attained a particular fruit
    or a particular samāpatti
```

Most importantly, the Bhāṣya says that in such a field wholesome or unwholesome karma can become determinate **even without** intense affliction/confidence and even without continuity.

```text
exceptional field
    itself amplifies karmic efficacy
```

### 5. Killing either parent

`pitarau` is explicitly unpacked as mother and father. Karma involving the killing of either becomes determinate.

```text
mātṛghāta
or
pitṛghāta
    → niyata
```

Thus determinacy can arise through the force of the agent's motivation, the persistence of performance, the exceptional status of the field, or the gravity of a privileged relation.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu** — explanatory prose throughout.

## New Technical Terms

- **tīvra-kleśa** — intense affliction
  - **status:** previously familiar constituent vocabulary, newly functioning as a condition of karmic determinacy
  - **function:** causal intensification of unwholesome karma

- **tīvra-prasāda** — intense confidence, serene faith, lucid trust
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-significant practical term
  - **function:** causal intensification of wholesome karma

- **sātatya** — continuity, sustained repetition
  - **status:** newly important in this karmic context
  - **function:** makes karma determinate through repeated reinforcement

- **guṇakṣetra** — field of qualities
  - **status:** newly defined here
  - **function:** exceptionally weighty field in which karma can become determinate independently of intensity or repetition

- **phala-samāpatti-viśeṣa-prāpta** — one who has attained a particular fruit or a particular samāpatti
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only explanatory specification here
  - **function:** example of a distinguished person constituting a `guṇakṣetra`

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya does not reduce karmic determinacy to the external type of act. The same general action can differ in causal force according to its ground and relation.

```text
NIYATA-KARMA can arise through:

1. motivational intensity
   tīvra-kleśa
   tīvra-prasāda

2. temporal reinforcement
   sātatya

3. relational field
   guṇakṣetra

4. exceptional relational gravity
   killing mother or father
```

A particularly important correction to the Kārikā-only analysis is that these are not best modeled as factors that must accumulate multiplicatively. The Bhāṣya shows that an exceptional `guṇakṣetra` can itself suffice for determinacy.

## Logical Determination

VAK 4.54 supplies the **grounds of necessity** for the modal distinction introduced in VAK 4.50.

Previously:

```text
karma
    → niyata / aniyata
```

Now:

```text
WHY niyata?

because the causal relation has acquired sufficient force
through one of several determinate grounds
```

Formally:

```text
INTENSE_AFFLICTION(K)
    → NIYATA(K)

INTENSE_CONFIDENCE(K)
    → NIYATA(K)

CONTINUOUSLY_PERFORMED(K)
    → NIYATA(K)

EXCELLENT_FIELD(K)
    → NIYATA(K)

KILLS_PARENT(K)
    → NIYATA(K)
```

The important structural principle is:

```text
necessity of result
    is generated through
intensification of causal determination
```

But the intensification may belong to different moments of the relation:

```text
agent-side
    motive

activity-side
    repetition

object-side
    field

relation-side
    privileged gravity
```

## Effect on Kārikā Translation

The first-pass translation is basically sound, but the Bhāṣya clarifies that `guṇakṣetra` should remain the technical **field of qualities**, not be weakened to a generic "worthy recipient."

Preferred translation:

> **Karma performed through intense affliction or intense confidence, through sustained continuity, or in a field of qualities becomes determinate; likewise determinate is karma that kills either one's mother or one's father.**

## Organon Contact Point

This verse provides a restrained but important contact point with practical necessity.

```text
possible causal tendency
    → strengthened ground or relation
    → determinate trajectory
```

Necessity here is not externally imposed on action. It is **generated from the internal strength of the causal configuration**.

That strength may arise intensively, extensively, or relationally:

```text
intensive
    strength of motive

extensive
    continuity through time

relational
    exceptional field or privileged relation
```

This is a genuine upgrade in the present block: VAK 4.50 classified karmic necessity; VAK 4.54 gives its **genesis**.

## Commit Note

Bhāṣya pass for VAK 4.54. Identifies the distinct grounds by which karma becomes determinate: intense affliction, intense confidence, continuity, an exceptional `guṇakṣetra`, and killing either parent. Adds Vasubandhu's concrete definition of `guṇakṣetra` and the crucial clarification that such a field can independently make wholesome or unwholesome karma determinate.
