# VAK_4.09

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> संप्रयोगेण तद्युक्ताः समुत्थानात् क्रियादयः ।
>
> विपर्ययेणाकुशलं परमाव्याकृते ध्रुवे ॥ ४.९ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> saṃprayogeṇa tadyuktāḥ samutthānāt kriyādayaḥ /
>
> viparyayeṇākuśalaṃ paramāvyākṛte dhruve // 4.9 //

VAK 4.08 introduced four grounds according to which ethical determination is
predicated:

```text
paramārthataḥ
    ultimately

svabhāvataḥ
    intrinsically

saṃprayogataḥ
    through association

samutthānataḥ
    through originating condition
```

It then identified liberation as ultimately wholesome and five factors—the
three wholesome roots, `hrī`, and `apatrāpya`—as intrinsically wholesome.

VAK 4.09 completes the structure.

It shows how ethical determination passes through association into a cognitive
complex and through origination into action and further products.

It then reverses the entire structure for the unwholesome and distinguishes
ultimate ethical determination from classification relative to karmic
maturation.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
saṃprayogeṇa              → saṃprayogeṇa
tad-yuktāḥ                → tadyuktāḥ
samutthānāt               → samutthānāt
kriyā-ādayaḥ              → kriyādayaḥ
viparyayeṇa               → viparyayeṇa
akuśalam                  → akuśalam
parama-avyākṛte           → paramāvyākṛte
dhruve                    → dhruve
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| saṃprayogeṇa | instrumental singular of `saṃprayoga` | through association or conjunction |
| tad | prior member of `tad-yukta` | those intrinsically wholesome factors stated in VAK 4.08 |
| yuktāḥ | nominative plural past participle of `√yuj` | joined or associated with |
| samutthānāt | ablative singular of `samutthāna` | from an originating condition; because produced by it |
| kriyā | compound member | action, activity, performance |
| ādayaḥ | nominative masculine plural | beginning with; and the rest |
| viparyayeṇa | instrumental singular | through reversal; by the contrary arrangement |
| akuśalam | nominative/accusative neuter singular used collectively | the unwholesome field or structure |
| parama | compound member | ultimate; without qualification in the relevant sense |
| avyākṛte | nominative neuter dual | the two indeterminate ones |
| dhruve | nominative neuter dual | the two permanent ones |

**The reference of `tad`**

`Tad` refers to the factors identified as intrinsically wholesome in VAK 4.08:

```text
three wholesome roots
hrī
apatrāpya
```

Dharmas conjoined with those factors are wholesome through association.

**The range of `kriyādayaḥ`**

`Kriyādayaḥ` is broader than a single observable action.

The Bhāṣya includes:

```text
bodily karma
verbal karma

and conditioned formations dissociated from mind,
including arising and related determinations,
acquisition,
the attainment of cessation,
and the non-percipient attainment
```

These do not receive their ethical quality through simultaneous mental
association. They receive it through the wholesome or unwholesome complex that
produces them.

**The dual `paramāvyākṛte dhruve`**

The two permanent, ultimately indeterminate dharmas are identified by the
Bhāṣya:

```text
ākāśa
apratisaṃkhyā-nirodha
```

They are both unconditioned.

**Witness cautions**

The prose witness contains visible spacing and typographical disturbances,
including forms corresponding to `samutthānāt`, `kāyavākkarmaṇi`, and the
compressed lists of formations.

The argument and principal identifications are secure. The exact segmentation
of every item in the long list should remain accountable to the source witness
rather than be silently regularized.

The discussion of `prāpti` also introduces an explicit complication. Some
acquisitions appear to be produced by a mind of a different ethical type. The
Bhāṣya marks this as requiring an explanation; the simple origination rule
must not be presented as exceptionless without qualification.

## 4. Grammar

The first pāda has an understood ethical predicate:

```text
tad-yuktāḥ [dharmāḥ]
    saṃprayogeṇa [kuśalāḥ]

dharmas joined with those [intrinsically wholesome factors]
    are [wholesome] through association
```

The instrumental `saṃprayogeṇa` expresses the relation through which goodness
belongs.

The next phrase shifts from association to production:

```text
kriyā-ādayaḥ
    samutthānāt [kuśalāḥ]

actions and the rest
    are [wholesome] from their originating condition
```

The ablative `samutthānāt` expresses causal source.

The Bhāṣya supplies the full relation:

```text
dharmas associated with
    wholesome roots, hrī, and apatrāpya

produce
    bodily and verbal karma
    and specified dissociated formations

those products
    are wholesome through that production
```

The third phrase reverses the preceding fourfold wholesome structure:

```text
viparyayeṇa
    akuśalam [veditavyam]

the unwholesome
    is to be understood through the contrary arrangement
```

The Bhāṣya expands the ellipsis:

```text
saṃsāra
    → ultimately unwholesome

unwholesome roots + shamelessness + lack of moral concern
    → intrinsically unwholesome

dharmas associated with them
    → unwholesome through association

bodily/verbal karma and other products generated by them
    → unwholesome through originating condition
```

The final dual predication is direct:

```text
dhruve [dve asaṃskṛte]
    parama-avyākṛte

the two permanent [unconditioned dharmas]
    are ultimately indeterminate
```

The Bhāṣya identifies them as space and non-analytical cessation.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> Those joined with them are wholesome through association; actions and the
> rest are wholesome through their originating condition. The unwholesome is
> constituted by the contrary arrangement. The two permanent dharmas are
> ultimately indeterminate.

The repeated “wholesome” is syntactically supplied from the fourfold
classification opened in VAK 4.08.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Dharmas conjoined with the three wholesome roots, ethical self-respect, and
> moral circumspection are wholesome through association, like water mixed
> with medicine. Bodily and verbal karma and the related dissociated
> formations produced by that wholesome cognitive complex are wholesome
> through their originating condition, like a nurse's milk affected by
> medicine she has consumed. The unwholesome field is articulated through the
> inverse fourfold structure. Space and non-analytical cessation—the two
> permanent unconditioned dharmas other than liberation—are indeterminate in
> the ultimate sense.

The analogies, the extended range of `kriyādayaḥ`, the inverse fourfold
unwholesome scheme, and the identification of the permanent dual are Bhāṣya
determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Ethical determination propagates through a functional order. Some factors
> are good through what they intrinsically are. Other factors receive goodness
> through participation in the same cognitive complex. Action and further
> products receive goodness through the complex that brings them forth. The
> same pipeline can transmit unwholesome determination in the contrary
> direction. Yet ultimate characterization and practical classification by
> karmic result remain different Aspects: saṃsāra can be unwholesome as a whole
> while particular contaminated dharmas remain functionally wholesome or
> indeterminate relative to maturation.

The term “Aspect” belongs to the Organon vocabulary.

It names a determinate functional relation within one articulated Dharma
process. It does not replace the Sanskrit terms, and it does not turn
association, origin, or ultimate status into independent substances.

**Controlled Organon term**

| Organon term | Function here |
|---|---|
| Aspect | a determinate way Dharma operates or a predicate belongs within a functional pipeline |

The correspondences are:

```text
intrinsic Aspect
    → source determination

associative Aspect
    → co-function within one cognitive complex

originative Aspect
    → production of action and further formations

ultimate Aspect
    → determination relative to liberation or saṃsāra as a whole

result Aspect
    → classification relative to karmic maturation
```

“Aspect” does not mean a merely subjective point of view.

Each Aspect specifies an objective relation under which the determination is
valid.

## 8. Logical Determination

**The wholesome functional pipeline**

The four grounds can now be displayed as an articulated order:

```text
ultimate completion
    liberation / nirvāṇa

intrinsic ethical sources
    three wholesome roots
    hrī
    apatrāpya

association within a cognitive complex
    other conjoined dharmas become wholesome
    through saṃprayoga

production from that cognitive complex
    bodily karma
    verbal karma
    dissociated formations
    become wholesome through samutthāna
```

In directional form:

```text
intrinsically wholesome factors
    → associated cognitive complex
        → originating condition
            → bodily/verbal action
            → other conditioned products
```

The arrows do not all express the same relation.

```text
intrinsic factor ↔ associated factor
    → conjunction within one event

cognitive complex → action/product
    → causal origination
```

This distinction prevents `saṃprayoga` from being reduced to causation and
prevents `samutthāna` from being reduced to mere coexistence.

**The medicinal analogies**

The Bhāṣya makes the difference concrete.

Association is compared to water mixed with medicine:

```text
medicine
    + water
        → medicinal mixture

intrinsically wholesome factors
    + associated dharmas
        → wholesome cognitive complex
```

Origination is compared to the milk of a nurse affected by medicine she has
consumed:

```text
medicine taken by the nurse
    → affects the milk subsequently produced

wholesome cognitive complex
    → produces ethically determined action and formations
```

The first analogy concerns mixture or conjunction.

The second concerns transmission through genesis.

**The inverse unwholesome pipeline**

`Viparyayeṇa` does not mean a random negation of goodness.

It means that the same relational architecture is filled by contrary
determinations:

```text
ultimately
    saṃsāra
        → continuation of suffering
        → supreme insecurity

intrinsically
    unwholesome roots
    shamelessness
    lack of moral concern

through association
    dharmas conjoined with those factors

through origination
    bodily/verbal karma and formations produced by them
```

Thus ethical opposition does not destroy the pipeline form.

It reverses its determining content.

**The `prāpti` complication**

The Bhāṣya notices that some acquisitions may be produced by a mind of a
different ethical class.

This creates a problem for the simple inference:

```text
product has ethical quality Q
if and only if
its immediately originating citta has ethical quality Q
```

The passage mentions cases connected with restoration of wholesome roots and
with return to or loss from a domain.

Vasubandhu marks the issue as something that must be explained.

The official study therefore retains the qualification:

```text
samutthāna transmits ethical determination

but the exact rule governing every prāpti
    cannot be inferred from this verse alone
```

**Ultimate and maturation-relative classification**

Calling saṃsāra ultimately unwholesome generates an objection:

```text
every contaminated dharma lies within saṃsāra

saṃsāra is ultimately unwholesome

therefore
    every contaminated dharma must be unwholesome

therefore
    no contaminated dharma can be wholesome or indeterminate
```

Vasubandhu blocks the inference by distinguishing Aspects of classification.

```text
ultimate Aspect
    saṃsāra as the continuation of suffering
        → unwholesome

maturation Aspect
    contaminated dharma producing desirable maturation
        → called wholesome

    contaminated dharma not specified toward such maturation
        → called indeterminate
```

The predicates differ because their standards differ.

There is no contradiction:

```text
one determination
    may belong to saṃsāra
        and therefore be ultimately unwholesome

while also
    producing desirable maturation
        and therefore being functionally wholesome
```

**What is ultimately indeterminate?**

If indeterminacy is demanded at the ultimate level itself, only two permanent
unconditioned dharmas qualify:

```text
ākāśa
apratisaṃkhyā-nirodha
```

They are neither liberation, which is ultimately wholesome, nor saṃsāra, which
is ultimately unwholesome.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Dharma arranges itself as functional pipelines**

VAK 4.09 gives textual substance to the proposition that Dharma arranges
itself in functional pipelines.

The pipeline is not an external diagram imposed upon inert items.

Each stage expresses how ethical determination becomes transmissible:

```text
intrinsic source
    → associative field
        → originating complex
            → action
                → conditioned continuation and maturation
```

The initial factors do not disappear when the next stage arises. Their
determination is preserved under a new functional relation.

This is why we call the stages Aspects.

```text
an Aspect
    = Dharma functioning under a determinate relation

not
    = a separately existing atom
```

Association and origination are genuinely different Aspects.

Within association, several factors constitute one cognitive event. Within
origination, that event becomes productive of bodily action, verbal action,
and further conditioned determinations.

The pipeline preserves identity through transformation without requiring an
unchanging particle to travel from stage to stage.

**Persistence without a travelling substance**

This provides another control for our account of persistence.

A crude model imagines ethical quality as a tiny property attached to an atom
and transported unchanged through time.

The Bhāṣya gives a relational model:

```text
goodness is intrinsic in one function

goodness is associative in another

goodness is originative in the product

goodness is assessed through maturation at a later stage
```

What persists is the determination.

How it belongs changes with the Aspect.

This is precisely the difference between essential persistence and sensible
duration. The same ethical determination does not need to remain present as
one unaltered appearance. It persists by governing the relations through which
the pipeline develops.

**Aspects preserve logical levels**

The objection concerning saṃsāra shows why the term is necessary.

Without Aspect discrimination, the reasoning collapses:

```text
saṃsāra is ultimately unwholesome
    → everything in saṃsāra is simply unwholesome
```

With Aspect discrimination:

```text
as a moment of saṃsāra's whole
    → contaminated existence is ultimately unwholesome

as a cause evaluated through vipāka
    → a particular contaminated dharma may be wholesome,
      unwholesome,
      or indeterminate
```

The same content is determined differently because the relation of judgment is
different.

This is not relativism. The Aspect is specified by an objective functional
question:

```text
What is its final status?
What is it intrinsically?
With what does it co-function?
What does it produce?
What maturation does it yield?
```

**Abhidharma as pipeline discrimination**

Here Abhidharma begins to show its positive meaning as Absolute
Discrimination.

It does not merely divide a field into increasingly small units.

It identifies:

```text
the source determination
the associative complex
the productive transition
the generated action
the resultant classification
the ultimate status
```

These are not six things accidentally placed in sequence.

They are six Aspects through which one practical determination articulates its
complete actuality.

The more adequate movement is therefore:

```text
Abhidharma
    → discriminates functional relations

Dharma
    → arranges itself through those relations

Aspects
    → preserve the difference of functions

pipeline
    → exhibits their ordered unity
```

This is an Organon reconstruction, but it is closely answerable to the
Bhāṣya's actual distinctions between intrinsic nature, association,
origination, maturation, and ultimate status.

**Consequence for the positive `avijñapti`**

The positive theory of `avijñapti` remains open, as agreed.

VAK 4.09 gives it another essential constraint.

If `avijñapti` belongs within the practical pipeline, we should not seek one
unchanging hidden object travelling from intention to result.

We should ask which Aspect of persistence it expresses:

```text
originating determination
    → action
        → non-manifest continuation
            → restraint or karmic efficacy
                → maturation
```

The Kant-clean question is about the conditions under which this continuing
determination can be validly conceived.

The Yoga-clean question is about the field and disciplined transformation
through which it is retained and actualized.

The answer must preserve real practical continuity without converting that
continuity into a transcendent carrier.

VAK 4.09 does not yet provide the completed answer. It shows why a functional
Aspect account is possible.
