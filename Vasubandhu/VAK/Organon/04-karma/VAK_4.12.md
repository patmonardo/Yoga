# VAK_4.12

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> प्रवर्तके शुभादौ हि स्यात्त्रिधाप्यनुवर्तकम् ।
>
> तुल्यं मुनेः शुभं यावन्नोभयं तु विपाकजम् ॥ ४.१२ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> pravartake śubhādau hi syāt tridhāpy anuvartakam /
>
> tulyaṃ muneḥ śubhaṃ yāvan nobhayaṃ tu vipākajam // 4.12 //

VAK 4.10 distinguished the cognition that projects an action from the cognition
that accompanies its execution.

VAK 4.11 distributed cognition-types across those two functions.

VAK 4.12 now asks whether the ethical quality of the continuator must match the
ethical quality of the initiator.

The answer is no.

```text
causal continuity
    does not require
ethical identity at every cognitive station
```

The verse then gives two qualifications:

```text
the Buddha
    → constrained and asymmetric correspondence

maturation-born cognition
    → neither initiator nor continuator
```

| vipākajam | nominative neuter singular compound adjective | born from karmic maturation |

**The range of `śubhādi`**

The Bhāṣya expands `śubha` and “so forth” into the standard threefold ethical
classification:

```text
kuśala
akuśala
avyākṛta

wholesome
unwholesome
indeterminate
```

For each possible initiator, the continuator may belong to any of the three
classes.

**The referent of `muni`**

`Muni` is not left as a generic sage in the commentary.

The Bhāṣya identifies the referent:

```text
buddhasya tu bhagavataḥ
    for the Buddha, the Blessed One
```

The Buddha-case must therefore be translated specifically.

**The compressed `tulyaṃ ... śubhaṃ yāvat`**

The kārikā compresses an asymmetric rule.

The Bhāṣya explains:

```text
wholesome initiator
    → wholesome continuator

indeterminate initiator
    → indeterminate continuator
    → or wholesome continuator

never:
wholesome initiator
    → indeterminate continuator
```

Thus `tulya` does not establish exceptionless identity. It establishes a
restricted correspondence whose possible variation is only toward the
wholesome.

**Witness cautions**

The verse line in the prose witness reads `vipāpkajam`; the sense and following
explanation require `vipākajam`.

Another prose form reads `kuślaṃ` for expected `kuśalaṃ`.

The list of indeterminate Buddha cognitions is transmitted with disturbed
spacing. It is read as:

```text
vipākaja-citta
airyāpathika-citta
nirmāṇa-citta

maturation-born cognition
posture-related cognition
magical-creation cognition
```

The functional argument does not depend upon concealing these textual
irregularities.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The first half establishes a conditional range:

```text
pravartake
    śubha-ādau [sati]

anuvartakam
    tridhā api
    syāt

when the initiator is wholesome and so forth,
the continuator may nevertheless be threefold
```

The Bhāṣya repeats the construction for every ethical value:

```text
if pravartaka = kuśala,
    anuvartaka may be kuśala / akuśala / avyākṛta

if pravartaka = akuśala,
    anuvartaka may be kuśala / akuśala / avyākṛta

if pravartaka = avyākṛta,
    anuvartaka may be kuśala / akuśala / avyākṛta
```

The second half introduces the Buddha exception:

```text
muneḥ
    [anuvartakam pravartakena] tulyam
    śubhaṃ yāvat

for the Sage,
the continuator corresponds with the initiator,
with variation extending only toward the wholesome
```

The Bhāṣya, rather than the surface compression alone, fixes the asymmetric
meaning.

The final predication excludes maturation-born cognition:

```text
vipāka-jam [cittam]
    na ubhayam

maturation-born cognition
    is neither [initiator nor continuator]
```

The prose gives the reason:

```text
nirabhisaṃskāra-vāhitvāt
    because it proceeds without deliberate constructive activity
```

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> When the initiator is wholesome and so forth, the continuator may
> nevertheless be threefold. For the Sage it corresponds, with variation only
> as far as the wholesome. Maturation-born [cognition], however, is neither.

The second sentence deliberately preserves some compression. Its exact rule is
given in the Bhāṣya-informed translation.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> In an ordinary action-series, whether the initiating cognition is wholesome,
> unwholesome, or indeterminate, the cognition accompanying performance may
> independently be wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate. In the Buddha the
> relation is constrained: a wholesome initiator is followed by a wholesome
> continuator; an indeterminate initiator may be followed by an indeterminate
> or wholesome continuator, but a wholesome initiator is never followed by an
> indeterminate one. Maturation-born cognition functions as neither initiator
> nor continuator because it proceeds without deliberate constructive
> activity.

The full ninefold ordinary range, the asymmetric Buddha rule, and the causal
explanation of the `vipākaja` exclusion are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> A deed can remain one causally ordered process while the ethical quality of
> its successive cognitive functions changes. The determination that begins
> the deed does not mechanically dictate every cognition participating in its
> execution. In the Buddha this variability is normatively constrained: the
> course may move from indeterminate functioning into wholesome participation,
> but never from wholesome initiation into merely indeterminate continuation.
> A cognition that merely manifests the maturation of prior karma occupies a
> resultant Aspect and lacks the deliberate constructive force required to
> produce or sustain present action.

The verse therefore separates:

```text
unity of the action-series

from

identity of ethical quality at every station
```

It also separates receiving a prior result from actively constructing present
karma.

**Controlled Organon terms**

| Organon term | Function here |
|---|---|
| Ethical Aspect | ethical determination at one functional station of an action-series |
| Resultant Aspect | reception or manifestation of prior karmic maturation |
| Productive Aspect | initiation or operative continuation of present action |
| Aspect transition | lawful change of determination between stations in one pipeline |

The difference is:

```text
Aspect transition
    → change within an organized practical process

not
    → destruction of the process's unity
```

## 8. Logical Determination

**The ordinary ninefold matrix**

The Bhāṣya rejects an exceptionless matching rule:

```text
initiator has ethical quality Q
    does not entail
continuator has ethical quality Q
```

The ordinary range is the full Cartesian product:

| Initiator | Wholesome continuator | Unwholesome continuator | Indeterminate continuator |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| wholesome | possible | possible | possible |
| unwholesome | possible | possible | possible |
| indeterminate | possible | possible | possible |

Thus:

```text
3 initiator values
    ×
3 continuator values
    =
9 possible ethical pairings
```

The action-series is causally connected without being ethically homogeneous.

**The Buddha's constrained transition relation**

No unwholesome cognition is assigned to the Buddha in this discussion.

The permitted transitions are:

```text
kuśala pravartaka
    → kuśala anuvartaka

avyākṛta pravartaka
    → avyākṛta anuvartaka
    → or kuśala anuvartaka
```

The forbidden transition is:

```text
kuśala pravartaka
    ↛ avyākṛta anuvartaka
```

This is not simple equality.

It is monotonic ethical constraint:

```text
indeterminate
    may continue as indeterminate
    or rise into wholesome

wholesome
    does not fall into indeterminate continuation
```

“Monotonic” is an Organon description of the transition rule, not a Sanskrit
term in the passage.

**The school dispute about Buddha-cognition**

Another school argues:

```text
Buddhas are always concentrated

their continua are exclusively wholesome

therefore
    Buddhas possess no indeterminate cognition
```

The cited verse describes the Buddha as concentrated while:

```text
walking
standing
sleeping
sitting
```

The Vaibhāṣika response distinguishes concentration from universal ethical
wholesomeness:

```text
always concentrated
    → mind does not scatter involuntarily

does not entail
    → every Buddha-cognition is wholesome
```

The Vaibhāṣikas therefore retain indeterminate:

```text
maturation-born cognition
posture-related cognition
magical-creation cognition
```

The Bhāṣya preserves the disagreement rather than erasing it.

**Why maturation-born cognition is neither**

The functional contrast is:

```text
Productive Aspect
    → deliberately constructs or maintains present action

Resultant Aspect
    → carries the maturation of prior karma
    → proceeds without deliberate construction
```

Therefore:

```text
vipākaja-citta
    → not pravartaka
    → not anuvartaka
```

This exclusion does not mean maturation-born cognition is nonexistent or
causally unrelated to the continuum.

It means that receiving a result is not the same function as actively
projecting or sustaining a new deed.

**Continuity without qualitative identity**

The action-process can be represented as:

```text
pravartaka(Q1)
    → projects action A

anuvartaka(Q2)
    → accompanies execution of A

Q1 may differ from Q2

yet A remains one causally organized action-series
```

Identity of process is therefore grounded in causal-functional organization,
not in repetition of one ethical predicate at every moment.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Closure of the difficult opening architecture**

VAK 4.12 completes the analysis begun when bodily and verbal karma were divided
into manifest and non-manifest modes.

The movement from VAK 4.01 through 4.12 can now be seen provisionally:

```text
karma classified
    → mental, bodily, verbal

bodily and verbal karma differentiated
    → vijñapti / avijñapti

their material support and ethical range determined

ethical predication differentiated by ground
    → ultimate
    → intrinsic
    → associative
    → originative

origination internally differentiated
    → initiator
    → continuator

cognition-types distributed across those functions

ethical variation permitted within the continuing action-series
```

This is not yet a declaration that the next verse must have a predetermined
meaning. But 4.12 does complete a coherent causal-functional block.

**A pipeline is not ethical automation**

The functional pipeline does not mechanically reproduce the ethical quality of
its first station.

```text
initial quality
    ≠ destiny of every later cognitive moment
```

This is existentially important.

An action already initiated can be accompanied by a cognition of a different
ethical character. Continuation is therefore active participation, not passive
repetition.

The continuator does not merely preserve what was given. It contributes its
own Ethical Aspect to the deed as performed.

This prevents two reductions:

```text
action
    ≠ one frozen intention stretched through time

continuity
    ≠ automatic reproduction of the beginning
```

**Persistence and transformation**

We can now state more precisely how a practical determination persists through
a vanishing field.

```text
the initiating cognition arises and vanishes

the action remains causally projected

the continuing cognition arises
    with its own ethical determination

the one deed is actualized
    through the relation between them
```

Persistence therefore includes the possibility of transformation.

If persistence meant the exclusion of all difference, only one identical
cognitive atom could count as the deed's bearer. The Bhāṣya instead permits
different cognitions and different ethical values within one action-series.

Essence persists by organizing difference.

**The Buddha as disciplined transition**

The Buddha-case does not abolish functional plurality.

It disciplines its transitions.

```text
ordinary series
    → all nine ethical pairings possible

Buddha-series
    → indeterminate may remain indeterminate
    → indeterminate may become wholesome
    → wholesome remains wholesome
```

This gives continuous concentration a precise, non-mystical interpretation.

The Vaibhāṣika does not need to claim that every Buddha-cognition is ethically
wholesome. The Buddha's mind is always composed because it does not scatter
involuntarily.

```text
samādhi
    → governance of transition

not necessarily
    → erasure of every functionally indeterminate cognition
```

This strongly supports our understanding of discipline as organization of the
field rather than suppression of differentiation.

**Result is not production**

The exclusion of maturation-born cognition introduces a decisive pipeline
boundary.

```text
vipāka
    → actuality received from prior karma

abhisaṃskāra
    → deliberate construction of present karma
```

A cognition can belong to the causal continuum as a result without functioning
as a producer in the present action.

This is another case where Aspect discrimination prevents category error.

```text
the same continuum
    contains resultant Aspects
    and productive Aspects

but result
    is not thereby production
```

The distinction will matter whenever Karma is treated as a functional system.
A pipeline must identify which stages receive, which transform, which project,
and which sustain.

**The transition toward discipline**

If 4.13 begins the restraint material anticipated by the chapter map, the
preceding block has prepared it thoroughly.

Restraint will not enter as an isolated moral command.

It will enter a field already differentiated by:

```text
intention
manifestation
non-manifest continuation
ethical source
association
origination
initiation
operative accompaniment
result
```

That makes a later comparison with yama and niyama potentially substantive.

But the exact transition belongs to VAK 4.13 and must be established from that
text after the coffee boundary.
