# VAK_4.56

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
ये निरोधारणामैत्रीदर्शनार्हत्फलोत्थिताः ।
तेषु कारापकारस्य फलं सद्योऽनुभूयते ॥ ४.५६ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```iast
ye nirodhāraṇāmaitrīdarśanārhatphalotthitāḥ /
teṣu kārāpakārasya phalaṃ sadyo 'nubhūyate // 4.56 //
```

Padapāṭha-style separation:

```iast
ye nirodha-araṇā-maitrī-darśana-arhat-phala-utthitāḥ /
teṣu kāra-apakārasya phalam sadyaḥ anubhūyate //
```

The repeated force of `utthita`, “emerged,” applies to all five states. The
Bhāṣya emphasizes the freshly transformed condition of the continuum.

| `maitrī` | compound member | loving-kindness |

**`utthita / vyutthita`**

The verse does not merely name persons who possess an attainment. It concerns
persons just emerged from a powerful attainment or path-event.

**`kāra / apakāra`**

Benefit and injury express opposite ethical relations to the same exceptional
field. The polarity changes the quality of the fruit; the field accelerates
its maturation.

**`sadyaḥ`**

“Immediately” means that the result is necessarily experienced in the present
life, with exceptional temporal proximity emphasized by the passage.

## 4. Grammar

**The five relative members**

```text
ye
nirodhāt utthitāḥ
araṇāyāḥ utthitāḥ
maitryāḥ utthitāḥ
darśanāt utthitāḥ
arhat-phalāt utthitāḥ
```

> Those who have emerged from cessation, non-contention, loving-kindness, the
> Path of Seeing, or the fruit of arhatship.

The compressed compound distributes the participle across five ablative
relations.

**Benefit and injury toward them**

```text
teṣu
kāra-apakārasya
phalam
sadyaḥ anubhūyate
```

> The fruit of benefit or injury directed toward them is experienced
> immediately.

The locative `teṣu` expresses the exceptional recipient-field in relation to
which the action is performed.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> For those emerged from cessation, non-contention, loving-kindness, the Path
> of Seeing, or the fruit of arhatship, the result of benefit or injury done
> toward them is experienced immediately.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Benefit or injury directed toward someone freshly emerged from cessation
> attainment, non-contention, loving-kindness, the Path of Seeing, or the fruit
> of arhatship necessarily bears fruit in the present life. In each case the
> recipient's continuum has just been exceptionally calmed, benevolently
> oriented, or purified; that freshly transformed condition makes it an
> especially powerful karmic field.

The emphasis on freshness, the state of each continuum, and the present-life
necessity of the result are supplied by the Bhāṣya.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The causal force of an action depends partly on the actuality of the field
> into which it enters. A continuum freshly transformed by cessation,
> immeasurable benevolence, seeing, or completed liberation is not a passive
> recipient. Its achieved determination intensifies the relation itself, so
> that benefit or injury directed toward it passes rapidly into experienced
> result. Field-state therefore participates internally in the constitution of
> karmic efficacy.

The same formal acceleration applies to benefit and injury while their ethical
results remain opposed.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**Emerged from cessation**

The person emerging from `nirodhasamāpatti` possesses exceptional peace of
mind because the attainment resembles nirvāṇa. The Bhāṣya strikingly compares
the person to one who has gone to nirvāṇa and returned.

**Emerged from non-contention**

The continuum is pervaded by an elevated orientation toward preventing
conflict among immeasurable beings and by immeasurable cultivation of merit.

**Emerged from loving-kindness**

The continuum is pervaded by an elevated intention for the happiness of
immeasurable beings and by immeasurable cultivation of merit.

**Emerged from the Path of Seeing**

All afflictions abandoned by seeing have just been abandoned. The Bhāṣya calls
the continuum freshly stainless through transformation of its basis.

**Emerged from arhat-fruition**

All factors abandoned by cultivation have just been abandoned. The continuum
is freshly purified through a more complete transformation of its basis.

**`āśrayaparivṛtti`**

Here this means transformation or alteration of the supporting psychophysical
continuum. The expression should not be loaded automatically with every later
Yogācāra determination of “transformation of the basis.”

**Negative boundary**

Persons emerging from the remaining incomplete stages of the Path of
Cultivation are not assigned the same field-power because their fruit-nature
and fresh purification are not complete in the required way.

## 8. Logical Determination

**Field-state determines causal force**

```text
ATTAINMENT_OR_PATH_EVENT(recipient)
    → FRESH_TRANSFORMATION(recipient continuum)
    → EXCEPTIONAL_FIELD(recipient)
```

Then:

```text
ACTION(agent, recipient)
    + EXCEPTIONAL_FIELD(recipient)
    → IMMEDIATE_PRESENT_FRUIT(action)
```

**Status alone is insufficient to explain the rule**

```text
abstract rank
    ≠ complete field-determination

freshly actualized continuum-state
    → exceptional field-power
```

The timing relative to the attainment is doctrinally significant.

**Ethical polarity and temporal acceleration**

```text
benefit toward exceptional field
    → immediate favorable fruit

injury toward exceptional field
    → immediate unfavorable fruit
```

Field-power accelerates both without neutralizing their opposition.

**Continuation of sufficient-ground analysis**

```text
freshly transformed field
    is sufficient ground
    for immediate maturation
```

This specifies one of the field-based grounds introduced in VAK 4.54–4.55.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**The recipient is an active moment of Karma as Ground**

```text
agent
    + action
    + recipient in a determinate state
    → complete karmic relation
```

The field is not an inert Object receiving an already complete act. Its
determination participates in grounding the causal force and timing of the
result.

**Fresh transformation has maximal relational force**

The Bhāṣya repeatedly stresses the newly calmed, benevolent, or purified
continuum. The Path-event has just reorganized the field; action entering that
field encounters the attained determination in its fresh actuality.

**Dharmapāda contact**

The sufficient Ground is now explicitly relational:

```text
subjective orientation of agent
    is not the whole ground

objective determination of recipient-field
    enters the ground itself
```

This supports the Dharmapāda architecture without making the primary
translation Hegelian.

**Doctrinal limit**

The five cases and their explanations belong to Vasubandhu's karmic system.
Their broader logical form can be retained without turning every encounter
with a spiritually accomplished person into the technical rule stated here.

This is the Organon contact. The five fields, their transformed states, and
the negative boundary belong to the Bhāṣya.
