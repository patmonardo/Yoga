# VAK_6.64 Bhāṣya — Two Liberations; Trainee Complete Under a Cut

Give the wisdom-liberated Arhat her due. Missing cessation-attainment is not a hole in the cut of the kleśas.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> nirodhalābhy ubhayatovimuktaḥ prajñayetaraḥ |
> samāpattīndriyaphalaiḥ pūrṇaḥ śaikṣo 'bhidhīyate || 6.64 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> One who has attained cessation is liberated in both respects; the other is
> liberated through prajñā. A trainee is designated complete with respect to
> contemplative attainments, faculties, and fruits.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.64.md`](./VAK_6.64.md). Next: 6.65.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Who is liberated both ways, who through prajñā? She who has cessation-attainment
> is liberated both ways: by the powers of prajñā and samādhi, free of the kleśa-
> cover and the cover on the releases. The other is liberated through prajñā: by
> that power alone, free of the kleśa-cover.
>
> By what is a trainee complete? Completeness is three: fruit, faculty, attainment.
> Fruit alone: faith-resolved non-returner who is not a body-witness. Faculty alone:
> seeing-attained, not yet free of passion. Fruit and faculty; fruit and attainment;
> all three. Completeness by attainment alone, or by attainment and faculty without
> fruit, does not stand.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
prajñā cuts kleśa-cover for both Arhats
samādhi adds the release-cover cut
trainee-pūrṇa is under a named axis
attainment does not stand without fruit
```

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *itaraḥ* is a full Arhat;
- *pūrṇa* is relational;
- no samāpatti-only completeness.
