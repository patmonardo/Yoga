# VAK_6.01 Bhāṣya — Abandonment Already Taught; Status of the Two Paths

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kleśaprahāṇam ākhyātaṃ satyadarśanabhāvanāt |
> dvividho bhāvanāmārgo darśanākhyas tv anāsravaḥ || 6.01 ||

> Abandonment of the afflictions has been declared: through seeing the truths
> and through cultivation. The path of cultivation is twofold; the one called
> seeing is uncontaminated.

Truths and their order: 6.02.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.01.md`](./VAK_6.01.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> uktaṃ yathā prahāṇaṃ parijñākhyāṃ labhate / tad api ca kleśaprahāṇam ākhyātaṃ satyadarśanabhāvanāt /
> darśanaheyā bhāvanāheyāś ca kleśā iti vistareṇākhyātam /
> tāv idānīṃ darśanabhāvanāmārgau kim anāsravau sāsravāv iti vaktavyam /
> dvidho bhāvanāmārgo laukiko lokottaraś ca /
> darśanamārgas tu lokottara eva traidhātukapratipakṣatvāt /
> navaprakārāṇāṃ darśanaheyānāṃ sakṛtprahāṇāc ca /
> na hi laukikasya eṣā śaktir asti /

**Witness.** Pradhan 327|03–10. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> How an abandonment takes the name *parijñā* has been taught. Afflictions are
> abandoned through seeing the truths and through cultivation — seeing-abandonable
> and cultivation-abandonable. Now: are these two paths uncontaminated or
> contaminated?
>
> Cultivation-path: two — mundane and supramundane. Seeing-path: supramundane
> only — counteragent to the three domains, and it abandons at once the nine
> kinds that seeing abandons. A mundane path does not have that power.

## 5. Twofold ≠ Two Equals

`Anāsrava` is explained by what seeing does. `Lokottara` is not a place outside
the world. Nine kinds invoked, not listed here.

## 6. Split

| path | status |
|---|---|
| bhāvanā | laukika or lokottara |
| darśana | lokottara / anāsrava only |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `dvividhaḥ` = mundane and supramundane cultivation;
- `tu` blocks the same split for seeing;
- two reasons: three-domain counter-work; sakṛt abandonment.
