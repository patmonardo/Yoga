# VAK_6.44 Bhāṣya — Aśaikṣa Opens: Diamond Path at the Last Grade

Darśanamārga closed at 6.43. This verse opens the aśaikṣa section.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ābhavāgrāṣṭabhāgakṣid arhattve pratipannakaḥ |
> navamasyāpy ānantaryapathe vajropamaś ca saḥ || 6.44 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> Destroying [the grades] up through the eighth portion at the summit of existence,
> one is proceeding toward arhatship. Even on the uninterrupted Path against the
> ninth, that [Path] is diamond-like.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.44.md`](./VAK_6.44.md). Running source first-half: `pratipakṣakaḥ`. Next: 6.45, kṣayajñāna.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> The non-returner is the subject. From detachment of one first-dhyāna grade through
> eight grades gone at the summit, he proceeds toward arhatship. Even on the
> uninterrupted Path that abandons the ninth summit-grade, still a candidate.
>
> That uninterrupted Path is called diamond-like concentration: it can break every
> latent affliction. It does not now break all — they are already broken — but it
> is able to break all, being the strongest of the uninterrupted Paths.
>
> They describe many diamond-forms: by object, knowledge-aspect, ground; fifty-two
> at the preparatory level; more if Path-anvaya takes each ground's counter separately;
> still more by lineage and faculty. Same last function, not many last fruits.
