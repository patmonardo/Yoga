# VAK_6.06 Bhāṣya — Two Withdrawals; Discontent and Great Desire

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vyapakarṣadvayavato nāsantuṣṭa-mahecchayoḥ |
> labdhe bhūyaḥ-spṛhātuṣṭir alabdhecchā mahecchatā || 6.06 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> [Cultivation is accomplished] for one possessing the two withdrawals, not
> for the discontented or greatly desirous. Longing for more with respect to
> what has been obtained is discontent; desire for what has not been obtained
> is great desire.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.06.md`](./VAK_6.06.md). Next: 6.07, the opposed pair.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> For one thus applied to cultivation, how does cultivation come about? He says:
> for one who has the two withdrawals. That is: if one is withdrawn by body and
> mind — by putting association and unwholesome thought far off. For whom is that
> pair easy? For one of few wishes who is content. Not for the discontented and
> the greatly desirous. What is this discontent, and what is great desire?
>
> Longing for more toward what is obtained is discontent; desire for what is not
> obtained is great desire. The Abhidharmikas: discontent is wanting more of fine
> robes and the like already obtained; great desire is wanting those same things
> while unobtained.
>
> But that extra wanting is itself of what is *not* obtained, not of what is
> obtained. What then is the difference? This fits: discontent is vexation at
> what one has, because it is not excellent and not abundant. Great desire is
> wanting the excellent and abundant that one has not obtained.

## 5. Obtained ≠ the Difference

Wanting *more* of what one has is still wanting what one does not have. The
verse-pair stands; the prose will not let obtained/unobtained carry it alone.
These are conditions of cultivation, not the Path of Seeing of 6.01.

## 6. The Repair

| | inherited | Vasubandhu |
|---|---|---|
| asaṃtuṣṭi | more of what is already got | vexation that what is got is poor or scant |
| mahecchatā | wanting what is not got | wanting the excellent and abundant not got |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- two withdrawals = kāya and citta;
- positive pair supplied: alpeccha, saṃtuṣṭa;
- second half kept, then rewritten by relation, not by inventory.
