# VAK 6.68 — Ten Dharmas beneath Thirty-Seven Functions

## 1. Sanskrit Devanāgarī

श्रद्धा वीर्यं स्मृतिः प्रज्ञा समाधिः प्रीत्युपेक्षणे ।
प्रश्रब्धिशीलसंकल्पाः प्रज्ञा हि स्मृत्युपस्थितिः ॥ ६.६८ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit IAST

śraddhā vīryaṃ smṛtiḥ prajñā samādhiḥ prītyupekṣaṇe |
praśrabdhiśīlasaṃkalpāḥ prajñā hi smṛtyupasthitiḥ || 6.68 ||

VAK 6.67 announced that the thirty-seven Bodhi-aligned functions reduce to ten underlying dharmas. This verse enumerates the ten and begins demonstrating the reduction through the first canonical group.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

**Padaccheda**

| Form | Analysis | Contextual force |
|---|---|---|
| **śraddhā** | feminine nominative singular | confidence or lucid trust |
| **vīryam** | neuter nominative singular | energy, exertion |
| **smṛtiḥ** | feminine nominative singular | recollection, mindful retention |
| **prajñā** | feminine nominative singular | discriminative understanding |
| **samādhiḥ** | masculine nominative singular | collectedness |
| **prīti** | feminine compound member | joy or rapture |
| **upekṣaṇe** | feminine nominative dual | equanimous observation; dual by coordination with *prīti* |
| **praśrabdhi** | feminine compound member | calm pliancy |
| **śīla** | neuter compound member | ethical discipline |

| **smṛtyupasthitiḥ** | feminine nominative singular used collectively | establishment of recollection; the group of four |

The mixed genders and numbers are coordinated as a ten-member nominal list. The second sentence explains the first reduction rather than adding an eleventh item.

## 4. Grammar

The first three pādas form an asyndetic enumeration whose understood predicate is “are the ten dravyas.” Sandhi and compounding compress the latter members, but the semantic count is:

```text
1 śraddhā       6 prīti
2 vīrya         7 upekṣā
3 smṛti         8 praśrabdhi
4 prajñā        9 śīla
5 samādhi      10 saṃkalpa
```

In **prajñā hi smṛtyupasthitiḥ**, both nouns are nominative. The construction identifies the principal operative nature of the establishments with prajñā. The singular **smṛtyupasthitiḥ** functions generically for the four establishments.

The particle **hi** marks an explanation: the four establishments do not add four new dravyas because they are counted principally as prajñā.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Confidence, energy, recollection, prajñā, collectedness, joy, equanimity, pliancy, ethical discipline, and resolve are [the ten]. For the establishment of recollection is prajñā.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

The thirty-seven Bodhi-aligned functions are constituted principally by ten operative dharmas: confidence, energy, recollection, discriminative understanding, collectedness, joy, equanimity, calm pliancy, ethical discipline, and resolve. The four establishments of recollection are counted under prajñā because discriminative examination is their predominant operative nature, although recollection and other associated qualities remain present.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The Path’s thirty-sevenfold articulation does not require thirty-seven independent realities. Ten capacities recur through different functional positions. A practice may therefore be named from one aspect of its organization while another dharma supplies its governing operation.

The establishments retain their objects through recollection, but their defining Path-work is discriminative examination. Functional name, predominant operation, and associated conditions form one complex without becoming interchangeable.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**The ten operative dharmas**

```text
śraddhā
    confidence

vīrya
    energetic exertion

smṛti
    recollective retention

prajñā
    discriminative understanding

    collectedness

    equanimity

praśrabdhi
    calm pliancy

śīla
    ethical discipline

```

These are not ten factors added beside the thirty-seven. They are the recurring dharma-realities by which the thirty-seven positions are principally constituted.

**The reduction map**

The Bhāṣya unfolds the main recurrences:

| Underlying dharma | Bodhipakṣya functions principally counted under it |
|---|---|
| **prajñā** | four smṛtyupasthānas; investigation-of-dharmas awakening-factor; right view; wisdom faculty and power |
| **vīrya** | four right abandonments; energy awakening-factor; right effort; energy faculty and power |
| **samādhi** | four bases of spiritual power; collectedness awakening-factor; right collectedness; collectedness faculty and power |
| **smṛti** | recollection awakening-factor; right recollection; recollection faculty and power |
| **śraddhā** | confidence faculty and power |
| **prīti** | joy awakening-factor |
| **praśrabdhi** | pliancy awakening-factor |
| **upekṣā** | equanimity awakening-factor |
| **saṃkalpa** | right resolve |
| **śīla** | the ethical Path-limbs |

The faculties and powers are grasped directly according to their names (**nāmagrāhikayā**). The more instructive cases are those in which the name of a practice-group differs from its principal dharma.

**Why smṛtyupasthāna is counted as prajñā**

The sentence must not be flattened into:

```text
smṛti = prajñā
```

The technical relation is:

```text
smṛti
    retains and sustains the object

prajñā
    examines and discriminates the object

smṛtyupasthāna
    named through the establishment of recollection
    principally constituted by discriminative examination
```

The Bhāṣya states that the establishments, right abandonments, and bases of spiritual power respectively have the natures (**svabhāva**) of prajñā, vīrya, and samādhi. VAK 6.69 immediately qualifies this as designation through the predominant factor.

**Principal dharma is not the whole practice**

The reduction counts the leading operation:

```text
named practice-complex
    contains principal dharma
    + associated wholesome dharmas
```

Consequently, identifying the principal dharma neither eliminates the companions nor converts the whole practice into an isolated mental atom.

**The Vaibhāṣika count of eleven**

The Bhāṣya records a school qualification:

```text
Kārikā count
    śīla counted as one
    total = ten

Vaibhāṣika count
    bodily ethical action distinct from verbal ethical action
    ethical limbs = two dravyas
    total = eleven
```

Their reason is the non-merging (**asaṃbhinnatva**) of bodily and verbal karma. The dispute shows that *dravya* is an analytical criterion of operative differentiation, not a self-evident metaphysical substance.

**Nāman and dravya revisited**

```text
nāman
    identifies a function in the canonical Path-system

dravya
    identifies the recurrent operative dharma
    principally realizing that function
```

Neither side can replace the other. Dravya without function would not explain the thirty-seven; functional names without operative identity would not explain their systematic recurrence.

## 8. Logical Determination

The reduction has two forms:

```text
RepeatedFunctionNames
    mapTo SameUnderlyingDharma

PracticeComplex
    isClassifiedBy PrincipalDharma
```

For the first example:

```text
Smṛtyupasthāna
    hasPrincipalDharma Prajñā
    hasSupportingDharma Smṛti
```

Therefore:

```text
hasPrincipalDharma(x, y)
    does not entail
x is identical to y in every respect
```

The thirty-seven-to-ten relation is many-to-fewer:

```text
37 PathFunctions
    → 10 PrincipalOperativeDharmas
```

But the Vaibhāṣika alternative demonstrates that the codomain depends on a justified criterion of individuation:

```text
merge bodily and verbal ethical operation
    → 10

distinguish them as separate dravyas
    → 11
```

Thus even the underlying unit must be rationally determined.

## 9. Interpretive Note

Here the NLP principle “only Meaning emerges in a System” receives an exact Kośa model. None of the ten dharmas carries the complete practical meaning of its factor in isolation. Meaning arises when an operative capacity occupies a specified role within Bodhi’s Path-organization.

```text
prajñā in isolation
    operative capacity

prajñā as smṛtyupasthāna
    capacity organized around sustained examination
    of body, feeling, mind, and dharmas

prajñā as right view
    the same capacity under another Path-relation
```

The same underlying dharma is neither unchanged in practical meaning nor transformed into a numerically different substance. Its systematic position determines what it is doing.

The verse therefore gives a disciplined form of copular determination:

```text
smṛtyupasthāna “is” prajñā

means

prajñā is its predominant operative reality
while smṛtyupasthāna determines
the relational form of that operation
```

This reciprocal formulation is an Organon reconstruction. The textual basis is the Kārikā’s identification and the Bhāṣya’s analysis by *svabhāva*; the following verse supplies the crucial limiting term *pradhānagrahaṇa*, designation by taking the predominant factor.
