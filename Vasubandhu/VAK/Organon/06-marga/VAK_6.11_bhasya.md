# VAK_6.11 Bhāṣya — Attention Past the Support; Aśubha Classified

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> atikrānta-manaskāro bhrūmadhye citta-dhāraṇāt |
> alobho daśabhūḥ kāma-dṛśyālambā nṛjāśubhā || 6.11 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> Through sustaining citta between the eyebrows, one is [called] a practitioner
> whose attention has passed beyond. The unattractive [cultivation] is
> non-greed, belongs to ten grounds, has desire-domain visible form as its
> object-support, and is generated among humans.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.11.md`](./VAK_6.11.md). Next: 6.12, ānāpānasmṛti.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Through sustaining citta between the eyebrows, one is the practitioner whose
> attention has passed beyond. Having let go even half the skull, he holds citta
> between the brows. This, they say, is the yogācāra whose attention has passed
> beyond *with respect to the unattractive*.
>
> The unattractive admits a fourfold: limited by smallness of support, or not;
> limited by lack of mastery, or not. The cases turn on mastered or unmastered
> attention, and on a support of one’s own body or as far as the ocean.
>
> What is its own-nature? On how many grounds? What support? Where does it arise?
> In order: own-nature non-greed; ten grounds (the four dhyānas with their
> neighbors, and the desire-domain); support is visible form of the desire-domain.
> It arises only among humans, in no other destiny, and not in Uttarakuru.
>
> Because it is resolutive attention, it is contaminated. It may be gained by
> detachment or produced by application, according as one is already used to it
> or not. The mark of the unattractive, with its divisions, has been stated.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
drop last half-skull → hold citta at the brows
name scoped: aśubhāyām, not “past citta”
extent ≠ mastery (four cases)
four questions → alobha / ten grounds / kāma-visible / human
extra: sāsrava by adhimukti-manaskāra
```

## 5. Past the Image ≠ Uncontaminated

Wholesome, alobha, mastered — still sāsrava. 6.09’s bar is not lifted. The
stage-name does not empty citta.

## 6. The Type

| axis | determination |
|---|---|
| svabhāva | alobha |
| grounds | ten |
| ālambana | kāma-domain visible form |
| production | human, not Uttarakuru |
| status | sāsrava |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `atikrānta-manaskāra` scoped to aśubha;
- verse answers four questions in order;
- contamination and two provenances are prose-only.
