# VAK_6.49 Bhāṣya — Mundane Path: Peace Above, Coarse Below

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vimuktyānantaryapathā laukikās tu yathākramam |
> śāntādyudārādyākārā uttarādharagocarāḥ || 6.49 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> But the mundane liberation-paths and uninterrupted paths have, respectively,
> aspects beginning with peacefulness and aspects beginning with coarseness, and
> the higher and lower [grounds] as their object-fields.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.49.md`](./VAK_6.49.md). Next: 6.50.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Mundane liberation Paths take aspects beginning with peace; uninterrupted Paths,
> coarseness. Respectively: higher ground, lower ground.
>
> Liberation Path regards the higher as peaceful, excellent, an exit — as it applies.
> Uninterrupted Path regards the lower as coarse, defective, a thick wall. Coarse:
> not peaceful, more constructing work. Defective: not excellent, heavier rigidity,
> adverse. Wall: that ground is no exit from itself, as a wall is no door. Reverse
> those three and you have peace, excellence, exit. The side-matter is finished.
