# VAK_6.03 Bhāṣya — Three Modes of Suffering; All Contaminated Formations

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> duḥkhās triduḥkhatāyogād yathāyogam aśeṣataḥ |
> manāpā amanāpāś ca tadanye caiva sāsravāḥ || 6.03 ||

> They are suffering by connection with the three modes of suffering, as
> applicable, without remainder: the agreeable, the disagreeable, and the other
> contaminated [formations].

Two truths (saṃvṛti / paramārtha): 6.04.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.03.md`](./VAK_6.03.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> duḥkhaduḥkhatā / vipariṇāmaduḥkhatā / saṃskāraduḥkhatā /

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> If only some feeling is painful by nature, how are *all* contaminated
> formations called suffering? By the three modes, as each applies, and without
> remainder.
>
> Disagreeable: suffering as pain. Agreeable: suffering through transformation.
> The rest: suffering through conditioned formation. Deeper: formation-suffering
> runs through the contaminated field, not only the neutral remainder.
>
> Suffering is what is adverse. The path is conditioned, yet not this first
> truth — it works toward exhaustion of all suffering, so it is not adverse to
> the noble.
>
> Pleasant feeling is pleasant by its own characteristic. Seeing it as duḥkha
> is another aspect: impermanent, hard to hold, transformable, able to feed
> attachment. That seeing conduces to dispassion, not to a lie that pleasure
> never occurred.

## 5. Conditioned ≠ Duḥkha-satya

`Yathāyogam` ≠ every mode on every item the same way. `Aśeṣataḥ` ≠ phenomenal
pain everywhere. Pleasure under duḥkha ≠ pleasure was fake.

## 6. Modes

| | distinctive mode | depth |
|---|---|---|
| amanāpa | duḥkha-duḥkhatā | saṃskāra-duḥkhatā also |
| manāpa | vipariṇāma-duḥkhatā | same |
| tadanya | saṃskāra-duḥkhatā | same |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- subject = sāsrava saṃskāras;
- three modes distributed, then deepened;
- path kept off this truth.
