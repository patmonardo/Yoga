# VAK_6.37 Bhāṣya — Five Non-Returners; Mixed Dhyāna to Akaniṣṭha

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> so 'ntarotpanna-saṃskārāsaṃskāra-parinirvṛtiḥ |
> ūrdhvasrotāś ca sa dhyāne vyavakīrṇo 'kaniṣṭhagaḥ || 6.37 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> That one attains final nirvāṇa in the intermediate state, after arising, with
> exertion, or without exertion; and [that one may be] an upward-streamer. When
> intermingled with respect to dhyāna, that one goes to Akaniṣṭha.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.37.md`](./VAK_6.37.md). Next: 6.38, leap types.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> That same non-returner is fivefold. Intermediate-completer: final nirvāṇa in the
> interval. After-arising: soon after rebirth, because the carrying Path is strongly
> engaged — with remainder; others say without remainder; rejected: no mastery over
> giving up the lifespan.
>
> With-exertion: application has not eased; completion through effort. Without-
> exertion: no such renewed effort. Others explain by Path taking conditioned vs
> unconditioned objects; no — over-stretch. Sūtra lists the effortless first; that
> fits: without effort vs with effort. Soon-after-rebirth has a stronger carrying
> Path and milder residual tendencies.
>
> Upward-streamer: course goes higher; not finished in the first rebirth-place.
> Stream and course are one sense here. Mixed as to dhyāna, goes to Akaniṣṭha.
> Twofold by cause (mixed / unmixed dhyāna) and by result (Akaniṣṭha or summit of
> existence as furthest). Whose dhyāna is mixed goes to Akaniṣṭha and finishes there.
