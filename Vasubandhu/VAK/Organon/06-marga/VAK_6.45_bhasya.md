# VAK_6.45 Bhāṣya — Knowledge of Exhaustion; the Arhat

6.35 opened the fruit-stretch. 6.44 opened aśaikṣa as candidate. This verse gives her the fruit.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tatkṣayāptyā kṣayajñānam aśaikṣo 'rhann asau tadā |
> lokottareṇa vairāgyaṃ bhavāgrād anyato dvidhā || 6.45 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> With attainment of its destruction, knowledge of exhaustion [arises]; that one is
> then beyond training, an arhat. Dispassion from the summit of existence is by the
> supramundane [Path]; from elsewhere it is twofold.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.45.md`](./VAK_6.45.md). Next: 6.46.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> The ninth summit-grade, abandoned by the diamond: with attainment of that
> destruction, knowledge of exhaustion arises — last liberation Path, right after
> diamond concentration — because it is born together with attainment of the
> exhaustion of all influxes.
>
> Then that candidate is beyond training and an arhat: the fruit is got. Beyond
> training because nothing remains to train toward another fruit. Arhat because the
> work required is done, and she is worthy of honour even from those still attached.
> The other seven are trainees: they keep training for influx-exhaustion in higher
> discipline, higher mind, higher wisdom. An ordinary person practicing those is
> not thereby a trainee — the Truths are not known as they are, and contrary training
> is still possible. Nirvāṇa itself is neither trainee nor beyond-training dharma.
>
> Eight Noble names; five as distinct positions on the sequential course. Dispassion
> from the summit: only the supramundane Path. No mundane ground above it; its own
> ground can still host the latent of its own affliction, so cannot finally cut it.
> From every other ground: mundane or supramundane.

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- destruction, attainment, knowledge, status are not one mush;
- aśaikṣa = no further *fruit* to train for, not “no mind”;
- Nirvāṇa is not a person-class.
