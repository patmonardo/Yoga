# Indriyanirdeśa Bhāṣya

Complete Chapter 2 reading edition: 73 Bhāṣya studies, VAK 2.01–2.73,
including the chapter colophon. The studies remain provisional where
textual questions or review qualifications are recorded.

Canonical editable sources are `Organon/02-indriya/VAK_2.01_bhasya.md`
through `VAK_2.73_bhasya.md`. The PDF preserves the full Sanskrit,
continuous conventional translations, and focused studies in sequence.
Commentary-unit boundaries sometimes cross kārikā boundaries as explained
in the individual studies. Kārikā-only studies are not build inputs.

Run `./build.sh` from this directory. Requires Pandoc, XeLaTeX,
and the FreeSerif, Lato, and FreeMono fonts.

Output: `Organon-Kosa-Indriya-Bhasya-Nirdesa.pdf`
