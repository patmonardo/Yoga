# VAK_8.04 Bhāṣya — Formless Names: Preparatory Practice and Attenuated Perception

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ākāśānantya-vijñānānantyākiṃcanya-saṃjñakāḥ |
> tathāprayogān māndyāt tu naivasaṃjñānāsaṃjñakaḥ || 8.04 ||

> The first three are designated infinity of space, infinity of consciousness,
> and nothingness because of application in those ways. The fourth, however, is
> designated neither-perception-nor-non-perception because of attenuation.

Eight fundamental attainments by savoring, purity, influx-free: 8.05.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.04.md`](./VAK_8.04.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Are space and the rest their objects alone, that they are so named? No. First
> the three: because of application in those ways. They apply themselves by
> attending respectively: “space is infinite,” “consciousness is infinite,”
> “there is nothing.” Hence these names.
>
> The fourth is called neither-perception-nor-non-perception because perception
> is mild. It is not sharp perception, and it is not that there is no perception
> at all. Even though they also apply themselves there by regarding perception
> as disease, swelling, thorn, non-perception as delusion, and this sphere as
> peaceful and excellent — why do they take it so? Precisely this has been said:
> because of the mildness of perception.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
name ≠ exclusive object
first three: respective manasikāra
fourth: saṃjñā weak, still there
evaluative preparation admitted; naming-reason is attenuation
```

## 5. Name ≠ Inventory

`Vijñāna` here is consciousness, not Chapter-7 jñāna and not omniscience.
“There is nothing” ≠ no dharmas (four aggregates still). Fourth ≠ nirodha-
samāpatti. Preparation exists for the fourth too; it is not the naming ground.

## 6. Two Naming Grounds

| | why so called |
|---|---|
| space / consciousness / nothingness | corresponding application |
| neither-perception-nor-non-perception | saṃjñā attenuated |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- opening `eva` rejected;
- `tathāprayogāt` governs the three;
- `māndyāt` = mildness of saṃjñā.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Unbounded words become determinations only inside the series. Vidyā: the name
records the mode of approach, or the state of a remaining factor. Technē of
attention is given as formula, not as a protocol we will write. Appearance would
read “nothing” as ontology. Leave project “knowledge” for vijñāna out of the
close translation.
