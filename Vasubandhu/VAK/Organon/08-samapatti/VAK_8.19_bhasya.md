# VAK_8.19 Bhāṣya — Completed Skipping Attainment and Its Supports

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vyutkrāntakasamāpattir visabhāgatṛtīyagā |
> svādhobhūmyāśrayā eva dhyānārūpyāḥ vṛthādharam || 8.19 ||

> Skipping attainment goes to the third in a dissimilar mode. The dhyānas and
> formless attainments have supports only at their own or lower levels. The
> lower is without use.

Exception to the support rule: 8.20.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.19.md`](./VAK_8.19.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> yadā tu prathamāt sāsravāt tṛtīyam anāsravaṃ dhyānaṃ samāpadyate tasmāt sāsravam ākāśānantyāyatanaṃ tasmād anāsravam ākiñcanyāyatanam / evaṃ punaḥ pratilomam /
> tadā visabhāgatṛtīyadravyagamanād abhiniṣpannā bhavati /
> ativiprakṛṣṭatvān na caturthī samāpadyate /
> tāṃ ca triṣu dvīpeṣv asamayavimukta evārhann utpādayati / niḥkleśatvāt samādhivaśitvāc ca /
> kenāśrayeṇa kati dhyānārūpyāḥ saṃmukhīkriyante / svādhobhūmyāśrayā eva /
> kiṃ kāraṇam ūrdhvopapanno nādharāṃ samāpattiṃ saṃmukhīkaroti / nahi tasyādhareṇa … kiñcit prayojanam / nīhīnatvāt /

**Witness.** Pradhan 446|06–21. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> When from contaminated first dhyāna one enters uncontaminated third, from that
> contaminated infinite space, from that uncontaminated nothingness — and likewise
> reverse — then, by going to the third item in a dissimilar mode, it is
> accomplished. One does not enter the fourth [relative station] — too remote.
> In the three continents only the occasion-independent liberated arhat produces
> it: freedom from defilement *and* mastery of samādhi. View-attained: mastery,
> not freedom from kleśa. Occasion-dependent liberated: freedom, not that mastery.
>
> On what support are how many dhyānas and ārūpyas made present? Only own or
> lower ground. Why does one reborn above not make a lower attainment present?
> The lower has no relevant purpose for that one — it is inferior.

## 5. Three Distinctions

Rebirth-support ≠ previous sitting in the skip. Reverse traversal is practice,
not “being born above.” “Fourth” = fourth relative station, not a ban on fourth
dhyāna. Lower sensory Knowing of 8.13 ≠ entering a lower samāpatti.

## 6. Perfected Pattern

| | level | mode |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1st dhyāna | sāsrava |
| 2 | 3rd dhyāna | anāsrava |
| 3 | infinite space | sāsrava |
| 4 | nothingness | anāsrava |

Then reverse. Who: both qualifications, three continents.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `tṛtīya` counted from the predecessor;
- `visabhāga` = sāsrava/anāsrava, not kliṣṭa/kuśala;
- `vṛthā` scoped to ūrdhvopapanna.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Freedom here is command of the middle, then omission *and* mode-change. Vidyā:
support, traversal, purpose are three. Technē of the crown is not empty light —
it is Knowing that can skip a named step. Appearance would call the hop a blank.
The hop is full: two determinations move at once.
