# VAK_8.37 Bhāṣya — Acquisition, Habituation, and Support

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> nirodha uktaḥ vairāgyaprayogāptaṃ tu śeṣitam |
> tridhātvāśrayam ārūpyasaṃjñaṃ śeṣaṃ manuṣyajam || 8.37 ||

> Cessation has been explained. The remainder, however, is acquired through
> dispassion or application. What is designated formless has support in the three
> domains; the rest is human-born.

Causes of production in rūpa and ārūpya: 8.38.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.37.md`](./VAK_8.37.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> nirodhavimokṣaḥ pūrvam evoktaḥ sarvaiḥ prakāraiḥ /
> nirodhād anyāni vimokṣādīni vairāgyalābhikāni prāyogikāṇi ca / ucitānucitatvāt /
> ārūpyavimokṣā ārūpyakṛtsne ca traidhātukāśrayāṇi śeṣaṃ manuṣyāśrayam eva /
> upadeśasāmarthyenotpādanāt /

**Witness.** Pradhan 458|11–18. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Cessation-release was already taught in every mode. The other releases and the
> related practices are obtained through dispassion *or* through application —
> according as one is habituated or not.
>
> Formless releases and the two formless totalities have support in the three
> domains. The rest: human support only — produced by the efficacy of instruction.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
set nirodha aside
two routes for the rest, by habit
then two remainders: formless / not
```

## 5. Route ≠ Species

Support ≠ object ≠ attainment-level. Human production ≠ later possession after
every rebirth. Instruction here ≠ no teaching exists outside humans as a cosmic law.

## 6. Split

| | |
|---|---|
| acquisition (minus nirodha) | vairāgya if habituated; prayoga if not |
| three-domain support | four formless releases + two formless totalities |
| human production | first three releases, eight mastery-bases, first eight totalities |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `śeṣitam` excludes nirodha from the two-route rule;
- `ārūpyasaṃjñam` is the three-domain subject;
- second `śeṣam` is the human-production group.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

The agent of the House is formed. Vidyā: route, habit, support. Technē of
instruction is how the non-formless work is born in kāmadhātu. Appearance would
call mastery a gift of empty light. The text names habit and teaching. Yama does
not Mara that pedagogy. He still asks whether samādhi was raised to Pure Reason.
