# VAK_8.07 Bhāṣya — Five Factors of the First Dhyāna and Four of the Second

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> pañcādye tarkacārau ca prītisaukhyasamādhayaḥ |
> prītyādayaḥ prasādaś ca dvitīye 'ṅgacatuṣṭayam || 8.07 ||

> In the first are five: vitarka and vicāra, joy, ease, and concentration. In
> the second, the three beginning with joy and clarity form the set of four.

Third and fourth dhyāna factors: 8.08.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.07.md`](./VAK_8.07.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> eṣāṃ ca samāpattidravyāṇāṃ dhyānāny eva bhavanti nārūpyāḥ /
> vitarko vicāraḥ prītiḥ sukhaṃ cittaikāgratā cety etāni pañcāṅgāni /
> samādhiḥ kilāṅgaṃ dhyānaṃ ca / śeṣāṇy aṅgānīti /
> yathā caturaṅgā senā evaṃ pañcāṅgaṃ dhyānam /
> dvitīye dhyāne catvāry aṅgāni / adhyātmasaṃprasādaḥ prītiḥ sukhaṃ cittekāgratā ca /

**Witness.** Pradhan 437|13–438|03. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Of these attainments, it is the dhyānas that [have these factors], not the
> formless. In the first there are five limbs: vitarka, vicāra, joy, ease, and
> one-pointedness of mind. Samādhi, it is said, is a limb and also dhyāna; the
> rest are limbs. As an army is four-limbed, so this dhyāna is five-limbed.
>
> In the second dhyāna, four limbs: inward clarity, joy, ease, and one-pointedness
> of mind.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
aṅga-count is for dhyāna, not ārūpya
1st: five named
samādhi = both a counted limb and the whole's name
2nd: drop the pair; count prasāda
```

## 5. Counted Limb ≠ Every Associated Dharma

The opening sentence restricts *this* inventory. It does not unmake 8.05's
formless modes. Not counting prasāda in the first list ≠ proving that dharma
absent there. Prīti ≠ sukha. Lists ≠ a practice recipe.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `tarka-cāra` = vitarka, vicāra;
- `prītyādayaḥ` = the triad already named;
- `prasāda` = adhyātmasaṃprasāda.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Samādhi is Idea of collected Knowing *and* one counted determination in the
whole. Appearance would either dissolve the limbs into one glow or treat five
limbs as five dhyānas. Vidyā keeps defining nature and factor-constitution.
Technē of ascent is not mere deletion: a positive limb is selected.
