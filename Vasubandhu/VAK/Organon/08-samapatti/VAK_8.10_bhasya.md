# VAK_8.10 Bhāṣya — Factors Absent from Defiled Dhyānas

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kliṣṭeṣv asat prītisukhaṃ prasādaḥ saṃpradhīḥ smṛtiḥ |
> upekṣāsmṛtiśuddhiś ca kecit prasrabdhyupekṣaṇe || 8.10 ||

> In the defiled [dhyānas], rapture-and-pleasure, clarity, clear comprehension
> and mindfulness, and purification of equanimity and mindfulness are absent.
> Some [say] pliancy and equanimity [are absent].

Fourth dhyāna's immovability: 8.11.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.10.md`](./VAK_8.10.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> kiṃ punaḥ kliṣṭeṣv api dhyāneṣu yathāvihitāny aṅgāni bhavanti /
> prathame dhyāne vivekajaṃ prītisukhaṃ nāsti / kleśāviviktatvāt /
> dvitīye … 'dhyātmasaṃprasādo nāsti / kleśāvilatvāt /
> tṛtīye smṛtisaṃprajanyaṃ nāsti / kliṣṭasukhasaṃbhramitatvāt /
> caturthe upekṣāsmṛtipariśuddhir nāsti / kleśamalinatvāt /
> evaṃ tāvat kecid āhuḥ /
> kecit punaḥ prathamadvitīyayoḥ kliṣṭayoḥ prasrabdhir nāsti /
> tṛtīyacaturthayor upekṣā nāsti / kuśalamahābhūmikatvād anayoḥ /

**Witness.** Pradhan 441|01–11. Voices: question; first account (`kecid`); second
account (`kecit punaḥ`).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Do the prescribed limbs also occur in defiled dhyānas? What is not found in
> them?
>
> In the first: rapture and pleasure born of separation are absent — because
> there is no separation from defilements. In the second: inward clarity is
> absent — turbidity of defilements. In the third: mindfulness and clear
> comprehension are absent — bewilderment by defiled pleasure. In the fourth:
> purification of equanimity and mindfulness is absent — stain of defilements.
> Thus some say.
>
> Others: in the first two defiled, pliancy is absent; in the third and fourth,
> equanimity is absent — because those two belong to the wholesome great-ground
> class.

## 5. Defiled ≠ All Contaminated

`Vivekaja` and `pariśuddhi` are the denied items, not every joy and every
mindfulness. Third-dhyāna reason still mentions defiled pleasure — so pleasure
is not universally cancelled. Absence of purification ≠ proof that unpurified
upekṣā remains. Knowing is not emptied as a skandha; a named limb fails.

## 6. Two Accounts

| defiled | A absent | B absent |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | vivekaja prīti-sukha | prasrabdhi |
| 2nd | prasāda | prasrabdhi |
| 3rd | smṛti-saṃprajanya | upekṣā |
| 4th | upekṣā-smṛti-pariśuddhi | upekṣā |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- absences are distributed, not global;
- `saṃpradhīḥ smṛtiḥ` = smṛtisaṃprajanya;
- second pāda after `kecit` is another school.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Same level-name, different actuality of the limbs. Vidyā keeps kliṣṭa off the
śuddhaka / anāsrava axis of 8.05. Technē: a stain is a determination, not a
vague fog. Appearance would either deny defiled dhyāna or call it the real one
with the oil poured out. Neither.
