# VAK_8.13 Bhāṣya — First-Dhyāna Knowledge in Higher Dhyāna Rebirth

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāyākṣiśrotravijñānaṃ vijñaptyutthāpakaṃ ca yat |
> dvitīyādau tadādyāptam akliṣṭāvyākṛtaṃ ca tat || 8.13 ||

> Body-, eye-, and ear-knowledge, and that which initiates manifest action — in
> the second and subsequent [dhyānas], that belongs to the first, and it is
> undefiled and indeterminate.

Local close of the dhyāna stretch. Acquisition of attainments: 8.14.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.13.md`](./VAK_8.13.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> yadi dvitīyādiṣu … trayo vijñānakāyā na saṃvidyante … kathaṃ te paśyanti … /
> na tu svabhūmikāḥ /
> prathamadhyānabhūmikaṃ cakṣuḥśrotrakāyavijñānaṃ vijñaptisamutthāpakaṃ ca … saṃmukhīkurvanti /
> nirmāṇacittavad yena te paśyanti yāvad vijñaptiṃ samutthāpayanti /
> anivṛtāvyākṛtaṃ ca tat … na kliṣṭaṃ vītarāgatvān na kuśalaṃ hīnatvāt /
> avasitaṃ dhyānakāryam /

**Witness.** Pradhan 442|07–18. Voices: objector; Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> If in the second and higher the three knowledge-bodies are not found, and
> vitarka and vicāra are not, how do they see, even to touching, and how do they
> initiate manifestation? Eye-knowledge and the rest are not absent for those
> reborn there. They are just not of their own level.
>
> They bring into operation eye-, ear-, and body-knowledge of the first-dhyāna
> level, and the knowledge that initiates manifestation — as with a mind for
> magical creation. That knowledge is unobstructed-neutral: not defiled, because
> of freedom from passion [for that lower level]; not wholesome, because it is
> inferior. The work on dhyāna is concluded.

## 5. Level of Being ≠ Level of Operation

Rebirth question, not a sitting that stays fourth while the eyes work. Neutral
here ≠ upekṣā-feeling ≠ anāsrava. Nirmāṇacitta is comparison, not “all seeing
is magic.” No module-stack.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `ādya` = first-dhyāna level;
- `dvitīyādau` = those reborn there;
- two reasons for the ethical cell.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Knowing is not emptied at altitude. A higher rebirth still *uses* first-level
Knowing without making that Knowing a native limb of the higher ground. Vidyā:
belongs-to ≠ brought-to-bear. Technē of seeing is first-dhyāna work done from a
higher seat. Appearance would either blind the gods or promote the eye to the
fourth.
