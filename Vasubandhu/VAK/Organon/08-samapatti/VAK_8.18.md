## 4. Grammar

**Four numerals correspond to four predecessors.**
The understood arising verb is supplied by the prose.
The order is decline-, stability-, distinction-, and
penetration-conducive. The counts concern which of
these four pure types immediately follow each one.

**The quantifier's domain is explicit.** The introductory
question says `eṣāṃ caturṇām`, of these four. “Only
one” after nirvedhabhāgīya therefore means only one
of the four pure types, not only one possible
successor across every mode of attainment.

**Two directions and two modes are separate.**
Gatvā and āgamya are glossed as forward and reverse
entry. Dvidhā is glossed as contaminated and uncontaminated.
It does not merely repeat “forward and backward.”

**Eight is qualified by mode availability.** The verse
names the eight principal levels; the prose specifies
eight contaminated and seven uncontaminated attainments.
It does not assign an uncontaminated attainment to
the summit of existence.

**The absolutives introduce preparation.** The second
half does not supply a self-contained finite conclusion.
Its construction continues into the next verse.
The commentary calls the procedure explained here
prayoga, preparation for the perfected skipping attainment.

**Close construe.** After the four pure types beginning
with the decline-conducive, respectively two, three,
three, and one of those types arise. Having entered
and returned through the levels in two modes, first
contiguously and then skipping one intervening level,
[one completes the preparation described in the prose].

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> Immediately after the decline-conducive and subsequent
> [types], two, three, three, and one [arise].
> Having gone and returned through the eight levels
> in two modes, contiguously and with one [level]
> skipped …

The ellipsis marks the verse-boundary continuation;
it does not indicate missing witness text.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Among the four pure types, the decline-conducive
> is immediately followed by either itself or the
> stability-conducive. The stability-conducive permits
> three, excluding the penetration-conducive. The distinction-conducive
> permits three, excluding the decline-conducive. After
> the penetration-conducive, only that same pure type follows.
>
> For the preparation in skipping attainments, one
> first masters forward and reverse entry through
> eight contaminated levels and seven uncontaminated
> levels in sequence. Thereafter one practices skipping
> one intervening level, forward and in reverse,
> in the contaminated series and likewise in the
> uncontaminated series. This is the preparation;
> the perfected form is determined next.

Bhāṣya study and running source. The source quotes
the opening of VAK 8.19 within this span. The
present study stops at the explicit conclusion identifying
this procedure as preparation.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> A subtype's orientation does not by itself enumerate
> its immediate successors. The commentary specifies
> their membership as well as their number. It
> then turns to a different question: preparation
> for moving through the ordered attainment levels,
> where mastery of contiguous forward and reverse
> entry precedes practice in skipping a level.

These are two related analyses with different objects:
the first concerns pure subtypes, the second the
ordered dhyāna and formless levels under two modes.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Hānabhāgīya / sthitibhāgīya / viśeṣabhāgīya /
nirvedhabhāgīya`.** The four pure types defined in
VAK 8.17. Here they serve as both predecessors
and the restricted set of successors being counted.

**`Anuloma / pratiloma`.** Forward / reverse order.
The prose uses these to explain going and returning
through the attainment sequence.

**`Śliṣṭa`.** Contiguous. The commentary gives anukrameṇa,
in sequence, as its explanation.

**`Ekalaṅghita`.** With one intervening level skipped.
A movement from first to third dhyāna skips the
second. It is a displacement of two level-steps,
consistent with the range clarified in VAK 8.15.

**`Sāsrava / anāsrava`.** Contaminated / uncontaminated.
Contaminated should not be replaced with defiled:
the two categories are not coextensive. The preparation
is organized by these two modes with their different
numbers of available levels.

**`Prayoga`.** Preparation or preparatory practice.
The concluding prose expressly distinguishes the work
described here from the accomplished form whose explanation
continues in VAK 8.19.

## 8. Logical Determination

**The successor sets, not just their sizes.**

| Pure predecessor | Permitted successors among the four pure types | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Decline-conducive | Decline, stability | 2 |
| Stability-conducive | Decline, stability, distinction | 3 |
| Distinction-conducive | Stability, distinction, penetration | 3 |
| Penetration-conducive | Penetration | 1 |

The relation is directed. Distinction can be followed
by penetration, while penetration cannot be followed
immediately by distinction within this pure-type inventory.
A bidirectional chain joining all four would be inaccurate.

**The singleton does not cancel uncontaminated arising.**
VAK 8.17 explicitly identifies penetration-conducive
pure attainment as the condition from which uncontaminated
attainment arises. The present singleton says which
pure type can follow. Its restricted domain preserves
both statements without contradiction.

**The training field has unequal mode counts.**

| Mode | Available principal levels | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Contaminated | Four dhyānas and four formless attainments | 8 |
| Uncontaminated | Four dhyānas and the first three formless attainments | 7 |

Thus “eight levels in two modes” must not be
expanded into sixteen available level-mode combinations.
The summit contributes only to the contaminated series.

**The stated preparation order.** First master sequential
forward and reverse entry in the contaminated and
uncontaminated series. Then practice one-level skipping
in forward and reverse order in the contaminated
series, and likewise in the uncontaminated series.
The source does not present adjacent entry and skipping
as interchangeable starting exercises.

**The explicit contaminated example.**

```text
First dhyāna
    → Third dhyāna
    → Infinite space
    → Nothingness
```

The intervening levels skipped are second dhyāna,
fourth dhyāna, and infinite knowledge. The prose
then directs reverse mastery and corresponding practice
in the uncontaminated series. This example illustrates
the skip pattern; it does not undo the preceding
requirement to master all eight contaminated levels
in sequential entry.

**Preparation remains distinct from completion.** The
following passage describes a sequence changing between
contaminated and uncontaminated modes while skipping.
That perfected form and the qualifications of its
practitioner belong to VAK 8.19. The present two-mode
preparation should not already be labeled the completed
alternating-mode accomplishment.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**The verse crosses between two questions.** Its first
half completes the pure-type successor inventory. Its
second half begins the preparation question and continues
syntactically into the next verse. Preserving both
the verse and argument boundaries requires marking
that continuation rather than inventing a final clause.

**Witness cautions.** The running source separates
`hāna bhāgādyanantaram` and has the damaged
`ākṃicanyāvatanam` in the skipping example. The local
extract supplies the recognizable nothingness-sphere
reading. The research kārikā also reports a spacing
problem in śliṣṭaikalaṅghitāḥ, although the running
witness inspected here prints that compound continuously.
The official verse follows the local study; originals
remain unchanged.

**Organon reconstruction — Yoga Vidyā and Yoga Technē.**
Yoga Vidyā articulates the exact successor sets, their
restricted domain, and the ordered levels available
under each mode. Yoga Technē, its kriyā–karma companion,
concerns the prescribed exercise: sequential mastery
followed by controlled skipping. The preparation's
order matters independently of the arithmetic of
the pure-type successor sets.

For Organon representation, distinguish a relation
among subtypes from a traversal of levels. Preserve
direction, self-successors, unavailable mode-level combinations,
and the difference between preparation and completion.
This is project reconstruction, not an additional
formal system attributed to the Bhāṣya.
