# VAK_8.03 Bhāṣya — Formless Naming, Absence of Form, and Its Renewed Arising

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vibhūtarūpasaṃjñākhyāḥ saha sāmantakais tribhiḥ |
> nārūpye rūpasadbhāvaḥ rūpotpattis tu cittataḥ || 8.03 ||

> They are designated as having form-perception passed away, together with three
> access attainments. There is no presence of form in the formless domain; the
> arising of form, however, is from citta.

Modes of attention that name the four: 8.04.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.03.md`](./VAK_8.03.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Access to infinite space does not receive the designation: its object is still
> the fourth dhyāna, so form-perception has not gone. The four-aggregate claim
> must be established: there is no form in the formless.
>
> If form were there, why call them formless? Because of slight form, like a
> slightly tawny thing? What slight form? Restraint alone cannot stand without
> elements. Canonical pairings of life and heat, name and form, consciousness
> and the rest cannot be read without scope. Scripture: escape from forms;
> beings who have passed beyond form-perception. The whole form-class is
> crossed, not the feeling-class. Dhyāna lists begin with form; formless lists
> begin with feeling. Therefore: no presence of form there.
>
> Then how, after form cut off for many kalpas, does form arise again for those
> who fall from that existence? From citta — citta conditioned by the cause whose
> maturation is that form, when functioning is obtained. That we do not see mind
> without form here does not settle the higher domains, any more than not seeing
> life without morsel-food here settles the form-realm.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
name: form-perception gone (minus first access)
"slight form" fails
scripture scoped, not cancelled
enumeration omits rūpa → absence now
arising later ≠ hidden form now
cittataḥ qualified by vipāka-cause
```

## 5. Now ≠ Later

Access ≠ principal attainment. Domain constitution ≠ a sitting meditator's body
vanishing. Later form ≠ the same old lump stored somewhere. `Cittataḥ` ≠ mind
makes matter at will.

## 6. Who Gets the Name

| state | form-perception gone |
|---|---|
| four principal ārūpyas | yes |
| access to infinite space | no (4th dhyāna still the object) |
| access to the other three | yes |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- three sāmantakas = latter three accesses;
- first half = present constitution;
- second half = later genesis.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Absence from the present constitution is not absence from lawful genesis. Vidyā
keeps those two questions apart. Technē of return is karmically qualified citta,
not a stash of subtle rūpa. Appearance would either smuggle form back in or deny
rebirth-body. Do not sell "Meaning = regenerate the file."
