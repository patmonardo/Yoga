# VAK_8.34 Bhāṣya — Release Objects and the Test of Mastery

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāmāptadṛśyaviṣayāḥ prathamāḥ ye tv arūpiṇaḥ |
> te 'nvayajñānapakṣordhvasvabhūduḥkhādigocarāḥ || 8.34 ||

> The first releases have desire-domain visible objects. Those that are formless
> have as their field the anvayajñāna side and suffering and the following items
> of higher and own levels.

Eight bases of mastery: 8.35. Anvayajñāna stays the ūrdhva-side Path-form — not
English “inference.”

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.34.md`](./VAK_8.34.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> For the first [three]: desire-domain visible form as object — unpleasant or
> pleasant as fits. Formless releases: suffering of own and higher grounds, its
> cause and cessation, and the whole path on the anvayajñāna side. Also name
> non-analytical cessation, and space for one of them.
>
> Why none in third dhyāna? No second-dhyāna-level attachment to appearance, and
> disturbance by pleasure. Hence one produces the beautiful release — to brighten
> a continuum sunken by the unattractive, or to test: are the first two finished?
> They are finished if, even attending under the beautiful, kleśa does not arise.
>
> Two reasons yogins produce releases and the rest: keep kleśa at a distance;
> mastery of attainment. Also to bring forth qualities such as non-conflict and
> noble supernormal power. Why the realization-formula only for the third and
> eighth? Prominence, and they sit at the end of domain and ground.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `prathamāḥ` = the three form-related;
- anvayajñāna-side binds the *path* object;
- `ādi` unpacked in prose.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

The test is the House at work: restore beauty, watch whether grab returns. Vidyā:
field-content vs governing relation. Technē of the beautiful sitting is not a
smile-vibe. Appearance would call mastery the unattractive held forever. The text
asks whether kleśa stays dead when the form is fair again. Yama still outside.
Paramārtha in kāmadhātu includes this check.
