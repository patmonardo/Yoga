# VAK_8.39 Bhāṣya — Transmission, Realization, and Their Bearers

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> saddharmo dvividhaḥ śāstur āgamādhigamātmakaḥ |
> dhātāras tasya vaktāraḥ pratipattāra eva ca || 8.39 ||

> The Teacher's true Dharma is twofold, consisting of transmission and realization.
> Its bearers are precisely the expounders and the practitioners.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> How long will this true Dharma remain — the one in which such varieties of
> dharmas are discerned? The Teacher's true Dharma is twofold: transmission and
> realization. Transmission: Sūtra, Vinaya, Abhidharma. Realization: the factors
> conducive to awakening.
>
> Bearers of transmission: those who retain and speak. Bearers of realization:
> those who practice. So long as these remain, the true Dharma is to be understood
> as remaining. They say a thousand years. Others: that for realization;
> transmission lasts longer.

## 5. Archive ≠ Realization

Twofold ≠ same lifespan. One person may both speak and practice. Reported years
are not a diagnosis of this age. “Empty transmission” is not the source's name.

## 6. Two Forms

| | content | bearer |
|---|---|---|
| āgama | Sūtra, Vinaya, Abhidharma | retain and expound |
| adhigama | bodhipakṣya | practice |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `ātmaka` = constitution of the two forms;
- `dhātāraḥ` unpacked as retainers + speakers vs practitioners;
- duration reports stay attributed.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

The House has a spoken body and a practiced body. Vidyā is not the same act as
Technē, and neither replaces adhigama. Appearance would call the PDF the Dharma.
The text asks for living bearers. We do not Mara the saints who keep the books.
Yama still says: raise samādhi to Pure Reason or be refuted. That refutation is
not this verse. This verse names who carries the teaching.
