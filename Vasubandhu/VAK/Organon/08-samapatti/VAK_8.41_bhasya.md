# VAK_8.41 Bhāṣya — Witnesses and the Disordering of the Teaching

> adṛṣṭatattvair niravagrahaiḥ kṛtaṃ kutārkikaiḥ śāsanam etad ākulam || 8.41 ||

> With the Teacher, the Eye of the World, closed, and the body of witnesses

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> When the Teacher — the Eye of the World — had closed, and when the body of
> witnesses had for the most part gone to extinction, this teaching was made
> disordered by unchecked bad reasoners who have not seen reality.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

He just finished the most analytical chapter in the book. Then he names *bad*
reasoners — not Vidyā. Appearance would use this line to kill thinking. We do not
Mara the saints, and we do not Mara tarka. Yama's line still stands outside:
raise samādhi to Pure Reason or be refuted. That is not this verse. This verse
is a lament when the Eye is shut and most witnesses are gone.
