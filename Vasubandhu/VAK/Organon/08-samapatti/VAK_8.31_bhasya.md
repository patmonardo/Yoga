# VAK_8.31 Bhāṣya — Immeasurable Levels, Training, and Limits of Counteraction

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dhyānayor muditā anyāni ṣaṭsu kecit tu pañcasu |
> na taiḥ prahāṇaṃ nṛṣv eva janyante tryanvito dhruvam || 8.31 ||

> Sympathetic joy is in the two dhyānas; the others on six levels, though some
> say five. Abandonment is not effected by them. They are generated only among
> humans. [The attainer] is certainly endowed with three.

Eight liberations: 8.32.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Sympathetic joy: first and second dhyāna — it is gladness. The other three: six
> grounds — anāgamya, intermediate dhyāna, four dhyānas. Some drop anāgamya and
> say five. Others say ten, bringing in desire-domain and access, collected and
> uncollected, principal and preparatory.
>
> Do they abandon kleśa? Not by them. Principal-dhyāna level, resolutive attention,
> beings as object. By their *preparation* ill will and the rest are suppressed.
> Having suppressed them, one abandons by the paths of abandonment. After gaining
> the principal immeasurables in the dispassionate condition, one is not overcome
> even when strong conditions appear.
>
> Beginner: resolve the happiness one knows in oneself or hears of in Buddhas and
> noble disciples onto beings. If kleśa is too strong: liked friends (strong,
> middling, mild), then neutrals, then enemies (mild, middling, strong). When the
> wish holds as toward the closest friend, expand village, region, direction, world.
> One who takes qualities produces equal friendliness quickly. Compassion and joy
> analogously. Equanimity starts from neutrals.
>
> Generated among humans only. One reborn in third or fourth dhyāna lacks muditā.
> The attainer is always endowed with the three.

## 5. Counterforce ≠ Abandoning Path

Viṣkambhaṇa ≠ prahāṇa. Three possessed ≠ three sit at once. Graded fallback ≠
the only allowed start. Guṇagrāhin ≠ every person is the same.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `dhyānayoḥ` = first and second;
- `na taiḥ prahāṇam` kept strong;
- `tryanvitaḥ` = the attainer.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

The House is built, not presupposed. Vidyā: suppress, abandon, protect — three
works. Technē equalizes friend, neutral, enemy, then widens the field. Appearance
would call immeasurable an instant glow. It is trained relation in kāmadhātu.
Yama still does not Mara. He waits for Pure Reason. This verse is how one stands
in the House while that wait continues.
