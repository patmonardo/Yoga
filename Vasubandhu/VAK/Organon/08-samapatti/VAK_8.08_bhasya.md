# VAK_8.08 Bhāṣya — Third and Fourth Dhyāna Factors: Eighteen by Name

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tṛtīye pañca tūpekṣā smṛtiḥ prajñā sukhaṃ sthitiḥ |
> catvāry ante 'sukhāduḥkhopekṣāsmṛtisamādhayaḥ || 8.08 ||

> In the third are five: equanimity, mindfulness, discriminative knowing, ease,
> and stability. In the last are four: neither pleasure nor pain, equanimity,
> mindfulness, and concentration.

Count by distinct dharma: 8.09.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.08.md`](./VAK_8.08.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> tṛtīye tu dhyāne pañcāṅgāni / upekṣā smṛtiḥ saṃprajñānaṃ sukhaṃ samādhiś ca /
> samādhiparyāyo hi sthitiḥ / "samyaksamādhiḥ katamaḥ / yā cittasya sthitiḥ" /
> caturthaṃ dhyānam antyam / tatra catvāry aṅgāni /
> aduḥkhāsukhā vedanā upekṣāpariśuddhiḥ smṛtipariśuddhiḥ samādhiś ca /
> tāny etāny aṣṭādaśa dhyānāṅgāni bhavanti / nāmata evam /

**Witness.** Pradhan 438|04–12. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> In the third dhyāna, five limbs: equanimity, mindfulness, clear comprehension,
> ease, and samādhi. For stability is a synonym of samādhi — from the sūtra:
> what is right concentration? That stability of mind.
>
> The last is the fourth dhyāna. There, four limbs: feeling that is neither pain
> nor pleasure, purification of equanimity, purification of mindfulness, and
> samādhi. These are eighteen dhyāna-limbs — by name.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
3rd: five; prajñā = saṃprajñāna; sthiti = samādhi
4th: four; feeling remains (neutral); two factors purified
5+4+5+4 = 18 nāmataḥ
dravyataḥ postponed
```

## 5. Name-Count ≠ Kind-Count

Upekṣā ≠ the neutral feeling next to it. Neutral feeling ≠ no feeling.
Pariśuddhi ≠ anāsrava-mode. Not listing saṃprajñāna in the fourth ≠ no Knowing
there. Ante = fourth dhyāna, not bhavāgra.

## 6. Two Inventories

| 3rd | 4th |
|---|---|
| upekṣā | upekṣā-pariśuddhi |
| smṛti | smṛti-pariśuddhi |
| saṃprajñāna | — as named limb |
| sukha | aduḥkhāsukhā vedanā |
| samādhi (sthiti) | samādhi |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `prajñā` here = saṃprajñāna;
- `sthiti` = samādhi;
- fourth upekṣā/smṛti carry pariśuddhi.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

The fourth is not an emptied lamp. Feeling remains; mindfulness remains; they
are qualified. Vidyā: same name, different determination of the factor in the
whole. Technē of purification is not the mode-axis of 8.05. Appearance would
call eighteen names eighteen substances — that is 8.09's question, not this
verse's answer.
