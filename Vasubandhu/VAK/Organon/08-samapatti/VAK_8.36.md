# VAK_8.36 — Ten Totalities and Their Object Fields

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
दश कृत्स्नानि अलोभाष्टौ ध्यानेऽन्त्ये गोचरः पुनः ।
कामाः द्वे शुद्धकारूप्ये स्वचतुःस्कन्धगोचरे ॥ ८.३६ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
daśa kṛtsnāni alobhāṣṭau dhyāne 'ntye gocaraḥ punaḥ |
kāmāḥ dve śuddhākārūpye svacatuḥskandhagocare || 8.36 ||
```

The local scripts are retained. Their śuddha-compound
spellings differ: Devanāgarī gives śuddhakārūpye, while
the local IAST has śuddhākārūpye. The running
verse prints śuddhakārupye, and its explanatory prose
has śuddhakārūpyasvabhāve. The analysis below follows
the prose-supported śuddhaka-ārūpya without silently altering
the displayed witnesses.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

Alobhāḥ + aṣṭau gives alobhāṣṭau. Dhyāne + antye
is displayed with avagraha as dhyāne 'ntye.
Sva in the final compound is specified by
the prose's svabhūmika, “belonging to one's own level.”

## 4. Grammar

**The total is partitioned into eight and two.**
Daśa kṛtsnāni supplies an implicit “there are.”
Aṣṭau then selects the first eight. Dve
introduces the final two, with both following
dual expressions referring to that pair.

**Non-greed is a nature-predicate.** Alobhāḥ does
not merely say that practitioners ought to avoid
greed while undertaking the exercises. The Bhāṣya
explicitly supplies alobhasvabhāvāni: the first eight
have non-greed as their nature.

**The last dhyāna means the fourth exclusively.**
Dhyāne 'ntye qualifies the first eight. The
prose's caturtha eva dhyāne removes any ambiguity
about whether “last” might mean the final
formless attainment or a practitioner's latest session.

**Object and level are separate predicates.** Gocaraḥ
punaḥ kāmāḥ turns from the attainment's level
to its object. Fourth-dhyāna location and desire-domain
visible objects can therefore be asserted together.
Neither statement replaces the other.

**Own level is distributive across the final pair.**
Svacatuḥskandhagocare assigns each totality the four
aggregates of its respective level. It does
not place both totalities in one common level
or say that both take the same individual
mental event as their object.

**Close construe.** There are ten totalities. The
first eight have non-greed as nature and occur
in the last dhyāna; their object-field is
in the desire domain. The remaining two are
pure formless attainments, each with the four
aggregates of its own level as object-field.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> There are ten totalities. Eight are non-greed,
> in the last dhyāna; their object-field, again,
> is desire-domain objects. Two are pure formless
> attainments, having the four aggregates of their
> own level as their object-field.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> There are ten totality-spheres, so called because
> of uninterrupted complete pervasion: earth, water,
> fire, wind, blue, yellow, red, white, and
> the totalities of the spheres of infinite
> space and infinite knowledge.
>
> The first eight have non-greed as their
> nature and belong exclusively to the fourth
> dhyāna. Their object is desire-domain visible form.
> Some authorities, however, assign the tangible field
> as the object of the wind totality.
>
> The final two have the nature of pure
> formless attainments. Each takes the four aggregates
> belonging to its own level as object.

Source: Pradhan 457|13–458|10, checked against the local
kārikā and Bhāṣya studies and the running source.
The end of this span also discusses the
relation between the three practice groups and
the persons in whose continuities they can occur.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Totality concerns complete pervasion of a contemplative
> field. Its classification still distinguishes the nature
> of the practice, the level at which it
> occurs, and the objects it takes. Even
> this developed capacity can occur in an
> ordinary person's continuum; its accomplishment alone
> does not establish noble status.

This formulation draws on the explanatory prose.
“Totality” does not entail that all dharmas
are simultaneously known or that every object
falls within each totality's range.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Kṛtsna / kṛtsnāyatana`.** Totality / totality-sphere.
The prose explains the name by complete pervasion.
The selected determination and the completeness of
its extension must remain distinguishable.

**`Nirantarakṛtsnaspharaṇa`.** Uninterrupted complete pervasion.
This gives the local explanation of the name;
it is more precise than merely describing the
object as large or calling the practice universal.

**`Alobhasvabhāva`.** Having non-greed as nature.
“Non-appropriation” can develop the project's interpretation,
but non-greed retains the explicit technical predicate.

**`Rūpāyatana / spraṣṭavyāyatana`.** Visible-form field /
tangible field. The principal account gives the
former for the first eight; unnamed authorities
assign the latter to wind. Earth, water,
fire, and wind in this inventory should not
automatically be substituted by four tangible objects.

**`Śuddhakārūpya`.** Pure formless attainment. Śuddhaka
retains the chapter's technical pure classification;
it must not be silently translated as anāsrava,
uncontaminated. “Pure” here is not a new
claim that all ten totalities are noble paths.

**`Ākāśānantyāyatana / vijñānānantyāyatana`.** Sphere of
infinite space / sphere of infinite knowledge.
These identify the final two totalities. Vijñāna
is rendered “knowledge” according to the project's
standing vocabulary, without equating it with liberative
prajñā merely on that basis.

**`Svabhūmikāś catvāraḥ skandhāḥ`.** The four aggregates
of the respective level: feeling, recognition, formations,
and knowledge. The formless context excludes the
form aggregate from this fourfold range.

**`Prāveśika / uttarottaraviśiṣṭatva`.** Entry-related /
successive superiority. The first term links the
three practice groups; the second gives the
explicit reason for their graded relation.

**`Pṛthagjanāryasāṃtānika`.** Belonging to the continuities
of ordinary and noble persons. This is a
bearer classification, distinct from an attainment's
level, object, and technical mode.

## 8. Logical Determination

**The ten members and their assignments.**

| Members | Nature and level | Object assignment |
|---|---|---|
| Earth, water, fire, wind | Non-greed; fourth dhyāna only | Desire-domain visible form in the principal account |
| Blue, yellow, red, white | Non-greed; fourth dhyāna only | Desire-domain visible form |
| Infinite-space totality | Pure formless attainment of the infinite-space level | Four aggregates of its own level |
| Infinite-knowledge totality | Pure formless attainment of the infinite-knowledge level | Four aggregates of its own level |
| Wind, according to some | Alternative object account | Tangible field |

The final row records a view about one
member, not an eleventh totality. The attribution
ity eke, “so some,” prevents treating that
view as the unqualified principal assignment.

**Completeness has an object-relative sense.** The
name's explanation specifies pervasion. The tenfold
list still differentiates earth from blue and
the form-related totalities from the formless pair.
Complete pervasion does not erase these distinctions.

**Attainment-level is not object-level.** The first
eight belong to fourth dhyāna while taking
desire-domain visible objects. For the last two,
the object rule is explicitly own-level. A
single “same-level objects throughout” rule would
contradict the first assignment.

**The entry relation needs a controlled rendering.**
The running source reads vimokṣaprāveśikāny abhibhvāyatanāni
and abhibhvāyatanaprāveśikāni kṛtsnāyatanāni, followed by
uttarottaraviśiṣṭatvāt. It thus relates mastery bases
to releases and totalities to mastery bases,
explicitly invoking the superiority of each subsequent
group. The local research varies in how
it paraphrases the direction of “entry.”

The progression release → mastery → totality is
retained here as the graded order of exposition
and increasing distinction. The compressed prāveśika
compounds alone are not used to establish a
mandatory acquisition sequence for every practitioner.
Their exact directional English remains a review point.

**Bearer range and the cessation exception.** The
closing prose applies to all these practices
beginning with releases: they may belong to
ordinary or noble continuities, except for cessation-release.
It does not say that an ordinary person
necessarily possesses all the practices. Cessation-release
is an exception within the wider release complex,
not a member of the ten totalities.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Source voice.** The principal exposition assigns
visible form to the first eight. The wind
exception is attributed to unnamed authorities. These
are kept distinct in the translation and table;
no school identity is supplied beyond the witness.

**Witness cautions.** The śuddha-compound discrepancy
between scripts and running text is disclosed
above. The prose also prints anyor in the
four-aggregate sentence, where the context supplies the
reference to the final two. The study follows
that clear dual reference without editing the source.
The directional force of the entry compounds
remains open for philological review.

**Organon reconstruction — Yoga Vidyā and Yoga Technē.**
Yoga Vidyā distinguishes selected determination, complete
pervasion, technical nature, attainment-level, object-range,
and bearer status. Yoga Technē, its kriyā–karma
companion, concerns the effective cultivation of a
field pervaded by the selected determination.

The research's “universality at the level of
appearance” is useful project language for this
operation. Its scope remains the contemplative field.
Neither pervasion nor non-greed by itself establishes
an unrestricted constitutive Principle of Knowledge.
The source's ordinary/noble bearer distinction helps
keep practical accomplishment and liberative status separate.

**Review status.** Provisional, Bhāṣya-controlled study.
The tenfold inventory, first-eight nature and exclusive
fourth-dhyāna assignment, visible-object rule and attributed
wind alternative, final-two own-level objects, and
cessation exception have been checked against the
local witnesses and running source. The entry-compound
rendering remains explicitly qualified.
