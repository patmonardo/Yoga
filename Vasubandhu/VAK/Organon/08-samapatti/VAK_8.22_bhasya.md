# VAK_8.22 Bhāṣya — Eight Access Attainments and the First Access Exception

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> aṣṭau sāmantakāny eṣāṃ śuddhāduḥkhāsukhāni hi |
> āryaṃ cādyaṃ tridhā kecid atarkaṃ dhyānam antaram || 8.22 ||

> There are eight access attainments for these; they are pure and neither painful
> nor pleasant. The first is also noble. Some [say it is] threefold. Intermediate
> dhyāna is without vitarka.

Modes and result of intermediate dhyāna: 8.23. Chapter not closed.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.22.md`](./VAK_8.22.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> ekaikasy aikaikaṃ yena tatpraveśaḥ /
> śuddhakāni ca tāny upekṣendriyasaṃprayuktāni ca … vairāgyapathatvāc ca nāsvādanāsaṃprayuktāni /
> ādyaṃ sāmantakam anāgamyaṃ tac chuddhakaṃ cānāsravaṃ ca /
> yady api sāmantakacittena pratisaṃdhibandhaḥ kliṣṭo bhavati / samāhitasya tu kliṣṭatvaṃ pratiṣidhyate /
> kecit punar icchanti / āsvādanāsaṃprayuktam apy anāgamyam /
> sāmantakaṃ hi vairāgyamārgaḥ / dhyānam eva hi vitarkāsaṃprayuktaṃ dhyānāntaram /

**Witness.** Pradhan 447|18–448|11. Voices: Vasubandhu; `kecit`. Damaged
`yantabāhyatvāt` left unresolved.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> How many access [items]? Eight — one for each, by which one enters that
> [principal]. Are they also threefold, and is feeling the same? No. They are
> pure and joined with the faculty of neither-pain-nor-pleasure. Because they are
> a path of dispassion, they are not savoring-associated.
>
> The first access — anāgamya — is both pure and uncontaminated. Even if
> rebirth-linking by an access-level mind is defiled, defiledness of the collected
> [state] is denied. Some want savoring-associated anāgamya as well.
>
> Access is a path of dispassion. Intermediate dhyāna is dhyāna itself without
> vitarka — a variety of dhyāna. Hence it is not set up in the second and later:
> that distinguishing mark is absent.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
eight entries, not eight copies of the principals
feeling: upekṣendriya; mode: śuddha (plus anāsrava at first)
kecit: first also savoring
sāmantaka ≠ dhyānāntara
```

## 5. Two Intermediates

Neutral feeling ≠ ethically indeterminate. Collected access ≠ rebirth-linking
mind. `Tridhā kecit` ≠ the later three samādhis by vitarka/vicāra.

## 6. Principal Account

| | |
|---|---|
| how many | 8, one per principal |
| feeling | upekṣendriya |
| mode | śuddha; first also anāsrava |
| savoring | excluded here (kecit add it at anāgamya) |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `eṣām` = the eight principals;
- `āryam` = anāsrava of anāgamya;
- last pāda opens dhyānāntara, finished next.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

The door is not an empty interval. Access has feeling, mode, work. Vidyā keeps
entry-path distinct from a dhyāna missing one limb. Technē of dispassion is
content: it looks at the lower ground. Appearance would call every “in between”
the same shine. Two intermediates, two determinations.
