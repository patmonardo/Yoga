# VAK_2.14

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> कामेष्वादौ विपाको द्वे लभ्यते नोपपादुकैः ।
>
> तेः षड्वा सप्त वा अष्टौ वा षड् रूपेषु एकमुत्तरे ॥ २.१४ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> kāmeṣv ādau vipāko dve labhyate nopapādukaiḥ /
>
> teḥ ṣaḍ vā sapta vā aṣṭau vā ṣaḍ rūpeṣu ekam uttare // 2.14 //

The transmitted Kārikā witness has `teḥ`. The Bhāṣya quotation and syntax
support `taiḥ`, instrumental plural, “by them,” referring to spontaneously
born beings. The witness is preserved above; the Bhāṣya-supported reading is
used in the analysis and translations.

`Kāmāḥ` and `rūpāṇi` are plural realm-designations explained by the Bhāṣya
through the predominance of desire and form respectively.

## 4. Grammar

The opening asks which faculties are acquired initially as maturation-result:

```text
kāmeṣu ādau vipākaḥ dve labhyate
    → in the desire realm, at the beginning,
      two are acquired as maturation-result
```

The singular `labhyate` occurs in the transmitted verse despite dual `dve`.
The Bhāṣya understands two faculties: body and life.

The negative instrumental restricts the first case:

```text
na upapādukaiḥ
    → not by the spontaneously born
```

The Bhāṣya therefore applies it to egg-born, womb-born, and moisture-born
beings.

The next clause uses the Bhāṣya-supported `taiḥ`:

```text
taiḥ ṣaṭ vā sapta vā aṣṭau vā
    → by spontaneously born beings,
      either six, seven, or eight
```

The final two locatives supply realm-specific counts:

```text
ṣaṭ rūpeṣu
    → six in the form realm

ekam uttare
    → one in the higher, formless realm
```

These are counts of faculties first acquired **as maturation-result**, not
counts of every faculty or mental event present at the first moment.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> In the desire realm, initially, two are acquired as maturation-result—not by the spontaneously born. By them [reading `taiḥ`], six, seven, or eight [are acquired]; six in the form realm, one in the higher realm.

### Bhāṣya-informed translation

> At the beginning of a non-spontaneous birth in the desire realm, body and life are first acquired as maturation-result. A spontaneously born being initially acquires six, seven, or eight resultant faculties according to whether it has no sexual characteristic, one, or both. Six resultant faculties are initially acquired in the form realm. In the formless realm, only life is initially acquired as maturation-result.

The resulting configurations are:

```text
desire realm, egg-, womb-, or moisture-born:
    body + life = 2

desire realm, spontaneously born:
    eye + ear + nose + tongue + body + life = 6
    + one sexual faculty = 7
    + both sexual faculties = 8

form realm:
    eye + ear + nose + tongue + body + life = 6

formless realm:
    life = 1
```

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> A new continuum does not begin with one invariant faculty package. Its initial matured configuration depends upon realm, mode of birth, and sexual differentiation. Non-spontaneous generation begins with a minimal bodily and life support from which further faculties develop. Spontaneous generation begins with a more complete sensory configuration. Form-realm generation has the sixfold resultant configuration without sexual differentiation, while formless generation initially receives only life as maturation-result.

Organon rendering:

> The Kośa now models genetic initialization. Execution-context and generation-mode determine which faculty-capabilities are available as inherited result at the first moment. Presence must be distinguished from resultant acquisition: mind and neutral feeling occur at relinking but are not members of the `vipāka` initialization set because their instances are necessarily afflicted then.

“Initialization” and “inherited result” are Organon renderings. The textual
categories are initial acquisition, relinking, birth-mode, realm, and
maturation-result.

## 8. Logical Determination

Initial acquisition is parameterized:

```text
InitialVipakaSet
    = f(Realm, BirthMode, SexualConfiguration)
```

For non-spontaneous desire-realm birth:

```text
Realm = Desire
∧ BirthMode ∈ {EggBorn, WombBorn, MoistureBorn}
    → InitialVipakaSet = {Body, Life}
    → Count = 2
```

For spontaneous desire-realm birth:

```text
Realm = Desire
∧ BirthMode = Spontaneous
∧ SexualConfiguration = None
    → InitialVipakaSet = {Eye, Ear, Nose, Tongue, Body, Life}
    → Count = 6

SexualConfiguration = One
    → Count = 7

SexualConfiguration = Both
    → Count = 8
```

For the higher realms:

```text
Realm = Form
    → InitialVipakaSet = {Eye, Ear, Nose, Tongue, Body, Life}
    → Count = 6

Realm = Formless
    → InitialVipakaSet = {Life}
    → Count = 1
```

Presence and result-status are separate predicates:

```text
PresentAtRelinking(x)
    ↛ AcquiredAsVipaka(x)

MindAndNeutralFeelingAtRelinking(x)
∧ Afflicted(x)
    → PresentAtRelinking(x)
    ∧ Not(AcquiredAsVipaka(x))
```

This continues VAK 2.10's distinction between faculty identity and causal
status.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 2.14 begins `lābha`, the acquisition analysis, after VAK 2.13 closes the
long classification of faculty-types. The question is genetic: which
faculties are first acquired as maturation-result when a new continuum is
established?

The smallest desire-realm configuration belongs to non-spontaneous birth.
Body and life are initially acquired, while the remaining faculties develop
in the course of embodiment. Spontaneous birth does not pass through that
gradual process; it begins with all five sensory faculties and life, with one
or two sexual faculties added where applicable.

The form realm begins with the same six resultant faculties as a sexually
undifferentiated spontaneously born desire-realm being. The formless realm
reduces the initial maturation set to life alone. This does not mean that no
mind or feeling occurs there. It means only that no other faculty is first
acquired **as vipāka** in the classification under discussion.

The Bhāṣya makes that distinction explicit in the desire-realm objection.
Mind and neutral feeling occur at relinking, but they are necessarily
afflicted then and therefore are not counted as maturation-result. The Agent
model must consequently track at least four non-identical facts:

```text
faculty presence
faculty acquisition
time of acquisition
causal status of the acquired instance
```

For our layered Object Model, this is the first true genetic constructor:

```text
given
    object-kind
    genetic layer
    execution realm
    generation mode

construct
    the admissible initial faculty configuration

then permit
    later acquisition and transformation
```

The natural/spiritual fivefold Object and ten Samyama-bhūmis are our
Yoga–Organon architecture. VAK 2.14 supplies the textual principle that an
Agent's operative powers have a conditioned genesis and need not all be
present as inherited result at initialization.
