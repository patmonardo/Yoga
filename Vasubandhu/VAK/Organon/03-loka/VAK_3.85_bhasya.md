## Continuous Conventional Translation

So, among these beings with lifespans, does death occur before the lifespan
is complete, or not? It does in general. Outside Uttarakuru, premature death
can occur. In Uttarakuru, beings with fixed lifespans necessarily live out
their entire lifespan; elsewhere, this is not assured.

For many persons, however, death before the completion of the term does not
occur. The transmitted list includes a difficult Bodhisattva/Tuṣita compound,
a final-existence being, those instructed by or become a Jina, followers of
faith and Dharma, and a difficult final compound involving a Bodhisattva,
cakravartin, and their offspring. Its exact segmentation is uncertain, so
these categories are reported cautiously rather than normalized.

Places and bodies have been described by measures in yojanas, and lifespans
by measures in years; their limiting measures have not yet been stated.
Since their arrangements differ, their limits must also be stated. Thus the
analysis begins by naming them in a common sequence: the ultimate atom, the
letter, and the moment are the respective limits of form, name, and time.

The limit of form as it is diminished is the ultimate atom. The limit of
time is the moment; the limit of a name is the letter, as in the word “cow.”
What, then, is the measure of a moment? When its conditions are complete, it
is the time in which a dharma attains its own occurrence; alternatively, for
a moving dharma, it is the time in which it travels from one ultimate atom to
the next. The Abhidharmikas say that sixty-five moments pass in the time it
takes a strong man to snap his fingers.

## The Two Determinations

The passage first qualifies lifespan and then changes to measurement's
smallest limits:

| Domain | Determination |
|---|---|
| Uttarakuru | Fixed lifespan is necessarily completed |
| Elsewhere | Premature death is possible, with listed exceptions |
| Form (`rūpa`) | Limit: ultimate atom (`paramāṇu`) |
| Name (`nāma`) | Limit: letter (`akṣara`) |
| Time (`adhvan`/`kāla`) | Limit: moment (`kṣaṇa`) |

The definition of `kṣaṇa` is operational: it is measured either by a
conditioned dharma attaining its occurrence under complete conditions, or by
the traversal of one ultimate-atom interval by a moving dharma. The
sixty-five-moment finger-snap comparison is expressly attributed to the
Abhidharmikas.

## Doctrinal Note

An allocated lifespan and a necessarily completed lifespan are not the same
determination. Uttarakuru guarantees completion; outside it, premature death
may occur, though the Bhāṣya preserves exceptions. The later triad does not
collapse form, naming, and temporal course into one unit: each has its own
limiting measure.

## Organon Contact Point

Light contact: the passage distinguishes a domain's terminal unit from the
unit used in another domain. This is a project-level observation, not
Vasubandhu's technical terminology.
