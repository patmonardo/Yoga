# VAK_3.41

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> इह पुष्ट्यर्थमाश्रयाश्रितयोर्द्वयम् ।
>
> द्वयमन्यभवाक्षेपनिर्वृत्त्यर्थं यथाक्रमम् ॥ ३.४१ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> iha puṣṭyartham āśrayāśritayor dvayam /
>
> dvayam anyabhavākṣepanirvṛttyarthaṃ yathākramam // 3.41 //

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
iha                         → iha
puṣṭi-artham               → puṣṭyartham
āśraya-āśritayoḥ           → āśrayāśritayoḥ
dvayam                      → dvayam
dvayam                      → dvayam
anya-bhava-ākṣepa-nirvṛtti-artham
                            → anyabhavākṣepanirvṛttyartham
yathā-kramam                → yathākramam
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| iha | adverb | here; in already-arisen existence |
| puṣṭyartham | accusative neuter singular purpose compound | for the sake of nourishment or strengthening |
| āśrayāśritayoḥ | genitive dual compound | of the support and that which is supported |
| dvayam | nominative/accusative neuter singular | a pair; the first two nutriments |
| anyabhava | compound member | another becoming; renewed existence not yet arisen |
| ākṣepa | compound member | projection / throwing forward |
| nirvṛtti | compound member | production, realization, or emergence |
| artham | accusative neuter singular used for purpose | for the sake of |
| yathākramam | indeclinable adverb | respectively, in the stated order |

The research witness's initial `nirvṛttiś ca` belongs to the preceding
explanation of intermediate existence and is not part of this Kārikā. The
Bhāṣya also fixes the final compound as `ākṣepa-nirvṛtti`, projection and
production—not `ākṣepa-nivṛtti`, projection and cessation.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The first hemistich assigns the first pair of nutriments to present existence:

```text
iha
    āśraya-āśritayoḥ
    puṣṭi-artham
    dvayam

here [in already-arisen existence],
    for the nourishment
    of the support and supported,
    [there is] a pair
```

The second hemistich assigns the latter pair to future existence:

```text
dvayam
    anya-bhava-ākṣepa-nirvṛtti-artham
    yathā-kramam

[the other] pair is
    for projecting and producing another becoming,
    respectively
```

`Yathākramam` governs both ordered pairings. The exact four correspondences
are supplied by the Bhāṣya.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Here, a pair [serves] to nourish the support and what is supported. The
> [other] pair [serves] respectively to project and produce another becoming.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Two nutriments principally maintain existence already arisen: morsel
> nutriment nourishes the support, the body endowed with faculties, while
> contact nourishes what depends upon that support, consciousness and its
> associated mental operations. The other two principally bring about
> existence not yet arisen: mental volition projects renewed becoming, and
> from the seed of differentiated cognition conditioned by karma the projected
> becoming emerges. Thus the four are ordered respectively as bodily
> maintenance, citta-caitta maintenance, projection of another becoming, and
> production of that projected becoming.

The identifications of support and supported, the four exact assignments, and
the karma-conditioned `vijñāna`-seed are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The World System must both maintain what is actual and generate what is not
> yet actual. Its present embodiment has a support—the body with faculties—and
> a supported conscious life—the citta-caitta complex. Material nutriment
> sustains the former; contact sustains the latter. Its future embodiment is
> likewise divided between direction and realization: volition throws another
> becoming forward, while karma-conditioned differentiated cognition brings
> the projected station into emergence. Maintenance and generation are thus
> reciprocally ordered without being confused.

kavaḍīkāra   → nourishment of āśraya

## 8. Logical Determination

### Why precisely four?

The Bhāṣya concedes that all conditioned dharmas may contribute to becoming,
but these four are taught because of their predominant functions:

```text
all four nutriments
    = principal maintainers and generators

not:
    exhaustive list of every contributing conditioned factor
```

Classification by predominance selects the factors that explain the system's
primary support operations.

### Present becoming: support and supported

```text
already-arisen bhava
    ├── āśraya
    │     = body with faculties
    │     ← nourished by morsel nutriment
    │
    └── āśrita
          = citta-caitta
          ← nourished by contact
```

The copula is functional and reciprocal:

```text
body supplies the support of conscious operation
conscious operation makes the body an inhabited Agent-support
contact maintains their active relation
```

The final line is an Organon determination of the textual support–supported
structure; it is not a quotation from the Bhāṣya.

### Future becoming: projection and production

```text
mental volition
    → projects renewed becoming

karma-conditioned vijñāna-seed
    → produces the projected becoming
```

Projection is not yet realization:

```text
ākṣepa
    = determination directed toward a future station

nirvṛtti
    = emergence of that station from its conditioned seed
```

The two operations form a Hypothetical structure:

```text
if renewed becoming is projected through volition
and its cognition-seed is conditioned by karma,
then the projected becoming can emerge.
```

### Two temporal pairs

```text
first pair:
    nurse-like
    maintains the already arisen

second pair:
    mother-like
    generates the not yet arisen
```

The four nutriments thereby span actuality and futurity without treating them
as the same causal role.

### Ordinary ingestion and technical nutriment differ

The subsequent four-cornered analysis preserves the distinction:

```text
kavaḍīkāra
    = something ingested as a morsel

āhāra
    = something performing nutritive work
```

Their extensions overlap but are not simply identical. A harmful intake may
still have performed a sustaining operation at its initial incidence, even if
its later result is destructive. The Bhāṣya preserves this as a Vaibhāṣika
functional determination rather than reducing nutriment to ordinary naming.

## 9. Interpretive Note

This verse gives exact form to the transition the user has identified:

```text
Human Dharma
    → begins from lived embodiment:
      body, food, contact, present maintenance

Abhidharma
    → discriminates the conditions and functions:
      support, supported, projection, production
```

“Human Dharma” and this contrast are our interpretive names, not Vasubandhu's
labels. But they identify the method accurately. The Kośa begins from what any
embodied person knows—life must be fed—and then discovers that “feeding” is a
fourfold functional architecture extending from bodily support to the
production of another becoming.

This is Kantian realism in the intended project sense. The Kośa does not leave
the ordinary World behind for a hidden second World. It begins with the real
conditions of embodied appearance and discriminates the lawful operations
through which that appearance is maintained and projected.

```text
empirical actuality:
    an embodied being eats, encounters, intends, cognizes

Abhidharma determination:
    these operations occupy different roles
    in maintaining present and producing future becoming
```

The explicit `citta-caitta` determination is central. Contact does not merely
occur beside consciousness. It nourishes consciousness and its associated
operations as the supported life of the embodied Agent. This is the practical
Caitta theory we were seeking:

```text
āśraya: embodied faculty-system
    ↔
āśrita: citta-caitta operation
```

Morsel nutriment sustains the Client's embodied support; contact sustains its
active semantic interface. Without contact, the citta-caitta complex is not
fed by a World.

The latter pair advances from maintenance into Learning and generation.
Volition supplies the future-directed determination; karma-conditioned
differentiated cognition retains the productive seed from which that
determination can become another station.

```text
purpose
    → projection

learned cognitive lineage
    → realization
```

That is stronger than a linear data pipeline. The Agent does not merely
receive inputs and emit outputs. Its contacts maintain its current conscious
organization; its volitions project possible future organization; and its
conditioned cognitive seed can realize that projection as a renewed World.

For Oculus, the four nutriments can be projected as two coordinated services:

```ts
interface PresentMaintenance {
  embodiedSupport: MorselNutriment;
  consciousOperations: ContactNutriment;
}

interface FutureGeneration {
  projectedStation: MentalVolition;
  realizedStation: KarmaConditionedVijnanaSeed;
}
```

This is Organon architecture, not a premature repository implementation. It
makes the philosophical division visible: present state requires maintenance;
future state requires both projection and a productive lineage capable of
realization.

The food verses have therefore moved completely beyond food as an object. They
now state the general law by which an embodied conscious system maintains
itself and produces its successor.
