# VAK_3.41 Bhāṣya — Present Maintenance and Future Production

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

```iast
iha puṣṭyartham āśrayāśritayor dvayam /
dvayam anyabhavākṣepanirvṛttyarthaṃ yathākramam // 3.41 //
```

> Here, two serve to nourish the support and what is supported. The other
> two serve respectively to project and produce another becoming.

The Bhāṣya first asks whether all four nutriments maintain both already-arisen
beings and beings seeking birth. After narratives about expectation, memory,
and sustained life, it states why four nutriments are singled out, assigns
each its predominant function, and tests the functional definition through
four-cornered cases and the nutriment of hell beings.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

How many nutriments serve the persistence of beings, and how many support
those seeking birth? The Vaibhāṣikas say that all four serve both purposes.
Morsel nutriment also conduces to renewed becoming for those attached to it,
for the Blessed One said: “These four nutriments are the root of disease, the
thorn of the boil, and the condition of aging and death.”

Mental volition, conversely, is also seen to maintain beings here. They tell
the following story. A father stricken by famine filled a bag with ashes,
hung it from a peg, and comforted his two sons by saying, “This is barley
meal.” As long as they imagined it so, they remained alive. When someone
opened the bag and they saw that it was ashes, they fell into despair and
died.

Likewise, men whose vessel had broken in the great ocean rushed toward a
large mass of foam, thinking it dry land. When they touched it and lost hope,
they died.

The Saṃgīti exposition also says that large aquatic creatures emerge from the
ocean, deposit eggs on a sandy bank, cover them with sand, and return to the
ocean. The eggs of mothers whose recollection directed toward them is not lost
do not rot; those of mothers whose recollection is lost do rot.

The Sautrāntikas do not explain this in that way, lest one being's nutriment
be nutriment through another being. They say instead that eggs whose
recollection directed toward the mother is not lost do not rot, while those
whose recollection is lost do rot: they remember the condition of contact
with her.

Why, then, are exactly four nutriments taught? Are not all contaminated
dharmas nourishers of becoming? Although that is so, these four are stated
because of their predominance.

Here, two serve to nourish the support and what is supported. The other two
serve respectively to project and produce another becoming. The support is
the body endowed with faculties, and morsel nutriment nourishes it. What is
supported is mind and its associated mental factors, and contact nourishes
them. These first two are predominant in nourishing becoming already arisen
here.

Through mental volition renewed becoming is projected. What has been projected
then emerges from the seed of differentiated cognition conditioned by action.
These latter two are predominant in producing becoming not yet arisen. Hence
four nutriments are taught. The first pair is like nurses because they nourish
what has been born; the latter pair is like mothers because they generate it.

Is every morsel nutriment? A morsel can exist that is not nutriment; the issue
is analyzed four ways. First is a morsel upon which the faculties decline and
the great elements are disrupted. Second are the three nutriments other than
morsel nutriment. Third is a morsel upon which the faculties increase and the
great elements grow. Fourth comprises cases left after those configurations
are excluded. Four-cornered analyses should be constructed as appropriate for
contact and the other nutriments as well.

There may be increase of faculties and growth of the great elements dependent
upon contact and the others without those factors being nutriments—for
example, factors belonging to another level or factors without outflows.

Even something consumed that later harms the consumer can still be nutriment
because it gave support at its initial incidence. The Vaibhāṣikas say that
nutriment performs its nutritive function at two times: while being consumed
and while being digested.

How many nutriments occur in each destiny? All four occur in every destiny,
and likewise in every mode of birth. How can morsel nutriment occur in hell?
There are blazing iron balls and boiling copper.

If even what harms is nutriment, does this not undermine the four-cornered
analysis and the treatise definition of morsel nutriment as that upon which
the faculties increase, the great elements grow, and maintenance continues?
There is no contradiction, because that statement intends nutriment that
produces increase. In hell there is nutriment of decrease because it meets
the defining characteristic; it can at least remove hunger and thirst. More
securely, the presence of morsel nutriment in isolated hells in a manner
comparable to humans establishes its occurrence throughout the five
destinies.

## 4. Arguments and Schools

The unit preserves several distinguishable voices:

- The Vaibhāṣikas say that all four nutriments serve both present maintenance
  and future becoming, and later that nutriment operates both during
  consumption and digestion.
- Narrative authorities are invoked to show mental volition or expectation
  maintaining present life.
- The Sautrāntikas reject the maternal-memory account if it makes one being's
  state directly nourish another, relocating the relevant memory to the eggs'
  own continuum.
- Vasubandhu's exposition explains why the four are nevertheless singled out
  by predominant function and subjects the category to four-cornered tests.
- An objection concerning harmful hell nutriment is answered by distinguishing
  nutriment of increase from nutriment of decrease and by citing isolated
  hells.

The Study preserves these positions without treating every illustrative
story as an uncontested empirical report.

## 5. All Four Can Serve Both Functions

The initial Vaibhāṣika thesis prevents an overly rigid partition:

```text
all four nutriments
    can support already-arisen beings
    and can support birth-seeking beings
```

Morsel nutriment can contribute to renewed becoming when attachment to it is
operative. Mental volition can sustain present life through expectation or
hope. The stories of the ash-filled bag and foam mistaken for land dramatize
this claim: an intended or imagined future can temporarily maintain the
present organism, while collapse of that orientation coincides with death.

These narratives do not establish that a false belief supplies material
calories. They are adduced to show the life-maintaining efficacy assigned to
mental volition. Material and purposive nutriment remain distinct even when
both affect continued existence.

## 6. The Sautrāntika Correction

The egg narrative raises a bearer problem. On one reading, the mother's
uninterrupted recollection keeps the eggs from rotting. The Sautrāntikas
reject this because it would make something in one continuum function as
nutriment for another continuum.

Their alternative places the operative recollection with the eggs:

```text
mother's memory nourishes eggs
    rejected

eggs retain memory of contact-condition with mother
    accepted alternative
```

Whatever the empirical status of the example, its doctrinal function is
clear. Nutriment must be located in the continuum whose persistence it
supports. Causal influence across continuums is not denied generally; the
specific nutritive function cannot be reassigned without identifying its
bearer.

## 7. The Two Ordered Pairs

The verse assigns predominant functions:

```text
present becoming already arisen
    kavaḍīkāra → nourishes āśraya
    sparśa      → nourishes āśrita

future becoming not yet arisen
    saṃcetanā   → projects punarbhava
    vijñāna     → produces the projected becoming
```

The bodily support is the `sendriyakāya`, the body endowed with faculties.
What depends upon it is the `citta-caitta` complex. Contact nourishes this
supported conscious life.

For future becoming, projection and production must remain distinct. Mental
volition throws forward or determines renewed becoming; the projected
becoming emerges from a cognition-seed conditioned by action. The Bhāṣya thus
fixes the corrupted verse compound as `ākṣepa-nirvṛtti`, projection and
production—not projection and cessation.

The nurse and mother analogies summarize the temporal difference:

```text
first pair  = nurse-like, maintaining what has arisen
second pair = mother-like, generating what has not arisen
```

## 8. Nutriment as a Functional, Four-Cornered Category

Ordinary morsel and technical nutriment overlap without being identical:

| Corner | Morsel? | Nutriment? | Bhāṣya case |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| first | yes | no | intake causing decline and disruption |
| second | no | yes | contact, volition, and cognition nutriments |
| third | yes | yes | intake producing increase and growth |
| fourth | no | no | remaining cases |

The same method applies to the other nutriments according to context. A
factor can contribute to bodily increase without being technical nutriment if
it belongs to another level or is uncontaminated. Observable benefit alone
therefore does not erase realm and contamination constraints.

The harmful-food discussion introduces temporal qualification. According to
the Vaibhāṣikas, an intake may nourish at initial consumption or digestion
even if it later harms. The hell case stretches this definition to nutriment
of decrease, while the treatise citation describes nutriment of increase.
The text itself registers the resulting pressure rather than hiding it.

## 9. Philosophical and Organon Study

The fourfold architecture distinguishes maintenance of actual organization
from projection and production of a future organization:

```text
PresentMaintenance {
    embodiedSupport    ← morsel nutriment
    supportedCognition ← contact nutriment
}

FutureGeneration {
    projectedState ← mental volition
    producedState  ← karma-conditioned cognition-seed
}
```

This is an Organon reconstruction, not the Bhāṣya's literal notation. Its
source-ground is strong: `āśraya`, `āśrita`, `ākṣepa`, and `nirvṛtti` are
explicitly differentiated functions.

For Ocular Datasets, this means present persistence and future generation
need different provenance. A maintained artifact is not thereby a projected
successor; a proposed configuration is not yet produced; a production event
requires a conditioned lineage capable of realizing the projection.

The Sautrāntika objection adds bearer identity. A maintenance signal in one
agent or Dataset cannot silently be credited as the nutritive state of
another. Cross-system causation may occur, but state, possession, and
functional support require explicit alignment.

Finally, the four-cornered analysis prevents classification by surface form.
Something may look like ordinary input yet fail the technical function; a
non-material operation may be genuine nutriment; an initially sustaining
input may later be harmful. Type, function, time, realm, and effect are
separate dimensions. This is exactly the kind of multidimensional
discrimination an Ocular analysis must preserve.

## 10. Review Status

This Study restores the complete natural unit at 153.21–155.05: the
Vaibhāṣika two-purpose thesis, the ash-bag and foam narratives, the egg-memory
dispute with the Sautrāntikas, the principle of predominance, the four ordered
nutriment functions, the nurse and mother analogies, the four-cornered
analysis, harmful intake, and nutriment across all destinies and births.

The Sanskrit and translation remain provisional. The narratives, the bearer
of recollection in the egg passage, `ākṣepa-nirvṛtti`, the four corners, the
two times of nutritive action, and the distinction between increase- and
decrease-nutriment require comparison with critical Sanskrit, Tibetan,
Chinese, and established translations. The Study stops with the five-destiny
argument and does not begin VAK 3.42.
