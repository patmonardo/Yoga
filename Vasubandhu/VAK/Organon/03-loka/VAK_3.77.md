# VAK_3.77

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> अर्धार्धवृद्धिरूर्ध्वं तु परीत्ताभेभ्य आश्रयः ।
>
> द्विगुणद्विगुणा हित्वानभ्रकेभ्यस्त्रियोजनम् ॥ ३.७७ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> ardhārdhavṛddhir ūrdhvaṃ tu parīttābhebhya āśrayaḥ /
>
> dviguṇadviguṇā hitvānabhrakebhyas triyojanam // 3.77 //

The transmitted verse is extremely compressed. In particular, the scope of
the half-increment rule and the force of the Anabhraka clause cannot be made
secure from the metrical wording alone. The Bhāṣya supplies the distribution
that governs the study translation below.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
ardha-ardha-vṛddhiḥ      → ardhārdhavṛddhiḥ
ūrdhvam                  → ūrdhvam
tu                       → tu
parītta-ābhebhyaḥ        → parīttābhebhyaḥ
āśrayaḥ                  → āśrayaḥ
dvi-guṇa-dvi-guṇāḥ       → dviguṇadviguṇāḥ
hitvā                    → hitvā
anabhrakebhyaḥ           → anabhrakebhyaḥ
tri-yojanam              → triyojanam
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| ardhārdhavṛddhiḥ | nominative feminine singular compound | increase by one half-unit at each step |
| ūrdhvam | adverb | upward; at the levels above |
| tu | particle | but; introduces the change from addition to multiplication |
| parīttābhebhyaḥ | ablative masculine plural | above or beyond the Parīttābha devas |
| āśrayaḥ | nominative masculine singular | support; here, the embodied support being measured |
| dviguṇadviguṇāḥ | distributive/reduplicated compound | successively twofold; doubled at each step |
| hitvā | absolutive of √hā | having excluded, excepting, with an adjustment made |
| anabhrakebhyaḥ | ablative masculine plural | at the Anabhraka point; the exceptional class in the series |
| triyojanam | neuter singular measure expression | three yojanas |

`ardha` receives its unit from the continuing context of 3.76: one half of a
yojana, not one half of the preceding body's magnitude. `āśraya` designates
the bodily support appropriate to a station of birth.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The opening expression completes the forward pointer `tataḥ param` at the end
of 3.76:

```text
[tataḥ param]
    ardha-ardha-vṛddhiḥ [āśrayaḥ]
```

> Beyond that, the bodily support increases by successive half-yojana
> increments.

The Bhāṣya limits this rule to three stations above the Brahmakāyikas:
Brahmapurohita, Mahābrahman, and Parīttābha. Thus the progression culminates
at two yojanas.

The next construction changes the operation:

```text
parīttābhebhyaḥ ūrdhvam tu
    āśrayaḥ
    dviguṇa-dviguṇaḥ [bhavati]
```

> But above the Parīttābhas, the bodily support is successively doubled.

The final clause introduces an exception:

```text
anabhrakebhyaḥ
    tri-yojanam hitvā
```

> At the Anabhraka level, subtracting three yojanas.

The surface sequence is elliptical; the Bhāṣya resolves its operation as
`(64 × 2) − 3 = 125`. It then states that doubling resumes above this point.
That resumed rule and the final Akaniṣṭha value are commentary determinations,
not separately expressed in the verse.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> The bodily support increases upward by successive halves; but above the
> Parīttābhas it is successively doubled, except that at the Anabhraka level
> three yojanas are subtracted.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> From the Brahmakāyika measure of one-half yojana, the next three form-realm
> stations increase by one-half yojana each, reaching two yojanas at
> Parīttābha. Above Parīttābha, bodily measure doubles at each station through
> Śubhakṛtsna at sixty-four yojanas. The expected next double is reduced by
> three, giving the Anabhrakas a measure of 125 yojanas. Above them, successive
> doubling resumes and culminates in the Akaniṣṭhas at 16,000 yojanas.

The limits of the additive series, the intermediate class-values, the exact
Anabhraka calculation, the resumed doubling, and the Akaniṣṭha terminus are
supplied or entailed by the Bhāṣya's exposition.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The hierarchy of form-realm embodiment is a composed law rather than a
> single progression. First bodily support advances by equal additions; then
> it advances by repeated multiplication. At one determinate station the
> general multiplication is locally modified by subtraction, after which the
> general rule resumes. The exception does not disrupt the World System: it is
> itself encoded as part of the rule by which each station receives its
> appropriate embodiment.

| ardhārdhavṛddhi | successive half-yojana increase | additive rule governing the three stations through Parīttābha |
| dviguṇavṛddhi | successive doubling | multiplicative rule above Parīttābha |

| Parīttābha | Parīttābha deva | terminal station of the additive sequence and base of the first doubling sequence |
| Śubhakṛtsna | Śubhakṛtsna deva | station reaching 64 yojanas under the first doubling sequence |
| Anabhraka | Anabhraka deva | exceptional station assigned 125 rather than 128 yojanas |
| Puṇyaprasava | Puṇyaprasava deva | first station above Anabhraka, where doubling resumes |

`āśraya` should not be inflated here into an unconditioned metaphysical
ground. Its local function is precise: the measurable bodily support produced
at a given form-realm station.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Stage I: additive initialization

VAK 3.76 supplies the base:

```text
Brahmakāyika = 0.5 yojana
```

For the next three stations:

```text
Mₙ₊₁ = Mₙ + 0.5 yojana
```

| Station | Measure |
|---|---:|
| Brahmakāyika | 0.5 yojana |
| Brahmapurohita | 1 yojana |
| Mahābrahman | 1.5 yojanas |
| Parīttābha | 2 yojanas |

### Stage II: first doubling sequence

Above Parīttābha:

```text
Mₙ₊₁ = 2 × Mₙ
```

| Station | Measure |
|---|---:|
| Parīttābha | 2 yojanas |
| Apramāṇābha | 4 yojanas |
| Ābhāsvara | 8 yojanas |
| Parīttaśubha | 16 yojanas |
| Apramāṇaśubha | 32 yojanas |
| Śubhakṛtsna | 64 yojanas |

### Stage III: determinate exception

Straight doubling would produce:

```text
2 × 64 = 128 yojanas
```

The Anabhraka adjustment is:

```text
Anabhraka = (2 × 64) − 3 = 125 yojanas
```

This is not a rival rule for the whole series. It is a local override whose
input remains the expected doubled value.

### Stage IV: restored doubling

The Bhāṣya states that doubling resumes above Anabhraka:

| Station | Measure | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Anabhraka | 125 yojanas | explicitly stated |
| Puṇyaprasava | 250 yojanas | arithmetic consequence |
| Bṛhatphala | 500 yojanas | arithmetic consequence |
| Avṛha | 1,000 yojanas | arithmetic consequence |
| Atapa | 2,000 yojanas | arithmetic consequence |
| Sudṛśa | 4,000 yojanas | arithmetic consequence |
| Sudarśana | 8,000 yojanas | arithmetic consequence |
| Akaniṣṭha | 16,000 yojanas | explicitly stated terminus |

The logical form is therefore:

```text
initialize
    → add by a fixed difference
        → multiply by a fixed ratio
            → apply one local subtraction
                → restore the ratio
```

## 9. Interpretive Note

The methodological yield of this ostensibly physical catalogue is substantial.
The Kośa represents a World not merely as a list of places and inhabitants,
but as a system capable of assigning a determinate bodily support to every
ordered station. The classification and the quantitative rule reciprocally
determine one another:

```text
station
    → selects the applicable operation and bodily measure

operation and measure
    → make the station determinate within the ordered World
```

This is why the exception matters. A merely syntactic extraction might store
“increase,” “double,” and “subtract” as neighboring words. The semantic
structure is a small rule engine with scoped transitions:

```text
scope A: three additive steps
scope B: repeated doubling through Śubhakṛtsna
scope C: one corrected double at Anabhraka
scope D: resumed doubling through Akaniṣṭha
```

The Anabhraka case is especially valuable for the Organon. Systematic order
does not require abstract uniformity. The local exception is not noise outside
the law; once explicitly placed and bounded, it belongs to the concrete law
of the series. This is a strongly agential form of physical description: a
rule must know its domain, its current station, when to change operation, and
when an exception has been exhausted.

```ts
type MeasureOperation =
  | { kind: "Add"; yojanas: 0.5 }
  | { kind: "Double" }
  | { kind: "DoubleThenSubtract"; yojanas: 3 };

interface RupaBodyRule {
  fromStation: string;
  throughStation: string;
  operation: MeasureOperation;
  resumesWith?: "Double";
}
```

This is explanatory project notation, not Vasubandhu's vocabulary. It exposes
the control structure already resolved by the Bhāṣya while preserving the
difference between explicit values and arithmetic consequences.
