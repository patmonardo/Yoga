# VAK_3.17 Bhāṣya — The Grounds of Lucid Womb-Descent

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> garbhāvakrāntayas tisraś cakravartisvayaṃbhuvām /
> karmajñānobhayeṣāṃ vā viśadatvād yathākramam // 3.17 //

> Three modes of womb-descent belong to the wheel-turning ruler and the two
> self-arisen ones—or, respectively, arise through the clarity of karma, of
> knowing, and of both.

The Bhāṣya identifies the compressed three persons, assigns to each one of the
progressively lucid profiles established in VAK 3.16, and specifies the
formative ground of each profile.

remains unchanged.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Here, moreover, the following is taught: “Three modes of womb-descent belong
to the wheel-turning ruler and the self-arisen ones.” That is, to the
wheel-turning ruler and to the two self-arisen ones, the Pratyekabuddha and
the perfectly awakened Buddha. The word “respectively” will be stated at the
end.

Of these, the first belongs to the wheel-turning ruler. He enters with clear
awareness, but does not remain or emerge with clear awareness. The
Pratyekabuddha also remains with clear awareness. The Buddha also emerges
with clear awareness. Here too the designation is made according to a future
state.

“Or, respectively, through the clarity of karma, of knowing, and of both.”
The first belongs to those whose karma is clear and whose meritorious activity
is exalted. The second belongs to those whose knowing is clear, who possess
extensive learning and have practiced discrimination. The third belongs to
those whose meritorious karma and knowing are both clear. These very beings,
beginning with the wheel-turning ruler, appropriately possess these qualities
respectively. It is thereby established that the fourth mode belongs to the
remainder.

## 4. Movement and Voice of the Commentary

Vasubandhu speaks throughout this short unit. There is no staged objection.
The movement is a double correlation:

```text
three exceptional persons
    → three extents of transition-awareness

three formative grounds
    → clarity of karma / knowing / both
```

The prose also carries forward VAK 3.16's rule of prospective designation.
The being entering the womb is called a cakravartin, Pratyekabuddha, or Buddha
according to what that continuity will become.

## 5. The Three Persons and Three Profiles

The compound `cakravartisvayaṃbhuvām` compresses three persons. The Bhāṣya
unpacks the two `svayaṃbhūs` as the Pratyekabuddha and the perfectly awakened
Buddha:

```text
future cakravartin
    clear at entry

future Pratyekabuddha
    clear at entry and residence

future Buddha
    clear at entry, residence, and emergence
```

The cumulative force established in VAK 3.16 remains active. “Also remains”
includes lucid entry; “also emerges” includes lucid entry and residence.
These names do not imply that the fetus presently exercises the attained
office or awakening designated by them.

## 6. Karma, Knowing, and Both

The second half of the verse gives a corresponding causal-cognitive axis:

```text
cakravartin       ↔ clarity of karma
Pratyekabuddha    ↔ clarity of knowing
Buddha            ↔ clarity of karma and knowing
```

The Bhāṣya does not leave these abstractions empty. Clear karma is specified
through exalted meritorious activity. Clear knowing is specified through
extensive learning and practiced discrimination. Complete transition-clarity
belongs to the conjunction of both formations.

`Jñāna` is therefore rendered here as “knowing.” The passage does not use
`prajñā`, and its own gloss directs attention to a cognition formed through
learning and discrimination rather than to an unexplained luminous power.

## 7. The Fourth Mode by Remainder

The three named persons exhaust the three lucid profiles from VAK 3.16. The
Bhāṣya consequently establishes the fourth profile for everyone remaining:

```text
three exceptional continuities
    → progressively lucid entry, residence, and emergence

remaining continuities
    → delusion through all three phases
```

This is an inference by remainder explicitly drawn in the prose. It should not
be mistaken for a fourth member directly named by the present kārikā.

## 8. Bhāṣya Decisions for the Kārikā

The prose settles four compressed points:

- `svayaṃbhuvām` denotes two persons here: Pratyekabuddha and
  Samyaksaṃbuddha; it does not assert metaphysical self-causation.
- `yathākramam` coordinates both the persons with their awareness-profiles and
  the profiles with their formative grounds.
- The personal designations are prospective, as in VAK 3.16's “egg-born.”
- `viśadatva` is clarity expressed in transition-awareness, grounded in
  already cultivated karmic and cognitive formation.

The particle `vā` introduces the explanatory formulation through karma,
knowing, and both. It does not require the two correlations to be treated as
mutually exclusive doctrines; the commentary explicitly fits the same three
persons to the same three grounds.

## 9. Philosophical and Organon Study

The passage makes lucid embodiment derivative without making it accidental.
The clarity manifested during transition is the efficacy of formations
already achieved: meritorious action, cultivated knowing, or their unity.
The cognition-profile is thus neither a bare privilege of the person nor an
isolated decision made at the threshold of birth.

For the Organon, the important result is that a process-state can carry a
quality prepared in another temporal phase. Prior formations configure not
only the destination but the clarity with which the transition is undergone:

```text
cultivated formation
    → quality of the transition-field
    → extent of lucid awareness across its phases
```

This is a source-grounded relation among karma, knowing, and manifestation.
It does not yet identify the three with a general Oculus architecture, nor
does it turn the Buddha's clarity into an abstract metaphysical subject. The
continuity is named prospectively, while the operative grounds remain
specific and acquired.

## 10. Review Status

This Study covers the complete natural unit at 128.26–129.04. It preserves
the correlation of three persons, three cognition-profiles, and three grounds
of clarity; it also retains the prospective status of the personal names and
the fourth profile's derivation by remainder.

The Sanskrit and translation remain provisional. The corrupt compound at
129.02 and the exact force of `vā` require comparison with critical Sanskrit,
Tibetan, Chinese, and established translations during the later learning
pass. The Study stops before the externalists' anti-transmigration objection
at 129.05 and does not enter VAK 3.18.
