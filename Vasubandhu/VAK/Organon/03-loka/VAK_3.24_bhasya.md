# VAK_3.24 Bhāṣya — Becoming as Renewal-Producing Karma

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sa bhaviṣyadbhavaphalaṃ kurute karma tad bhavaḥ /
> pratisaṃdhiḥ punar jātir jarāmaraṇam ā vidaḥ // 3.24 //

> One produces karma whose result is future existence; that is becoming.
> Rebirth-junction, in turn, is birth; aging-and-death extends up to the
> feeling-stage.

The Bhāṣya completes the twelve-member sequence. Appropriative pursuit
accumulates karma productive of renewed existence; its future rebirth-junction
is designated birth, while the ensuing development through feeling is
gathered under aging-and-death.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> tathā ca paridhāvan—
>
> sa bhaviṣyadbhavaphalaṃ kurute karma tad bhavaḥ /
>
> sa viṣayāṇāṃ prāptihetoḥ paridhāvan paunarbhavikaṃ karmopacinoti / so
> 'sya bhavaḥ / tena hi karmaṇā punar itaḥ pracyutasyāyatyāṃ
> pratisaṃdhir bhavati / yo 'sau—
>
> pratisaṃdhiḥ punar jātiḥ /
>
> yad eveha vijñānāṅgaṃ tad evāsyānyatra janmani jātiḥ / tata ūrdhvam—
>
> jarāmaraṇam ā vidaḥ // 3.24 //
>
> jāteḥ pareṇa yāvad vedanāvasthā jarāmaraṇam / yāny eveha catvāry
> aṅgāni nāmarūpaṣaḍāyatanasparśavedanās tāny evānyatra
> jarāmaraṇam / ity etāni dvādaśāṅgāni /

This natural unit runs from printed 132.18 through 132.27 in
`Vasubandhu/Sources/kosabhasaya.txt`. The next sentence, `sa caiṣa
pratītyasamutpādaś caturvidha ucyate`, opens the fourfold classification of
dependent arising that culminates in VAK 3.25 and is not included here.

Word division and punctuation are editorial. The transmitted final phrase
`jarāmaraṇam ā vidaḥ` is uncertain. Its prose gloss unambiguously gives
`yāvad vedanāvasthā`, “up to the feeling-stage”; the translation follows that
gloss without silently emending the Sanskrit verse. The running source
remains unchanged.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Running about in that way, “one produces karma whose result is future
existence; that is becoming.” Running about for the sake of obtaining objects,
one accumulates karma productive of renewed existence. This is one's
becoming. Through that karma, after one has fallen away from this life, a
future rebirth-junction occurs.

That “rebirth-junction, in turn, is birth.” What is the consciousness-member
here in the present birth is birth for that same continuum in another birth.

Thereafter, “aging-and-death extends up to the feeling-stage.” The stage after
birth and continuing through the feeling-stage is aging-and-death. The four
members that here are name-and-form, the six sense spheres, contact, and
feeling are, in the other—future—birth, aging-and-death. In this way the
twelve members are complete.

## 4. Movement and Voice of the Commentary

Vasubandhu speaks throughout, carrying forward the person who “runs
everywhere” in appropriation from VAK 3.23:

```text
appropriative pursuit
    → accumulation of renewal-producing karma
    → becoming
    → death from the present life
    → future rebirth-junction
    → birth
    → development through feeling
    → aging-and-death
```

No rival position is introduced. The principal exegetical work is to show
that the same structural phases receive different names according to their
temporal position in the three-life presentation.

## 5. Becoming Is the Productive Karma

The verse distinguishes karma from its future result:

```text
present karma
    produces future-existence as its result

that renewal-producing karma
    = bhava in the twelve-member series
```

`Bhava` is therefore not primarily the future existence already achieved. It
is `paunarbhavika karma`, karma productive of renewed existence, accumulated
while the being pursues objects for acquisition.

This gives appropriation a projective consequence. The search-field of VAK
3.23 does not terminate when an object is obtained; action performed within
that field accumulates the causal efficacy from which another embodied course
will arise.

## 6. Rebirth-Junction Viewed as Birth

The Bhāṣya explicitly identifies two descriptions of the same structural
position:

```text
viewed within the present-life middle
    rebirth-junction = vijñāna member

viewed from the present as a future result
    rebirth-junction = jāti member
```

This is not an identity between the concepts `vijñāna` and `jāti` in every
context. It is a perspectival equivalence within the three-life articulation:
the junction called consciousness when analyzing the present middle is called
birth when it stands at the future limit.

The aggregate-continuum, not an independent traveler, remains the underlying
range to which both designations apply.

## 7. Aging-and-Death as a Compressed Future Range

The same perspectival compression governs `jarāmaraṇa`. Four stages analyzed
separately in the present life are gathered as one future-result member:

```text
present-life detailed articulation
    nāmarūpa
    ṣaḍāyatana
    sparśa
    vedanā

future life viewed from the present
    jarāmaraṇa
```

Thus aging-and-death does not mean only the final biological moments of old
age and dying in this passage. Within this specific three-life scheme, it
ranges from after future birth through the future feeling-stage.

The corrupt final words of the verse should therefore be interpreted through
the prose boundary, not repaired by conjecture without further witnesses.

## 8. Completion of the Twelve Members

VAK 3.21–3.24 has now supplied the complete distribution:

```text
past causes
    avidyā, saṃskāra

present results and causes
    vijñāna, nāmarūpa, ṣaḍāyatana, sparśa,
    vedanā, tṛṣṇā, upādāna, bhava

future results
    jāti, jarāmaraṇa
```

The sequence is not twelve objects on one uniform chronological scale. Some
members name broad aggregate-states, and future members compress ranges
analyzed more finely in the present middle. The following discussion will
make the principle of stage-designation explicit, but that belongs to the
next Study.

## 9. Philosophical and Organon Study

The present middle is productive rather than merely intermediate. What the
being feels, desires, appropriates, and does determines the future not as an
abstract possibility but as accumulated projective efficacy:

```text
object pursued
    → action accumulated
    → future junction projected
    → another embodied field
```

For the Organon, VAK 3.24 also demonstrates that labels depend upon analytic
position and scale. One event-range can be typed “consciousness” when viewed
as a present junction and “birth” when viewed as the future result of present
causes. Four finely resolved present stages can be represented by one
prospective result-member.

This is not equivocation if the viewpoint and range are recorded. It becomes
confusion only when the dataset treats the labels as interchangeable without
their temporal and explanatory coordinates. An Ocular analysis would
therefore need to retain:

```text
underlying continuum
selected temporal range
causal role
analytic viewpoint
assigned member-name
```

These are project consequences of the Bhāṣya's analysis. Vasubandhu's direct
claim remains the completion of the three-life twelve-member scheme.

## 10. Review Status

This Study restores the proper VAK 3.24 unit at 132.18–132.27. It separates
the verse from the extensive fourfold classification that begins afterward,
and preserves becoming as renewal-producing karma, the future junction as
birth, and the future developmental range through feeling as aging-and-death.

The translation remains provisional because the transmitted
`jarāmaraṇam ā vidaḥ` is corrupt or at least obscure. Its interpretation is
grounded in the immediate prose, while its Sanskrit form awaits comparison
with critical Sanskrit, Tibetan, Chinese, and established translations. The
Study stops before the fourfold classification and does not enter VAK 3.25.
