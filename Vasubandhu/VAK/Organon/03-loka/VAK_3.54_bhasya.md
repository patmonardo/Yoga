# VAK_3.54 Bhāṣya — Jambūdvīpa and Prāgvideha by Form

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

```iast
sārdhatriyojanaṃ tv ekaṃ prāgvideho 'rdhacandravat /
pārśvatrayaṃ tathāsyaikaṃ sārdhaṃ triśatayojanam // 3.54 //
```

> Jambūdvīpa's one remaining side is three and one-half yojanas. Prāgvideha
> is shaped like a half-moon; three of its sides have the same measure as
> Jambūdvīpa's, while one side is three hundred fifty yojanas.

The Bhāṣya resolves the opening pronoun by carrying Jambūdvīpa forward from
VAK 3.53. It then locates Prāgvideha east of Sumeru and makes explicit the
elliptical 2,000-yojana measure of its other three sides.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Sārdhatriyojanaṃ tv ekam.

Caturtham asya pārśvaṃ sārdhāni trīṇi yojanāni. Ata eva hy asau
śakaṭākṛtiḥ.

Prāgvideho 'rdhacandravat.

Itaḥ pūrveṇa Sumerupārśve Pūrvaṃ Videho dvīpaḥ. So 'rdhacandra
ivābhinirvṛttaḥ. Parimāṇatas tu

pārśvatrayaṃ tathāsya.

Yathā Jambūdvīpasya dve dve yojanasahasre.

Ekaṃ sārdhaṃ triśatayojanam.

Caturthaṃ pārśvaṃ sārdhāni trīṇi yojanaśatāni.
```

The continuous text regularizes punctuation, spacing, and the evident
mechanical form `cturthamasya`. The alternation between `Prāgvideha` and
`Pūrva Videha` is retained as the text's own naming relation rather than
silently standardized away.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Jambūdvīpa's one remaining side is three and one-half yojanas. Its fourth
side measures three and one-half yojanas, and precisely because of this it is
cart-shaped.

Prāgvideha is like a half-moon. To the east of here, on the eastern side of
Sumeru, is the continent Eastern Videha. It has come into formation like a
half-moon.

As for its measure, three of its sides are likewise the same: as in the case
of Jambūdvīpa, each measures two thousand yojanas. Its one remaining side is
three hundred fifty yojanas. That fourth side measures three hundred fifty
yojanas.

## 4. Natural Movement and Voice

The unit has two successive subjects:

```text
Jambūdvīpa carried forward from 3.53
    → fourth side: 3½ yojanas
    → cart shape thereby explained

Prāgvideha east of Sumeru
    → half-moon shape
    → three sides inherited at 2,000 each
    → fourth side: 350 yojanas
```

Vasubandhu speaks directly throughout. There is no objector or alternative
school in this short descriptive unit.

The opening `ekam`, “one,” cannot refer to Prāgvideha's fourth side because
Prāgvideha has not yet been introduced. Canonical sequence and the Bhāṣya's
`caturtham asya pārśvam` firmly attach it to Jambūdvīpa.

## 5. Why Jambūdvīpa Is Cart-Shaped

Combining VAK 3.53 and the opening of 3.54 yields:

| Jambūdvīpa side | Measure |
|---|---:|
| first | 2,000 yojanas |
| second | 2,000 |
| third | 2,000 |
| fourth | 3½ |

The Bhāṣya explicitly makes the shape predicate consequential upon the
exceptional fourth side:

```text
three long sides
    + one extremely short side
        → śakaṭākṛti, cart-shaped
```

`Ata eva`, “precisely because of this,” shows that the comparison is not an
unexplained ornamental image. It summarizes a metric configuration.

The numbers should not be homogenized for plausibility. The transmitted
account deliberately contrasts the three 2,000-yojana sides with a fourth
side of only 3½ yojanas.

## 6. Prāgvideha's Position and Form

The next continent is located relationally:

```text
reference point     Sumeru
direction           east
region              Prāgvideha / Eastern Videha
shape               half-moon
```

`Itaḥ pūrveṇa` means eastward from here within the sequence of the four sides
of Sumeru. The Bhāṣya calls the continent `Pūrva Videha`, making explicit the
direction encoded by `Prāgvideha`.

The phrase `ardhacandra ivābhinirvṛttaḥ` says that the continent has come
into formation like a half-moon. Shape is therefore a determination of the
produced container-world, not merely a later diagrammatic convention.

The text does not yet describe inhabitants or special functional capacities
of Prāgvideha. Its present determinations are position, measure, and form.

## 7. Shared Measure and Specific Difference

`Tathā`, “likewise,” carries forward Jambūdvīpa's three-side measure:

| Continent | Three corresponding sides | Fourth side | Form |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Jambūdvīpa | 2,000 each | 3½ | cart |
| Prāgvideha | 2,000 each | 350 | half-moon |

The two regions share a substantial geometrical template while differing in
one decisive parameter:

```text
shared:
    three sides × 2,000 yojanas

varied:
    fourth side

result:
    distinct continent form
```

This is not a claim that the fourth-side measure alone exhausts every aspect
of either shape. It is the relation the Bhāṣya uses to explain and contrast
the transmitted forms.

## 8. Translation Consequences

Two ellipses require Bhāṣya control:

1. `sārdhatriyojanaṃ tv ekam` lacks an expressed noun and subject; the
   commentary supplies Jambūdvīpa's fourth side.
2. `pārśvatrayaṃ tathāsya` lacks the repeated number; the commentary carries
   forward two thousand yojanas for each of Prāgvideha's three sides.

Without those restorations, the opening measure could be wrongly attached to
Prāgvideha and `tathā` could be weakened into a vague likeness.

The two uses of `sārdha` also operate at different numerical scales:

```text
sārdha-tri-yojana
    3 + ½ yojanas = 3½

sārdhaṃ tri-śata-yojanam
    300 + ½ hundred = 350 yojanas
```

The same modifier must be resolved through the unit and grouping it qualifies.

## 9. Philosophical and Organon Study

This small unit shows how a named form can function as a compressed semantic
result of structured measurements:

```text
ordered side values
    → geometrical configuration
        → shape predicate
```

For the Organon Dataset, “cart-shaped” and “half-moon-shaped” should therefore
not be stored as free-floating poetic labels. They belong with the side
measurements, the order of those sides, the region's position, and the source
voice that asserts the comparison.

The two continents also illustrate configuration through constrained
variation:

```text
shared feature-shape
    three sides of 2,000

permitted variation
    fourth-side measure

consequence
    distinct regional morphology
```

This is an Organon reconstruction, not Vasubandhu's formal vocabulary. It
preserves the text's exact achievement: sameness and difference are not
abstract opposites but indexed to particular features within one comparison.

The produced Loka is thereby differentiated without treating each region as
an unrelated item. A common schema makes the distinctive measure maximally
informative.

## 10. Review Status

This Study restores the complete VAK 3.54 unit at 161.16–161.24:
Jambūdvīpa's 3½-yojana fourth side and the explanation of its cart shape,
followed by Prāgvideha's eastern position, half-moon form, three 2,000-yojana
sides, and 350-yojana fourth side.

The Sanskrit and translation remain provisional. The geometrical meaning of
the cart and half-moon comparisons, the relation of the names `Prāgvideha`
and `Pūrva Videha`, and the exact force of `abhinirvṛtta` require comparison
with critical Sanskrit, Tibetan, Chinese, and established translations.

The Study stops before `Godānīyaḥ sahasrāṇi sapta sārdhāni`, the opening of
VAK 3.55. It does not begin the western circular continent or square Kuru.
