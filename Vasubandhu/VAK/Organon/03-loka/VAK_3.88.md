# VAK_3.88

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तेऽष्टौ योजनमित्याहुर्विंशं क्षणशतं पुनः ।
>
> तत्क्षणस्ते पुनः षष्टिर्लवस्त्रिंशद्गुणोत्तराः ॥ ३.८८ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> te 'ṣṭau yojanam ity āhur viṃśaṃ kṣaṇaśataṃ punaḥ /
>
> tatkṣaṇas te punaḥ ṣaṣṭir lavas triṃśadguṇottarāḥ // 3.88 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

```text
te                    → te
aṣṭau                 → 'ṣṭau (after avagraha)
yojanam               → yojanam
iti                   → ity (before ā-)
āhuḥ                  → āhur (before v-)
viṃśam śatam          → viṃśaṃ ... śatam
kṣaṇa-śatam           → kṣaṇaśatam
punaḥ                 → punaḥ
tat-kṣaṇaḥ            → tatkṣaṇas (before t-)
te                    → te
ṣaṣṭiḥ                → ṣaṣṭir (before l-)
lavaḥ                 → lavas (before t-)
triṃśat-guṇa-uttarāḥ  → triṃśadguṇottarāḥ
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| te | nominative masculine plural | those preceding units, the krośas |
| aṣṭau | cardinal numeral | eight |
| yojanam | nominative neuter singular | yojana, completed large spatial unit |
| ity āhuḥ | quotative plus third-person plural perfect | “so they say”; received measure-definition |
| viṃśaṃ śatam | compound numerical expression | one hundred and twenty, as fixed by the Bhāṣya |
| kṣaṇa | compound member | moment; minimal temporal unit established in 3.85 |
| tatkṣaṇaḥ | nominative masculine singular | tatkṣaṇa; unit enclosing 120 moments |
| ṣaṣṭiḥ | cardinal numeral | sixty |
| lavaḥ | nominative masculine singular | lava; unit enclosing 60 tatkṣaṇas |
| triṃśadguṇottarāḥ | nominative masculine plural | the following units proceed successively by a factor of thirty |

The Bhāṣya's prose gloss `kṣaṇānāṃ viṃśaṃ śatam` secures the idiomatic value
120. The final plural `triṃśadguṇottarāḥ` looks forward to the subsequent
time-units; it does not retroactively govern the factors 120 and 60.

## 4. Grammar

The opening completes the spatial ladder:

```text
te [krośāḥ] aṣṭau
    yojanam [bhavanti]
    iti āhuḥ
```

> Eight of those krośas are called one yojana.

The verse then initializes the temporal ladder:

```text
kṣaṇānāṃ viṃśaṃ śatam
    ekaḥ tatkṣaṇaḥ [bhavati]
```

> One hundred and twenty moments make one tatkṣaṇa.

```text
te [tatkṣaṇāḥ] punaḥ ṣaṣṭiḥ
    lavaḥ [ucyate]
```

> Sixty of those tatkṣaṇas are called one lava.

The final predicate supplies a forward recurrence:

```text
[uttarāḥ kāla-mānāḥ]
    triṃśad-guṇa-uttarāḥ [bhavanti]
```

> The following time-measures increase successively thirtyfold.

The Bhāṣya immediately identifies the first application as thirty lavas to one
muhūrta; the continuation belongs formally to 3.89.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Eight of those are said to make one yojana. Again, one hundred and twenty
> moments make one tatkṣaṇa; sixty of those make one lava; the following units
> are successively thirtyfold.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Eight krośas constitute one yojana, completing the spatial measure-series.
> The temporal series then begins: 120 moments constitute one tatkṣaṇa, and 60
> tatkṣaṇas constitute one lava. The next temporal units proceed by thirtyfold
> aggregation, beginning with 30 lavas to one muhūrta.

The interpretation of the compressed numeral as 120 and the forward scope of
the thirtyfold rule are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Having enclosed bodily and terrestrial extension within the yojana, the
> World System returns to the moment and constructs duration upward. Minimal
> occurrences are enclosed as a tatkṣaṇa, tatkṣaṇas as a lava, and subsequent
> units through a new aggregation ratio. Spatial and temporal extension are
> not treated as empty containers already given; each becomes operationally
> available through a typed hierarchy of resolution-changing enclosures.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Completion of spatial enclosure

```text
8 krośas = 1 yojana
```

From 3.87:

```text
1 krośa = 500 dhanus
```

Therefore:

```text
1 yojana = 8 × 500 = 4,000 dhanus
```

Using the complete atomic conversion derived through 3.86–3.87:

```text
1 yojana = 8 × 284,735,050,992,000
          = 2,277,880,407,936,000 ultimate atoms
```

This ultimate-atom total is a project derivation from the stated conversion
rules, not a number written by Vasubandhu.

### Initialization of temporal enclosure

Let `K` denote one moment, `T` one tatkṣaṇa, and `L` one lava:

```text
T = 120K
L = 60T
```

Therefore:

```text
L = 60 × 120K = 7,200K
```

The aggregation factors belong to their conversion edges:

| Lower unit | Factor | Higher unit |
|---|---:|---|
| kṣaṇa | 120 | tatkṣaṇa |
| tatkṣaṇa | 60 | lava |
| lava | 30 | muhūrta |

### Forward thirtyfold scope

The rule is not:

```text
every temporal conversion in 3.88 = ×30
```

It is:

```text
after lava:
    selected following units = ×30
```

The first consequence confirmed by the Bhāṣya is:

```text
1 muhūrta = 30 lavas = 216,000 moments
```

The next conversion to day-night is stated in the continuation and is treated
fully in 3.89.

### Parallel measure architecture

```text
spatial minimum
    → successive enclosures
        → yojana

temporal minimum
    → successive enclosures
        → ordinary calendar units
```

The two ladders share an architecture without asserting that spatial and
temporal units are mutually reducible.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.88 shows the Mind of the Enclosure switching domains without changing
its logical vocation:

```text
spatial enclosure:
    8 krośas count as 1 yojana

temporal enclosure:
    120 moments count as 1 tatkṣaṇa
    60 tatkṣaṇas count as 1 lava
```

The enclosure is not one more content among those enclosed. One yojana is not
a ninth krośa, and one lava is not a sixty-first tatkṣaṇa. The higher unit is
the resolution under which the lower plurality becomes operatively one.

This sharpens your statement that the `kṣaṇa` is “big enough” to home a
`vijñāna`. At the Organon level, the kṣaṇa is not empty homogeneous time. It is
the minimal temporal enclosure under which a differentiated cognition can be
stationed as one occurrence. The present verse then encloses such moments into
larger durations:

```text
vijñāna-occurrence
    → stationed under one kṣaṇa

120 kṣaṇas
    → stationed under one tatkṣaṇa

60 tatkṣaṇas
    → stationed under one lava
```

The contents multiply; the Mind of the Enclosure changes resolution while
preserving their ordered temporal placement. This is our Organon synthesis,
not an explicit claim in the kārikā that each kṣaṇa necessarily contains a
`vijñāna`.

The hinge from yojana to kṣaṇa is therefore conceptually elegant. The Kośa
finishes enclosing material Existence at a large spatial scale and immediately
returns to the quick simplicity of occurrence, from which mediated duration
is rebuilt.
