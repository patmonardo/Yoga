# VAK_3.86

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> लोहापशशाविगोच्छिद्ररजोलिक्षास्तदुद्भवाः ।
>
> यवस्तथाङ्गुलीपर्व ज्ञेयं सप्तगुणोत्तरम् ॥ ३.८६ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> lohāpaśaśāvigocchidrarajolikṣās tadudbhavāḥ /
>
> yavas tathāṅgulīparva jñeyaṃ saptaguṇottaram // 3.86 //

The first line is a highly compressed technical list. Its members are not
translated by conjecturally decomposing the metrical compound. The sequence
below follows the explicit forms and relations supplied by the Bhāṣya.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
loha-apa-śaśa-avi-go-cchidra-rajaḥ-likṣāḥ
                                      → lohāpaśaśāvigocchidrarajolikṣāḥ
tat-udbhavāḥ                         → tadudbhavāḥ
yavaḥ                                → yavas (before t-)
tathā                                → tathā
aṅgulī-parva                         → aṅgulīparva
jñeyam                               → jñeyaṃ
sapta-guṇa-uttaram                   → saptaguṇottaram
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| rajas | compound-final nominal member | named minute dust-measure in the compressed series |
| likṣāḥ | nominative feminine plural | likṣā units; stage preceding the yūkā |
| tadudbhavāḥ | nominative plural compound | those arising from them; explained as yūkās arising from likṣās |
| yavaḥ | nominative masculine singular | barley-grain measure |
| aṅgulīparva | nominative neuter singular | finger-joint measure |
| jñeyam | gerundive, nominative neuter singular | is to be understood |
| saptaguṇottaram | nominative neuter singular compound | successively sevenfold greater |

The Bhāṣya resolves the list operationally as `loharajas`, `avrajas`,
`dāśarajas`, `aiḍakarajas`, `gorajas`, `vātāyanacchidrarajas`, `likṣā`, and
`yūkā`. These technical source-forms are retained where their lexical history
is less secure than their place in the measure-series.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The verse continues the sequence begun by `paramāṇur aṇus tathā` at the end of
3.85. Its distributed predicate is:

```text
paramāṇu-aṇu-[named rajas measures]-likṣā-tadudbhava-yava-aṅgulīparva
    saptaguṇottaram
    jñeyam
```

> The series from the ultimate atom through the named dust-measures, likṣā,
> what arises from it, barley grain, and finger-joint is to be understood as
> successively sevenfold.

`tadudbhavāḥ` does not state that every later measure arises biologically from
the preceding one. The Bhāṣya assigns it locally:

```text
likṣāyāḥ udbhavā yūkā
```

> The yūkā is what arises from the likṣā.

The singular neuter `jñeyaṃ saptaguṇottaram` treats the complete ordered scale
collectively. The Bhāṣya then distributes the sevenfold rule pairwise across
every adjacent level.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> The named dust-measures, the likṣās and what arises from them, the barley
> grain, and the finger-joint are to be understood as forming a successively
> sevenfold series.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Beginning with the ultimate atom, each next material measure is constituted
> from seven of the preceding measure: seven ultimate atoms make one aṇu;
> seven aṇus one loharajas; and the same sevenfold aggregation proceeds through
> avrajas, dāśarajas, aiḍakarajas, gorajas, aperture-dust, likṣā, yūkā, barley
> grain, and finger-joint. Three finger-joints constitute one finger.

The exact technical members, the insertion of `yūkā` as what arises from a
`likṣā`, and the three-joints-to-one-finger relation are Bhāṣya
determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Material magnitude is constructed by recursive enclosure. Seven units at
> one resolution are constituted as one unit at the next resolution, and that
> new whole becomes available as the member of a still higher aggregation.
> The World does not leap from ultimate atom to embodied scale: it mediates
> immediacy through a repeated law by which plurality is enclosed, counted,
> and made operative as one.

The sequence is a metrology: its function is to establish convertible levels
of material measure. The technical dust-names need not be converted into
speculative zoological images for that function to remain exact.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Recursive enclosure rule

Let `U₀` be one ultimate atom and `Uₙ₊₁` the immediately higher measure:

```text
Uₙ₊₁ = Enclose(7 × Uₙ)
```

The relation has two resolutions:

```text
viewed from below:
    Uₙ₊₁ contains seven Uₙ units

viewed at its own level:
    Uₙ₊₁ counts as one unit
```

The higher unit is therefore not an additional eighth member. It is the new
measure under which the seven-member plurality is handled as one.

### Complete material scale

| n | Measure `Uₙ` | Composition | Ultimate atoms |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 0 | paramāṇu | base | 1 |
| 1 | aṇu | 7 paramāṇus | 7 |

| 8 | likṣā | 7 aperture-dust units | 5,764,801 |

| 10 | yava | 7 yūkās | 282,475,249 |
| 11 | finger-joint | 7 yavas | 1,977,326,743 |

The last column is an arithmetic consequence of repeated sevenfold
aggregation:

```text
magnitude(Uₙ) = 7ⁿ paramāṇus
```

### Additional bodily measure

The Bhāṣya adds:

```text
3 finger-joints = 1 finger
```

Therefore one finger corresponds, under this scale, to:

```text
3 × 7¹¹ = 5,931,980,229 ultimate atoms
```

This number is derived from the stated rules; it is not separately written in
the verse.

### Enclosure invariant

Every transition preserves the same structural law:

```text
many at resolution n
    → one at resolution n+1
        → member of a new many at resolution n+1
```

This is neither simple addition nor replacement. The lower units remain the
constituents of the higher measure, while the higher measure introduces a new
counting identity.

## 9. Interpretive Note

Your “Mind of the Enclosure, not the Enclosed” gives the right Organon contact
for this verse. The source supplies a sevenfold aggregation rule; our project
interpretation identifies its semantic form as recursive container logic:

```text
contents:
    seven units at the present resolution

enclosure:
    the higher measure under which they count as one

Mind of the Enclosure:
    the invariant rule that knows how plurality changes resolution
```

The “Mind” here is not an eighth particle hidden among the seven. It is the
operative form of their unity—the rule by which the enclosed plurality
becomes one determinate unit capable of entering the next operation.

This also clarifies the preceding relation between `kṣaṇa` and `vijñāna`.
The moment need not be imagined as one more content placed beside cognition.
It is the temporal enclosure-condition under which a differentiated cognition
can attain occurrence as one station. Analogously, the next material measure
is the quantitative enclosure under which seven lower units count as one.

The two structures are not asserted as identical Abhidharma doctrines, but
their Organon form corresponds:

```text
kṣaṇa : occurrent cognitive station
    ::
higher material unit : enclosed lower plurality
```

In the Hegelian-triguṇa language of our present inquiry, the `paramāṇu` is the
simple material limit; the sevenfold rule is Rajasic mediation; and the
resulting hierarchy is the gigantic articulated Existence into which that
minimal immediacy is built. The source supplies the metrology. The
Being–Essence–Existence articulation is our comparative reconstruction.
