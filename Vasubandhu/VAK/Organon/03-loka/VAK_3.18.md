# VAK_3.18

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> नात्मास्ति स्कन्धमात्रं तु क्लेशकर्माभिसंस्कृतम् ।
>
> अन्तराभवसंतत्या कुक्षिमेति प्रदीपवत् ॥ ३.१८ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> nātmāsti skandhamātraṃ tu kleśakarmābhisaṃskṛtam /
>
> antarābhavasaṃtatyā kukṣim eti pradīpavat // 3.18 //

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
na + ātmā + asti                 → nātmāsti
skandha-mātram                   → skandhamātram
tu                               → tu
kleśa-karma-abhisaṃskṛtam        → kleśakarmābhisaṃskṛtam
antarābhava-saṃtatyā             → antarābhavasaṃtatyā
kukṣim + eti                     → kukṣim eti
pradīpa-vat                      → pradīpavat
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| na | indeclinable negation | not |
| ātmā | nominative masculine singular | enduring individual principle asserted by the objector and denied here |
| asti | third-person singular present of √as | exists; is |
| skandhamātram | nominative neuter singular compound | aggregates only; the aggregate-series and nothing further at this locus |
| tu | contrastive particle | but; rather |
| kleśa | compound member | affliction |
| karma | compound member | action and its karmic efficacy |
| abhisaṃskṛtam | nominative neuter singular past participle | formed, prepared, conditioned |
| antarābhava-saṃtatyā | instrumental feminine singular compound | through the continuity of intermediate existence |
| kukṣim | accusative feminine singular | womb; uterine cavity |
| eti | third-person singular present of √i | goes to; enters |
| pradīpavat | comparative indeclinable | like a lamp or flame |

The syntax opposes the objector's proposed transition-principle to the positive
account: `na ... tu ...`—not the former, but the aggregate-series conditioned
by affliction and karma.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The opening is categorical:

```text
na ātmā asti
```

> No ātman exists.

In the argumentative setting, an externalist infers such a principle from the
language of a being passing through intermediate existence. Vasubandhu answers
with the positive subject of the remaining construction:

```text
skandha-mātram tu
    kleśa-karma-abhisaṃskṛtam
    antarābhava-saṃtatyā
    kukṣim eti
    pradīpavat
```

> Rather, the aggregate-series alone—formed by affliction and karma—enters the
> womb through the continuity of intermediate existence, like a flame.

`Kleśakarmābhisaṃskṛtam` agrees with the neuter singular
`skandhamātram`. The instrumental `antarābhavasaṃtatyā` states the means or
mediating continuity; `kukṣim` is the destination of `eti`.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> No ātman exists; rather, the aggregate-series alone, formed by affliction and
> karma, enters the womb through the continuity of intermediate existence,
> like a flame.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> The continuity from death through intermediate existence to rebirth does not
> require the individually persistent principle inferred by the externalist.
> What reaches the womb is the causally continuous aggregate-series, formed by
> affliction and karma and transmitted through intermediate existence as one
> flame conditions another.

The externalist inference and the interpretation of the flame as serial causal
continuity are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Rebirth-continuity is completely articulated at its own level by conditioned
> relations. Affliction and action form the aggregate-series; intermediate
> existence mediates its passage; the new embodied phase arises in dependence
> upon the preceding phase. The explanatory work belongs to this lawful
> process, not to an additional individual carried through it.

This translation states the local force of the argument. It does not convert
the mechanics of rebirth into a verdict about every possible meaning of
ultimate truth.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

| Sanskrit | Controlled rendering | Determination in this unit |
|---|---|---|
| nātmāsti | no ātman exists | categorical anti-adhyāsa warning directed against the externalist's inferred rebirth-principle |
| skandhamātra | aggregates only / aggregate-series alone | positive determination of what undergoes conditioned continuity here |
| tu | but / rather | turns from the rejected inference to the positive causal account |
| kleśa | affliction | formative condition of the aggregate-series |
| karma | action / karmic efficacy | formative and projective condition of the series |
| abhisaṃskṛta | formed / conditioned | describes the preparation of the series by affliction and action |
| antarābhava-saṃtati | continuity of intermediate existence | mediating series between death and womb-entry |
| kukṣi | womb / uterine cavity | birth-matrix reached by the conditioned series |
| pradīpavat | like a lamp or flame | model of serial ignition and causal transmission |

The source-level negation is registered as the anti-adhyāsa warning that
controls this argument. The study then proceeds with Vasubandhu's positive
determinations: aggregates, affliction, karma, intermediate continuity, and
womb-entry.

The research Bhāṣya treats this as the answer to an externalist inference from
the preceding transition-language. The working e-text remains non-critical;
no textual variant is silently resolved here.

## 8. Logical Determination

### The externalist inference

```text
Premise:
    a sentient continuity dies,
    traverses intermediate existence,
    and enters another birth

Externalist conclusion:
    an individually persistent principle
    must perform the traversal
```

Vasubandhu rejects the inference because the transition has already received a
sufficient causal articulation.

### Positive transition account

```text
kleśa + karma
    form and project
        ↓
skandha-series
        ↓
antarābhava-saṃtati
        ↓
womb-entry
        ↓
new embodied aggregate-phase
```

The conclusion is not discontinuity. It is more highly determined continuity.

### Force of `skandhamātra`

At the rebirth locus:

```text
required explanatory terms
    = skandha
      + kleśa
      + karma
      + saṃtati
      + birth-conditions

additional individual transition-principle
    contributes no required causal operation
```

This is the Buddhist victory. The empirical or cosmological proprietor is not
needed to explain moral continuity, transition, or embodiment.

### Flame-model

```text
flame₁
    conditions flame₂
        conditions flame₃

serial dependence
    preserves continuity of ignition
    through successive flame-events
```

The relevant likeness is causal succession. The detailed physics of flame is
not itself the doctrinal conclusion.

### Limit of the demonstrated conclusion

This verse establishes causal sufficiency for the rebirth-process:

```text
World-process explained
    without an added individual transition-principle
```

It does not analyze:

```text
nirodha-samāpatti
the unconditioned determinations in dharmadhātu
the Pāramārthika comprehension of the World System
```

Those questions cannot be decided merely by showing that an empirical
traveler is absent from the rebirth mechanism.

## 9. Interpretive Note

### Round 1: Vasubandhu lands the punch

The Buddhist argument should be granted without qualification at its proper
target. Continuity does not force us to insert an individualized cosmic
proprietor. The first seventeen verses have already constructed a remarkably
complete transition system; 3.18 now refuses the redundant addition.

```text
gati
yoni
ākṣepa
antarābhava
karmarddhi
viparyaya
garbhāvakrānti
skandha-saṃtati
```

This is a profound victory over adhyāsa. It protects Buddhist practice from
the caricature that a practitioner must become a null or an NPC. What is
defeated is the counterfeit empirical proprietor, not agency, ethical
formation, cultivated clarity, achieved knowing, or liberation.

### The bell has not ended the match

Our counter-position does not reinsert Ātman into the transition diagram. It
asks a different question:

```text
When appropriative and constructed operation is stilled,
what is disclosed through nirodha-samāpatti
in relation to the unconditioned in dharmadhātu?
```

The project answer is:

```text
Ātman
    is not an empirical rebirth-carrier
    is not one item in the World System
    is the One True Thing disclosed
      when adhyāsa and constructed cognition cease
```

That is our Śaṅkara-guided philosophical claim, not Vasubandhu's statement.
It neither repairs nor cancels the Buddhist causal account. It denies that
causal completeness within the World System is identical with ultimate
comprehension of that World System.

### `Jagan mithyā`

VAK 3.18 may therefore be accepted as the exact Technē of rebirth within
appearance:

```text
skandha-series
    causally real within World
    morally formed
    capable of continuity and liberation

World System
    cannot by those operations alone
    establish itself as ultimate truth
```

`Jagan mithyā` does not make the Kośa's analysis false or useless. It places
the entire causal architecture within a truth that comprehends and sublates
it. Pāramārthika is not another destination above the devas; it encloses the
whole field in which destination, continuity, birth, and death operate.

### Round 1 decision

```text
Against the transmigrating empirical proprietor:
    Vasubandhu wins decisively.

Against Ātman as the One True Thing:
    no contact has yet been made,
    because that contender was never placed
    inside the rebirth mechanism.
```

The disciplined result is not a compromise. It is a distinction of levels:
the Kośa gives the victorious causal analysis of the World System; the Organon
asks what becomes Known when that complete World System is itself sublated.
