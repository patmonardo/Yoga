# VAK_3.24

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> स भविष्यत् भवफलं कुरुते कर्म तद् भवः ।
>
> प्रतिसंधिः पुनर्जातिः जरामरणमा विदः ॥ ३.२४ ॥

The Devanāgarī preserves the supplied witness. Its final `मा विदः` is
textually uncertain; the Bhāṣya unambiguously interprets the phrase as “up to
the feeling-stage.”

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> sa bhaviṣyat bhavaphalaṃ kurute karma tad bhavaḥ /
>
> pratisaṃdhiḥ punar jātiḥ jarāmaraṇam ā vidaḥ // 3.24 //

The transmitted `ā vidaḥ` is likely corrupt. The prose gloss
`yāvad vedanāvasthā` strongly suggests a form meaning “up to feeling,” perhaps
related to the preceding Kārikā's `vitti`; no silent emendation is adopted in
the Sanskrit witness.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
saḥ                     → sa
bhaviṣyat               → bhaviṣyat
bhava-phalam            → bhavaphalam
kurute                  → kurute
karma                   → karma

prati-saṃdhiḥ           → pratisaṃdhiḥ
punaḥ                   → punar
jātiḥ                   → jātiḥ
jarā-maraṇam            → jarāmaraṇam
ā vidaḥ                 → ā vidaḥ [uncertain witness]
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| saḥ | nominative masculine singular pronoun | the one pursuing objects in 3.23 |
| bhaviṣyat | future active participial form used in compound relation | future; forthcoming |
| bhavaphalam | accusative neuter singular compound | renewed existence as result |
| kurute | third-person singular middle present of √kṛ | makes; performs; produces |
| karma | accusative neuter singular | karmic action accumulated through pursuit |
| tat | nominative neuter singular pronoun | that karma |
| bhavaḥ | nominative masculine singular | becoming; here, karma productive of renewed existence |
| pratisaṃdhiḥ | nominative masculine singular | rebirth-junction; renewed conjunction |
| punaḥ | indeclinable | again; in turn |
| jātiḥ | nominative feminine singular | birth; future rebirth-junction viewed as result |
| jarāmaraṇam | nominative neuter singular compound | aging-and-death; subsequent future-life development through feeling |
| ā vidaḥ | uncertain transmitted expression | interpreted by the Bhāṣya as “up to the feeling-stage” |

`Bhaviṣyad-bhava-phala` is the result produced by the karma, while the Bhāṣya
identifies the `bhava` member with the renewal-producing karma itself.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The first sentence continues the agent of 3.23:

```text
saḥ
    bhaviṣyad-bhava-phalaṃ karma
    kurute

tat [karma]
    bhavaḥ [ucyate]
```

> He produces karma whose result is future existence; that [karma] is called
> becoming.

The second half defines the final two members:

```text
pratisaṃdhiḥ
    punaḥ jātiḥ

tat ūrdhvam
    jarāmaraṇam
    [ā vedanā-avasthāyāḥ]
```

> Rebirth-junction, in turn, is birth; what follows through the feeling-stage
> is aging-and-death.

The bracketed limit is supplied by the Bhāṣya to resolve the uncertain final
expression.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> He produces karma whose result is future existence; that is becoming.
> Rebirth-junction, in turn, is birth; aging-and-death [extends] up to the
> feeling-stage.

The final phrase is translated through the Bhāṣya because the transmitted
Kārikā reading is not independently secure.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Running about to obtain desired objects, one accumulates karma productive
> of renewed existence; this renewal-producing karma is designated
> **becoming**. When, after death from the present life, that karma produces a
> future rebirth-junction, the junction is designated **birth**. The ensuing
> future-life development—from name-and-form through the stage of feeling—is
> gathered prospectively under **aging-and-death**.

Renewal-producing karma, the perspectival equivalence of rebirth-junction and
birth, and the range gathered under aging-and-death are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Appropriative pursuit does not terminate in possession. It accumulates a
> projective power whose result is another embodied course. What the present
> analysis calls becoming becomes the condition of a future junction; what is
> called differentiated cognition at the junction of this life is called
> birth when the same structural position is viewed as the future result; and
> the developing stages of that future embodiment are gathered as
> aging-and-death. The middle thereby produces the subsequent limit.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

| Sanskrit | Controlled rendering | Determination in this unit |
|---|---|---|
| bhaviṣyad-bhava-phala | future-existence result | renewed existence produced as the maturation of present karma |
| paunarbhavika karma | karma productive of renewed existence | Bhāṣya's precise determination of the `bhava` member |
| karma-upacaya | accumulation of karma | practical mediation from appropriation to becoming |
| bhava | becoming | renewal-producing karma, not primarily the future existence-result itself |
| pracyuti | falling away / death from here | departure from the present life before future junction |
| āyati | future time | temporal field in which reconnection occurs |
| pratisaṃdhi | rebirth-junction | renewed conjunction produced by karmic projection |
| jāti | birth | future junction viewed from the present causal series |
| vijñānāṅga | differentiated-cognition member | name of the corresponding junction-stage when analyzed as present |
| jarāmaraṇa | aging-and-death | prospective gathering of post-birth development through feeling |
| vedanāvasthā | feeling-stage | endpoint of the development gathered here under aging-and-death |

The crucial equivalences supplied by the Bhāṣya are:

```text
present-life analysis:
    junction = vijñāna

future-result analysis:
    corresponding junction = jāti

present-life detailed analysis:
    nāmarūpa + ṣaḍāyatana + sparśa + vedanā

future-result compression:
    the corresponding developmental stretch = jarāmaraṇa
```

## 8. Logical Determination

### Appropriation becomes projection

```text
upādāna
    object pursued for acquisition

        ↓ activity accumulates karma

bhava
    renewal-producing karmic power

        ↓ after falling away from this life

pratisaṃdhi / jāti
    future rebirth-junction

        ↓ develops through

jarāmaraṇa
    future embodied course through feeling
```

The practical field constructed in 3.23 therefore regenerates the ontological
conditions of another practical field.

### The member-names are indexical

The same structural position receives different names according to its place
within the explanatory articulation:

```text
viewed as this life's initial middle:
    rebirth-junction = vijñāna

viewed from this life as future result:
    rebirth-junction = jāti
```

Likewise, four stages distinguished within the present middle are compressed
into one future-result member:

```text
nāmarūpa → ṣaḍāyatana → sparśa → vedanā
    =
jarāmaraṇa when viewed prospectively
```

The twelve members are therefore not twelve self-standing objects occupying
one invariant temporal scale. They are determinations of one conditioned
course according to position, scale, and explanatory function.

### Completion of the 2 / 8 / 2 structure

```text
Prior limit:
    avidyā, saṃskāra

Present middle:
    vijñāna, nāmarūpa, ṣaḍāyatana, sparśa,
    vedanā, tṛṣṇā, upādāna, bhava

Subsequent limit:
    jāti, jarāmaraṇa
```

The condensed enumeration is now complete. The governing principle of its
stage-designations will be stated explicitly in 3.25.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.24 reveals why the Middle is the productive Dharma of the World System.
The present middle does not simply mediate a past and future that already
exist. Through appropriation and karma, it **produces the future as a
determinate destination of the present course**.

```text
object pursued
    → karma accumulated
    → future junction projected
    → embodied World renewed
```

This also deepens the determination that the World is established in Me. The
object pursued in the present is not merely one content among others. Through
the action organized around it, the object participates in configuring the
future World in which differentiated cognition will again obtain footing.
The object-field becomes destiny through karma.

The perspectival identity `vijñāna here = jāti there` is especially powerful
for Yogācāra assimilation. The members do not name metaphysically sealed
pieces. A single transition is differentiated according to the standpoint
from which the World System comprehends its function. Likewise,
`jarāmaraṇa` is not merely the last biological episode of old age followed by
death; within this three-life presentation it gathers the already developing
future course through feeling.

Your expectation about the exposition is therefore confirmed with one
adjustment. VAK 3.24 completes the condensed component-list. The Bhāṣya then
begins reflecting on its principle, and 3.25 formally states it: the intended
series is stage-based, with each complex five-aggregate stage named according
to its predominant determination. We stop here so that principle can receive
its own complete study.
