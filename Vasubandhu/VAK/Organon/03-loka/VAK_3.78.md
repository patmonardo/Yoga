# VAK_3.78

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> सहस्रमायुः कुरुषु द्वयोरर्धार्धवर्जितम् ।
>
> इहानियतमन्ते तु दशाब्दा आदितोऽमितम् ॥ ३.७८ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> sahasram āyuḥ kuruṣu dvayor ardhārdhavarjitam /
>
> ihāniyatam ante tu daśābdā ādito 'mitam // 3.78 //

The opening is construed as `sahasram āyuḥ`, “lifespan is one thousand,” in
accordance with the Bhāṣya's segmented citation and explanation. This removes
an unstable vowel division in the immediate metrical witness without changing
the verse's meaning.

## 3. Lexical Analysis

```text
sahasram            → sahasram
āyuḥ                 → āyuḥ
kuruṣu               → kuruṣu
dvayoḥ               → dvayor (before a-)
ardha-ardha-varjitam  → ardhārdhavarjitam
iha                  → iha
aniyatam             → aniyatam
ante                 → ante
tu                   → tu
daśa-abdāḥ           → daśābdāḥ
āditaḥ               → ādito (before a-)
amitam               → 'mitam (after avagraha)
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| sahasram | cardinal numeral, neuter singular | one thousand years |
| āyuḥ | nominative neuter singular | lifespan; duration of embodied life |
| kuruṣu | locative masculine plural | among the Kurus; in Uttarakuru |
| dvayoḥ | genitive/locative dual | for the other two fixed-lifespan continents |
| ardhārdhavarjitam | nominative neuter singular compound agreeing with `āyuḥ` | reduced successively by one half |
| iha | indeclinable deictic | here, in Jambūdvīpa |
| aniyatam | nominative neuter singular | not fixed, not governed by one determinate quantity |
| ante | locative masculine singular | at the terminal point of decline |
| tu | particle | but; marks a qualified limiting case |
| daśābdāḥ | nominative masculine plural measure expression | ten years |
| āditaḥ | ablatival adverb | from the beginning; at the kalpa's beginning |
| amitam | nominative neuter singular | immeasurable, not numerically delimited |

The Bhāṣya identifies the dual `dvayoḥ` as Godānīya and Pūrvavideha. It also
glosses `amitam` with `aparimāṇam`: the initial lifespan is not merely large
but unavailable to numerical determination.

## 4. Grammar

The first clause fixes the Uttarakuru lifespan:

```text
kuruṣu
    āyuḥ
    sahasram [varṣāṇām asti]
```

> Among the Kurus, lifespan is one thousand years.

The dual clause derives two further fixed values:

```text
dvayoḥ [dvīpayoḥ]
    āyuḥ
    ardha-ardha-varjitam
```

> In the two continents, lifespan is successively diminished by one half.

The Bhāṣya distributes the operation in the order Godānīya, then
Pūrvavideha: 1,000 becomes 500, and 500 becomes 250.

The second line changes the type of temporal determination:

```text
iha [jambūdvīpe]
    āyuḥ aniyatam

ante tu
    daśa-abdāḥ

āditaḥ
    amitam
```

> Here, in Jambūdvīpa, lifespan is not fixed: at the end it is ten years, but
> at the beginning it is immeasurable.

`ante` and `āditaḥ` do not state the ordinary maximum and minimum of every
individual life. The Bhāṣya locates them at opposite phases of the kalpa:
terminal decline and the first human generation.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Among the Kurus lifespan is one thousand years; in the other two continents
> it is successively reduced by one half. Here it is not fixed: at the end it
> is ten years, while at the beginning it is immeasurable.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Human lifespan is 1,000 years in Uttarakuru, 500 years in Godānīya, and 250
> years in Pūrvavideha. Here in Jambūdvīpa no single lifespan is fixed: as the
> kalpa declines it eventually reaches ten years, whereas the first humans of
> the kalpa have an immeasurable lifespan, one that cannot be expressed even
> by a numerical reckoning beginning in thousands.

The named assignment of 500 and 250 years, the variability formula “sometimes
greater, sometimes less,” and the kalpic subjects of the two extrema are
Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The World System now measures embodiment temporally. Three human domains
> assign life a fixed duration, ordered through successive halving.
> Jambūdvīpa, however, is determined through variability itself: its duration
> cannot be represented by one invariant value because it changes with the
> historical phase of the World. Its extreme contraction and its initial
> immeasurability are not arbitrary anomalies; together they define a human
> field whose temporal parameter belongs to a process.

| Sanskrit | Controlled rendering | Determination in this unit |

| āyus | lifespan | temporal duration assigned to embodied existence |
| āyuṣo niyama | fixed determination of lifespan | Bhāṣya criterion present in three continents and absent in Jambūdvīpa |
| Kuru | Kuru / Uttarakuru | human domain with a fixed 1,000-year lifespan |
| Godānīya | Godānīya | human domain with a fixed 500-year lifespan |
| Pūrvavideha | Pūrvavideha | human domain with a fixed 250-year lifespan |
| iha | here / Jambūdvīpa | deictic identification of the variable human domain |
| aniyata | unfixed / variable | absence of one invariant lifespan value |
| hīyamāna | undergoing decline | Bhāṣya term for the process reaching ten years |
| prāthamakalpika | belonging to the first kalpa-period | the first humans whose lifespan is immeasurable |
| amita / aparimāṇa | immeasurable | not expressible through an ordinary numerical scale |
| abda / saṃvatsara | year | unit used for the fixed lifespans and terminal minimum |

`aniyata` does not mean that lifespan is causally unconditioned. It means that
Jambūdvīpa lacks the one fixed numerical duration assigned to the other three
continents.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Fixed continental parameters

Let the Uttarakuru lifespan be the base:

```text
U = 1000 years
```

The dual clause applies successive halving:

```text
Godānīya    = U ÷ 2 = 500 years
Pūrvavideha = U ÷ 4 = 250 years
```

| Human domain | Lifespan rule | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| Uttarakuru | fixed base | 1,000 years |
| Godānīya | first halving | 500 years |
| Pūrvavideha | second halving | 250 years |

### Jambūdvīpa as variable process

Jambūdvīpa is not assigned a fourth constant:

```text
JambūdvīpaLifespan ≠ one fixed number
```

Instead:

```text
phase = first humans of the kalpa
    → lifespan = immeasurable

phase = terminal decline
    → lifespan = 10 years

intervening phases
    → lifespan sometimes greater, sometimes less
```

The extremes delimit a temporal process; they should not be converted into a
simple closed interval of individual possibilities without the kalpic phase
index.

### Change of measured predicate

```text
3.75–3.77:
    world-station → magnitude of bodily support

3.78:
    world-station and world-phase → duration of embodied life
```

Thus the carrier remains embodied existence, while the predicate changes from
spatial magnitude to temporal duration.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.78 confirms that the so-called Physical World is already an agential
system. Loka does not merely contain bodies; it determines how long a class of
embodied agents can operate under a given domain and phase. The relevant
structure is therefore not bare chronology but **world-indexed temporal
affordance**:

```text
domain
    → selects fixed or variable temporal schema

world-phase
    → if the schema is variable, determines its present value

lifespan
    → bounds the duration of embodied agency
```

The semantic distinction between `niyata` and `aniyata` is crucial. A shallow
syntax would treat all four continents as rows in one table, with the fourth
row merely missing a number. The Kośa gives Jambūdvīpa a different logical
form. Its governing determination is variability across the arising and
decline of a World. The absent constant is positive information about the
domain, not an omission from the catalogue.

The verse also introduces a precise relation between World and history.
Jambūdvīpa's “here” is not just a spatial coordinate; it is a field whose
conditions vary with cosmic time. Spatial station and temporal phase therefore
reciprocally determine the concrete lifespan available to an embodied agent.

```ts
type HumanLifespanSchema =
  | { kind: "Fixed"; continent: "Uttarakuru"; years: 1000 }
  | { kind: "Fixed"; continent: "Godaniya"; years: 500 }
  | { kind: "Fixed"; continent: "Purvavideha"; years: 250 }
  | {
      kind: "PhaseDependent";
      continent: "Jambudvipa";
      initial: "Immeasurable";
      terminalYears: 10;
    };
```

This is explanatory project notation, not Vasubandhu's terminology. Its union
type preserves the categorical difference between a fixed parameter and a
phase-dependent rule.
