# VAK_3.80

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> द्विगुणोत्तरमूर्ध्वानामुभयं रूपिणां पुनः ।
>
> नास्त्यहोरात्रमायुस्तु कल्पैः स्वाश्रयसंमितैः ॥ ३.८० ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> dviguṇottaram ūrdhvānām ubhayaṃ rūpiṇāṃ punaḥ /
>
> nāsty ahorātram āyus tu kalpaiḥ svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ // 3.80 //

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
dvi-guṇa-uttaram        → dviguṇottaram
ūrdhvānām               → ūrdhvānām
ubhayam                 → ubhayaṃ
rūpiṇām                 → rūpiṇāṃ
punaḥ                   → punaḥ
na asti                 → nāsti
ahas-rātram             → ahorātram
āyuḥ                    → āyus (before t-)
tu                      → tu
kalpaiḥ                 → kalpaiḥ
sva-āśraya-saṃmitaiḥ    → svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| dviguṇottaram | nominative neuter singular compound agreeing with `ubhayam` | successively twofold at each higher station |
| ūrdhvānām | genitive masculine plural | of the higher desire-realm devas |
| ubhayam | nominative neuter singular collective | both temporal measures together |
| rūpiṇām | genitive masculine plural | of the form-realm devas |
| punaḥ | indeclinable | in turn; introduces the new realm case |
| nāsti | negative existential | there is no |
| ahorātram | nominative neuter singular | day-and-night as a calendar unit |
| āyuḥ | nominative neuter singular | lifespan |
| tu | particle | but; contrasts absent day-night with persisting lifespan |
| kalpaiḥ | instrumental masculine plural | by kalpas; in kalpa units |
| svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ | instrumental masculine plural compound qualifying `kalpaiḥ` | commensurate with their own bodily support |

The Bhāṣya resolves `ubhayam` as `ahorātraś ca āyuś ca`: both the day-night
conversion and the lifespan. It also renders `svāśrayasaṃmita` mathematically
exact rather than loosely comparative.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The first construction continues the kāma-deva sequence announced after 3.79:

```text
ūrdhvānām [kāma-devānām]
    ubhayam
    dvi-guṇa-uttaram [bhavati]
```

> For the higher desire-realm devas, both measures increase successively
> twofold.

The collective singular `ubhayam` takes the singular predicate
`dviguṇottaram`. The two measures are not named within this half-verse; their
identity comes from the preceding verse and is confirmed by the Bhāṣya:

```text
1. human years corresponding to one deva day-night
2. deva years constituting the lifespan
```

The second construction changes temporal regime:

```text
rūpiṇāṃ punaḥ
    ahorātraṃ na asti

āyuḥ tu
    kalpaiḥ
    sva-āśraya-saṃmitaiḥ [asti]
```

> For the form-realm devas, in turn, there is no day-and-night; but lifespan
> is measured in kalpas commensurate with their own bodily support.

The contrastive `tu` prevents the absence of day-and-night from being mistaken
for an absence of temporal duration. Lifespan persists, but its measurement no
longer passes through a day/month/year calendar.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> For the higher devas, both measures are successively doubled. For the
> form-realm devas, in turn, there is no day-and-night; but their lifespan is
> in kalpas commensurate with their own bodily support.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> At each higher desire-realm deva station, both the human duration
> corresponding to one divine day-and-night and the number of divine years in
> the lifespan are doubled. Form-realm devas, however, have no day-and-night.
> Their lifespan is measured directly in kalpas, with the number of kalpas
> equal to the number of yojanas assigned to their bodily support—from one-half
> yojana and one-half kalpa through 16,000 yojanas and 16,000 kalpas at
> Akaniṣṭha.

The two members of `ubhayam`, the complete desire-realm sequence, and the
exact yojana-to-kalpa commensurability are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Higher desire-realm stations preserve one calendar-form while scaling two
> of its parameters together: the magnitude of the day and the number of years
> lived. The form realm introduces a stronger transformation. Daily time is no
> longer operative, yet duration remains exactly measurable through a direct
> correspondence between embodiment and lifespan. A change of World-domain
> can therefore modify either the scale of a clock or the very form by which
> temporal determination is organized.

`nāsty ahorātram` denies the day-night unit for form-realm devas. It does not
deny sequence, duration, lifespan, or temporal determination as such.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Coupled doubling in kāmadhātu

Let each station carry a pair:

```text
Tₙ = <Hₙ, Lₙ>

Hₙ = human years per deva day-night
Lₙ = deva years per lifespan
```

The recurrence is:

```text
Tₙ₊₁ = <2Hₙ, 2Lₙ>
```

| Deva class | Human years per day-night | Lifespan in divine years |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cāturmahārājakāyika | 50 | 500 |
| Trāyastriṃśa | 100 | 1,000 |
| Yāma | 200 | 2,000 |
| Tuṣita | 400 | 4,000 |
| Nirmāṇarati | 800 | 8,000 |
| Paranirmitavaśavartin | 1,600 | 16,000 |

The two components double independently but remain coupled to the same realm
transition. Consequently, conversion into human years would grow by a factor
of four at each station, because both the length of the divine day and the
number of divine years double.

### Temporal signs without astronomy

The Bhāṣya raises a local problem:

```text
above Yugandhara
    → no sun and moon
    → no astronomical marker of day and night
```

Its answer preserves temporal differentiation through recurrent signs:

```text
flowers contract / open
birds cease / resume calling
sleepiness arrives / departs
```

Illumination is separately accounted for by the devas' self-luminosity. Thus
the absence of the ordinary marker does not entail the absence of the marked
temporal distinction within kāmadhātu.

### Change of temporal schema in rūpadhātu

For form-realm devas:

```text
day-night calendar = not applicable
lifespan unit       = kalpa
```

The commensurability rule is:

```text
if bodily support = x yojanas,
then lifespan      = x kalpas
```

Examples explicitly supplied by the Bhāṣya are:

```text
0.5 yojana → 0.5 kalpa
1 yojana   → 1 kalpa
16,000 yojanas at Akaniṣṭha → 16,000 kalpas
```

This is numerical isomorphism between two differently typed magnitudes, not
identity between body and lifespan.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.80 offers a remarkably systematic theory of realm-specific time. It
distinguishes three levels that a weaker representation would conflate:

```text
temporal markers
    → phenomena through which a phase is recognized

temporal units
    → day-night, year, or kalpa used in measurement

temporal duration
    → the lifespan measured through the applicable units
```

The higher kāma-deva case shows that a clock can survive the loss of its usual
astronomical implementation. Sun and moon disappear, but day and night remain
discriminable through floral, animal, and experiential rhythms. The World
System changes the sensor-array through which temporal phase becomes
available without yet changing the calendar's logical form.

The form realm crosses the stronger boundary. Here day-night is not merely
detected differently; it is absent from the lifespan schema. Duration is
instead coupled directly to embodied support. The semantic copula in the
commensurability rule must preserve both identity and difference:

```text
x yojanas of bodily support
    ↔ x kalpas of lifespan

same numerical magnitude
    ≠ same kind of quantity
```

This is an exact typed correspondence. Embodiment determines duration by
supplying its numerical index; duration reciprocally gives the embodied
station its temporal reach. The Physical World is therefore already
agentially organized: it supplies rules, sensors, units, conversions, and
typed correspondences by which a being's world can be practically known.

```ts
type TemporalRegime =
  | {
      kind: "CalendarTime";
      realm: "Kama";
      humanYearsPerDayNight: number;
      lifespanInDivineYears: number;
    }
  | {
      kind: "SupportCommensurateTime";
      realm: "Rupa";
      bodyYojanas: number;
      lifespanKalpas: number;
    };
```

This is explanatory project notation, not Vasubandhu's vocabulary. It forbids
the kāma-deva calendar operations from being applied after the transition to
the form-realm schema.
