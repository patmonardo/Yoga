# VAK_3.26

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> क्लेशास्त्रीणि द्वयं कर्म सप्त वस्तु फलं तथा ।
>
> फलहेत्वभिसंक्षेपो द्वयोर्मध्यानुमानतः ॥ ३.२६ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> kleśās trīṇi dvayaṃ karma sapta vastu phalaṃ tathā /
>
> phalahetvabhisaṃkṣepo dvayor madhyānumānataḥ // 3.26 //

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| kleśāḥ | nominative masculine plural | afflictions; functional nature of three members |
| trīṇi | nominative neuter plural numeral | three members |
| dvayam | nominative neuter singular collective | a pair; two members |
| karma | nominative neuter singular | karma; functional nature of two members |
| sapta | nominative plural numeral | seven members |
| vastu | nominative neuter singular collective/predicate | conditioned basis or support; not substance here |

| tathā | indeclinable | likewise; in the same way |

| madhyānumānataḥ | ablative compound | because of inference from the middle; by middle-based inference |

The Bhāṣya explicitly explains `vastu` through `kleśa-karmāśrayatva`: the
seven are the support of affliction and karma. The same seven are `phala`
when determined as products of prior causes.

## 4. Grammar

The first half gives three functional classifications:

```text
trīṇi [aṅgāni]
    kleśāḥ

dvayam [aṅgānām]
    karma

sapta [aṅgāni]
    vastu
    tathā phalam
```

> Three members are afflictions; two are karma; seven are conditioned
> supports and likewise results.

The second half explains the compressed presentation of the two limits:

```text
dvayoḥ [pūrvāntāparāntayoḥ]
    phala-hetu-abhisaṃkṣepaḥ
    madhya-anumānataḥ
```

> Cause and result are compressed at the two limits because they can be
> inferred from the middle.

The Bhāṣya specifies that past cause and future result need not be expanded a
second time because their full structure is reconstructible from the present
middle.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Three are afflictions, two are karma, and seven are conditioned supports and
> likewise results. Cause and result are compressed at the two limits because
> of inference from the middle.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Ignorance, craving, and appropriation have the functional nature of
> **affliction**; formations and becoming have the functional nature of
> **karma**. Differentiated cognition, name-and-form, the six spheres,
> contact, feeling, birth, and aging-and-death are conditioned **supports** of
> affliction and karma, and those same seven are **results** of prior causes.
> The past cause and future result are presented compactly because their
> expanded structures can be inferred from the fully articulated present
> middle.

The member-assignments, the `āśraya` function of `vastu`, and the reason for
compression are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The twelve-stage aggregate is now comprehended through three functional
> natures: affliction supplies distorted orientation, karma gives that
> orientation projective efficacy, and the resulting embodied structure
> supplies the ground on which affliction and action can operate again. Result
> becomes support; support becomes the field of renewed cause. Because this
> recursion is manifest in the middle, Reason can infer both its inherited
> ground and its projected consequence.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Result becomes support for renewed cause

```text
kleśa
    supplies afflictive orientation

        ↓ generates through

karma
    projective action

        ↓ produces

phala
    conditioned embodied result

        ↓ functions as

vastu / āśraya
    support for renewed kleśa and karma
```

The same seven members have a double determination:

```text
relative to prior conditions:
    phala

relative to renewed activity:
    vastu / āśraya
```

This is the internal reproductive logic of the World System. A result does
not remain inert; it becomes the Ground–Condition for another causal cycle.

### Two architectures intersect

The temporal view gives:

```text
past cause — 2
present middle — 8
future result — 2
```

The functional view gives:

```text
affliction — 3
karma — 2
support/result — 7
```

These are not rival classifications. The temporal View answers where a stage
stands in the three-life articulation; the functional View answers what role
it performs in causal reproduction.

### The middle licenses inference

The present middle contains both sides of the conditional structure:

```text
inherited result:
    vijñāna → nāmarūpa → ṣaḍāyatana → sparśa → vedanā

renewed cause:
    tṛṣṇā → upādāna → bhava
```

Therefore:

```text
analyze the manifest middle
    → infer the corresponding prior cause
    → infer the corresponding future result
```

In our logical register:

```text
If kleśa and karma obtain,
then a corresponding phala / vastu arises.

If this middle exhibits the structure of a conditioned result,
its adequate prior conditions are inferable.

If this middle generates renewed kleśa and karma,
their corresponding future result is inferable.
```

The Truth of Ground–Condition is therefore expressed through hypothetical
inference, while `madhyānumāna` supplies its empirical footing.

## 9. Interpretive Note

This verse gives remarkably direct support to our long-standing architecture:

```text
hetu
    productive ground

phala
    conditioned result

āśraya
    footing on which renewed determination operates

anumāna
    inferential comprehension of their relation
```

`Vastu` is especially important. It must not be translated here as an inert
substance or merely as a “real thing.” The Bhāṣya defines its role: it is the
support of affliction and karma. Yet the same support is the result of prior
affliction and karma. Thus Ground and consequence exchange functional place
across the cycle:

```text
prior cause
    → present result
    → present support
    → renewed cause
    → future result
```

This is why the Kośa has more skin in the game than a general statement that
action has consequences. It identifies the exact members functioning as
cause, the members functioning as result, and the conversion through which a
result becomes the platform of further action.

The middle is simultaneously ontological and epistemic:

```text
ontological middle:
    inherited result becomes renewed cause

epistemic middle:
    the hidden two limits become rationally inferable
```

For our Yogācāra assimilation, the World System established in Me is the
manifest middle from which its invisible temporal horizons can be
reconstructed. The prior limit need not be remembered as a biography, nor the
future limit seen as a spectacle. Their law is present in the structured
conditionality of the middle.

This is Post-Ascension Dhyāna in a newly exact form: not travel to the unseen
limits, but inferential comprehension of them through the Ground–Condition
structure presently manifest. Vasubandhu has turned life and death into a
logical science without evacuating their concrete Technē.
