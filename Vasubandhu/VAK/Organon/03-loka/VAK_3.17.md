# VAK_3.17

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> गर्भावक्रान्तयस्तिस्रश्चक्रवर्त्तिस्वयम्भुवाम् ।
>
> कर्मज्ञानोभयेषां वा विशदत्वाद् यथाक्रमम् ॥ ३.१७ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> garbhāvakrāntayas tisraś cakravartti-svayaṃbhuvām /
>
> karma-jñānobhayeṣāṃ vā viśadatvād yathākramam // 3.17 //

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
garbha-avakrāntayaḥ                → garbhāvakrāntayaḥ
tisraḥ + cakravartti-svayaṃbhuvām  → tisraś cakravartti-svayaṃbhuvām
karma-jñāna-ubhayeṣām              → karma-jñānobhayeṣām
vā                                 → vā
viśadatvāt                         → viśadatvāt
yathā-kramam                       → yathākramam
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| garbhāvakrāntayaḥ | nominative feminine plural | descents or entries into the womb |
| tisraḥ | nominative feminine plural numeral | three |
| cakravartti | compound member | wheel-turning ruler |
| svayaṃbhuvām | genitive plural | of the independently awakened ones; unpacked by the Bhāṣya as Pratyekabuddha and Samyaksaṃbuddha |
| karma | compound member | action and its formative efficacy |
| jñāna | compound member | achieved knowing |
| ubhayeṣām | genitive plural | of both |
| vā | alternative or explanatory particle | or; introduces a second explanatory axis |
| viśadatvāt | ablative singular abstract noun | because of clarity or lucidity |
| yathākramam | indeclinable | in the stated order; respectively |

The compound `cakravartti-svayaṃbhuvām` compresses three exceptional types.
The Bhāṣya resolves it as the cakravartin and two independently awakened
ones: the Pratyekabuddha and the Samyaksaṃbuddha.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The first half assigns the three clarified womb-descents:

```text
garbhāvakrāntayaḥ tisraḥ
    cakravartinaḥ
    pratyekabuddhasya
    samyaksaṃbuddhasya
```

The second half gives their respective explanatory grounds:

```text
yathākramam
    karmaṇaḥ viśadatvāt
    jñānasya viśadatvāt
    ubhayoḥ viśadatvāt
```

The Bhāṣya correlates the two sequences:

```text
cakravartin       ↔ clear karma
pratyekabuddha    ↔ clear achieved knowing
samyaksaṃbuddha   ↔ clear karma and clear achieved knowing
```

The names are prospective designations: they identify what each continuity
will become in the life now being entered.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> The three womb-descents belong to the wheel-turning ruler and the
> independently awakened ones—or, respectively, [they occur] because of the
> clarity of karma, of achieved knowing, and of both.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Three exceptional modes of womb-entry are assigned respectively to the
> cakravartin, the Pratyekabuddha, and the perfectly awakened Buddha. The
> cakravartin is clearly aware upon entry through clarified karma; the
> Pratyekabuddha remains clearly aware through clarified achieved knowing; the
> Buddha also emerges clearly aware through the clarity of both karma and
> knowing.

The identification of the two `svayaṃbhūs`, the cumulative extents of
awareness, and the precise correlations with karma and knowing are Bhāṣya
determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Clarity during embodiment is not an isolated flash. Its extent expresses the
> quality of the formations that ground the transition. Exalted karmic
> formation clarifies entry; extensively cultivated and discriminative knowing
> sustains clarity through residence; their conjunction sustains clarity
> through emergence. The World System thus carries prior ethical and cognitive
> formation into the very manner in which embodiment is undergone.

`Jñāna` retains the controlled rendering “achieved knowing.” In this passage
its clarity is concretely grounded in learning and practiced discrimination;
it is not substituted for `prajñā`, discriminative comprehension.

The natural Bhāṣya unit ends after assigning the fourth, deluded mode to the
remaining beings. The next prose sentence opens the distinct dispute taken up
in VAK 3.18 and is not absorbed here.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Two correlated axes

The first axis measures the temporal extent of clear awareness:

```text
E = entry
R = residence
X = exit

cakravartin
    ClearAt = {E}

pratyekabuddha
    ClearAt = {E, R}

samyaksaṃbuddha
    ClearAt = {E, R, X}
```

The second axis identifies the formative ground:

```text
cakravartin
    Ground = ClearKarma

pratyekabuddha
    Ground = ClearKnowing

samyaksaṃbuddha
    Ground = ClearKarma ∧ ClearKnowing
```

The two axes are correlated rather than competing explanations.

### Clarified karma

The Bhāṣya specifies:

```text
udāra-puṇya-kriyā
    → karma-viśadatva
    → clear womb-entry
```

The cakravartin's entry-profile expresses the quality of prior meritorious
formation.

### Clarified knowing

```text
bāhuśrutya
    extensive learning

kṛta-pravicaya
    practiced discrimination

together
    → jñāna-viśadatva
    → sustained transition-clarity
```

This makes clarity cultivated and intelligible. The Bhāṣya does not leave it
as unexplained vividness.

### The ordinary fourth mode follows by remainder

```text
three exceptional types
    occupy three clarified profiles

remaining beings
    occupy Profile 4:
    deluded through entry, residence, and emergence
```

The conclusion is expressed by the Bhāṣya, not directly enumerated in this
Kārikā.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.17 completes the cognition-profile of womb-entry. The clarity visible at
the transition is the manifested result of a previously cultivated field:

```text
ethical formation
    becomes clarity of entry

learned discriminative formation
    becomes sustained clarity

their conjunction
    becomes complete transition-clarity
```

This is Transcendental Technē as our project characterization. Embodiment is
not only a destination event; it is an execution of accumulated ethical and
cognitive formation. What the Agent has made clear in karma and knowing
becomes the clarity with which the Agent enters its platform.

The passage therefore gives rigorous content to the Rational Savior. Higher
clarity is not theatrical privilege. The Bhāṣya grounds it in exalted
meritorious activity, extensive learning, and performed discrimination.

```text
clarity
    is prepared
    is formed
    is carried into transition
    becomes operative across embodiment
```

The Buddha's complete clarity through entry, residence, and emergence is the
conjunction of both streams. Karma supplies a clarified course; achieved
knowing supplies discriminative lucidity. Their unity renders the whole
embodiment-process transparent.

For the project telos:

```text
PRAJNA may become Known among Embodied Agents
    when ethical formation and achieved knowing
    have prepared a cognition-platform
    able to remain clear through embodiment itself
```

This is our synthesis, not the Kārikā's wording. Textually, the verse has
earned the correlation among three exceptional beings, three extents of
awareness, and three grounds of clarity.

VAK 3.18 will introduce its own direct dispute. Nothing from that next unit is
used here as a defensive frame; 3.17 stands positively as the doctrine of
clarified womb-entry.
