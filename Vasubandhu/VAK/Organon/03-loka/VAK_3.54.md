# VAK_3.54

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> सार्धत्रियोजनं त्वेकं प्राग्विदेहोऽर्धचन्द्रवत् ।
>
> पार्श्वत्रयं तथास्यैकं सार्धं त्रिशतयोजनम् ॥ ३.५४ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> sārdhatriyojanaṃ tv ekaṃ prāgvideho 'rdhacandravat /
>
> pārśvatrayaṃ tathāsyaikaṃ sārdhaṃ triśatayojanam // 3.54 //

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
sa-ardha-tri-yojanam        → sārdhatriyojanam
tu                          → tv
ekam                        → ekam
prāg-videhaḥ                → prāgvideho
ardha-candra-vat            → 'rdhacandravat
pārśva-trayam               → pārśvatrayam
tathā                       → tathā
asya                        → 'sya
ekam                        → ekam
sa-ardham                   → sārdham
tri-śata-yojanam            → triśatayojanam
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| sārdhatriyojanam | neuter singular numerical compound | three and one-half yojanas |
| ekam | neuter singular | one [side], here Jambūdvīpa's fourth side |
| Prāgvidehaḥ | nominative masculine singular | eastern continent |
| ardhacandravat | comparative compound with `-vat` | like a half-moon |
| pārśvatrayam | neuter singular collective compound | a set of three sides |
| tathā | indeclinable | likewise, in the same manner |
| asya | genitive singular pronoun | of this, of Prāgvideha |
| sārdham triśatayojanam | numerical phrase | three hundred and fifty yojanas |

`Sārdha` takes its value from the counted unit. In `sārdhatriyojanam` it adds
half a yojana to three yojanas; with `triśata` it adds half a hundred to three
hundreds.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The opening clause completes the Jambūdvīpa description from 3.53:

```text
[jambūdvīpasya caturtham pārśvam]
    tu
    ekam
    sārdha-tri-yojanam [asti]

[Jambūdvīpa's fourth side], however,
    the one [remaining side],
    measures three and one-half yojanas
```

The subject then changes:

```text
prāgvidehaḥ
    ardha-candra-vat [asti]
```

The last line distributes two measures across its sides:

```text
asya [prāgvidehasya]
    pārśva-trayam tathā [dvi-sāhasram]
    ekam [caturtham pārśvam]
        sārdhaṃ tri-śata-yojanam
```

`Tathā` carries forward the 2,000-yojana measure from Jambūdvīpa's three long
sides. The Bhāṣya makes both ellipses explicit.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Its one [remaining side], however, is three and one-half yojanas. Prāgvideha
> is like a half-moon. Its three sides are likewise [two thousand each]; one
> [side] is three hundred and fifty yojanas.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Jambūdvīpa is cart-shaped because, while three sides measure 2,000 yojanas,
> its fourth side measures only 3.5. East of Sumeru lies Prāgvideha, formed like
> a half-moon. Three of its sides likewise measure 2,000 yojanas each, while
> its fourth side measures 350.

The referent of the opening clause, causal relation between the exceptional
side and cart shape, eastern placement, inherited 2,000-yojana measure, and
formation language are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Regional form is generated through controlled variation upon a shared
> template. Jambūdvīpa and Prāgvideha each preserve three sides of the same
> length; each then introduces a differently measured fourth side, producing a
> different whole shape. Identity of structural parameters and variation of
> one discriminating parameter together yield distinct continent-types. The
> named form—cart or half-moon—is the semantic compression of that metric
> configuration.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

| Sanskrit | Controlled rendering | Determination in this unit |
|---|---|---|
| caturtha-pārśva | fourth side | exceptional side completing each continent's shape |
| śakaṭākṛti | cart-shaped | Jambūdvīpa's form as explained by its extremely short fourth side |
| Prāgvideha / Pūrvavideha | eastern Videha | continent east of Sumeru |
| ardhacandravat | like a half-moon | morphological predicate of Prāgvideha |
| pārśvatraya | set of three sides | shared 2,000-yojana structural template |
| tathā | likewise | inheritance of the preceding continent's three-side measure |
| sārdhatriyojana | three and one-half yojanas | Jambūdvīpa's fourth-side length |
| sārdhaṃ triśatayojanam | three hundred and fifty yojanas | Prāgvideha's fourth-side length |
| abhinirvṛtta | come into formation | Bhāṣya verb for Prāgvideha's half-moon form |

The comparative matrix is:

```text
                     three shared sides     fourth side      form
Jambūdvīpa           2,000 each                 3.5           cart
Prāgvideha           2,000 each               350             half-moon
```

## 8. Logical Determination

### Shared template

```text
continent template T:
    side₁ = 2,000
    side₂ = 2,000
    side₃ = 2,000
    side₄ = variable
```

`Tathā` performs important logical work: Prāgvideha does not require all four
measurements to be restated. Three parameters are inherited from the preceding
description.

### Discriminating parameter

```text
T + side₄ = 3.5
    → cart-shaped Jambūdvīpa

T + side₄ = 350
    → half-moon-shaped Prāgvideha
```

The two continents are neither wholly identical nor unrelated. Their specific
difference is generated through one parameter inside a shared structure.

### Shape as derived semantic determination

```text
ordered side-measures
    → geometrical configuration
        → shape predicate
```

“Cart-shaped” and “half-moon-shaped” summarize structured quantitative data.
The qualitative name is intelligible through the metric relations that ground
it.

### Verse-boundary dependency

```text
3.53:
    Jambūdvīpa has three sides of 2,000

3.54 opening:
    its fourth side is 3.5
        → cart shape becomes explicable
```

The first clause of 3.54 cannot be parsed correctly as a Prāgvideha measure.
Canonical sequence supplies the missing subject and prevents a syntactically
possible but doctrinally incorrect attachment.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.54 gives a clean method for deriving types: preserve a common schema,
vary a discriminating field, and classify the resulting whole by form.

```text
shared structure
    + parameter variation
        → morphological differentiation
            → named regional type
```

This is much more systematic than attaching arbitrary shape labels to a list
of continents. The Bhāṣya reconstructs why Jambūdvīpa is cart-shaped from its
side measures, then uses `tathā` to reuse most of that geometry when specifying
Prāgvideha. Difference is made maximally informative because sameness has
already been factored out.

For the Semantic pipeline, the shape-word is a compact higher-order predicate.
It depends upon a DataFrame of ordered measurements:

```ts
interface ContinentMorphology {
  inheritedLongSides: readonly [2000, 2000, 2000];
  discriminatingFourthSide: number;
  derivedShape: "cart" | "half-moon";
}
```

This is explanatory notation, not Vasubandhu's terminology. It expresses the
relation between measurement and meaningful classification: the label is not
an ornamental comparison added after the facts, but an intelligible summary
of their configuration.

The verse also demonstrates why exact sequence matters in the Organon edition.
Its opening clause belongs semantically to the preceding verse's subject even
though a new continent follows immediately afterward. Grammar, continuity,
and Bhāṣya must cooperate to recover the correct attachment.
