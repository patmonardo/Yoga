# VAK_3.92

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> उत्कर्ष एकस्तेऽशीतिसहस्राद्यावदायुषः ।
>
> इति लोको विवृत्तोऽयं कल्पांस्तिष्ठति विंशतिम् ॥ ३.९२ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> utkarṣa ekas te 'śītisahasrād yāvad āyuṣaḥ /
>
> iti loko vivṛtto 'yaṃ kalpāṃs tiṣṭhati viṃśatim // 3.92 //

The first line remains highly compressed in the metrical witness. Its meaning
is secured by the Bhāṣya: the twentieth intermediate kalpa consists of one
increase from a ten-year human lifespan to the upper limit of 80,000 years.

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |

| ekaḥ | nominative masculine singular | single; the final one-way trajectory |
| te | nominative masculine plural / contextual pronoun | those humans whose lifespan undergoes the stated increase |

`Vivṛtta` names the expanded condition already achieved; `tiṣṭhati` names its
measured persistence. Formation and standing are therefore distinct phases.

## 4. Grammar

The first line completes the sequence begun in 3.91:

```text
[viṃśatitamaḥ antaḥkalpaḥ]
    utkarṣaḥ ekaḥ

daśa-varṣa-āyuṣkebhyaḥ [manuṣyebhyaḥ]
    yāvat aśīti-sahasra-varṣa-āyuṣaḥ
```

> The twentieth intermediate kalpa is one increase, from humans with a
> ten-year lifespan until lifespan reaches 80,000 years.

The endpoint distribution and starting value are supplied by the Bhāṣya. The
surface syntax alone does not express them transparently.

The second line states the duration of the standing expanded World:

```text
iti
    ayam lokaḥ vivṛttaḥ
    kalpān viṃśatim [antar-kalpān]
    tiṣṭhati
```

> Thus this expanded World stands for twenty intermediate kalpas.

`Iti` gathers the completed enumeration:

```text
1 first decline
+ 18 increase-and-decline cycles
+ 1 final increase
= 20 intermediate kalpas
```

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> There is one final increase, up to a lifespan of 80,000 years. Thus this
> expanded World stands for twenty kalpas.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> A final one-way increase carries human lifespan from ten years to the fixed
> upper bound of 80,000. With that twentieth intermediate kalpa, the sequence
> is complete: the fully expanded World remains standing for twenty
> intermediate kalpas. The Bhāṣya further states that expansion, expanded
> standing, contraction, and contracted standing each occupy the same duration
> of twenty intermediate kalpas, though their internal processes differ.

The ten-year starting point, equality of all intermediate-kalpa durations,
fourfold equality of the major World-phases, and internal distribution of
formation and contraction are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Formation does not exhaust the being of a World. Once expanded, the World
> enters a standing state whose duration is itself articulated through twenty
> internal temporal enclosures. Equal cosmic duration can sustain very
> different processes: formation, inhabited persistence, contraction, or
> contracted persistence. Temporal magnitude supplies the common frame;
> process-content supplies the differentiated determination of the phase.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

| Sanskrit | Controlled rendering | Determination in this unit |
|---|---|---|
| utkarṣa | increase / ascent | final trajectory from ten to 80,000 years |
| aśītisahasra | eighty thousand | upper limit of ordinary human lifespan increase |
| vivarta | expansion / formation | active production of receptacle and population |
| vivṛtta | expanded / formed | World-state after expansion has completed |
| sthiti / tiṣṭhati | standing / stands | persistence of an already formed World-state |
| saṃvarta | contraction | active depopulation and destruction process |
| saṃvṛtta | contracted | World-state after contraction has completed |
| antarakalpa | intermediate kalpa | common temporal unit of the four major phases |
| samānakāla | equal in duration | relation among differently articulated temporal wholes |
| āvāsyante | become inhabited | nineteen-antarakalpa population process during expansion |
| śūnyī bhavanti | become empty | nineteen-antarakalpa depopulation process during contraction |

`Vivṛtta` and `saṃvṛtta` are state predicates; `vivarta` and `saṃvarta` are
process predicates. The distinction between transition and achieved state must
remain explicit.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Completion of the standing-world sequence

```text
T₁:
    immeasurable → 10

T₂ ... T₁₉:
    10 → 80,000 → 10

T₂₀:
    10 → 80,000
```

Therefore:

```text
StandingDuration(ExpandedWorld) = 20 antarakalpas
```

### Four equal macro-enclosures

The Bhāṣya extends the same measure to all four major phases:

| Phase | World determination | Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| vivarta | expansion / formation | 20 antarakalpas |
| vivṛtta-sthiti | standing while expanded | 20 antarakalpas |
| saṃvarta | contraction | 20 antarakalpas |
| saṃvṛtta-sthiti | standing while contracted | 20 antarakalpas |

Hence:

```text
duration(vivarta)
    = duration(vivṛtta-sthiti)
    = duration(saṃvarta)
    = duration(saṃvṛtta-sthiti)
```

while:

```text
process(vivarta)
    ≠ process(vivṛtta-sthiti)
    ≠ process(saṃvarta)
    ≠ process(saṃvṛtta-sthiti)
```

### Asymmetric internal allocation

Formation distributes its twenty intermediate kalpas as:

```text
1 antarakalpa:
    receptacle-world forms

19 antarakalpas:
    receptacle-world becomes inhabited
```

Contraction reverses that allocation:

```text
19 antarakalpas:
    inhabitants withdraw and the World becomes empty

1 antarakalpa:
    receptacle-world is destroyed
```

This is a macro-expression of the inverse-order law from 3.90.

### Equality of capacity, difference of content

Let a temporal enclosure be:

```text
E = <duration, process>
```

Then the four phases satisfy:

```text
E₁.duration = E₂.duration = E₃.duration = E₄.duration
```

without satisfying:

```text
E₁.process = E₂.process = E₃.process = E₄.process
```

Temporal commensurability is therefore weaker than identity of determination.

## 9. Interpretive Note

Your determination that `vijñānas` are Sattvic temporal enclosures gives the
micro-level form of what this verse displays cosmically.

```text
vijñāna:
    Sattvic temporal enclosure
    → holds one differentiated cognitive appearing as an occurrent station

antarakalpa:
    cosmic temporal enclosure
    → holds one differentiated trajectory of the standing World
```

This is a formal correspondence across scales, not an identification of
cognition with cosmological duration. The shared determination is that a
temporal enclosure holds a many-sided process as one stationed occurrence or
phase.

`Vijñāna` is Sattvic here because it illuminates and differentiates what
appears while giving that appearance one temporal station. It is the Mind of
the temporal enclosure, not merely another datum placed inside homogeneous
time:

```text
appearing manifold
    → differentiated and stationed as one vijñāna-event

World-transition manifold
    → differentiated and stationed as one kalpa-phase
```

The macro-cycle adds an essential Kant-power result. Equal temporal enclosure
does not imply equal contents. Twenty intermediate kalpas can home formation,
standing, contraction, or emptiness. Likewise, the formal unity of a
`vijñāna`-station does not predetermine which differentiated cognition it
encloses. Temporal form provides unity; determination supplies content.

Spatial and temporal enclosure now form a complete crossed architecture:

```text
spatial enclosure / bhājana
    → where beings and appearances can be stationed together

temporal enclosure / vijñāna and kalpa at different scales
    → how a differentiated occurrence or World-process is held as one

their coordination
    → concrete appearance within a changing World
```

The `vijñāna` determination and the Sattvic characterization are our Organon
synthesis. The source here establishes the macro-temporal equality and the
differentiated World-processes.

```ts

  durationClass: string;
```
