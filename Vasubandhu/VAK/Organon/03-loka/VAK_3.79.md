# VAK_3.79

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> नृणां वर्षाणि पञ्चाशदहोरात्रो दिवौकसाम् ।
>
> कामेऽधराणां तेनायुः पञ्चवर्षशतानि तु ॥ ३.७९ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> nṛṇāṃ varṣāṇi pañcāśad ahorātro divaukasām /
>
> kāme 'dharāṇāṃ tenāyuḥ pañcavarṣaśatāni tu // 3.79 //

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
nṛṇām                 → nṛṇāṃ
varṣāṇi               → varṣāṇi
pañcāśat              → pañcāśad (before a-)
ahas-rātraḥ           → ahorātraḥ → ahorātro (before d-)
diva-okasām           → divaukasām
kāme                  → kāme
adharāṇām             → 'dharāṇāṃ (after avagraha)
tena                  → tena
āyuḥ                  → āyuḥ
pañca-varṣa-śatāni    → pañcavarṣaśatāni
tu                    → tu
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| nṛṇām | genitive masculine plural | of humans; identifies the source time-scale |
| varṣāṇi | nominative neuter plural | years |
| pañcāśat | cardinal numeral | fifty |
| ahorātraḥ | nominative masculine singular compound | one complete day-and-night |
| divaukasām | genitive masculine plural | of the heaven-dwellers |
| kāme | locative masculine singular | in the desire realm |
| adharāṇām | genitive masculine plural | of the lowest ones |
| tena | instrumental neuter singular | by that; using that established measure |
| āyuḥ | nominative neuter singular | lifespan |
| pañcavarṣaśatāni | nominative neuter plural measure expression | five hundred years |
| tu | particle | indeed / in turn; closes the lifespan determination |

The Bhāṣya makes `adharāṇām` exact: these are the Cāturmahārājakāyikas, the
lowest class of desire-realm devas. It also makes explicit that the five
hundred years are divine years constituted through the preceding conversion.

## 4. Grammar and Construe

The first line is an equation between two differently indexed temporal units:

```text
nṛṇāṃ varṣāṇi pañcāśat
    =
divaukasām kāme 'dharāṇām
    ekaḥ ahorātraḥ
```

> Fifty human years are one day-and-night for the lowest heaven-dwellers in
> the desire realm.

Although `kāme 'dharāṇām` occurs metrically in the second line, it qualifies
the heaven-dwellers whose day-and-night is being defined, not merely the
possessors of the lifespan in the closing clause.

The second construction uses that established unit:

```text
tena [ahorātreṇa]
    [teṣām] āyuḥ
    pañca-varṣa-śatāni [divyāni]
```

> Reckoned by that day-and-night, their lifespan is five hundred divine years.

`tena` is therefore operational. It does not mean only “therefore”; it carries
the day-and-night just established into the calculation of the complete
lifespan.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Fifty human years are a day-and-night of the lowest heaven-dwellers in the
> desire realm; measured by that, their lifespan is five hundred years.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> For the Cāturmahārājakāyika devas, fifty human years constitute one divine
> day-and-night. Thirty such day-nights constitute their month and twelve such
> months their year; measured by this divine calendar, their lifespan is five
> hundred divine years.

The identification of the deva class, the thirty-day month, the twelve-month
year, and the divine status of the five hundred years are Bhāṣya
determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> A World does not merely assign its inhabitants a duration; it establishes
> the unit through which that duration is counted. Human years become the
> input from which a deva day-and-night is constituted. That day-and-night is
> then composed into months and years, and only through this articulated
> calendar can the deva lifespan be stated. Temporal magnitude is therefore
> inseparable from the domain-specific clock that makes it measurable.

The identical English word “year” must not conceal the unit distinction:
`human year` is used to define the deva day-night, whereas `divine year` names
a higher-order unit assembled within the resulting calendar.

## 8. Logical Determination

### Cross-domain conversion

Let `HY` denote one human year and `CD` one Cāturmahārājakāyika day-and-night:

```text
1 CD = 50 HY
```

This is a conversion of temporal units, not an identity of the human and deva
world-domains.

### Calendar composition

The Bhāṣya supplies the complete internal calendar:

```text
30 CD = 1 divine month
12 divine months = 1 divine year
500 divine years = 1 Cāturmahārājakāyika lifespan
```

Therefore:

```text
1 divine year
    = 30 × 12 divine day-nights
    = 360 CD

1 lifespan
    = 500 × 360 CD
    = 180,000 CD
```

Converted entirely into human years:

```text
180,000 × 50 HY = 9,000,000 HY
```

The nine-million-year value is an arithmetic consequence of the Bhāṣya's
calendar, not a number separately asserted by the verse.

### Dependency order

```text
establish day-night conversion
    → compose divine month
        → compose divine year
            → state lifespan in divine years
```

If the first conversion is omitted, “five hundred years” is semantically
underspecified and can be mistaken for five hundred human years.

### Forward rule

The Bhāṣya closes by announcing that for the higher desire-realm devas both
quantities increase successively twofold:

```text
higher station
    → doubled human-year value of one divine day-night
    → doubled number of divine years in the lifespan
```

This is the transition to VAK 3.80; its full distribution belongs there.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.79 turns the World System into a system of clocks. The important result
is not merely longevity but **nested temporal determination**:

```text
human duration
    → converted into a deva day-night
        → composed into a deva calendar
            → used to measure a deva lifespan
```

The syntax says that fifty years “are” one day-and-night, but the semantic
copula is a calibrated relation between measures. It does not erase the
difference between human years and a divine day. Each side determines the
other for purposes of comparison:

```text
50 human years
    → determine the magnitude of one deva day-night

one deva day-night
    → provides the conversion through which those human years acquire
      significance inside the deva time-regime
```

This is a practical theory of temporal measurement. An agent calculating a
lifespan must carry the provenance of every unit: population, realm, calendar
level, and conversion factor. A bare numeric value such as `500` is not yet a
temporal determination. It becomes one only as `500 divine years` under a
specified clock.

```ts
interface TemporalUnit {
  realm: "Human" | "Caturmaharajakayika";
  kind: "Year" | "Month" | "DayNight";
}

interface TemporalConversion {
  source: { unit: TemporalUnit; magnitude: number };
  target: { unit: TemporalUnit; magnitude: number };
}

const baseConversion: TemporalConversion = {
  source: { unit: { realm: "Human", kind: "Year" }, magnitude: 50 },
  target: {
    unit: { realm: "Caturmaharajakayika", kind: "DayNight" },
    magnitude: 1,
  },
};
```

This is explanatory project notation, not Vasubandhu's terminology. It makes
the semantic type of the unit explicit and prevents invalid arithmetic across
world-clocks.
