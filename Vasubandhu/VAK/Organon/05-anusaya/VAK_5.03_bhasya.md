# VAK 5.03 Bhāṣya — Five Views and the Tenfold Base

## Kārikā Anchor

> dṛṣṭayaḥ pañca satkāyamithyāntagrāhadṛṣṭayaḥ |
> dṛṣṭiśīlavṛtaparāmarśāv iti punar daśa || 5.03 ||

> The views are five: satkāya-view, false view, extreme-grasping view,
> adherence to views, and adherence to discipline and observance. Thus,
> in turn, the anuśayas are ten.

## Continuous Conventional Translation

Again, these six anuśayas are made into ten in the Abhidharma. How?
“The views are five: satkāya-view, false view, extreme-grasping view,
adherence to views, and adherence to discipline and observance. Thus,
again, ten.” By making the view among the six anuśayas fivefold, they
become ten. Five have the nature of view: satkāya-view,
extreme-grasping view, false view, adherence to views, and adherence
to discipline and observance. Attachment, resistance, conceit,
ignorance, and doubt are the other five [the source's connecting
word is uncertain].

These ten anuśayas, again, are made ninety-eight in the Abhidharma:
thirty-six belonging to the desire-domain, thirty-one to the
form-domain, and thirty-one to the formless-domain. How? In summary,
the anuśayas of the three domains are to be abandoned either through
seeing or through cultivation. Among those belonging to the
desire-domain, thirty-two are to be abandoned through seeing. Which
are they? The next verse states them.

## Argument and Doctrinal Determination

The operation is precise: the single `dṛṣṭi` in the original six is
replaced by five forms of view. Thus `6 − 1 + 5 = 10`. The prose lists
the five in a different order from the compressed Kārikā; the
membership is the same. Their detailed meanings and abandonment
conditions remain to be established later in the chapter.

The Bhāṣya immediately makes the ten a base for a further
classification, giving the domain totals `36 + 31 + 31 = 98` and
introducing abandonment by seeing and by cultivation. It does not
derive those totals within 5.03. The terminal question directs the
reader to 5.04, where the first thirty-two are enumerated.

`Darśanaprahātavya` and `bhāvanāprahātavya` designate distinct modes
of abandonment. They cannot yet be collapsed into a generic “error
removal” operation, and the five views cannot be treated as five
mere opinions detached from their domain and path relations.

## Translation and Organon Study

**Translation decisions.** The five view names remain technical
compounds because this unit counts them but does not define them.
The uncertain `yathā` sentence is rendered by its evident
classificatory contrast and flagged above. `Abhidharme` locates the
tenfold and ninety-eightfold schemes in the source's doctrinal
classification, not in a project taxonomy.

**Organon reconstruction.** Here a concept becomes more determinate
through internal division and subsequent placement in a domain and
a mode of transformation. That is a useful model for our Science of
Citta, provided the source's views, the path's abandonment modes,
and any later Rust or TypeScript representation remain separately
accountable. This passage supplies a classificatory procedure; it
does not itself implement or authorize a software type system.
