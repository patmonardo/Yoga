# VAK 5.49 Bhāṣya — Completing the Ten, Opening the Six Stains

## Kārikā Anchor

> kaukṛtyaṃ vicikitsātaḥ krodherṣye pratighānvaye |
> anye ca ṣaṭ kleśa-malāḥ māyā śāṭhyaṃ madas tathā || 5.49 ||

> Remorse arises from doubt; anger and envy from hostility.
> There are also six other afflictive stains, beginning
> with deceit, crookedness, and intoxication.

## Continuous Conventional Translation

Remorse arises from doubt. Anger and envy belong to
hostility; the prose explains that they arise from it.
These complete the ten upakleśas that are derivatives of
kleśas. There are also six other afflictive stains. Which
ones? Deceit, crookedness, intoxication, and—as the next
verse continues—three more.

## Argument and Doctrinal Determination

The first half closes the source-affiliation account of the
ten Vaibhāṣika *paryavasthānas*. `Pratighānvaye` is glossed
as `pratighasamutthe`, so “arise from hostility” is more
exact here than mere membership in a lineage. The collective
`kleśaniḥṣyandā upakleśāḥ` covers the ten while preserving
5.48's reported dispute over concealment's specific ground.

`Anye ca` introduces a different sixfold class of
upakleśas, the *kleśa-malas*. Only its first three members
are named in this verse. The remaining names and the
individual definitions belong to the distributed 5.50
unit; their source affiliations follow its later half.
Class membership is thus established before the present
passage gives the six factors' complete identity or origin.

## Translation and Organon Study

**Translation decisions.** `Kaukṛtya` is “remorse” in this
list. `Pratighānvaya` is rendered through the Bhāṣya's
stronger `samuttha` gloss. `Mala` is provisionally “stain”;
this short unit does not provide a general definition of
staining. The distributed verse boundary keeps the other
three names out of 5.49's Sanskrit and translation.

**Organon reconstruction.** The text closes one genetic
classification and opens another class without yet giving
its whole inventory. A Science of Citta account can retain
such an incomplete determination while marking the exact
next step. This is a project-level reading of the textual
movement, separate from any implemented model.
