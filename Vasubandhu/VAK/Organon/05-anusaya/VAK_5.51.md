# VAK_5.51

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
दृष्ट्यामर्शात् प्रदाशस्तु शाठ्यं दृष्टिसमुत्थितम् ।
तत्राह्रीक्यानपत्राप्यस्त्यानमिद्धोद्धवा द्विधा ॥ ५.५१ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
dṛṣṭyāmarśāt pradāśas tu śāṭhyaṃ dṛṣṭi-samutthitam |
tatrāhrīkyānapatrāpyastyānamiddhoddhavā dvidhā || 5.51 ||
```

VAK 5.51 completes the genealogy of the six stains and then changes the axis
of analysis from afflictive origin to mode of abandonment. Five
paryavasthānas are twofold because their Path-status follows the kleśas with
which they are conjoined.

**Textual caution.** As in VAK 5.47, the kārikā witness has `uddhava`, while
the doctrinal factor is identified as `auddhatya`. The Bhāṣya e-text also has
`āhlīkya` for expected `āhrīkya`. Witness and interpreted technical form remain
distinct.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
dṛṣṭi-āmarśāt | pradāśaḥ | tu | śāṭhyam | dṛṣṭi-samutthitam |
tatra | āhrīkyam | anapatrāpyam | styānam | middham | uddhavaḥ | dvidhā
```

| Form | Morphology | Contextual sense |
|---|---|---|
| `dṛṣṭi-āmarśāt` | ablative singular compound | from grasping at view |
| `pradāśaḥ` | nominative singular masculine | obstinate fault-clinging or resistance to correction |
| `tu` | adversative particle | but, marking the remaining genealogy |
| `śāṭhyam` | nominative singular neuter | crookedness or duplicity |
| `dṛṣṭi-samutthitam` | nominative singular neuter compound | arisen from view |
| `tatra` | locative adverb | among these, in that group |
| `āhrīkyam` | nominative singular neuter | shamelessness |
| `anapatrāpyam` | nominative singular neuter | lack of moral concern |
| `styānam` | nominative singular neuter | torpor |
| `middham` | nominative singular neuter | sleepiness |
| `uddhavaḥ` | transmitted nominative singular masculine | excitation or restlessness (`auddhatya`) |
| `dvidhā` | indeclinable adverb | twofold with respect to abandonment |

The Bhāṣya specifies `dvidhā` as `darśana-bhāvanā-prahātavya`: abandoned
through seeing or through cultivation.

## 4. Grammar

**The remaining stain genealogies**

```text
pradāśaḥ tu
    dṛṣṭi-āmarśāt [samutthitaḥ]

śāṭhyam
    dṛṣṭi-samutthitam
```

The first clause supplies an ablatival source and elides the participle made
explicit in the second. The Bhāṣya sharpens the latter as `śāṭhyaṃ
dṛṣṭi-niḥṣyandaḥ`, crookedness is a derivative of view.

**Five twofold paryavasthānas**

```text
tatra
    āhrīkyam
    anapatrāpyam
    styānam
    middham
    uddhavaḥ [auddhatyam]
        dvidhā
```

`Tatra` refers to the ten paryavasthānas already taught. The mixed grammatical
genders are coordinated under the indeclinable predicate `dvidhā`.

**Bhāṣya's relational explanation**

```text
ete pañca dharmāḥ
    darśana-prahātavyāḥ
    bhāvanā-prahātavyāḥ ca
        ubhaya-prakāra-kleśa-saṃprayogāt
```

Their twofold status is caused by conjunction with both kinds of kleśa. It is
not an unexplained intrinsic duplication of each named factor.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> `Pradāśa`, however, arises from grasping at view, while crookedness arises
> from view. Among these [envelopments], shamelessness, lack of moral concern,
> torpor, sleepiness, and excitation are twofold.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Obstinate fault-clinging arises from grasping at view, while crookedness is a
> derivative of view. This completes the source-affiliations of the six stains.
> The analysis then turns to abandonment: among the ten envelopments,
> shamelessness, lack of moral concern, torpor, sleepiness, and excitation can
> be abandoned through seeing or through cultivation because they are conjoined
> with kleśas of both types. An instance conjoined with a kleśa abandoned by a
> particular seeing is itself abandoned through that corresponding seeing.

The explicit `dṛṣṭi-niḥṣyanda` relation, the two abandonment modes, and the
conjunction rule are supplied by the Bhāṣya.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> An afflictive factor is not fully determined by its origin. Once the final
> stains are traced to view and grasping at view, the text asks a new question:
> by what Path-operation is each factor removed? For five envelopments the
> answer depends upon conjunction. The same named kind receives seeing-
> abandonment when operating with a seeing-abandoned kleśa and cultivation-
> abandonment when operating with a cultivation-abandoned kleśa. Path-status
> is therefore relational and event-sensitive: it belongs to the factor as
> concretely associated, not to its detached name alone.

The two modes are source doctrine; the broader formulation in terms of
relation-indexed classification is the Organon's analytical reconstruction.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Dṛṣṭyāmarśa` — grasping at view**

The stated source of `pradāśa`. The compound marks grasping or insistence in
relation to view, but this passage does not license expanding it into a full
psychology of personal identification with one's doctrine.

**`Pradāśa` — obstinate fault-clinging**

Previously defined as firm adherence to a blameworthy matter that refuses
proper correction. Here its genetic source is assigned to grasping at view.

**`Dṛṣṭi-samutthita` — arisen from view**

The source-relation assigned to crookedness. The Bhāṣya restates it as
`dṛṣṭi-niḥṣyanda`, a derivative or outflow of view.

**`Śāṭhya` — crookedness or duplicity**

Crookedness of mind preventing disclosure of matters as they are. Its class,
operation, and origin are now all stated on separate textual grounds.

**The five twofold factors**

```text
āhrīkya       shamelessness
anapatrāpya   lack of moral concern
styāna         torpor
middha         sleepiness
auddhatya      excitation
```

Each may be seeing-abandoned or cultivation-abandoned according to its
conjunction.

**`Darśana-prahātavya` — to be abandoned through seeing**

A Path-status acquired here when one of the five is conjoined with a kleśa
abandoned by the corresponding seeing.

**`Bhāvanā-prahātavya` — to be abandoned through cultivation**

The complementary Path-status when the factor is conjoined with a
cultivation-abandoned kleśa.

**`Ubhayaprakāra-kleśa-saṃprayoga` — conjunction with both kinds of kleśa**

The explicit reason why the five factor-kinds are `dvidhā`. `Saṃprayoga` is
the mediating relation between factor and abandonment class.

## 8. Logical Determination

The two final genealogies are:

```text
ArisesFrom(Pradāśa, Dṛṣṭyāmarśa)
IsNihsyandaOf(Śāṭhya, Dṛṣṭi)
```

The abandonment rule is instance- and relation-sensitive:

```text
For x in {Āhrīkya, Anapatrāpya, Styāna, Middha, Auddhatya}:

ConjoinedWith(x, k)
AND AbandonedBySeeing(k, seeingType)
    → AbandonedBySeeing(x, seeingType)

ConjoinedWith(x, k)
AND AbandonedByCultivation(k)
    → AbandonedByCultivation(x)
```

Therefore:

```text
FactorKind(x)
    ⇏ UniqueAbandonmentMode(x)

AbandonmentMode(x)
    = function of Conjunction(x, KleśaInEvent)
```

The passage distinguishes at least four independent axes:

```text
Class(x)
Origin(x)
Conjunction(x, k)
AbandonmentMode(x, pathOperation)
```

No one axis can be substituted for the others.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Source determination.** The first half finishes the stain genealogy left
open by VAK 5.50. The second half begins a new question, stated explicitly in
the Bhāṣya: “Which of these is to be abandoned in which way?”

**Twofold through conjunction.** The five factors are not assigned two fixed
internal essences. Their abandonment classification changes with the kleśa
present in the same associated event:

```text
same factor-kind
    + seeing-abandoned associate
        → seeing-abandoned instance

same factor-kind
    + cultivation-abandoned associate
        → cultivation-abandoned instance
```

This continues the chapter's insistence that meaning and status emerge through
specified relations rather than isolated names.

**No speculative inflation of the genealogies.** The source states that
`pradāśa` arises from grasping at view and that crookedness is a derivative of
view. The official study does not add that doctrinal opposition is necessarily
experienced as personal attack or that crookedness is always strategic
self-presentation; those mechanisms are not supplied here.

**Organon interpretation.** The Dataset must reify the contextual assertion:

```text
AbandonmentAssertion
    hasFactorInstance
    conjoinedWith KleśaInstance
    hasPathOperation
    hasSeeingType when applicable
    supportedBy SourceSpan
```

This prevents a global monadic label from erasing the conjunction that
actually determines Path-status. The architecture is project interpretation,
while the conjunction rule is explicit Bhāṣya doctrine.
