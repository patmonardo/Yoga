# VAK_5.10

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
सप्त मानाः नवविधास्त्रिभ्यो दृग्भावनाक्षयाः ।
वधादिपर्यवस्थानं हेयं भावनया तथा ॥ ५.१० ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
sapta mānāḥ navavidhās tribhyo dṛg-bhāvanā-kṣayāḥ |
vadhādi-paryavasthānaṃ heyaṃ bhāvanayā tathā || 5.10 ||
```

The chapter moves from malformed view and inversion to afflicted
self-measurement. The final `tathā`, “likewise,” also carries the
cultivation-abandonment rule forward into VAK 5.11.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
sapta | mānāḥ | nava-vidhāḥ | tribhyaḥ |
dṛk-bhāvanā-kṣayāḥ |
vadha-ādi-paryavasthānam | heyam | bhāvanayā | tathā
```

| Form | Morphology | Contextual sense |
|---|---|---|
| `sapta` | numeral | seven |
| `mānāḥ` | nominative plural masculine | conceits, afflicted self-estimations |
| `nava-vidhāḥ` | nominative plural masculine compound | of nine kinds |
| `tribhyaḥ` | ablative plural numeral | from three |
| `dṛk-bhāvanā-kṣayāḥ` | nominative plural masculine compound | exhausted through seeing and cultivation |
| `vadha-ādi-paryavasthānam` | nominative singular neuter compound | afflictive occupation beginning with killing |
| `heyam` | nominative singular neuter gerundive | to be abandoned |
| `bhāvanayā` | instrumental singular feminine | through cultivation |
| `tathā` | adverb | likewise; in the same manner |

`Māna` is related to √mā, “measure,” but the Bhāṣya gives the operative
definition `cittasya unnati`, elevation of consciousness. Its sevenfold
difference arises through `pravṛttibheda`, difference in operation.

## 4. Grammar

**Sevenfold and ninefold determinations**

```text
sapta mānāḥ [santi]
    the conceits are seven

[te] tribhyaḥ navavidhāḥ [bhavanti]
    [they] become ninefold from three
```

The Bhāṣya identifies the three generators of the secondary ninefold scheme
as `mānātimāna`, `ūnamāna`, and `māna`. The nine are not a second independent
list added to the seven.

**Two modes of exhaustion**

```text
[te] dṛk-bhāvanā-kṣayāḥ
    they are exhausted through seeing and cultivation
```

The compound is distributive across determinate occurrences of conceit. It
does not say that every occurrence requires both operations jointly.

**Cultivation-abandonable manifestation**

```text
vadha-ādi-paryavasthānam
    bhāvanayā heyam

afflictive occupation beginning with killing
    is to be abandoned through cultivation
```

`Tathā` completes the present comparison and opens the continuation into VAK
5.11.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> The conceits are seven and become ninefold from three; they are exhausted
> through seeing and cultivation. Afflictive occupation beginning with killing
> is to be abandoned through cultivation; likewise—

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Conceit, generically the elevation of consciousness, has seven forms
> according to differences in its operation. A secondary ninefold scheme is
> generated from three of these—mānātimāna, ūnamāna, and māna. Determinate
> occurrences of the seven are exhausted through seeing or cultivation.
> Afflictive occupation under which one deliberately acts from killing through
> false speech is cultivation-abandonable because it takes cultivation-
> abandonable dharmas as its cognitive support; the same rule continues into
> the next verse.

The seven definitions, the generators of the nine, the distributive force of
the two Path modes, and the range of `vadhādi` are supplied by the Bhāṣya.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Once a field has been misconstrued, the agent measures itself within that
> field. Conceit is this elevation of consciousness differentiated through
> comparative operation: superiority, equality, inferiority, attainment, and
> self-appropriation become forms in which the continuum assigns itself a
> position. Some forms lose their ground through seeing; others persist as
> dispositions requiring cultivation. Moreover, an affliction can remain
> structurally un-abandoned without being presently manifest. The Path must
> therefore distinguish classification, capacity for occurrence, actual
> manifestation, and completed removal.

The movement from VAK 5.09 is:

```text
view-inversion
    configures the field

māna
    measures the self within that configured field

paryavasthāna
    occupies consciousness strongly enough to issue in deliberate action
```

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Māna` — elevation of consciousness**

The Bhāṣya's generic definition is `cittasya unnati`. It is not restricted to
boastfulness. Even judgments of equality or inferiority can preserve the
afflicted elevation by making self-comparison the governing relation.

**The seven conceits**

| Term | Bhāṣya determination |
|---|---|
| `māna` | superior to an inferior, or equal to an equal |
| `atimāna` | superior to an equal |
| `mānātimāna` | superior even to one who is superior |
| `asmimāna` | the five aggregates taken as self or mine |
| `abhimāna` | an unattained distinction taken as attained |
| `ūnamāna` | oneself as only slightly inferior to one greatly superior |
| `mithyāmāna` | oneself as possessing excellence when excellence is absent |

The local prose appears to write `abhimāna` where the sequence calls for the
definition of `atimāna`; the controlled list follows the stated sevenfold
enumeration while preserving this source caution.

**`Navavidhā mānāḥ` — nine conventional modes**

The nine judgments concern being superior, equal, or inferior, together with
having or not having someone superior, equal, or inferior. The Bhāṣya derives
them from `mānātimāna`, `ūnamāna`, and `māna`, with their allocation varying
according to the comparative relation intended.

**`Pravṛttibheda` — difference in operation**

The seven are not seven substances. One generic elevation of consciousness is
differentiated according to the way it operates.

**`Paryavasthāna` — afflictive occupation**

An active, enveloping manifestation of affliction. Here it conditions
deliberate misconduct from killing through false speech.

**`Samudācāra` — active manifestation**

The Bhāṣya distinguishes an anuśaya's remaining un-abandoned from its current
manifestation. Structural availability does not entail present occurrence.

## 8. Logical Determination

The qualitative classification is:

```text
Mana
    -- differentiated by PravrittiBheda --> SevenManaTypes
```

The secondary comparative system is:

```text
mānātimāna
ūnamāna
māna
    → nine conventional self-positionings
```

Every determinate comparison includes at least:

```text
measuring standpoint
    + comparative other
    + standard of rank or attainment
    + posited relation
    → determinate māna
```

The Path-status distinction is:

```text
NotYetAbandoned(x)
    ⇏ PresentlyManifest(x)

PresentlyManifest(x)
    → NotYetAbandoned(x)
```

Thus the system must not infer actual occurrence merely from the persistence
of the underlying possibility.

For deliberate harmful occupation:

```text
Paryavasthana(x)
AND HasCultivationAbandonableSupport(x)
    → CultivationAbandonable(x)
```

This continues the `ālambana` rule: the cognitive support helps determine the
valid removal-mode.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 5.10 introduces Measure into the subjective development. The Subject is
already inherent in Concrete Existence, but under anuśaya it elevates itself
by making its position relative to another the governing truth. The error is
not comparison as such; it is the afflicted standard and appropriative
self-relation through which the comparison is made.

```text
objective display
    presents determinate differences

afflicted measure
    converts difference into self-elevation

māna
    is the resulting malformed Subject-position
```

This is why formal correctness cannot guarantee truth. A comparison may be
computed consistently while its standpoint, standard, or claimed attainment
is false. The correction must reach the measure itself.

The distinction between un-abandoned and manifest is equally important for
implementation. A latent capacity, an active state, and a completed
transformation are different determinations:

```text
available disposition
    ≠ active occurrence
    ≠ enacted consequence
    ≠ abandoned disposition
```

This is where Anuśaya begins to yield an executable state model. The Product
must preserve not only what kind of determination exists, but whether it is
available, manifest, enacted, or removed. That is Organon reconstruction; the
textual basis is the Bhāṣya's explicit denial that every un-abandoned
cultivation-removable affliction must presently occur in an ārya.
