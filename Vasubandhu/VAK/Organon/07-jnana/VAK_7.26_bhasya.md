# VAK_7.26 Bhāṣya — Cultivated Levels and the History of Acquisition

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> yadvairāgyāya yallābhas tatra cādhaś ca bhāvyate |
> sāsravāś ca kṣayajñāne labdhapūrvaṃ na bhāvyate || 7.26 ||

> For whichever level's detachment the path operates, and whichever level is
> acquired, there and below [uncontaminated knowledge] is cultivated. At
> exhaustion-knowledge, contaminated [qualities] also [are cultivated]. What
> has been acquired before is not cultivated.

New question: on which path, knowledge of how many *levels*. Category-count
was 7.22–7.25. Four senses of cultivation: 7.27.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.26.md`](./VAK_7.26.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Now, on which path is knowledge of how many levels cultivated?
>
> Conventional knowledge first: future [knowledge] of the path's level and of
> the level first acquired is cultivated.
>
> Uncontaminated knowledge is not only of the path's level. For whichever
> level's detachment, and whichever level is gained by detachment, uncontaminated
> knowledges of that level and of lower levels enter cultivation — on both kinds
> of path, from the preparatory onward.
>
> At exhaustion-knowledge, all-level contaminated qualities also — those acquired
> through exhaustion-knowledge — enter cultivation: mindfulness of breathing,
> applications of mindfulness, immeasurables, liberations, and so on.
>
> What is acquired for the first time is cultivated. What was lost and is
> acquired again is not cultivated, because it had been cultivated and then
> relinquished.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
conventional: path-level + first-acquired level
uncontaminated: target-level + acquired-level + below (eligible supports)
kṣaya: + wholesome contaminated qualities of that acquisition, all-level range
history: first acquisition counts; reacquisition after bhāvita-utsṛṣṭa does not
```

## 5. Coordinates, Not One Rule

`Sāsravāḥ` = `guṇāḥ` — wholesome contaminated qualities, not “impure knowledges,”
not unwholesome states. `Sarvabhūmika` does not give every quality every support.
Level-extension does not invent uncontaminated knowledge where the chapter
forbade it.

`Labdhapūrvam` is not “anything already possessed.” Exact case: cultivated,
relinquished, reacquired. Possession now does not show that history.

“Whatever is acquired is cultivated” at 410|15 is immediately qualified.
Do not freeze the unqualified line.

## 6. Sovereignty Image

`Svacittādhirājya`: wholesome dharmas come forward through their acquisitions.
Source image, not an Organon add-on. Does not mean automatic present exercise
of every lower capacity.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first half answers the *uncontaminated* level-question;
- conventional has its own narrower preface;
- third pāda changes subject to qualities;
- fourth pāda = reacquisition after loss.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Technē now has vertical and historical axes. First establishment ≠ restoration
≠ continued exercise. Paramārtha attributes as Appearance include these
wholesome contaminated qualities at kṣaya — still Appearance of determinations,
not the Absolute as Appearans. 7.27 will refuse to reduce cultivation to
acquisition alone.
