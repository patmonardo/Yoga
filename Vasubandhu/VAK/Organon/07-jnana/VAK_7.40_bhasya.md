# VAK_7.40 Bhāṣya — Linguistic Levels, Complete Acquisition, and Six Final-Limit Qualities

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāmadhyāneṣu dharme vit vāci prathamakāmayoḥ |
> vikalābhir na tallābhī ṣaḍ ete prāntakoṭikāḥ || 7.40 ||

> The discriminating knowledge concerning dharma is in the desire domain and
> the dhyānas; that concerning speech is in the first dhyāna and desire domain.
> One does not acquire that with incomplete members. These six are final-limit
> qualities.

`Dharme vit` kept. Prose: `dharmapratisaṃvit pañcabhūmikā`. Definition of
prāntakoṭika-dhyāna: 7.41.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Dharma-pratisaṃvid belongs to five levels — desire-domain and the four
> dhyānas — because above there are no name-collections.
>
> Speech and nirukti have one meaning here. Nirukti-pratisaṃvid belongs to
> the desire-domain and first dhyāna, because above there is no vitarka.
>
> Whoever has one necessarily has four. One is not an acquirer of the
> pratisaṃvids by incomplete members.
>
> These six qualities beginning with araṇā: their acquisition is by the power
> of final-limit dhyāna.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
dharma: 5 levels (nāmakāya ends)
nirukti: 2 levels (vitarka ends)
co-acquisition: one ⇔ four
six: araṇā, praṇidhi, four pratisaṃvid — acquired through prāntakoṭika power
nirukti still not fourth-dhyāna-supported
```

## 5. Object-Availability Sets the Ceiling

Not arbitrary floors. Name-complexes gone → no dharma-pratisaṃvid. Vitarka
gone → no nirukti-pratisaṃvid. Ordinary linguistic skill ≠ the fourfold
attainment. Complete possession ≠ interchangeable functions.

Shared acquisition-ground ≠ identical intrinsic support. Nirukti is the sixth
dependent quality; it is not itself a fourth-dhyāna form.

Preparation: calculation, Buddha-word, linguistic science, medicine, reasoning
— five names against four knowledges, attachment unclear. Alternative: Buddha-
word alone for all four. Disagreement kept.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first pāda = five-level dharma-pratisaṃvid;
- `vāci` = nirukti, two levels;
- `vikalābhiḥ` denies incomplete lābha;
- `ete` = the six from araṇā.
