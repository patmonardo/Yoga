# VAK_7.46 Bhāṣya — Vidyā: Intrinsic Status and Designation through the Continuum

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> aśaikṣyantyā tadākhye dve tatsaṃtānamudbhavāt |
> iṣṭe śaikṣasya nokte tu vidye sāvidyasaṃtateḥ || 7.46 ||

> The last is beyond training. The two bear that designation because they arise
> in that continuum. They are admitted for the learner, but are not called
> vidyās, because the continuum is accompanied by ignorance.

`Tatsaṃtānamudbhavāt` unrestored; meaning from prose. Next: three prātihāryas.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> āsāṃ paramārthena āsravakṣayajñānasākṣātkriyaivāśaikṣī vidyā /
> anye dve aśaikṣyasaṃtānasaṃbhūtatvād aśaikṣyāv ucyete /
> naiva tu te śaikṣyo nāśaikṣyau /
> kiṃ punar ete abhijñe śaikṣasya nocyete / yataḥ śaikṣyau vidye nocyete /
> na hi sāvidyasaṃtāne vidyāvyavasthānaṃ yujyate / punar apy avidyābhibhavāt /

**Witness.** Pradhan 423|20–424|07. `Tadāsye`, `śaikṣsya` unrestored. Voices:
Vasubandhu; objector (why not learner-vidyās?).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Of these, in the strict sense, only direct realization of influx-exhaustion
> is the beyond-training vidyā. The other two are called beyond-training because
> they arise in a beyond-training continuum. In themselves they are neither
> learner nor beyond-training.
>
> Then are these two higher knowledges not admitted for the learner, since they
> are not called learner vidyās? They are admitted for the learner, but not
> called vidyās. Establishment as vidyā is not fitting in a continuum accompanied
> by ignorance — because ignorance can overpower again.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
strict aśaikṣī = exhaustion only
other two: name from continuum, not from own nature
objection: then denied to the learner?
reply: capacity admitted; vidyā-name withheld (renewed avidyā possible)
```

## 5. Occurrence ≠ Designation

Not: the learner has no knowledge. Not: every aśaikṣa cognition is intrinsically
aśaikṣa. Not: the two become śaikṣa-by-nature when a learner has them. Ethical
classes from 7.45 stand. `Paramārthena` here = strict sense of the name — not
the project overlay. Renewed overpowering ≠ this memory later turns false.

## 6. Four Cells

| | in itself | as named here |
|---|---|---|
| exhaustion | aśaikṣa | aśaikṣa-vidyā |
| former abodes / death-rebirth in aśaikṣa | neither | aśaikṣa-vidyā by continuum |
| those two in a learner | neither | admitted abhijñā; not vidyā |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `antyā` = third of 7.45;
- `tadākhye` = the first two named aśaikṣa;
- `iṣṭe` admits the capacities;
- `nokte` withholds the name.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Vidyā is not an event-trophy. The Idea holds the cognition; the continuum is
whether avidyā can still govern. Local correctness inside a learner is Concept
without the Idea's closure. Appearance would either deny the śraṇaka his two
abhijñās or hand him the vidyā-name. Ārya-path still not Arhat-obsession: the
cut is designation, not contempt for the learner.
