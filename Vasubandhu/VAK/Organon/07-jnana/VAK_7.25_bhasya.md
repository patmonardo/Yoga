# VAK_7.25 Bhāṣya — Exhaustion-Knowledge and the Nine-, Ten-, and Eightfold Ranges

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> navānāṃ tu kṣayajñāne akopyasya daśa bhāvanā |
> tatsaṃcare 'ntyamuktau ca proktaśeṣe 'ṣṭabhāvanā || 7.25 ||

> At knowledge of exhaustion, there is cultivation of nine knowledges; for
> the immovable practitioner, of ten. Also at the final liberation path in
> transition to that state, ten. In the remaining stated cases, eight.

Ninth Bhavāgra-liberation path = kṣayajñāna. Completes 7.23's eight vimukti
paths and 7.24's Bhavāgra-ānantarya six.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.25.md`](./VAK_7.25.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> yas tv akopyadharmā bhavati tasya daśānāṃ jñānānāṃ bhāvanā /

> śaikṣasyaivam /

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> The ninth liberation path of detachment from Bhavāgra is exhaustion-knowledge.
> There, cultivation of nine knowledges, except non-arising-knowledge.
>
> For one of immovable nature, cultivation of ten — from acquisition of
> non-arising-knowledge.
>
> Also for one passing into immovability, at the final liberation path:
> cultivation of ten.
>
> What is the remainder? [enumerated trainee cases.] In all those, eight
> knowledges are cultivated — by future cultivation, leaving out exhaustion-
> and non-arising-knowledge. Thus for the trainee.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
Bhavāgra ninth vimukti = kṣaya → T minus anutpāda = 9
immovable at that occasion → T = 10 (anutpāda acquired)
final vimukti of transition into immovability → 10
proktaśeṣa, future cultivation → T minus kṣaya and anutpāda = 8
then aśaikṣa / pṛthagjana qualifications — not this eight
```

## 5. Not a Scale

Eight, nine, ten are different conditions of the two terminal knowledges
(7.07: accomplished work vs non-repetition). Not “more knowledge.” Not ten
present events. Future cultivation ≠ guaranteed later arising.

Eightfold *includes* conventional and paracitta. Do not import 7.23–7.24
ānantarya exclusions into these vimukti / prayoga / viśeṣa rooms.

`Śaikṣasyaivam` does not make immovability a trainee attainment. Keep the
eight-path group attached to the immovability undertaking; its last vimukti
is already the tenfold cell.

Two higher-knowledge vimukti paths: no future cultivation because avyākṛta.
Not named here. Ordinary person: conventional and paracitta in listed rooms;
paracitta out at nirvedhabhāgīya (entourage of Seeing). Elsewhere on a new
path: future conventional only. Then 7.26 asks levels of what is cultivated.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first locative named as ninth Bhavāgra-vimukti;
- ten = acquisition of anutpāda;
- `tat` = immovability;
- `proktaśeṣa` enumerated, not a dump-bin.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Technē of completion. Nine is work done without the further holding of
non-repetition; ten includes that holding. Paramārtha attributes as
Appearance of the Absolute would have to carry *which* terminal determination
is acquired. The Absolute as Appearans is not “all ten cultivated.”
