# VAK_7.09 Bhāṣya — Object-Range and Counteractive Efficacy

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dharmajñānaṃ nirodhe yan mārge vā bhāvanāpathe |
> tridhātupratipakṣas tat kāmadhātos tu nānvayam || 7.9 ||

> That dharma-knowledge which concerns cessation or the Path in the Path
> of Cultivation is a counteragent to the three domains; anvaya-knowledge,
> however, is not a counteragent to the desire domain.

This qualifies 7.08's pratipakṣa assignment. It does not replace it.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.09.md`](./VAK_7.09.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> sakalasya sakalapratipakṣatvāt kāmadhātupratipakṣo dharmajñānam ity uktam /
> api tu /
> dharmajñānaṃ nirodhe yan mārge vā bhāvanāpathe /
> tridhātupratipakṣas tat /
> nirodhamārgajñāne bhāvanāmārgasaṃgṛhīte tridhātupratipakṣaḥ /
> kāmadhātos tu nānvayam /
> anvayajñānam tu sarvathā nāsti kāmadhātupratipakṣaḥ /

**Witness.** Pradhan 395|10–16. Joined opening `dharmajñānanirodhe` in raw
quotation; study separates `dharmajñānaṃ nirodhe`. Forms `pratīpakṣo`,
`kāmadhātuporatipakṣaḥ` unrestored. `Sakala` sentence compressed; working
sense: complete dharma-knowledge as complete desire-domain counteraction —
not each event abandoning every desire-domain affliction at once. Voice:
Vasubandhu. No opponent.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Because of being the complete counteragent of the complete [desire domain],
> dharma-knowledge was said to be the counteragent to the desire domain.
>
> However:
>
> That dharma-knowledge which concerns cessation or the Path in the Path of
> Cultivation — that is a counteragent to the three domains.
>
> The cessation- and Path-knowledges included in the Path of Cultivation are
> counteragents to the three domains.
>
> Anvaya-[knowledge], however, is not [a counteragent] of the desire domain.
>
> Anvaya-knowledge is in no way a counteragent to the desire domain.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
7.08 assignment recalled (dharma → desire-domain counteraction)
    → api tu: qualification
    → dharma + (nirodha or mārga) + included in Cultivation
         → counteragent to three domains
    → anvaya sarvathā not counteragent to desire domain
```

Two locatives: `nirodhe` / `mārge` = truth concerned; `bhāvanāpathe` =
Path-membership. They are not the same relation.

## 5. Object-Range Is Not Counteractive Range

7.03 assigned truth-fields. 7.09 assigns efficacy. Inferring the second
from the first is the mistake this verse exists to block.

Positive rule — all conditions required:

```text
dharma-knowledge
AND (cessation-knowledge OR Path-knowledge)
AND included in Cultivation
    → counteragent across the three existential domains
```

Not: every dharma-knowledge counters three domains. Suffering- and
origination-dharma-knowledge are not in this extension. Seeing is not
interchangeable with Cultivation (VAK 6.28: sixteenth begins Cultivation).

Negative rule — categorical:

```text
anvaya-knowledge
    → in no way (sarvathā) counteragent to the desire domain
```

No reciprocal exception. Wider reach one way does not prove the other way.
Dharma-knowledge does not become anvaya-knowledge by gaining three-domain
efficacy. Cessation known under dharma-knowledge is not thereby a
desire-domain dharma.

`Tridhātu` here = kāma, rūpa, ārūpya — not Chapter 1's eighteen Domains.
`Pratipakṣa` = opposition to the afflictive field, not demolition of realms
as places, not mere information about them.

## 6. Why Pratipakṣa Had to Be Its Own Ground

7.08 listed counteraction separately from aspect and object-support. 7.09
shows why: the same knowledge-kind can have a stable object-field and a
conditionally wider efficacy. One criterion would have hidden the `api tu`.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `yat … tat` picks the qualified dharma-knowledge, not the whole class;
- `bhāvanāpathe` = `bhāvanāmārgasaṃgṛhīta`;
- `sarvathā` strengthens the anvaya exclusion;
- `nirodhamārgajñāne` are forms of the governing dharma-knowledge.

Literal Organon line already carries this. Keep “counteragent.”

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Jñāna turns around artha: what is held (object-range) is not yet what the
holding can undo (counteractive range). Meaning does not automatically
become work. Work does not rewrite the object into another knowledge-kind.
That is Practical Reason specifying efficacy without a Concept-triad.

`Sarvathā` on the anvaya ban is the same adverb as 1.01's darkness-destroyed
in every way — here as a *limit* of a Particular knowing, not as Buddha Mind.
The ārya path still has to close as instruction; this verse only fixes an
asymmetry of counteraction.

Heaven and hell are not the topic. Cultivation is Path-membership, not a
reward-realm. Sevā is keeping object and efficacy from collapsing into
Appearance (“pure knowledge fixes everything upstairs and downstairs”).
