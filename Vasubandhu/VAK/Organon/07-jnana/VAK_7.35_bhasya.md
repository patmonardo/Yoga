# VAK_7.35 Bhāṣya — Shared Qualities and the Distribution of Attainment

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> śiṣyasādhāraṇā anye dharmāḥ kecit pṛthagjanaiḥ |
> araṇāpraṇidhijñānapratisaṃvidguṇādayaḥ || 7.35 ||

> Other qualities are shared with disciples, some with ordinary persons —
> qualities beginning with freedom from conflict, knowledge through resolve,
> and discriminating knowledges.

Eighteen closed. These are still Buddha-qualities; sharing is the new
relation. `Kecit` and `yathāyogam` govern the ordinary-person clause.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.35.md`](./VAK_7.35.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> ime tāvad aṣṭādaśa buddhānām āveṇikā dharmā ucyante /
> śrāvakasādhāraṇās tv anye guṇā buddhānām /
> ke punas ta iti yathāyogam /
> araṇāpraṇidhijñānapratisaṃvidabhijñādhyānārūpyāpramāṇavimokṣābhibhvāyatanakṛtsnāyatanādayaḥ /

**Witness.** Pradhan 416|17–417|02. Long compound compressed. Araṇā
introduction 417|02–05 belongs to 7.36's determination. Voice: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> These, then, are called the eighteen distinctive qualities of the Buddhas.
>
> But other excellent qualities of Buddhas are shared with śrāvakas. Some
> with ordinary persons. Which? As applicable: freedom from conflict,
> knowledge through resolve, discriminating knowledges, higher knowledges,
> dhyānas, formless attainments, immeasurables, liberations, spheres of
> mastery, totality-spheres, and so on.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
āveṇika closed
    → other Buddha-qualities
    → shared with śrāvakas
    → some also with pṛthagjana, yathāyogam
    → open catalogue (not a second eighteen)
    → 7.36: araṇā's constitution
```

## 5. Sharing ≠ Equivalence

Buddha-possession ≠ exclusive possession. Shared kind ≠ every disciple has
it. Shared kind ≠ every ordinary person has it. Shared kind ≠ same range or
mastery. `Pṛthagjana` is practitioner-status, not “anyone kind.” `Śiṣya` =
śrāvaka here.

`Ādi` opens the class; it does not dump every human capacity into the list.

## 6. Opening Hint of Araṇā (Not This Verse's Result)

An arhat, knowing suffering from afflictions and himself a distinguished
recipient of offerings, produces knowledge so that attachment, hatred, and
pride do not arise toward him. Relational: others' afflictions with him as
object. Limits: 7.36.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `anye dharmāḥ` = other *Buddha*-qualities;
- `śiṣya` = śrāvaka;
- `kecit` + `yathāyogam` restrict the pṛthagjana share;
- `guṇādayaḥ` expanded, not closed at three names.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Same Qualitative Concept-kind can be instantiated in different continua.
The Idea keeps those Concepts; it does not make the instantiations equal.
Āveṇika remains Singular-closure. Appearance would either seal three
ontologies or flatten all attainments. Technē starts here with distribution;
individual analyses supply the masks.
