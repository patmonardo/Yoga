# VAK_7.39 Bhāṣya — Object, Knowledge-Constitution, and Level of the Pratisaṃvids

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vāṅmārgālambanā cāsau nava jñānāni sarvabhūḥ |
> daśa ṣaḍvārthasaṃvit sā sarvatrānye tu sāṃvṛtam || 7.39 ||

> That [fourth] has speech and the Path as its objects; nine knowledges; every
> level. Meaning-discrimination: ten or six, everywhere. The other two:
> conventional knowledge.

`Asau` = pratibhāna. Dharma- and nirukti-levels: 7.40.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.39.md`](./VAK_7.39.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> vāk ca mārgaś ca tasyā ālambanam /
> navajñānasvabhāvā pratibhānapratisaṃvid anyatra nirodhajñānāt /
> sarvabhūmikā cāsau kāmadhātau yāvad bhavāgre vāṅmārgayor anyatarālambanāt /
> arthapratisaṃvit sarvadharmāś ced arthā daśa jñānāni /
> nirvāṇaṃ ced arthaḥ ṣaḍ jñānāni /
> dharmānvayanirodhakṣayānutpādasaṃvṛtijñānāni /
> sā punar eṣārthapratisaṃvit sarvabhūmikā /
> anye tu dve dharmaniruktipratisaṃvidau saṃvṛtijñānasvabhāve nāmakāyādivāgālambanasvabhāvatvāt /

**Witness.** Pradhan 418|19–419|11. `Ālmvanam` unrestored. Voice: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Speech and Path are its object-support. Pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid has the nature
> of nine knowledges, except cessation-knowledge. It belongs to all levels,
> from the desire-domain through Bhavāgra, because either speech or Path may
> be its object.
>
> If all dharmas are the meanings, artha-pratisaṃvid is ten knowledges. If
> nirvāṇa is the meaning, six: dharma, anvaya, cessation, exhaustion,
> non-arising, conventional. This too belongs to all levels.
>
> The other two, dharma- and nirukti-pratisaṃvid, have the nature of conventional
> knowledge, because their objects are name-collections and the rest, and speech.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
pratibhāna: vāk | mārga; T minus nirodha; all levels (either object)
artha: scope of artha → 10 or 6; all levels
dharma / nirukti: conventional (linguistic objects); levels next
```

## 5. Three Axes

Object ≠ constitution ≠ level. All-level does not put every category on every
level. Speech or Path ≠ speech is the Path. Conventional here is the right
nature for names and speech, not a demotion.

Six ≠ ten. Two definitions of `artha`, two ranges — same pattern as 7.29.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `asau` = fourth;
- nine by explicit nirodha-exclusion;
- `daśa ṣaḍ vā` = two meanings of artha;
- `anye` = dharma and nirukti.
