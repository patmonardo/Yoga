# VAK_7.33 Bhāṣya — Great Compassion: Constitution, Greatness, and Eight Distinctions

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> mahākṛpā saṃvṛtidhīḥ saṃbhārākāragocaraiḥ |
> samatvād ādhimātryāc ca nānākaraṇam aṣṭadhā || 7.33 ||

> Great compassion is conventional knowing. It is great through accumulation,
> aspect, and object-field, through equality and surpassing intensity. The
> differentiation is eightfold.

Last of the eighteen. Prose uses `mahākaruṇā`. Five grounds of greatness ≠
the eightfold comparison with ordinary compassion.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.33.md`](./VAK_7.33.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Great compassion has conventional knowledge as its nature. Otherwise it
> would not be established as taking all beings as object, nor as having the
> aspects of threefold suffering.
>
> Why is it called great? From accumulation: it comes forth from a great
> accumulation of merit and knowledge. From aspect: threefold suffering.
> From object-support: the three domains. From equality: equal operation
> toward all beings. From surpassing intensity: there is none greater than it.
>
> What is the difference of compassion and great compassion? Differentiation
> is eightfold: by nature, non-hatred / non-delusion; by aspect, one / three
> sufferings; by object, one / three domains; by level, four dhyānas / fourth
> dhyāna; by continuum, disciples and the rest / Buddha; by acquisition,
> desire-domain detachment / Bhavāgra detachment; by non-protection /
> protection; and [the compressed last pair].

## 5. Conventional Here Is Not Inferior

Classification, so the field can be beings and their sufferings. Not every
conventional cognition is great compassion. Object-range (three domains) ≠
support (fourth dhyāna) ≠ acquisition (Bhavāgra-detachment). Equal operation
≠ identical act toward every being. Protection ≠ every being immediately
liberated. Four-dhyāna / kāma-detachment locate *technical* compassion, not
every kindness.

Threefold suffering: duḥkha-duḥkhatā, vipariṇāma, saṃskāra — study gloss;
this sentence only says three.

Do not replace the eighth pair with adhimātratva from the greatness-list.

## 6. Two Tables, Not One

Greatness: accumulation, aspect, field, equality, unsurpassed degree.

Comparison (secure six + protection + open eighth): nature, aspect, object,
support, continuum, acquisition, paritrāṇa, last wording.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `saṃvṛtidhīḥ` = constitutive, with the all-beings reason;
- instrumentals + two ablatives = five grounds;
- `nānākaraṇam aṣṭadhā` = a second question.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Jñāna-model: conventional knowledge. Artha-features: all beings, three
sufferings, three domains. The quality mahākaruṇā is Qualitative Concept
*inside* that Idea — Hegel keeps Concept within the Idea. Paramārtha does
kāmadhātu: the last unshared quality is not flight from the visible enclosure.
Protection is Technē. Revelation is not “conventional, therefore great.”
