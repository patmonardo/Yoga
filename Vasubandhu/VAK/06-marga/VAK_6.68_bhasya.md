# VAK 6.68 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

> *śraddhā vīryaṃ smṛtiḥ prajñā samādhiḥ prītyupekṣaṇe /*  
> *praśrabdhiśīlasaṃkalpāḥ prajñā hi smṛtyupasthitiḥ // VAK_6.68 //*

**Working translation:**

Confidence, energy, recollection, prajñā, samādhi, joy, equanimity, pliancy, ethical discipline, and resolve are the ten. For the establishments of recollection are, in their principal operative reality, prajñā.

The Kārikā file already established the governing problem from 6.67: the thirty-seven Bodhi-aligned determinations are thirty-seven by designation but ten according to underlying dharma-reality. The Bhāṣya now performs that reduction explicitly.

## Bhāṣya Source

Pradhan 383.08–384.02.

> *daśa dravyāṇi sarve bodhipakṣyāḥ / katamāni daśa /*

All the Bodhi-aligned [dharmas] consist of ten dravyas. Which ten?

> *śraddhā vīryaṃ smṛtiḥ prajñā samādhiḥ prītyupekṣaṇe / praśrabdhiśīlasaṃkalpāḥ*

Confidence, energy, recollection, prajñā, samādhi, joy, equanimity, pliancy, ethical discipline, and resolve.

> *ity etāni daśa dravyāṇi / kathaṃ kṛtvā /*

These are the ten dravyas. How is this reckoned?

> *prajñā hi smṛtyupasthitiḥ //*

For the establishments of recollection are prajñā.

> *vīryaṃ samyakprahāṇākhyam ṛddhipādāḥ samādhayaḥ /*

Energy is what is called the right abandonments; the bases of accomplishment are samādhis.

The Bhāṣya then expands the reduction:

> *prajñā-vīrya-samādhi-svabhāvā hi smṛtyupasthāna-samyakprahāṇa-ṛddhipādāḥ*

The establishments of recollection, right abandonments, and bases of accomplishment have respectively the nature of prajñā, energy, and samādhi.

The faculties and powers are taken simply according to their names as confidence, energy, recollection, samādhi, and prajñā. The establishments of recollection, the awakening-factor of discrimination of dharmas, and right view are all prajñā. The right abandonments, the awakening-factor of energy, and right effort are all energy. The bases of accomplishment, the awakening-factor of samādhi, and right samādhi are all samādhi. The awakening-factor of recollection and right recollection are recollection itself.

Vasubandhu then asks:

> *kim avaśiṣyate /*

What remains?

The remaining five are joy, pliancy, equanimity as awakening-factors, right resolve, and the ethical limbs. Thus the Bodhi-aligned dharmas amount to ten dravyas.

Finally a school qualification is recorded:

> *vaibhāṣikāṇām ekādaśa / kāya-vāk-karmaṇor asaṃbhinnatvāt śīlāṅgāni dve dravye iti /*

**Voice: Vaibhāṣika position**

For the Vaibhāṣikas there are eleven, because bodily and verbal action are distinct; therefore the ethical limbs constitute two dravyas.

## Argument Reconstruction

Vasubandhu is not merely listing ten items. He is explaining a systematic compression of the canonical thirty-seven Bodhi-aligned determinations.

The decisive distinction is:

```text
functional designation
    nāman

underlying operative determination
    dravya / svabhāva
```

Several differently named Path-functions can therefore be instances of the same underlying dharma.

The reduction can be reconstructed as follows.

```text
PRAJÑĀ
    4 smṛtyupasthānas
    dharmapravicaya-saṃbodhyaṅga
    samyagdṛṣṭi

VĪRYA
    4 samyakprahāṇas
    vīrya-saṃbodhyaṅga
    samyagvyāyāma

SAMĀDHI
    4 ṛddhipādas
    samādhi-saṃbodhyaṅga
    samyaksamādhi

SMṚTI
    smṛti-saṃbodhyaṅga
    samyaksmṛti

ŚRADDHĀ
    śraddhendriya
    śraddhābala

VĪRYA
    vīryendriya
    vīryabala

SMṚTI
    smṛtīndriya
    smṛtibala

SAMĀDHI
    samādhīndriya
    samādhibala

PRAJÑĀ
    prajñendriya
    prajñābala

REMAINING DISTINCT DRAVYAS
    prīti
    praśrabdhi
    upekṣā
    samyaksaṃkalpa
    śīla
```

Hence the thirty-seven canonical positions do not constitute thirty-seven independent operative realities.

## Why Smṛtyupasthāna Is Prajñā

The most striking example is the first:

> *prajñā hi smṛtyupasthitiḥ*

The practice is named after *smṛti*, but its defining operative nature is *prajñā*.

This confirms a distinction already suggested by the Kārikā analysis:

```text
smṛti
    sustains / recollects the object

prajñā
    discriminates and determines it

smṛtyupasthāna
    Path-formation named through recollection
    but principally constituted by prajñā
```

Thus a practice-name need not be identical with the dharma that supplies its principal liberating operation.

This point also prepares 6.69, where Vasubandhu will explicitly state that these identifications are made by taking the **principal factor** (*pradhānagrahaṇa*), while the full practices include other associated qualities.

## The Five Faculties and Five Powers

The Bhāṣya says that the faculties (*indriya*) and powers (*bala*) are understood *nāmagrāhikayā*—by taking their names directly.

Thus:

```text
śraddhendriya / śraddhābala → śraddhā
vīryendriya / vīryabala     → vīrya
smṛtīndriya / smṛtibala     → smṛti
samādhīndriya / samādhibala → samādhi
prajñendriya / prajñābala   → prajñā
```

Here the functional designation and the underlying dharma are comparatively transparent. The more interesting reductions occur where a whole canonical practice-group bears a different name from its principal operative constituent.

## Vaibhāṣika Qualification: Ten or Eleven?

The Bhāṣya does not leave the tenfold reduction completely uncontested.

**Voice: Vasubandhu** — the thirty-seven reduce to ten dravyas.

**Voice: Vaibhāṣika position** — they reduce to eleven.

The disagreement concerns *śīla*.

Vasubandhu's tenfold enumeration counts ethical discipline as one operative item. The Vaibhāṣikas distinguish bodily action and verbal action as irreducibly distinct, so the ethical limbs supply two dravyas rather than one.

```text
Vasubandhu's count
    śīla = 1
    total = 10

Vaibhāṣika count
    bodily ethical action = 1
    verbal ethical action = 1
    total = 11
```

This is a useful warning against reading *dravya* here as a simple modern notion of "substance." It is an Abhidharma criterion of real differentiability, and schools can dispute the correct level at which that differentiation is made.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — question and tenfold reduction of the thirty-seven Bodhi-aligned dharmas.
- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — identification of the first three groups principally with prajñā, vīrya, and samādhi and the subsequent reduction of repeated functions.
- **Voice: Vaibhāṣika position** — eleven rather than ten because bodily and verbal ethical action are distinct.

## New Technical Terms

- **nāmagrāhikayā** — by taking according to the name; **status:** rare in the Kārikās; **function:** explains cases where the functional name transparently identifies its underlying dharma.
- **svabhāva** — operative nature in this classificatory passage; **status:** previously encountered; **function:** identifies the dharma principally constituting a named Path-group.
- **dharmapravicaya-saṃbodhyaṅga** — awakening-factor of discrimination/investigation of dharmas; **status:** not individually named in VAK 6.68; **function:** example reduced to prajñā.
- **asaṃbhinna** — not merged, distinct; **status:** Bhāṣya-specific in this unit; **function:** ground for the Vaibhāṣika elevenfold count.
- **śīlāṅga** — ethical limb(s) of the Path; **status:** represented compactly as *śīla* in the Kārikā; **function:** locus of the ten-versus-eleven disagreement.

## Doctrinal and Logical Determination

The essential result is not merely numerical compression. It is a distinction between **systematic function** and **operative identity**.

```text
one operative dharma
    can occupy several canonical functions

one canonical practice-name
    can designate a complex whose principal factor
    is another dharma
```

Thus identity and difference operate at two levels:

```text
Difference by Path-position
    37

Identity by principal operative dharma
    10
```

The thirty-seven are not rendered unreal by reduction to ten. Their difference remains valid as functional articulation of the Path. Conversely, the ten are not ten additional factors beside the thirty-seven; they are the operative determinations through which the thirty-seven functions are realized.

This is why the formula from 6.67 must be preserved precisely:

```text
nāmataḥ 37
    functional articulation

drav yataḥ 10
    operative differentiation
```

## Translation Consequences for the Kārikā

The Bhāṣya strongly supports translating *dravyataḥ* here by something like **"according to operative reality"** or **"according to underlying dharma-reality"**, rather than importing a heavy metaphysical sense of "substance."

It also confirms that *prajñā hi smṛtyupasthitiḥ* should not be flattened into "mindfulness is wisdom." The claim is technical and classificatory: the establishments of recollection are counted under prajñā because prajñā is their principal operative nature.

The Kārikā's ten therefore should be read as a reduction according to principal dharma, not as an exhaustive statement that no other associated factors occur in those practices. VAK 6.69 immediately clarifies this.

## Restrained Organon Contact

The logical structure here is unusually clean:

```text
one reality
    → multiple functions

multiple functions
    → recoverable principal determination
```

This is not a collapse of plurality into abstract unity. The thirty-seven remain differentiated according to their role in the Path, while the ten identify recurring operative principles across those roles.

That makes 6.68 a strong example of **systematic identity through functional difference**.

But the Bhāṣya itself prevents us from absolutizing the reduction. The Vaibhāṣika ten/eleven disagreement shows that determining the proper unit of operative reality is itself an analytical problem. The system does not merely announce identity; it must justify the level at which identity is asserted.

For the Dream of Philosophy this remains a particularly useful confirmation rather than a new principle: one determination can regenerate itself through many functional positions without those positions becoming either independent substances or empty names. The genuinely new question raised here is narrower: **the level of identity must itself be rationally determined**, as the ten/eleven dispute demonstrates. That point should be watched in later Organon work rather than promoted yet into a global thesis.

## Determinate Summary

VAK 6.68 supplies the ten operative dharmas underlying the thirty-seven Bodhi-aligned Path-functions. Vasubandhu reduces the establishments of recollection to prajñā, the right abandonments to energy, and the bases of accomplishment to samādhi; he likewise identifies repeated awakening-, Path-, faculty-, and power-functions with their corresponding operative dharmas. Functional multiplicity therefore does not entail an equal multiplicity of dravyas.

At the same time, the Vaibhāṣikas count eleven rather than ten because they distinguish bodily and verbal ethical action. The passage consequently teaches not only reduction but also the need to justify the precise level at which operative identity is claimed.
