# VAK 6.16 Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

**sa dharma-smṛtyupasthāne samastālambane sthitaḥ |**  
**ānityaduḥkhataḥ śūnyānātmatas tān vipaśyati || 6.16 ||**

Established in the Dharma-Foundation of Recollection whose object-support is the totality, the practitioner sees those objects distinctly as impermanent, suffering, empty, and not-self.

## Bhāṣya Source

> evaṃ kāyādyālambanāni smṛtyupasthānānyabhyasya
>
> sa dharmasmṛtyupasthāne samastālambane sthitaḥ /
> ānatyaduḥkhataḥ śūnyānātmatastānvipaśyati //
>
> saṃbhinnālambane dharmasmṛtyupasthāne sthitastān kāyādīn sarvānabhisamasya caturbhirākāraiḥ paśyati /
> anityato duḥkhataḥ śūnyato 'nātmataśca /
>
> tat ūṣmagatotpattiḥ
>
> tasmāddharmasmṛtyupasthānādevamabhyastāt krameṇoṣmagataṃ nāma kuśalamūlamutpadyate /
> ūṣmagatamivoṣmagatam /
> kleśendhanadahanasyāryamārgāgneḥ pūrvarūpatvāt /
>
> taccatuḥsatyagocaram /
>
> tadūṣmagataṃ prākarṣikatvāccatuḥsatyālambanam /
>
> ṣoḍaśākāram /
>
> duḥkhaṃ caturbhirākāraiḥ paśyaty anityato duḥkhataḥ śūnyato 'nātmataśca /
> samudayaṃ caturbhir hetutaḥ samudayataḥ prabhavataḥ pratyayataśca /
> nirodhaṃ caturbhiḥ nirodhataḥ śāntataḥ praṇītato niḥsaraṇataśca /
> mārgaṃ caturbhir mārgato nyāyataḥ pratipattito nairyāṇikataśca /

## Natural Argument

Vasubandhu has now completed the differentiated cultivation of the four smṛtyupasthānas. The first three have unmixed object-supports, while the fourth, dharma-smṛtyupasthāna, can operate in two ways. It may take dharmas alone, or it may take body, feeling, cognition, and dharmas together.

VAK 6.16 concerns this second case. The practitioner is established in a **saṃbhinna-ālambana dharma-smṛtyupasthāna**: a Dharma-Foundation whose object-support is combined. The previously differentiated fields are gathered into one articulated total field and examined under four universal modes:

```text
anitya
    impermanent

duḥkha
    suffering / unstable

śūnya
    empty

anātman
    not-self
```

From repeated cultivation of this totalized examination, a new wholesome root arises: **uṣmagata**, Heat.

## Voice

**Voice:** Vasubandhu, reporting the Abhidharma path sequence and explaining its causal logic.

No major objection-response exchange structures this unit. The passage is primarily genetic and classificatory.

## The Synthetic Object-Support

The key Bhāṣya term is:

**saṃbhinna-ālambana** — combined or mixed object-support.

The first three smṛtyupasthānas are **asaṃbhinna-ālambana** when each takes only its own field:

```text
kāya
vedanā
citta
```

Dharma-smṛtyupasthāna can also be unmixed when it takes dharmas alone. But when it takes two, three, or all four fields together, it becomes combined.

Thus 6.16 does not introduce an undifferentiated totality. The sequence is:

```text
differentiation
    body
    feeling
    cognition
    dharmas
        ↓
recollection of their distinctions
        ↓
combined object-support
        ↓
synthetic examination of the whole field
```

The total field is therefore **articulated before it is synthesized**.

## Four Universal Modes

The practitioner sees the entire combined field through four ākāras:

```text
anityataḥ
    as impermanent

duḥkhataḥ
    as suffering

śūnyataḥ
    as empty

anātmataḥ
    as not-self
```

This is the completion of the movement announced in 6.14:

```text
svalakṣaṇa
    differentiated intrinsic determination

sāmānyalakṣaṇa
    universal determination
```

The fields are first discriminated according to what each is, then brought under universal determinations valid across the total field.

## The Arising of Heat

The Bhāṣya then gives a crucial genetic statement:

> From dharma-smṛtyupasthāna cultivated in this way, gradually there arises a wholesome root called **uṣmagata**.

So Heat is not an arbitrary stage-name. It is **generated from the repeatedly cultivated synthetic examination**.

Vasubandhu explains the metaphor:

> it is called Heat because it is the preliminary sign of the fire of the Noble Path which burns the fuel of the afflictions.

The structure is exact:

```text
kleśa
    fuel

āryamārga
    fire

uṣmagata
    heat preceding the fire
```

Heat is therefore not yet the Noble Path itself. It is the first sensible sign, as it were, that the causal conditions for that fire have become sufficiently developed.

This gives a particularly important refinement of the śakti problem from 6.01:

```text
preparatory cultivation
    ↓
synthetic Prajñā-field
    ↓
uṣmagata
    ↓
prefiguration of āryamārga-agni
    ↓
future definitive kleśa-prahāṇa
```

The special power of the supramundane Path is still not present, but a determinate precursor of it now is.

## Four Truths and Sixteen Modes

Heat takes the **Four Noble Truths** as its object-support and examines them through sixteen modes.

### Duḥkha

```text
anitya
    impermanent

duḥkha
    suffering

śūnya
    empty

anātman
    not-self
```

### Samudaya

```text
hetu
    cause

samudaya
    arising

prabhava
    production

pratyaya
    condition
```

### Nirodha

```text
nirodha
    cessation

śānta
    peace

praṇīta
    excellent / sublime

niḥsaraṇa
    release / emergence out
```

### Mārga

```text
mārga
    path

nyāya
    right method / regularity

pratipatti
    practice / realization

nairyāṇika
    leading out
```

Thus the synthetic field of 6.16 does not terminate in a vague total vision. It generates a more highly articulated sixteen-mode cognition of the Four Truths.

## New Technical Terms

- **saṃbhinna-ālambana** — combined object-support; status: newly decisive in this passage; function: names the synthetic object-field of dharma-smṛtyupasthāna.
- **asaṃbhinna-ālambana** — unmixed object-support; status: implicit from 6.15 and clarified by the Bhāṣya; function: restricted single-field examination.
- **uṣmagata** — Heat; status: new major Path stage; function: first wholesome root arising from the mature cultivation of the synthetic Dharma-Foundation.
- **pūrvarūpa** — preliminary sign or precursor; status: important explanatory term; function: explains Heat as the precursor of the fire of the Noble Path.
- **āryamārga-agni** — fire of the Noble Path; status: metaphorical doctrinal expression; function: what burns the fuel of affliction.
- **ṣoḍaśākāra** — sixteen modes; status: newly systematized here; function: four modes for each of the Four Truths.

## Relation to 6.14–6.15

The sequence now becomes very clear:

```text
6.14
    śamatha accomplished
    ↓
    four fields examined
    through two lakṣaṇas

6.15
    intrinsic nature = Prajñā
    ↓
    Prajñā + associates + object-support
    ↓
    four smṛtyupasthānas oppose four inversions

6.16
    differentiated fields synthesized
    into saṃbhinna-ālambana
    ↓
    total field seen through four universal modes
    ↓
    uṣmagata arises
```

The enumerations are therefore stages in a controlled transformation of the cognitive field.

## Light Organon Contact

Without leaving the Kośa, we can safely say that Vasubandhu has now supplied a recognizable architecture of synthesis:

```text
analysis of determinate fields
    ↓
retention of distinctions
    ↓
construction of a combined field
    ↓
universal predication across that field
    ↓
emergence of a new Path-capacity
```

The significant point is not merely that there are four objects and four predicates. The operative fact is that **their ordered combination produces the next stage of cultivation**.

## Determinate Summary

VAK 6.16 is the first unmistakably synthetic stage of the formal vipaśyanā sequence.

```text
body
feeling
cognition
dharmas
    ↓
combined object-support
    ↓
seen as
    impermanent
    suffering
    empty
    not-self
    ↓
repeated cultivation
    ↓
uṣmagata
    Heat
    ↓
precursor of the Noble Path-fire
```

The Path is no longer merely classifying objects. It is reorganizing differentiated fields into a synthetic Prajñā-field whose stable cultivation generates a new causal capacity.
