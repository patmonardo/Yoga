# VAK 6.73 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

*kāmadhātau bhavāgre ca bodhimārgāṅgavarjitāḥ /*  
*trisatyadarśane śīladharmāvetyaprasādayoḥ // VAK 6.73 //*

## Bhāṣya Source

Pradhan 386|10–386|18.

## Literal Prose Translation

“In the desire domain and at the summit of existence they are without the factors of Bodhi and the factors of the Path.” In both there are twenty-two Bodhi-aligned factors, because there is no uncontaminated Path there.

For one who is proceeding among the Bodhi-aligned factors, at what stage is the acquisition of verified confidence (*avetyaprasāda*) to be understood?

“At the seeing of the three truths, [there is acquisition] of verified confidence in ethical discipline and Dharma.”

When directly realizing the truths of suffering, origin, and cessation, one acquires verified confidence in Dharma and the ethical disciplines dear to the noble ones.

When directly realizing the truth of the Path, one acquires verified confidence in the Buddha and in his community of disciples. For confidence in those two is confidence in the dharmas, beyond training, which constitute a Buddha, and in the dharmas, both in training and beyond training, which constitute the Saṃgha.

The word *api* indicates that at the realization of the Path one also acquires verified confidence in ethical discipline and Dharma.

## Course of Argument

Vasubandhu first completes the distribution problem opened in 6.71–6.72:

```text
kāmadhātu
    22 bodhipakṣya factors

bhavāgra
    22 bodhipakṣya factors
```

The reason is explicit:

```text
no anāsrava-mārga
    → no bodhyaṅgas
    → no mārgāṅgas
```

The verse then changes subject. Vasubandhu asks not what factors occur where, but when a new epistemic-practical determination is acquired:

```text
bodhipakṣeṣu vartamāna
    practitioner moving within the Bodhi-aligned field
        ↓
when is avetyaprasāda acquired?
```

The answer is distributed across realization of the Four Truths.

### Seeing the first three truths

Direct realization of:

```text
duḥkha
samudaya
nirodha
```

produces verified confidence in:

```text
Dharma
āryakānta-śīla
```

### Seeing the Path truth

Direct realization of *mārga-satya* produces verified confidence in:

```text
Buddha
Saṃgha
```

and, because of *api*, Dharma and ethical discipline are also acquired there.

Thus the four verified confidences are not arbitrary devotional additions. Their acquisition is genetically connected with direct realization.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu** — completion of the ground-distribution analysis and explanation of when the verified confidences are acquired.

No explicit school dispute is introduced in this unit.

## New Technical Terms

- **avetyaprasāda** — verified confidence arising from direct comprehension
  - **status:** rare in the Kārikās / newly thematic here
  - **function:** consequence of direct realization

- **āryakānta-śīla** — ethical disciplines dear to the noble ones
  - **status:** Bhāṣya elaboration of the Kārikā’s *śīla*
  - **function:** object of verified confidence

- **abhisamaya** — direct realization or penetration of a truth
  - **status:** previously encountered
  - **function:** epistemic event grounding acquisition

## Doctrinal and Logical Determination

The important relation is:

```text
direct realization
    ↓
verified determination of the object
    ↓
settled confidence
```

So *prasāda* here should not be reduced to belief in the ordinary sense. Its condition is *abhisamaya*.

The Bhāṣya also makes the Buddha and Saṃgha objects intelligible through dharmic constitution:

```text
Buddha
    constituted by aśaikṣa dharmas

Saṃgha
    constituted by śaikṣa + aśaikṣa dharmas
```

Confidence in Buddha and Saṃgha therefore remains internally connected to the Path-field rather than becoming an external cultic relation.

## Translation Consequences for the Kārikā

`avetyaprasāda` is best rendered **verified confidence** or **confidence grounded in direct comprehension**. “Faith” alone is too weak because it suppresses the causal relation to realization expressed by *avetya* and *abhisamaya*.

The phrase `trisatyadarśane` should be read literally as the seeing of the first three truths, with the acquisition connected to the fourth truth supplied by the opening of VAK 6.74.

## Light Organon Contact

This passage is useful for the emerging scientific structure of the Kośa because it distinguishes:

```text
statement about an object
    ≠
verified confidence in that object
```

Verified confidence is a result within a chain of dependence:

```text
truth
    → direct realization
        → determinate acquisition
            → stable practical orientation
```

The Path therefore generates not merely propositions but transformed epistemic capacities. That fits the developing conception of Practical Reason as an ordered science of dependencies rather than a collection of statements.

## Determinate Summary

VAK 6.73 closes the distribution of the Bodhi-aligned factors by assigning twenty-two to both the desire domain and bhavāgra, where the uncontaminated Bodhi and Path factors are absent. It then introduces *avetyaprasāda* as a determination acquired through direct realization: seeing suffering, origin, and cessation yields verified confidence in Dharma and noble ethical discipline, while realization of the Path yields verified confidence in Buddha and Saṃgha, together with the former two.
