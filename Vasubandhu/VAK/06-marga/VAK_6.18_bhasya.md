# VAK 6.18 Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

**ubhayākaraṇaṃ dharmeṇānyair api tu vardhanam |**  
**tebhyaḥ kṣāntir dvidhā tadvat kṣāntyā dharmeṇa vardhanam || 6.18 ||**

## Bhāṣya Source

> eṣāṃ punarubhayeṣamūṣmagatamūrdhnāṃ dharmasmṛtyupasthānenākaraṇam /
> kimidamākaraṇam /
> satyeṣvākārāṇāṃ prathamatovinyasanam /
> anyairapi tu vardhanam /
> caturbhirapi smṛtyupasthānaireṣāṃ vardhanam /
> vivardhayataḥ pūrvapratilabdhānāmaṃsaṃmukhībhāvo 'vahumānatvāt /
> mṛdumadhyādhimātrakramābhivṛddhebhyaḥ punarutpadyate tebhyaḥ kṣāntiḥ /
> adhimātrasya kṣamaṇādaparihāṇitaḥ /
> sā 'pi triprakārā mṛdvī madhyā 'dhimātrā ca /
> dvidhā tadvat /
> yathā mūrdhāna uktāḥ evaṃ mṛdumadhye kṣāntī tathaivākaraṇāt /
> vivardhane tvayaṃ viśeṣaḥ /
> sarvasyāḥ kṣāntyā dharmeṇa vardhanam /
> smṛtyupasthānenaiva nānyena /

## Literal Translation

The initial formation of both Heat and the Summits is through the Dharma-Foundation of Recollection. What is this formation? It is the first placement of the aspects upon the Truths. Their strengthening, however, may also occur through the other Foundations: they are strengthened through all four Foundations of Recollection. As they are strengthened, previously acquired determinations become increasingly present because they are no longer weak.

From these, as they increase successively through weak, medium, and strong grades, Acceptance arises. It is so called because the strongest stage is accepted without falling away. Acceptance itself is of three grades: weak, medium, and strong. The weak and medium forms are formed in the same way as the Summits. In their strengthening, however, there is this difference: all Acceptance is strengthened through the Dharma-Foundation alone and not through any other Foundation.

## Natural Argument

VAK 6.18 distinguishes two operations in the genesis of the preparatory Path:

```text
ākaraṇa
    initial formation

vardhana
    subsequent strengthening
```

Heat and the Summits are first **formed** through dharma-smṛtyupasthāna. Vasubandhu defines formation very precisely:

```text
satyeṣu ākārāṇāṃ prathamatovinyasanam
    the first placement of the aspects upon the Truths
```

The Path is therefore being constructed through the ordered application of the sixteen Truth-aspects to the Four Truths.

Once this structure has been formed, Heat and the Summits may be strengthened through all four smṛtyupasthānas. Their previously acquired determinations become increasingly present and stable.

From this graded strengthening arises **kṣānti** — Acceptance.

## Kṣānti

Here kṣānti is not ordinary patience. It is the increasingly stable acceptance or receptivity of the Truth-structure.

Vasubandhu distinguishes three grades:

```text
mṛdu
    weak

madhya
    medium

adhimātra
    strong
```

The weak and medium forms are initially formed in the same manner as the Summits. But in their development there is a decisive narrowing:

```text
all Acceptance
    is strengthened only through
    dharma-smṛtyupasthāna
```

The Path-field is becoming progressively concentrated upon the Truth-structured Dharma-field.

## Voice

**Voice:** Vasubandhu explaining the Vaibhāṣika path-structure.

No major objection is raised in this unit; the prose is chiefly analytic and genetic.

## New Technical Terms

- **ākaraṇa** — initial formation; here specifically the first placement of the sixteen aspects upon the Four Truths.
- **vinyasana** — placing, arranging, applying; the active organization of the Truth-aspects.
- **vardhana** — strengthening or development of an already acquired cognitive determination.
- **saṃmukhībhāva** — becoming present or manifest to cognition.
- **kṣānti** — Acceptance; stable receptivity to the Truth-structure, not ordinary patience.
- **mṛdu / madhya / adhimātra** — weak, medium, and strong grades.
- **aparihāṇi** — non-decline, non-falling-away.

## Doctrinal Determination

The sequence now becomes:

```text
dharma-smṛtyupasthāna
    ↓
first placement of sixteen aspects
    ↓
Heat
    ↓
strengthening
    ↓
Summits
    ↓
further strengthening
    ↓
Acceptance
```

The important transition is from **formation** to **stability**.

Heat and Summits still belong to the unstable wholesome roots. Acceptance marks the point at which the Truth-structure is becoming difficult to reverse.

## Relation to Path-Power

VAK 6.17 showed that the same object-field can become more powerful without acquiring new content. VAK 6.18 explains how:

```text
same Truth-field
    + repeated strengthening
    → increased presence
    → increased stability
    → Acceptance
```

So the emerging Path-capacity is not merely a larger body of propositions. It is a transformation in the **mode of possession of the Truths**.

The Truths become less like something merely represented and more like a stable cognitive determination that the practitioner can no longer easily lose.

## Light Organon Note

A useful structural distinction is:

```text
formation
    establishes the rule-set

strengthening
    increases effective control of the field

Acceptance
    marks stabilization of the rule-set as operative cognition
```

This remains a Kośa-internal interpretation of the practical logic of the passage.

## Determinate Summary

VAK 6.18 distinguishes the initial construction of the Truth-field from its subsequent strengthening. Heat and the Summits are first formed by placing the sixteen aspects upon the Four Truths through dharma-smṛtyupasthāna, then strengthened through the four Foundations. From their graded maturation arises kṣānti, Acceptance. Weak and medium Acceptance are formed like the Summits, but all Acceptance is strengthened only through the Dharma-Foundation.

The central result is:

> The Path advances not simply by acquiring new content, but by making the Truth-structure increasingly present, stable, and irreversible in cognition.
