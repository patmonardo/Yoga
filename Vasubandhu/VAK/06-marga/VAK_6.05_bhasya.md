# VAK 6.05 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

### Devanāgarī

वृत्तस्थः श्रुतचिन्तावान् भावनायां प्रयुज्यते ।
नामोभयार्थविषयाः श्रुतमय्यादिका धियः ॥ ६.५ ॥

### IAST

vṛttasthaḥ śrutacintāvān bhāvanāyāṃ prayujyate |
nāmobhayārthaviṣayāḥ śrutamayyādikā dhiyaḥ || 6.5 ||

## Bhāṣya Source

The Bhāṣya asks how the Four Truths are actually seen and begins from the practitioner's preparation:

> satyāni ha draṣṭukāma ādita eva śīlaṃ pālayati /
> tataḥ satyadarśanasyānulomaṃ śrutam udgṛhṇāti arthaṃ vā śṛṇoti /
> śrutvā cintayati aviparītaṃ cintayitvā bhāvanāyāṃ prayujyate /

One who wishes to see the Truths first guards ethical conduct. Then one receives learning conformable to the seeing of the Truths, or hears the Meaning. Having heard, one reflects; having reflected without inversion, one applies oneself to cultivation.

The Bhāṣya then gives the genetic sequence:

> samādhau tasya śrutamayīṃ prajñāṃ niśritya cintāmayī jāyate /
> cintāmayīṃ niśritya bhāvanāmayī jāyate /

Within samādhi, relying upon Prajñā formed from learning, Prajñā formed from reflection arises; relying upon Prajñā formed from reflection, Prajñā formed from cultivation arises.

## Voice

**Voice:** Vasubandhu — framing the path of preparation and reporting the doctrinal sequence.

**Voice:** Vaibhāṣika position — the name / name-and-Meaning / Meaning characterization and swimmer analogy.

**Voice:** Other teachers (`apare`) — objection that the Vaibhāṣika characterization cannot successfully establish a distinct reflective Prajñā.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu — offers the stronger causal characterization by authoritative testimony, reasoning, and samādhi.

## Initial Path Sequence

The opening prose gives a precise preparation for Seeing:

```text
śīla
    disciplined conduct
        ↓
śruta
    received teaching conformable to Seeing
        ↓
cintā
    non-inverted reflection
        ↓
bhāvanā
    cultivation
```

This is not an optional educational preface. Vasubandhu introduces it in answer to the question:

```text
How does seeing of the Truths occur?
```

The answer therefore begins before the Path of Seeing proper. The supramundane capacity of Seeing is genetically prepared through conduct, learning, reflection, and cultivation.

## The Three Prajñās

Vasubandhu explicitly names all three as forms of **Prajñā**:

```text
śrutamayī prajñā
    Prajñā formed from learning

cintāmayī prajñā
    Prajñā formed from reflection

bhāvanāmayī prajñā
    Prajñā formed from cultivation
```

The genetic dependence is explicit:

```text
śrutamayī prajñā
        ↓ niśritya
cintāmayī prajñā
        ↓ niśritya
bhāvanāmayī prajñā
```

`niśritya` is important: the later Prajñā arises **relying upon** the preceding one. The progression is therefore not merely a classification of three independent cognitive states.

## The Kārikā Definition

The Kārikā characterizes the three according to their object-supports:

```text
śrutamayī prajñā
    nāma-ālambanā
    object-support: name

cintāmayī prajñā
    nāma-artha-ālambanā
    object-support: name + Meaning

bhāvanāmayī prajñā
    artha-ālambanā
    object-support: Meaning alone
```

The Bhāṣya explains reflective Prajñā as moving in both directions:

```text
sometimes:
    expression → Meaning

sometimes:
    Meaning → expression
```

Cultivational Prajñā proceeds toward Meaning independently of verbal expression:

> sā hi vyañjananirapekṣā arthe pravartate

It operates toward Meaning without dependence upon verbal formulation.

## The Vaibhāṣika Swimmer Analogy

The Vaibhāṣikas illustrate the three stages with learning to swim.

```text
untrained swimmer
    cannot release support
    = śrutamayī

partly trained swimmer
    sometimes releases support,
    sometimes grasps it
    = cintāmayī

well-trained swimmer
    crosses independently of support
    = bhāvanāmayī
```

The analogy captures a genuine progression in independence from linguistic support.

## The Objection

Other teachers object:

> asyāṃ tu kalpanāyāṃ cintāmayī prajñā na siddhyati

On this construction, reflective Prajñā is not successfully established.

Why?

If a cognition takes **name** as its object, it falls under learning-born Prajñā. If it takes **Meaning** as its object, it falls under cultivation-born Prajñā. Merely saying that reflective Prajñā alternates between the two does not provide a sufficiently secure defining characteristic.

The objection is logically sharp:

```text
name-object
    → classified as śrutamayī

Meaning-object
    → classified as bhāvanāmayī

therefore:
    where is the intrinsic mark of cintāmayī?
```

The tripartition by object-support alone threatens to collapse the middle term.

## Vasubandhu's Stronger Definition

Vasubandhu responds that a completely faultless definition is difficult, and then gives a much stronger characterization by **genesis**:

> āptavacanaprāmāṇyajātaniścayaḥ śrutamayī /
> yuktinidhyānajaś cintāmayī /
> samādhijo bhāvanāmayīti /

Thus:

```text
śrutamayī prajñā
    certainty born from the authority of reliable teaching

cintāmayī prajñā
    born from examination / contemplation through reasoning

bhāvanāmayī prajñā
    born from samādhi
```

This is the decisive Bhāṣya development of the verse.

The suffix `-maya` is taken causally: that from which something is produced or constituted. Vasubandhu supports this by ordinary examples such as life being dependent upon food and cows being sustained by grass.

## Why the Causal Definition Is Stronger

The Kārikā's object-model gives:

```text
nāma
    ↓
nāma + artha
    ↓
artha
```

The Bhāṣya's causal model gives:

```text
authoritative teaching
    ↓
śrutamayī prajñā

reasoning / examination
    ↓
cintāmayī prajñā

samādhi
    ↓
bhāvanāmayī prajñā
```

These should not be treated as rival systems that simply cancel one another. The first tracks **what cognition depends upon as its presented support**; the second distinguishes the three more securely according to **how each cognition is generated**.

The second model is philosophically deeper because it preserves the middle term.

## Samādhi and Bhāvanā

The strongest new determination is:

```text
bhāvanāmayī prajñā
    = samādhija
    born from samādhi
```

Bhāvanā is therefore not merely repeated thought about a doctrine, nor merely cognition whose object is Meaning.

Its specific genesis is **samādhi**.

That gives the Path a much more exact architecture:

```text
received truth
    śruta
        ↓
reasoned truth
    cintā
        ↓
truth stabilized / generated in samādhi
    bhāvanā
        ↓
preparation for direct Seeing
```

The transition from reflection to cultivation is consequently a transformation in the **mode of generation of Prajñā**.

## Relation to VAK 6.01–6.03

VAK 6.01 introduced the extraordinary `śakti` of supramundane Seeing.

VAK 6.02 determined `abhisamaya` as right knowing according to reality and oriented toward nirvāṇa.

VAK 6.03 showed that adequate determination of the same experienced content can alter its practical consequence: bondage or liberation.

VAK 6.05 now supplies part of the genesis by which such adequate knowing becomes possible:

```text
conduct
    ↓
learning
    ↓
reflection
    ↓
samādhi-born cultivation
    ↓
Seeing of the Truths
```

The Path's effective capacity is therefore being prepared through a structured transformation of Prajñā rather than appearing inexplicably at the instant of Seeing.

## New Technical Terms

- **anuloma** — conformable, proceeding in the direction appropriate to the Seeing of the Truths.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-expansive term.
  - **function:** characterizes preparatory learning as oriented toward the coming realization.

- **niśritya** — relying upon, having as basis.
  - **status:** important Bhāṣya relational vocabulary.
  - **function:** expresses the genetic dependence of later Prajñā upon earlier Prajñā.

- **āptavacana-prāmāṇya** — authority / validity of reliable teaching.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only technical determination in this unit.
  - **function:** causal ground of learning-born Prajñā.

- **niścaya** — certainty, settled determination.
  - **status:** important epistemic vocabulary.
  - **function:** identifies the result generated through reliable teaching.

- **yukti-nidhyāna** — examination, sustained contemplation, or investigation through reasoning.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only technical determination here.
  - **function:** causal ground of reflective Prajñā.

- **samādhija** — born from samādhi.
  - **status:** crucial Bhāṣya determination.
  - **function:** defines cultivation-born Prajñā by its genesis.

- **vyañjana** — verbal expression / wording.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory vocabulary.
  - **function:** linguistic support from which cultivated Prajñā becomes independent.

## Logical Determination

There are two superposed structures:

```text
OBJECT STRUCTURE

name
    ↓
name + Meaning
    ↓
Meaning
```

and:

```text
GENETIC STRUCTURE

testimony
    ↓
reasoning
    ↓
samādhi
```

The Bhāṣya shows why the second is necessary: an object-based classification alone cannot securely discriminate the reflective middle.

Thus the deeper scientific principle is:

```text
A cognition is not completely determined
merely by what it is about.

It must also be determined
by the ground from which it arises.
```

## Determinate Summary

VAK 6.05 begins the actual genesis of the Path of Seeing.

```text
śīla
    establishes conduct

śruta
    receives the teaching

cintā
    establishes non-inverted reflective determination

bhāvanā
    produces Prajñā through samādhi
```

The Kārikā presents the elegant progression:

```text
name → name-and-Meaning → Meaning
```

but the Bhāṣya subjects that scheme to criticism and gives the stronger genetic determination:

```text
śrutamayī prajñā
    born from authoritative teaching

cintāmayī prajñā
    born from reasoning

bhāvanāmayī prajñā
    born from samādhi
```

The central result is therefore not simply that cultivation reaches Meaning without words. It is that **cultivational Prajñā is a distinct mode of knowing because its generating ground is samādhi**.
