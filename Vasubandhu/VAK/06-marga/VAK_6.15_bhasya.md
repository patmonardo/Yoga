# VAK 6.15 Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

**IAST**

prajñā śrutādimayī anye saṃsargālambanāḥ kramaḥ |
yathotpatti catuṣkaṃ tu viparyāsavipakṣataḥ || 6.15 ||

**Working translation**

The intrinsic Foundation of Recollection is Prajñā produced through hearing and the succeeding modes; the others consist of its associated factors and its object-support. Their sequence follows the order of arising, while the fourfold division exists because it opposes the inversions.

See: [VAK_6.15.md](./VAK_6.15.md)

## Bhāṣya Source and Natural Segmentation

Vasubandhu begins by identifying the intrinsic nature of the smṛtyupasthāna:

> prajñā

He then asks what kind of Prajñā this is and answers:

> śrutādimayī

that is, śrutamayī, cintāmayī, and bhāvanāmayī Prajñā.

He next distinguishes three senses of smṛtyupasthāna:

1. **svabhāva-smṛtyupasthāna** — the intrinsic Foundation, namely Prajñā;
2. **saṃsarga-smṛtyupasthāna** — the associated dharmas arising together with that Prajñā;
3. **ālambana-smṛtyupasthāna** — the object-support upon which that Prajñā operates.

Vasubandhu then asks why Prajñā is called a smṛtyupasthāna at all. The Vaibhāṣika answer is that smṛti predominates. Vasubandhu offers a more exact explanation: Prajñā is called smṛtyupasthāna because smṛti is established through it, especially by verbally retaining and articulating what has been seen.

The four foundations are then ordered according to their arising. One explanation is that the coarser object is seen first. The Vaibhāṣika gives a causal sequence: body is the basis of sensual desire; feeling is desired because of that body; cognition becomes undisciplined through that desire; and affliction persists because it is not abandoned.

Finally, the fourfold structure is justified as a fourfold counterforce to the four inversions:

- impure taken as pure;
- suffering taken as pleasure;
- impermanent taken as permanent;
- not-self taken as self.

The first three foundations have unmixed object-supports; the dharma-foundation may operate either on dharmas alone or on a mixed field including body, feeling, cognition, and dharmas together.

## Literal Prose Translation

The Foundation of Recollection is Prajñā. What kind of Prajñā? Prajñā produced through hearing, reflection, and cultivation. Thus the Foundations of Recollection are threefold according to whether they are produced through hearing, reflection, or cultivation.

The other dharmas that arise together with this Prajñā constitute the associated Foundation of Recollection; that which serves as its object-support constitutes the object Foundation of Recollection. The intrinsic Foundation of Recollection is Prajñā.

Why then did the Blessed One call Prajñā a Foundation of Recollection? The Vaibhāṣikas say: because recollection predominates. That is, because the operation depends upon the strength of recollection, like a wedge holding together pieces of wood.

A more appropriate explanation is this: it is called a Foundation of Recollection because recollection becomes established through this Prajñā, through the verbal articulation of what has been seen.

Their sequence is according to their arising. Why do they arise in this order? Because the coarser is seen first. Or, according to the Vaibhāṣikas: body is the basis of sensual desire; feeling is desired because of it; cognition becomes undisciplined because of that; and this occurs because the afflictions have not been abandoned.

There are four Foundations because they are the counterforces to the four inversions of purity, pleasure, permanence, and self. Thus neither more nor fewer than four are taught.

## Argument Reconstruction

The passage makes four distinct determinations.

### 1. Intrinsic nature

```text
smṛtyupasthāna
    intrinsically
        = prajñā
```

The practice is not intrinsically smṛti. Smṛti is indispensable, but Prajñā is the discriminative factor that actually performs the seeing.

### 2. Genetic modes of Prajñā

```text
śrutamayī prajñā
    ↓
cintāmayī prajñā
    ↓
bhāvanāmayī prajñā
```

The same threefold genesis established in VAK 6.05 now reappears inside the smṛtyupasthāna system.

### 3. Three senses of “Foundation of Recollection”

```text
svabhāva
    Prajñā itself

saṃsarga
    associated co-arising dharmas

ālambana
    the object-support
```

This prevents the category from being flattened into either a mental factor or an object alone.

### 4. Fourfold counterforce

```text
kāya-smṛtyupasthāna
    ↔ śuci-viparyāsa

vedanā-smṛtyupasthāna
    ↔ sukha-viparyāsa

citta-smṛtyupasthāna
    ↔ nitya-viparyāsa

dharma-smṛtyupasthāna
    ↔ ātma-viparyāsa
```

The fourfold schema is therefore determined by the afflictive errors it must oppose.

## Voice Analysis

**Voice: Vasubandhu** — identifies the intrinsic Foundation as Prajñā; distinguishes intrinsic, associated, and object-support senses; offers his preferred explanation of why Prajñā is nevertheless called smṛtyupasthāna.

**Voice: Vaibhāṣika position** — explains the name through predominance of smṛti and gives one account of the order body → feeling → cognition → dharmas.

**Voice: Vasubandhu** — accepts the fourfold arrangement as the counterforce to the four inversions and clarifies the mixed/unmixed object-ranges.

## New Technical Terms

- **svabhāva-smṛtyupasthāna** — intrinsic Foundation of Recollection; here identified with Prajñā.
  - **status:** explicit Bhāṣya technical distinction.
  - **function:** separates intrinsic nature from associates and object.

- **saṃsarga-smṛtyupasthāna** — associated Foundation of Recollection.
  - **status:** explicit Bhāṣya technical distinction.
  - **function:** names the co-arising dharmas that accompany Prajñā.

- **ālambana-smṛtyupasthāna** — object Foundation of Recollection.
  - **status:** explicit Bhāṣya technical distinction.
  - **function:** names the object-support taken by the practice.

- **anupaśyanā** — seeing/examining in accordance with the object.
  - **status:** central to the smṛtyupasthāna formula.
  - **function:** identified here with Prajñā.

- **viparyāsa-vipakṣa** — counterforce to inversion.
  - **status:** explicit doctrinal function of the four foundations.
  - **function:** explains why there are exactly four.

## Doctrinal Determination

VAK 6.15 is not merely defining four contemplations. It gives the internal architecture of the practice:

```text
intrinsic discriminative factor
    Prajñā

operating associates
    co-arising dharmas

object-field
    ālambana

functional role
    counterforce to inversion
```

The practice is therefore an integrated cognitive event rather than a bare object-attention exercise.

The important Bhāṣya correction is that the word **smṛti** in the compound does not determine the intrinsic nature of the practice. Smṛti supports and stabilizes the operation, but Prajñā performs the discriminative seeing.

## Logical Determination

The structure can be represented as a four-field investigative system governed by one intrinsic cognitive power:

```text
Prajñā
    operates with associated factors
    upon a determinate object-support
    to oppose a specific inversion
```

The sequence body → feeling → cognition → dharmas is not arbitrary. It is given either by increasing subtlety or by a causal-psychological progression within sensual attachment.

The first three have non-mixed object supports; the fourth can gather the entire field together. This makes dharma-smṛtyupasthāna the point at which the previous determinations become available for synthetic examination.

## Translation Consequences

1. `smṛtyupasthāna` should not be translated in a way that suggests smṛti alone is its intrinsic nature.
2. `prajñā` must remain visible in translation because Vasubandhu explicitly identifies it as the svabhāva.
3. `saṃsarga` here is not a loose “context” but the associated co-arising dharmas.
4. `ālambana` should remain object-support rather than being weakened to “topic.”
5. The fourfold structure should be read functionally as a fourfold pratipakṣa to four viparyāsas.

## Relation to VAK 6.05 and 6.14

VAK 6.05 gave the genetic sequence:

```text
śruta
    ↓
cintā
    ↓
bhāvanā
```

VAK 6.14 established that accomplished śamatha becomes the basis for smṛtyupasthāna cultivation and the completion of vipaśyanā.

VAK 6.15 now identifies the discriminative core of that cultivation:

```text
vipaśyanā-process
    is carried by
Prajñā
    generated as
śruta → cintā → bhāvanā
```

Thus the chapter continues to build Path-capacity genetically rather than treating liberating seeing as an inexplicable event.

## Light Organon Contact Point

The most useful structural point is modest but strong:

```text
cognitive power
    +
associated conditions
    +
object-support
    +
error to be countered
        =
scientifically specified practice
```

Vasubandhu is classifying not merely contents of cognition but the complete functional relation by which an error is opposed.

## Determinate Summary

VAK 6.15 establishes that the intrinsic nature of the Foundations of Recollection is Prajñā. That Prajñā may be produced through hearing, reflection, or cultivation. The wider practice also includes its associated dharmas and its object-supports. The four foundations arise in an ordered sequence and are exactly four because they counter the four inversions of purity, pleasure, permanence, and self.

The central result is:

```text
smṛtyupasthāna
    is named through smṛti
    but intrinsically is Prajñā

and Prajñā becomes Path
by operating as a determinate counterforce
against specific inversion.
```
