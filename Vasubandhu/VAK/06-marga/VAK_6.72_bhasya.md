# VAK 6.72 Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

द्वितीयेऽन्यत्र संकल्पाद् द्वयोस्तद्द्वयवर्जिताः ।
ध्यानान्तरे च शीलाङ्गैस्ताभ्यां च त्रिष्वरूपिषु ॥

*dvitīye ’nyatra saṃkalpāt dvayos tad-dvaya-varjitāḥ /*
*dhyānāntare ca śīlāṅgais tābhyāṃ ca triṣv arūpiṣu //*

## Bhāṣya Source

Pradhan 386.01–09.

## Natural Argument

Vasubandhu continues the question raised in 6.71: in which meditative grounds are how many of the bodhipakṣya factors available?

The second dhyāna contains thirty-six, lacking only right resolve (*samyaksaṃkalpa*), because *vitarka* is absent there.

The third and fourth dhyānas contain thirty-five, lacking both joy (*prīti*) and right resolve (*saṃkalpa*).

The intermediate dhyāna likewise contains thirty-five, lacking the same two.

In the first three formless attainments the system is further reduced: right speech, right action, and right livelihood are unavailable, together with joy and right resolve. Thus thirty-two factors remain.

## Literal Prose Translation

“In the second dhyāna, except for resolve”: in the second dhyāna there are only thirty-six, with right resolve excluded, because *vitarka* is absent there.

“In the two, deprived of that pair”: in the third and fourth dhyānas there are thirty-five, deprived of joy and resolve.

“And in the intermediate dhyāna”: there too there are thirty-five, deprived of those same two.

“With the ethical limbs, and with those two, in the three formless [attainments]”: ‘deprived’ continues to govern the construction. In the formless attainments there are thirty-two, deprived of right speech, right action, and right livelihood, and also of joy and resolve.

## Voice

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The passage is direct expository classification without an objection-response exchange.

## New Technical Terms

- **tad-dvaya** — “that pair,” here *prīti* and *saṃkalpa*.
  - **status:** passage-specific shorthand.
  - **function:** logical compression of the ground-distribution rule.

- **śīlāṅga** — the ethical limbs of the Noble Path: right speech, right action, and right livelihood.
  - **status:** previously encountered doctrinal material, newly active in this distribution problem.
  - **function:** identifies the form-dependent members excluded in formless attainment.

## Doctrinal Determination

The distribution is:

```text
first dhyāna
    37

anāgamya
    36
    - prīti

second dhyāna
    36
    - saṃkalpa

third dhyāna
    35
    - prīti
    - saṃkalpa

fourth dhyāna
    35
    - prīti
    - saṃkalpa

intermediate dhyāna
    35
    - prīti
    - saṃkalpa

first three ārūpya attainments
    32
    - prīti
    - saṃkalpa
    - samyagvāc
    - samyakkarmānta
    - samyagājīva
```

The exclusions are not arbitrary omissions from a fixed checklist. They follow from the structural capacities of the ground.

Most explicitly, the Bhāṣya says that *saṃkalpa* is absent in the second dhyāna because *vitarka* is absent there. The ethical Path-limbs are excluded from the formless attainments because those attainments do not support the bodily and verbal form through which those limbs operate.

## Logical Determination

VAK 6.72 completes a strong rule already implicit in 6.71:

```text
Ground structure
    determines available operations

available operations
    determine possible Path-composition
```

The same bodhipakṣya system therefore does not instantiate itself identically in every contemplative domain. Its concrete form is conditioned by what that ground can sustain.

This is another reason not to read the thirty-seven factors as a flat inventory. Their actuality is relational and conditional.

## Translation Consequences for the Kārikā

The terse *tad-dvaya* must be resolved as **prīti + saṃkalpa**, supplied from the previous and current clauses.

The phrase *triṣv arūpiṣu* is best read as the first three formless attainments, since the summit of existence (*bhavāgra*) is treated separately in the following verse.

## Light Organon Contact

The conceptual importance is not that higher meditative grounds simply possess “less.” Rather, each ground permits a different concrete articulation of the same Path-system.

```text
same universal Path-field
    ↓
change of ground
    ↓
change of admissible determinations
    ↓
new concrete configuration
```

Thus a determination is not fully characterized by its abstract identity alone; one must also know the field in which it can actually function.

This extends the result of 6.69–6.71: the Kośa’s Practical Concept is an organized whole whose members are not merely internally related but **conditioned by the concrete medium of their realization**.

## Determinate Summary

VAK 6.72 completes the meditative-ground distribution of the bodhipakṣya factors. The second dhyāna excludes right resolve; the third, fourth, and intermediate dhyānas exclude both joy and resolve; and the first three formless attainments exclude those two together with right speech, right action, and right livelihood. The Bhāṣya thereby makes contemplative ground a constitutive condition of possible Path-configuration.
