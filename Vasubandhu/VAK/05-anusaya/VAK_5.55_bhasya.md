# VAK 5.55 Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

**VAK 5.55**

```text
sukhābhyāṃ saṃprayukto hi rāgaḥ dveṣo viparyayāt /
mohaḥ sarvair asaddṛṣṭir mano-duḥkha-sukhena tu // 5.55 //
```

**Translation:** Attachment is associated with the two pleasant feeling-faculties; hostility, conversely, with the two painful ones. Delusion is associated with all five; false view, however, with mental pain and mental pleasure.

See: [VAK_5.55.md](./VAK_5.55.md)

## Translation

Among the five feeling-faculties beginning with pleasure, with which faculty is each kleśa or upakleśa associated?

Attachment is associated with the two pleasant faculties, namely bodily pleasure (*sukha*) and mental gladness (*saumanasya*).

Hostility is associated with the reverse pair: bodily pain (*duḥkha*) and mental distress (*daurmanasya*). Rāga and dveṣa thus operate respectively in modes of gladness and dejection, and both belong to the six-consciousness field.

Delusion is associated with all five feeling-faculties because ignorance is capable of association with every kleśa.

The "false view" of the verse is specifically *mithyādṛṣṭi*. It is associated with mental pain and mental pleasure, that is, with *daurmanasya* and *saumanasya*. The Bhāṣya relates these respectively to meritorious and evil actions.

## Course of Argument

**Voice: Vasubandhu / Abhidharma exposition**

VAK 5.55 changes the classificatory axis once again. The question is now not:

```text
What is the root?
Where does the affliction occur?
By what is it abandoned?
```

but:

```text
With which vedanā-indriya
can this kleśa be conjoined?
```

The five feeling-faculties are:

```text
sukha         bodily pleasure
saumanasya    mental gladness
duḥkha        bodily pain
daurmanasya   mental distress
upekṣā        equanimous / neutral feeling
```

### 1. Rāga and the two pleasant faculties

The Bhāṣya states directly:

```text
rāga
    ↔ sukha + saumanasya
```

The relation is *saṃprayoga*: conjunction in the same mental event, not a claim that pleasure temporally produces attachment.

### 2. Dveṣa and the reverse pair

`viparyayāt` is immediately glossed as the two painful faculties:

```text
dveṣa
    ↔ duḥkha + daurmanasya
```

The Bhāṣya explains the opposition through `harṣa` and `dainya`, gladness and dejection, together with the fact that both rāga and dveṣa can belong to the six-consciousness field.

### 3. Moha with all five

The reason given is exact:

```text
avidyā
    sarva-kleśa-saṃprayogitva
    capacity for association with every kleśa
        ↓
association with all five feeling-faculties
```

Thus moha's universal feeling-range is not merely inferred from a general phenomenology of confusion. It follows from the doctrinal association-range of avidyā.

### 4. `Asaddṛṣṭi` means mithyādṛṣṭi

The Bhāṣya removes any ambiguity in the Kārikā expression:

```text
asaddṛṣṭi
    = mithyādṛṣṭi
```

This false view is associated only with mental feeling:

```text
mithyādṛṣṭi
    ↔ daurmanasya + saumanasya
```

The prose adds that the two feelings correspond respectively to its relation to meritorious and evil karma. The immediate point is that the same false cognition can be affectively distressed or pleased depending on the content toward which it is directed.

## New Technical Terms

- **pañcendriya (vedanā-context)** — the five feeling-faculties: sukha, duḥkha, saumanasya, daurmanasya, upekṣā.
  - **status:** components previously encountered; explicit classificatory use here.
  - **function:** affective-association matrix.

- **harṣa** — gladness, elated or pleased mode.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory vocabulary.
  - **function:** explains rāga's compatibility with pleasant feeling.

- **dainya** — dejection, miserable or downcast mode.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory vocabulary.
  - **function:** explains dveṣa's compatibility with painful feeling.

- **sarvakleśasaṃprayogitva** — capacity for association with every kleśa.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory formulation.
  - **function:** ground for avidyā/moha's compatibility with all five feelings.

- **asaddṛṣṭi = mithyādṛṣṭi** — the Bhāṣya explicitly identifies the Kārikā's defective view as false view.
  - **status:** clarification of Kārikā vocabulary.
  - **function:** fixes the extension of the verse's `asaddṛṣṭi`.

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse establishes a compatibility matrix rather than an identity relation.

```text
rāga         {sukha, saumanasya}
dveṣa        {duḥkha, daurmanasya}
moha         {all five}
mithyādṛṣṭi  {daurmanasya, saumanasya}
```

Feeling and affliction therefore remain distinct determinations.

```text
vedanā
    affective tone

kleśa
    afflicted orientation
```

A pleasant feeling is not identical with rāga; it is a feeling with which rāga may be conjoined. Likewise pain is not dveṣa.

This distinction is doctrinally necessary because VAK 5.56 will add a universal relation with equanimity and further delimit these associations by domain.

## Effect on Kārikā Translation

**Clarification required.**

The first-pass translation is substantially correct, but two points should now be fixed:

1. `asaddṛṣṭi` is specifically **mithyādṛṣṭi**, not defective view in general.
2. `sukhābhyām` and the reverse painful pair are explicitly the two pleasant and two painful **feeling-faculties**.

Preferred translation:

> Attachment is associated with the two pleasant feeling-faculties; hostility, conversely, with the two painful ones. Delusion is associated with all five; false view (*mithyādṛṣṭi*), however, with mental distress and mental gladness.

## Logical Determination

The operative form is **conjunctive compatibility**:

```text
Affliction × Feeling
    → permitted saṃprayoga
```

This must be distinguished from genesis:

```text
co-occurs-with
    ≠ arises-from
```

and from ethical status:

```text
co-occurs-with pleasant feeling
    ≠ wholesome
```

The verse therefore adds another genuinely independent coordinate to the Chapter 5 system.

The especially instructive case is moha:

```text
one affliction
    compatible with
all affective polarities
```

Hence affective polarity cannot by itself diagnose cognitive clarity.

## Minimal Organon Contact Point

For the later clarifying pass, preserve only the formal distinction:

```text
Feeling-state
    ≠
Afflictive orientation
```

and:

```text
saṃprayoga
    is a compatibility/conjunction relation,
not a genetic relation.
```

The larger Organon synthesis should wait until the complete feeling matrix of VAK 5.55-5.58 has been reconstructed.
