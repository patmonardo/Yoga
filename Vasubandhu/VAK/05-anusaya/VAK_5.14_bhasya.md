## Literal Prose Translation

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

Of these ninety-eight anuśayas, how many have contaminated cognitive supports and how many have uncontaminated cognitive supports?

> False view, doubt, ignorance associated with those two, and independent ignorance—the six abandoned through seeing cessation and the path have uncontaminated cognitive supports.

There are three anuśayas to be abandoned through seeing cessation: false view, doubt, and ignorance—both the ignorance associated with those two and the independent form. The same three are also to be abandoned through seeing the path.

Thus these six have uncontaminated cognitive supports.

It is thereby established that the remainder have contaminated cognitive supports.

---

## Course of the Argument

VAK 5.14 introduces a new classificatory axis for the ninety-eight anuśayas:

```text
What kind of ālambana do they take?

sāsrava
    contaminated

anāsrava
    uncontaminated
```

The answer is extremely restricted.

For `nirodha`:

```text
mithyādṛṣṭi
vicikitsā
avidyā
```

For `mārga`:

```text
mithyādṛṣṭi
vicikitsā
avidyā
```

Hence:

```text
3 + 3 = 6 anāsrava-gocara anuśayas
```

Everything else among the ninety-eight has a contaminated cognitive support.

The crucial logical distinction is therefore:

```text
status of cognition
    ≠
status of cognitive support
```

The six are themselves afflicted anuśayas, yet their objects—cessation and path—are uncontaminated.

---

## Associated and Independent Avidyā

The Kārikā compresses two modes of ignorance:

```text
tābhyāṃ yuktā avidyā
    ignorance associated with false view or doubt

kevalā avidyā
    independent ignorance
```

The Bhāṣya confirms that both belong to the local `avidyā` category in relation to cessation and path.

This matters because ignorance is not reducible to an accompaniment of another false cognition.

```text
associated avidyā
    functions with a determinate afflicted cognition

independent avidyā
    can occur without that particular conjunction
```

But for the sixfold count the operative species remain:

```text
mithyādṛṣṭi
vicikitsā
avidyā
```

across two truth-fields.

---

## Why This Is Not a Contradiction

At first sight there is a tension:

```text
anuśaya
    is sāsrava

but

its ālambana
    can be anāsrava
```

The Bhāṣya accepts this distinction directly.

The affliction belongs to the mode of cognitive relation, not to the ontological status of cessation or the path.

Thus one can:

```text
deny cessation
    → mithyādṛṣṭi

doubt cessation
    → vicikitsā

fail to know cessation
    → avidyā
```

and likewise with the path.

The uncontaminated object is not converted into something contaminated by being falsely apprehended.

## New Technical Terms

- **sāsravālambana** — having a contaminated cognitive support
  - **status:** technically explicit in the Bhāṣya question
  - **function:** object-domain classification of anuśayas

- **anāsravālambana / anāsrava-gocara** — having an uncontaminated cognitive support / ranging over an uncontaminated field
  - **status:** Kārikā term clarified by the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** identifies the exceptional six anuśayas

- **saṃprayukta avidyā** — associated ignorance
  - **status:** Bhāṣya clarification of `tābhyāṃ yuktā avidyā`
  - **function:** distinguishes ignorance occurring in conjunction with false view or doubt

- **āveṇikī / kevalā avidyā** — independent ignorance
  - **status:** previously encountered at VAK 5.12; reconfirmed here
  - **function:** distinguishes ignorance not reducible to association with the other two

- **nirodhadarśanaprahātavya** — to be abandoned through seeing cessation
  - **status:** previously established Path classification
  - **function:** one of the two truth-fields generating the six

- **mārgadarśanaprahātavya** — to be abandoned through seeing the path
  - **status:** previously established
  - **function:** second truth-field generating the six

## Doctrinal Determination

The ninety-eight anuśayas can now be partitioned by object-status:

```text
98 anuśayas
    ├── 6 anāsrava-gocara
    │      nirodha: 3
    │      mārga:   3
    │
    └── 92 sāsrava-ālambana
```

The six are generated through the relation:

```text
3 afflicted modes
    ×
2 uncontaminated truth-fields
    =
6
```

This is another instance of the Kośa producing a larger taxonomy from a small rule-set rather than merely enumerating unrelated entities.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The first-pass translation correctly identified the six as anuśayas with uncontaminated objects. The Bhāṣya now fixes the count and the complement unambiguously.

A tightened canonical translation is:

> False view, doubt, and ignorance—whether associated with those or independent—when directed toward cessation and the path form six anuśayas with uncontaminated cognitive supports; all remaining anuśayas have contaminated supports.

The phrase `anāsrava-gocara` should therefore be read relationally: it characterizes the field of cognition, not the purity of the anuśaya itself.

---

## Logical Determination

VAK 5.14 separates two predicates that might otherwise be conflated:

```text
quality of cognition
quality of object
```

An afflicted cognition can be related to an uncontaminated object.

Therefore:

```text
Afflicted(Cognition)
    does not entail
Contaminated(Object)
```

and conversely:

```text
Uncontaminated(Object)
    does not entail
Uncontaminated(Cognition)
```

This distinction is structurally important because the Path requires not merely the presence of a liberative object, but the adequate cognition of it.

---

## Light Organon Contact Point

The cleanest later Organon relation is a typed separation among:

```text
Object status
Relation status
Cognitive status
```

A valid or uncontaminated target does not guarantee a valid determination of that target.

The Kośa therefore already requires a relational semantics:

```text
cognition
    hasĀlambana
        object

with predicates belonging separately
    to cognition
    and to object
```

This is text-generated and should remain subordinate to the Abhidharma distinction itself.

---

## Bhāṣya Pass Result

VAK 5.14 establishes a sharply bounded class:

> **Only six of the ninety-eight anuśayas take uncontaminated cognitive supports: false view, doubt, and ignorance directed toward cessation, and the same three directed toward the path. All remaining anuśayas have contaminated supports.**

The verse therefore separates the status of the afflictive cognition from the status of what it apprehends and prepares VAK 5.15, which asks exactly how cessation and the path function as cognitive supports across the different levels.
