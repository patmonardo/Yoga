# VAK 5.51 Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

**VAK 5.51**

```text
dṛṣṭyāmarśāt pradāśas tu śāṭhyaṃ dṛṣṭi-samutthitam /
tatrāhrīkyānapatrāpyastyānamiddhoddhavā dvidhā // 5.51 //
```

**Translation:** Pradāśa arises from grasping at view, while śāṭhya arises from view. Among these, shamelessness, lack of moral concern, torpor, sleepiness, and restlessness are twofold.

See: [VAK_5.51.md](./VAK_5.51.md)

## Translation

Pradāśa arises from grasping at view, while śāṭhya arises from view.

The latter is fitting in light of the verse saying, in effect, “What is crooked? An evil view.” Thus śāṭhya is a **niḥṣyanda of view**.

Now, which of these factors are to be abandoned in which way? First, with respect to the ten paryavasthānas already taught: shamelessness, lack of moral concern, torpor, sleepiness, and restlessness are twofold.

These five dharmas are of two kinds with respect to abandonment: they are abandoned through seeing and through cultivation, because they are associated with kleśas of both types.

Whatever one of these factors is associated with a kleśa abandoned by seeing is itself abandoned by that corresponding seeing.

## Course of Argument

**Voice: Vasubandhu / reported Abhidharma analysis**

VAK 5.51 performs two distinct tasks.

### 1. It completes the genealogy of the six kleśa-malas

The two unresolved stains from VAK 5.50 are now assigned:

```text
dṛṣṭyāmarśa
    ↓
pradāśa

dṛṣṭi
    ↓ niḥṣyanda
śāṭhya
```

The Bhāṣya is especially explicit about **śāṭhya**:

```text
śāṭhyaṃ dṛṣṭiniḥṣyandaḥ
    crookedness is a niḥṣyanda of view
```

So the relation is genetic, not merely associative.

The source supports the Kārikā's `dṛṣṭi-samutthitam` by citing a verse that identifies crookedness with evil view.

### 2. It opens a new classificatory axis: mode of abandonment

The Bhāṣya then asks:

```text
ka eṣāṃ kiṃprahātavyaḥ
    which of these is to be abandoned in which way?
```

This is a major transition. The analysis is no longer only:

```text
what class does this factor belong to?
what root does it arise from?
```

It now asks:

```text
by which path-operation is it abandoned?
```

Among the ten paryavasthānas, five are **dvidhā**:

```text
āhrīkya
anapatrāpya
styāna
middha
auddhatya
```

The Bhāṣya explains `dvidhā` unambiguously:

```text
darśana-prahātavya
    abandoned through seeing

bhāvanā-prahātavya
    abandoned through cultivation
```

The reason is equally explicit:

```text
ubhayaprakāra-kleśa-saṃprayoga
    association with kleśas of both types
```

Thus the five are not intrinsically split into two substances or two permanent subclasses. Their mode of abandonment follows the kind of kleśa with which they are associated.

```text
associated with a seeing-abandoned kleśa
    → abandoned through that seeing

associated with a cultivation-abandoned kleśa
    → abandoned through cultivation
```

This is a relational determination of path-status.

## New Technical Terms

- **dṛṣṭyāmarśa** — grasping at or insistence upon a view.
  - **status:** the components are familiar; this compound is locally decisive.
  - **function:** genetic ground assigned to `pradāśa`.

- **dṛṣṭi-niḥṣyanda** — derivative/outflow of view.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya formulation clarifying the Kārikā's `dṛṣṭi-samutthitam`.
  - **function:** explicit genetic relation defining `śāṭhya`.

- **darśana-prahātavya** — to be abandoned through seeing.
  - **status:** established path vocabulary, newly made the classificatory axis of this section.
  - **function:** mode of abandonment.

- **bhāvanā-prahātavya** — to be abandoned through cultivation.
  - **status:** established path vocabulary, newly made the classificatory axis of this section.
  - **function:** mode of abandonment.

- **ubhayaprakāra-kleśa-saṃprayoga** — association with kleśas of both types.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only explanatory formulation here.
  - **function:** explains why the five factors are `dvidhā`.

## Doctrinal Determination

VAK 5.51 completes one taxonomy and immediately overlays another.

The genealogy of the six stains is now complete:

```text
rāga
    → māyā
    → mada

pratigha
    → upanāha
    → vihiṃsā

dṛṣṭyāmarśa
    → pradāśa

dṛṣṭi
    → śāṭhya
```

But the second half of the verse shows that genetic origin does not by itself determine path-status.

A paryavasthāna may receive its mode of abandonment from the kleśa with which it is associated:

```text
same named factor
    + different kleśa-association
    → different abandonment classification
```

Thus:

```text
genesis
    ≠
mode of abandonment
```

and:

```text
class-membership
    ≠
path-location
```

The Kośa is now clearly working with several intersecting axes of determination.

## Effect on Kārikā Translation

**Clarification required.**

The first half is essentially correct, but `śāṭhyaṃ dṛṣṭi-samutthitam` is sharpened by the Bhāṣya into:

```text
śāṭhyaṃ dṛṣṭiniḥṣyandaḥ
```

The second half must be read specifically in terms of **two modes of abandonment**, not merely two forms of occurrence.

Preferred translation:

> Pradāśa arises from grasping at view, while crookedness arises from view. Among the ten envelopments, shamelessness, lack of moral concern, torpor, sleepiness, and restlessness are twofold with respect to abandonment.

## Logical Determination

VAK 5.51 gives us a powerful distinction among relations.

A determination can be specified by at least four independent questions:

```text
1. What is it?
   class

2. Whence does it arise?
   genetic ground

3. With what is it associated?
   saṃprayoga relation

4. How is it abandoned?
   path-operation
```

The last relation is not simply inferred from the first two.

For the five `dvidhā` factors:

```text
removalMode(x)
    is mediated by
association(x, kleśa)
```

Formally:

```text
if
    saṃprayukta(x, kleśa_y)
and
    darśana-heya(kleśa_y)
then
    darśana-heya(x)
```

This is exactly the kind of determination that a flat taxonomy cannot represent.

## Organon Contact Point

This verse strengthens the case for postponing a full Organon commentary until after a clarifying pass.

The Kośa is generating a multi-relational system in which the same dharma can have distinct determinations according to:

```text
source
association
function
mode of abandonment
```

A premature synthesis would risk treating one of these axes as the essence of the whole system.

The disciplined contact point for now is simply:

```text
Determination is relation-indexed.
```

More specifically:

```text
path-status is generated through association,
not merely read off from the dharma's name.
```

That is a genuine internal mediation and can later support Organon reconstruction without forcing the text.
