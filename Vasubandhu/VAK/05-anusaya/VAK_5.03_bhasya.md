# VAK_5.03 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

> dṛṣṭayaḥ pañca satkāyamithyāntagrāhadṛṣṭayaḥ |
> dṛṣṭiśīlavṛtaparāmarśāv iti punar daśa || 5.3 ||

**Best current translation:**  
The views are five: satkāya-view, false view, extreme-grasping view, adherence to views, and adherence to discipline and observance. Thus, again, the anuśayas are ten.

The natural argument does not end with the fivefold division of `dṛṣṭi`; it uses that division to establish the tenfold base from which the famous ninety-eight anuśayas are generated. VAK 5.04 begins the detailed derivation.

---

## Literal Prose Translation

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

By making the `dṛṣṭi` among the six anuśayas fivefold, they become ten.

Five are of the nature of view: satkāya-view, extreme-grasping view, false view, adherence to views, and adherence to discipline and observance. Attachment, resistance, conceit, non-knowing, and doubt are not of the nature of view.

These ten anuśayas, again, are made into ninety-eight in the Abhidharma: thirty-six belonging to the desire-domain, thirty-one belonging to the form-domain, and thirty-one belonging to the formless-domain.

How is this done?

In summary, these anuśayas throughout the three domains are either to be abandoned through seeing or to be abandoned through cultivation. Of those belonging to the desire-domain, thirty-two are first to be abandoned through seeing. Which are they? [VAK 5.04 answers.]

## Argument Reconstruction

VAK 5.03 performs two distinct operations.

First, it differentiates the single genus `dṛṣṭi`:

```text
dṛṣṭi
    ↓ fivefold determination
satkāyadṛṣṭi
antagrāhadṛṣṭi
mithyādṛṣṭi
dṛṣṭiparāmarśa
śīlavṛtaparāmarśa
```

The original sixfold anuśaya system therefore becomes ten:

```text
5 non-view anuśayas
    rāga
    pratigha
    māna
    avidyā
    vicikitsā

+

5 view anuśayas

=

10
```

Second, Vasubandhu immediately turns this tenfold classification into a generative matrix:

```text
10 anuśayas
    ↓ distributed by domain
    ↓ distributed by mode of abandonment
98 anuśayas
```

The numerical expansion is therefore not an arbitrary scholastic multiplication. It is the result of applying two further discriminators to the ten basic forms:

```text
1. Where does the anuśaya operate?
    kāma / rūpa / ārūpya

2. How is it abandoned?
    seeing / cultivation
```

VAK 5.04 and following will perform the derivation in detail.

## Voice Identification

The local unit is straightforward:

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

No opponent is introduced. The expression `abhidharme 'ṣṭānavatiḥ kriyante` explicitly locates the ninety-eightfold classification within Abhidharma, but the passage is presented as the governing classificatory account rather than as an objection or disputed school voice.

---

## New Technical Terms

- **dṛṣṭisvabhāva** — having the nature of view
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only classificatory expression in this unit
  - **function:** divides the ten anuśayas into five views and five non-views

- **pañcadhā kṛtvā** — making fivefold / differentiating into five
  - **status:** prose operation not stated explicitly in the Kārikā
  - **function:** makes the classificatory act itself explicit

- **aṣṭānavati** — ninety-eight
  - **status:** new chapter-level total introduced by the Bhāṣya before its derivation
  - **function:** target of the next classificatory sequence

- **kāmāvacara / rūpāvacara / ārūpyāvacara** — belonging to the desire, form, and formless domains
  - **status:** previously encountered domain terminology
  - **function:** first discriminator generating the ninety-eightfold system

- **darśanaprahātavya** — to be abandoned through seeing
  - **status:** previously anticipated in the Kārikās; now structurally operative
  - **function:** Path classification of anuśayas according to their mode of removal

- **bhāvanāprahātavya** — to be abandoned through cultivation
  - **status:** previously anticipated; now structurally operative
  - **function:** complementary Path classification

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya confirms an important correction to the first-pass reading: the tenfold count is obtained by taking the original six and replacing the single `dṛṣṭi` with its five species.

```text
6 - 1 + 5 = 10
```

But VAK 5.03 is more important than this arithmetic suggests. It reveals the scientific procedure by which Abhidharma constructs its classificatory field:

```text
basic genus
    ↓ internal differentiation
basic species
    ↓ domain discrimination
    ↓ Path discrimination
complete operative matrix
```

The five `dṛṣṭis` are therefore not merely a list of wrong opinions. They are five distinct forms of afflicted cognition that must later be located in domains and assigned a specific mode of abandonment.

The passage also gives a clean conceptual partition:

```text
dṛṣṭisvabhāva
    five anuśayas whose very nature is view

adṛṣṭisvabhāva
    rāga
    pratigha
    māna
    avidyā
    vicikitsā
```

This will matter later because a cognitive error and an affective or dispositional affliction do not necessarily have the same Path-removal structure.

---

## Logical Determination

The key operation of VAK 5.03 is **determinate division**.

```text
one determination: dṛṣṭi
    ↓
internal difference made explicit
    ↓
five species of view
```

But Vasubandhu does not stop at division. The result becomes the input to a higher classification:

```text
10 basic anuśayas
    × domain distinctions
    × abandonment distinctions
    → 98 operative cases
```

This is a genuine systematic construction. Meaning is produced through successive discriminators, not through a flat vocabulary list.

The progression from 5.01–5.03 can now be stated cleanly:

```text
5.01
    root principle
    anuśaya is the root of bhava

5.02
    first internal differentiation
    rāga divides according to mode/domain

5.03
    second internal differentiation
    dṛṣṭi divides according to cognitive form

then
    domain + Path discrimination
    generate the full field
```

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The first-pass translation is structurally correct, but two points are now fixed by the Bhāṣya:

1. `punar daśa` means exactly that the original six are recalculated as ten by making `dṛṣṭi` fivefold.
2. The five views should remain technical terms for now. Their precise meanings are not yet fully defined here; the later Bhāṣya, especially around VAK 5.7–5.8, supplies the substantive definitions and Path consequences.

For that reason, the Bhāṣya-pass translation should avoid prematurely expanding `satkāyadṛṣṭi`, `dṛṣṭiparāmarśa`, or `śīlavṛtaparāmarśa` into interpretive English prose.


## Bhāṣya Pass Result

VAK 5.03 establishes the ten basic anuśayas by differentiating `dṛṣṭi` into five forms, and immediately turns that tenfold classification into the basis of the ninety-eightfold Abhidharma matrix. The decisive advance is therefore not the number ten itself, but the method:

> **internal differentiation of a cognitive genus, followed by domain and Path discrimination, generates the operative structure of the afflictive field.**

VAK 5.04 now begins the explicit derivation of that field.
