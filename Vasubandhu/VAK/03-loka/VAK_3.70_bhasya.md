# VAK_3.70 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
pañcavarṣopamo yāvat daśavarṣopamaḥ śiśuḥ /
saṃbhavatyeṣu saṃpūrṇāḥ savastrāścaiva rūpiṇaḥ // VAkK_3.70 //
```

**Working translation:**

Among these six deva classes, a newborn appears successively like a child from five through ten years of age. The form-realm gods arise complete and clothed.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.70.md`.

## Natural Argument

The prose first explains divine parentage: when a divine boy or girl appears in the lap (`utsaṅga`) of a god or goddess, that being is regarded as their son or daughter. It then asks the newborn's size.

The answer is distributive across the six kāma-deva classes:

```text
1st class → like a 5-year-old
2nd class → like a 6-year-old
3rd class → like a 7-year-old
4th class → like an 8-year-old
5th class → like a 9-year-old
6th class → like a 10-year-old
```

This follows from `yathāsaṃkhyaṃ ṣaṭsu devanikāyeṣu`, “respectively in the six classes of gods.” These divine children then mature very rapidly.

The second half of the verse concerns the rūpāvacara gods: they arise with complete bodies and already clothed. Vasubandhu adds that all devas speak the ārya language.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — question, distributive explanation, rapid maturation, and explanation of rūpāvacara birth.

## Bhāṣya Translation

### Literal Translation

When a divine boy or divine girl is born in the lap of a god or goddess, he becomes their son and she their daughter. How large are they when born?

“A child resembling one of five years, up to one resembling one of ten years, arises among these”—respectively among the six classes of gods. But they grow very quickly.

“The form-possessing ones are complete and clothed”: the gods belonging to the form realm arise with complete bodies and enveloped in clothing. All gods speak the ārya language.

### Philosophical Translation

Divine birth is classified by realm and class rather than by ordinary biological infancy. Among the six desire-realm deva classes, birth appears in a graded child-form from approximately five to ten human years, followed by rapid maturation. In the form realm, birth is more immediately complete: the gods arise with fully formed bodies and clothing already present.

## New Technical Terms

- **utsaṅga** — lap
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** determines divine parentage by the locus in which the child appears

- **yathāsaṃkhyam** — respectively, in corresponding order
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** distributes the five-to-ten-year sequence across the six deva classes

- **devanikāya** — class/group of gods
  - **status:** previously encountered doctrinal vocabulary
  - **function:** classification of the six kāma-deva populations

- **abhivardhate** — grows, matures
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** distinguishes initial birth-form from rapid subsequent maturation

- **rūpāvacara** — belonging to the form realm
  - **status:** previously encountered
  - **function:** identifies the referent of `rūpiṇaḥ` in the second half of the verse

- **āryabhāṣā** — ārya language
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only addition in this passage
  - **function:** common linguistic determination attributed to all gods

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya distinguishes two modes of divine arising:

```text
kāmāvacara devas
    → graded childlike birth-form
    → rapid maturation

rūpāvacara devas
    → complete body at arising
    → already clothed
```

The crucial point is that the verse's `saṃpūrṇāḥ savastrāś caiva rūpiṇaḥ` does not simply describe the same six kāma-devas. The Bhāṣya explicitly takes this pāda as describing **rūpāvacara devas**.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Translation should be revised.**

The first-pass file treated the entire verse as describing the six kāma-devas. The Bhāṣya divides it:

```text
first half
    → six kāma-deva classes

second half
    → rūpāvacara gods
```

It also resolves the five-to-ten range as a corresponding sixfold sequence through `yathāsaṃkhyam`.

## Logical Determination

```text
class of rebirth
    → mode of initial embodiment
        → degree of immediate completion
```

As the cosmological hierarchy rises, embodiment is presented as increasingly immediate and complete. This remains a determination of conditioned birth, not liberation from birth.

## Organon Contact Point

The useful distinction here is simply between **birth** and the **form in which birth immediately appears**. Greater completeness of manifestation does not abolish conditioned arising.
