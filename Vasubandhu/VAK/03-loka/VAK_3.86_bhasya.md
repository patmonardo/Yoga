### Source Caution

The transmitted Kārikā compound is highly compressed. The Bhāṣya, not speculative segmentation of the verse alone, governs the sequence. In particular, the prose gives the names `loharajaḥ`, `avrajas`, `dāśarajas`, `aiḍakarajaḥ`, `gorajaḥ`, and `vātāyanacchidrarajaḥ`. These source forms are preserved here without silent normalization.

## Natural Argument

Vasubandhu continues directly from the `paramāṇu` introduced at VAK 3.85. The governing statement is explicit:

```text
etat paramāṇvādikaṃ saptaguṇottaraṃ veditavyam
```

The series beginning with the ultimate atom is to be understood as increasing sevenfold at each successive stage.

He then spells out the scale:

```text
7 paramāṇus
    → 1 aṇu

7 aṇus
    → 1 loharajas

7 loharajas
    → 1 avrajas

7 avrajas
    → 1 dāśarajas

7 dāśarajas
    → 1 aiḍakarajas

7 aiḍakarajas
    → 1 gorajas

7 gorajas
    → 1 vātāyanacchidrarajas

7 vātāyanacchidrarajas
    → 1 likṣā

7 likṣās
    → 1 yūkā

7 yūkās
    → 1 yava

7 yavas
    → 1 aṅgulīparva
```

The Bhāṣya additionally explains `tadudbhavā`: what arises from a `likṣā` is a `yūkā`. Finally, three finger-joints constitute a finger; Vasubandhu says this is so well known that it was not stated in the Kārikā.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The passage is straightforward exegetical measurement. No objection or competing school position appears.

## New Technical Terms

- **loharajas** — a named minute dust-measure following `aṇu`
  - **status:** absent as an explicit independent term from the Kārikā's segmented first-pass analysis
  - **function:** member of the sevenfold material measure series

- **vātāyanacchidrarajas** — dust visible in an aperture/window opening
  - **status:** made explicit by the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** named material measure immediately below `likṣā`

- **yūkā** — the organism arising from a `likṣā`; next measure before `yava`
  - **status:** absent explicitly from the Kārikā
  - **function:** mediating unit in the sevenfold scale

- **saptaguṇottara** — successively sevenfold greater
  - **status:** present in the Kārikā, explicitly operationalized in the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** governing rule of the entire measure sequence

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya converts a compressed list into a formally recursive scale of rūpa-measure. The important determination is not merely that there are progressively larger particles, but that each level is generated by a fixed numerical aggregation of the preceding level.

```text
material minimum
    → fixed aggregation rule
        → increasingly gross measurable form
```

This is still produced, conditioned Loka. The sequence describes how material magnitude is measured within the world-field; it is not a genetic metaphysical Principle.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Revision required.**

The first-pass Kārikā file provisionally parsed the compressed compound as iron-, water-, rabbit-, sheep-, cow-dust and similar items. The Bhāṣya shows that the intended technical series must instead be read through its explicit prose names. The central translation should therefore emphasize the sevenfold sequence rather than speculative lexical decompositions of the compound.

The Bhāṣya also adds a unit absent from the verse's explicit surface list:

```text
likṣā
    → tadudbhavā yūkā
    → yava
    → aṅgulīparva
```

## Logical Determination

The reasoning form is recursive quantitative generation:

```text
x
    × 7
        → next measure
```

Repeated aggregation connects the analytically minimal `paramāṇu` to ordinary embodied measure. The relation is therefore one of determinate quantity, not vague transition from imperceptible to perceptible.

## Organon Contact Point

A useful restrained contact point is:

```text
Determinate magnitude arises through a fixed rule of aggregation.
```

This is a local quantitative structure internal to produced Loka. No transcendental significance should be imported into the sevenfold sequence itself.
