# VAK_3.60_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
ardhena meroś candrārkau pañcāśat-saika-yojanau /
ardharātro 'staṃgamanaṃ madhyāhna udayaḥ sakṛt // VAK_3.60 //
```

**Working translation:** At half the height of Meru move the moon and sun; the lunar disk is fifty yojanas and the solar disk fifty-one. Midnight, sunset, midday, and sunrise occur simultaneously in the four continents.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.60.md`.

## Bhāṣya Source

The Bhāṣya asks how far the moon and sun are from the earth-region and explains `ardhena meroḥ` by saying that they move level with the summit of Yugandhara. It then interprets the measure exactly: the lunar disk is fifty yojanas and the solar disk fifty-one.

It describes a fiery crystalline disk beneath the solar vimāna, heating and illuminating, and a watery disk beneath the lunar vimāna, cool and luminous. Their effects upon beings, sight, bodies, fruits, flowers, crops, and medicines occur, beneficially or harmfully as appropriate, through the karma of living beings.

The Bhāṣya next asks whether the one sun performs the same function simultaneously in all four continents. It answers no. Rather:

```text
Uttarakuru      midnight
Pūrvavideha     sunset
Jambūdvīpa      midday
Godānīya        sunrise
```

The same pattern is to be applied cyclically elsewhere. Differences in the sun's course ground the increase and decrease of night and day.

## New Technical Terms

- **Yugandhara-giri** — Yugandhara mountain
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** fixes the vertical level of sun and moon

- **candra-maṇḍala** — lunar disk
  - **status:** Bhāṣya expansion
  - **function:** specifies the fifty-yojana measure

- **sūrya-maṇḍala** — solar disk
  - **status:** Bhāṣya expansion
  - **function:** specifies the fifty-one-yojana measure

- **sphaṭika-maṇḍala** — crystalline disk
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only explanatory term here
  - **function:** explains solar heating and illumination

- **taijasa** — fiery / fire-produced
  - **function:** characterizes the solar disk

- **āpya** — watery / water-produced
  - **function:** characterizes the lunar disk

  - **function:** frames whether one sun performs its function simultaneously in all four continents

- **gati-bheda** — difference in course
  - **function:** ground of changing lengths of night and day

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Material clarification.**

The Bhāṣya explicitly resolves `pañcāśat-saika-yojanau` as:

```text
moon = 50 yojanas
sun  = 51 yojanas
```

`ardhena meroḥ` is clarified through the statement that both move level with the summit of Yugandhara.

The temporal clause is also made concrete by assigning midnight, sunset, midday, and sunrise to the four continents.

## Doctrinal Determination

The passage converts bhājanaloka from a static spatial map into a dynamically differentiated world-field:

```text
Meru / Yugandhara spatial order
    → sun and moon motion
        → heating / cooling / illumination
            → four-continent temporal differentiation
                → changing lengths of day and night
```

The karmic remark is significant: the effects of the celestial bodies belong to the karmically produced field of living beings.

## Logical Determination

```text
one ordered celestial process
    → multiple simultaneous local determinations
```

Midnight, sunset, midday, and sunrise are reciprocal determinations of one process relative to distinct locations. They are relational, not absolute.

## Organon Contact Point

This is a clean Rational–Empirical example of differentiated appearance within one produced world-order. The unity of the process does not erase the plurality of local determinations; nor are those determinations independent absolutes.

It remains derivative Loka, not the genetic Principle itself.
