## Natural Argument

### 1. `daśā` means “state,” not “ten”

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

This is the principal correction supplied by the Bhāṣya.

The Kārikā segment:

```iast
pūrvakleśā daśā 'vidyā
```

is explained directly as:

```iast
yā pūrvake janmani kleśāvasthā sehāvidyetyucyate
```

> The state of affliction in the former birth is here called ignorance.

Thus `daśā` is not the numeral `daśa` (“ten”). It is `daśā`, **state / condition**.

The correct segmentation is:

```text
pūrva-kleśa-daśā
    = the former afflictive state

avidyā
    = the name given to that state here
```

The Bhāṣya removes the ambiguity completely.

### 2. Avidyā names the whole former afflictive state by predominance and association

Vasubandhu explains why the entire former kleśa-state receives the name `avidyā`:

```iast
sāhacaryāt tadvaśena teṣāṃ samudācārācca
```

Ignorance accompanies the other afflictions, and their active occurrence takes place under its influence.

The analogy is royal:

```text
“The king comes”
    ↓
includes the coming of the king's attendants
```

Likewise:

```text
avidyā
    ↓
stands for the whole afflictive state
    because the other kleśas accompany it
    and operate under its influence
```

This is not numerical inclusion. It is **designation by predominance / accompaniment**.

### 3. The same word `daśā` carries forward to saṃskāra

The next gloss is explicit:

```iast
daśeti varttate
```

> “State” continues [to be understood].

So:

```iast
saṃskārāḥ pūrvakarmaṇaḥ
```

means that the **state of former karma** is here called `saṃskāra`.

Vasubandhu specifies:

```iast
pūrvajanmanyeva yā puṇyādikarmāvasthā seha saṃskārā ityucyante yasya karmaṇa iha vipākaḥ
```

The karmic state in the former birth—meritorious and so forth—whose result matures here in the present birth is designated `saṃskāra`.

The two prior members are therefore parallel:

```text
former kleśa-state
    → avidyā

former karma-state
    → saṃskāra
```

### 4. Vijñāna is not merely one isolated cognition at the junction

The Kārikā says:

```iast
saṃdhiskandhās tu vijñānam
```

The Bhāṣya explains:

```iast
mātuḥ kukṣī pratisaṃdhikṣaṇe pañca skandhā vijñānam
```

> At the moment of rebirth-linking in the mother's womb, the five aggregates are [called] vijñāna.

This is another important refinement.

The member `vijñāna` does not here denote merely a single cognitive event abstracted from the rest of the aggregates. **The five aggregates present at the rebirth-linking moment are designated by the predominant member `vijñāna`.**

```text
pratisaṃdhi-kṣaṇa
    ↓
five aggregates
    ↓
designated “vijñāna”
```

This anticipates the general principle later made explicit at VAK 3.25: each member names an entire five-aggregate stage according to the dharma predominant in that stage.

### 5. Nāmarūpa is a temporal stage after the linking moment

The Kārikā closes:

```iast
nāmarūpam ataḥ param
```

> After this, name-and-form.

The definition requires the first pāda of 3.22:

```iast
prāk ṣaḍāyatanotpādāt
```

Vasubandhu explains:

> From after the linking mind until the six spheres have arisen, that stage is called nāmarūpa.

Thus `nāmarūpa` is not merely a static psycho-physical pair. Here it names a **developmental stage** of the same aggregate continuum.

```text
saṃdhi-citta
    ↓
nāmarūpa-stage
    ↓
production of ṣaḍāyatana
```

The Bhāṣya adds a technical clarification: strictly speaking one might have expected reference to the arising of four spheres, but the text says “six spheres” because the stage is established doctrinally in that manner.

## Philosophical Translation of the Bhāṣya

What, then, are these members beginning with ignorance?

The afflictive state belonging to the former birth is here called **ignorance**. It receives that designation because ignorance accompanies the other afflictions and because those afflictions become active under its influence, just as saying “the king comes” includes the coming of his attendants.

The word **state** is to be carried forward into the next member as well. The karmic state of the former birth—meritorious and otherwise—whose result matures in the present birth is here called **formations**.

At the moment of rebirth-linking in the mother's womb, the five aggregates are designated **knowledge (`vijñāna`)**. After that linking moment, until the six spheres arise, the aggregate-stage is designated **name-and-form (`nāmarūpa`)**.

Thus the members are not isolated entities standing one after another. Each is the name of a temporally determinate aggregate-state, designated according to the factor that is principal within it.

---

## The Crucial Correction: `daśā` ≠ `daśa`

The previous Kārikā analysis treated:

```text
daśā
    as
“ten”
```

and consequently read:

```text
pūrvakleśā daśāvidyā
    ≈ ten former afflictions are ignorance
```

The Bhāṣya makes this reading untenable.

It explicitly glosses:

```text
daśā
    = avasthā
    = state / condition
```

and then says:

```iast
daśeti varttate
```

showing that the same “state” construction carries into the definition of `saṃskāra`.

The corrected structure is:

```text
pūrva-kleśa-daśā
    → avidyā

pūrva-karma-daśā
    → saṃskāra
```

This is a substantive correction to the Kārikā translation and analysis.

## New Technical Terms

- **daśā** — state, condition.
  - **status:** crucially clarified by the Bhāṣya; previously misread in the Kārikā pass.
  - **function:** defines avidyā and saṃskāra as names of whole prior-life states rather than isolated items or numerical sets.

- **kleśāvasthā** — afflictive state.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only formulation in this unit.
  - **function:** gives the referent of the avidyā member.

- **sāhacarya** — accompaniment, association.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya reflective vocabulary.
  - **function:** explains why the entire kleśa-state can be designated “avidyā.”

- **samudācāra** — active occurrence / manifestation.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya technical vocabulary.
  - **function:** explains that the other afflictions become active under the influence of avidyā.

- **karmāvasthā** — karmic state.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only formulation in this unit.
  - **function:** gives the referent of the saṃskāra member.

- **pratisaṃdhikṣaṇa** — moment of rebirth-linking.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-specific temporal determination here.
  - **function:** defines the vijñāna member.

- **saṃdhicitta** — linking mind / mind at conjunction.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-specific formulation.
  - **function:** temporal boundary after which the nāmarūpa stage begins.

- **vyavasthāpana** — systematic establishment / assignment.
  - **status:** recurring Bhāṣya reflective vocabulary.
  - **function:** explains doctrinal naming of the developmental stage by the six-sphere boundary.

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya now reveals the method underlying the twelve-member scheme:

```text
one skandha-saṃtāna
    ↓
divided into avasthāḥ
    ↓
each avasthā named by its predominant determination
```

For the first four members:

```text
former afflictive state
    = avidyā

former karmic state
    = saṃskāra

five aggregates at rebirth-linking
    = vijñāna

post-linking aggregate stage before ṣaḍāyatana
    = nāmarūpa
```

This means the twelve limbs should not yet be treated as twelve separately existing substances or even as twelve isolated events. They are **stage-designations within a conditioned aggregate continuum**.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Substantive correction required.**

The existing analysis must abandon the reading “tenfold ignorance.”

### Corrected literal translation

> The state of former affliction is ignorance; the formations are the state of former karma. But the aggregates at the junction are knowledge; after this is name-and-form.

### Corrected philosophical translation

> The afflictive state of the prior birth is designated ignorance, and the karmic state of that prior birth is designated formations. At the rebirth-junction the five aggregates are designated knowledge; the subsequent aggregate-stage is designated name-and-form.

This translation is directly controlled by Vasubandhu's prose.

A later repository update to `VAK_3.21.md` should correct the segmentation and remove the interpretation of `daśā` as the numeral ten.

## Logical Determination

The important movement is no longer:

```text
individual cause
    → individual cause
    → individual cognition
    → psycho-physical entity
```

but:

```text
conditioned continuum
    ↓
differentiation into stages
    ↓
designation by predominance
```

The first four members are therefore reflective determinations of one continuity:

```text
kleśa-avasthā
    ↓
avidyā

karma-avasthā
    ↓
saṃskāra

pratisaṃdhi-avasthā
    ↓
vijñāna

post-linking developmental avasthā
    ↓
nāmarūpa
```

The Bhāṣya is already preparing the explicit thesis of VAK 3.25 that this dependent co-arising is `āvasthika`—a stage-based presentation.

---

## Organon Contact Point

The restrained contact point is powerful: **a named determination may designate an entire state because it is principal within that state**.

That gives us a useful rational-empirical pattern:

```text
concrete state
    ↓
predominant determination
    ↓
name of the state
```

The universal term does not replace the concrete field; it expresses the factor through which that field is intelligibly organized.

This is much cleaner than treating the twelve members as a flat chain of atomized causes.
