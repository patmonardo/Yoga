# VAK_3.54_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
sārdhatriyojanaṃ tv ekaṃ prāgvideho 'rdhacandravat /
pārśvatrayaṃ tathāsyaikaṃ sārdhaṃ triśatayojanam // VAkK_3.54 //
```

**Working translation:**

Jambūdvīpa's fourth side is three and a half yojanas. East of Sumeru, Prāgvideha is shaped like a half-moon; three of its sides are like Jambūdvīpa's, while the fourth is three hundred fifty yojanas.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.54.md`.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya first closes the geometrical description of Jambūdvīpa by explaining why it is cart-shaped: its fourth side is only three and a half yojanas.

It then turns east of Sumeru to Pūrvavideha/Prāgvideha. This continent is formed like a half-moon. Three sides have the same two-thousand-yojana measure as Jambūdvīpa's long sides, while the fourth is three hundred fifty yojanas.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — descriptive exposition.
- No separate opponent appears.

## New Technical Terms

- **śakaṭākṛti** — cart-shaped
  - **function:** form of Jambūdvīpa explained by its short fourth side

- **ardhacandravat** — like a half-moon
  - **function:** form of Prāgvideha

- **abhinirvṛtta** — formed / come into formation
  - **function:** expresses the produced shape of the continent

## Doctrinal Determination

The continents are not interchangeable regions in homogeneous space. Their shapes and measures are determinate features of the produced bhājanaloka.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The Bhāṣya resolves the relation of the opening numerical phrase to Jambūdvīpa and gives the exact shape and dimensions of Prāgvideha.
