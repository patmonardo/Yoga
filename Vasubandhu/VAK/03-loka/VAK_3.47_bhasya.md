# VAK_3.47_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
tiryak trīṇi sahasrāṇi sārdhaṃ śatacatuṣṭayam /
lakṣadvādaśakaṃ caiva jalakāñcanamaṇḍalam // VAkK_3.47 //
```

**Working translation:**

The water- and gold-maṇḍalas extend transversely twelve lakṣas, three thousand, and four hundred fifty yojanas.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.47.md`.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya clarifies that the two supports introduced in the preceding verse — the water-maṇḍala and the golden maṇḍala — have the same horizontal extent. The Kārikā gives their transverse measure; the prose then explains the following phrase `samantatas tu triguṇam` by giving the circumferential reckoning as three times the transverse measure.

The important distinction is therefore between:

```text
vertical differentiation
    water and gold differ in depth

horizontal equality
    water and gold share one extent

circumferential reckoning
    the surrounding measure is threefold
```

The Bhāṣya is exegetical here rather than polemical. No opponent is introduced.

## Literal Prose Translation

These two are equal in extent. When reckoned all around by circumference, however, the measure becomes threefold: thirty-six lakṣas, ten thousand, and three hundred fifty yojanas.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — direct numerical clarification.
- No independent objector or school position appears in this unit.

## New Technical Terms

- **vistāra** — extent / breadth
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** specifies horizontal measure

- **parikṣepa** — circumference / surrounding measure
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** gives the encircling measurement of the maṇḍala

- **parigaṇyamāna** — being reckoned / calculated
  - **status:** ordinary explanatory vocabulary
  - **function:** indicates the numerical operation used for circumference

## Doctrinal Determination

VAkK 3.47 completes the basic dimensional determination of the water-and-gold support system. The world-container is not merely layered; it is measured along distinct spatial axes.

```text
udvedha / ucchraya
    vertical measure

vistāra / tiryak
    horizontal measure

parikṣepa
    circumferential measure
```

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The Bhāṣya confirms that the numerical expression applies equally to both the water- and gold-maṇḍalas in horizontal extent.

## Logical Determination

The sequence from 3.45–3.47 is:

```text
formation
    → vertical differentiation
        → horizontal equality
            → circumferential determination
```

The bhājanaloka has become a geometrically articulated support-field.

## Organon Contact Point

The significant relation here is internal to the text: a produced world is progressively determined through measure. Loka is not itself a genetic Principle or Power; it is a produced field whose articulation can nevertheless be systematic.
