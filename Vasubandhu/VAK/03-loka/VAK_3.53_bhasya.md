# VAK_3.53_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
lakṣatrayaṃ sahasrāṇi viṃśatirdve ca tatra tu /
jambūdvīpo dvisāhasrastripārśvaḥ śakaṭākṛtiḥ // VAkK_3.53 //
```

**Working translation:**

The outer ocean is three lakṣas and twenty-two thousand yojanas wide. In it, Jambūdvīpa has three sides of two thousand yojanas and is shaped like a cart.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.53.md`.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya first completes the measure of the outer ocean and states that the four great continents lie in it on the four sides of Sumeru. It then turns to Jambūdvīpa, whose three long sides are each two thousand yojanas and whose overall form is compared to a cart.

The prose adds a major soteriological determination absent from the verse: in the middle of Jambūdvīpa, on the golden earth, the `vajrāsana` comes into formation. All Bodhisattvas sit there when they generate `vajropama-samādhi`. No other support or place is capable of bearing it.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — descriptive exposition.
- No independent opponent appears.

## New Technical Terms

- **vajrāsana** — diamond seat
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only addition in this verse unit
  - **function:** unique locus for the Bodhisattva's diamond-like samādhi

- **vajropama-samādhi** — diamond-like samādhi
  - **status:** Bhāṣya technical term
  - **function:** terminal Bodhisattva concentration requiring the vajrāsana

- **āśraya** — support
  - **function:** expresses the unique capacity of the vajrāsana

- **pradeśa** — place / locus
  - **function:** spatial qualification of the Path-event

## Doctrinal Determination

This passage shows that the Kośa's geography is not soteriologically neutral. A determinate place within the produced world can have a unique Path-capacity.

```text
Jambūdvīpa
    → vajrāsana
        → supports vajropama-samādhi
```

The Bhāṣya explicitly denies that any other support or locus can bear this event.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The verse's geography stands, but the Bhāṣya adds the decisive vajrāsana doctrine.

## Logical Determination

The movement is from general inhabited world to a singular privileged locus within it. Spatial determination can therefore become functionally relevant to the Path.

## Organon Contact Point

A produced Loka can contain loci with distinct capacities. This should not be inflated into the claim that Loka itself is a genetic Power; rather, the Path can require a specifically determined support within manifestation.
