# VAK_3.17 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

**VAkK 3.17**

```iast
garbhāvakrāntayas tisraś cakravartti-svayaṃbhuvām /
karma-jñāna-ubhayeṣāṃ vā viśadatvād yathākramam //
```

**Best current translation**

> There are three modes of womb-entry for the cakravartin and the self-arisen ones, respectively according to the clarity of karma, of knowledge, or of both.

## Bhāṣya Source

The natural unit begins with the prose continuation from the preceding verse and closes before the explicit anti-ātman objection introduced immediately afterward.

## Segmentation by Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya proceeds in four movements.

### 1. Clear awareness is defined negatively and positively

The clearly aware being knows correctly:

```text
I enter the mother's womb.
I remain here.
I leave from here.
```

This is not merely increased vividness. Vasubandhu explicitly says that in this case the inverted `saṃjñā` and `adhimokṣa` do not occur.

### 2. The first three awareness-profiles are assigned to three exceptional beings

The three refined modes of `garbhāvakrānti` are assigned respectively to:

```text
cakravartin
    clear at entry

pratyekabuddha
    clear at entry and residence

samyaksaṃbuddha
    clear at entry, residence, and exit
```

The prose thus resolves the compressed `cakravartti-svayaṃbhuvām`: the `svayaṃbhū` category is unpacked as pratyekabuddha and samyaksaṃbuddha.

### 3. A second explanatory axis is introduced

The three refined womb-entries can also be explained through the clarity of:

```text
karma
jñāna
both karma and jñāna
```

### 4. The ordinary fourth mode is inferred by remainder

Once the three exceptional modes have been assigned, Vasubandhu concludes:

```iast
śeṣāṇāṃ caturthīti siddhaṃ bhavati
```

For the remainder, the fourth mode is thereby established: confusion through the process.

## Literal Prose Translation

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

> One who is clearly aware knows correctly: “I am entering my mother's womb; I remain here; from here I shall emerge.” In him the inverted perception and determination do not occur.
>
> Here another classification is taught: “There are three womb-entries of the cakravartin and the self-arisen ones.” This means the cakravartin and the two self-arisen ones, the Pratyekabuddha and the perfectly awakened Buddha. “Respectively” will be stated at the end. The first belongs to the cakravartin: he enters with clear awareness, but does not remain or emerge with it. The Pratyekabuddha also remains with clear awareness; the Buddha also emerges with clear awareness. Here too the designation is made in view of the future state.
>
> Or, respectively, because of the clarity of karma, knowledge, or both. The first belongs to those whose karma is clear, whose meritorious activity is exalted. The second belongs to those whose knowledge is clear, who are learned and have practiced discrimination. The third belongs to those whose meritorious karma and knowledge are both clear. These very beings beginning with the cakravartin fit these conditions respectively. For the remainder, the fourth [mode] is thereby established.

## Argument Reconstruction

VAkK 3.16 gave a phenomenology of birth-entry:

```text
clear entry
clear residence
clear exit
or confusion throughout
```

VAkK 3.17 now asks what grounds those differences.

The answer is not one-dimensional.

### Axis A — extent of clear awareness

```text
cakravartin
    entry

pratyekabuddha
    entry + residence

Buddha
    entry + residence + exit
```

### Axis B — source of clarity

```text
clear karma
    → first mode

clear knowledge
    → second mode

clear karma + clear knowledge
    → third mode
```

The two axes are correlated:

```text
clarity of transition
    is grounded in determinate qualities
    of karma and/or knowledge
```

This is an important advance over a merely psychological reading. The degree of lucid awareness is not presented as a free-standing inner achievement at that moment. It reflects already determined differences in the causal and cognitive field.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu** throughout this unit.

No objection is staged within the unit. The next prose sentence introduces the externalists who seize upon the language of transmigration to argue for an ātman; that belongs to the next natural argument.

## New Technical Terms

- **udāra-puṇya-kriyā** — exalted meritorious activity
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only specification in this passage
  - **function:** causal explanation of the first clarified womb-entry

- **bāhuśrutya** — extensive learning / learnedness
  - **status:** prose specification
  - **function:** explanatory condition of clarified knowledge

- **kṛta-pravicaya** — having practiced discrimination / analysis
  - **status:** prose specification
  - **function:** explanatory condition of clarified knowledge

- **bhāvinī saṃjñā** — designation made in view of a future state
  - **status:** prose clarification
  - **function:** controls how names such as cakravartin, pratyekabuddha, and Buddha are applied to the pre-birth continuity

- **viśada-karma / viśada-jñāna** — clear karma / clear knowledge
  - **status:** verse terms made technically operative by prose
  - **function:** grounds for differentiated clarity in womb-entry

## Doctrinal and Logical Determination

The Bhāṣya makes three corrections to a loose reading of the Kārikā.

### 1. `svayaṃbhū` is not metaphysical self-generation

The prose explicitly identifies the two `svayaṃbhūs` as:

```text
pratyekabuddha
samyaksaṃbuddha
```

So `svayaṃbhū` here is a classificatory designation for these awakened types, not a claim about an Absolute Self or a self-caused substance.

### 2. `jñāna` is concretely specified

The prose defines clarified knowledge through:

```text
bāhuśrutya
    extensive learning

kṛta-pravicaya
    practiced discrimination
```

This is important. The relevant `jñāna` is not an unexplained luminous principle. It is knowledge made clear through learning and discriminative analysis.

### 3. Clarity is field-grounded

The three refined womb-entries arise through differences in:

```text
karma
knowledge
karma + knowledge
```

Thus lucidity at the transition is itself determined by prior causal and cognitive formation.

The logical form is:

```text
quality of determining field
    ↓
quality of transition-awareness
```

not:

```text
isolated individual
    spontaneously possesses lucidity
```

---

## Translation Consequences for the Kārikā

The first-pass translation remains basically sound, but two points should be made explicit in future revision.

First, `svayaṃbhuvām` is best understood contextually as referring to the Pratyekabuddha and the Samyaksaṃbuddha, not left as an unrestricted metaphysical “self-arisen ones.”

Second, `viśadatva` is better translated as **clarity** or **lucidity** than as mere “distinctness,” because the prose directly correlates it with clarified karma and clarified knowledge.

A stronger working translation is therefore:

> There are three clarified modes of womb-entry: that of the cakravartin, the Pratyekabuddha, and the perfectly awakened Buddha, respectively through clarity of karma, of knowledge, or of both.

## Light Organon Contact Point

This verse gives us a very useful correction to the notion of “individual awareness.”

The individual does not simply wake up inside the transition and add truth by being more self-conscious. Its clarity is intelligible only through the determining field:

```text
karma
    causal formation

jñāna
    learned and discriminative formation

ubhaya
    their conjunction
```

So the stronger formula is:

```text
clarity is not added by individuality

clarity is the appearance,
in the individual transition,
of a more highly determined field
```

And Vasubandhu's explanation of `jñāna` is especially important for the Rational Kośa:

```text
jñāna
    is clarified by learning
    and by pravicaya, discrimination
```

The “Rational Savior” is therefore not merely the unusually vivid protagonist of the cosmic drama. The higher clarity appears where the field has already been transformed through discriminative knowing.

That remains a light Organon contact point; the primary textual result is simply that the three exceptional womb-entries are grounded respectively in clear karma, clear knowledge, and both.
