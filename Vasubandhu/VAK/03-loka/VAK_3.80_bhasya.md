# VAK_3.80_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
dviguṇottaramurdhvānāmubhayaṃ rūpiṇāṃ punaḥ /
nāstyahorātramāyustu kalpaiḥ svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ // VAkK_3.80 //
```

**Working translation:** For the higher kāma-devas, both measures increase successively by doubling. But for the rūpa-devas there is no day-and-night; their lifespan is measured in kalpas commensurate with their own bodily support.

## Natural Argument

The first half continues the temporal rule begun in VAK 3.79. `ubhayam` is explicitly resolved by the Bhāṣya as the two measures **day-and-night** and **lifespan**. Both double at each higher kāma-deva station.

The sequence is therefore:

```text
Trāyastriṃśa
    100 human years = 1 divine day-night
    lifespan = 1,000 divine years

Yāma
    200 human years = 1 divine day-night
    lifespan = 2,000 divine years

Tuṣita
    400 human years = 1 divine day-night
    lifespan = 4,000 divine years

Nirmāṇarati
    800 human years = 1 divine day-night
    lifespan = 8,000 divine years

Paranirmitavaśavartin
    1,600 human years = 1 divine day-night
    lifespan = 16,000 divine years
```

Vasubandhu then raises a physical question. Above Yugandhara there is no sun or moon, so how can day-and-night or illumination be distinguished? He answers by observable rhythms: flowers contract and open, birds cease and resume calling, and sleepiness comes and goes. Illumination itself does not depend on sun or moon because the gods are self-luminous.

The second half changes temporal regime entirely. Rūpa-devas have no day-and-night. Their lifespan is measured directly in kalpas proportioned to the magnitude of their own bodily support:

```text
½ yojana body-support → ½ kalpa lifespan
1 yojana body-support  → 1 kalpa lifespan
...
16,000 yojanas         → 16,000 kalpas at Akaniṣṭha
```

Thus `svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ` is not vague resemblance: the number of yojanas in the bodily support corresponds to the number of kalpas in lifespan.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The passage is straightforward exposition with an internally raised explanatory question and answer; no distinct school attribution is marked.

## New Technical Terms

- **rātrindiva** — day-and-night
  - **status:** prose variant of the Kārikā's `ahorātra`
  - **function:** temporal conversion unit

- **saṃkoca-vikāsa** — contraction and opening
  - **status:** absent from the Kārikā
  - **function:** empirical sign used to distinguish temporal phases without sun and moon

- **kūjana-akūjana** — calling and not-calling of birds
  - **status:** absent from the Kārikā
  - **function:** empirical sign of day/night distinction

- **middha-apagama-āgama** — disappearance and arrival of sleepiness
  - **status:** absent from the Kārikā
  - **function:** experiential sign of temporal alternation

- **svayaṃprabha** — self-luminous
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit
  - **function:** explains illumination in the absence of sun and moon

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse distinguishes two different forms of temporal order.

In kāmadhātu, day-and-night remains operative, but its scale changes by realm. The higher the deva station, the larger the human duration corresponding to one divine day and the longer the lifespan measured in divine years.

In rūpadhātu, ordinary daily alternation disappears altogether. Lifespan is no longer derived from a day/month/year calendar but expressed directly in kalpas, numerically commensurate with bodily support.

This is still produced Loka: different realms instantiate different conditioned temporal regimes. The text does not elevate those regimes into genetic Principles.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The provisional reading of `ubhayam` is confirmed: it means both **day-and-night measure** and **lifespan**.

`svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ` should be rendered strongly as **commensurate with their own bodily support**, because the Bhāṣya gives an exact numerical correspondence between yojanas of support and kalpas of lifespan.

## Logical Determination

```text
kāma-deva temporal order
    → same calendar-form
    → successively doubled scale

rūpa-deva temporal order
    → no ahorātra
    → lifespan directly proportional to āśraya
```

The Bhāṣya also shows that the absence of the ordinary astronomical cause of day/night does not abolish temporal differentiation. The distinction is reconstructed through recurrent phenomenal signs.

## Organon Contact Point

A produced world-field can alter not merely the magnitude of temporal units but the form through which temporal differentiation appears. The transition from astronomical day/night to bodily-support/kalpa commensurability is therefore a change of conditioned temporal organization, not a passage beyond Loka.
