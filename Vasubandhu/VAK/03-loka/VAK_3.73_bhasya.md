## Bhāṣya Translation

### Literal Translation

What is this small thousandfold world-system? And what are the two-thousandfold and three-thousandfold systems?

A thousand Jambūdvīpas, a thousand Pūrvavidehas, a thousand Aparagodānīyas, a thousand Uttarakurus; a thousand suns, moons, and Sumerus; a thousand sets of gods from the Cāturmahārājakāyikas up through the Paranirmitavaśavartins; and a thousand Brahma-worlds—this is called the small thousandfold world-system.

### Philosophical Translation

The Bhāṣya removes any ambiguity about what is being multiplied. The small thousandfold world-system is not merely one local world enlarged in size. It is an aggregate formed by repeating the complete four-continent cosmological unit one thousand times, together with its celestial bodies, Meru axis, desire-realm deva hierarchy, and Brahma-world.

## Course of the Argument

### Question or Problem

The preceding verses established the vertical order of deva and dhyāna worlds. Vasubandhu now asks how the larger `lokadhātu` aggregates are constituted.

### Initial Determination

The Kārikā states that one thousand complete world-units constitute the `sāhasra-cūḍika lokadhātu`.

### Reply / Resolution

The prose enumerates exactly what is repeated one thousand times:

```text
1,000 Jambūdvīpas
1,000 Pūrvavidehas
1,000 Aparagodānīyas
1,000 Uttarakurus
1,000 suns
1,000 moons
1,000 Sumerus
1,000 sets of kāma-devas
    Cāturmahārājakāyika
    through Paranirmitavaśavartin
1,000 Brahma-worlds
```

### Result

A `sāhasra-cūḍika lokadhātu` is therefore a thousandfold aggregate of complete cosmological units, not a single enlarged spatial container.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — question, enumeration, and definition of the small thousandfold world-system.

## New Technical Terms

- **sāhasra-cūḍika lokadhātu** — small thousandfold world-system
  - **status:** Kārikā term explicitly defined by the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** cosmological aggregate composed of one thousand complete world-units

- **lokadhātu** — world-domain / world-system
  - **status:** previously encountered doctrinal term
  - **function:** names a structured cosmological domain capable of aggregation into larger systems

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya shows that the unit being multiplied is structurally complete. Each repetition includes:

```text
four continents
    + sun and moon
    + Sumeru
    + kāma-deva hierarchy
    + Brahma-world
```

Thus the cosmological field now becomes explicitly iterable.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The existing translation is essentially correct, but the Bhāṣya establishes that `kāmadivaukasām` means the complete desire-realm deva series from the Cāturmahārājakāyikas through the Paranirmitavaśavartins.

## Logical Determination

```text
complete world-unit
    × 1,000
        → sāhasra-cūḍika lokadhātu
```

The important structural move is multiplication of a determinate whole. The larger world-system is produced by repetition of an already articulated unit rather than by indefinite spatial enlargement.

## Organon Contact Point

This remains produced Loka. The useful point is simply that a determinate totality can itself function as a unit within a larger totality:

```text
whole
    → unit
        → repeated whole
```

No stronger transcendental claim is required here.
