## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya begins with an explicit cosmographic question:

```text
Where are the hells located?
How large are they?
```

It then resolves the compressed wording of the Kārikā very precisely.

### 1. Avīci's location and measure

`asya` refers to **this very Jambūdvīpa**. Avīci lies twenty thousand yojanas beneath it. `tanmātraḥ` means that Avīci itself is twenty thousand yojanas in both vertical extent (`udvedha`) and breadth (`vistāra`). Consequently its lower floor lies forty thousand yojanas beneath the surface reference point.

This resolves the uncertainty deliberately retained in the Kārikā pass.

### 2. Why it is called Avīci

The principal explanation is:

```text
duḥkha-nirantaratvāt avīciḥ
```

It is Avīci because suffering is **without interval** there.

The Bhāṣya contrasts this with other hells, where suffering can have an interval. In Saṃjīva, for example, beings whose bodies have been cut, split, and crushed are touched by cool winds and revived; hence the name **Saṃjīva**, “Reviving.”

An alternative explanation says Avīci is so called because there is no `sukha-vīci`, no interval or wave of pleasure, there. The reply qualifies this: pleasant feeling as **vipāka** is absent in the other hells as well, although a `naiḥṣyandika` pleasant feeling is not thereby excluded.

### 3. The seven hells above Avīci

The principal arrangement places seven hells successively above Avīci:

```text
Pratāpana
Tāpana
Mahāraurava
Raurava
Saṃghāta
Kālasūtra
Saṃjīva
```

An alternative view places them at the sides of Avīci rather than vertically one above another.

### 4. Sixteen utsadas for each

The eight principal hells each possess sixteen `utsada` regions. Vasubandhu then cites the Buddha to authorize the general structure: the eight hells are difficult to cross, filled through cruel karma, each furnished with sixteen subsidiary regions, four walls and four gates, iron-bounded and iron-covered, with blazing iron ground extending for hundreds of yojanas.

The detailed identification of the sixteen `utsadas` begins in VAK 3.59.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — opening question, principal location and dimensions of Avīci, primary etymology, principal vertical ordering of the seven hells, and explanatory transitions.
- **Voice: alternative opinion** — `ity apare`: Avīci is named from absence of a pleasure-interval; separately, the seven hells are located at Avīci's sides.
- **Voice: Vasubandhu / reply** — points out that pleasant feeling as karmic maturation is absent in the other hells as well, while a homogeneous-result (`naiḥṣyandika`) pleasant feeling is not ruled out.
- **Voice: Buddha, cited by Vasubandhu** — authoritative verse describing the eight hells and their sixteen subsidiary regions.

## New Technical Terms

- **mahānaraka** — great hell
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** classifies Avīci as a principal great hell

- **udvedha** — vertical extent / height-depth
  - **status:** Bhāṣya measurement term
  - **function:** specifies one dimension of Avīci

- **vistāra** — breadth / horizontal extent
  - **status:** previously encountered measurement vocabulary
  - **function:** specifies Avīci's other dimension

- **adharatala** — lower floor / lower surface
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only local term
  - **function:** derives the total depth of Avīci's lower boundary

- **duḥkha-nirantaratva** — absence of interval in suffering
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory compound
  - **function:** principal nirvacana of `Avīci`

- **sāntara** — having an interval
  - **status:** Bhāṣya contrast term
  - **function:** distinguishes suffering in other hells from Avīci

- **naiḥṣyandika** — belonging to homogeneous outflow/result
  - **status:** technical Abhidharma term used in an objection/reply
  - **function:** distinguishes a non-vipāka mode of pleasant feeling from pleasant feeling as karmic maturation

- **uparyupari saṃniviṣṭa** — arranged successively one above another
  - **status:** Bhāṣya spatial determination
  - **function:** principal ordering of the seven hells above Avīci

- **utsada** — subsidiary / adjoining hell-region
  - **status:** Kārikā term opened for explanation here and continued in 3.59
  - **function:** secondary painful region attached to each principal hell

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya transforms the Kārikā's compressed numerical statement into a determinate lower-world topology.

```text
Jambūdvīpa surface reference
    ↓ 20,000 yojanas
Avīci upper extent
    ↓ 20,000 yojanas
Avīci lower floor
```

Thus the lower floor is forty thousand yojanas below the surface reference.

The doctrine is not merely a list of hell names. It distinguishes:

```text
location
measure
internal dimension
name by characteristic suffering
ordering of neighboring hells
subsidiary regions
scriptural authorization
```

The important doctrinal clarification is the definition of **Avīci** by uninterrupted suffering. The alternative pleasure-based etymology immediately triggers an Abhidharma distinction between `vipāka` feeling and `naiḥṣyandika` feeling.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Translation should be revised slightly.**

The Bhāṣya settles both phrases marked uncertain in the first pass:

```text
asya
    = of / beneath this very Jambūdvīpa

tanmātraḥ
    = Avīci itself has that same twenty-thousand-yojana measure
      in both vertical extent and breadth
```

A stronger translation is therefore:

> Twenty thousand yojanas beneath this Jambūdvīpa lies Avīci, itself twenty thousand in height and breadth. Above it are seven hells; all eight possess sixteen subsidiary regions.

## Logical Determination

The preceding verse articulated regional surface geography. Here the same produced Loka acquires **depth**.

```text
surface position
    → downward relation
        → measured depth
            → bounded lower domain
                → ordered plurality of hells
                    → subsidiary differentiation
```

The Bhāṣya also demonstrates a characteristic reflective procedure:

```text
compressed name
    → defining characteristic
        → counter-definition
            → technical distinction
```

`Avīci` therefore becomes more than a proper name. Its meaning is tested through the distinction between uninterrupted suffering, absence of pleasant karmic maturation, and the possible presence of a non-vipāka pleasant feeling.

## Organon Contact Point

This remains fully within **produced Loka**. Nothing in the passage warrants treating Avīci or the vertical cosmography as a derivative metaphysical Principle.

What is logically significant is the increasing determinacy of the produced field: a region is not merely posited but located relative to another region, measured internally, bounded by a floor, differentiated into neighbors, and characterized through a distinctive mode of experience.

The reflective power lies in the relations among these determinations, not in elevating any one cosmographic object into the genetic source of the system.
