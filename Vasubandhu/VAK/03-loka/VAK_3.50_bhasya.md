# VAK_3.50_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
catūratnamayo meruḥ jale 'śītisahasrake /
magnāḥ ūrdhvaṃ jalāt merur bhūyo 'śītisahasrakaḥ // VAkK_3.50 //
```

**Working translation:**

Meru is made of four precious substances. The mountains are submerged eighty thousand yojanas in the water; Meru rises another eighty thousand above the water.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya first unpacks `catūratnamaya`: Sumeru has four sides composed respectively of gold, silver, vaidūrya, and crystal. It then adds a phenomenological consequence not explicit in the verse: the substance of each face, through its `anubhāva`, colors the appearance of the sky in that direction. The side facing Jambūdvīpa is said to be vaidūrya; through the coloring influence of its radiance, the sky here appears vaidūrya-colored.

The second half returns to measurement. The mountains are immersed in the water above the golden earth. Meru rises eighty thousand yojanas above the water in addition to its submerged extent.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — explanatory prose.
- **Voice: reported tradition** — `varṇayanti`, “they describe,” concerning the Jambūdvīpa-facing side as vaidūrya.

## New Technical Terms

- **catūratnamaya** — made of four precious substances
  - **function:** material differentiation of Meru's four sides

- **anubhāva** — influence / efficacy
  - **function:** explains the directional coloration of the sky

- **prabhā** — radiance
  - **function:** luminous mediation between Meru's face and visible sky

- **anurāga** — coloring / tinging
  - **function:** explains the appearance of the sky under Meru's radiance

- **magna** — submerged
  - **function:** fixes the mountains' depth within the water-support

## Doctrinal Determination

Meru functions as both a measured cosmological center and a source of directional appearance within the produced world. The Bhāṣya does not treat the sky's color as an independent sensible fact; it is explained relationally through Meru's material face and radiance.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The prose supplies the four materials and the directional-color explanation absent from the verse.

## Logical Determination

```text
central structure
    → four differentiated faces
        → radiance/influence
            → directional sensible appearance
```

The produced world-container conditions not only position but modes of appearance within it.

## Organon Contact Point

A useful later image for the project is that a Domain may be understood as a determinate Light of Substance: not Substance itself, but a field in which appearance takes on a determinate character. The Bhāṣya itself only gives the local cosmological relation of Meru, radiance, direction, and visible color; the stronger formulation belongs to our later synthesis.
