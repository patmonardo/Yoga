# VAK_3.90_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
saṃvartakalpo narakāsaṃbhavād bhājanakṣayaḥ /
vivartakalpaḥ prāgvāyor yāvan narakasaṃbhavaḥ // VAkK_3.90 //
```

**Working translation:** The contraction-kalpa extends from the non-arising of beings in the hells to the destruction of the receptacle-world. The expansion-kalpa extends from the first formative wind until beings arise again in the hells.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.90.md`.

### Source cautions

- The local source has a probable corruption at `[179|11]` (`thamādvāyoḥ`); the sense is secured by the Kārikā and surrounding prose: the vivarta-kalpa begins with the first wind.
- The Bhāṣya supplies the missing process behind the compressed Kārikā: contraction is first a withdrawal of beings and only afterward destruction of the receptacle-world.

## Natural Argument

The contraction-kalpa begins at the first unmistakable sign of cosmic withdrawal: beings die in the hells but no new beings arise there. This is not yet the destruction of the physical world. It is initially a contraction of the inhabited world.

Vasubandhu distinguishes two paired ways of describing contraction:

```text
gati-saṃvartanī     → contraction of destinies

dhātu-saṃvartanī    → contraction of realms

sattva-saṃvartanī   → contraction of beings

bhājana-saṃvartanī  → contraction of the receptacle-world
```

The depopulation proceeds in ordered stages. Hell-beings disappear first, then animals and pretas; human populations disappear continent by continent; kāma-devas disappear; finally beings in Brahmaloka ascend to Ābhāsvara. When no beings remain in Brahmaloka, `sattvasaṃvartanī` is complete.

Only then does `bhājana-saṃvartanī` occur. Seven suns arise and burn the emptied world, including the earth and Sumeru; the destructive process reaches the empty Brahma-world as appropriate to its level. Thus the saṃvarta-kalpa extends from the cessation of new hell-births through the final destruction of the receptacle-world.

Vivarta proceeds in the contrary direction. When the contracted world has remained only as space for a long period, the karma of beings causes subtle winds to arise in space. These increase until the wind-circle and then the remaining structures of the world-system are produced in the previously described order.

First the receptacle-world expands. Afterward beings appear: an Ābhāsvara being is reborn in the empty Brahma-world, other beings follow into the lower Brahma levels, then progressively into the kāma-world, the continents, pretas, animals, and finally the hells.

Vasubandhu gives the governing rule explicitly:

```text
yat paścāt saṃvartate tat pūrvaṃ vivartate
```

What contracts later expands earlier.

Hence contraction and expansion are inverse ordered processes, not merely two names for destruction and creation.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu**, presenting the standard Abhidharma cosmological account and its internal classifications.

## New Technical Terms

- **gati-saṃvartanī** — contraction of a destiny or mode of rebirth
  - **status:** introduced in the Bhāṣya here
  - **function:** classifies cosmic withdrawal according to rebirth-destinies

- **dhātu-saṃvartanī** — contraction of a realm
  - **status:** introduced in the Bhāṣya here
  - **function:** classifies withdrawal according to cosmological realm

- **sattva-saṃvartanī** — contraction of beings
  - **status:** introduced in the Bhāṣya here
  - **function:** marks depopulation of the world-system before environmental destruction

- **bhājana-saṃvartanī** — contraction of the receptacle-world
  - **status:** introduced in the Bhāṣya here
  - **function:** marks the destruction of the environmental world after beings have departed

- **karmādhipatya** — predominance / governing efficacy of karma
  - **status:** previously encountered in the cosmology
  - **function:** explains the re-emergence of the receptacle-world during expansion

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya makes clear that cosmic dissolution is not a single event.

```text
saṃvarta
    → births cease first in the lowest realm
    → beings progressively disappear upward
    → sattva-world becomes empty
    → receptacle-world is destroyed
```

Vivarta is correspondingly ordered:

```text
vivarta
    → formative winds arise
    → receptacle-world is produced
    → higher beings appear first
    → lower destinies are populated progressively
    → hell-beings appear last
```

The important doctrinal principle is inversion of order:

```text
later in contraction
    → earlier in expansion
```

This is a produced and conditioned Loka-process. Nothing here should be elevated into a transcendental or genetic Principle.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only, but substantial.**

The Kārikā translation is correct in its endpoints, but the Bhāṣya reveals that `narakāsaṃbhava` means specifically the point when hell-beings die without replacement, not simply an abstract “absence of hell.” Likewise `bhājanakṣaya` occurs only after a prior ordered depopulation of beings.

For `vivarta`, “first wind” should be understood as the first karmically conditioned formative winds appearing in space, not merely an already constituted wind-element.

## Logical Determination

The verse defines two inverse process-intervals by determinate boundary events:

```text
saṃvarta
    A: cessation of new hell-births
    → ordered depopulation
    → destruction of bhājana

vivarta
    B: first formative wind
    → production of bhājana
    → ordered repopulation
    → arising of hell-beings
```

The Bhāṣya therefore gives a reversible ordering relation:

```text
contraction order = reverse(expansion order)
```

This is a law of sequence internal to the produced world-system.

## Organon Contact Point

Light contact only:

```text
A world-process is determined by ordered transitions and boundary conditions.
```

The useful logical feature is not an analogy to transcendental genesis, but the explicit inverse ordering of two conditioned processes. Loka remains produced.
