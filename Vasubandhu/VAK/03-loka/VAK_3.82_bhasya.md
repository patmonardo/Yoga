# VAK_3.82 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
kāmadevāyuṣā tulyā ahorātrā yathākramam /
saṃjīvādiṣu ṣaṭsv āyus tais teṣāṃ kāmadevavat // VAkK_3.82 //
```

**Working translation:** In the six hells beginning with Saṃjīva, their day-and-nights correspond in order to the lifespans of the kāma-devas; by those units, their lifespans are reckoned like those of the kāma-devas.

## Natural Argument

Vasubandhu turns from the lifespans of favorable destinies to those of unfavorable destinies. For the first six hot hells, the temporal scale is defined by direct correspondence with the six kāma-deva classes.

The entire lifespan of one deva class becomes one day-and-night in the corresponding hell. The hell-being then lives the same numerical number of years as the corresponding deva class, but those years are composed of these enormously enlarged hell day-and-nights.

The correspondence is:

```text
Cāturmahārājakāyika lifespan → 1 Saṃjīva day-night
Saṃjīva lifespan             → 500 hell-years

Trāyastriṃśa lifespan        → 1 Kālasūtra day-night
Kālasūtra lifespan           → 1,000 hell-years

Yāma lifespan                → 1 Saṃghāta day-night
Saṃghāta lifespan            → 2,000 hell-years

Tuṣita lifespan              → 1 Raurava day-night
Raurava lifespan             → 4,000 hell-years

Nirmāṇarati lifespan         → 1 Mahāraurava day-night
Mahāraurava lifespan         → 8,000 hell-years

Paranirmitavaśavartin lifespan → 1 Tāpana day-night
Tāpana lifespan                → 16,000 hell-years
```

The crucial point is therefore twofold: the deva lifespan supplies the day-unit, while the deva's numerical lifespan supplies the year-count.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The passage is straightforward explanatory taxonomy. No objection or competing school voice appears in this unit.

## New Technical Terms

- **durgati** — unfavorable destiny
  - **status:** already familiar doctrinal category, newly operative here as the heading for the lifespan discussion
  - **function:** marks the transition from favorable destinies to hells and other lower destinies

- **rātrindiva / ahorātra** — day-and-night
  - **status:** previously encountered in the deva lifespan calculations
  - **function:** realm-specific temporal unit; here generated from the lifespan of another realm

- **Saṃjīva, Kālasūtra, Saṃghāta, Raurava, Mahāraurava, Tāpana** — first six hot hells
  - **status:** previously encountered in the cosmography of the hells
  - **function:** ordered recipients of the six kāma-deva lifespan measures

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya confirms that the verse is not merely saying that hell lifespans are vaguely analogous to divine lifespans. It defines an exact cross-realm conversion.

```text
corresponding kāma-deva lifespan
    ↓ becomes
one hell day-and-night

same numerical deva lifespan
    ↓ becomes
hell lifespan in hell-years
```

Thus the temporal magnitude is compounded. A long divine lifespan becomes only one day of a lower destiny.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification required.**

The Kārikā translation should make clear that `kāmadevāyuṣā tulyā ahorātrāḥ` means that the **lifespan of each corresponding kāma-deva class equals one day-and-night in the corresponding hell**. `kāmadevavat` then means that the hell lifespan takes the same numerical year-count as the corresponding deva lifespan.

A sharper translation is:

> In the six hells beginning with Saṃjīva, the day-and-nights correspond successively to the lifespans of the kāma-devas; reckoned by those day-and-nights, their lifespans are numerically like those of the kāma-devas.

## Logical Determination

The verse establishes a nested temporal conversion:

```text
lifespan in realm A
    = one day in realm B

numerical lifespan of A
    = numerical year-count of B
```

This is not a single homogeneous cosmic clock. The loka is articulated through internally ordered and mutually convertible temporal scales.

## Organon Contact Point

The useful contact remains modest:

```text
one produced temporal regime
    can function as the unit of another produced temporal regime
```

This remains entirely within conditioned Loka. The enormous multiplication of duration does not introduce a higher genetic Principle; it specifies the produced temporal structure of saṃsāric destinies.
