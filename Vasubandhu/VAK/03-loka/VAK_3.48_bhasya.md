# VAK_3.48_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
samantatas tu triguṇaṃ tatra merur yugandharaḥ /
īśādhāraḥ khadirakaḥ sudarśanagiris tathā // VAkK_3.48 //
```

**Working translation:**

Its circumferential measure is threefold. Upon the golden earth stand Meru, Yugandhara, Īśādhāra, Khadiraka, and Mount Sudarśana.

## Natural Argument

The opening pāda closes the measurement begun in 3.47. The prose then shifts from the dimensional foundation to the geography established upon the golden earth-maṇḍala.

Vasubandhu treats 3.48 together with the opening of 3.49 in order to enumerate eight great mountains. The decisive spatial clarification is:

```iast
madhye sumeruḥ
```

Sumeru is in the center, while the remaining mountains are positioned around it in enclosing arrangements.

Thus the world-container acquires a center and concentric organization.

## Literal Prose Translation

And upon that golden earth-maṇḍala situated above the waters are Meru, Yugandhara, Īśādhāra, Khadiraka, Mount Sudarśana, Aśvakarṇa, Vinitaka, and Mount Nimindhara. These are the eight great mountains established upon the golden maṇḍala. Sumeru is in the middle; the others are positioned around Sumeru.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — descriptive exposition.
- No separate opponent is introduced.

## New Technical Terms

- **mahīmaṇḍala** — earth-maṇḍala
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** names the golden terrestrial support above the waters

- **pratiṣṭhā** — established / based upon
  - **status:** recurring support vocabulary
  - **function:** states the mountains' dependence upon the golden maṇḍala

- **madhye** — in the middle
  - **status:** ordinary spatial term with structural importance
  - **function:** fixes Sumeru as cosmological center

- **paricāya-avasthita** — positioned around / in surrounding arrangement
  - **status:** Bhāṣya spatial clarification
  - **function:** determines the other mountains relative to Sumeru

## Doctrinal Determination

VAkK 3.48 begins the articulated geography of the produced container-world. The support-field now becomes oriented:

```text
measured extension
    → central point
        → surrounding mountain rings
```

Sumeru is not merely one mountain among others; the Bhāṣya explicitly gives it central status.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The prose confirms that the mountain list continues into 3.49 and that Sumeru alone occupies the center.

## Logical Determination

The bhājanaloka now receives the determination of center. Spatial extension becomes oriented space through a middle and surrounding structures.

## Organon Contact Point

This remains a determination of produced Loka, not a genetic Principle. The useful relation is that a produced Domain can be internally organized through center and circumference without thereby becoming the source of its own genesis.
