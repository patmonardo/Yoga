# VAK_3.56_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
dehā videhāḥ kuravaḥ kauravāścāmarāvarāḥ /
aṣṭau tadantaradvīpā gāṭhā uttaramantriṇaḥ // VAkK_3.56 //
```

**Working translation:**

Deha and Videha, Kuru and Kaurava, Cāmara and Avara, Gāṭha and Uttaramantriṇa are the eight intermediate islands associated with the four great continents.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.56.md`.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya asks which intermediate islands exist and how many there are. The verse names eight, and the prose assigns them in pairs to the four great continents:

```text
Pūrvavideha
    Deha, Videha

Uttarakuru
    Kuru, Kaurava

Aparagodānīya
    Gāṭha, Uttaramantriṇa

Jambūdvīpa
    Cāmara, Avara
```

Vasubandhu then says that all are inhabited by humans. An alternative opinion is recorded according to which one is inhabited by rākṣasas.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — question, enumeration, and principal assignment.
- **Voice: alternative opinion** — `ity apare`, “others say,” that one island is inhabited by rākṣasas.

## New Technical Terms

- **antaradvīpa** — intermediate island
  - **status:** Kārikā term explained by Bhāṣya
  - **function:** secondary inhabited region associated with a great continent

- **parivāra** — attendant / satellite region
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory term
  - **function:** expresses the paired dependence of intermediate islands upon a principal continent

- **āvāsita** — inhabited
  - **function:** confirms human habitation

- **rākṣasa** — rākṣasa
  - **status:** reported alternative
  - **function:** alternate inhabitant of one island according to another view

## Doctrinal Determination

The four-continent world is further particularized by secondary inhabited regions. The produced Loka therefore proceeds from large-scale supports and centers into increasingly local determinations of habitation.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The Bhāṣya supplies the exact pairings of the eight islands with the four great continents and records the alternative rākṣasa tradition.

## Logical Determination

```text
four principal continents
    → paired satellite regions
        → inhabited local differentiations
```

This is a movement into greater particularity within an already-produced world.

## Organon Contact Point

At this depth of Loka we are dealing with highly derivative determinations — continent, island, inhabitant — rather than the genetic Principle itself. The philosophical task is to preserve the chain of mediation without mistaking any derivative location for the living Copula that makes determination intelligible.
