# VAK_3.77_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
ardhārdhavṛddhir ūrdhvaṃ tu parīttābhebhya āśrayaḥ /
dviguṇadviguṇā hitvānabhrakebhya striyojanam // VAkK_3.77 //
```

**Working translation:** Above, the bodily support increases by half-yojana increments; from the Parīttābhas upward it doubles successively, except that for the Anabhrakas three yojanas are subtracted.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.77.md`.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya completes the body-measure sequence begun in VAK 3.76.

After the Brahmakāyikas at half a yojana, the next three stations increase by half a yojana each:

```text
Brahmakāyika       → ½ yojana
Brahmapurohita     → 1 yojana
Mahābrahman        → 1½ yojanas
Parīttābha         → 2 yojanas
```

Beginning above the Parīttābhas, the bodily support doubles at each successive level:

```text
Parīttābha         → 2
Apramāṇābha        → 4
Ābhāsvara          → 8
Parīttaśubha       → 16
Apramāṇaśubha      → 32
Śubhakṛtsna        → 64 yojanas
```

The Anabhrakas form a deliberate exception. Straight doubling from 64 would give 128, but the text subtracts three yojanas:

```text
64 × 2 = 128
128 − 3 = 125 yojanas
```

Thus the Anabhraka body is 125 yojanas. From the next level onward doubling resumes, culminating in an Akaniṣṭha body of 16,000 yojanas.

```text
Anabhraka          → 125
Puṇyaprasava       → 250
Bṛhatphala         → 500
Avṛha              → 1,000
Atapa              → 2,000
Sudṛśa             → 4,000
Sudarśana          → 8,000
Akaniṣṭha          → 16,000 yojanas
```

The source itself explicitly gives only the Anabhraka value and the Akaniṣṭha terminus; the intervening values above are the arithmetic consequences of Vasubandhu's stated resumed doubling rule.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

Straightforward exposition of the received cosmological measure-system; no objection is introduced.

## New Technical Terms

- **ardhārdhavṛddhi** — increase by successive half-yojana increments in the first three levels above the Brahmakāyikas
  - **status:** newly operative in this measure sequence
  - **function:** specifies the first rūpa-deva increase rule

- **dviguṇavṛddhi** — successive doubling
  - **status:** previously encountered as a quantitative pattern
  - **function:** governs the higher rūpa-deva body scale

- **Anabhraka** — name of a higher rūpa-deva class
  - **status:** new in this immediate passage
  - **function:** exceptional measure-point at which three yojanas are subtracted from the expected doubled value

- **Puṇyaprasava** — name of the class immediately above Anabhraka in the resumed doubling sequence
  - **status:** new in this immediate passage
  - **function:** point from which doubling resumes toward Akaniṣṭha

- **Akaniṣṭha** — highest class named in this body-measure sequence
  - **status:** previously known doctrinal term; newly quantified here
  - **function:** terminus of the rūpa-deva body scale at 16,000 yojanas

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse completes a purely conditioned hierarchy of embodiment. Higher rūpa levels do not cease to have `āśraya`; rather, bodily support is quantitatively remeasured according to the level of birth.

The important feature is that the hierarchy is not represented by one uninterrupted mathematical law. Two different rules appear:

```text
initial rūpa levels
    → + ½ yojana increments

from Parīttābha upward
    → successive doubling

Anabhraka
    → exceptional subtraction of 3 yojanas

above Anabhraka
    → doubling resumes
```

Thus the cosmological series is highly ordered without being reducible to a single abstract progression.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Substantive clarification.**

The prior Kārikā translation made `ardhārdhavṛddhi` sound as though the bodily support increases "by halves" from the Parīttābhas upward. The Bhāṣya shows that the half-increment rule governs the three stations culminating in Parīttābha, while **from Parīttābha upward the rule becomes successive doubling**.

The phrase concerning Anabhraka is also precise: the expected doubled measure is reduced by three yojanas, producing 125 yojanas.

## Logical Determination

```text
realm-level
    → bodily support
        → determinate measure
            → additive progression
            → multiplicative progression
            → local exception
            → restored multiplicative progression
```

The Kośa is not deriving these bodies from a transcendental principle. It is specifying the determinate quantitative order of produced Loka.

## Organon Contact Point

Very light contact only: the passage shows **rule-governed differentiation with an explicit exception**. The exception does not abolish the series; it becomes one of the determinations through which the series is concretely specified.
