# VAK_3.63_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
pariṣaṇḍāś catasro 'sya daśasāhasrikāntarāḥ /
ṣoḍaśāṣṭau sahasrāṇi catvāri dve ca nirgatāḥ // VAkK_3.63 //
```

**Working translation:**

Meru has four terraces, each separated vertically by ten thousand yojanas. They project outward respectively sixteen thousand, eight thousand, four thousand, and two thousand yojanas.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.63.md`.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya asks two questions: how many terraces Meru has, and what their dimensions are. It then explains the verse in two directions.

First, the terraces are vertically ordered. Each successive terrace is reached after rising ten thousand yojanas. Four such stages therefore occupy half the height of Sumeru.

Second, each terrace projects outward from Meru by a different amount:

```text
first terrace     16,000 yojanas
second terrace     8,000 yojanas
third terrace      4,000 yojanas
fourth terrace     2,000 yojanas
```

The outward projection is thus successively halved.

## Voice Identification

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — question, explanation of vertical intervals, and explicit assignment of the four projection measures.

## New Technical Terms

- **udgamya** — having risen / ascending
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory vocabulary
  - **function:** makes clear that the ten-thousand measure is vertical spacing between terraces

- **ākṣipta** — occupied / taken up / encompassed
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory vocabulary
  - **function:** states that the four terraces together occupy half of Sumeru's height

- **yathāsaṃkhyam** — respectively, in numerical order
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory vocabulary
  - **function:** fixes the correspondence between the four terraces and the four outward measures

## Doctrinal Determination

The Bhāṣya removes the main ambiguity in the Kārikā. `daśasāhasrikāntarāḥ` is not merely a generic interval: the terraces are located at successive vertical rises of ten thousand yojanas. Their combined vertical range reaches half the height of Sumeru.

At the same time, their horizontal projections diminish by halves:

```text
vertical ascent
    10,000 + 10,000 + 10,000 + 10,000

horizontal projection
    16,000 → 8,000 → 4,000 → 2,000
```

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The existing translation correctly identifies the four terraces and the 16,000 / 8,000 / 4,000 / 2,000 projection sequence. The Bhāṣya clarifies that the ten-thousand intervals are successive vertical ascents and that the four terraces together occupy half the height of Sumeru.

## Logical Determination

The verse now yields two coordinated series:

```text
ascending position
    → equal vertical intervals

outward extension
    → progressively halved projections
```

The same cosmological structure is therefore determined simultaneously by height and breadth.

## Organon Contact Point

This remains a produced spatial determination of Loka. The relevant point is simply that the structure becomes intelligible through reciprocal measures of ascent and extension, not through an isolated list of dimensions.
