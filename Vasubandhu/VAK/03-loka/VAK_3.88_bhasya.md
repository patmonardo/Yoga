# VAK_3.88 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
te 'ṣṭau yojanam ity āhur viṃśaṃ kṣaṇaśataṃ punaḥ /
tatkṣaṇaḥ te punaḥ ṣaṣṭir lavaḥ triṃśad-guṇottarāḥ // VAkK_3.88 //
```

**Working translation:** Eight of those are called a yojana. One hundred and twenty kṣaṇas make one tatkṣaṇa; sixty of those make one lava; the following units increase thirtyfold.

Existing Kārikā analysis: `VAK_3.88.md`.

Source caution: the reference e-text is not a critical edition. The compressed expression `viṃśaṃ kṣaṇaśatam` is controlled by Vasubandhu's prose gloss `kṣaṇānāṃ viṃśaṃ śatam`, which gives 120 kṣaṇas. The final `triṃśadguṇottarāḥ` anticipates the continuation into the next verse.

## Natural Argument

The passage completes spatial measure and immediately turns to temporal measure.

First, Vasubandhu closes the spatial sequence:

```text
8 krośas
    → 1 yojana
```

He then says that the measure of the yojana has been stated and that the measure of the year will now be explained. The temporal sequence begins from the already established minimal unit, the `kṣaṇa`.

The arithmetic is explicit:

```text
120 kṣaṇas
    → 1 tatkṣaṇa

60 tatkṣaṇas
    → 1 lava
```

The final phrase `triṃśadguṇottarāḥ` does not mean that the preceding transformations were thirtyfold. It announces the rule for the next units. The prose immediately confirms this at the beginning of the next verse:

```text
30 lavas
    → 1 muhūrta

30 muhūrtas
    → 1 ahorātra
```

Thus VAK 3.88 is the hinge between the completed spatial measure ladder and the temporal measure ladder.

## Translation

Eight of those are called one yojana. Again, one hundred and twenty moments make one tatkṣaṇa; sixty of those make one lava; the following units increase thirtyfold.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The prose is straightforward explanatory gloss. No objection or competing school position appears in this passage.

## New Technical Terms

- **tatkṣaṇa** — a temporal unit composed of 120 kṣaṇas
  - **status:** introduced technically in this passage
  - **function:** intermediate temporal measure

- **lava** — a temporal unit composed of 60 tatkṣaṇas
  - **status:** introduced technically in this passage
  - **function:** intermediate temporal measure

- **viṃśaṃ śatam** — one hundred and twenty
  - **status:** prose clarification of compressed Kārikā wording
  - **function:** resolves numerical ambiguity

- **triṃśadguṇottara** — proceeding by thirtyfold increase
  - **status:** present in the Kārikā, clarified by the immediately following prose
  - **function:** rule governing the next larger time-units

## Doctrinal Determination

The Kośa applies the same analytical discipline to time that it has just applied to material and spatial magnitude.

```text
rūpa-measure
    paramāṇu → ... → krośa → yojana

time-measure
    kṣaṇa → tatkṣaṇa → lava → muhūrta → ahorātra
```

The relevant point is not that time is derived metaphysically from atoms. Rather, both extension and duration are rendered determinate through ordered conventional measure-series.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Revision required.**

The earlier Kārikā file left `viṃśaṃ kṣaṇaśatam` provisional. The Bhāṣya fixes it as **120 kṣaṇas**, not an unspecified relation involving a hundred kṣaṇas.

The earlier interpretation of `triṃśadguṇottarāḥ` also needs precision: it governs the following larger units, beginning with 30 lavas to a muhūrta and 30 muhūrtas to an ahorātra. It does not govern the kṣaṇa → tatkṣaṇa or tatkṣaṇa → lava transitions.

## Logical Determination

The verse presents a measured transition from minimum duration toward ordinary calendrical time:

```text
minimal temporal determination
    → aggregation
        → intermediate temporal units
            → day / month / year
```

The immediate structure is therefore:

```text
120 × kṣaṇa = tatkṣaṇa
60 × tatkṣaṇa = lava
30 × lava = muhūrta
```

The last relation belongs to the continuation of the natural argument in VAK 3.89, but it is required to understand the force of `triṃśadguṇottarāḥ` here.

## Organon Contact Point

The useful contact point is simply that a world-field becomes calculable only when its magnitudes are articulated through determinate units and conversion rules. This remains produced Loka: a measured structure of conditioned appearance, not a genetic transcendental Principle.
