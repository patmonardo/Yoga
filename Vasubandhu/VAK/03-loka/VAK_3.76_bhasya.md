# VAK_3.76_bhasya — Lokanirdeśa

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
pādabṛddhayā tanuryāva tsārdhakrośo divaukasām /
kāmināṃ rūpiṇāṃ tvādau yojanārdhaṃ tataḥ param // VAkK_3.76 //
```

**Working translation:** For the desire-realm heavenly dwellers, bodily measure increases by a quarter-krośa at each successive level, up to one and a half krośas. For the form-realm gods, the first bodily measure is half a yojana; beyond that it increases further.

## Natural Argument

The Bhāṣya first defines the unit underlying `pādavṛddhi`: one `pāda` is one quarter of a krośa. It then distributes bodily measure across the six kāma-deva classes in an exact ascending series:

```text
Cāturmahārājakāyika      → 1 pāda   = 1/4 krośa
Trāyastriṃśa              → 2 pādas  = 1/2 krośa
Yāma                      → 3 pādas  = 3/4 krośa
Tuṣita                    → 4 pādas  = 1 krośa
Nirmāṇarati               → 5 pādas  = 1 1/4 krośas
Paranirmitavaśavartin     → 1 1/2 krośas
```

The argument then crosses from kāmadhātu into rūpadhātu. Among the form-realm gods, the first station is that of the Brahmakāyikas, whose bodies measure half a yojana. `tataḥ param` signals the continuation of the increasing series, whose detailed values are supplied in the following verse.

## Literal Prose Translation

“A `pāda` is the fourth part of a krośa. The body of the Cāturmahārājakāyikas is of that measure; that of the Trāyastriṃśas is two pādas; of the Yāmas three; of the Tuṣitas four; of the Nirmāṇaratis five; and that of the Paranirmitavaśavartins is one and a half krośas.

As for the form-realm gods, in their first station, that of the Brahmakāyikas, the body is of the measure of half a yojana. Beyond that [the measure increases further].”

## Argument Reconstruction

```text
measure-unit defined
    → pāda = 1/4 krośa

successive kāma-deva levels
    → successive bodily enlargement
    → maximum = 1 1/2 krośas

transition to rūpadhātu
    → new base unit
    → Brahmakāyika = 1/2 yojana
    → further increase continues in VAK 3.77
```

The Bhāṣya therefore removes any ambiguity in the Kārikā's compressed phrase `pādavṛddhyā`: it is not a vague proportional increase but an exact quarter-krośa progression across the six desire-realm deva classes.

## Voice Identification

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

Straightforward exegetical specification of measures; no objection or competing doctrinal voice appears in this unit.

## New Technical Terms

- **pādaḥ krośasya caturtho bhāgaḥ** — a quarter, explicitly defined as one fourth of a krośa
  - **status:** the prose makes the measure technically explicit
  - **function:** definition

- **tanmātraṃ śarīram** — a body of exactly that measure
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory phrase
  - **function:** specification

- **prathamaṃ sthānam** — the first station or level
  - **status:** recurrent cosmological vocabulary, made operative here for rūpadhātu scaling
  - **function:** hierarchical location

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse establishes that bodily magnitude is realm- and level-dependent. Within kāmadhātu, the six divine classes form a precise graded series. At the transition to rūpadhātu, the scale changes abruptly: the lowest form-realm gods already begin at half a yojana.

This remains a determination of produced Loka. Greater bodily magnitude does not imply a higher genetic or transcendental principle; it specifies the conditioned form proper to a particular world-level.

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Material clarification.**

The Kārikā translation should not leave `pādavṛddhyā` merely as “by quarter-increase.” The Bhāṣya establishes that the quarter is specifically **one quarter of a krośa**, added successively across the six kāma-deva classes.

Likewise, `rūpiṇāṃ tv ādau` is explicitly anchored to the **Brahmakāyikas**, whose bodies measure half a yojana.
