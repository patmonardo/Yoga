# VAK_2.30_bhasya — Neutral Citta Profiles and the Compatibility of Middha

## Kārikā

```text
nivṛte 'ṣṭādaśa anyatra dvādaśāvyākṛte matāḥ /
middhaṃ sarvāvirodhitvād yatra syād adhikaṃ hi tat // VAkK_2.30 //
```

## Continuous Translation of the Bhāṣya

A citta associated with the view of a real self or with an extreme view is obscured-indeterminate in the desire realm. In such a citta there are eighteen mental factors: the ten great-ground factors, the six afflicted great-ground factors, and initial and sustained thought. As before, the view is not additional.

What is indeterminate other than the obscured is unobscured-indeterminate. In it twelve mental factors are accepted: the ten great-ground factors together with initial and sustained thought.

The teachers of the outer regions also accept remorse as indeterminate. According to them, a citta associated with it has thirteen factors.

Sleepiness is incompatible with none of the mental factors described above, because it may be wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate. Therefore, wherever it occurs, it should be understood as an additional factor. Where there are twenty-two, there are twenty-three; where there are twenty-three, there are twenty-four; and so forth.

## Voice and Argument

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The main classification gives two neutral citta profiles:

```text
obscured-indeterminate citta
    = 18 factors

unobscured-indeterminate citta
    = 12 factors
```

**Voice:** Teachers of the outer regions (*bahirdeśakāḥ*)

```text
unobscured-indeterminate citta
+ neutral remorse
    = 13 factors
```

Vasubandhu reports this position without adopting it as the principal count.

## Reconstruction of the Counts

### Obscured-indeterminate citta: eighteen

```text
10 universal great-ground factors
6 afflicted great-ground factors
vitarka
vicāra
= 18
```

This applies in the desire realm to citta associated with:

```text
satkāyadṛṣṭi
antagrāhadṛṣṭi
```

Although a view is present, it does not add another factor because it is a determinate form of the already-counted factor prajñā.

### Unobscured-indeterminate citta: twelve

```text
10 universal great-ground factors
vitarka
vicāra
= 12
```

The absence of the six afflicted great-ground factors accounts for the difference between eighteen and twelve.

### External-school count: thirteen

```text
12 unobscured-indeterminate factors
+ neutral kaukṛtya
= 13
```

This is explicitly attributed to teachers outside the principal Vaibhāṣika region.

## Middha as an Additional Factor

The decisive expression is:

```text
sarvāvirodhitvāt
    because it is incompatible with none
```

*Middha* can be:

```text
wholesome
unwholesome
indeterminate
```

It therefore does not define one fixed ethical class. Instead, it may accompany any suitable profile and raises its count by one.

```text
22 -> 23
23 -> 24
18 -> 19
12 -> 13
```

The Bhāṣya formulates a general rule rather than merely another isolated count:

```text
base caitta profile
+ compatible middha
= base count + 1
```

## Analytical Determination

VAK 2.30 distinguishes:

```text
ethical determination
from
compatibility of association
```

A factor may itself occur under all three ethical determinations and therefore cannot be assigned exclusively to one of them.

The passage also preserves the earlier counting principle:

```text
special form of an already-counted factor
    does not increase the count

genuinely distinct associated factor
    increases the count
```

Thus view does not increase the obscured-neutral count, while remorse or sleepiness does.

## New Technical Terms

- **nivṛtāvyākṛta** — obscured-indeterminate; neutral in karmic determination but obscured by affliction
  - **status:** newly central in this passage
  - **function:** classifies citta associated with self-view or extreme view in the desire realm

- **anivṛtāvyākṛta** — unobscured-indeterminate
  - **status:** newly central in this passage
  - **function:** defines the twelve-factor neutral profile lacking the afflicted great-ground set

- **bahirdeśaka** — teacher of the outer regions
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only school designation in this unit
  - **function:** marks an alternative doctrinal count accepting neutral remorse

- **sarvāvirodhitva** — compatibility with all; non-opposition to every ethical class
  - **status:** explicit logical criterion introduced here
  - **function:** explains why middha is added wherever present rather than assigned to one fixed profile

- **middha** — sleepiness or torpor
  - **status:** previously named, now structurally determined
  - **function:** optional associated factor capable of being wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate

## Compact Result

```text
obscured-neutral citta      = 18
unobscured-neutral citta    = 12
external-school variant     = 13 with neutral remorse
any compatible profile      = +1 when middha is present
```

VAK 2.30 completes the desire-realm count profiles by adding a general compatibility rule: *middha* is not tied to one ethical class but is counted wherever it actually occurs.
