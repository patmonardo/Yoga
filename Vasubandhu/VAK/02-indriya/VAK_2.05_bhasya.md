# VAK_2.05 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā Anchor

**IAST**

```text
cittāśrayas tadvikalpaḥ sthitiḥ saṃkleśa eva ca /
```

## Bhāṣya Source Passage

```text
ādhipatyād indriyatve 'vidyādīnām upasaṃkhyānaṃ kartavyam /
avidyādīnām api hi saṃskārādiṣv ādhipatyam ata eṣām apīndriyatvam upasaṃkhyātavyam /

vāgādīnāṃ ca /
vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthānām api cendriyatvam upasaṃkhyātavyam /
vacanādānaviharaṇotsargānandeṣv ādhipatyāt /

na khalūpasaṃkhyātavyam /
yasmād iheṣṭam —

cittāśrayas tadvikalpaḥ sthitiḥ saṃkleśa eva ca /
saṃbhāro vyavadānaṃ ca yāvatā tāvad indriyam //

tatra cittāśrayaḥ ṣaḍindriyāṇi /
etac ca ṣaḍāyatanaṃ maulaṃ sattvadravyam /
tasya strīpuruṣavikalpaḥ strīpuruṣendriyābhyām /
sthitir jīvitendriyeṇa /
saṃkleśo vedanābhiḥ /
vyavadānasaṃbharaṇaṃ pañcabhiḥ /
vyavadānaṃ tribhiḥ /

ato nāvidyādīnām indriyatvam iṣṭam /
```

---

## Continuous Translation

> If being a faculty follows from governing efficacy, then ignorance and the other factors must also be added to the enumeration. For ignorance and the others likewise possess governing efficacy with respect to the formations and the remaining members of dependent arising. Therefore, they too would have to be included as faculties.
>
> Speech and the other organs would also have to be included. Speech, the hands, the feet, the anus, and the sexual organ would likewise have to be counted as faculties, because they exercise governing efficacy respectively over speaking, taking hold, moving about, excretion, and pleasure.
>
> They are not, however, to be added to the enumeration. For what is intended here is the following:
>
> “Support of mind, its differentiation, persistence, defilement, the equipment for purification, and purification itself: the faculties extend exactly so far as these functions extend.”
>
> Among these, the six faculties are the support of mind. This sixfold sense-base is the fundamental constitution of a living being. Its differentiation into female and male is effected by the female and male faculties. Its persistence is maintained by the life faculty. Its defilement occurs through the feelings. The equipment for purification is provided by the five faculties beginning with faith, and purification itself by the three realization faculties.
>
> Therefore ignorance and the other factors are not accepted as faculties.

## Voices

**Voice: Objector**

> If governing efficacy alone defines a faculty, ignorance and the remaining causal factors should also be faculties.

**Voice: Objector**

> Speech, hands, feet, anus, and sexual organ should also be faculties because they govern characteristic activities.

**Voice: Vasubandhu / Vaibhāṣika response**

> The list is not coextensive with every causal efficacy. It is restricted to the complete functional architecture of the living continuum and its purification.

---

## Argument Reconstruction

The objection exposes a danger in the definition established by VAK 2.1–2.4:

```text
indriya
    = that which possesses ādhipatya
```

Many things possess governing efficacy. Ignorance governs formations in dependent arising. Speech governs speaking. Hands govern grasping. Feet govern locomotion. If every strong causal relation were sufficient, the class of faculties would expand indefinitely.

Vasubandhu therefore adds a restriction. The question is not simply:

```text
Does this dharma cause something?
```

Nor even:

```text
Does it exercise predominant causal power in some activity?
```

The question is:

```text
Does it occupy one of the indispensable governing positions
in the complete constitution, continuation, defilement,
and purification of a living continuum?
```

The twenty-two faculties are justified because together they exhaust six required functions:

```text
1. citta-āśraya
   support of mind

2. tad-vikalpa
   differentiation of that living support

3. sthiti
   persistence

4. saṃkleśa
   defilement

5. vyavadāna-saṃbharaṇa
   preparation or equipment for purification

6. vyavadāna
   purification itself
```

Thus VAK 2.5 is not merely another classification. It supplies the principle limiting the classification.

---

## The Six Functions

### 1. Support of Mind

```text
cittāśrayaḥ ṣaḍindriyāṇi
```

> The six faculties are the support of mind.

These are the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind faculties. The Bhāṣya then calls the corresponding sixfold sense-base:

```text
maulaṃ sattvadravyam
```

> the fundamental constitution of a living being.

This phrase is stronger than the generic statement that the senses are instruments of cognition. The sixfold base is the primary organized basis upon which the living cognitive continuum operates.

The word *dravya* should not be overread here as an eternal substance. It marks the concrete constituent basis of the conventionally designated living being.

### 2. Its Differentiation

```text
tasya strīpuruṣavikalpaḥ strīpuruṣendriyābhyām
```

> Its differentiation into female and male is effected by the female and male faculties.

The pronoun *tasya* refers to the fundamental living constitution just mentioned. *Vikalpa* again means differentiation or articulated distinction, not “concept.”

The female and male faculties do not create a second substance. They differentiate the already constituted living continuum.

### 3. Persistence

```text
sthitir jīvitendriyeṇa
```

> Its persistence is maintained by the life faculty.

The life faculty governs the continued standing of the series. It does not produce the series from nothing, nor does it serve as a permanent self underlying the aggregates. It maintains the conditioned continuum in its present mode of existence.

### 4. Defilement

```text
saṃkleśo vedanābhiḥ
```

> Its defilement occurs through the feelings.

This restates the result of VAK 2.3. Feeling is the affective condition in dependence upon which attachment, aversion, and ignorance can remain latent and become active.

The Bhāṣya does not identify feeling with defilement. It assigns feeling governing efficacy in the genesis of defilement.

### 5. Equipment for Purification

```text
vyavadānasaṃbharaṇaṃ pañcabhiḥ
```

> The equipment for purification is provided by the five.

The five are faith, energy, recollection, samādhi, and prajñā.

This wording sharpens their status. They are not yet purification in its completed realization. They gather, prepare, and equip the continuum for purification.

```text
śraddhā
vīrya
smṛti
samādhi
prajñā
    = vyavadāna-saṃbhāra
```

They are the practical resources through which purification becomes possible.

### 6. Purification

```text
vyavadānaṃ tribhiḥ
```

> Purification itself is effected by the three.

These are the three realization faculties:

```text
anājñātam-ājñāsyāmīndriya
ājñendriya
ājñātāvīndriya
```

They govern seeing, cultivation, completed liberation, and the successive attainments described under VAK 2.4.

The distinction is therefore exact:

```text
five faculties
    prepare and equip purification

three realization faculties
    actualize purification
```

---

## Why Ignorance Is Not a Faculty

Ignorance undeniably has governing efficacy over formations. But this does not make it a faculty because the class *indriya* is not intended to include every factor with causal dominance.

Ignorance belongs within the process of defilement. It does not occupy a distinct structural position beyond the function already assigned to the feeling faculties under *saṃkleśa*.

The response is therefore architectural rather than merely verbal:

```text
avidyā has causal efficacy
but does not add a new indispensable faculty-function
```

The same reasoning prepares the later rejection of speech and the motor organs. Their activities are real, but they do not require the positing of new faculties beyond the existing bodily and cognitive organization.

---

## Translation Consequences

The Kārikā’s *saṃbhāra* should be translated in direct relation to purification:

```text
vyavadāna-saṃbharaṇa
    = equipping or preparing purification
```

“Accumulation” is possible, but “equipment for purification” better preserves the practical function assigned by the Bhāṣya.

The phrase:

```text
yāvatā tāvad indriyam
```

should not be understood merely as “there are only this many faculties.” It says:

> The extension of the class of faculties is exactly coextensive with these required governing functions.

That is the scientific closure of the first enumeration.

---

## Provisional Logical Determination

VAK 2.2 distinguished faculty from ordinary cause by predominant governing power.

VAK 2.5 now adds a second criterion:

```text
indriya
    = predominant governing efficacy
      occupying an indispensable structural function
      in the living and liberative continuum
```

Therefore:

```text
not every cause is a faculty
not every dominant cause is a faculty
only a governing power required by the complete architecture
of support, differentiation, persistence, defilement,
preparation, and purification is counted as a faculty
```

This is a classificatory science governed by functional completeness.

---

## Practice Contact Point

The practical sequence is now explicit:

```text
living support
    becomes differentiated
        persists
            becomes defiled
                gathers the means of purification
                    is purified
```

The five path faculties are therefore not merely virtues added to an otherwise complete subject. They are the equipment by which the defiled continuum becomes capable of transformation.

This is where the Kośa can become a principle of practice without ceasing to be empirical and analytical. It identifies not an abstract moral command, but the determinate powers that must become operative in the continuum:

```text
confidence
energy
recollection
samādhi
prajñā
```

The realization faculties then carry that prepared continuum through seeing, cultivation, and completed liberation.

## New Technical Terms

- **upasaṃkhyāna** — supplementary inclusion in an enumeration
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit
  - **function:** objection demanding that additional causal powers be counted as faculties

- **cittāśraya** — support of mind
  - **status:** present in the Kārikā and explained in the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** role of the six faculties

- **ṣaḍāyatana** — sixfold sense-base
  - **status:** previously encountered doctrinal term
  - **function:** concrete basis identified with the six faculties

- **maula-sattva-dravya** — fundamental constitution of a living being
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit
  - **function:** characterizes the sixfold base as the primary constituent basis of the living continuum

- **tad-vikalpa** — its differentiation
  - **status:** Kārikā term clarified by the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** differentiation of the living constitution into female and male

- **vyavadāna-saṃbharaṇa** — equipping or preparing purification
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanation of *saṃbhāra*
  - **function:** work of the five faculties beginning with faith

- **yāvatā tāvat** — as far as this extends, precisely so far
  - **status:** Kārikā expression
  - **function:** establishes exact functional coextension of the twenty-two faculties
