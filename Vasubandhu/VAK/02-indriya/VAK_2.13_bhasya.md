# VAK_2.13_bhasya — Faculties and Abandonment

## Kārikā Anchor

```text
manovittitrayaṃ tredhā dviheyā durmanaskatā /
nava bhāvanayā pañca tv aheyāny api na trayam // VAkK_2.13 //
```

### Working Translation

```text
Mind and three feelings are threefold.
Distress is abandoned in two ways.
Nine are abandoned by cultivation.
The five may also be not abandoned;
the final triad is not abandoned at all.
```

## Continuous Bhāṣya Translation

> How many faculties are to be abandoned by seeing, how many are to be abandoned by cultivation, and how many are not to be abandoned?
>
> Mind and the three feelings are threefold.
>
> Which three feelings? Pleasure, gladness, and neutral feeling. Each of these may be abandoned by seeing, abandoned by cultivation, or not abandoned.
>
> Distress is abandoned in two ways. The faculty of distress is to be abandoned either by seeing or by cultivation.
>
> Nine are abandoned by cultivation. The expression “are to be abandoned” is understood here. The seven faculties beginning with the eye, with the life faculty as the eighth, together with the pain faculty, are to be abandoned only by cultivation.
>
> The five, however, may also be not abandoned. The five faculties beginning with faith may be abandoned by cultivation, but they may also be not abandoned, because they can be either with outflows or without outflows.
>
> The final triad is not to be abandoned. The three beginning with the faculty called “I shall know” are never to be abandoned, because they are without outflows. What is faultless is not fit to be abandoned.
>
> The distinctions of type have now been explained.

## Argument Reconstruction

The Bhāṣya distinguishes three path-relations:

```text
darśana-prahātavya
    to be abandoned by seeing

bhāvanā-prahātavya
    to be abandoned by cultivation

aprahātavya / aheya
    not to be abandoned
```

### 1. Mind and three feelings are threefold

The relevant feelings are:

```text
sukha
saumanasya
upekṣā
```

These faculties can occur in contaminated forms connected with errors removed by seeing, in forms connected with residual afflictions removed through cultivation, or in uncontaminated forms that are not abandoned.

### 2. Distress is twofoldly abandoned

```text
daurmanasya
    → abandoned by seeing
    or
    → abandoned by cultivation
```

No uncontaminated instance of distress remains as something not to be abandoned.

### 3. Nine are abandoned only through cultivation

```text
five sensory faculties
female faculty
male faculty
life faculty
pain faculty
```

These are not removed by the initial seeing of truth. Their conditioned instances cease through the progressive transformation and exhaustion of the continuum.

### 4. The five path faculties are mixed

```text
śraddhā
vīrya
smṛti
samādhi
prajñā
```

Because they may be contaminated or uncontaminated, they may either be abandoned by cultivation or remain as not to be abandoned.

### 5. The realization triad is never abandoned

```text
ājñāsyāmīndriya
ājñendriya
ājñātāvīndriya
```

These are exclusively uncontaminated. Vasubandhu gives the governing maxim:

```text
na hi nirdoṣaṃ prahāṇārham
what is faultless is not fit to be abandoned
```

## Voice

- **Voice:** Vasubandhu — frames the classificatory question and explains each count.
- **Voice:** Abhidharma classificatory tradition — represented in the technical threefold abandonment scheme.
- **Voice:** no explicit objector in this unit.

## New Technical Terms

- **darśana-prahātavya** — to be abandoned by seeing.
  - **status:** newly explicit in this Bhāṣya unit.
  - **function:** classifies faults removed through direct seeing of truth.

- **bhāvanā-prahātavya** — to be abandoned by cultivation.
  - **status:** newly explicit in this Bhāṣya unit.
  - **function:** classifies conditioned or afflicted factors removed through repeated development.

- **aprahātavya / aheya** — not to be abandoned.
  - **status:** newly explicit as a technical path category.
  - **function:** marks uncontaminated or otherwise faultless factors retained rather than rejected.

- **nirdoṣa** — faultless.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only explanatory term here.
  - **function:** supplies the reason that uncontaminated faculties are not objects of abandonment.

- **prakārabheda** — distinction of types or modes.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya transition term.
  - **function:** closes the extended classification of the twenty-two faculties.

## Translation Consequence

The path does not abandon “the faculty” simply as a fixed entity. It abandons determinate contaminated instances or modes of a faculty. The same faculty-name can therefore span:

```text
what seeing removes
what cultivation removes
what the path preserves
```

The classificatory apparatus is indeed elaborate, but its purpose is practical discrimination: one must know what is eliminated by insight, what requires sustained cultivation, and what must not be discarded.

## Restrained Organon Contact

In the planning-system reading, the faculty is a higher functional category whose instances can bear different path-statuses. The Idea governing the system remains stable while particular implementations are rejected, transformed, or retained.

```text
faculty-Idea
    → contaminated instance: abandon
    → cultivational instance: transform and exhaust
    → uncontaminated instance: preserve
```

This is not merely classification for its own sake. It is a typed transformation policy for the practical system.

## Unit Closure

With `uktaḥ prakārabhedaḥ`—“the distinctions of type have been explained”—Vasubandhu closes the long classificatory block beginning with purity, maturation, ethical quality, realm, and abandonment. The next unit turns from classification to acquisition: which faculties are first obtained in each realm and mode of birth.
