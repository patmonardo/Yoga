# VAK_2.19_bhasya — Necessary Configurations of the Path Faculties

## Kārikā

```text
aṣṭābhir ekādaśabhis tv ājñājñātendriyānvitaḥ /
ājñāsyāmīndriyopetas trayodaśabhir anvitaḥ // VAkK_2.19 //
```

## Continuous Bhāṣya Translation

> One who possesses any one of the faculties beginning with the female faculty is, in each case, endowed with eight faculties.
>
> In the case of the female faculty, these are the seven already mentioned together with the female faculty as the eighth. One who possesses the male faculty is endowed with those same seven together with the male faculty as the eighth. The same applies to the distress faculty.
>
> One who possesses any one of the faculties beginning with faith is endowed with those five faculties together with neutral feeling, life, and mind.
>
> One who possesses either the faculty of knowledge or the faculty of one who has known is endowed with eleven faculties.
>
> The expression “faculty of one who has known” means the faculty belonging to one who has known. Whoever possesses the faculty of knowledge is necessarily endowed with eleven: pleasure, gladness, neutral feeling, life, mind, the five faculties beginning with faith, and the faculty of knowledge itself.
>
> The same applies to the faculty of one who has known: the same ten faculties are present together with the faculty of one who has known.
>
> One who possesses the faculty called “I shall know” is endowed with thirteen faculties.
>
> Which thirteen? Mind, life, and body; four feelings; the five faculties beginning with faith; and the faculty called “I shall know.”

## Voice

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

The passage is an expository calculation of necessary co-possession. No explicit objection is introduced.

## Reconstruction of the Counts

### Eight-faculty configurations

The opening **aṣṭābhiḥ**, “with eight,” completes the construction begun at the end of VAK 2.18.

```text
female faculty:
    neutral feeling
    life
    mind
    body
    five sensory conditions already required in the prior count,
    with the female faculty completing the eight
```

More securely stated from the Bhāṣya’s own compressed reference:

```text
seven previously required faculties
    + female
    = eight

seven previously required faculties
    + male
    = eight

seven previously required faculties
    + distress
    = eight
```

For any one of the five faculties beginning with faith:

```text
faith
energy
recollection
concentration
prajñā
neutral feeling
life
mind
    = eight
```

Because the five path faculties occur together, possession of any one entails possession of all five.

### Eleven-faculty configurations

For the faculty of knowledge:

```text
pleasure
gladness
neutral feeling
life
mind
faith
energy
recollection
concentration
prajñā
faculty of knowledge
    = eleven
```

For the faculty of one who has known, the same ten supporting faculties occur, with the completed-realization faculty as the eleventh.

### Thirteen-faculty configuration

For the faculty called “I shall know”:

```text
mind
life
body
four feelings
faith
energy
recollection
concentration
prajñā
“I shall know” faculty
    = thirteen
```

The four feelings here are the four compatible with the path of seeing; distress is excluded.

## Doctrinal Determination

The three realization faculties do not merely differ by temporal labels. Their necessary supporting configurations differ.

```text
ājñāsyāmīndriya
    prospective realization
    path of seeing
    thirteen-faculty configuration

ājñendriya
    operative knowledge
    path of cultivation
    eleven-faculty configuration

ājñātāvīndriya
    completed knowledge
    path of no-more-training
    eleven-faculty configuration
```

The first realization faculty requires the body faculty and a broader feeling-range because the path of seeing can arise in a desire-realm embodiment. The later two are counted through a more refined path configuration.

## Functional Interpretation

This passage confirms that an *indriya* is not treated as an isolated item. Each faculty belongs to a dependency structure.

```text
possession of one faculty
    entails
possession of a determinate supporting network
```

The five faculties beginning with faith are especially clear:

```text
one is never possessed alone
one implies the coordinated five
```

They function as one practical path-system articulated into five distinguishable powers.

The realization faculties then stand as higher organizing determinations of that system:

```text
five path faculties
    + mind, life, feeling, and embodiment conditions
    -> determinate realization-stage
```

This fits the interpretation of the realization faculty as one path-Idea organizing many subordinate capacities, while the Bhāṣya itself remains committed to exact co-possession counts.

## New Technical Terms

- **ājñāta-indriya** — explanatory form for the faculty belonging to one who has known
  - **status:** Bhāṣya clarification of *ājñātāvīndriya*
  - **function:** grammatical and doctrinal explanation of completed realization

- **samanvāgata** — endowed with; possessing in one’s continuum
  - **status:** previously encountered
  - **function:** marks lawful possession, not necessarily current manifestation

- **pratyekam** — in each individual case
  - **status:** ordinary term used technically here
  - **function:** prevents combining female, male, and distress into one simultaneous count

## Translation Consequence

The opening **aṣṭābhiḥ** must be read with the final phrase of VAK 2.18. It should not be attached only to the realization faculties of VAK 2.19.

The syntactic sequence is:

```text
strīndriyādimān aṣṭābhiḥ
    one possessing the female faculty and the following faculties is endowed with eight

ekādaśabhis tv ājñājñātendriyānvitaḥ
    but one possessing the knowledge faculty or completed-knowledge faculty is endowed with eleven
```

## Compact Formula

```text
VAK 2.19 completes the minimum co-possession table:
female, male, distress, and the five path faculties entail eight;
the knowledge and completed-knowledge faculties entail eleven;
and the “I shall know” faculty entails thirteen.
```
