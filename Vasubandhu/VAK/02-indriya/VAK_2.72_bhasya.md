# VAK_2.72_bhasya — Functional Subdivision of Indeterminate Citta

## Kārikā

```text
vipākajairyāpathikaśailpasthānikanairmitam /
caturdhā 'vyākṛtaṃ kāme rūpe śilpavivarjitam // VAkK_2.72 //
```

## Working Translation

In the Desire Realm, unobscured-indeterminate citta is fourfold: maturation-born, deportment-related, craft-based, and constructed. In the Form Realm it is threefold, the craft-based class being absent.

## Bhāṣya — Continuous Translation

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

In the Form Realm, unobscured-indeterminate citta is divided threefold, because the craft-based class is excluded: there is no craft there. In this way the twelve cittas previously enumerated become twenty. Wholesome citta is divided sixfold, and unobscured-indeterminate citta sevenfold.

Deportment-related and the other such cittas do not occur in the Formless Realm, because the corresponding deportments and activities are absent there. Visible form, smell, taste, and tangible objects are the objects of the deportment-related cittas; sound is additionally an object of craft-based citta. These are all mental consciousnesses. The five sensory consciousness-groups, however, may function as preparatory factors in deportment-related and craft-based activity. Some also maintain that a deportment-produced mental consciousness exists whose object-domain may extend over the twelve sense-fields.

Now, among these twenty cittas, which may occur immediately after which?

For the eight Desire-Realm cittas, after a practice-produced wholesome citta ten cittas may arise: seven belonging to its own realm, excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result citta, together with the Form-Realm practice-produced wholesome citta and the trainee and beyond-training cittas. That practice-produced Desire-Realm citta itself may arise after eight cittas: the wholesome and afflicted cittas of its own realm, the practice-produced and afflicted cittas of the Form Realm, and the trainee and beyond-training cittas.

After a wholesome citta acquired through rebirth, nine may arise: seven in its own realm, excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result, and the afflicted cittas of the Form and Formless Realms. Such a rebirth-acquired wholesome citta may itself arise after eleven cittas: the seven cittas of its own realm, as above, the Form-Realm practice-produced and afflicted cittas, and the trainee and beyond-training cittas.

After an unwholesome or obscured-indeterminate Desire-Realm citta, seven cittas may arise, namely the seven of its own realm just specified. Those cittas may themselves arise after fourteen cittas: the seven of their own realm; four Form-Realm cittas, excluding the practice-produced and supernormal-knowledge-result types; and three Formless-Realm cittas, excluding the practice-produced type.

After deportment-related or maturation-born citta, eight cittas may arise: six in their own realm, excluding the practice-produced and supernormal-knowledge-result cittas, together with the afflicted cittas of the Form and Formless Realms. These two types themselves arise after seven cittas, namely those of their own realm as already explained.

After craft-based citta, six cittas may arise, namely the same own-realm cittas excluding the practice-produced and supernormal-knowledge-result types. Craft-based citta itself arises after seven own-realm cittas, excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result.

After a supernormal-knowledge-result citta, two cittas may arise: another supernormal-knowledge-result citta of the same type and a Form-Realm practice-produced citta. It too arises only after these two.

We now turn to the six Form-Realm cittas. After a practice-produced wholesome citta, twelve cittas may arise: Desire-Realm wholesome citta and supernormal-knowledge-result citta; the six Form-Realm cittas; Formless-Realm practice-produced wholesome citta; and the trainee and beyond-training cittas. Such a Form-Realm practice-produced citta itself may arise after ten cittas: the Desire-Realm practice-produced and supernormal-knowledge-result cittas; four of its own realm, excluding deportment-related and maturation-born cittas; the Formless-Realm practice-produced and afflicted cittas; and the trainee and beyond-training cittas.

After a Form-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome citta, eight cittas may arise: afflicted Desire-Realm citta, five of its own realm excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result, and afflicted Formless-Realm citta. It may itself arise after five cittas of its own realm, excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result.

After afflicted Form-Realm citta, nine cittas may arise: the four Desire-Realm wholesome and afflicted cittas, and five of its own realm excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result. Such afflicted Form-Realm citta itself may arise after eleven cittas: Desire-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome, deportment-related, and maturation-born cittas; five of its own realm excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result; and three Formless-Realm cittas excluding the practice-produced.

After Form-Realm deportment-related citta, seven may arise: afflicted Desire-Realm citta, four of its own realm excluding practice-produced and supernormal-knowledge-result, and afflicted Formless-Realm citta. It itself may arise after five of its own realm, excluding the supernormal-knowledge-result. The same pattern applies to maturation-born citta. After supernormal-knowledge-result citta, two may arise: the Form-Realm practice-produced and supernormal-knowledge-result cittas; and it likewise arises only after these two.

We now turn to the four Formless-Realm cittas. After a practice-produced wholesome citta, seven may arise: Form-Realm practice-produced wholesome citta, the four Formless-Realm cittas, and the trainee and beyond-training cittas. It itself may arise after six cittas: Form-Realm practice-produced wholesome citta; three of its own realm excluding maturation-born; and the trainee and beyond-training cittas.

After a Formless-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome citta, seven may arise: its four own-realm cittas and the afflicted cittas of the lower realms. It itself arises only after the four cittas of its own realm. After afflicted Formless-Realm citta, eight may arise: the four of its own realm, Form-Realm practice-produced and afflicted cittas, and afflicted Desire-Realm citta. It itself may arise after ten cittas: the four of its own realm plus the Desire- and Form-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome, deportment-related, and maturation-born cittas. After maturation-born Formless-Realm citta, six may arise: three of its own realm excluding the practice-produced, and the three afflicted cittas of the lower realms. It itself arises only after the four cittas of its own realm.

After trainee citta, six may arise: the practice-produced wholesome citta of each of the three realms, Desire-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome citta, trainee citta, and beyond-training citta. Trainee citta itself may arise after four cittas: the three practice-produced wholesome cittas and trainee citta. After beyond-training citta, five may arise, the same as after trainee citta except that trainee citta is omitted. Beyond-training citta itself may arise after five: the three practice-produced wholesome cittas, trainee citta, and beyond-training citta.

Why can maturation-born, deportment-related, and craft-based cittas arise after a practice-produced citta, while practice-produced citta does not arise immediately after those? Because the former operate through deportment, craft, or weak and relatively unconstructed activity; they are not naturally favorable to the stronger directed operation of a practice-produced citta. A mind of emergence, however, is weakly constructed, so it can appropriately arise after a practice-produced citta.

One might object that, by the same reasoning, practice-produced citta should not arise after afflicted citta either. But afflicted citta is contrary in quality rather than simply weak; when one has become wearied by the active occurrence of affliction and seeks to know it fully, practice-produced citta can become manifest.

Desire-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome citta, being comparatively sharp, can arise after trainee and beyond-training cittas and after Form-Realm practice-produced citta. The reverse transitions do not occur because those weaker cittas do not carry the requisite constructive force. Form-Realm afflicted citta may be followed by Desire-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome citta because the latter is sharp; but after afflicted Formless-Realm citta, Form-Realm rebirth-acquired wholesome citta does not arise, because it is not sufficiently sharp for that transition.

There are three kinds of attention (*manaskāra*): attention to the specific characteristic, attention to the general characteristic, and attention based on resolute determination. Attention to a specific characteristic considers, for example, “form has the characteristic of being form.” Attention to general characteristics is associated with the sixteen aspects. Resolute attention operates in practices such as impurity meditation, immeasurables, formless attainments, liberations, spheres of mastery, and totality meditations.

According to one position, the noble path may be made manifest immediately after any of these three kinds of attention, and any of the three may likewise occur after the path. Others say that the noble path becomes manifest only after general-characteristic attention, though all three may follow it. On that account, after subduing the mind through impurity meditation one brings forth the path after general-characteristic attention; the scriptural statement that one develops the mindfulness awakening-factor accompanied by impurity is then understood through an indirect sequence.

Some also maintain that only general-characteristic attention follows the noble path. But difficulties arise depending on the meditative level used as support for entry into the fixed course. If entry occurs supported by access or the neighboring lower levels, a Desire-Realm general-characteristic attention may follow; but if entry occurs supported by the second or higher dhyānas, such a Desire-Realm cognition is too remote. Nor has an ordinary cognition of that higher level necessarily been acquired apart from the factors conducive to penetration, and a noble one does not return to those preparatory factors after attaining the fruit. Therefore another general-characteristic cognition of the same type must be cultivated, for example: “all formations are impermanent; all dharmas are non-self; nirvāṇa is peaceful,” and that is what will subsequently be made manifest. This explanation is not accepted by all.

For one attaining arhatship supported by access concentration, the emergence-citta belongs either to that level or to the Desire Realm. If arhatship is attained supported by the sphere of nothingness, emergence may occur through a citta of that level or of the summit of existence. In the remaining cases, emergence occurs only in the same level.

In the Desire Realm there are three kinds of attention according to source: produced by hearing, produced by reflection, and acquired through rebirth; there is no cultivation-produced attention there in this classification. In the Form Realm there are three: produced by hearing, produced by cultivation, and acquired through rebirth; reflection-produced attention is absent, because when beings there begin to reflect, concentration itself immediately becomes established. In the Formless Realm there are two: cultivation-produced and rebirth-acquired. The noble path can become manifest after five kinds of attention other than those merely acquired through rebirth, because the path depends on preparation. After the path, however, even a Desire-Realm rebirth-acquired cognition may become manifest, owing to its sharpness.

## New Technical Terms

- **vipākaja** — maturation-born citta.
  - **status:** previously encountered in causal/result discussions; newly used here as a citta subtype.
  - **function:** functional-origin feature for unobscured-indeterminate citta.
- **airyāpathika / īryāpathika** — deportment-related citta.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-level refinement.
  - **function:** citta connected with bodily deportment/activity.
- **śailpasthānika** — craft- or skill-based citta.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-level refinement.
  - **function:** functional citta associated with skilled/craft activity.
- **nairmita / nirmāṇacitta** — constructed or magically produced citta.
  - **status:** previously encountered in transition examples.
  - **function:** special constructed citta, including supernormal production-context.
- **prāyogika** — practice-produced / application-produced.
  - **status:** introduced in the refined twenty-citta matrix.
  - **function:** marks citta arising from directed cultivation/application rather than mere rebirth-acquisition.
- **upapattilābhika / upapattipratilambhika** — acquired through rebirth.
  - **status:** introduced in the refined matrix.
  - **function:** acquisition-mode feature.
- **manaskāra** — attention / directing of mind.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya expansion.
  - **function:** further classifies cognitive preparation for path manifestation.
- **svalakṣaṇa-manaskāra** — attention to specific characteristic.
  - **status:** new here.
  - **function:** object-specific cognitive determination.
- **sāmānyalakṣaṇa-manaskāra** — attention to general characteristic.
  - **status:** new here.
  - **function:** universal/general-characteristic cognition, especially important before noble-path manifestation.
- **adhimukti-manaskāra** — resolute/determined attention.
  - **status:** new here.
  - **function:** intentional attention in constructed contemplative exercises.

## Translation Consequences

1. `avyākṛta` must not be rendered as if it meant featureless neutrality. Here it contains several operationally distinct subclasses.
2. The twenty-citta matrix is not merely a finer taxonomy; it changes the permitted immediate-successor relations.
3. `prāyogika` and `upapattilābhika` are best treated as an acquisition-mode feature pair.
4. The long Bhāṣya following the verse is structurally part of the refinement introduced by 2.72: once the twelve cittas become twenty, the entire `samanantara-pratyaya` transition matrix has to be recomputed.

## Logical Determination

VAK 2.72 turns a flat twelve-state matrix into a feature-refined transition grammar.

```text
CittaFeatureStructure:
    realm
    ethical/afflictive status
    acquisitionMode
    functionalOrigin
    pathStatus

functionalOrigin:
    vipākaja
    airyāpathika
    śailpasthānika
    nairmita

acquisitionMode:
    prāyogika
    upapattilābhika
```

The important logical rule is:

```text
feature refinement
    -> transition refinement
```

A subtype is not merely a more detailed label. It inherits some transitions from its parent class while gaining exclusions and context-sensitive productions of its own.

## Organon Interpretation

This passage is exceptionally valuable for an Organon Workbook because it demonstrates a mature Feature Grammar pattern:

```text
BaseClass
    Citta[realm,status]

Refinement
    + acquisitionMode
    + functionalOrigin

Production
    Citta[A] -> Citta[B]

Constraint
    licensed only if feature-distance,
    meditative context,
    path status,
    and strength/sharpness conditions permit
```

The Bhāṣya also supplies explanations for failed productions rather than only a table of allowed transitions. That makes it appropriate not only as Dataset content but as a future **Solutions Manual** layer: the rule, the exception, and the reason for the exception are all preserved.

## Voice Notes

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — dominant explanatory voice throughout the matrix.
- **Voice: Objector** — brief objections concerning why practice-produced citta cannot arise after weaker neutral cittas and why analogous restrictions should apply to afflicted cittas.
- **Voice: other teachers / uncertain school attribution** — introduced with `apare` in the competing accounts of what kinds of attention can precede or follow the noble path.
