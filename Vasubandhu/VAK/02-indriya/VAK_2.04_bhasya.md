# VAK_2.04_bhasya — The Three Faculties of Realization

## Kārikā Anchor

```text
ājñāsyāmyākhyam ājñākhyam ājñātāvīndriyaṃ tathā /
uttarottarasaṃprāptinirvāṇādyādhipatyataḥ // VAK 2.4 //
```

> Likewise, the faculty called “I shall know,” the faculty called “knowledge,” and the faculty of one who has known are faculties because they exercise governing efficacy with respect to successive attainment, nirvāṇa, and related results.

Kārikā file: [VAK_2.04.md](./VAK_2.04.md)

## Bhāṣya Source

```text
pratyekam indriyam ity upadarśanārthaṃ tathāśabdaḥ /
tatrājñāsyāmīndriyasyājñendriyasya prāptāv ādhipatyam /
ājñendriyasyājñātāvīndriyaprāptau /
ājñātāvīndriyasya parinirvāṇe /
na hy avimuktacittasyāsti parinirvāṇam iti /
ādiśabdo 'nyaparyāyadyotanārthaḥ /
katamo 'nyaḥ paryāyaḥ /
darśanaheyakleśaprahāṇaṃ praty ājñāsyāmīndriyasyādhipatyam /
bhāvanāheyakleśaprahāṇaṃ praty ājñendriyasya /
dṛṣṭadharmasukhavihāraṃ praty ājñātāvīndriyasya /
vimuktiprītisukhasaṃvedanād iti /
```

Source: Abhidharmakośabhāṣya 2, approximately [040|03–040|08].

## Continuous Translation

> The word *tathā*, “likewise,” is used to indicate that each of the three is individually a faculty. Among them, the faculty called “I shall know” has governing efficacy with respect to the attainment of the faculty of knowledge. The faculty of knowledge has governing efficacy with respect to the attainment of the faculty of one who has known. The faculty of one who has known has governing efficacy with respect to parinirvāṇa, for there is no parinirvāṇa for one whose mind has not been liberated.
>
> The expression “and so forth” indicates another mode of explanation. What is that other mode? The faculty called “I shall know” has governing efficacy with respect to the abandonment of the afflictions to be abandoned by seeing. The faculty of knowledge has governing efficacy with respect to the abandonment of the afflictions to be abandoned by cultivation. The faculty of one who has known has governing efficacy with respect to pleasant abiding in the present life, because through it one experiences the joy and happiness of liberation.

## Segmented Translation

### The function of *tathā*

> The word “likewise” indicates that each is individually a faculty.

**Voice:** Vasubandhu

### The first realization faculty

> The faculty called “I shall know” governs the attainment of the faculty of knowledge.

```text
ājñāsyāmīndriya → ājñendriya
```

### The second realization faculty

> The faculty of knowledge governs the attainment of the faculty of one who has known.

```text
ājñendriya → ājñātāvīndriya
```

### The third realization faculty

> The faculty of one who has known governs parinirvāṇa, for one whose mind has not been liberated does not attain parinirvāṇa.

```text
ājñātāvīndriya → liberated mind → parinirvāṇa
```

### The second explanation

> “And so forth” introduces another explanation.

The three faculties are then distinguished by their practical work:

```text
ājñāsyāmīndriya
    → abandonment of afflictions abandoned by seeing

ājñendriya
    → abandonment of afflictions abandoned by cultivation

ājñātāvīndriya
    → pleasant abiding in the present life
    → experience of the joy and happiness of liberation
```

## Argument Reconstruction

The Bhāṣya gives two complementary accounts.

### Successive attainment

```text
first faculty → second faculty
second faculty → third faculty
third faculty → parinirvāṇa
```

### Distinctive path function

```text
first faculty → seeing removes one class of affliction
second faculty → cultivation removes another class
third faculty → liberated happiness is experienced here and now
```

The three faculties are therefore not merely future, present, and completed forms of knowing. They are distinct practical powers in the architecture of the path.

## Translation Consequences

The phrase *uttarottara-saṃprāpti* means the attainment of each successively higher faculty. It is more precise than a general “progressive realization.”

The word *ādi* in *nirvāṇādi* introduces a second doctrinal explanation rather than merely adding an indefinite list of results.

The final faculty governs not only parinirvāṇa but also *dṛṣṭa-dharma-sukha-vihāra*, pleasant abiding in the present life. Completion of knowledge therefore has a positive lived expression.

## Doctrinal Determination

```text
knowledge to be realized
    → transforms through seeing

knowledge under cultivation
    → transforms through repeated practice

knowledge completed
    → supports liberated abiding and parinirvāṇa
```

This is Practical Reason internal to the Kośa itself. Knowledge is defined through the transformation it governs. Yet the passage is also a principle of practice: it determines what the practitioner is able to accomplish at each stage.

## Light Organon Contact Point

```text
knowing as task
knowing as cultivation
knowing as accomplished freedom
```

The Organon need not replace the Abhidharma sequence. It can recognize that practice is not an external application of knowledge. Knowledge becomes practical through the governing efficacy by which it transforms the continuum.

## New Technical Terms

- **uttarottara-saṃprāpti** — attainment of each successively higher faculty
  - **status:** Kārikā term determined by the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** orders the three realization faculties

- **avimukta-citta** — mind not yet liberated
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit
  - **function:** explains why the final faculty governs parinirvāṇa

- **darśana-heya-kleśa** — affliction abandoned by seeing
  - **status:** Bhāṣya path terminology
  - **function:** object of the first realization faculty

- **bhāvanā-heya-kleśa** — affliction abandoned by cultivation
  - **status:** Bhāṣya path terminology
  - **function:** object of the second realization faculty

- **dṛṣṭa-dharma-sukha-vihāra** — pleasant abiding in the present life
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit
  - **function:** positive result governed by the final faculty

- **vimukti-prīti-sukha-saṃvedana** — experience of the joy and happiness of liberation
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit
  - **function:** explains liberated abiding
