# VAK_2.14_bhasya — Initial Acquisition of Faculties

## Kārikā Anchor

```text
kāmeṣv ādau vipāko dve labhyete nopapādukaiḥ /
taiḥ ṣaḍ vā sapta vā aṣṭau vā ṣaḍ rūpeṣv ekam uttare // VAkK_2.14 //
```

Working translation:

> At the beginning of existence in the desire realm, those not spontaneously born acquire two resultant faculties. Those spontaneously born acquire six, seven, or eight. In the form realm six are acquired; in the higher realm, one.

## Continuous Translation

Acquisition must now be explained. How many faculties, in which realm, are first acquired as karmic maturation?

At the beginning of existence in the desire realm, two resultant faculties are acquired: the body faculty and the life faculty.

These, however, are acquired by beings who are not spontaneously born. Since spontaneously born beings are excluded, this statement should be understood as referring to beings born from eggs, from wombs, or from moisture.

Why are the mind faculty and the neutral-feeling faculty not included? Because at the moment of relinking both are necessarily afflicted.

How many faculties, then, are acquired by spontaneously born beings?

They acquire six if they are without a sexual characteristic, as were the beings at the beginning of the cosmic age. The six are the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and life faculties.

They acquire seven if they possess one sexual characteristic, as among gods and similar beings.

They acquire eight if they possess both sexual characteristics.

Are there spontaneously born beings possessing both sexual characteristics? There are, in the unfortunate destinies.

This explains the desire realm. What occurs in the form and formless realms?

In the form realm, six faculties are first acquired as karmic maturation. They are the same six acquired in the desire realm by spontaneously born beings without a sexual characteristic.

The desire realm is designated in the plural as “desires” because desire predominates there. The form realm is similarly designated as “forms” because form predominates there. The sūtra likewise says: “Monks, those peaceful liberations that transcend forms are the formless attainments.”

In the higher realm, one faculty is acquired. The formless realm is higher than the form realm because it is superior both as meditative attainment and as mode of rebirth. There only the life faculty, and no other faculty, is first acquired as karmic maturation.

## Voice Map

- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — introduces the acquisition problem and explains the counts.
- **Voice: Objector** — asks why mind and neutral feeling are excluded, and whether spontaneously born beings may possess both sexual characteristics.
- **Voice: Vasubandhu** — answers through relinking-state, birth mode, and realm structure.

## Argument Reconstruction

The question is no longer what a faculty is, but which resultant faculties are present at the first moment of a newly established continuum.

```text
initial faculty acquisition
    depends on:
        realm
        mode of birth
        sexual differentiation
        whether the faculty is karmic maturation at relinking
```

### Desire realm: non-spontaneous birth

```text
egg-born / womb-born / moisture-born
    → body faculty
    → life faculty
```

Mind and neutral feeling are present, but not counted here as resultant acquisitions because at relinking they are necessarily afflicted.

### Desire realm: spontaneous birth

```text
without sexual characteristic
    → six faculties

with one sexual characteristic
    → seven faculties

with both sexual characteristics
    → eight faculties
```

The basic six are:

```text
eye
 ear
 nose
 tongue
 body
 life
```

One or two sexual faculties are then added according to the configuration of the being.

### Form realm

```text
six faculties:
    eye
    ear
    nose
    tongue
    body
    life
```

The form realm therefore begins with the same resultant configuration as a spontaneously born, sexually undifferentiated being in the desire realm.

### Formless realm

```text
one faculty:
    life
```

The formless continuum begins without material faculties and without a feeling faculty counted as resultant at that first moment.

## Translation Consequences

The term **prathamataḥ labhyante** should be translated “are first acquired” or “are acquired at the outset,” not merely “exist.” The passage concerns the initial installation of a resultant faculty-set at rebirth.

**Pratisaṃdhi** is the relinking moment by which a new continuum is established. The mind and neutral feeling occurring at that moment are not denied; they are excluded specifically from the class of faculties acquired there as karmic maturation because they are afflicted.

The term **vyañjana** here means sexual characteristic or sexual differentiation. It should not be translated as “expression” in this context.

## New Technical Terms

- **lābha** — acquisition; here, initial acquisition of faculties at rebirth.
  - **status:** newly central in this passage.
  - **function:** introduces a new classification problem after the earlier typologies.

- **prathamataḥ** — initially, at the first moment.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya clarification of the Kārikā’s *ādau*.
  - **function:** limits the discussion to the beginning of the new continuum.

- **pratisaṃdhi-kāla** — moment of relinking.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only technical specification here.
  - **function:** explains why mind and neutral feeling are not counted as resultant faculties.

- **upapāduka** — spontaneously born.
  - **status:** present in the Kārikā and explained in the Bhāṣya.
  - **function:** differentiates complete instantaneous acquisition from gradual embryonic acquisition.

- **aṇḍaja** — egg-born.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit.
  - **function:** one non-spontaneous mode of birth.

- **jarāyuja** — womb-born.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit.
  - **function:** one non-spontaneous mode of birth.

- **saṃsvedaja** — moisture-born.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this unit.
  - **function:** one non-spontaneous mode of birth.

- **avyañjana / ekavyañjana / ubhayavyañjana** — without, with one, or with both sexual characteristics.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya clarification of the variable counts.
  - **function:** determines whether six, seven, or eight faculties are initially acquired.

## Doctrinal Determination

This is less a metaphysical doctrine of birth than a rule-governed inventory of the initial conditions of a continuum.

The classification is cumbersome, but the functional question is exact:

```text
Given a realm and mode of embodiment,
what is the minimum resultant faculty-set
with which a new life-series begins?
```

The answer distinguishes faculties that are materially installed as maturation from mental events that are present but ethically afflicted at relinking.

## Restrained Organon Contact

The planning-system analogy is useful here if kept subordinate to the text.

```text
realm
    = execution environment

birth mode
    = initialization procedure

faculty count
    = initial resultant configuration
```

But the Bhāṣya is not proposing a general computational ontology. It is specifying which elements of the twenty-two-faculty model are instantiated at the first moment under different cosmological conditions.

The strongest structural point is that the same faculty-Idea admits different initial configurations without losing its systematic unity.

## Compact Result

```text
non-spontaneous desire-realm birth:
    2 resultant faculties

spontaneous desire-realm birth:
    6, 7, or 8

form-realm birth:
    6

formless-realm birth:
    1
```

The unit ends with the acquisition analysis completed and prepares the transition to the loss or cessation of faculties at death.
