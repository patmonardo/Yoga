# VAK_7.28 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

> aṣṭādaśāveṇikāstu buddhadharmā balādayaḥ /
> sthānāsthāne daśa jñānāni aṣṭau karmaphale nava // VAK_7.28 //

**Best current translation:**  
The qualities distinctive to a Buddha are eighteen, beginning with the powers. In the power concerning what is possible and impossible there are ten knowledges; in the power concerning karmic maturation, eight. Nine [in the powers taken up next].

**Kārikā analysis:** [`VAK_7.28.md`](./VAK_7.28.md)

## Bhāṣya Source

## Natural Argument

### 1. From general cultivation to Buddha-specific cultivation

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The preceding discussion described, in general, the cultivation of qualities at `kṣayajñāna` for persons broadly.

Vasubandhu now restricts the field:

```text
ye buddhasyaiva bhagavataḥ
    those belonging only to the Blessed Buddha

kṣayajñāne bhāvanāṃ gacchanti
    which enter cultivation at knowledge of destruction

nānyasya
    and not for another
```

Thus VAK 7.28 does not merely append a list of Buddha qualities. It identifies a special cultivation-result belonging only to a Buddha.

### 2. What are the eighteen?

The eighteen are:

```text
10 balāni
    powers

4 vaiśāradyāni
    fearlessnesses / grounds of confidence

3 smṛtyupasthānāni
    establishments of recollection

1 mahākaruṇā
    great compassion
```

The Bhāṣya then glosses the governing adjective:

```text
asādhāraṇaṃ hi āveṇikam ity ucyate
    what is unshared is called distinctive
```

So `āveṇika` should be read first as **unshared / distinctive**, not as an unexplained honorific.

### 3. First power: knowledge of possibility and impossibility

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

```text
sthānāsthānajñānabala
    power of knowledge of what is possible and impossible
```

is constituted by:

```text
daśa jñānāni
    all ten knowledges
```

No knowledge-species is excluded.

### 4. Second power: knowledge of karmic maturation

```text
karmavipākajñānabala
    power of knowledge of karmic maturation
```

is constituted by eight knowledges.

The Bhāṣya specifies the exclusion exactly:

```text
nirodhajñāna
mārgajñāna
```

Hence:

```text
10 knowledges
- extinction-knowledge
- Path-knowledge
= 8
```

The final `nava` carries forward into VAK 7.29.

## Bhāṣya Translation

### Literal Translation

In general, the cultivation of qualities at knowledge of destruction has been stated for all persons. But there are eighteen qualities distinctive to a Buddha, beginning with the powers, which enter cultivation at the Blessed Buddha's knowledge of destruction alone and not for another. What are the eighteen? Ten powers, four fearlessnesses, three establishments of recollection, and great compassion. For what is unshared is called distinctive.

Among these, in [the power concerning] what is possible and impossible there are ten knowledges: the power of knowledge of the possible and impossible consists of the ten knowledges. In [the power concerning] karmic maturation there are eight: the power of knowledge of karmic maturation consists of eight knowledges, excluding knowledge of extinction and knowledge of the Path. Nine ...

### Philosophical Translation

The general cultivation attained at knowledge of destruction does not exhaust the cognitive transformation of Buddhahood. A Buddha alone cultivates eighteen unshared qualities at that moment: ten powers, four fearlessnesses, three establishments of recollection, and great compassion. These powers are not additional mysterious faculties outside the system of knowledge; they are determinate organizations of the ten knowledges according to a distinctive field of competence. The first power deploys the whole tenfold system to determine possibility and impossibility. The second deploys eight, excluding extinction- and Path-knowledge, to determine the maturation of action.

## New Technical Terms

- **āveṇika** — distinctive, specifically unshared
  - **status:** technically clarified in the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** marks qualities cultivated only by a Buddha and not by another practitioner

- **asādhāraṇa** — unshared, not held in common
  - **status:** Bhāṣya gloss of `āveṇika`
  - **function:** gives the defining sense of Buddha-specific distinctiveness

- **bala** — power
  - **status:** introduced as the first class among the eighteen Buddha-specific qualities
  - **function:** stable Buddha-specific cognitive competence constituted by determinate knowledges

- **sthānāsthānajñānabala** — power of knowledge of possibility and impossibility
  - **status:** first of the ten powers
  - **function:** determines possible and impossible conditions through all ten knowledges

- **karmavipākajñānabala** — power of knowledge of karmic maturation
  - **status:** second of the ten powers
  - **function:** determines karmic maturation through eight of the ten knowledges

## Doctrinal Determination

VAK 7.28 establishes the internal architecture of the eighteen Buddha-specific qualities:

```text
18 āveṇika-buddhadharmas
    =
10 powers
+ 4 fearlessnesses
+ 3 establishments of recollection
+ 1 great compassion
```

The decisive doctrinal point is their relation to cultivation:

```text
general kṣayajñāna
    cultivation of qualities for liberated persons generally

Buddha's kṣayajñāna
    cultivation of eighteen additional unshared qualities
```

Thus Buddhahood is not introduced as a wholly separate cognitive ontology. The distinctive qualities emerge within the same cultivation framework but reach a range unavailable to others.

The first two powers are themselves analyzed through the already established tenfold knowledge-system:

| Power | Knowledges | Exclusions |
|---|---:|---|
| possibility / impossibility | 10 | none |
| karmic maturation | 8 | extinction, Path |

## Effect on the Kārikā Translation

**Clarification only.**

The Kārikā-pass rendering of `āveṇika` as “distinctive / unshared” is confirmed directly by the Bhāṣya's `asādhāraṇa`.

`karmaphala` should be sharpened in this context toward **karmic maturation**, because the Bhāṣya explicitly names the power `karmavipākajñānabala`.

The final `nava` remains syntactically carried into VAK 7.29.

## Logical Determination

The most important movement is:

```text
cultivation
    ↓
complete liberated cognition
    ↓
Buddha-specific unshared organization of cognition
    ↓
power
```

`Bala` is therefore not mere force. It is an achieved organization of knowing with a determinate object-field.

The first two powers reveal two different selection rules over the same tenfold system:

```text
Power 1:
field = possibility / impossibility
selection = all ten knowledges

Power 2:
field = karmic maturation
selection = all except extinction and Path
```

So the identity of a power is determined not merely by how many knowledges it contains but by:

```text
object-field
+ admissible knowledge-types
+ exclusions
+ Buddha-specific completion
```

The numerical count is once again generated from a more fundamental relational structure.

## Organon Contact Point

A restrained but strong contact appears here.

The Kośa has moved from:

```text
knowledge-types
    → cultivation-rules
    → stabilized cultivated capacities
    → powers
```

This resembles a transition from an articulated Feature system to higher-order competencies generated from determinate configurations of those Features.

But the textual point must remain primary: Vasubandhu himself reduces the first Buddha powers to selected organizations of the ten knowledges.

Thus the Buddha's distinctive power is not outside the knowledge-system. It is the knowledge-system organized with a completeness and range unavailable to others.
