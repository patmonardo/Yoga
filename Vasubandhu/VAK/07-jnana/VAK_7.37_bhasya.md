# VAK_7.37 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

> tathaiva praṇidhijñānaṃ sarvālambaṃ tu tat tathā /
> dharmārthayorniruktau ca pratibhāne ca saṃvidaḥ // VAK_7.37 //

**Best current translation:**

Knowledge arising from resolve is likewise of the same kind, but it has all things as its object-support. The discriminating knowledges concern dharma, meaning, linguistic explanation, and ready articulation.

**Kārikā analysis:** [`VAK_7.37.md`](./VAK_7.37.md)

## Bhāṣya Source

Pradhan 417|18–418|12.

The natural unit has two movements. First Vasubandhu determines `praṇidhijñāna` by comparison with `araṇā`, then explains its specific object-range and genesis through resolve. Second he opens the four `pratisaṃvids`, leaving their differentiated object, level, and nature to VAK 7.38–7.40.

```text
yathā cāraṇoktā

     tathaiva praṇidhijñānaṃ

tadapi hi saṃvṛtijñānaṃ dhyāne 'ntye 'kopyadharmaṇaḥ manuṣyāśrayaṃ ca

     sarvālambaṃ tu tat

sarvadharmālambanaṃ tu praṇidhijñānamityeva viśeṣaḥ /
ārupyāstu na sākṣāt prāṇidhijñānena jñāyante /
kiṃ tarhi /
niṣpannacaritaviśeṣāt /
karṣakanidarśanaṃ cātreti vaibhāṣikāḥ /

praṇidhipūrvakaṃ jñānaṃ praṇidhijñānaṃ yaddhi praṇidhāya prāntakoṭikaṃ caturtha dhyānaṃ samāpadyate /
idaṃ jānīyāmiti tadyathābhūtaṃ jānāti /
sarvastatsamādhiviṣayaḥ

     tathā /
     dharmārthayorniruktau ca pratibhāne ca saṃvidaḥ // VAkK_7.37 //

catasro hi pratisaṃvidaḥ /
dharmapratisaṃvidarthapratisaṃvinniruktipratisaṃvitpratibhānapratisaṃvicca /
tā api dharmārthaniruktipratibhānapratisaṃvidastathaiva yathā 'raṇā /
kimāsāṃ tathaiva akopyadharmamanuṣyāśrayatvam /
ālambanabhūmisvabhāvaviśeṣastvāsāṃ pṛthagucyate /
```

### Source cautions

- The supplied e-text has `prāṇidhijñānena` in one occurrence; this is preserved as transmitted and treated as an evident textual irregularity rather than silently corrected in the quoted source.
- The page-line marker before the final sentence is corrupt in the e-text (`[441|811-41812]`). Context places the sentence at approximately Pradhan 418|11–12.
- The source explicitly attributes the qualification concerning formless attainments and the farmer illustration to the Vaibhāṣikas. It should not be flattened into an unmarked statement of Vasubandhu's own final commitment.

## Natural Argument

### 1. `Praṇidhijñāna` is like `araṇā`

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

What was said of `araṇā` applies likewise to knowledge arising from resolve:

```text
saṃvṛtijñāna
    conventional knowledge

antya-dhyāna
    based on the final / fourth dhyāna

akopyadharman
    belonging to an immovable arhat

manuṣyāśraya
    having a human support
```

Thus VAK 7.37 does not introduce an entirely new cognitive substrate. It modifies the already established structure by changing principally the object-range and mode of activation.

### 2. Its distinguishing mark is universal possible object-support

The Kārikā says `sarvālambam`. Vasubandhu glosses this directly:

```text
sarvadharmālambanam
    having all dharmas as object-support
```

This is the specific difference from `araṇā`, whose object-range was restricted to unarisen desire-domain, object-based afflictions.

The contrast is therefore:

```text
araṇā
    restricted preventive object-field

praṇidhijñāna
    all dharmas as possible object-support
```

### 3. Vaibhāṣika qualification: the formless attainments are not known directly

**Voice: Vaibhāṣika position**

The formless attainments (`ārūpya`) are not known directly (`sākṣāt`) by praṇidhijñāna. They are known through the distinctive completed conduct (`niṣpanna-carita-viśeṣa`) associated with them. The text also refers to a farmer illustration (`karṣaka-nidarśana`).

The important point for this pass is that `sarvālamba` does not license a careless claim of homogeneous direct intuition of every object in exactly the same manner. The tradition represented here preserves universal range while distinguishing modes of access.

### 4. Resolve is genetically constitutive of the knowledge

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The prose gives an etymological-genetic explanation:

```text
praṇidhi-pūrvakaṃ jñānam
    knowledge preceded by resolve
        = praṇidhijñāna
```

The practitioner first resolves:

```text
idaṃ jānīyām
    "May I know this."
```

Then one enters the `prāntakoṭika` fourth dhyāna, and the intended object is known `yathābhūtam`—as it actually is.

The structure is therefore not:

```text
unrestricted omniscience already standing present
```

but:

```text
resolve
    → limit-developed fourth dhyāna
    → determinate object
    → knowledge of that object as it is
```

All things may fall within the range of such samādhi-governed knowing, but the knowledge arises through a determinate directed act.

### 5. The four discriminating knowledges are introduced

Vasubandhu next enumerates four `pratisaṃvids`:

```text
dharma-pratisaṃvid
artha-pratisaṃvid
nirukti-pratisaṃvid
pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid
```

They too are said to be `tathaiva yathā 'raṇā`—like `araṇā` in the respects already established. But Vasubandhu immediately signals that their:

```text
ālambana
bhūmi
svabhāva
```

have distinct determinations and must therefore be explained separately. That differentiation begins in VAK 7.38.

## New Technical Terms

- **praṇidhipūrvaka-jñāna** — knowledge preceded by resolve
  - **status:** Bhāṣya definition of Kārikā `praṇidhijñāna`
  - **function:** gives the genetic rather than merely lexical definition of resolute knowledge.

- **sarvadharmālambana** — having all dharmas as object-support
  - **status:** Bhāṣya expansion of `sarvālamba`
  - **function:** establishes the specific difference of praṇidhijñāna from araṇā.

- **sākṣāt** — directly, immediately
  - **status:** operative in the Vaibhāṣika qualification
  - **function:** distinguishes direct knowledge from mediated determination of the formless attainments.

- **niṣpanna-carita-viśeṣa** — distinctive completed conduct / accomplished mode of activity
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only in this local argument
  - **function:** explains the indirect route by which the formless attainments are known according to the Vaibhāṣikas.

- **karṣaka-nidarśana** — farmer illustration
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only reference
  - **function:** example invoked by the Vaibhāṣikas; the e-text does not reproduce the illustration here.

- **prāntakoṭika** — brought to the limiting edge / limit-developed
  - **status:** technical meditative qualifier already encountered in the chapter
  - **function:** specifies the perfected fourth-dhyāna basis entered after the resolve.

- **yathābhūta** — as it actually is
  - **status:** recurrent doctrinal term, newly operative in this local genesis
  - **function:** characterizes the result of the resolved knowledge.

- **dharma-pratisaṃvid** — discriminating knowledge concerning dharma
  - **status:** introduced in the Kārikā and enumerated explicitly in prose
  - **function:** first of the four discriminating knowledges.

- **artha-pratisaṃvid** — discriminating knowledge concerning meaning
  - **status:** introduced in the Kārikā and enumerated explicitly in prose
  - **function:** second discriminating knowledge.

- **nirukti-pratisaṃvid** — discriminating knowledge concerning linguistic explanation
  - **status:** introduced in the Kārikā and enumerated explicitly in prose
  - **function:** third discriminating knowledge.

- **pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid** — discriminating knowledge concerning ready articulation
  - **status:** introduced in the Kārikā and enumerated explicitly in prose
  - **function:** fourth discriminating knowledge.

## Doctrinal Determination

### `Praṇidhijñāna` is directed universal capacity, not undifferentiated omniscience

The phrase `sarvālamba` is strong but structurally controlled. The Bhāṣya does not say that every object is simultaneously manifest. Rather:

```text
all dharmas
    are within its possible range

but a determinate resolve
    selects what is to be known
```

The knowledge is universal by possible extension and determinate in each exercise.

### The object is known through a practical sequence

The Bhāṣya explicitly generates the knowledge:

```text
praṇidhi
    deliberate resolve

samāpatti
    entry into limit-developed fourth dhyāna

jñāna
    knowing the intended object as it is
```

This is crucial. `Praṇidhi` is not merely an accompanying wish. It organizes the exercise of the cognitive capacity.

### Universal object-range does not erase differences in mode of access

The Vaibhāṣika qualification concerning the `ārūpyas` prevents `sarvālamba` from being construed as a simple one-mode relation between cognition and every possible object. A universal range can contain internally differentiated modes of access.

That is a valuable doctrinal restraint for the later analysis of Buddha knowledge as well: universality of range and uniformity of cognitive mediation are not equivalent claims.

### The four `pratisaṃvids` begin here but are not yet defined here

VAK 7.37 gives the four domains but does not yet tell us exactly what each knowledge is. The Bhāṣya explicitly postpones their differences of object-support, level, and nature. We should therefore resist overdetermining `dharma`, `artha`, `nirukti`, and `pratibhāna` before VAK 7.38–7.40 supply the controls.

## Translation Consequences

**Status: clarification and one restraint.**

The first-pass translation of the first half is substantially sound, but `knowledge arising from resolve` can now be made more exact as **knowledge preceded and governed by a determinate resolve**.

The Bhāṣya also requires restraint on the phrase `sarvālamba`. It means that all dharmas can serve as its object-support; it should not be expanded into simultaneous or uniformly direct omniscience.

A refined translation is:

> Knowledge preceded by resolve is likewise [conventional knowledge of the same basis and bearer], but it can take all dharmas as its object-support. The four discriminating knowledges concern dharma, meaning, linguistic explanation, and ready articulation.

The bracketed phrase is supplied from the Bhāṣya and should not be mistaken for words present in the Kārikā itself.

For the four `pratisaṃvids`, the current translations remain provisional pending VAK 7.38–7.40.

## Logical Determination

The strongest logical form in this passage is the union of **universal range with singular determination**:

```text
Universal:
    every dharma is a possible object-support

Particular:
    a resolve specifies the domain to be known

Singular:
    "this" is known yathābhūtam
```

But the text does not present these as three independent cognitive faculties. They are moments of one exercise:

```text
possible universality
    → directed resolve
    → actual knowledge
```

That is more exact than the first-pass formula `resolve → selected object-support → knowledge`, because the Bhāṣya adds the mediating actuality of the `prāntakoṭika` fourth dhyāna.

The complete local genesis is:

```text
capacity for all-object support
        ↓
praṇidhi
        ↓
prāntakoṭika-dhyāna
        ↓
yathābhūta-jñāna of this object
```

## Organon Contact Point

This passage gives us an unusually clean model of **rationally directed inquiry**.

Knowledge-capacity does not become actual simply because an object exists within its possible range. A rational determination first specifies:

```text
"this I would know"
```

and then the appropriate cultivated cognitive condition mediates the passage from possible knowability to actual knowledge.

For the Organon, the important relation is therefore not merely:

```text
Query → Answer
```

but:

```text
universal knowable field
    → explicit rational resolve
    → adequate cognitive mediation
    → determinate knowledge
```

This remains subordinate to Vasubandhu's concrete account. The Kośa is describing a cultivated yogic knowledge, not a software architecture. But the logical form is strong enough to preserve for the later Rational Kośa.

There is also a useful continuity with VAK 7.36:

```text
araṇā
    knowledge determines one's conduct
    through the possible determination of another

praṇidhijñāna
    resolve determines one's knowing
    within the universal possible field of dharmas
```

In both cases knowledge becomes actual through a mediating practical determination rather than remaining a merely possessed faculty.

## Transition to VAK 7.38

The second half of VAK 7.37 only opens the four discriminating knowledges. The Bhāṣya explicitly announces that their differences must be determined by:

```text
ālambana
    object-support

bhūmi
    level

svabhāva
    nature
```

VAK 7.38 begins that differentiation by identifying the first three with irreversible knowledge concerning names, meanings, and speech, while the fourth concerns mastery of appropriate and free articulation.
