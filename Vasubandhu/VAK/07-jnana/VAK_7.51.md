# VAK_7.51 — Construction and Speech Require Different Cognitive Operations

## Sanskrit

```text
svabhūmikena nirmāṇaṃ bhāṣaṇaṃ tvadhareṇa ca /
nirmātraiva sahāśāstuḥ adhiṣṭhāyānyavarttanāt // VAK_7.51 //
```

## Padaccheda

```text
sva-bhūmikena
nirmāṇam

bhāṣaṇam
tu
adhareṇa
ca

nirmātrā
eva
saha
śāstuḥ

adhiṣṭhāya
anya-vartanāt
```

| Term | Working sense |
|---|---|
| **svabhūmikena** | by a mind belonging to the same level or ground |
| **nirmāṇam** | construction, projected formation |
| **bhāṣaṇam** | speaking, linguistic operation |
| **adhareṇa** | by a lower-ground mind |
| **nirmātrā saha** | simultaneously with the constructor |
| **śāstuḥ** | in the case of the Teacher |
| **adhiṣṭhāya** | having sustained, empowered, or established |
| **anya-vartanāt** | because another cognitive operation then functions |

## Literal translation

Construction occurs through a mind of its own ground, but speech also occurs through a lower-ground mind. Only in the case of the Teacher does the constructed form speak simultaneously with the constructor; in other cases, after the construction has been sustained, another mind operates.

## Philosophical translation

Construction and linguistic expression are distinct cognitive operations. A projected form is generated by a ground-appropriate construction-citta, while speech requires a lower-level operation. For ordinary adepts these occur sequentially through sustained persistence; only the Teacher coordinates construction and speech simultaneously.

# 1. Construction and Speech Are Different Operations

```text
nirmāṇa
    one cognitive operation

bhāṣaṇa
    another cognitive operation
```

The constructed figure does not automatically speak merely because it has been projected.

Formation and linguistic expression require different operational conditions.

```text
construction
    produces an external form

speech
    produces a communicative event
```

This confirms the distinction seen in VAK 7.49 between stable object-features and event-functions.

# 2. Why Speech Requires a Lower Mind

```text
bhāṣaṇaṃ tvadhareṇa
    speech occurs through a lower-ground mind
```

The construction-citta belongs to the dhyānic level from which the projection is generated. Ordinary speech belongs to a lower cognitive field, closely tied to the desire realm and first dhyāna.

```text
higher construction-ground
    produces the projected form

lower linguistic operation
    animates speech
```

This is a division of labor between cognitive powers.

# 3. The Constructed “Mind” Is Not a Constructed Person

A careless reading imagines a yogin creating a fake person with its own mind. The kārikā describes something more exact:

```text
one cognitive operation
    constructs the form

another cognitive operation
    produces the speech

adhiṣṭhāna
    preserves the constructed form
    while the second operation functions
```

There is no need to posit a second substantial subject. The projected figure is an externally sustained operative interface. Its apparent speech results from a sequence of cognitive operations belonging to the constructor’s continuum.

```text
nirmita
    projected object-form

not:
    independent jīva

not:
    autonomous pudgala

not:
    second enduring consciousness-stream
```

Citta here means a typed cognitive event or operational mode, not a soul-container.

# 4. Adhiṣṭhāna as Persistence Control

```text
adhiṣṭhāya
    having established or sustained
```

The construction-mind first produces the projected form. Then the projection is held in operation while another citta performs speech.

```text
construct
    ↓

stabilize
    ↓

switch cognitive operation
    ↓

speak
```

Adhiṣṭhāna is the operation by which an already generated configuration remains active while the originating citta is no longer the currently functioning one.

# 5. Sequential Multiplexing

For ordinary accomplished practitioners, the process is sequential.

```text
Citta 1
    construction

Citta 2
    speech
```

The first operation establishes and stabilizes its result before the second begins.

```text
initialize object

persist object-state

yield execution

run speech-operation
```

The apparent unity of the speaking construction is produced by coordinated succession, not by one undifferentiated mind doing everything at once.

# 6. The Teacher’s Simultaneity

```text
nirmātrā eva saha śāstuḥ
    only in the case of the Teacher,
    together with the constructor
```

For the Buddha, construction and speech can occur simultaneously. The distinction between functions remains, but their operations no longer require serial exclusion.

```text
ordinary adept
    construct
    then speak

Teacher
    construct and speak
    without operational interruption
```

The Buddha’s superiority is complete concurrency of rational powers.

# 7. Unity Without Confusion

The Teacher does not collapse construction and speech into one indistinct function.

```text
distinct operations
    remain distinct

yet

their execution
    is simultaneous
```

Scientific unity coordinates determinate powers without erasing their difference.

# 8. Citta as Operation Token

A provisional definition emerges:

> A citta is a temporally instantiated cognitive operation belonging to a determinate ground and function.

Thus:

```text
nirmāṇa-citta
    formation operation

bhāṣaṇa-citta
    speech operation

śuddha-dhyāna-citta
    ground-establishing operation
```

These are not personalities. They are operations in one continuum.

# 9. Citta–Caitta and Functional Composition

Each citta is constituted for a different function.

A speech-citta may require:

```text
intention
designation
recognition
articulation
attention
resolve
```

A construction-citta requires another configuration:

```text
samādhi
resolve
formation
realm-compatibility
object-projection
```

Thus:

```text
same continuum

different caitta configurations

different global citta-operation
```

# 10. Object and Interface

The constructed figure has two dimensions:

```text
object-dimension
    projected sensible form

interface-dimension
    vehicle of communicative action
```

The first is produced by nirmāṇa. The second is activated by bhāṣaṇa.

# 11. No Independent Inner Subject Required

The construction appears externally, persists, speaks, and acts as a communicative locus, yet none of this requires an independent enduring self behind it.

```text
organized operation
    can produce
the appearance of a unified agent
```

A functioning agent-form does not prove a substantial agent-entity.

# 12. But It Is Not “Fake”

Calling the construction fake is also inadequate.

```text
not self-grounded

but

operationally effective
```

Constructed means dependently generated, operationally sustained, and conditionally effective.

# 13. Nature–Spirit Architecture

```text
Nature
    external constructed form

Spirit
    speech, meaning, communication

Adhiṣṭhāna
    mediation preserving the form
    while Spirit operates through it
```

The constructed body becomes a vehicle of expression.

# 14. Śāsana Returns

VAK 7.47 established instruction as the highest prātihārya. Here we see the technical architecture through which a projected form may participate in teaching.

```text
nirmāṇa
    supplies an appearing teacher-form

bhāṣaṇa
    supplies communicative expression

śāsana
    orders the speech toward rational transformation
```

# 15. Hegel-Clean Interpretation

```text
nirmāṇa
    Concept becomes external object

bhāṣaṇa
    external object becomes vehicle of Spirit
```

The object becomes spiritually actual when meaning operates through it. In the Teacher, Concept, Objectivity, and Expression operate without temporal fracture.

# 16. Fichtean Interpretation

```text
productive activity
    forms the appearance

practical-linguistic activity
    operates within the appearance
```

The construction is sustained as a posited field while another operation functions.

```text
ordinary adept:

    at a time

    without interference
```

The Buddha’s continuum behaves as a fully integrated multi-operation system.

# 20. Scientific Principle

> Apparent agent-unity can be generated through the coordinated persistence and sequencing of distinct cognitive operations.

```text
one visible agent-form

may express

many coordinated cognitive functions

without being

an independent substantial subject
```

# 21. Beginning–End Structure

At the End of the Elements, the Kośa shows a highly articulated operative system:

```text
ground
construction-citta
projected form
sustaining operation
speech-citta
communicative event
```

Chapter 8 will return to the Beginning and ask how cognition, object, and operation first coincide within samāpatti.

```text
Samāpatti
    immediate cognitive conjunction

Jñāna
    scientific decomposition
    of that conjunction into lawful operations

Method
    reconstruction of their unity
    without loss of distinction
```

# Condensed Principle

> A constructed “mind” is not a fabricated soul. It is a ground-specific cognitive operation within a coordinated system that can construct, sustain, and speak through an externally projected form.

```text
not that distinctions vanish

but that distinct operations
can function together
without obstruction
```
