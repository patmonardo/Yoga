# VAK_7.39 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

> vāṅmārgālambanā cāsau nava jñānāni sarvabhūḥ /
> daśa ṣaḍvārthasaṃvit sā sarvatra anye tu sāṃvṛtam // VAK_7.39 //

**Best current translation:**

That fourth discriminating knowledge has speech and the Path as its object-support; it consists of nine knowledges and belongs to all grounds. The discriminating knowledge of meaning consists of ten knowledges, or six, and occurs everywhere; the other two are conventional knowledge.

**Kārikā analysis:** [`VAK_7.39.md`](./VAK_7.39.md)

## Bhāṣya Source

Pradhan 418|19–419|11.

The natural unit continues directly from VAK 7.38. Vasubandhu now specifies the object-support, knowledge-composition, and level-range of `pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid` and `artha-pratisaṃvid`, then identifies the cognitive nature of `dharma-pratisaṃvid` and `nirukti-pratisaṃvid`.

```text
vāṅmārgālambanā cāsau

vākca mārgaśca tasyā ālambanam /

nava jñānāni

navajñānasvabhāvā pratibhānapratisaṃvidanyatra nirodhajñānāt /

sarvabhūḥ /

sarvabhūmikā cāsau kāmadhātau yāvat bhavāgre vāṅmārgayoranyatarālambanāt /

daśa ṣaḍvā 'rthasaṃvit

arthapratisaṃvit sarvadharmāśccedarthā daśa jñānāni /
nirvāṇaṃ cedarthaḥ ṣaṭ jñānāni /
dharmānvayanirodhakṣayānutpādasaṃvṛtijñānāni /

sā sarvatra

sā punareṣā 'rthapratisaṃvit sarvabhūmikā /

anye tu sāṃvṛtam // VAkK_7.39 //

anye tu dve dharmaniruktipratisaṃvidau saṃvṛtijñānasvabhāve nāmakāyādivāgālambanasvabhāvatvāt /
```

### Source cautions

- The e-text at 419|05 reads a compressed form around `arthapratisaṃvit`; the doctrinal sense is clear from the surrounding sentences.
- `sarvabhūḥ` and `sarvatra` both concern level-range, but they apply to different pratisaṃvids: the former to `pratibhāna`, the latter to `artha`.
- The detailed level restrictions of `dharma` and `nirukti` belong to VAK 7.40 and should not be imported into this verse prematurely.

## Natural Argument

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The fourth discriminating knowledge, `pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid`, has two kinds of object-support:

```text
vāk
    speech

mārga
    Path
```

Its nature is constituted by nine of the ten knowledges. The one excluded is `nirodhajñāna`.

It is `sarvabhūmikā`: it can occur from the desire-domain up through `bhavāgra`, because its object can be either speech or Path.

The discriminating knowledge of meaning, `artha-pratisaṃvid`, has two possible cognitive constitutions depending on what counts as `artha`.

If `artha` means all dharmas, it consists of all ten knowledges.

If `artha` means nirvāṇa, it consists of exactly six:

```text
dharmajñāna
anvayajñāna
nirodhajñāna
kṣayajñāna
anutpādajñāna
saṃvṛtijñāna
```

`Artha-pratisaṃvid` is likewise available on all levels.

The remaining two—`dharma-pratisaṃvid` and `nirukti-pratisaṃvid`—are of the nature of conventional knowledge (`saṃvṛtijñāna`) because their object-supports are name-complexes and speech.

## New Technical Terms

- **navajñānasvabhāva** — having nine knowledges as its cognitive nature
  - **status:** Bhāṣya expansion of `nava jñānāni`
  - **function:** specifies the internal constitution of `pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid`.

- **anyatra nirodhajñānāt** — excluding cessation-knowledge
  - **status:** Bhāṣya-only explicit qualification
  - **function:** identifies exactly which one of the ten knowledges is absent from `pratibhāna`.

- **sarvabhūmika** — belonging to all levels
  - **status:** Bhāṣya gloss on `sarvabhūḥ` / `sarvatra`
  - **function:** specifies the vertical range of `pratibhāna` and `artha`.

- **sarvadharmāḥ ced arthāḥ** — if all dharmas are taken as `artha`
  - **status:** Bhāṣya interpretive condition
  - **function:** grounds the ten-knowledge reading of `artha-pratisaṃvid`.

- **nirvāṇaṃ ced arthaḥ** — if nirvāṇa is taken as `artha`
  - **status:** Bhāṣya interpretive condition
  - **function:** grounds the six-knowledge reading.

## Doctrinal Determination

### `Pratibhāna` is ninefold in cognitive constitution

The Bhāṣya removes any ambiguity from the Kārikā's `nava jñānāni`:

```text
pratibhāna
    = nine knowledges
    = the ten-knowledge system minus nirodhajñāna
```

This is not a metaphorical plurality. It is an exact Abhidharma classification.

### `Artha` has two legitimate scopes

The ten/six alternative is generated by the scope of `artha` itself:

```text
artha = all dharmas
    → ten knowledges

artha = nirvāṇa
    → six knowledges
```

The narrower reading is also exact, because the Bhāṣya lists all six constituent knowledges rather than leaving the number unexplained.

### Conventional cognition is structurally necessary

`Dharma-pratisaṃvid` and `nirukti-pratisaṃvid` are `saṃvṛtijñāna` because their objects are linguistic structures—name-complexes and speech. The Bhāṣya does not treat this conventionality as a defect. It is simply the cognitive type appropriate to those object-supports.

## Translation Consequences

The first-pass translation is substantially confirmed, but two points should be made more exact.

First, `nava jñānāni` means precisely the nine knowledges other than `nirodhajñāna`.

Second, the six knowledges of the nirvāṇa-oriented `artha-pratisaṃvid` are explicitly:

```text
dharmajñāna
anvayajñāna
nirodhajñāna
kṣayajñāna
anutpādajñāna
saṃvṛtijñāna
```

A fuller interpretive translation may therefore read:

> The fourth discriminating knowledge takes speech and the Path as its object-support; it is constituted by nine knowledges, all except cessation-knowledge, and occurs on every level. The discriminating knowledge of meaning consists of all ten knowledges when its meaning-field is all dharmas, or six when its meaning is nirvāṇa, and it too occurs on every level. The other two discriminating knowledges are conventional knowledge.

## Logical Determination

VAK 7.39 is a clean lesson in **orthogonal classification**.

A cognitive capacity is not exhausted by naming its object. It must also be classified by:

```text
object-support
    what it knows

cognitive constitution
    which knowledge-types constitute it

level-range
    where it can occur
```

Thus:

```text
same object-field
    does not determine
same cognitive constitution

same cognitive type
    does not determine
same level-range
```

This is a strong methodological discipline for the Rational Kośa: capacities must be located in a matrix rather than flattened into single labels.

## Transition to VAK 7.40

VAK 7.39 has completed the knowledge-composition and universal level-range of `pratibhāna` and `artha`, while identifying `dharma` and `nirukti` as conventional knowledge.

VAK 7.40 now asks where those latter two can actually occur:

```text
dharma-pratisaṃvid
    desire-domain + four dhyānas

nirukti-pratisaṃvid
    desire-domain + first dhyāna
```

It will then add an important possession rule: one does not possess the pratisaṃvids incompletely.
