# VAK_7.23 — Bhāṣya

## Kārikā

> saptabhūmijayābhijñākopyāptyākīrṇabhāvite /
> ānantaryapatheṣūrdhvaṃ muktimārgāṣṭake 'pi ca // VAK_7.23 //

**Best current translation:**  
In the conquest of the seven levels, in the superknowledges, in attainment of the immovable state, and in mixed cultivation, seven knowledges are cultivated on the uninterrupted paths; and above that, also on the eight paths of liberation.

**Kārikā analysis:** [`VAK_7.23.md`](./VAK_7.23.md)

## Natural Argument

### 1. The governing rule is carried forward

**Voice: Vasubandhu**

The phrase `saptajñānāni bhāvyante` — “seven knowledges are cultivated” — is supplied from the preceding verse.

VAK 7.23 therefore does not introduce a new numerical principle. It specifies several additional path-settings in which sevenfold cultivation occurs.

The Bhāṣya immediately defines the “seven levels”:

```text
four dhyānas
+ three ārūpya attainments
= seven levels
```

Their “conquest” (`jaya`) is explicitly glossed as:

```text
vairāgya
    detachment / dispassion
```

Thus `saptabhūmijaya` means detachment with respect to those seven meditative levels.

### 2. Four settings of sevenfold cultivation

The Bhāṣya names four situations in which seven knowledges are cultivated on uninterrupted paths (`ānantaryamārga`):

```text
1. detachment from the seven levels
2. the five superknowledges
3. realization / attainment of the immovable state
4. mixed cultivation of dhyāna
```

The text says that for the trainee (`śaikṣa`), in all the relevant uninterrupted paths belonging to these settings, seven knowledges are cultivated.

### 3. Present cognition depends on whether the path is worldly or transworldly

If the Path of Cultivation is worldly:

```text
saṃvṛtijñāna
    conventional knowledge
    is present
```

If it is transworldly:

```text
one of the four anvayajñānas
or
one of two dharmajñānas
```

is present.

Thus once again the cognition actually occurring in the present is not identical with the full cultivated set.

### 4. Special substitution at attainment of the immovable state

A decisive qualification follows.

At realization of the immovable state (`akopyaprativedha`), conventional knowledge is **not** cultivated.

Reason:

```text
bhavāgrāpratipakṣatvāt
    because it is not a counteragent to Bhavāgra
    the summit of existence
```

Therefore the seventh knowledge here is not `saṃvṛtijñāna`.

The Bhāṣya says:

```text
jayajñāna
    is to be understood as the seventh
```

So the number seven is preserved, but its internal composition changes according to function.

### 5. The eight liberation paths in detachment from Bhavāgra

After detachment from the seven lower levels, the practitioner proceeds to detachment from `bhavāgra`.

In the eight liberation paths (`vimuktimārga`) there, seven knowledges are cultivated:

```text
dharmajñāna
anvayajñāna
duḥkhajñāna
samudayajñāna
nirodhajñāna
mārgajñāna
paracittajñāna
```

Conventional knowledge is again excluded:

```text
saṃvṛtijñānaṃ na bhāvyate /
bhavāgrāpratipakṣatvāt /
```

because it cannot function as the counteragent to Bhavāgra.

The seventh member is therefore `paracittajñāna`.

The cognition presently occurring in these liberation paths is one among:

```text
four anvayajñānas
or
two dharmajñānas
```

## New Technical Terms

- **saptabhūmijaya** — conquest of the seven levels
  - **status:** kārikā term explicitly glossed in the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** means detachment (`vairāgya`) with respect to the four dhyānas and first three ārūpya levels

- **akopyaprativedha** — realization / penetration of the immovable state
  - **status:** technically important in the Bhāṣya
  - **function:** marks the path-setting in which conventional knowledge drops from the cultivated sevenfold set

- **vyavakīrṇabhāvita-dhyāna** — dhyāna cultivated in mixed form
  - **status:** Bhāṣya path terminology
  - **function:** one of the settings in which seven knowledges are cultivated

- **jayajñāna** — knowledge of conquest
  - **status:** introduced here as replacement seventh member
  - **function:** substitutes for conventional knowledge in attainment of the immovable state

- **bhavāgrāpratipakṣatva** — capacity to act as counteragent to Bhavāgra
  - **status:** Bhāṣya explanatory criterion
  - **function:** determines whether a knowledge can belong to the cultivated set in the highest-domain path

## Doctrinal Determination

VAK 7.23 establishes that the formula “seven knowledges cultivated” is not a rigid list.

The number is stable across several path-settings, but the membership is controlled by function.

```text
ordinary sevenfold cultivation
    includes saṃvṛtijñāna

but

where opposition to Bhavāgra is required
    saṃvṛtijñāna is excluded
```

The path-system therefore uses a functional criterion:

```text
Can this knowledge operate as the relevant counteragent?
```

If not, it cannot be counted merely because it appeared in the previous sevenfold list.

### Stable number, variable internal composition

This yields at least three sevenfold configurations across VAK 7.22–7.23:

```text
A. desire-associated Path of Cultivation
    six pure knowledges
    + conventional knowledge

B. attainment of the immovable state
    six core knowledges
    + knowledge of conquest

C. liberation paths of detachment from Bhavāgra
    six core knowledges
    + knowledge of another's mind
```

The number seven therefore expresses a structural breadth of cultivation, not a single immutable inventory.

### Present cognition and cultivated field remain distinct

The Bhāṣya again separates:

```text
what is presently occurring
from
what is being cultivated
```

A worldly path has conventional knowledge presently occurring.
A transworldly path has one of a restricted set of pure knowledges presently occurring.
Yet the cultivated field may include seven determinations.

This confirms that `bhāvanā` is not reducible to the identity of the current cognition.

## Translation Consequences

The Kārikā-pass translation is broadly sound, but the Bhāṣya adds two controls that should govern later revision:

1. `jaya` in `saptabhūmijaya` should be understood specifically as **detachment / conquest through vairāgya**.
2. “seven knowledges” must not be presented as one fixed list across the whole verse.

The phrase is numerically stable but functionally variable.

## Logical Determination

The most important logical result is:

```text
quantity of cultivated determinations
    can remain constant
while
quality / membership changes
```

Thus numerical identity does not imply conceptual identity.

The decisive mediating principle is counteractive function (`pratipakṣatva`).

```text
path-condition
    ↓
required counteraction
    ↓
eligible knowledge-species
    ↓
resulting cultivated set
```

This is a strong example of rule-governed substitution inside a stable structural form.

The path does not preserve determinations abstractly. It preserves only those that remain adequate to the work required at that stage.

## Organon Contact Point

Light contact only:

VAK 7.23 provides a useful model of a system whose **cardinality remains invariant while its determinate membership changes according to function**.

```text
same structural capacity
    sevenfold cultivation

but

different stage
    → different admissible seventh member
```

This is not arbitrary replacement. The replacement is generated by the path-task itself.

In particular:

```text
saṃvṛtijñāna
    is not discarded because it is “lower” in the abstract

it is excluded
    because it cannot perform the required opposition to Bhavāgra
```

The criterion is therefore practical-logical adequacy.

No larger Organon identification is imposed here.
