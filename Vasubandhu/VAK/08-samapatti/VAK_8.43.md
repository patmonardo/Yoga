# VAK_8.43

## Sanskrit

इति कण्ठगतप्राणं विदित्वा शासनं मुनेः ।
बलकालं मलानां च न प्रमाद्यं मुमुक्षुभिः ॥

## IAST

`iti kaṇṭha-gata-prāṇaṃ viditvā śāsanaṃ muneḥ /`

`bala-kālaṃ malānāṃ ca na pramādyaṃ mumukṣubhiḥ //`

## Padaccheda

`iti | kaṇṭha-gata-prāṇam | viditvā | śāsanam | muneḥ | bala-kālam | malānām | ca | na | pramādyam | mumukṣubhiḥ`

## Literal Translation

Knowing thus that the Sage's Teaching has its breath lodged in its throat, and that this is the time of strength for the defilements, those who seek liberation must not be negligent.

## Light Commentary

The final verse turns the preceding lament into an urgent practical injunction. The Teaching is imagined as being near death, while the defilements have entered their season of strength.

For that reason, **mumukṣus**—those who seek liberation—must not fall into **pramāda**, negligence or heedlessness. The chapter therefore closes by converting its survey of meditation, attainment, and decline into a direct demand for vigilance.
