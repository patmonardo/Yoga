# VAK_8.38

## Sanskrit

हेतुकर्मबलाद्धात्वोरारूप्योत्पादनं द्वयोः ।
ध्यानानां रूपधातौ तु ताभ्यां धर्मतयापि च ॥

## IAST

hetu-karma-balād dhātvor ārūpyotpādanaṃ dvayoḥ /
dhyānānāṃ rūpadhātau tu tābhyāṃ dharmatayāpi ca //

## Padaccheda

hetu-karma-balāt | dhātvoḥ | ārūpya-utpādanam | dvayoḥ | dhyānānām | rūpa-dhātau | tu | tābhyām | dharmatayā | api | ca

## Literal Translation

The production of the formless attainments in the two realms occurs through the power of cause and karma. But the Dhyānas in the form realm arise through those two and also by the regularity of dharmas.

## Philosophical Translation

The arising of the formless attainments is conditioned by causal preparation and karmic force in the two relevant realms. The Dhyānas of the form realm arise not only through cause and karma, but also through the intrinsic regularity of the dharmic order. The verse therefore distinguishes cultivated acquisition from a lawful mode of arising proper to the structure of the realm itself.

## Technical Vocabulary

- **hetu** — cause; here the causal preparation supporting attainment
- **karma-bala** — the force of karma
- **ārūpya** — formless attainment or formless state
- **utpādana** — production or arising
- **dhātu** — realm or domain
- **dhyāna** — determinate meditative analysis
- **rūpa-dhātu** — the form realm
- **dharmatā** — the regularity, intrinsic law, or suchness of dharmas

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse classifies the arising of higher meditative states by their operative conditions. The formless attainments are produced through causal and karmic force in the two relevant realms. The Dhyānas in the form realm may arise through those same conditions and additionally through dharmatā, the regularity proper to the dharmic order.

This is a statement about the conditions of production, not a definition of Samādhi or Samāpatti.

## Logical Determination

The verse distinguishes three explanatory grounds:

1. **hetu** — causal preparation,
2. **karma** — accumulated force or efficacy,
3. **dharmatā** — lawful regularity intrinsic to the domain.

Thus not every arising is explained merely by deliberate practice. Some attainments belong to a structured order whose regularity itself contributes to their manifestation.

## Organon Interpretation

The important logical point is the difference between an externally prepared result and a state arising according to the immanent law of its sphere.

Cause and karma explain why a particular practitioner reaches a state. Dharmatā explains why that state can arise as a lawful possibility of the form realm at all.

This is still practical classification. We should not force the verse into a final equation among Dhyāna, Samādhi, and Samāpatti before the bhāṣya pass.
