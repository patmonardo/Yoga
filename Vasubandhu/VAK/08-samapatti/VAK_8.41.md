# VAK_8.41

## Sanskrit

निमीलिते शास्तरि लोकचक्षुषि क्षयं गते साक्षिजने च भूयसा ।
अदृष्टतत्त्वैर्निरवग्रहैः कृतं कुतार्किकैः शासनमेतदाकुलम् ॥

## IAST

nimīlite śāstari lokacakṣuṣi kṣayaṃ gate sākṣijane ca bhūyasā /
adṛṣṭatattvair niravagrahaiḥ kṛtaṃ kutārkikaiḥ śāsanam etad ākulam //

## Padaccheda

nimīlite | śāstari | loka-cakṣuṣi | kṣayam | gate | sākṣi-jane | ca | bhūyasā | adṛṣṭa-tattvaiḥ | niravagrahaiḥ | kṛtam | ku-tārkikaiḥ | śāsanam | etat | ākulam

## Literal Translation

When the Teacher, the Eye of the World, had closed his eyes, and when the community of direct witnesses had for the most part passed away, this Teaching was thrown into confusion by bad reasoners who had not seen reality and who lacked restraint.

## Light Commentary

The verse laments the historical condition of the Dharma after the Buddha and the direct witnesses were gone.

- **śāstṛ / lokacakṣus** — the Teacher as the Eye of the World
- **sākṣijana** — those who directly witnessed or realized the teaching
- **adṛṣṭatattva** — those who had not seen reality
- **kutārkika** — bad reasoners or sophistical disputants
- **ākula** — confused, disordered, disturbed

The contrast is simple:

- direct Seeing and disciplined transmission
- versus reasoning detached from realization
