# VAkK 8.19

## Sanskrit

व्युत्क्रान्तकसमापत्तिर्विसभागतृतीयगा ।
स्वाधोभूम्याश्रया एव ध्यानारूप्याः वृथाधरम् ॥ VAkK_8.19 ॥

## IAST

vyutkrāntaka-samāpattir visabhāga-tṛtīya-gā /
svādhobhūmyāśrayā eva dhyānārūpyāḥ vṛthādharam // VAkK_8.19 //

## Source Caution

The source prints the compounds continuously. They are separated here for analysis. The final expression `vṛthādharam` is compact and is best understood through the bhāṣya as stating the uselessness of a lower attainment for one established above it.

## Grammar

- **vyutkrāntaka-samāpattiḥ** — nominative singular feminine, “crossed attainment” or “transitional attainment.”
- **visabhāga-tṛtīya-gā** — nominative singular feminine, “going to the third [level], dissimilar in type.”
- **svādhobhūmyāśrayāḥ** — nominative plural, “having as support beings of their own level or a lower level.”
- **dhyānārūpyāḥ** — nominative plural, “the dhyānas and the formless attainments.”
- **vṛthā adharam** — “the lower is useless,” that is, a lower attainment has no practical function for one established above it.

## Literal Translation

Crossed attainment goes to the third level of a dissimilar type. The dhyānas and formless attainments have as their support beings of their own level or of a lower level. A lower attainment is useless.

## Philosophical Translation

Crossed attainment is a controlled transition to the third station by passing over the intervening level and changing the mode of attainment. Dhyāna and ārūpya attainments can be produced by beings established either on their own ground or on a lower ground; but once a higher ground has been mastered, returning to a lower attainment has no liberative use.

## Technical Vocabulary

- **vyutkrāntaka-samāpatti** — crossed, skipped, or transitional attainment.
- **visabhāga** — dissimilar in class or mode.
- **tṛtīya-gā** — going to the third, after passing over one intervening level.
- **āśraya** — support; the embodied or existential basis capable of producing an attainment.
- **svabhūmi** — one’s own level.
- **adhobhūmi** — a lower level.

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse completes the definition begun in VAkK 8.18.

Crossed attainment is not arbitrary jumping. The practitioner first masters the eight grounds in ordinary forward and reverse sequence. Only then can one pass over an intervening ground and enter the third station in a different mode.

The second half distinguishes the level of the practitioner from the level of the attainment:

1. a being may produce an attainment belonging to its own level;
2. a being on a lower level may produce a higher attainment;
3. a being already established above gains no useful result by producing a lower attainment.

Thus an attainment may exceed its present existential support, but a lower attainment does not advance one who already possesses the higher.

## Logical Determination

The path has now acquired two relations:

```text
support relation
    lower or same level
        → may produce higher attainment

utility relation
    higher attainment mastered
        → lower attainment adds nothing
```

The system is therefore not merely hierarchical. It is directional. Lower grounds remain causal supports for ascent, but once sublated into a higher possession they no longer function as independent goals.

## Organon Light

VAkK 8.19 expresses a simple but important principle of staged Idea:

```text
the lower can support the production of the higher,
but the higher does not require regression to the lower.
```

Crossed attainment is possible only after the whole ordinary sequence has been mastered. Freedom of transition is therefore produced by disciplined possession of order, not by contempt for order.

This remains a Samāpatti doctrine: the concern is which attained configuration can be entered from which support, and with what practical use.
