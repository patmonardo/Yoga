# VAK_8.26

## Sanskrit

आलम्बेते अशैक्षं द्वौ शून्यतश्चाप्यनित्यतः ।
आनिमित्तानिमित्तस्तु शान्ततोऽसंख्यया क्षयम् ॥

## IAST

ālambete aśaikṣaṃ dvau śūnyataścāpyanityataḥ /
ānimittānimittastu śāntato 'saṃkhyayā kṣayam //

## Padaccheda

ālambete | aśaikṣam | dvau | śūnyataḥ | ca | api | anityataḥ | ānimitta-animitttaḥ | tu | śāntataḥ | asaṃkhyayā | kṣayam

## Literal Translation

Two take an aśaikṣa Recognition as object: one under emptiness, the other under impermanence. Signlessness-of-signlessness, however, takes cessation through non-discrimination as object under the aspect of peace.

## Philosophical Translation

Emptiness-of-Emptiness recognizes perfected Emptiness Recognition itself as empty. Non-Direction-of-Non-Direction recognizes perfected Non-Directed Recognition itself as impermanent. Signlessness-of-Signlessness recognizes cessation through non-discrimination under peace.

## Technical Vocabulary

- **ālambete** — the two take as object-support
- **aśaikṣa** — beyond training; perfected
- **śūnyataḥ** — under emptiness
- **anityataḥ** — under impermanence
- **ānimitta-ānimitta** — signlessness-of-signlessness
- **śāntataḥ** — under peace
- **kṣaya** — cessation / exhaustion

## Doctrinal Determination

The three reflexive Recognitions prevent the liberated Recognitions from becoming new objects of attachment. Emptiness cannot harden into a substance; non-direction cannot become a permanent possession; signlessness cannot become a new sign.

## Logical Determination

The first triad frees Recognition from the conditioned field. The second triad frees Recognition from attachment to its own liberated form.

This is not "absorption squared." It is Recognition becoming analytical about its own attained form.

## Organon Interpretation

Recognition turns upon Recognition. Yet this reflexive act remains analytical: it speaks about attained Recognition rather than Pure Reason speaking immediately for itself.

Yoga Samādhi goes further. In *artha-mātra-nirbhāsa* with *svarūpa-śūnyam iva*, the yogin's analytic form—full of objective determinations—no longer stands independently. The determinations are sublated into the determinate Concept, and meaning speaks with its own authority.
