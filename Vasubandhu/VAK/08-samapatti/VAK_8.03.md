# VAK_8.03 — Formless Naming and the Arising of Form

## Sanskrit

### Devanāgarī

```text
विभूतरूपसंज्ञाख्याः सह सामन्तकैस्त्रिभिः ।
नारूप्ये रूपसद्भावः रूपोत्पत्तिस्तु चित्ततः ॥ ८.३ ॥
```

### IAST

```text
vibhūtarūpasaṃjñākhyāḥ saha sāmantakais tribhiḥ /
nārūpye rūpasadbhāvaḥ rūpotpattis tu cittataḥ // VAK_8.03 //
```

## Source Caution

The first compound is densely compressed:

```text
vibhūta-rūpa-saṃjñā-ākhyāḥ
```

This first pass takes it as naming the formless attainments through the disappearance or transcendence of form-perception. The precise distribution of the designation among the principal attainments and the three preparatory states belongs to the bhāṣya pass.

The second half states two different claims that must not be collapsed:

```text
in the formless domain
    form is not present

nevertheless
    later arising of form occurs from mind
```

The verse therefore distinguishes present constitution from subsequent causal emergence.

## Grammar

The first half supplies a designation:

```text
[vibhūta-rūpa-saṃjñā]-ākhyāḥ
    they are named through the disappearance
    of form-perception

saha sāmantakais tribhiḥ
    together with three preparatory attainments
```

The understood subject is the formless attainments carried forward from VAK 8.02.

The second half is an adversative construction:

```text
na ārūpye rūpa-sadbhāvaḥ
    there is no presence of form
    in the formless domain

rūpa-utpattiḥ tu cittataḥ
    but the arising of form is from mind
```

The ablative `cittataḥ` marks source or ground:

```text
citta
    → rūpotpatti
```

This does not assert that form is secretly present in the formless domain. It asserts that the continuity capable of producing form is retained on the side of mind.

## Literal Translation

They are named from the disappearance of form-perception, together with the three preparatory attainments. In the formless domain there is no presence of form; but the arising of form is from mind.

## Philosophical Translation

The formless attainments receive their designation from the removal of form-perception, together with their three neighboring preparatory states. Their present constitution contains no form. Yet the absence of form at that level does not abolish the possibility of form altogether: form can arise subsequently from the mental continuity.

## Technical Vocabulary

### vibhūta-rūpa-saṃjñā

```text
rūpa-saṃjñā
    perception or representation of form

vibhūta
    disappeared
    absent
    surpassed
```

The designation is therefore privative:

```text
formless attainment
    is named through what is no longer operative
```

The verse does not yet define each of the four formless attainments positively. VAK 8.04 will provide their determinate names.

### sāmantaka

```text
sāmantaka
    neighboring
    adjacent
    preparatory
```

A sāmantaka is not merely another principal attainment. It is the threshold from which entry into a higher level becomes possible.

The presence of three here should be retained without forcing a complete doctrinal distribution before the bhāṣya is consulted.

### rūpa-sadbhāva

```text
rūpa-sadbhāva
    the presence
    or actual existence
    of form
```

The negation is local and determinate:

```text
no form
    in the constitution
    of the formless domain
```

It is not the universal proposition:

```text
form can never arise again
```

### cittataḥ

```text
cittataḥ
    from mind
    from the mental continuity
```

The phrase preserves causal continuity across a change of domain.

```text
form absent now
    ≠
form impossible later
```

The mind-stream can become the source-condition for renewed embodiment.

## Doctrinal Determination

VAK 8.03 establishes three points.

### 1. The formless attainments are named privatively

Their designation depends on the disappearance of form-perception.

```text
form-perception operative
    lower field

form-perception removed
    formless field
```

### 2. The formless domain is genuinely without form

The verse denies `rūpa-sadbhāva` in the formless domain.

```text
ārūpya
    four aggregates
    no material aggregate
```

This continues VAK 8.02, which identified the formless attainments as four-aggregate configurations.

### 3. Form may arise again from mind

The absence of form in the present attainment does not interrupt the causal series.

```text
formless mental continuity
    ↓
subsequent arising of form
```

The verse therefore distinguishes:

| Question | Determination |
|---|---|
| Is form present in the formless domain? | No |
| Can form arise subsequently? | Yes |
| From what source? | From mind |

## Logical Determination

The verse separates **constitution** from **genesis**.

```text
constitution
    what determinations are presently contained?

genesis
    from what can a later determination arise?
```

Applied here:

```text
formless domain as constituted
    contains no form

mental continuity as ground
    can produce renewed form
```

This blocks two opposite errors.

### Error 1 — concealed materialism

```text
form must remain secretly present
    because it later reappears
```

The verse rejects this by denying the presence of form in the formless domain.

### Error 2 — absolute discontinuity

```text
if form is absent now
    form can never arise later
```

The verse rejects this by grounding renewed form in mind.

The logical structure is therefore:

```text
absence at one level
    does not entail
impossibility of later production
```

## Organon Light

This kārikā pass should remain restrained. The immediate point is not yet a Hegel-clean reconstruction of the form and formless meditations. It is the precise doctrinal distinction:

```text
rūpa absent as present constituent
    while
citta remains capable of generating rūpa
```

In platform language:

```text
current dataset state
    contains no rūpa-instance

transition rule
    permits a later rūpa-state
    from citta-continuity
```

This is the chapter's first explicit warning that the classification of a state must not be confused with the full range of transitions available from that state.

The broader grahya–grahaṇa–grahītṛ reconstruction is therefore held in reserve for the later Organon pass.
