# VAK_8.42

## Sanskrit

गतेऽथ शान्तिं परमां स्वयंभुवि स्वयंभुवः शासनधूर्धरेषु च ।
जगत्यनाथे गणघातिभिर्मतैर्निरङ्कुशैः स्वैरमिहाद्य चर्यते ॥

## IAST

`gate 'tha śāntiṃ paramāṃ svayaṃbhuvi svayaṃbhuvaḥ śāsana-dhūrdhareṣu ca /`

`jagaty anāthe gaṇa-ghātibhir matair niraṅkuśaiḥ svairam ihādya caryate //`

## Padaccheda

`gate | atha | śāntim | paramām | svayaṃbhuvi | svayaṃbhuvaḥ | śāsana-dhūrdhareṣu | ca | jagati | anāthe | gaṇa-ghātibhiḥ | mataiḥ | niraṅkuśaiḥ | svairam | iha | adya | caryate`

## Literal Translation

Then, when the Self-Arisen had entered supreme peace, and when the chief bearers of the Self-Arisen's Teaching had also departed, the world was left without a protector. Today, here, unrestrained doctrines destructive of the community move about freely.

## Light Commentary

The verse continues the lament of VAK_8.41. The Teacher has entered final peace, the principal custodians of the Teaching are gone, and the world is described as **anātha**—without a protector.

The final accusation is severe: doctrines that damage or fragment the community now circulate without restraint.
