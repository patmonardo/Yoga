# VAK_8.37

## Sanskrit

निरोध उक्तः वैराग्यप्रयोगाप्तं तु शेषितम् ।
त्रिधात्वाश्रयमारूप्यसंज्ञं शेषं मनुष्यजम् ॥

## IAST

nirodha uktaḥ vairāgya-prayogāptaṃ tu śeṣitam /
tridhātv-āśrayam ārūpya-saṃjñaṃ śeṣaṃ manuṣya-jam //

## Padaccheda

nirodhaḥ | uktaḥ | vairāgya-prayoga-āptam | tu | śeṣitam | tri-dhātu-āśrayam | ārūpya-saṃjñam | śeṣam | manuṣya-jam

## Literal Translation

Cessation has been explained. What remains is attained either through dispassion or through application. It has support in the three realms; what is called formless is the remainder, and it is generated in human existence.

## Philosophical Translation

The cessation-attainment has already been classified. The remaining higher practices are acquired either by the path of dispassion or by deliberate meditative application. Their supports extend across the three realms, while the specifically formless attainments constitute the remaining class and are first generated in human existence.

## Technical Vocabulary

- **nirodha** — cessation; here, the cessation-attainment already discussed
- **vairāgya** — dispassion; release from attachment through detachment from the lower field
- **prayoga** — deliberate application or meditative effort
- **āpta** — obtained or acquired
- **tri-dhātu-āśraya** — supported in the three realms
- **ārūpya-saṃjña** — designated as formless
- **manuṣya-ja** — generated in human existence

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse distinguishes two modes of acquisition: attainment through dispassion and attainment through deliberate application. It also classifies the supports of these practices by realm and preserves the special role of human existence as the place in which the formless practices are initially generated.

## Logical Determination

The practical field is now classified by genesis rather than merely by object. One path proceeds through withdrawal from a lower determination; the other through positive cultivation. The resulting attainments are then distributed according to their support across the three realms.

## Organon Interpretation

The verse marks a shift from the structure of a practiced field to the conditions under which that field is acquired. Dispassion and application are two distinct practical mediations: one frees by loosening attachment to the prior field, while the other produces a new stable capacity through deliberate work.

This is not yet a reason to identify every attainment with either Samāpatti or Samādhi. The verse is classifying how practices are obtained and where they are supported.
