# VAK_8.40

## Sanskrit

काश्मीरवैभाषिकनीतिसिद्धः प्रायो मयायं कथितोऽभिधर्मः ।
यद्दुर्गृहीतं तदिहास्मदागः सद्धर्मनीतौ मुनयः प्रमाणम् ॥

## IAST

kāśmīra-vaibhāṣika-nīti-siddhaḥ prāyo mayāyaṃ kathito 'bhidharmaḥ /
yad durgṛhītaṃ tad ihāsmad-āgaḥ saddharma-nītau munayaḥ pramāṇam //

## Padaccheda

kāśmīra-vaibhāṣika-nīti-siddhaḥ | prāyaḥ | mayā | ayam | kathitaḥ | abhidharmaḥ | yat | durgṛhītam | tat | iha | asmat-āgaḥ | sat-dharma-nītau | munayaḥ | pramāṇam

## Literal Translation

This Abhidharma has been taught by me chiefly as established according to the method of the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣikas. Whatever has been poorly understood here is our fault; in the guidance of the True Dharma, the sages are the authority.

## Light Determination

Vasubandhu explicitly marks the doctrinal standpoint of the work as predominantly Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika, while refusing to identify his own exposition with faultless authority.

The contrast is simple:

- **our error** — whatever has been badly grasped or expressed
- **the sages as authority** — the measure in matters concerning the True Dharma

The verse is therefore both a statement of school affiliation and a formal act of intellectual humility.
