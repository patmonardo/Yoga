# VAK_8.34

## Sanskrit

कामाप्तदृश्यविषयाः प्रथमाः ये त्वारूपिणः ।
तेऽन्वयज्ञानपक्षोर्ध्वस्वभूदुःखादिगोचराः ॥

## IAST

kāmāpta-dṛśya-viṣayāḥ prathamāḥ ye tv arūpiṇaḥ /
te 'nvaya-jñāna-pakṣordhva-svabhū-duḥkhādi-gocarāḥ //

## Padaccheda

kāma-āpta-dṛśya-viṣayāḥ | prathamāḥ | ye | tu | arūpiṇaḥ | te | anvaya-jñāna-pakṣa | ūrdhva | sva-bhū | duḥkha-ādi-gocarāḥ

## Literal Translation

The first [Deliverances] have visible objects belonging to the desire realm. But those that are formless have, on the side of subsequent knowledge, as their field the suffering and the remaining truths of their own and higher planes.

## Philosophical Translation

The first Deliverances operate upon visible form within the desire realm. The formless Deliverances no longer take visible form as their field; their objects are the truths beginning with suffering, as they apply to their own and superior planes, in the mode associated with subsequent knowledge.

## Technical Vocabulary

- **kāma-āpta** — belonging to or included within the desire realm
- **dṛśya-viṣaya** — a visible object-field
- **prathamāḥ** — the first Deliverances, here the form-oriented members of the series
- **arūpin** — formless; belonging to the immaterial attainments
- **anvaya-jñāna** — subsequent or inferential knowledge; the exact Abhidharma scope is to be confirmed in the bhāṣya pass
- **sva-bhū** — one’s own plane or level of existence
- **ūrdhva-bhū** — a higher plane
- **duḥkha-ādi** — suffering and the remaining noble truths
- **gocara** — cognitive range, object-field, or domain of operation

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse distinguishes the Deliverances by object-field.

1. The initial Deliverances remain connected with visible form in the desire realm.
2. The formless Deliverances take no visible form as object.
3. Their cognitive field consists of the truths beginning with suffering as they pertain to their own and higher planes.
4. The phrase **anvaya-jñāna-pakṣa** marks a technical relation to subsequent knowledge that should be resolved more exactly from the commentary.

The verse therefore does not define Deliverance merely by subjective intensity. Each practice is classified by what kind of field it can enter and master.

## Logical Determination

The series advances by transforming the object-domain:

```text
visible form in the desire realm
    →
formless fields
    →
truths applying to one’s own and higher planes
```

Deliverance is therefore not simple withdrawal from objects. It is a disciplined reconfiguration of the range within which cognition can operate without domination by that range.

## Organon Interpretation

The verse is best read as practical taxonomy rather than as a final theory of Samāpatti or Samādhi.

The first Deliverances work within a determinate visible world. The formless Deliverances alter the very type of objectivity available to practice. The shift is not from “something” to mere blankness, but from visible form to a subtler field structured by the truths beginning with suffering.

Thus the Deliverances show how meditative practice changes not only the content of experience but the scale and category of the field into which cognition can be absorbed.
