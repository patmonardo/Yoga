# VAK_8.42_bhasya

## Kārikā

### IAST

`gate 'tha śāntiṃ paramāṃ svayaṃbhuvi svayaṃbhuvaḥ śāsanadhūrdhareṣu ca /`
`jagaty anāthe gaṇaghātibhir matair niraṅkuśaiḥ svairam ihādya caryate //`

### Best Current Translation

Then, when the Self-Arisen had gone to supreme peace, and when the chief bearers of the Self-Arisen's Teaching had also departed, the world was left without a protector; today unrestrained doctrines destructive of the community move about freely here.

## Bhāṣya Translation

Then, when the Self-Arisen had entered supreme peace, and when the principal bearers of the Self-Arisen's Teaching had also gone, the world was left without a protector. Today, here, unrestrained doctrines destructive of the community move about freely.

## Natural Argument

VAK 8.42 intensifies the previous verse. The problem is no longer only that unrealized reasoners confuse the teaching. The disappearance of the Buddha and of the major bearers of the teaching leaves the world `anātha`—without a protector or guardian.

In that condition, doctrines (`mata`) capable of damaging or fragmenting the community circulate `niraṅkuśa`, without restraint, and `svairam`, freely or arbitrarily.

The verse is polemical, but its structural point is clear: when the living centers of authority and realization disappear, doctrinal plurality can become not simply diversity but a force of disintegration.

## Argument Reconstruction

```text
Buddha departs
+ principal bearers depart
        ↓
world becomes anātha
        ↓
no effective restraint on destructive doctrines
        ↓
fragmentation / injury to the community
```

## New Technical Terms

- **svayaṃbhū** — Self-Arisen; epithet of the Buddha.
  - **status:** poetic designation in the Kārikā.
  - **function:** identifies the Teacher in the final lament.

- **śāsanadhūrdhara** — bearer of the burden/yoke of the Teaching; principal custodian.
  - **status:** striking compound in the Kārikā.
  - **function:** names those who sustain responsibility for the Teaching after the Teacher.

- **anātha** — without protector, guardian, or refuge.
  - **status:** Kārikā term.
  - **function:** describes the condition of the world after the disappearance of authoritative bearers.

- **gaṇaghātin mata** — doctrine destructive of the community/group.
  - **status:** Kārikā expression; source spelling should be watched across editions.
  - **function:** names teachings that produce communal injury or fragmentation.

- **niraṅkuśa** — unrestrained, unchecked.
  - **status:** Kārikā term.
  - **function:** emphasizes absence of effective constraint.

- **svairam** — freely, arbitrarily, at will.
  - **status:** Kārikā adverb.
  - **function:** characterizes unconstrained circulation of doctrine.

## Voice

**Voice:** Vasubandhu, in the concluding lament.

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse is not itself a technical doctrinal classification. It is a diagnosis of historical vulnerability.

The True Dharma described in VAK 8.39 depends upon bearers. Once those bearers disappear, the problem is not merely archival loss. The interpretive and communal field itself loses regulating centers.

Thus the final sequence now reads:

```text
living transmission + realization
        ↓
loss of realized witnesses
        ↓
unrestrained reasoning
        ↓
loss of custodians
        ↓
disordering doctrines proliferate
```

## Translation Consequences

- `anātha` should retain the force of “without protector/guardian,” not merely “lonely.”
- `śāsanadhūrdhara` is stronger than “teacher”: these are bearers of the burden of sustaining the Teaching.
- `mata` means doctrine/view/opinion here; the negative force comes from the modifiers, not from the word itself.
- The transmitted source reads `gunaghātibhir` in the local e-text line; the expected compound is commonly understood as `gaṇaghātibhir`, “destructive of the community/group.” The source form should not be silently normalized in quotation.

## Logical Determination

This verse supplies a social counterpart to VAK 8.41's epistemic warning:

```text
epistemic failure
    reasoning detached from realization

social failure
    doctrine detached from responsible bearers
```

The two failures reinforce one another.

## Light Organon Contact

For later system work, the useful point is not to import the polemic wholesale but to notice the dependency structure: a system is preserved not only by correct propositions but by institutions, practices, and agents capable of maintaining criteria of correction.

That is a later problem of system governance. Here it remains Vasubandhu's historical warning about a teaching whose custodial structure is disappearing.
