# VAK_8.01 — Dhyāna, Rebirth, and the Definition of Samāpatti

## Sanskrit

### Devanāgarī

```text
द्विधा ध्यानानि चत्वारि प्रोक्तास्तदुपपत्तयः ।
समापत्तिः शुभैकाग्र्यं पञ्चस्कन्धास्तु सानुगम् ॥ ८.१ ॥
```

### IAST

```text
dvidhā dhyānāni catvāri proktās tadupapattayaḥ /
samāpattiḥ śubhaikāgryaṃ pañcaskandhās tu sānugam // VAK_8.01 //
```

## Source Caution

The supplied kārikā source reads:

```text
śubhaikāgyraṃ
```

This is treated here as a transposition or typographical corruption of:

```text
śubhaikāgryaṃ
```

The normalized form is grammatically and doctrinally required:

```text
śubha + ekāgrya
    wholesome one-pointedness
```

The verse is highly compressed. In particular, `pañcaskandhās tu sānugam` should not be read as reducing samāpatti to a bare mental factor. The phrase indicates that, when its associated field is included, the meditative attainment is represented through the five aggregates.

This first kārikā pass preserves that structure without importing the full bhāṣya analysis prematurely.

## Grammar

The first half gives a twofold determination of the four dhyānas:

```text
dhyānāni catvāri
    the four dhyānas

dvidhā proktāḥ
    are declared in two ways

samāpatti
    as meditative attainments

upapatti
    as corresponding modes of rebirth
```

The compound `tadupapattayaḥ` resolves as:

```text
tad-upapattayaḥ
    their corresponding rebirths
```

The second half is definitional:

```text
samāpattiḥ
    subject

śubhaikāgryaṃ
    predicate
```

Thus:

```text
samāpatti
    is wholesome one-pointedness
```

The final phrase adds the associated aggregate-field:

```text
pañcaskandhās tu sānugam
    but, together with its accompaniment,
    it is the five aggregates
```

The grammatical compression reflects two standpoints:

```text
samāpatti in its defining mark
    wholesome one-pointedness

samāpatti with its associated constitution
    the five aggregates
```

## Literal Translation

The four dhyānas are declared in two ways: as meditative attainments and as the rebirths corresponding to them. Samāpatti is wholesome one-pointedness; together with its accompaniment, it consists of the five aggregates.

## Philosophical Translation

The four dhyānas must be distinguished according to whether they are considered as actual meditative attainments or as the corresponding forms of existence into which beings are born. As attainment, samāpatti is defined by wholesome one-pointedness. Yet the attained state is not an isolated mental abstraction: taken together with its associated determinations, it is present as a complete five-aggregate configuration.

## Technical Vocabulary

### dhyāna

The verse begins by refusing a univocal use of `dhyāna`.

```text
dhyāna
    as samāpatti
        an attained meditative determination

    as upapatti
        a corresponding mode of rebirth
```

This distinction governs the chapter. The same name may designate:

```text
a path-state
    entered through cultivation

or

a world-state
    entered through rebirth
```

The two are related but must not be conflated.

### samāpatti

`Samāpatti` is formed from:

```text
sam-ā-pad
    to come together
    to enter fully
    to arrive at
```

Its immediate definition here is:

```text
samāpatti
    = śubha-ekāgrya
    = wholesome one-pointedness
```

This is the determinate Being of meditative collectedness: samādhi exists here as an entered, stabilized, and articulable meditative field.

### ekāgrya

```text
eka
    one

agra
    point; summit; leading edge

ekāgrya
    one-pointedness
```

The term does not merely mean numerical singularity. It indicates the gathering of the mental field into one determinate orientation.

The qualification `śubha` is essential:

```text
not every concentration
    is samāpatti in the present technical sense

samāpatti
    is wholesome one-pointedness
```

### upapatti

`Upapatti` here means the mode of arising or rebirth corresponding to a dhyāna-level.

```text
samāpatti
    cultivated entrance into dhyāna

upapatti
    birth within the world corresponding to that dhyāna
```

The distinction is practical and ontological:

```text
attainment
    concerns cultivated entry

rebirth
    concerns the resultant mode of existence
```

### sānugam

```text
sa-anugam
    with what follows it
    with its accompaniment
    with its associated retinue
```

The one-pointed determination does not occur alone. It is accompanied by associated mental and embodied determinations and therefore belongs to an aggregate-complex.

## Doctrinal Determination

VAK 8.01 establishes three foundational distinctions.

### 1. Four dhyānas, two standpoints

| Dhyāna considered as | Determination |
|---|---|
| samāpatti | cultivated meditative attainment |
| upapatti | corresponding mode of rebirth |

The chapter must therefore continually distinguish:

```text
meditative level as practice
    from
meditative level as world
```

### 2. Definition by dominant determination

Samāpatti is defined by its dominant mark:

```text
śubhaikāgrya
    wholesome one-pointedness
```

This is not yet a complete inventory of everything present in an attainment. It identifies the determination by which the state is what it is.

### 3. Constitution by associated aggregates

Taken with its accompaniment, samāpatti is represented by the five aggregates.

```text
defining mark
    one-pointedness

concrete constitution
    aggregate-complex
```

Thus the verse does not identify samāpatti with a detached, purely inward point. The attainment is concretely instantiated through a complete conditioned field.

## Logical Determination

VAK 8.01 opens the chapter through a double division.

### First division: mode of presentation

```text
dhyāna
    ├── samāpatti
    │       cultivated attainment
    │
    └── upapatti
            corresponding rebirth
```

This is a distinction between:

```text
activity entered through practice
    and
existence received as result
```

The dhyāna determination is therefore both:

```text
subjective-path form
    and
objective-world form
```

### Second division: definition and concrete field

```text
samāpatti as defining unity
    śubha-ekāgrya

samāpatti with its accompaniment
    pañca-skandha field
```

The one-pointedness is the dominant universal determination. The aggregate-field is its concrete instantiation.

This gives the opening logical structure:

```text
Universal determination
    wholesome one-pointedness

Concrete existence
    five-aggregate meditative configuration
```

The verse therefore already prevents two reductions:

```text
samāpatti
    is not merely a rebirth-world

samāpatti
    is not merely an isolated mental point
```

It is a cultivated unity that exists concretely through an associated conditioned field.

## Organon Light

The governing distinction for Chapter 8 is:

```text
Samādhi
    = Concept of Samāpatti

Samāpatti
    = Idea of Samādhi
```

VAK 8.01 begins from the side of Samāpatti. It gives the realized meditative field in its first immediate determination:

```text
Samādhi
    as conceptual unity

appears in Samāpatti as
    wholesome one-pointedness
    concretely instantiated
    through the aggregate-field
```

In platform language:

```text
Samādhi
    schema / Concept

Samāpatti
    realized dataset / Idea
```

The analogy must remain controlled. The verse does not yet present the self-comprehension of the field. It specifies the first data structure of that field:

```text
dhyāna
    can be indexed as attainment
    or as rebirth

samāpatti
    has a defining determination
    and an associated aggregate constitution
```

The higher movement remains ahead:

```text
Samādhi comprehending Samāpatti
    Supreme Samādhi
    Knowledge Born from Discrimination
```

That conclusion is not imposed upon VAK 8.01 itself. The present verse establishes the determinate field that such comprehension must later master.
