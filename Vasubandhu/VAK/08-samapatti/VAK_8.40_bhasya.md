# VAK_8.40_bhasya

## Kārikā

### IAST

`kāśmīravaibhāṣikanītisiddhaḥ prāyo mayāyaṃ kathito 'bhidharmaḥ /`
`yad durgṛhītaṃ tad ihāsmadāgaḥ saddharmanītau munayaḥ pramāṇam //`

### Best Current Translation

This Abhidharma has been expounded by me chiefly as established according to the method of the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣikas. Whatever has been badly grasped here is our fault; in the guidance of the True Dharma, the sages are the authority.

Kārikā anchor: `VAK_8.40.md`

## Bhāṣya Source

Pradhan 459|15–460|03:

```text
yo 'yamiha śāstre 'bhidharma uktaḥ kimeṣa eva śāstrābhidharmo deśitaḥ /

kāśmīravaibhāṣikanītisiddhaḥ prāyo mayā 'yaṃ kathito 'bhidharmaḥ /

yadatrāsmābhirdurgṛhītaṃ so 'smākamaparādhaḥ /
saddharmanītau tu punarbuddhā eva pramāṇaṃ buddhaputrāśca /
```

## Bhāṣya Translation

Is the Abhidharma taught in this treatise exactly the Abhidharma taught in the canonical treatises?

This Abhidharma has been explained by me chiefly as established according to the method of the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣikas. Whatever has been badly grasped here is our fault; in the guidance of the True Dharma, the sages are the authority.

For this Abhidharma has been explained by us for the most part as established according to the method and doctrine of the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣikas. Whatever has been badly understood here by us is our own error. But in the guidance of the True Dharma, the Buddhas themselves are the authority, together with the sons of the Buddhas.

## Natural Argument

The question is methodological: does the exposition just completed possess the same authority as the canonical Abhidharma itself?

Vasubandhu answers by distinguishing three levels:

1. the Abhidharma as transmitted in authoritative teaching;
2. the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika method through which most of this treatise has been organized;
3. Vasubandhu's own acts of exposition and understanding, which remain corrigible.

The verse therefore neither disowns the Vaibhāṣika framework nor identifies it with final authority. Vasubandhu explicitly accepts responsibility for misunderstanding (`durgṛhīta`) while relocating ultimate authority in the Buddhas and Buddha-sons.

## Argument Reconstruction

```text
canonical / authoritative Dharma
        ↓
Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika nīti
        ↓
Vasubandhu's exposition
        ↓
possible error remains attributable to expositor
```

Hence:

```text
school method
    ≠
final pramāṇa
```

## New Technical Terms

- **nīti** — method, doctrinal procedure, or line of interpretation.
  - **status:** present in Kārikā; important methodological term.
  - **function:** identifies the interpretive framework governing most of the treatise.

- **durgṛhīta** — badly grasped, misunderstood.
  - **status:** present in Kārikā.
  - **function:** marks corrigible error in the expositor's appropriation of doctrine.

- **aparādha / āgaḥ** — fault, error, offense.
  - **status:** Bhāṣya glosses the verse's `āgaḥ` with `aparādha`.
  - **function:** assigns responsibility for misunderstanding to the author rather than to the Dharma.

- **pramāṇa** — authority / valid measure.
  - **status:** major technical term; here used normatively rather than as a detailed epistemological taxonomy.
  - **function:** identifies the Buddhas and Buddha-sons as the final standard in matters of Saddharma.

## Voice

**Voice:** Vasubandhu explicitly speaking in his own authorial voice.

## Doctrinal Determination

The verse is a formal disclaimer of infallibility. The Kośa is predominantly a Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika exposition, but Vasubandhu refuses to collapse:

```text
Abhidharma
=
Vaibhāṣika interpretation
=
his own formulation
```

Instead, the exposition is acknowledged as mediated and fallible. The authorial stance is therefore critical from within the tradition rather than merely external to it.

## Translation Consequences

- `prāyaḥ` should be preserved as “chiefly / for the most part”; Vasubandhu does not say that every claim belongs straightforwardly to Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika doctrine.
- `nīti` should not be flattened to “opinion.” It denotes an organized method or doctrinal line.
- `durgṛhīta` concerns faulty grasping or interpretation, not necessarily textual corruption.
- `munayaḥ` in the verse is made explicit in the Bhāṣya as Buddhas and Buddha-sons.

## Logical Determination

The closing methodological rule is exceptionally clean:

```text
systematic exposition
    requires a method

method
    does not guarantee infallibility

error
    remains attributable to the interpreter

truth-standard
    exceeds the interpreter and school
```

The Kośa thus ends its doctrinal exposition by placing its own system under a higher criterion.

## Light Organon Contact

For later comparison, this is an important model of disciplined system-building: a system can be extraordinarily precise while still explicitly distinguishing its **construction** from its **criterion of truth**.

That distinction is enough for this pass. We should resist using the verse to vindicate any later philosophical architecture in advance.
