# VAK_4.101 — Division of the Dharma Chakra and Communal Action

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
चक्रभेदः स च मतो जम्बूद्वीपे नवादिभिः ।
कर्मभेदस्त्रिषु द्वीपेष्वष्टभिरधिकैश्च सः ॥ ४.१०१ ॥
```

Here `karman` in `karmabheda` means the Saṅgha's communal action, not the
divider's culpable action or its later maturation.

## 4. Grammar

The first clause identifies the preceding Saṅgha division:

```text
saḥ ca
    cakra-bhedaḥ mataḥ [asti]
    jambū-dvīpe
    nava-ādibhiḥ [bhikṣubhiḥ]
```

The Bhāṣya explains the number as eight monks constituting a Saṅgha and a
ninth as divider; it also requires that the Saṅgha stand in two parties.
It does not specify an equal number of monks in each party.

The second clause introduces a distinct type:

```text
karma-bhedaḥ
    triṣu dvīpeṣu
    aṣṭabhiḥ adhikaiḥ [bhikṣubhiḥ]
    saḥ [bhavati]
```

The Bhāṣya defines its condition as communal actions performed in a divided
manner within one boundary (`sīmā`). The verse gives eight or more monks;
the Bhāṣya does not derive that number as two groups of four.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation
> That is held to be division of the wheel in Jambūdvīpa with nine or more;
> division of communal action occurs in the three continents with eight or
> more.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> That is held to be wheel-division in Jambūdvīpa with nine or more [monks];
> division of communal action occurs in the three continents with eight or more.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> That is held to be division of the wheel in Jambūdvīpa with nine or more; division of communal action occurs in the three continents with eight or more.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

- **`Cakrabheda`** — division of the Dharma Chakra. The Bhāṣya explains
  that the Buddha's Dharma Chakra is divided through displacement of the
  operation of the Path.
- **`Mārgapravṛtti-viṣṭhāpana`** — displacement or disruption of the
  Path's operation; the Bhāṣya's stated ground for identifying this
  Saṅgha division as `cakrabheda`.
- **`Karmabheda`** — division of communal action. In this compound,
  *karman* refers to the Saṅgha's communal act, not to a culpable action
  and its maturation.
- **`Sīmā`** — the shared boundary within which separate performance of
  communal actions constitutes the second type of division.
- **`Navādibhiḥ` / `aṣṭābhir adhikaiḥ`** — nine or more for
  `cakrabheda`; eight or more for `karmabheda`. The Bhāṣya explains the
  former as eight monks constituting a Saṅgha plus a ninth divider; it
  does not specify equal factions or derive the latter as four plus four.

## 8. Logical Determination

```text
Cakrabheda:
    location = Jambūdvīpa
    condition = displacement of the Path's operation
    participants = nine or more monks
    stated structure = eight constitute a Saṅgha; ninth is divider;
                       two parties must be represented

Karmabheda:
    condition = communal action performed in a divided manner
    boundary = one shared sīmā
    location = three continents where the Teaching exists
    participants = eight or more monks
    stated structure = no further numerical distribution specified
```

`Cakrabheda` and `karmabheda` are distinct forms of Saṅgha division; the
second is not simply another name for the first.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya specifies that the first count comprises eight monks who
constitute a Saṅgha and a ninth who divides it. Two parties must be
represented, but their relative size is not specified. For the second
division, the source gives eight or more monks without further deriving
their distribution. The Bhāṣya-based expansion formerly embedded in the study translation is retained here: The division of the Dharma Chakra described in the preceding verses is held to be wheel-division in Jambūdvīpa with nine or more monks; division of communal action occurs in the three continents where the Teaching is established, with eight or more monks.

This passage gives a concrete test for the emerging BEP-protocol reading:
it distinguishes what is divided—the Dharma Chakra's Path-operation or
communal action—and, for `karmabheda`, specifies the shared boundary in
which that action is divided. This is a useful local structural relation,
not yet a complete Base–Essence–Principle mapping of dependent arising.

The source unit begins with the explanation at `[261|23]` after the
reunion statement of 4.100 and ends with the kārikā at `[262|09]`. It
stops before the next question, on the six times when `cakrabheda` does
not occur, at `[262|10]`. The witness phrase `cakrādaścocyate` is
retained and interpreted by context as naming both wheel-division and
Saṅgha-division.

**Former Philosophical Translation.**
> Collective division has distinct forms. In one, the operation of the Path
> within the Dharma Chakra is displaced; in the other, communal action is
> performed in a divided manner within one shared boundary. The two forms
> differ in what is divided, where they occur, and the stated minimum
> number of monks.
