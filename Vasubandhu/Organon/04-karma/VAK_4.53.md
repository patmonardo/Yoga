# VAK_4.53 — Twenty-Two Karmic Possibilities in Intermediate Existence

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
द्वाविंशतिविधं कामेष्वाक्षिपत्यन्तराभवः ।
दृष्टधर्मफलं तच्च निकायो ह्येक एव सः ॥ ४.५३ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```iast
dvāviṃśatividhaṃ kāmeṣv ākṣipaty antarābhavaḥ /
dṛṣṭadharmaphalaṃ tac ca nikāyo hy eka eva saḥ // 4.53 //
```

Padapāṭha-style separation:

```iast
dvāviṃśati-vidham kāmeṣu ākṣipati antarābhavaḥ /
dṛṣṭa-dharma-phalam tat ca nikāyaḥ hi ekaḥ eva saḥ //
```

The Bhāṣya derives the twenty-two kinds from eleven maturation-targets, each
of which may be determinate or indeterminate.

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Morphology | Force in this passage |
|---|---|---|
| `dvāviṃśatividham` | accusative neuter singular compound | twenty-twofold; of twenty-two kinds |
| `kāmeṣu` | locative masculine plural | in the Desire Realm |
| `ākṣipati` | third-person singular present | projects; casts forth |
| `antarābhavaḥ` | nominative masculine singular | intermediate existence |
| `dṛṣṭadharmaphalam` | nominative/accusative neuter singular compound | having its fruit in the present existence |
| `tat` | nominative/accusative neuter singular pronoun | that [karma] |
| `ca` | conjunction | and; also |
| `nikāyaḥ` | nominative masculine singular | class-continuity; rebirth-class |
| `hi` | explanatory particle | for; because |
| `ekaḥ eva` | emphatic nominative phrase | one and the same |
| `saḥ` | nominative masculine singular pronoun | that [continuum] |

**`antarābhava`**

Intermediate existence is not a passive interval. The verse attributes to it
the capacity to produce or project karma with determinate future targets.

**`nikāya` and `nikāyasabhāga`**

The Bhāṣya specifies one rebirth-class continuum projected by one karma. Its
continuity spans intermediate existence and the ten developmental stages that
follow.

**`dṛṣṭadharmaphala`**

The phrase means that the result belongs to the presently constituted karmic
existence. “Present” is indexed to the same projected continuum, not merely to
one visible bodily phase.

## 4. Grammar

**Twenty-twofold karmic production**

```text
kāmeṣu
antarābhavaḥ
dvāviṃśati-vidham [karma]
ākṣipati
```

> In the Desire Realm, intermediate existence produces twenty-two kinds of
> karma.

The accusative `dvāviṃśatividham` qualifies the understood `karma` as the
object of `ākṣipati`.

**Present-existence fruit**

```text
tat [niyatam karma]
ca dṛṣṭa-dharma-phalam [bhavati]
```

> Its determinate portion also has its fruit in the present existence.

The Bhāṣya restricts this claim to the eleven determinate cases.

**Ground in continuum identity**

```text
hi
saḥ nikāyaḥ
ekaḥ eva [asti]
```

> For that rebirth-class continuum is one and the same.

The explanatory `hi` gives continuity of `nikāya` as the reason for the
temporal classification.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation
> In the Desire Realm, intermediate existence produces twenty-two kinds of
> karma. Its determinate portion has its fruit in the present existence, for
> that rebirth-class continuum is one and the same.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> In the Desire Realm, intermediate existence projects twenty-two kinds of
> karma. The determinate kinds have their result in the present existence, since
> the rebirth-class continuum is one and the same.
## 6. Philosophical Translation

> In the Desire Realm, intermediate existence produces twenty-two kinds of karma. Its determinate portion has its fruit in the present existence, for that rebirth-class continuum is one and the same.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**Eleven maturation-targets**

```text
intermediate existence
    1. antarābhava

five embryonic stages
    2. kalala
    3. arbuda
    4. peśī
    5. ghana
    6. praśākhā

five post-birth stages
    7. bāla
    8. kumāra
    9. yuvan
    10. madhya
    11. vṛddha
```

The source compresses the third post-birth member as `va`; `yuvan` is supplied
from the announced fivefold count and remains an editorial inference.

**The twenty-twofold derivation**

```text
11 maturation-targets
    ×
2 modal statuses
    determinate / indeterminate
    =
22 karmic possibilities
```

**The eleven determinate cases**

Each target can receive determinate karma. All eleven such cases are classed
as `dṛṣṭadharmavedanīya` because they mature within the same projected
continuum.

**No separate intermediate-existence temporal class**

The Bhāṣya explains that no independent `antarābhavavedanīya` category is
needed. Intermediate existence is itself projected by next-birth-result karma,
while karma produced during it and maturing within the resulting continuum is
present-existence-result karma.

## 8. Logical Determination

**Product classification**

```text
TARGET_STAGE(K) ∈ eleven stages
MODAL_STATUS(K) ∈ {determinate, indeterminate}

11 × 2 = 22
```

The number is generated by two independent dimensions.

**Continuum-relative temporal identity**

```text
PHASE(A) ≠ PHASE(B)

but

NIKAYA(A) = NIKAYA(B)

therefore

karma produced in A and maturing in B
may be present-existence-result karma
```

**Identity without unchanged constitution**

```text
one projected continuum
    → intermediate phase
    → embryonic phases
    → post-birth phases
```

Every phase is different; the causal series remains one.

**Projection and maturation remain distinct**

```text
upapadyavedanīya karma
    projects intermediate existence

karma produced in intermediate existence
    matures within its projected continuum
```

The cause establishing the field and the causes maturing within that field
must not be collapsed. The repeated root `ākṣip` marks distinct relations:
intermediate existence projects target-specific karma, while one karma
projects the rebirth-class continuum.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The eleven stages, multiplication by modal status, and
restriction of the present-existence designation to the determinate cases are
supplied by the Bhāṣya. The twenty-two consist of eleven stages, each with
determinate and indeterminate forms: intermediate existence, five embryonic stages
(`kalala`, `arbuda`, `peśī`, `ghana`, `praśākhā`), and five post-birth stages. The
third post-birth label is compressed in the witness. The present-existence
designation applies to the eleven determinate forms because intermediate existence
and the ensuing stages belong to one rebirth-class continuum.

**The life is a causal unit, not a visible body**

The classification refuses to identify “this life” with the currently visible
organism. Intermediate and embodied existence differ in appearance while
belonging to one karmically projected `nikāyasabhāga`.

**Application of the Loka continuity theory**

The Loka account of intermediate existence now performs work inside Karma
theory:

```text
continuity through rebirth transition
    → one projected rebirth-class continuum
    → determinate temporal classification of karma
```

This is a direct test of whether the earlier account of the world and its
beings can support practical causality.

**Persistence without a permanent subject**

The same continuum does not mean an unchanged self travels through the stages.
The invariant is the causal organization that differentiates itself into
successive developmental configurations.

**Developmental specificity**

Karma is not merely addressed to “later in life.” The Bhāṣya permits precise
developmental targets, showing how intensively temporal and embodied this
Kriyā/Karma theory becomes.

This is the Organon contact. The eleven-stage enumeration, twenty-twofold
derivation, and one-continuum explanation belong to the Bhāṣya.

**Former Philosophical Translation.**
> A karmically projected continuum maintains its identity through radical
> changes of developmental form. Karma produced in intermediate existence may
> mature in embryonic development, childhood, maturity, or old age and still
> count as fruit within the same present existence. The relevant identity is
> neither bodily sameness nor persistence of a substance, but continuity of
> one causal organization through differentiated phases.

Chronological distance within one continuum therefore does not by itself
constitute a different karmic life.
