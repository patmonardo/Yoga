# VAK_2.70 Bhāṣya — Formless and Uncontaminated Succession

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> nava cittāni tat ṣaṇṇāṃ nivṛtāt sapta tat tathā /
>
> caturbhyaḥ śaikṣam asmāt tu pañca aśaikṣaṃ tu pañcakāt // 2.70 //

> Nine minds follow formless wholesome, which follows six. Seven
> follow formless obscured, which likewise follows seven. Trainee mind
> follows four and is followed by five; beyond-training follows that
> group of five.

## 3. Continuous Translation

From wholesome Formless Realm mind, nine kinds of mind can arise:
all except wholesome Desire Realm mind and the unobscured-
indeterminate minds of the Desire and Form Realms. Conversely,
wholesome Formless mind can follow six kinds: the three of its
own level, wholesome Form Realm mind, trainee mind, and
beyond-training mind.

Seven kinds follow obscured Formless mind: the three of its own
level, wholesome and obscured Form Realm mind, and the two
afflicted Desire Realm kinds. Obscured Formless mind itself can
follow seven kinds. The excluded predecessors are the afflicted
minds of Desire and Form and the two minds without *āsrava*.

Trainee mind can follow four kinds: the wholesome mind of each
of the three Realms and trainee mind itself. Five kinds follow
trainee mind: those four plus beyond-training mind. Beyond-
training mind can itself follow those same five kinds. Four
kinds then follow beyond-training mind: the wholesome minds
of the three Realms and beyond-training mind itself. The
twelve-kind succession account is complete.

## 4. Reading Control

The verse completes directed succession counts: nine/six for
Formless wholesome, seven/seven for Formless obscured, four/five
for trainee, and five predecessors for beyond-training. The
Bhāṣya then adds beyond-training's four successors. *Tat ṣaṇṇām*
marks predecessors of Formless wholesome; *tat tathā* repeats the
seven predecessor count for Formless obscured. The four successors
of beyond-training close the inventory without being part of the
quoted 2.70 kārikā.
