## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Among these, first. Acquisition is obtaining and possession. Acquisition is twofold: obtaining what was not obtained or had been relinquished, and possessing what has been obtained. Non-acquisition is established by the reverse. Of what? Of what belongs to one's own continuum. Not of what belongs to another. No one possesses what belongs to another. Not of what belongs to no continuum. No one possesses what is classified as non-sentient. This is the rule for the conditioned. Among the unconditioned, the two cessations.

All sentient beings possess cessation not through Discrimination. Hence the Abhidharma: who possesses uncontaminated dharmas? All sentient beings. All noble persons, except one still fully bound at the initial moment, and some ordinary persons, possess cessation through Discrimination. No one possesses space. Therefore it has no acquisition. Where there is no acquisition, there is also no non-acquisition. This is the established doctrine.

Is acquisition some distinct entity? A sūtra says that through the arising, obtaining, and possession of the ten dharmas of one beyond training, one becomes noble. Then possession would apply to non-sentient things and to other beings. A wheel-turning king possesses seven treasures. There the word means mastery, authority and freedom of action concerning those treasures. Why mastery there and a distinct entity elsewhere? Neither its own nature is discerned, as form or attachment is, nor its activity, as the eye or the ear. A real dharma is untenable.

If it were a cause of arising, it would not apply to the unconditioned. How would what was not acquired, or relinquished by a change of level or by dispassion, arise? If the cause is acquisition arising together with them, what work is left for arising? Among those still fully bound, mild, middling, and intense afflictions would not differ. Let whatever accounts for the difference account for the arising. Not a cause of arising. A cause of determining status. Without it, noble and ordinary would not be determined when consciousness is worldly. That can be the difference of abandoned and not abandoned. How is that difference itself established? Through a difference in the support. By the power of the paths of seeing and cultivation, the support is so transformed that it can no longer produce the abandoned afflictions. Like rice burned by fire, the support has ceased to be a seed. Or the seed-capacity impaired by a worldly path. The reverse is non-abandonment. Possessing the unabandoned, and not possessing the abandoned, is designated.

Wholesome dharmas: without effort, and through effort. Unimpaired capacity to be their seed is possession of the effortless. Impaired, one is said not to possess them, and to have severed wholesome roots. The impairment is by wrong view. Not completely eradicated from the continuum. Those through effort, once arisen, are possessed by unimpeded mastery of bringing them forth. The seed, unremoved, unimpaired, or developed when mastery is present, receives the name. Not another entity.

What is the seed? Name-and-form capable of producing a result, directly or through a succession, by a particular transformation of the continuum. Transformation is the continuum becoming otherwise. The continuum is conditioned dharmas of the three times as cause and result. One possessing greed is incapable of cultivating the four establishments of recollection: possession there is tolerating greed or not expelling it. Possession in every case is a dharma by designation, not a real dharma. Its negation is non-possession. The Vaibhāṣikas declare both real. Because this is our established doctrine.

Incapable repairs a missing negation against the quotation. The initial-moment exception is contextual. Not a collation.

## 4. Movement and Voice of the Commentary

Definition, then range, then a dispute. Scripture, causal work, and noble status are tested. The seed account is the alternative. The Vaibhāṣika reply closes the unit. Designation is the critical account, not a consensus. The seed account is not assigned a school in the local text.

## 5. Obtaining and Range

Not a new event alone. Own continuum for the conditioned. The two cessations for the unconditioned. The quotation says uncontaminated, not unconditioned. It supports universal possession of one cessation. Not every uncontaminated dharma, and not the noble path.

The fully bound person at the initial moment is the exception. Not the first moment of a bondage-sequence. Space is outside both. Non-acquisition is not the complement of everything.

## 6. Scripture and Causal Work

The treasures test the inference from the word to a real. Mastery is allowed there. The challenge is what warrants a distinct entity in the other passage. Wording is not denied.

Production meets the unconditioned, the unacquired, co-arising, and degrees. It is disowned. Status remains, because worldly consciousness does not display the path. The critical account has to explain abandonment, not only deny an entity.

## 7. Support and Seed

Abandonment is the support's altered capacity. Noble paths: cannot generate. Worldly path: seed impaired. Burned rice belongs to the first. Effortless wholesome: unimpaired capacity. Wrong view impairs. Not eradicated. Effortful: mastery of arising. Not one claim that every possessed dharma is manifest.

The seed is capable name-and-form. Not a hidden extra beside the continuum. Transformation and continuum are the alternative's terms. Not an agreement with the Vaibhāṣikas.

## 8. Local Uses

The king's mastery, the seed, and tolerating greed are three uses. The critical argument attends to the difference before it concludes designation. The temporal classification opens at 2.37.

## 9. Interpretation

A determination that is not the present event. A real acquisition-dharma, or the support's capacity. Both address possession and abandonment. They differ on what must exist.

Attribution is not its ground. Rejecting the entity does not remove the burden. The seed account is the competing ground. The reply stands. Not settled.

The two cessations are this verse. The look-ahead is not a grade. Pass and fail are the pair inside the range. Not the translation. Objective Logic on the mind is the pass. Not the final pass.
