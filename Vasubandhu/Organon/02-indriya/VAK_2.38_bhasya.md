# VAK_2.38 Bhāṣya — Status Does Not Inherit

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tridhā naśaikṣāśaikṣāṇām aheyānāṃ dvidhā matā /
> avyākṛtāptiḥ sahajābhijñānairmāṇikād ṛte // 2.38 //

> Threefold, of what is neither trainee nor beyond training. Of what is not to be abandoned, held to be twofold. Acquisition of the indeterminate is co-arisen, except the superknowledges and the magical creation.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Acquisition of what is neither trainee nor beyond training is threefold: it may be trainee, beyond training, or neither. Acquisition of what is not to be abandoned is twofold: abandoned through cultivation, or itself not to be abandoned. Acquisition of the unobscured-indeterminate is co-arisen, except the two indeterminate superknowledges and the magical-creation mind, whose acquisition, by special preparatory practice, may precede, accompany, and follow.

## 3. Movement

Two non-inheritance rules, then a temporal rule and its exception. Sahajā is co-arising, not innateness. The compound is provisional.

## 4. Organon Reading

The credit records the path that made the dharma available, and the reach of cultivated power. A trained capacity is not its present invocation. Not the translation. Not the final pass.

## 5. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. The form continuation is 2.39.
