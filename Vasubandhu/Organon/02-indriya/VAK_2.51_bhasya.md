# VAK_2.51 Bhāṣya — Followers of Consciousness

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> caittā dvau saṃvarau teṣāṃ cetaso lakṣaṇāni ca /
> cittānuvartinaḥ kālaphalādiśubhatādibhiḥ // 2.51 //

> The mental factors, the two restraints, and the marks of those and of consciousness, are followers of consciousness, by time, result, and the rest, and by wholesomeness and the rest.

From the question at 83.25 through the close at 85.07. The homogeneous cause at 85.08 is not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

What follows consciousness? The mental factors associated with it, the two restraints, and the marks of those and of consciousness. Ten reasons. Together in arising, persistence, cessation, and period. Together in result, maturation, and homogeneous outflow. Ethical status with consciousness. Fifty-eight out, fifty-four in. Fourteen not approved. Co-arisen is wider than co-arisen cause. Lamp and light, sprout and shadow, three staffs. Other causes granted. The co-arisen cause is kept. Not an uncontested proof.

## 3. Movement and Voice

Who, then how, then the counts, then the wider co-arisen, then the debate. The Kāśmīra teachers are named. The close concedes other causes and keeps this one.

## 4. Organon Reading

Follower is correspondence, not sequence. The rule-system follows produced consciousness. Acquisition can precede and outlast, so it is not a follower. Not the translation. Not the final pass.
