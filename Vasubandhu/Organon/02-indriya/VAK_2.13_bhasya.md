# VAK_2.13 Bhāṣya — What Is and Is Not to Be Abandoned

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> manovittitrayaṃ tredhā dviheyā durmanaskatā /
> nava bhāvanayā pañca tv aheyāny api na trayam // 2.13 //

> Mind and the triad of feelings are threefold in abandonment. Distress is
> to be abandoned in two ways; nine by cultivation. The five, too, are both
> to be abandoned and not abandoned; the triad is not to be abandoned.

The question supplies the three statuses. Mind with three feelings is four faculties.

The unit is printed 47.04–15 in `Vasubandhu/Sources/kosabhasya.txt`. Word division is editorial. Dauarmanasyaṃ is read as daurmanasyaṃ. Bhāvanayā follows the running verse. No independent collation is claimed.

Uktaḥ prakārabhedaḥ closes the distinctions. Lābha idānīṃ vaktavyaḥ opens acquisition and is 2.14.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

How many faculties are to be abandoned by seeing, how many by cultivation, and how many are not to be abandoned? Mind and the three feelings are threefold. Which three feelings? Pleasure, gladness, and neutral feeling. Distress is to be abandoned in two ways: distress is to be abandoned by both seeing and cultivation. Nine by cultivation: to be abandoned continues to apply. Those beginning with the eye, with life as the eighth, together with the pain-faculty, are to be abandoned only by cultivation. The five, however, may also be not to be abandoned: the five beginning with faith are both to be abandoned by cultivation and not to be abandoned, because they can be with outflows or without outflows. The triad is not: the three beginning with the faculty “I shall know” are not to be abandoned at all, because they are without outflows. For what is faultless is not fit to be abandoned. The distinctions of kind have been explained.

## 4. Movement and Voice of the Commentary

A question, then the distribution. The compressed groups are named. The implied predicate is made explicit. Outflow is the reason for the last two assignments. No opponent. No school.

The last sentence closes the distinctions opened at 2.09. Outflow, maturation, ethical quality, realm-connection, and abandonment have been examined. Acquisition is next. It is not this status under another name.

## 5. The Five Groups

| Faculties | Status | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Mind, pleasure, gladness, neutral feeling | seeing, cultivation, or not | 4 |
| Distress | seeing or cultivation | 1 |
| Seven of form, life, pain | cultivation only | 9 |
| Five beginning with faith | cultivation, or not | 5 |
| The three | not | 3 |

Twenty-two. The ethical threefold of 2.11 had four feelings. Pain is now with the nine. Threefold does not travel.

Both seeing and cultivation distributes instances. One instance is not abandoned twice. The same holds for the other mixed groups.

## 6. Cultivation-Abandonable Is Not Unwholesome

The nine include faculties that 2.11 called indeterminate. The five are wholesome, and their with-outflow instances are still cultivation-abandonable. The predicate is not ethically bad.

Distress can be wholesome or unwholesome, and it has no class that is not to be abandoned. Tone, quality, and abandonment stay three determinations.

The paragraph does not give the mechanism. It is not an instruction to destroy the bodily faculties or to end life. It is not a claim that seeing removes the body. Ordinary cessation is not this category.

## 7. The Five and the Faultless Triad

The five are with outflows or without. Cultivation-abandonable is the first. Not to be abandoned is the second. Not every faith, vigor, mindfulness, concentration, or Discrimination is to be discarded.

The triad is exclusively without outflows, and so not to be abandoned. Na hi nirdoṣaṃ prahāṇārham: what is faultless is not fit to be abandoned.

Not to be abandoned is the path-status. It is not permanence, not uninterrupted operation, and not exemption from analysis.

## 8. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for Translation

Prahātavya, praheya, and heya are to be abandoned. The gerundive is the classification, not a report that abandonment has occurred. Aprahātavya and aheya are not to be abandoned.

Heyānīty adhikāraḥ carries to be abandoned into nine by cultivation. Api adds the other class for the five. The negation on the triad admits no abandonable class.

Durmanaskatā is distress. Vittitraya is pleasure, gladness, and neutral feeling. Jīvitāṣṭama is the seven with life as eighth. Pain stays in the nine.

## 9. Organon Reading

A faculty-name does not say whether seeing, cultivation, or keeping applies. For the five, outflow decides. A faulted instance is not the rejection of the type. Faultlessness is not the permanence of the event.

Citta supports. The path abandons or keeps the qualified caitta. The power-system is not deleted because one deployment was with outflows. The faultless triad is not discarded after use. That is how the agent is guided. The principles remain deposited. Not the verse English.

## 10. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Domain withdrawn. The five groups and the maxim are kept. Abandonment is not ethical quality, and not an instruction against life.

2.14 has not been opened.
