# VAK_2.65 Bhāṣya — Elements and Derived Form

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dvidhā bhūtāni taddhetur bhautikasya tu pañcadhā /
> tridhā bhautikam anyonyaṃ bhūtānām ekadhaiva tat // 2.65 //

> The elements are cause of those in two ways, and of derived form in five. Derived form, of one another, in three. Of the elements, that, in one way only.

From 102.19 through 103.08. Succession of minds at 103.09 is 2.66. Not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Elements cause elements by homogeneous and co-arisen causes. They cause derived form by five functions of the conditioning cause: generation, dependent support, foundation, sustaining, increase. Derived form causes derived form by co-arisen, homogeneous, and maturation causes, with the restrictions. It causes the elements by maturation alone. The conditioning cause is not always counted.

## 3. Movement and Voice

A return to the two-condition clause, then four directions. The five are one cause divided. The mutual case is narrowed to action that follows mind. Not a collation.

## 4. Organon Reading

The numbers are not one list. Result in one relation, cause in another. Not the translation. Not the final pass.

## 5. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Element kept for bhūta. Domain withdrawn.

2.66 has not been opened.
