## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya excludes the two afflicted desire kinds and the two uncontaminated kinds from the eight predecessors of Form obscured; its six successors comprise the three of its level and desire kinds other than unobscured-indeterminate. Form unobscured has three same-level predecessors and six successors, including the two afflicted desire kinds and formless obscured. The same pattern holds in the formless: three of its level before it, and six after, including the lower realms' afflicted kinds.

The verse states the constraints for Form obscured and unobscured and notes the formless pattern. The next verse continues with formless wholesome.
