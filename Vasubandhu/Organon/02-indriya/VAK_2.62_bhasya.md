# VAK_2.62 Bhāṣya — The Other Three Conditions

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> cittacaittā acaramā utpannāḥ samanantaraḥ /
> ālambanaṃ sarvadharmāḥ kāraṇākhyo 'dhipaḥ smṛtaḥ // 2.62 //

> Arisen mind and mental factors, not final, are the immediately antecedent. All dharmas are the object-condition. What is named the conditioning cause is held to be the predominant.

From 98.09 through 100.18. Activity of the conditions is 2.63. Not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Arisen mind and factors, except an arhat's final, are the immediately antecedent. The whole complex, of the next whole complex. Not own-kind only. Form and the dissociated are mixed, and are not this condition. Future is unordered. Emergence after a mindless attainment is immediate because no other mind intervenes. Objects are all dharmas, as appropriate, and remain objects when not apprehended. The conditioning cause is the predominant condition, of every conditioned dharma other than itself. Not of the unconditioned.

## 3. Movement and Voice

Three definitions, each closed. Vasumitra and the elder are named. The Sautrāntikas close the future-knowledge dispute without settling it. Support wording is damaged.

## 4. Organon Reading

Succession, object, and predominance are three relations. Immediate is not the clock. Support is not the antecedent. Not the translation. Not the final pass.
