## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Indeterminate in the desire realm, fourfold: maturation-born, deportment, craft, and creation. In the form realm, excluding craft.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Desire unobscured-indeterminate is four. Form is three, no craft there. Deportment and the rest are absent in the formless, the activities absent. These are mental cognitions. The five sensory cognitions prepare deportment and craft. Others: a deportment-produced mental cognition takes the twelve essences. Not settled.

Twelve become twenty. Wholesome sixfold. Unobscured-indeterminate sevenfold in the cut, eight with the formless one left undivided. Desire eight, form six, formless four, and the two uncontaminated.

The twenty have their own succession. A coarse list is not assigned to a subtype. Creation-mind follows and is followed by its own kind and form application-produced only. Application-produced is not followed by a reverse from deportment, craft, or maturation-born: those are weak, or prepare the activity. Afflicted can precede application-produced, for one wearied by affliction. Keenness explains some birth-acquired edges. Attention before and after the path is disputed. Not settled.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Neutrality is not one function. Maturation, deportment, craft, and supernormal creation stay apart. Realm decides which occur. The finer kind does not inherit the parent list.

Organon rendering:

Twenty kinds, and the edges checked again. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
