## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Both the non-reflective attainment and the attainment of cessation arise in the desire realm and the form realm, when that support is present. The one called cessation is first produced among humans, and later in the form realm by those who had lost it.

Loss is stated. Otherwise the Udāyin sūtra is contradicted. A monk enters and emerges from cessation of reflection and feeling, and may be born in a divine mind-made body and enter it there. That body is form-realm. The attainment is of the summit. Without loss, the rebirth does not fit. A fourth-dhyāna attainment of cessation is not established. Nine successive attainments. Out of order is mastery. The beginner is successive.

Differences: level, application, continuum, result, maturation, first production. The names follow what the preparation opposes. The cessation is of consciousness and the mental factors.

Return of consciousness: the past as immediately preceding condition; consciousness and the faculty-body as seeds of one another; an attainment that has consciousness; without consciousness. How an attainment: equilibrium of the great elements, and entry by the consciousness of entry. Real, because of obstruction? The entry-consciousness itself obstructs. Mere non-operation is designated. They do not accept it. The two attainments have been explained.

## 4. Movement and Voice

Support against level. Loss from the sūtra. An unnamed school on the fourth dhyāna. Then the comparison. Then the return, in distinct voices. Ghoṣaka against Vasumitra. The Vaibhāṣika close is without consciousness. The designation account is not accepted. Not an agreement.

## 5. Support and Loss

A desire- or form-realm support does not make the attainment a desire- or form-level attainment. First production is human. Later production presupposes loss. Skipping does not withdraw the beginner's order, and does not settle the level.

## 6. Names

Cessation of reflection and feeling is the sūtra name. It does not leave the other mental factors operating. The analogy is knowledge of another's consciousness.

## 7. Return

Immediately preceding, here, is a condition. The question presupposes a long interval. Reciprocal seed is the earlier teachers. Consciousness in the attainment is Vasumitra. Contact and feeling are Ghoṣaka's objection. The cases are unequal. Without consciousness is the Vaibhāṣika word for the state. Not the class-name of the non-reflective. Real entity against designation stays open.

## 8. Organon Reading

Non-operation designated is one account of the interval. It is not a pass, and not the nirodha of virodha. The return is how a Determinate Dharma starts again. Not the translation. Not the final pass.
