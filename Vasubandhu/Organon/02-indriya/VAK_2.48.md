# VAK_2.48 — Result-Status and the Close of Dissociated Formations

## Source

> sabhāgatā sā tu punar vipāko 'py āptayo dvidhā /
> lakṣaṇāni ca niḥṣyandāḥ samāpatty-asamanvayāḥ // 2.48 //

The commentary cites the verse at 82.09–15. VAK 2.47's concluding
classification is carried forward at 82.06–08; the account of the
dissociated formations closes at 82.18, and the question leading into
VAK 2.49 follows at 82.19–20.

## Word Division

*sabhāgatā sā tu punaḥ vipākaḥ api āptayaḥ dvidhā /
lakṣaṇāni ca niḥṣyandāḥ samāpatti-asamanvayāḥ*

## Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Morphology | Force in this passage |
|---|---|---|
| *sabhāgatā* | nominative feminine singular | Commonality, carrying forward from the preceding discussion. |
| *sā* | nominative feminine singular pronoun | That commonality. |
| *tu punaḥ* | adversative particle and adverb | But further / however, in addition. |
| *vipākaḥ api* | nominative masculine singular with particle | Also a maturation-result. |
| *āptayaḥ* | nominative feminine plural | Acquisitions; the commentary expands as *prāptayaḥ*. |
| *dvidhā* | indeclinable | Twofold: homogeneous outflow or maturation-result. |
| *lakṣaṇāni ca* | nominative neuter plural with conjunction | The conditioned marks also; “twofold” carries over. |
| *niḥṣyandāḥ* | nominative plural predicate | Homogeneous outflows. |
| *samāpatti-asamanvayāḥ* | compressed expression | The two attainments without Mind and non-acquisition, resolved by the Bhāṣya. |

## Scientific Translation

That commonality, however, is also a maturation-result, not only a
homogeneous outflow. Acquisitions may be homogeneous outflows or
maturation-results; the conditioned marks may be so as well. The two
attainments without Mind and non-acquisition are homogeneous outflows
only.

## Logical Movement

The Bhāṣya carries selected classifications from VAK 2.47 to commonality,
then adds maturation-result status. It extends the twofold distinction
between homogeneous outflow and maturation-result to acquisitions and
conditioned marks. The final *eva* restricts the two attainments and
non-acquisition to homogeneous outflows in this classification.

The verse is not saying that every particular acquisition or mark must
be both kinds of result at once. It assigns a twofold classification
to each category.

## Organon Note

This is a useful Hub test: a determinate Dharma is not exhausted by
membership in a single catalog category. Its result-status depends on
the relation being specified—homogeneous continuity or maturation.
The distinctions remain typed rather than collapsing into an
undifferentiated label “result.”

The Kośa-wide synthesis presents Vijñāna as Discriminative Cognition
joining and governing Perception and Conception; their unity is
Inconceivable as a homogeneous operation, with its Idea disclosed in
Cognition Base. Vijñāna governs Mind and guides the reading of Dharma
Base. *Avijñapti* is the same Dharma classified in Form Base and Dharma
Base, and Vijñāna bears a *prati* relation to it. This is the broader
systematic frame, not a claim that this short classification states
each relation.

## Review Status

Paired with the Bhāṣya through its closure of the dissociated-formations
account at 82.18. The transition question at 82.19–20 introduces the
six-cause discussion in VAK 2.49; it is not treated as part of this
verse's translation.
