# VAK_2.70 — Formless and the Two Uncontaminated

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Nine minds. That, from six. From obscured, seven. That, likewise. Trainee from four. From this, five. Beyond-training from the group of five.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Nine follow formless wholesome, excluding desire wholesome and the unobscured-indeterminate of desire and form. It follows six: its three, form wholesome, trainee and beyond-training.

Seven follow formless obscured: its three, form wholesome and obscured, the two afflicted desire kinds. It follows seven, excluding the afflicted of desire and form and the two uncontaminated.

Trainee follows the three realm-wholesome kinds and trainee. Five follow: those four and beyond-training. Beyond-training follows that group of five. Four follow it: the three realm-wholesome and itself. The twelve are complete.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Seven and seven are not a reversible list. Trainee can precede beyond-training. Beyond-training is not followed by trainee. That is immediate succession of kinds. Not a proof that a person never declines. The final mind of an arhat remains excluded from being an antecedent.

Realm-wholesome among the predecessors of the uncontaminated is still contaminated.

Organon rendering:

The twelve are closed as a succession. Not the chapter. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
