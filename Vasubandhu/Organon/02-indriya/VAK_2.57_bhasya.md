# VAK_2.57 Bhāṣya — Three Fruits

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vipāko 'vyākṛto dharmaḥ sattvākhyo vyākṛtodbhavaḥ /
> niḥṣyando hetusadṛśo visaṃyogaḥ kṣayo dhiyā // 2.57 //

> Maturation is an indeterminate dharma, classed with a sentient being, arisen from the determinate. Homogeneous outflow is similar to its cause. Disconnection is cessation by Discrimination.

From 95.09 through 96.02. Efficacy at 96.03 is 2.58. Not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Maturation is unobscured-indeterminate, classed with a sentient being, arisen later from the determinate, not together and not immediate, and unshared. Shared karmic products are predominance. Outflow is similar to its cause. Pervasive similarity is level and affliction, not specific type. Four cases. Disconnection is cessation by Discrimination. The gloss identifies it. It does not give a mechanism.

## 3. Movement and Voice

Each predicate is added because the previous was too wide. The fourfold keeps homogeneous and pervasive from collapsing. The last line is a gloss, not a close of the unconditioned dispute.

## 4. Organon Reading

Determinate cause, indeterminate unshared fruit. Likeness is not maturation. Cessation by Discrimination is not a product in this definition. Not the translation. Not the final pass.

## 5. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Discrimination kept. Discernment withdrawn. Not a new cause.

2.58 has not been opened.
