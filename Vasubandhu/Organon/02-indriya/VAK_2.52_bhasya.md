# VAK_2.52 Bhāṣya — Homogeneous Cause

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sabhāgahetuḥ sadṛśāḥ svanikāyabhuvo 'grajāḥ /
> anyo 'nyaṃ navabhūmis tu mārgaḥ samaviśiṣṭayoḥ // 2.52 //

> Homogeneous cause: similar, of their own class and level, prior-born. But the Path of nine levels, of one another, of the equal and the superior.

From 85.08 through the Path explanation. The extension at 87.20 is 2.53. Not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Similar of similar, within the order stated. Own class and own level. Prior-born, of later, arisen or not. Future is not this cause. Never-not-a-cause is the other three. State, not substance. The Path is adventitious, not appropriated, and crosses its nine levels toward the equal or the superior. Faculties and accumulation, not height.

## 3. Movement and Voice

Definition, then the restrictions, then the temporal debate, then the Path exception. The inferior reading is against the governing clause. Not repaired into a new rule.

## 4. Organon Reading

Continuity in kind requires a prior arising. The pass does not run downward. Not same-class. Not the translation. Not the final pass.
