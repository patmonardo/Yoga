# VAK_2.73 — Acquisition, and the Close

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> क्लिष्टे त्रैधातुके लाभः षण्णां षण्णां द्वयोः शुभे ।
>
> त्रयाणां रूपजे शैक्षे चतुर्णां तस्य शेषिते ॥ २.७३ ॥

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

In afflicted mind of the three realms, acquisition of six, of six, of two. In wholesome form-realm, of three. In trainee, of four. In the rest, of that.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

When desire afflicted is manifest, six: desire wholesome, by restoration of wholesome roots through doubt and by return; desire unwholesome and obscured, and form afflicted, by return and by decline; formless afflicted and trainee, by decline. Form afflicted, six: the three form kinds and desire unobscured, by return; formless afflicted and trainee, by decline. Formless afflicted, two: itself and trainee, by decline.

Form wholesome, three: itself, and desire and form unobscured. Trainee, four: itself, desire and form unobscured, and formless wholesome, on dispassion from desire and form by the path. Where acquisition was not explained, of that mind itself, not another.

Others, without the distinctions: nine with afflicted, six with wholesome, itself with indeterminate. The wholesome aggregate should be seven. A summary: at rebirth, attainment, dispassion, decline, and restoration of wholesome roots, acquisition is for one who does not possess it. The discussion of conditions is complete. The second chapter, the Exposition of the Rules, is complete.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Manifest is not acquired. Acquired need not be manifest. The lists are across occasions, not a bundle at every occurrence. Decline can acquire trainee. The successor list did not settle regression. Return is not automatically return to the desire realm.

The possession phrase in the witness is awkward. Marked, not repaired. The close says one who does not possess it.

Organon rendering:

Acquisition is not succession. The chapter ends. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
