# VAK_2.67

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> कामे नव शुभाच्चित्ताच्चित्तान्यष्टाभ्य एव तत् ।
> दशभ्योऽकुशलं तस्माच्चत्वारि निवृतं तथा ॥ २.६७ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> kāme nava śubhāc cittāc cittāny aṣṭābhya eva tat /
> daśabhyo 'kuśalaṃ tasmāc catvāri nivṛtaṃ tathā // 2.67 //

The display applies sandhi in *cittāny aṣṭābhyaḥ*. The local witness's
*nivṛta* spelling is retained for obscured-indeterminate mind. The
commentary at 103.19–104.08 resolves the counts; 104.09 begins the
unobscured-indeterminate account of 2.68.

*Śubha* is explicitly explained as *kuśala* by the Bhāṣya; its meaning
is no longer merely a tentative identification. “Mind” in the counts
means a kind within the twelvefold classification, not nine simultaneous
successors of one occurrence.

## 4. Grammar

The verse alternates the direction of the relation. With *śubhāt cittāt*,
wholesome mind is the source and *nava cittāni* the successors. With
*aṣṭābhyaḥ*, the eight kinds are sources and *tat* is the wholesome
successor. The next two clauses likewise alternate: unwholesome mind
arises from ten; four arise from it.

*Nivṛtaṃ tathā* repeats both the ten predecessors and four successors
for obscured-indeterminate desire-realm mind. It does not equate
unwholesome and obscured-indeterminate ethical status.

The verbs of arising and the immediate-succession qualification are
understood. The Bhāṣya expressly notes that *anantaram* is stated later
in the sequence and construes the present counts accordingly. *Kāme*
locates the three focal kinds, not every predecessor and successor:
the lists include higher-realm and uncontaminated kinds.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> In the desire realm, nine minds [arise] from wholesome mind;
> that [wholesome mind arises] only from eight. Unwholesome [mind
> arises] from ten; from it, four. Obscured [mind] is likewise.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Nine kinds of mind can arise immediately after desire-realm
> wholesome mind, while it can arise immediately after eight kinds.
> Desire-realm unwholesome mind has ten possible predecessor kinds
> and four possible successor kinds. Desire-realm obscured-indeterminate
> mind has the same ten-before and four-after counts.

These are permitted class-to-class successions in their relevant
contexts. They do not mean that every individual wholesome occurrence
can be followed by any of the nine regardless of the person's faculties,
attainment, or situation.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Once kinds of mind are distinguished, their relations remain to
> be learned. The kinds that can precede a given mind need not be
> the kinds that can follow it. Entry into attainment, emergence,
> and rebirth account for different connections within one
> classification of immediate succession.

This interpretation emphasizes the directions and contexts supplied
by the commentary. Numerical symmetry where it occurs does not erase
ethical distinctions between the kinds concerned.

| pratisaṃdhikāla | time of rebirth-linking | context for specified cross-realm afflicted successors |
| kliṣṭasamāpatti | afflicted attainment | context of returning to a wholesome supporting level |

| ākāra | cognitive aspect | another dimension of remoteness |

| pratipakṣa | counteragent | fourth dimension of remoteness |

The four kinds of remoteness explain why wholesome formless mind is
not an immediate successor of desire-realm wholesome mind here. The
passage names these dimensions without fully defining each distance.
It should not be expanded into an invented metric.

## 8. Logical Determination

**Wholesome desire-realm mind: outgoing and incoming lists.**

| Other kind or group | Can follow it | Can precede it |
|---|---:|---:|
| Four desire-realm kinds | 4 | 4 |
| Form-realm wholesome | 1 | 1 |
| Form-realm obscured-indeterminate | 1 | 1 |
| Formless obscured-indeterminate | 1 | 0 |
| Trainee uncontaminated | 1 | 1 |
| Beyond-training uncontaminated | 1 | 1 |
| Total | 9 | 8 |

The form-realm wholesome successor occurs on entry into attainment;
the reverse occurs on emergence. The form-realm and formless obscured
successors occur at rebirth-linking. The return from form-realm obscured
mind is explained differently: someone pressed by an afflicted attainment
resorts to the wholesome supporting level. The uncontaminated predecessors
are explained through emergence.

The missing formless wholesome successor is expressly excluded because
of remoteness. The absence of a formless predecessor in the incoming
eight is a separate fact of the stated list; no converse transition
should be inferred from the outgoing rebirth case.

**The two afflicted desire-realm kinds.** Each has the following profile:

```text
Predecessors:
    4 desire-realm kinds
    3 form-realm kinds
    3 formless-realm kinds
    total 10

Successors:
    4 desire-realm kinds
    total 4
```

Trainee and beyond-training uncontaminated minds are excluded from
those ten predecessors. The commentary explains the higher-realm
predecessors through rebirth-linking into the desire realm. This is
not a claim that every higher meditation is immediately followed by
an unwholesome mind during ordinary emergence.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Bhāṣya control.** The first-pass study correctly recorded the headline
counts but left their membership provisional. The commentary resolves
them and gives different contexts for relations that might otherwise
look like interchangeable arrows. The nine/eight asymmetry is specifically
the outgoing formless obscured case, not an unspecified difference of
one mind.

The source at 103.27–28 gives the compressed expression
*ādhārakuśalabhūmisaṃśrayaṇāt*. “Resorting to the wholesome supporting
level” preserves its sense without constructing an additional faculty
or a distinct thirteenth mind-kind. The designation of higher-realm
obscured minds remains ethically indeterminate; “afflicted” does not
convert them into unwholesome minds forbidden by 2.66.

**Organon interpretation.** Classification begins the analysis, but
the transition relation adds direction and context. The same named
kinds can be related through emergence in one direction and through
rebirth in another. Counting the connections cannot substitute for
knowing what makes each connection possible.

For the learning project, the important result is this: knowing that
a mind is wholesome does not yet determine either its antecedents or
its successors. Realm affiliation and the mode of transition also
matter. A model of succession can represent the permitted kinds, but
must retain these qualifications rather than treating the table as
an unrestricted program available to every individual.

**Review status.** Provisional polished study checked against the research
kārikā, research Bhāṣya, and running Sanskrit at 103.19–104.08. The counts
and their named members agree. Originals remain unchanged. VAK 2.68
continues with desire-realm unobscured-indeterminate and form-realm
wholesome mind.
