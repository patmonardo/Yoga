## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> From that, four consciousnesses. These twelve are twenty, by dividing
> wholesome [consciousness] in the three realms twofold.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Four consciousnesses follow beyond-training. These twelve become twenty
> by dividing wholesome consciousness in the three realms into two kinds.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The verse closes the twelve with the four successors of beyond-training and states that the twelve become twenty by the twofold division of wholesome consciousness in the three realms. The Bhāṣya notes that this cut alone does not complete the twenty; the further subdivision continues in the next verse.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The four are the wholesome consciousnesses of the three realms and beyond-training. The twelve become twenty by distinguishing application-produced wholesome consciousness from wholesome consciousness obtained through birth. These are alternatives, not two modes possessed by one and the same kind; the division continues beyond this verse.

The verse closes the twelve and begins the twenty. The next verse completes the subdivision of the unobscured kinds.
