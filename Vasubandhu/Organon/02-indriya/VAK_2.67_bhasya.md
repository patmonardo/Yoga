# VAK_2.67 Bhāṣya — Desire-Realm Succession

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāme nava śubhāc cittāc cittāni aṣṭābhya eva tat /
>
> daśabhyo 'kuśalaṃ tasmāc catvāri nivṛtaṃ tathā // 2.67 //

> In the Desire Realm, nine kinds of mind follow wholesome mind,
> which itself follows eight. Unwholesome mind follows ten and is
> followed by four; obscured mind likewise follows ten and is
> followed by four.

## 3. Continuous Translation

In the Desire Realm, nine kinds of mind can immediately follow a
wholesome mind. “Immediately” is supplied in the following line.
The nine are the four kinds of its own level; two Form Realm kinds,
wholesome on entering an attainment and obscured at rebirth-linking;
the obscured Formless kind at rebirth-linking; and the two kinds
without *āsrava*, belonging to one in training and one beyond
training. A wholesome Formless mind does not follow directly,
because it is too remote from Desire Realm mind. The Formless Realm
is remote from the Desire Realm in four respects: Basis, aspect,
Support, and counteragent.

Conversely, wholesome Desire Realm mind can arise immediately
after eight kinds. Four are of its own level. Two are Form Realm
kinds: after a wholesome Form mind on emerging from an attainment,
or after an obscured Form mind when someone oppressed by an
afflicted attainment resorts to the wholesome lower Basis. It can
also follow either kind without *āsrava* on emergence. These are
eight predecessors, distinct from its nine possible successors.

Unwholesome Desire Realm mind can follow ten kinds: any of the
ten with *āsrava*, but neither of the two without it. In
rebirth-linking it can directly follow minds of the Form and
Formless Realms. Only four kinds can immediately follow an
unwholesome Desire Realm mind, namely the four of its own level.
The same pattern applies to obscured-indeterminate Desire Realm
mind: it can follow ten kinds and be followed by four.

## 4. Reading Control

The ablatives *aṣṭābhyaḥ* and *daśabhyaḥ* count predecessors;
*tasmāt* counts successors. *Tathā* carries the ten/four pattern
from unwholesome to obscured-indeterminate mind. The four
remotenesses explain the absence of a direct wholesome Formless
successor after wholesome Desire Realm mind; the attainment and
rebirth cases explain other edges. These are directed succession
relations, not mere membership in the twelve-kind inventory.
