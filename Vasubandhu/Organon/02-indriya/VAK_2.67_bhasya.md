# VAK_2.67 Bhāṣya — Desire-Realm Succession

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāme nava śubhāc cittāc cittāny aṣṭābhya eva tat /
> daśabhyo 'kuśalaṃ tasmāc catvāri nivṛtaṃ tathā // 2.67 //

> In the desire realm, nine minds from a wholesome mind. That, only from eight. Unwholesome from ten. From it, four. Obscured, likewise.

From 103.19 through 104.08. Unobscured-indeterminate at 104.09 is 2.68. Not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Nine follow desire wholesome: four of its level, form wholesome at entry, form obscured and formless obscured at rebirth-linking, trainee and beyond-training. Not formless wholesome. Too remote, in four respects. It follows eight: the four, form wholesome at emergence, form obscured by resort to the wholesome support, and the two uncontaminated at emergence. Unwholesome follows the ten contaminated, and four follow it. Obscured likewise.

## 3. Movement and Voice

Outgoing, then incoming, then the two afflicted kinds with the same counts. Occasions are named. Not a collation.

## 4. Organon Reading

Direction is part of the rule. Remoteness excludes one successor. Not the translation. Not the final pass.

## 5. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Obscured kept. Domain withdrawn.

2.68 has not been opened.
