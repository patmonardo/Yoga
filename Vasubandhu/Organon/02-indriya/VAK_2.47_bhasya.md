# VAK_2.47 Bhāṣya — Groups of Names

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> nāmakāyādayaḥ saṃjñāvākyākṣarasamuktayaḥ /
> kāmarūpāptasattvākhyā niḥṣyandāvyākṛtās tathā // 2.47 //

> Groups of names and the rest are collections of designations, sentences, and phonetic elements. Included in the desire and form realms, classed with sentient beings, homogeneous outflows, and likewise indeterminate.

Cited in parts from 80.13. The prose ends before 2.48 at 82.09. Not a collation.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Groups of names, of sentences, and of phonetic elements. A name makes a designation. A sentence completes a meaning. A phonetic element is the audible minimum. Desire and form realms. Classed with the sentient being who signifies. Homogeneous outflows. Unobscured-indeterminate. Others: also formless, and inexpressible.

Succession pressures a single name-entity. The last sound does not yield the meaning. Noise does not produce a name. A sound with a fixed application already signifies. They maintain the groups. Not settled.

## 3. Movement and Voice

Definition, then realm, sentient class, result, ethical class. Then the substantial dispute. The close keeps the Vaibhāṣika groups.

## 4. Organon Reading

Function is not substance. Reflection is not this compound's English. The schema of the Ordinary is contained in the Transcendental. Not the translation. Not the final pass.
