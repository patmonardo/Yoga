## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Same-class is sameness among beings. The non-reflective among the non-reflective is cessation of consciousness and the mental factors, a maturation. They are the Great Fruit.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Same-class is sameness among beings. The non-reflective, among the non-reflective, is the cessation of consciousness and the mental factors, a maturation of the attainment. Those so born are the Great Fruit; they are reflective at birth and at death, and the arising of reflection ends their stay.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Same-class is the dispute about a shared designation. The universal is the comparison. The non-reflective is the interval, not the nirodha of virodha.
