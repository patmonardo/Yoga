# VAK_2.54 Bhāṣya — Pervasive Cause and Maturation Cause

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sarvatragākhyaḥ kliṣṭānāṃ svabhūmau pūrvasarvagāḥ /
> vipākahetur aśubhāḥ kuśalāś caiva sāsravāḥ // 2.54 //

> Called pervasive, of the afflicted, on their own level: the prior pervasive. Maturation cause: the unwholesome, and the wholesome with influx.

From 89.01 through 90.22. The temporal classification at 90.23 is 2.55. Not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Prior pervasive dharmas of a level cause later afflicted dharmas on that level, including another class. The list is later. A noble person's afflicted dharmas still have a seeing-abandoned cause, unmentioned because abandoned. Unwholesome and wholesome with influx mature. Indeterminate are weak. Uncontaminated are not moistened by craving. Ripening as dissimilar, and as culmination of a transformed series, is one account. Similar ripening of the other causes is the other. Not settled. The cause depends on a series. Not together with the karma, and not immediately after.

## 3. Movement and Voice

Two causes. The Kāśmīra teachers are named. The ripening dispute is not closed. Counts are reported, not restored where damaged.

## 4. Organon Reading

Class-crossing is not level-crossing. Maturation is not homogeneous outflow. The series is the dependence. Not the translation. Not the final pass.

## 5. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Domain withdrawn. The latent-tendency chapter is not opened.

2.55 has not been opened.
