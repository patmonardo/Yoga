# VAK_2.55 Bhāṣya — Times and Disconnection

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sarvatragaḥ sabhāgaś ca dvayadhvagau tryadhvagās trayaḥ /
>
> saṃskṛtaṃ savisaṃyogaṃ phalaṃ nāsaṃskṛtasya te // 2.55 //

> The pervasive and the homogeneous range over two times, past and present. The co-arisen, the associated, and the maturation cause range over three. The conditioning cause is of all times and dissociated from time. The fruit is the conditioned together with disconnection, cessation by Discrimination; these causes do not have the unconditioned as their fruit. The Path produces the acquisition and brings one to disconnection, without being its cause.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Pervasive and homogeneous are past and present. Co-arisen, associated, and maturation are three times. The conditioning cause is all times and dissociated from time. The fruit is the conditioned and cessation by Discrimination. The unconditioned does not have cause and fruit in that matrix. The Path produces the acquisition and brings one to disconnection. It is not the cause of disconnection. Entity or absence is disputed. Gone, for now.

## 4. Organon Reading

A fruit need not be a product. The grade is the acquisition. The disclosure is not a classmate. Not the translation. Not the nirodha of virodha. Not the final pass.
