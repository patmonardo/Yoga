# VAK_2.65 — Elements and Derived Form

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> द्विधा भूतानि तद्धेतुर्भौतिकस्य तु पञ्चधा ।
>
> त्रिधा भौतिकमन्योन्यं भूतानामेकधैव तत् ॥ २.६५ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> dvidhā bhūtāni taddhetur bhautikasya tu pañcadhā /
>
> tridhā bhautikam anyonyaṃ bhūtānām ekadhaiva tat // 2.65 //

Bhūta is the great element. Bhautika is derived form. Not form in general. The five are functions of the conditioning cause. Not five of the six.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

The elements are cause of those in two ways, and of derived form in five. Derived form, of one another, in three. Of the elements, that, in one way only.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

How are the elements cause-condition for the elements? In two ways: homogeneous and co-arisen. Of derived form, in five. Generation, dependent support, foundation, sustaining, increase. This is the conditioning cause, divided fivefold.

Generation: derived form arises from them. Dependent support: once arisen, it follows the elements. Foundation: they are the basis, as for a picture. Sustaining: a cause of non-interruption. Birth, alteration, basis, persistence, and increase. The fifth function is in the summary. No separate prose definition.

Of one another, in three: co-arisen, homogeneous, maturation. The conditioning cause is not always counted. It applies without the distinction. Mutual co-arisen is bodily and verbal action that follows mind. Other derived form is not. Homogeneous is all previously arisen, of the similar. Maturation is the action whose maturation is the eye and the rest. Of the elements, in one way only: bodily and verbal action whose maturation is the elements.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The numbers do not count the same list. Two and three and one are cause-types. Five are functions inside the conditioning cause. That cause is predominant condition, not a member of the cause-condition. The opening question was cause-condition. The fivefold answer draws on the other condition.

One way only does not erase the general conditioning relation. It is omitted where it does not distinguish the case.

Derived form can be result in one relation and cause in another. Not every instance, of every other, in the same way. Action that follows mind is the mutual co-arisen case. Not the rest of derived form.

Organon rendering:

Form is a profile of relations, not a mass. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
