# VAK_2.72 Bhāṣya — Fourfold Indeterminate

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vipākajairyāpathikaśailpasthānikanairmitam /
>
> caturdhāvyākṛtaṃ kāme rūpe śilpavivarjitam // 2.72 //

> Unobscured-indeterminate mind is fourfold in the Desire Realm:
> maturation-born, deportment-related, craft-related, and creation;
> in the Form Realm, it is without craft.

## 3. Continuous Translation

The word “dividing” continues from the preceding verse. In the
Desire Realm, unobscured-indeterminate mind divides into four:
maturation-born, concerned with deportment, concerned with crafts,
and a mind of creation. In the Form Realm it divides into three,
without the craft kind, since there are no crafts there. Thus the
twelve kinds of mind previously named become twenty: the three
wholesome kinds across the Realms become six, and the unobscured-
indeterminate kinds become eight in all. Deportment and the like
do not occur in the Formless Realm, since the corresponding
activities are absent there.

The Supports of these minds are visible Form, smell, taste, and
tangible; craft-associated mind can also have sound as Support.
These are kinds of mind Cognition. The five sensory Cognitions,
however, can prepare deportment and craft. Others say that a mind
Cognition produced by deportment has all twelve Essences as
possible Supports. The exposition now asks which of the twenty
kinds can follow which.

First are the eight Desire Realm kinds. Ten can follow an
application-produced wholesome mind: seven of its own level,
excluding the mind of creation; Form Realm application-produced
wholesome mind; and trainee and beyond-training minds. That
application-produced mind can itself follow eight: its own
wholesome and afflicted kinds, Form Realm application-produced
and afflicted minds, and trainee and beyond-training minds.

Nine can follow birth-acquired wholesome Desire Realm mind: the
same seven of its own level, excluding creation, together with
the afflicted minds of the Form and Formless Realms. Birth-acquired
wholesome mind can itself follow eleven: those seven of its own
level, Form Realm application-produced and afflicted minds, and
the trainee and beyond-training minds.

Seven can follow either unwholesome or obscured-indeterminate
Desire Realm mind: the seven kinds of their own level other than
creation. Either of these afflicted kinds can follow fourteen:
those seven of its own level; four Form Realm kinds other than
application-produced wholesome and creation; and three Formless
kinds other than application-produced wholesome.

Eight can follow either deportment-related or maturation-born
Desire Realm mind: six of its own level other than application-
produced wholesome and creation, plus the afflicted minds of the
Form and Formless Realms. Each of these two kinds can itself
follow seven of its own level, excluding creation. Six can follow
craft-related mind, all of its own level other than application-
produced wholesome and creation. Craft-related mind can itself
follow seven of its own level, excluding creation. A Desire Realm
mind of creation has only two immediate successors: another mind
of creation of its own kind and Form Realm application-produced
wholesome mind. It also has just those two predecessors.

Next are the six Form Realm kinds. Twelve can follow an
application-produced wholesome Form Realm mind: the two wholesome
Desire Realm kinds and its mind of creation, all six Form Realm
kinds, Formless application-produced wholesome mind, and the
trainee and beyond-training minds. That Form Realm application-
produced mind can itself follow ten: Desire Realm application-
produced wholesome and creation minds; four of its own level
other than deportment-related and maturation-born; Formless
application-produced and afflicted minds; and trainee and
beyond-training minds.

Eight can follow birth-acquired wholesome Form Realm mind: the
two afflicted Desire Realm kinds, five of its own level other
than creation, and Formless afflicted mind. It can itself follow
five of its own level, excluding creation. Nine can follow
obscured-indeterminate Form Realm mind: the two wholesome and
two afflicted Desire Realm kinds, and five of its own level
other than creation. It can itself follow eleven: Desire Realm
birth-acquired, deportment-related, and maturation-born minds;
five of its own level other than creation; and three Formless
kinds other than application-produced wholesome.

Seven can follow Form Realm deportment-related mind: two afflicted
Desire Realm kinds, four of its own level other than application-
produced wholesome and creation, and Formless afflicted mind.
It can itself follow five of its own level, excluding creation.
The maturation-born Form Realm kind follows the same pattern.
A Form Realm mind of creation can be followed only by its own
application-produced wholesome kind or another mind of creation;
it can itself arise only after those same two.

Now the four Formless Realm kinds. Seven can follow Formless
application-produced wholesome mind: Form Realm application-
produced wholesome mind, all four of its own level, and the
trainee and beyond-training minds. It can itself follow six:
Form Realm application-produced wholesome, three of its own
level other than maturation-born, and the trainee and beyond-
training minds. Seven can follow birth-acquired wholesome
Formless mind: all four of its own level and the three afflicted
kinds of the lower Realms. It can itself follow the four of its
own level.

Eight can follow afflicted Formless mind: all four of its own
level, Form Realm application-produced and afflicted minds,
and the two afflicted Desire Realm kinds. It can itself follow
ten: the four of its own level plus the birth-acquired,
deportment-related, and maturation-born kinds in each of the
two lower Realms. Six can follow maturation-born Formless mind:
three of its own level other than application-produced wholesome,
and the three afflicted lower-Realm kinds. It can itself follow
the four of its own level.

Six can follow trainee mind: application-produced wholesome minds
of the three Realms, birth-acquired wholesome Desire Realm mind,
trainee mind, and beyond-training mind. Trainee mind can follow
four: the three Realm application-produced minds and trainee
mind itself. Five can follow beyond-training mind, the trainee
successor list with trainee mind removed. Beyond-training mind
can follow five: the three Realm application-produced minds,
trainee mind, and beyond-training mind.

Why can maturation-born, deportment-related, and craft-related
minds arise after application-produced mind, while application-
produced mind does not arise after them? They turn toward
arranging deportment and craft, and are too weak and unprepared
to support application. A mind of emergence can follow an
application-produced mind without new preparation. Would the
same weakness exclude application-produced mind after afflicted
mind? Affliction is indeed adverse, but one wearied by its
manifestation can turn to application in order to understand it.
Birth-acquired wholesome Desire Realm mind is keen enough to
follow trainee or beyond-training mind and Form Realm application-
produced mind; those kinds do not follow it because it proceeds
without deliberate preparation. The birth-acquired kind can
follow afflicted Form Realm mind because it is keen, while
birth-acquired Form Realm mind does not follow afflicted Formless
mind because it lacks that keenness.

Three modes of attention are distinguished. Attention to an own
characteristic takes an item such as Form under its defining
mark. Attention to a common characteristic is associated with
the sixteen aspects. Resolute attention occurs in contemplations
of the unattractive, immeasurables, Formless attainments, releases,
spheres of mastery, totality spheres, and related practices. One
account says each of these three may immediately precede the
noble Path, and each may follow it. This makes sense of the
scriptural statement about cultivating the mindfulness factor
of awakening accompanied by contemplation of the unattractive.
Others say only common-characteristic attention immediately
precedes the noble Path, though all three may follow it. On
that account, contemplating the unattractive first tames the
mind, which then enters the Path through common-characteristic
attention; the scripture speaks of the whole sequence.

Others say only common-characteristic attention follows the
noble Path as well. An objection grants that one entering the
fixed course by relying on one of the three lower preparatory
levels might then present Desire Realm common-characteristic
attention. But what of entry relying on the second or a higher
meditation? Desire Realm attention is too remote. Such attention
on the same higher level has not previously been acquired apart
from what belongs to the stages leading to penetration, and an
ārya does not return to those preparatory stages after attaining
their result. The answer proposes that another attention of the
same general kind is developed there, contemplating that all
formations are impermanent, all dharmas are without self, and
nirvāṇa is peaceful. That answer is not accepted by the next
speaker [the local wording is compressed].

For one who attains arhatship depending on the preparatory level,
the emerging mind belongs to that level or to the Desire Realm.
For one depending on the sphere of nothingness, it belongs to
that sphere or the summit of existence; on the other levels it
belongs to its own level. The Desire Realm has three kinds of
attention by origin: from hearing, from thinking, and birth-
acquired; it has none formed through cultivation. The Form Realm
has hearing-produced, cultivation-produced, and birth-acquired
attention, but none formed through thinking, since beginning to
think there brings concentration itself. The Formless Realm has
cultivation-produced and birth-acquired attention. In these
Realms, five kinds of attention can precede the noble Path,
excluding the birth-acquired kinds because entry depends on
application. After the Path, even Desire Realm birth-acquired
attention may occur, owing to its keenness.

## 4. Reading Control

The verse completes the twenty-kind inventory by dividing
unobscured-indeterminate mind in the Desire and Form Realms.
The prose then tests immediately antecedent succession for all
twenty kinds, first listing successors and then predecessors
for each kind. The later dispute about attention and the noble
Path concerns whether an apparent succession is immediate or
passes through another mode of attention. Its alternative
positions should not be collapsed into a single rule.
