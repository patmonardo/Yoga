# VAK_2.63 Bhāṣya — Activity by State

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> nirudhyamāne kāritraṃ dvau hetū kurutas trayaḥ /
> jāyamāne tato 'nyau tu pratyayau tadviparyayāt // 2.63 //

> Two causes perform activity toward the ceasing. Three, toward the arising. The other two conditions, by the reverse of that.

From 100.19 through the close at 101.06. The next question is 2.64. Not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

In what state of a dharma? Ceasing is the present, directed toward cessation. Co-arisen and associated act toward it, on a fruit arisen with them. Arising is the future, directed toward arising. Homogeneous, pervasive, and maturation act toward it. The other two reverse that order. Antecedent gives an opening toward arising. Object is apprehended by present mind and factors. Predominance is non-obstruction in every state. The conditions, with their activities, have been explained.

## 3. Movement and Voice

The question governs the locatives. Five causes, two groups, then the reverse, then predominance added. No rival is named. Not a collation.

## 4. Organon Reading

Target-state is not cause-state. An opening is not a vacated place. Not the translation. Not the final pass.

## 5. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Three joined to arising. Domain withdrawn.

2.64 has not been opened.
