# VAK_2.58 — Efficacy and Predominance

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> यद्बलाज्जायते यत्तत्फलं पुरुषकारजम् ।
>
> अपूर्वः संस्कृतस्यैव संस्कृतोऽधिपतेः फलम् ॥ २.५८ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> yadvalāj jāyate yat tat phalaṃ puruṣakārajam /
>
> apūrvaḥ saṃskṛtasyaiva saṃskṛto 'dhipateḥ phalam // 2.58 //

Puruṣakāra is the figure for activity. Not Puruṣa. Apūrva is not earlier arisen, relative to the cause. Not creation from nothing.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Whatever arises through the force of which, that is the fruit born of efficacy. A conditioned dharma not earlier arisen is the fruit of predominance of the conditioned alone.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

A higher-level concentration, of a lower application-mind. An uncontaminated one, of a contaminated one. A transformation-mind, of a concentration-mind. And the rest. For cessation by Discrimination, say attained through its force. Not arisen.

A conditioned dharma other than one arisen earlier is the fruit of predominance of every conditioned dharma, relative to which it has not arisen earlier. Of the conditioned alone.

What distinguishes them? Fruit of efficacy, of the agent. Fruit of predominance, even of the non-agent. The crafted work is both, for the artisan. For others, predominance only.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

One work, two relations. Agency is the force through which it arises. Predominance is wider, and includes the non-agent. The others do not receive the artisan's agency.

The examples cross level and cross contamination. Efficacy is not duplication of the cause. The list is not a set of sufficient conditions.

Attained replaces arisen for the cessation. The unconditioned is not a new product. The dispute, entity or absence, is not closed.

Organon rendering:

The local concentration is a conditioned fruit. The ascent of Hetu:Pratyaya into Samādhi is not this example. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
