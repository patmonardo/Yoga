# VAK_2.57 — Three Fruits Defined

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> विपाकोऽव्याकृतो धर्मः सत्त्वाख्यो व्याकृतोद्भवः ।
>
> निःष्यन्दो हेतुसदृशो विसंयोगः क्षयो धिया ॥ २.५७ ॥

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Maturation is an indeterminate dharma, classed with a sentient being, arisen from the determinate. Homogeneous outflow is similar to its cause. Disconnection is cessation by Discrimination.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Unobscured-indeterminate. That could include what is not classed with a sentient being. Therefore classed with a sentient being. That could include increase, and homogeneous outflow. Therefore arisen from the determinate. Wholesome and unwholesome are determinate because they are specified toward maturation. What arises from them later, neither together nor immediately after, is maturation.

Why is a karmic product not classed with a sentient being not maturation? Shared. Another can use it. Maturation is unshared. One does not experience the maturation of another's karma. A predominant fruit is experienced because it arises from common karma.

Similar to its cause, as with the homogeneous and the pervasive. Why is the pervasive not simply the homogeneous? Similarity in level and in being afflicted, not in specific type. Where the type is also similar, it is accepted as homogeneous. Four cases. Homogeneous and not pervasive. Pervasive of another class. Pervasive of the same class. The rest, excluded.

Cessation is the gloss of kṣaya. Discrimination is the gloss of dhī. Cessation by Discrimination is the fruit of disconnection. No mechanism is given.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The cause is determinate. The maturation is indeterminate, later, and one continuum's. Shared use is predominance, from common karma. Not the other's maturation.

Outflow keeps a likeness. Level and affliction are the pervasive likeness. Type is the homogeneous likeness. Both can hold. Neither can hold.

Disconnection is not a fourth produced dharma in this definition. Cessation by Discrimination. The earlier dispute, entity or absence, is not closed. Wisdom destroying a capacity is not this gloss.

Organon rendering:

A course matures as an unshared later state. Continuation in kind is not that maturation. The disclosure is not a grade. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
