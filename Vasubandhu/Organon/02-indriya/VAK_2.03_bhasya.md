# VAK_2.03 Bhāṣya — Class, Defilement, Purification

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> nikāyasthitisaṃkleśavyavadānādhipatyataḥ /
> jīvitaṃ vedanāḥ pañca śraddhādyāś cendriyaṃ matāḥ // 2.03 //

> Because of governing efficacy over class-persistence, defilement, and purification, life, the five feelings, and those beginning with faith are held to be faculty.

The Bhāṣya limits those beginning with faith, here, to five, and confirms that each is individually a faculty. The three fields correspond to the three groups, in order.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit Witness

> nikāyasabhāgasthitau jīvitendriyasyādhipatyaṃ saṃkleśe vedanānām /
> tathā hi sukhāyāṃ vedanāyāṃ rāgo 'nuśete duḥkhāyāṃ pratigho
> 'duḥkhāsukhāyām avidyety uktaṃ sūtre /
> vyavadāne śraddhādīnāṃ pañcānām / tathā hi taiḥ kleśāś ca
> viṣkabhyante mārgaś cāvāhyate /
> ata ete 'pi pratyekam indriyam iṣṭāḥ /

The prose is printed 39.20–23 in `Vasubandhu/Sources/kosabhasya.txt`, after the verse at 39.18–19. It ends before the next kārikā at 40.01–02. Word division is editorial. No substantive emendation. The avagraha in 'duḥkhāsukhāyām is the elided a of aduḥkhāsukha, neither painful nor pleasant.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

The life-faculty has governing efficacy over the persistence of the homogeneous class of living beings, and the feelings over defilement. For it is said in the sūtra: attachment lies latent in pleasant feeling, aversion in painful feeling, and ignorance in neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. The five beginning with faith have governing efficacy over purification, for through them the afflictions are checked and the path is brought forward. Therefore each of these also is individually accepted as a faculty.

## 4. Movement and Voice of the Commentary

The paragraph continues the establishment of faculty. It assigns life its field, supports the feeling relation by a sūtra, explains the five beginning with faith, and closes with pratyekam, each individually.

There is no new objection and no named school. The sūtra explains the feelings. The other sentences are the surrounding commentary. The brevity stands.

## 5. Life and the Persistence of the Class

Nikāyasthiti is specified as nikāyasabhāgasthiti. The relation is persistence within a homogeneous class. The claim is sthiti, continued standing. It does not give a mechanism for the production of life, and it does not repair the difficult life-faculty reading in 2.01.

## 6. Five Faculties, Three Tones

The kārikā counts five feeling-faculties. The sūtra uses three tones: pleasant, painful, and neither. These are not competing totals.

The five are pleasure, pain, gladness, distress, and equanimity: sukha, duḥkha, saumanasya, daurmanasya, upekṣā. The quotation groups by tone. It does not restate the five definitions.

Anuśete, lies latent, is a relation of affliction to feeling. Pleasant feeling is not attachment. Painful feeling is not aversion. Neutral feeling is not ignorance. The quotation does not say every feeling produces its affliction. It does not unfold the status of latent tendencies, and it does not say that neutrality hides ignorance.

The preceding verse preserved a place for feeling in purification. This paragraph explains the assigned role in defilement.

## 7. The Two Operations of Purification

The five are śraddhā, vīrya, smṛti, samādhi, and prajñā: faith, vigor, mindfulness, concentration, and Discrimination. The prose specifies five. The opening verse grouped eight under purification. The remaining three are the next verse. They are not this unit.

Through them the afflictions are checked and the path is brought forward. Viṣkabhyante is checking. It is not irreversible abandonment. Āvāhyate is bringing the path forward. The two clauses are coordinated. The text does not say checking must finish before the path begins, and it does not assign a separate operation to each of the five. Pratyekam establishes individual faculty. It does not give five causal analyses.

## 8. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for Translation

The anchor's three fields follow the prose's order. Five, for the faith-group, is the Bhāṣya's specification. The kārikā says those beginning with faith. The singular indriyam is read through pratyekam, not as one faculty covering all eleven.

Sthiti is persistence. Checking and brought forward keep the two operations apart.

## 9. Organon Reading

Applied Indriya. The same predicate, governing efficacy, receives three fields: standing of the class, defilement, purification.

Defilement and purification are the Kośa Path. The five feelings and the five beginning with faith are its controllers. Life controls the standing of the class beside that path. Checking is not final abandonment. The negation, when it comes, is of the afflictive relation, not of the feeling and not of the principle in which the faculty was located. The principles remain deposited. Not the verse English.

## 10. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Domain withdrawn. Discernment withdrawn. The path-controllers are marked, not printed as the translation.

The complete commentary is one paragraph. The five faculties and the three tones stay distinct. 2.04 has not been opened.
