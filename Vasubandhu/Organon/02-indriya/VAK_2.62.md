# VAK_2.62 — The Other Three Conditions

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> चित्तचैत्ता अचरमा उत्पन्नाः समनन्तरः ।
>
> आलम्बनं सर्वधर्माः कारणाख्योऽधिपः स्मृतः ॥ २.६२ ॥

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Arisen mind and mental factors, not final, are the immediately antecedent. All dharmas are the object-condition. What is named the conditioning cause is held to be the predominant.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Excluding an arhat's final mind and factors. Equal and immediate. Form is not this condition. Its arising is mixed. A second stream of accumulated form while the first has not ceased, on one account. Lesser from greater, and greater from lesser, on another. Ashes from straw. A banyan from a seed.

Mental factors vary in number across wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate, and across the concentrations. Not within one kind: not several feelings from one feeling. Not therefore own-kind only. The whole complex is the condition of the next whole complex. Own-kind only would block the first uncontaminated mind. Dissociated formations are not this condition. Mixed manifestation.

Future is not this condition. No earlier and later there. How the Blessed One knows a future after a future is disputed: inference from past and present, a sign in the continuum, or knowing by wishing, the domain of a Buddha inconceivable. Not settled. A fixed path-succession is what has its arising bound to a given condition, as sprout from seed, even without this condition.

The final mind is not connected with another mind. It remains mind as support. Support is not this operative activity. A fruit taken by this condition cannot be prevented from arising. Emergence after an attainment without mind is immediate because no other mind intervenes. Not because the interval vanishes. The immediately antecedent condition has been explained.

All dharmas, as appropriate. Visible form of eye-cognition and what is associated. Sound, smell, taste, tangible, of the rest. All dharmas, of mental cognition. An object remains an object when not apprehended, as wood is fuel when not burning. Fixed by essence, entity, and characteristic. Support is a further question. The wording is damaged. Others keep past ones joined to support. The object-condition has been explained.

What is the conditioning cause is the predominant condition. More extensive: co-arisen dharmas are not one another's objects, and are one another's predominant conditions. Every dharma, of every conditioned dharma other than itself. A dharma is not a condition of itself, under any of the four. The conditioned is not a condition of the unconditioned, nor the unconditioned of the unconditioned.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Three relations. Succession of the mental complex. Object, whether or not now apprehended. Predominance, the wide non-obstruction, including what does not produce.

Own-kind succession is refused. The first uncontaminated mind is the refusal. Homogeneous cause is a different requirement, and still stands. Form can depend, as seed and sprout, without being this condition.

Immediate is no intervening mind. Not the previous clock-moment. The final mind supports and does not give a successor.

Organon rendering:

The rule-system follows as a complex, not factor by factor. Produced consciousness, not Citta the Universal. The local attainment is not the ascent. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
