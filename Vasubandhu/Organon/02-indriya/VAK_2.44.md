# VAK_2.44 — Support and First Production

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> बोधिलभ्या मुनेर्न प्राक् चतुस्त्रिंशत्क्षणाप्तितः ।
>
> कामरूपाश्रये भूते निरोधाख्यादितो नृषु ॥ २.४४ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

For the Sage, acquired with awakening, not beforehand, because attained in thirty-four moments. When a desire- or form-realm support is present. The one called cessation, at first, among humans.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

The first half stands as in 2.43. Not settled.

Both attainments arise with a support in the desire realm or the form realm. Denial of the non-reflective attainment in the form realm conflicts with the passage. The attainment of cessation is first produced among humans, and later in the form realm by those who had lost it. Loss is required. Otherwise the Udāyin sūtra is contradicted: a monk accomplished in conduct, concentration, and Discrimination enters and emerges from cessation of reflection and feeling, and, if not liberated in this life, is born in a divine mind-made body, and enters it there. That body is form-realm. The attainment is of the summit. Without loss, that rebirth would not fit.

Another school wants an attainment of cessation of the fourth dhyāna as well. Not established. The sūtra says nine successive attainments. Out of order is for one who has obtained full mastery. The rule is for the beginner.

They differ by level, application, continuum, result, maturation, and first production. Both are cessation of consciousness and the mental factors. The names follow the preparatory opposition: non-reflective, and cessation of reflection and feeling. As knowledge of another's consciousness also knows feeling and the rest.

How does consciousness arise again? The Vaibhāṣikas: the past exists, and is the immediately preceding condition. Others: form arises from consciousness after long cessation. Consciousness arises from the body endowed with faculties. The earlier teachers: each is seed of the other. Vasumitra: the fault belongs to an attainment without consciousness; mine has consciousness. Ghoṣaka: untenable. Where cognition is, contact is the meeting of the three, and feeling, reflection, and volition are conditioned by contact. They would not cease. The cases are unequal. Craving depends on feeling born of contact with ignorance. Contact is not so qualified for feeling. Therefore, the Vaibhāṣikas, without consciousness.

How is that an attainment? It brings the great elements to equilibrium. One enters it by the consciousness of entry. Real entities, because they obstruct arising? No. The entry-consciousness itself obstructs. For an interval, mere non-operation. That is designated the attainment. It was not before, and is not after emerging. Conditioned, by designation. Or the support brought to that condition. The non-reflective result likewise. They do not accept this. The two attainments have been explained.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Support and level stay apart. A form-realm body can carry an attainment of the summit. First production is human. Later entry presupposes loss. Mastery skips. The beginner does not.

The names do not narrow the cessation. Reflection and feeling are what the preparation opposes. Consciousness and the mental factors are what cease.

Return is the live dispute. A past consciousness as condition. Reciprocal seed of consciousness and the faculty-body. An attainment that has consciousness. Without consciousness, and a real obstruction. Or the entry-consciousness itself, and mere non-operation designated. Not settled.

Organon rendering:

The return is not a pass. Non-operation designated is not the nirodha of virodha. Not the verse English. Not the final pass.
