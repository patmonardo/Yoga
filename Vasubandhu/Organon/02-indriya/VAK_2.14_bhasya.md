# VAK_2.14 Bhāṣya — Initial Acquisition as Maturation

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāmeṣv ādau vipāko dve labhyate nopapādukaiḥ /
>
> taiḥ ṣaḍ vā sapta vā aṣṭau vā ṣaḍ rūpeṣv ekam uttare // 2.14 //

> In the desire realm, at the beginning, two faculties are acquired as maturation, the body-faculty and the life-faculty, but not by the spontaneously born. By the spontaneously born, six, seven, or eight are acquired: six without sexual characteristic, seven with one, eight with both. In the form realm, six. In the higher, the formless, one: life alone. Mind and neutral feeling are not among these, because at relinking both are necessarily afflicted.

Labhyate stays singular with dve. Taiḥ is the commentary's reading of teḥ. The question is initial acquisition as maturation, not every faculty present.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Acquisition must now be explained. How many faculties are first acquired as maturation, and in which realm? Initially in the desire realm, two are acquired as maturation: the body-faculty and the life-faculty. These, however, are acquired not by the spontaneously born. Since the spontaneously born are excluded, the statement is to be understood of the egg-born, womb-born, and moisture-born. Why not the faculties of mind and neutral feeling? Because at the time of relinking both are necessarily afflicted.

How many are acquired by the spontaneously born? By them, six, if they have no sexual characteristic, like the beings at the beginning of the cosmic age. Which six? Eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and life. Or seven, if they have one sexual characteristic, as among gods and others. Or eight, if they have both. Are there spontaneously born beings with both sexual characteristics? There are, in the unfortunate destinies.

So much for the desire realm. How is it in the form and formless realms? Six in the forms. The desire realm is designated desires because desire predominates there. The form realm is designated forms because form predominates there. It is also said in a sūtra: those peaceful liberations, monks, which, having transcended forms, are formless. In the form realm, six faculties are first acquired as maturation: the same ones acquired in the desire realm by spontaneously born beings without sexual characteristics.

One in the higher. The formless realm is higher than the form realm, because it is beyond it with respect to meditative attainment and superior with respect to rebirth. There only one faculty, the life-faculty, is first acquired as maturation, and no other. Acquisition has been explained.

## 4. Movement and Voice of the Commentary

The question specifies acquisition, the initial time, maturation, and realm. Desire-realm birth is split, then the spontaneously born by sexual characteristic. Form and formless complete the distribution.

Two interruptions: why mind and neutral feeling are out of the first count, and whether spontaneous birth can have both marks. Both are answered. No opponent. No school. The sūtra supports the realm-language. It is not a proof of the counts.

## 5. Presence and Acquisition

Afflicted at relinking is why mind and neutral feeling are not maturation. Occurrence is not denied. That continues 2.10: identity is not the causal status of the instance.

Two does not mean no mind and no feeling. One in the formless realm is the maturation-count. It is not formless existence as life without mental activity. The eight of 2.12 were another question.

## 6. The Desire-Realm Counts

| Condition | First acquired as maturation | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Egg-born, womb-born, moisture-born | body and life | 2 |
| Spontaneous, no characteristic | five and life | 6 |
| Spontaneous, one | the six plus one | 7 |
| Spontaneous, both | the six plus both | 8 |

The six include life, not mind. The seventh and eighth are the marks. The birth-modes and the unfortunate destinies stay the account. No embryonic theory is added.

## 7. The Two Higher Realms

Form receives the same six as the spontaneous desire-realm case without marks. The shared list is the maturation-count. It is not identical sensory activity.

Formless receives life alone under this predicate. Higher is attainment and rebirth. Those are not the same event. Neither higher nor the smaller count is liberation. The triad stays unconnected.

## 8. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for Translation

Lābha is acquisition. Prathamataḥ explains ādau. Vipāka is carried through every number. Dropping it would turn the inventory into every faculty present.

Vyañjana is sexual characteristic: none, one, both. Not arbitrary marks. Upapāduka is spontaneously born. The negative assigns the two to the other three modes.

Desires and forms are realm-names by predominance. Uttara is higher, not a direction.

## 9. Organon Reading

What can belong to a realm does not settle what is first acquired there as maturation. Presence, acquisition, time, and mode of arising stay apart. Mind and neutral feeling are the case: present at relinking, not in the count.

Membership and consequence are the inherited doctrine, from the Vidyā. This verse asks the consequence: which members are first acquired as the result. The indriyas are analyzed in that light. Not the verse English.
