# VAK_2.53

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> प्रयोगजास्तयोरेव श्रुतचिन्तामयादिकाः ।
>
> संप्रयुक्तकहेतुस्तु चित्तचैत्ताः समाश्रयाः ॥ २.५३ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> prayogajās tayor eva śrutacintāmayādikāḥ /
>
> saṃprayuktakahetus tu cittacaittāḥ samāśrayāḥ // 2.53 //

## 3. Padaccheda

```text
prayogajās                 → prayogajāḥ
tayor                      → tayoḥ
eva                        → eva
śrutacintāmayādikāḥ        → śruta-cintā-maya-ādikāḥ
saṃprayuktakahetus         → saṃprayuktaka-hetuḥ
tu                         → tu
cittacaittāḥ               → citta-caittāḥ
samāśrayāḥ                 → sama-āśrayāḥ
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| prayogajāḥ | nominative masculine plural compound | produced through application or deliberate practice |
| tayoḥ | locative dual pronoun | with respect to those two: the equal and the superior |
| eva | restrictive particle | only |
| śruta-cintā-maya-ādikāḥ | nominative masculine plural compound | beginning with those formed through hearing and reflection |
| saṃprayuktaka-hetuḥ | nominative masculine singular compound | associated cause |
| tu | adversative/transition particle | but / now |
| citta-caittāḥ | nominative masculine plural coordinative compound | citta and mental factors |
| sama-āśrayāḥ | nominative masculine plural compound | having the same support |

`Tayoḥ eva` resumes the equal-or-superior pair (`sama-viśiṣṭa`) from VAK
2.52. The first half therefore completes the homogeneous-cause rule before
`tu` turns to the associated cause.

## 4. Grammar

The first half has an understood causal predicate from the preceding verse:

```text
śruta-cintā-maya-ādikāḥ prayogajāḥ
    → tayoḥ eva [sabhāga-hetavaḥ]

application-produced qualities,
beginning with those born of hearing and reflection
    → are homogeneous causes only for the equal and superior
```

The restrictive `eva` is essential: deliberate application does not produce
an intentionally inferior homogeneous result.

The second half gives a new nominal definition:

```text
sama-āśrayāḥ citta-caittāḥ
    → saṃprayuktaka-hetuḥ

citta and mental factors having the same support
    → are associated cause
```

The plural members fall under the singular causal type. The Bhāṣya specifies
that “same” means numerically the very same support, not merely another
support of the same kind.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Qualities produced through application, beginning with those formed through hearing and reflection, [are homogeneous causes] only with respect to those two—the equal and the superior. But citta and mental factors having the same support are associated cause.

### Bhāṣya-informed translation

> Qualities generated by deliberate practice—those born through hearing, reflection, cultivation, and related modes—continue causally only into equal or superior qualities. Associated cause, by contrast, is the reciprocal causal relation of citta and its mental factors when they share the numerically identical faculty-support and satisfy the complete fivefold association-profile.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Practice is causally formative: hearing, reflection, and cultivation generate capacities whose homogeneous continuation preserves or intensifies their achieved grade. Within each cognitive occurrence, citta and its mental operations form one associated causal complex only when they arise from the same concrete support. Similar content and simultaneous occurrence do not by themselves constitute one act of cognition.

The two movements are:

```text
diachronic Technē:
    application → equal or superior cultivated capacity

synchronic association:
    one support → one internally articulated Citta-event
```

| pañca-samatā | five equalities | support, object, mode of apprehension, time, and individual occurrence |
| āśraya | support | faculty-moment grounding the cognitive event |
| ālambana | cognitive object-support | that with respect to which the cognition operates |
| ākāra | mode of apprehension | determinate manner in which the object is cognitively taken |

The fifth equality is often expressed through `dravya`, numerical
individuality. Here it should be understood as identity of the relevant
individual occurrence, not as an independently reified substance.

## 8. Logical Determination

The application-produced rule completes VAK 2.52:

```text
ProducedThroughApplication(x)
AND SabhagaHetu(x, y)
    → Grade(y) ≥ Grade(x)
```

The associated-cause definition is:

```text
Citta(c)
AND MentalFactor(m)
AND NumericallyIdentical(Support(c), Support(m))
AND SameObject(c, m)
AND SameModeOfApprehension(c, m)
AND SameTime(c, m)
AND SameIndividualOccurrence(c, m)
    → SamprayuktakaHetu(c, m)
    AND SamprayuktakaHetu(m, c)
```

The Bhāṣya reaches numerical identity of support by eliminating insufficient
criteria:

```text
SameObject(x, y)              ⇏ SameCognitiveEvent(x, y)
SameMode(x, y)                ⇏ SameCognitiveEvent(x, y)
SameTime(x, y)                ⇏ SameCognitiveEvent(x, y)
SameObjectAndTimeAndMode(x,y) ⇏ SameCognitiveEvent(x, y)
```

For example:

```text
PersonA looks at NewMoon
PersonB looks at NewMoon

SameObject = true
SameTime = true
PossiblySameMode = true
SameSupport = false

therefore:
    NOT OneAssociatedCittaEvent
```

Associated cause is a subtype of co-arisen cause:

```text
SamprayuktakaHetu(x, y)
    → SahabhuHetu(x, y)
```

but their explanatory intensions differ:

```text
SahabhuHetu
    → mutual causal efficacy and mutual resulthood

SamprayuktakaHetu
    → conjunction through the five equalities
```

After completing this definition, the Bhāṣya begins the next causal type:

```text
PriorPervasiveAffliction(x)
AND LaterAfflictedDharma(y)
AND SameLevel(x, y)
    → SarvatragaHetu(x, y)
```

Unlike homogeneous cause, pervasive cause can generate an afflicted dharma
of another class. That prose transition is Bhāṣya material beyond the two
definitions stated in the kārikā itself.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 2.53 joins two sides of Technē without confusing them. `Prayogaja`
describes diachronic production through disciplined activity:

```text
hearing → reflection → cultivation
```

These are not three labels for already possessed knowledge. They are modes
through which a capacity is generated and transmitted toward equal or
superior form. This gives our architectural phrase “Yoga-Technē” a precise
comparative basis: Yoga becomes technically operative when practice is
analyzed through the determinate transformations it can produce.

That does not make `Yogācāra` a literal Sanskrit synonym for the modern
compound “Yoga-Technē,” nor does it make Yogācāra identical with the Kośa's
Vaibhāṣika positions. The useful relation is systematic:

```text
Yoga
    → architecture and Path of realization

Kośa
    → exact conditioned analysis of practices,
      events, causes, results, and grades

Yoga-Technē
    → Yoga rendered operationally accountable through such analysis
```

The associated-cause analysis then gives the synchronic side. One Citta-event
cannot be inferred from matching representations alone. Shared object, time,
and mode may occur across distinct continua. Numerical identity of support is
what encloses citta and caittas as this cognitive occurrence.

For an Ocular Schema:

```text
AssociatedCittaEvent {
    supportId,
    object,
    apprehensionMode,
    time,
    occurrenceId,
    citta,
    caittas,
    reciprocalCausalProfile
}
```

This is directly relevant to Agent-to-Agent transmission. Two Agents can
process the same Corpus unit at the same time and even produce similar
interpretations without thereby sharing one Citta-event. Their supports,
provenance, and event identities remain distinct. Oculus can transmit a
reconstructible determination without pretending to collapse two Agents into
one continuum.

The Bhāṣya's subsequent pervasive-cause discussion shows another kind of
Technē: contamination can propagate across classes rather than merely
reproduce its exact likeness. That topic is recorded here as the prose
transition but should not displace the kārikā's actual focus on
application-produced qualities and associated cause.

The textual layers are:

```text
kārikā
    → completes the prayogaja homogeneous rule
      and defines associated cause by same support

Bhāṣya
    → establishes numerical support identity,
      five equalities, and the associated/co-arisen distinction;
      then begins pervasive cause

Organon reconstruction
    → distinguishes diachronic Yoga-Technē from
      the synchronic Ocular enclosure of one Citta-event
```
