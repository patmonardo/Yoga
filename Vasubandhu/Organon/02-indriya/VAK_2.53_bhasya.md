# VAK_2.53 Bhāṣya — Application and the Associated Cause

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> prayogajās tayor eva śrutacintāmayādikāḥ /
> saṃprayuktakahetus tu cittacaittāḥ samāśrayāḥ // 2.53 //

> Produced by application, of those two only: born of hearing, of thought, and the rest. But the associated cause: consciousness and the mental factors, of the same support.

From 87.20 through 88.27. The pervasive cause at 89.01 is not opened.

## 2. Continuous Conventional Translation

Worldly qualities produced by application are homogeneous causes of the equal or the superior only. Hearing, thought, cultivation, by realm, as each is present. Nine grades, downward in reach as the grade rises. Birth-acquired wholesome, and the afflicted, of one another. Four unobscured-indeterminate, of four, three, two, and one. Effort is not fruitless. Decline is still stated. Prior acquisition is not prior arising. A future dharma is not this cause.

Associated cause requires the same support. Aspect, object, and time are not enough. The new moon is the other-continuum case. Same means non-different: one faculty-moment. Associated is also co-arisen. Not the converse. Five equalities. The associated cause has been explained.

## 3. Movement and Voice

The Path rule extended, then contrasted with birth and affliction, then closed. The second half is a new cause, narrowed step by step. Voices stay in the objections. Not a collation.

## 4. Organon Reading

Grade-direction is the application. Association is the five, not a shared look at the moon. One instance is not one substance. Not the translation. Not the final pass.

## 5. Review Status

Fine-tuned with the kārikā. Thought is not reflection. Domain withdrawn.

2.54 has not been opened.
