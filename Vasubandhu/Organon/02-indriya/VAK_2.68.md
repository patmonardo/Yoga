## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> Unobscured [consciousness] follows five, and seven follow it. In the
> Form realm, eleven follow the wholesome, which follows nine.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Desire Realm unobscured-indeterminate follows five, and seven follow it.
> In the Form realm, eleven follow wholesome consciousness, which itself
> follows nine.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The verse states the succession constraints for desire unobscured and Form wholesome. The Bhāṣya enumerates the five and the seven, and notes that the eleven exclude formless unobscured-indeterminate.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The five predecessors are the four desire-level kinds and Form wholesome, from which supernormal-creation consciousness arises. Seven successors include the four desire-level kinds, Form wholesome after creation-consciousness, and Form and formless obscured at rebirth-linking. Eleven follow Form wholesome except formless unobscured-indeterminate; its nine predecessors exclude the two afflicted desire kinds and formless unobscured-indeterminate.

The verse states the constraints for desire unobscured and Form wholesome. The next verse continues with Form obscured and unobscured.
