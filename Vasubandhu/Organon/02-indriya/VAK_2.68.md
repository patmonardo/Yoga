# VAK_2.68 — Neutral Desire, Wholesome Form

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> पञ्चभ्योऽनिवृतं तस्मात्सप्त चित्तान्यनन्तरम् ।
>
> रूपे दशैकं च शुभान्नवभ्यस्तदनन्तरम् ॥ २.६८ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

Desire continues. Unobscured is unobscured-indeterminate. Ten and one are eleven. That is the form wholesome mind, arising after nine.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Unobscured from five. Immediately after it, seven minds. In the form realm, eleven from wholesome. That, immediately after nine.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Desire unobscured-indeterminate follows the four of its level and form wholesome, from which the mind of supernormal creation arises. Seven follow it: the four, form wholesome after the creation-mind, form obscured at rebirth-linking, formless obscured only at rebirth-linking.

Eleven follow form wholesome, excluding formless unobscured-indeterminate. It follows nine, excluding the two afflicted desire kinds and formless unobscured-indeterminate.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Creation-mind is the neutral member in the cross-realm pair. Not ordinary construction. Both directions are stated for that pair. Rebirth-linking is a different occasion. Afflicted in the higher realms is obscured-indeterminate. Unwholesome is absent there.

Eleven and nine are exclusions, not a converse. The two afflicted desire kinds follow form wholesome and do not precede it.

Organon rendering:

Direction stays in the count. Not the verse. Not the final pass.
