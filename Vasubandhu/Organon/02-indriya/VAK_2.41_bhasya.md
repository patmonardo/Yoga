# VAK_2.41 Bhāṣya — Same-Class and the Non-Reflective Result

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sabhāgatā sattvasāmyam āsaṃjñikam asaṃjñiṣu /
>
> nirodhaś cittacaittānāṃ vipākaḥ te bṛhatphalāḥ // 2.41 //

> Same-class is sameness among beings. The non-reflective, among the non-reflective, is the cessation of consciousness and the mental factors, a maturation of the attainment. Those so born are the Great Fruit; they are reflective at birth and at death, and the arising of reflection ends their stay.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

What is same-class? Sameness among beings. An entity, on that account. Undifferentiated, of all beings as beings. Differentiated, by realm, ground, destiny, birth, species, sex, discipline, and path-status. Four alternatives on dying and being reborn with or without a change of same-class. If ordinary same-class is an entity, ordinary-person status is a duplicate. People recognize sameness of kind without seeing it. What work? Why not the non-sentient? How are distinct same-classes designated as same-class? The Vaiśeṣika universal is the comparison. The sūtra says same-class with human beings. Said. Not said to be a distinct entity. The formations as constituted take the designation. Not further explained.

The non-reflective is cessation of consciousness and the mental factors, maturation of the attainment, a region of the Great Fruit. Reflective at birth and at death. Arising of reflection ends the stay. Like waking from a long sleep. Desire realm, nowhere else.

## 4. Organon Reading

Shared designation, resemblance, and a postulated real stay apart. The universal is the comparison. Not the translation. Non-conscious stays withdrawn. Not the final pass.
