# VAK_2.41 Bhāṣya — Same-Class and the Non-Reflective Result

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sabhāgatā sattvasāmyam āsaṃjñikam asaṃjñiṣu /
> nirodhaś cittacaittānāṃ vipākaḥ te bṛhatphalāḥ // 2.41 //

> Same-class is sameness among beings. The non-reflective among the non-reflective is cessation of consciousness and the mental factors, a maturation. They are the Great Fruit.

Commonality is withdrawn. It collapsed the verse into the Vaiśeṣika universal.

## 2. The Clarification

Sabhāgatā is same-class. Sattvasāmyam is the definition: sameness among beings. Nikāyasabhāga is the treatise's name: group same-class. Undifferentiated same-class is beings as beings. Differentiated same-class is realm, ground, destiny, birth, species, sex, discipline, path-status.

Sāmānya is a different word. The passage brings in the Vaiśeṣika category so that the dispute is visible: a common cognition, they say, requires a universal, one occurring in many, manifested or not. The Vaibhāṣika appeal is a sūtra that says same-class with human beings. The reply is that the word was said, and a distinct entity was not. The alternative is the formations as constituted. It is not further explained.

Same-class does not mean the universal. Commonality did.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

What is same-class? Sameness among beings. An entity, on that account. Four alternatives on dying and being reborn with or without a change of same-class. If ordinary same-class is an entity, ordinary-person status is a duplicate. People recognize sameness of kind without seeing it. What work? Why not the non-sentient? How are distinct same-classes designated as same-class?

The non-reflective is cessation of consciousness and the mental factors, maturation of the attainment, a region of the Great Fruit. Reflective at birth and at death. Arising of reflection ends the stay. Like waking from a long sleep. Desire realm, nowhere else.

## 4. Organon Reading

Shared designation, resemblance, and a postulated real stay apart. The universal is the comparison. Not the translation. Non-conscious stays withdrawn. Not the final pass.
