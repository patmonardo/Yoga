# VAK_2.45

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> लक्षणानि पुनर्जातिर्जरा स्थितिरनित्यता ॥ २.४५ ॥

## 4. Grammar

The first half defines `jīvita` through an equation and a relative clause:

```text
āyuḥ = jīvitam
    → lifespan is the life-faculty

yaḥ ūṣma-vijñānayoḥ ādhāraḥ
    → that which is the support
      of vital heat and differentiated cognition
```

The Bhāṣya reports a Vaibhāṣika inference from this support-function to
a distinct life-substance. Vasubandhu accepts the explanandum—the projected
continuance of a living series—while rejecting the additional substance.

The second half is another equational definition:

```text
lakṣaṇāni = jātiḥ, jarā, sthitiḥ, anityatā
    → the marks are arising, aging, persistence,
      and impermanence
```

The kārikā's order places `jarā` before `sthiti`; the Bhāṣya normally
explains the lifecycle as arising, persistence, aging, and impermanence.
The translation preserves the verse order while the logical reconstruction
states the genetic order explicitly.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> Lifespan is the life-faculty, that which is the support of vital heat and differentiated cognition. The marks, in turn, are arising, aging, persistence, and impermanence.

### Bhāṣya-informed translation

> `Jīvita` names the lifespan through which a living continuum sustains vital heat and differentiated cognition. According to Vasubandhu, it is not an additional life-substance but the karmically projected duration of a homogeneous continuum. The conditioned is marked by arising, persistence, transformation between earlier and later phases, and interruption of continuity; these marks describe the stream's own temporal structure rather than four entities acting upon it from outside.

## 6. Limited Organon Reading

The verse places the debate over lifespan beside the debate over
conditioned marks. The Bhāṣya distinguishes the projected duration of a
living continuum from the four marks and preserves the dispute over whether
those marks are separate entities or descriptions of a stream. Keep these
claims and their attributions distinct.

`Jīvita` and the four marks remain in the Kośa's catalog of formations
dissociated from citta. The catalog location is reported but does not govern
the Organon reconstruction; Vasubandhu's own analysis makes them temporal and
causal determinations of the continuum.

## 8. Logical Determination

The Vaibhāṣika life model is:

```text
RealJivitaEntity(L)
∧ Supports(L, VitalHeat)
∧ Supports(L, Vijnana)
    → LivingContinuity(S)
```

Vasubandhu's projected-duration model is:

```text
KarmicProjection(K, S, duration)
∧ HomogeneousContinuum(S)
    → PersistsFor(S, duration)

Jivita(S)
    := ProjectedPersistenceDuration(S)
```

The Vaibhāṣika marks model adds eight entities to a conditioned dharma:

```text
Dharma(D)
+ 4 PrimaryMarks(D)
+ 4 SecondaryMarks(primaryMarks)
```

Primary marks act on the other eight members, while each secondary mark acts
on its corresponding primary mark. This is the attributed attempt to stop
an infinite regress.

The preferred stream model requires no additional mark-substances:

```text
Jati(Stream)
    := Begins(Stream)

Sthiti(Stream)
    := ConnectedContinuation(Stream)

Jara(Stream)
    := EarlierPhase(Stream) ≠ LaterPhase(Stream)

Anityata(Stream)
    := ContinuationIsInterrupted(Stream)
```

At moment-resolution:

```text
Arising(m)
    := m occurs after prior nonexistence

Persistence(m1, m2)
    := m1 is causally connected with successor m2

Aging(m1, m2)
    := m1 and m2 differ within the connected sequence

Impermanence(m)
    := what arose fails to continue
```

Thus:

```text
MarkOf(D, M)
    ⇏ SeparateSubstance(M, D)
```

## 9. Interpretive Note

The life-faculty and the conditioned marks are both discussed as formations
dissociated from citta, but the Bhāṣya treats different questions in each
case. Its answer about projected lifespan should not be substituted for the
debate over the reality of the four marks; nor should the stream account be
mistaken for a denial that lifespan is discussed.
