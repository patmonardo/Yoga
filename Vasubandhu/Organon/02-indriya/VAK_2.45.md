# VAK_2.45 — Life and the Marks

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> आयुर्जीवितमाधार ऊष्मविज्ञायोर्हि यः ।
>
> लक्षणानि पुनर्जातिर्जरा स्थितिरनित्यता ॥ २.४५ ॥

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Life is lifespan, the support of warmth and cognition. The marks, in turn, are arising, aging, persistence, and impermanence.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

What is the life faculty? Lifespan of the three realms. What dharma is lifespan? The support of warmth and cognition. When lifespan, warmth, and cognition leave the body, it lies cast aside like a senseless piece of wood. If each supports the others, which ceases first? Perpetual non-cessation would follow. Karma, then, supports lifespan, for as far as karma has projected. Why not karma as the support of warmth and cognition? Lest all cognition until death be maturation. Warmth supporting cognition fails in the formless realm, where warmth is absent. Therefore lifespan is the support of both.

Not that lifespan does not exist. Not a distinct entity. The projection, by karma of the three realms, of how long the group same-class is to persist. Like the ripening-time of a crop, or the flight of a released arrow. An impetus-quality in the arrow does not explain the differences of speed and fall. They maintain a distinct real. Not settled.

Death is not only exhaustion of lifespan. Four cases. Exhaustion of lifespan-maturation. Exhaustion of enjoyment-maturation. Both. Failure to avoid harm. Relinquishment of lifespan falls in the first. When lifespan is exhausted, exhaustion of merit has no further power. Untimely death exists. Bound to the continuum, for desire-realm beings in the two attainments. Persisting once arisen, where the support is not impaired. One's own intention, another's, both, or neither, are four acquisitions of embodied existence. The summit is the sūtra's answer for neither. Life has been explained.

The marks of the conditioned. A dharma in which they occur is conditioned. The reverse is unconditioned. The sūtra says three. Persistence is the one not stated, or aging is alteration of persistence. Arising moves from future to present. Aging and impermanence from present to past. Persistence embraces. Secondary marks, arising-of-arising and the rest, operate on one. The primary on eight. No regress, on that account. Splitting space, say the Sautrāntikas. No evidence of separate entities. The marks are of the stream. Beginning, continuation, difference of earlier and later, interruption. A moment's arising is not what is discerned. Moment by moment, without separate entities: occurring after not occurring, connection with a successor, their difference, failure to continue. They do not accept this.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Life is the projected duration of a living series. Warmth and cognition are what it supports. A real life-entity is the inference from that support. The critical account keeps the duration and refuses the entity. The arrow is the test. An extra quality does not explain the flight.

The marks are the stream's own temporal structure, on the critical account. Four entities acting from outside, on the other. Three in the sūtra, four in the treatise. Secondary marks stop a regress by a stipulation. The stream account does not need them. Not settled.

The wood is senseless. That is the sūtra. It is not the non-reflective, and not unconsciousness.

Organon rendering:

Duration of the course is not a classmate. The marks are how a Determinate Dharma begins, continues, alters, and fails to continue. Not the verse English. Not the final pass.
