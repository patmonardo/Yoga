# VAK_2.73 Bhāṣya — Acquisition, Not Succession

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kliṣṭe traidhātuke lābhaḥ ṣaṇṇāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ dvayoḥ śubhe /
>
> trayāṇāṃ rūpaje śaikṣe caturṇāṃ tasya śeṣite // 2.73 //

> For afflicted minds across the three Realms, acquisition is of
> six, six, and two kinds; for wholesome Form Realm mind, of three;
> for trainee mind, of four; for the remainder, of that mind itself.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

Of the twelve kinds of mind previously listed, when a given kind
is present, how many kinds can be acquired? When an afflicted
Desire Realm mind is present, six can be acquired. They are
wholesome Desire Realm mind, through the restoration of wholesome
roots and return to the Realm; unwholesome and obscured-
indeterminate Desire Realm minds and afflicted Form Realm mind,
through return to the Realm or decline; and afflicted Formless
Realm mind and trainee mind through decline. The source's compact
phrasing also mentions doubt in the restoration case.

When afflicted Form Realm mind is present, six can be acquired:
the three Form Realm kinds and unobscured-indeterminate Desire
Realm mind through return to the Realm, and afflicted Formless
Realm mind and trainee mind through decline. When afflicted
Formless Realm mind is present, two can be acquired through
decline: that afflicted mind itself and trainee mind.

When wholesome Form Realm mind is present, three can be acquired:
that wholesome mind itself and unobscured-indeterminate minds of
the Desire and Form Realms. When trainee mind is present, four
can be acquired: trainee mind itself, the unobscured-indeterminate
minds of the Desire and Form Realms, and wholesome Formless Realm
mind. This occurs in dispassion from Desire and Form through the
noble Path. For any remaining kind of mind whose acquisition has
not been explained, only that mind itself is acquired, not another.

Others state the rule without these distinctions: when afflicted
mind is present, nine kinds are acquired; when wholesome mind is
present, six; when indeterminate mind is present, itself alone.
The reply says the wholesome count should be seven. These are
wholesome Desire Realm mind through restoration of wholesome roots
by right view; unobscured-indeterminate minds of Desire and Form
through dispassion; wholesome minds of Form and Formless through
obtaining the relevant concentration; and trainee and beyond-
training minds upon entry into the fixed course and attainment of
arhatship. The other cases follow from the preceding explanation.

A summarizing verse says that a mind is acquired by one who does
not yet possess it at rebirth, attainment, dispassion, decline,
and the restoration of wholesome roots. The discussion of
Conditions is complete. The *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya*'s second
chapter, the Exposition of the Faculties, is complete.

## 4. Reading Control

The question returns to the twelve broad kinds of mind and asks
what can be *acquired* when one kind is present. It does not ask
what immediately follows it. The ordered counts six/six/two
apply to afflicted minds of Desire, Form, and Formless. The
three-count belongs to wholesome Form Realm mind; the four-count
to trainee mind. The later alternative verse is reported and
corrected, not silently merged with the main enumeration.
