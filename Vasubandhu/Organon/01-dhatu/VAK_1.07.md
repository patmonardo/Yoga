# VAK_1.07

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> ते पुनः संस्कृतа धर्मा रूपादिस्कन्धपञ्चकम् ।
>
> स एवाध्वा कथावस्तु सनिःसाराः सवस्तुकाः ॥ १.०७ ॥॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> te punaḥ saṃskṛtā dharmā rūpādiskandhapañcakam /
>
> sa evādhvā kathāvastu saniḥsārāḥ savastukāḥ // 1.07 //

## 3. Padaccheda

te | punaḥ | saṃskṛtāḥ | dharmāḥ | rūpa-ādi-skandha-pañcakam |
saḥ | eva | adhvā | kathā-vastu | sa-niḥsārāḥ | sa-vastukāḥ

`Punaḥ` resumes the conditioned after the unconditioned have been named. It is not an interruption. English “again” is withdrawn.

`Sa eva` binds the second line to the first. Four names, one field.

## 4. Literal Translation

Those conditioned dharmas are the five aggregates beginning with form. That same field is the temporal course, the basis of discourse, possessed of an exit, and possessed of a *vastu*.

Kept so Pruden can be checked. Not the science.
