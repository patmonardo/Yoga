# VAK_1.32 — With Both, With One, With Neither

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> सवितर्कविचारा हि पञ्च विज्ञानधातवः ।
>
> अन्त्यास्त्रयस्त्रिप्रकाराः शेषा उभयवर्जिताः ॥ १.३२ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> savitarkavicārā hi pañca vijñānadhātavaḥ /
>
> antyās trayas triprakārāḥ śeṣā ubhayavarjitāḥ // 1.32 //

Vitarka and vicāra stay Sanskrit. The last three are the mind-principle, the dharma-principle, and the mind-cognition principle. The dharma-principle is not an essence.

## 4. Grammar

```text
pañca vijñāna-dhātavaḥ hi sa-vitarka-vicārāḥ
    the five are always with both

antyāḥ trayaḥ tri-prakārāḥ
    the last three are of three kinds

śeṣāḥ ubhaya-varjitāḥ
    the remaining ten are without both
```

The three kinds are not three principles. They are three ways the last three occur.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

The five cognition-principles are with vitarka and with vicāra. The last three are of three kinds. The rest are without both.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

The five sensory cognition-principles are accompanied by both vitarka and vicāra. The last three principles—the mind-principle, dharma-principle, and mind-cognition principle—admit three modes: with both, with vicāra alone, or with neither, according to their constituent dharmas and level of concentration. The remaining ten principles, being form, have neither.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The five cognitions do not occur without the pair. The last three take the pair, or one of the pair, or neither, by the level. The ten whose nature is form take neither, because association is not their way.

Vitarka and vicāra are not counted twice. Each is without a second of itself, and each is with the other. That is why a level with both has four kinds, not three.

## 9. Interpretive Note

The hinge is hi. The five are not sometimes with the pair. The last three are the ones that vary, and the variation is the level. The four kinds are required because vitarka and vicāra cannot associate with a second of themselves.
