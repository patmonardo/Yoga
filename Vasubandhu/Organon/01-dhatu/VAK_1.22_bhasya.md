## 3. Continuous Translation

The unconditioned is not stated among the bases, because the meaning does not
apply. Stated among them, it cannot be included in these, because the meaning
does not apply: it is not form, nor any of the rest, up through the principle.
Nor can a sixth base be stated. Why? Because the meaning does not apply. It has
been said that the meaning of base is collection. The unconditioned is not
divided by past and the rest, as form and the others are, so that all of it
could be gathered into one and reckoned an unconditioned base.

The expression “bases of appropriation” makes known the ground of affliction.
“Bases” makes known the ground of affliction and purification. The
unconditioned is neither, in that manner. So it is not established among them,
because the meaning does not apply.

## 5. Why the Meaning Does Not Apply

A base is a collection, gathered into one across the sūtra's distinctions.
The unconditioned is not that gathering. Three unconditioned dharmas have
already been named. The argument is not that the unconditioned has no plurality.
It is that this gathering does not fit.

Bases of appropriation name the ground of affliction. Bases name the ground of
affliction and purification. The unconditioned is neither, in that manner. That
does not remove it from the system. It removes it from the base.

## 10. Interpretation

The verse tests the scope of Base classification. Base gathers a
collectable multiplicity; the unconditioned does not meet that defining
operation and is therefore excluded from the Base, not from the
Dharma-system. Its inclusion under Dharma Essence and Dharma Principle
remains intact. The pot-cessation analogy fails because it treats the
different classifications as though they shared one defining operation.

The four ordering accounts converge on one sequence but do not become one
explanation. Grossness concerns relative manifestation. The account of
Affliction explains the attraction retrospectively, not as a forward
production chain. The meal analogy presents coordinated functions and
retains the vessel/food discrepancy in its recap. The levels account
concerns predominance across differentiated Principles, with Principle
established in their stations as seed in a field. These distinct grounds
do not collapse Base, Essence, and Principle into a flat inventory.

In the project's Kantian-transcendental frame, *artha-ayoga* marks a
limit of valid classification: the unconditioned is not excluded from
reality, but it cannot be subsumed under a category whose defining
operation does not fit. This is an Organon interpretation, not a
translation or historical attribution. The threefold promise against four
ordering accounts and the meal-recap discrepancy remain unresolved.
