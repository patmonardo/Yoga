**Feature chart checkpoints.** Classification spans [0, 5); feeling definition [5, 7); reflection definition [7, 9); the root spans [0, 9). The classification concerns the ten Form members only. No claim that *avijñapti* is among these ten, or that Reflection is Feeling, is generated by the verse.

**Close construe.** Those very Faculties and meanings are accepted as ten Essences and Principles. Feeling is undergoing. Reflection has taking up a mark as its nature.

The classification predicates concern the ten Form members only, not all twelve Essences or all eighteen Principles. A shared member variable expresses cross-classification without identifying an Essence slot with a Principle slot. The definition predicates do not derive the commentary's three tones or six contact-groups.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> These very Faculties and meanings are accepted as the ten Essences and the ten Principles. Feeling is undergoing. Reflection has the taking up of the mark as its nature.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> The five Faculties and five meanings are accepted as ten Essences and the same ten Principles. Feeling is experience; Reflection has taking up a mark as its nature.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Again, two lines. The first carries the ten. The second opens feeling and reflection.

These very ones. Not a new inventory. The Faculties and the meanings already counted in the Form Base are accepted as ten Essences, and as the same ten Principles. Eye Essence, Visible Form Essence, through Body Essence and Tangible Essence. Eye Principle, Visible Form Principle, through Body Principle and Tangible Principle. The lists alternate Faculty and meaning. *Yāvat* covers the middle.

*Avijñapti* is in the Form Base. It is not in these ten. The Form Base account closes before the new definitions begin.

Feeling is undergoing. Three tones: pleasant, painful, neither painful nor pleasant. Six groups, born of contact, from the eye through Mind. Three tones and six groups are not nine Bases.

Reflection takes up the mark. Blue and yellow, long and short, woman and man, pleasant and painful, and the transmitted *mitramitra*. Friend and enemy is a possible division, uncertain at the letter. Six groups, as with feeling. The pattern transfers. The tones do not.

The next verse takes up formations. Not this one.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya identifies the ten as the five Faculties and their five meanings, excluding *avijñapti*. It explains feeling as threefold experience in six contact-groups, from eye-contact through Mind-contact, and Reflection as taking up marks, likewise in six groups.

The hinge is *te eva*. Interpretation of the two arrangements is in the Bhāṣya.
