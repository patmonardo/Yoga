# VAK_1.14

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> इन्द्रियार्थास्त एवेष्टा दशायतनधातवः ।
>
> वेदनानुभवः संज्ञा निमित्तोद्ग्रहणात्मिका ॥ १.१४ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> indriyārthās ta eveṣṭā daśāyatanadhātavaḥ /
>
> vedanānubhavaḥ saṃjñā nimittodgrahaṇātmikā // 1.14 //

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

**Padaccheda**

indriya-arthāḥ | te | eva | iṣṭāḥ | daśa-āyatana-dhātavaḥ |
vedanā | anubhavaḥ | saṃjñā | nimitta-udgrahaṇa-ātmikā

| Form | Analysis | Contextual force |
|---|---|---|
| **indriya-arthāḥ** | faculties and Meanings | the 1.09 pair — sattva-rules and first Moment of Meaning |
| **te eva iṣṭāḥ** | those very ones are accepted | no new substances — not “three lists of the same stuff” |
| **daśa-āyatana-dhātavaḥ** | ten as *āyatana* and ten as *dhātu* | two arrangements, not one hybrid class |
| **vedanā anubhavaḥ** | feeling is undergoing | inner projector opens |
| **saṃjñā** | recognition | Conceiving from Principles |
| **nimitta-udgrahaṇa-ātmikā** | having the nature of taking up a mark | mark, not Object |

Bhāṣya: *anubhava* here is pleasant / painful / neither. *Nimitta* illustrated as blue, long, woman, friend — marks. Six *vedanā-kāyas*, six *saṃjñā-kāyas*. Avijñapti is not among these ten.

## 4. Grammar

```text
those very indriya-arthas
    are accepted as ten āyatanas
    and as ten dhātus
```

*Te eva* forbids extra entities. It does not make *skandha*, *āyatana*, and *dhātu* synonyms.

Second line: two definitions. *Vedanā = anubhava*. *Saṃjñā = nimitta-udgrahaṇa-ātmikā*.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Those very faculties and *arthas* are accepted as the ten *āyatanas* and the ten *dhātus*. Feeling is undergoing; *saṃjñā* has the nature of taking up a mark.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

The five faculties and five *arthas* already in the form-aggregate are the same factors arranged as ten *āyatanas* and as ten *dhātus*. Avijñapti is not in that ten. *Vedanā* is pleasant, painful, or neither-undergoing, sixfold from contact. *Saṃjñā* takes up a mark (blue, long, friend…) and is sixfold by analogy.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

### Received wisdom that fails here

```text
SAD = three lists of the same stuff
āyatana = sense-base / Sphere
dhātu = Domain as a box
artha = sensory object
vedanā = a mood
saṃjñā = a label-gun
te eva = proof that Abhidharma is only counting
```

That is the citta-killing inventory. Retired.

### What *te eva* actually does

No new substances. The sattva-rules and the Five Meanings **are** the ten Extensions and **are** the ten Territories. Same factors. Different grade of self-relation.

```text
skandha     Display of Meaning      Being
āyatana     Display of Essence      Extension
dhātu       Territory Will rules    particular dharma — both readings
samādhi     the two series as the Same Idea
```

*Te eva* is *satkārya* of the classification: the later grade is already in the earlier. Not a third pile.

### Nāma opens

Pāda b leaves *rūpa* and starts the inner projector — sattvic manifestation of Puruṣa, the Word:

```text
vedanā      undergoing     the Word as felt
saṃjñā      Conceiving     taking up a mark from Principles
```

*Nimitta* is a mark, not an Object. Pre-Loka. Object stays out. *Anubhava* is not consciousness.

## 8. Logical Determination

```text
IndriyaArtha = the ten
ArrangeAsAyatana(the ten) ≠ ArrangeAsDhatu(the ten)
Ayatana ≠ Dhatu ≠ Skandha     // grades, not synonyms
Avijnapti ∉ these ten
Vedana = Anubhava
Samjna = NimittaUdgrahana
```

Rūpa-skandha's inventory-work ends as *te eva*. Nāma begins. 1.15 takes *saṃskāra*.

## 9. Interpretive Note

End-stage of the *rūpa* teaching: the factors do not multiply when the grade rises. That is why *te eva* is powerful and why received wisdom is wrong about it. The Word then speaks as undergoing and as mark-taking. Not two new mental things glued onto matter.
