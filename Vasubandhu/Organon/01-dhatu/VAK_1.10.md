# VAK_1.10

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> रूपं द्विधा विंशतिधा शब्दस्त्वष्टविधः रसः ।
>
> षोढा चतुर्विधो गन्धः स्पृश्यमेकादशात्मकम् ॥ १.१० ॥॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> rūpaṃ dvidhā viṃśatidhā śabdas tv aṣṭavidhaḥ rasaḥ /
>
> ṣoḍhā caturvidho gandhaḥ spṛśyam ekādaśātmakam // 1.10 //

## 3. Padaccheda

rūpam | dvidhā | viṃśati-dhā | śabdaḥ | tu | aṣṭa-vidhaḥ | rasaḥ |
ṣoḍhā | catur-vidhaḥ | gandhaḥ | spṛśyam | ekādaśa-ātmakam

`Rūpa` here is one meaning, visible form. Not the *rūpa-skandha*. `Tu` turns to the next.

## 4. Grammar

```text
visible form     twofold, and twentyfold
sound            eightfold
taste            sixfold
odor             fourfold
tangible         eleven as its nature
```

The twenty is the same visible form, counted again. Not a second domain.

## 5. Translation

### Literal

Visible form is twofold and twentyfold. Sound is eightfold. Taste is sixfold. Odor is fourfold. The tangible is elevenfold in nature.

### Bhāṣya-informed

Visible form is twofold as color and configuration, and the same is enumerated as twenty. Sound is eightfold. Taste is sixfold. Odor is fourfold. The tangible has eleven as its nature: four great elements and seven further tangibles. The counts are in the verse. How a cognition takes one or many is in the prose.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

1.09 named five meanings. 1.10 counts inside them. The tree branches. The fruit is these counts.

```text
color : configuration     the one dyad
                          both sides stay

twenty, eight, six,
four, eleven              enumerations
                          not a second principle
```

A *dhātu* is Base::Meaning. This verse is the meaning-side, counted. Definition is not forced here. Domain remains available.

The prose will say the *vid* is knowing-sense, not existence-sense. That waits on the Bhāṣya. Not written into the count.

## 8. Logical Determination

```text

1.10  each meaning counted
VisibleForm = Color : Configuration
counts ≠ rational division
```
