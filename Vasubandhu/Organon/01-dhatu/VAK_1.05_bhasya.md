## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

**Question**

Which dharmas are Pure?

**Answer**

The Truth of the Path is Pure, and so is the threefold unconditioned.

What is the threefold? Space and the two cessations.

Which are the two? Cessation through discrimination, and cessation not through discrimination.

Thus the threefold unconditioned beginning with space, together with the Truth of the Path, are the Pure dharmas.

Why? Because the *āsravas* do not persist in them.

Among the three unconditioned dharmas just enumerated, space is non-obstruction. Its nature is non-obstruction. It is where form can move.

*“Without outflows” and “settle” are withdrawn. Pure is the headword. Persist is the verb of `anuśerata`.*

## 6. Space

```text
ākāśa
    = anāvṛti
    = anāvaraṇa-svabhāva
    = where rūpa can move
```

Not a container. Not an infinite substance. Not openness as a mood.

## 7. Decisions for the Kārikā

1. The subject is the Pure dharmas.
2. The Path is conditioned and Pure. The other three are unconditioned and Pure.
3. The two cessation-names are supplied here. Definitions wait for 1.06.
4. Space is non-obstruction, where form can move.
