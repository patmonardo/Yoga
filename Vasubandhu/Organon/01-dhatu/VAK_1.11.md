## 4. Grammar

```text
yaḥ anubandhaḥ … saḥ … avijñaptiḥ ucyate
```

*Api* blocks a restriction to the two striking cases. The stream also runs when mind is present and undistracted.

*Mahābhūtāny upādāya* distinguishes this stream from *prāpti-vāha*. Conventional force: dependence on the Elements. Vaibhāṣika force of *upādāya*: causal.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Whatever wholesome or unwholesome continuity belongs even to one whose mind is directed elsewhere or who is without mind, depending upon the great Elements — that indeed is called avijñapti.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Avijñapti is the wholesome or unwholesome stream of form that continues when the mind is different, absent, or present and undistracted. It depends upon the great Elements. Unlike *vijñapti*, it does not make something known to another. It may arise from manifestation or from samādhi. The causal reading of *upādāya* is Vaibhāṣika.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The first aggregate includes a stream of form that is ethically determined and need not be presently disclosed. It has the nature of form and action. It does not inform another as *vijñapti* does. Dependence on the Elements keeps it from collapsing into a mere stream of acquisition.

Organon rendering (project reconstruction; not Vasubandhu's wording):

```text
vijñapti  :  avijñapti
Concept   :  Idea
savitarka :  nirvitarka
```

*Avijñapti* is the inner division of Form — the cell dividing itself. It is the Idea-pole of the form-engine. Conventional *upādāya* is dependence. Science reads the same cell as **projection**: avijñapti Projects the *mahābhūtas*. It does not hide in them.

```text
Content        = saṃyoga of Bhūtādi and Tanmātra
mahābhūta      = what that Content exposes
avijñapti      = projector (wielder of Māyā, this one lamp)
rūpa           = Form Theory / Relational Mechanism
```

Do not psychologize the stream as memory or unconscious idea. Do not retire *citta* by killing it. The stream can run with mind present. That is already the verse's *api*.

## 9. Interpretive Note

This verse finishes the 1.09 inventory. The received reading treats *avijñapti* as an odd karmic leftover inside matter. The Bhāṣya already blocks that flattening: it is form *and* action; it does not inform another; it can arise from samādhi; *api* keeps the minded case.

Science: this is Form Theory's Idea-pole. *Rūpa* is one projector — wielder of Māyā. The inner projector is the remaining skandhas. 1.12 takes the Elements themselves.

1.01 / *pratisaṃkhyānirodha* is a later return. Conditioned:Condition is already at peace. This card does not reopen 1.05–1.06.
