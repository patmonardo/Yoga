# VAK_1.36 — The Outer Four

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> छिनत्ति छिद्यते चैव बाह्यं धातुचतुष्टयम् ।
>
> दह्यते तुलयत्येवं विवादो दग्धृतुल्ययोः ॥ १.३६ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

Cutting is defined. Burning and weighing are the same four, then disputed.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

The outer tetrad of principles cuts and is cut. It is burned, and it weighs. Likewise. There is a dispute about the burner and the weigher.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Of the eighteen principles, which cuts, which is cut, which burns, which is burned, which weighs, which is weighed? The outer tetrad of principles cuts and is cut: the one called visible, smell, taste, and tangible, designated as axe, wood, and the like. What dharma is this cutting? The production, as divided, of a stream of aggregates that arises in connection.

The body-organ and the others are not cut. When a limb is cut off without remainder, they are not made twofold by that. Organs do not become two. The severed limb is without the organ. Nor do they cut, because of clarity, like the radiance of a jewel.

As the outer tetrad cuts and is cut, so it is burned and it weighs. That same one is burned. That same one weighs. The organs do not, because of clarity, like the radiance of a jewel. Nor does sound, because it is cut off.

There is a dispute about the burner and the weigher. Some say that same tetrad of principles is the burner and the weigher. Some say the principled fire alone is the burner, and heaviness alone is what is weighed.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The outer four do the work and undergo it. Visible, smell, taste, tangible: axe and wood are names of that tetrad. Cutting is not a split of the organ. It is a divided arising of a connected stream. The organ does not become two. The severed limb is without it. Clarity keeps the organ from cutting, and from being burned, and from weighing. Sound is cut off, so it is not in the count.

The dispute is only about the burner and the weigher. One side keeps the four. The other names the principled fire, and heaviness. The fire is the element. It is not a new principle.

## 9. Interpretive Note

The hinge is the organ. A severed limb does not divide it. The dispute does not reopen the tetrad. It only asks what burns and what is weighed.
