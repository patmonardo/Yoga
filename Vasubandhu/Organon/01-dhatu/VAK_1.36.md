## 4. Grammar

Three clauses. The first predicates both voices of √chid of the external tetrad, neuter singular. The second predicates √dah in the passive and √tul in the active of the same tetrad, by *evam*. The third names the dispute with a genitive dual of the two roots. The voice features carry the hinge: the dvandva's first member must have the voice of *tulayati* and its second the voice of *dahyate*, so the dispute asks exactly the converse questions.

**Feature chart checkpoints.** Cut spans [0, 6); burned/weighs [6, 9); dispute [9, 11); the root spans [0, 11). Three clauses. No claim that the Faculty is cut is generated by the verse.

**Close construe.** The external tetrad of Principles cuts and is also cut. Likewise it is burned and weighs. There is a dispute about the burner and the weighed.

The verse settles four roles of the tetrad: agent and patient of cutting, patient of burning, agent of weighing. It leaves open the other two: agent of burning, patient of weighing. The `VA`/`VB` swap in the grammar is that complementarity; a dispute about "the burned and the weigher" would not parse.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> The external tetrad of Principles cuts and is also cut. Likewise it is burned and it weighs. There is a dispute about the burner and the weighed.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> The four external Principles—Visible Form, Odor, Taste, and Tangible—cut and are cut; they are burned and weigh. Whether this same fourfold group is also the burner and the weighed is disputed.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The outer four do the work and undergo it. Visible Form, Odor, Taste, Tangible: axe and wood are names of that tetrad. Cutting is not a split of the Faculty. It is a divided arising of a connected stream. The Faculty does not become two. The severed limb is without it. Clarity keeps the Faculty from cutting, and from being burned, and from weighing. Sound is cut off, so it is not in the count.

The dispute is only about the burner and the weighed. One side keeps the four. The other names the Fire Principle, and heaviness. The fire is an Element Principle, an instance in the Tangible. It is not one of the eighteen.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation correction.** *Tulya* is a gerundive, "to be weighed"; the earlier "weigher" made the dispute repeat what the verse had settled (*tulayati*, it weighs). The anchor now reads "the burner and the weighed".

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya identifies the four as Visible Form, Odor, Taste, and Tangible, and explains cutting as division in a stream of clusters. The Faculties are not cut or burned. It records two positions on the burner and the weighed: the same fourfold group, or the Fire Principle alone as burner and heaviness alone as what is weighed.

The hinge is the Faculty. A severed limb does not divide it. The dispute does not reopen the tetrad. It only asks what burns and what is weighed.
