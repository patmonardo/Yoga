# VAK_1.31 Bhāṣya — Three in the Formless

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ārūpyāptā manodharmamanovijñānadhātavaḥ /
> sāsravānāsravā ete trayaḥ śeṣās tu sāsravāḥ // 1.31 //

> Connected with the formless are the Mind Principle, Dharma Principle, and
> Mind-Cognition Principle. These three are Impure and Pure; the remaining
> fifteen are Impure.

Word division is editorial. The question on applied thought and investigation is 1.32 and is not this unit.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Connected with the formless are the mind-principle, the dharma-principle, and the mind-cognition principle.
>
> Those free of passion for form are born there. So the ten principles whose nature is form do not occur there, nor the five cognition-principles that have those as support and object.
>
> How many are Impure, how many Pure? These that have been stated, the mind-principle, the dharma-principle, and the mind-cognition principle: these three are Impure and Pure.
>
> Those included in the truth of the path, or in the unconditioned, are Pure. The others are Impure.
>
> The rest are Impure.
>
> The remaining fifteen principles are exclusively Impure.

## 4. Movement

The first sentence completes the realm count begun at 1.30. The reason is birth: free of passion for form, so no form-natured principle, and no cognition that takes one as support or object. The question of Impure and Pure then begins a new cut. These three admit both. The fifteen do not. There is no rival school in the unit.

## 5. Decisions

1. The formless has three: mind-principle, dharma-principle, mind-cognition principle.
2. The ten whose nature is form do not occur there.
3. The five cognition-principles that take those as support and object do not occur there.
4. These three are Impure and Pure. The pair is of instances, not one thing that is both.
5. Pure, in this cut, is what is included in the path or in the unconditioned.
6. The remaining fifteen are exclusively Impure.
7. The dharma-principle is not an essence.

## 6. Interpretation

The realm cut is Dharma:Formless:Form:Desire. Everything is in everything, and desire is covered by dharma. The birth-cut is still a cut. The desireling is the eating and mating machine. That machine is not born in the form realm, and it is not born in the formless. The formless drops the ten whose nature is form. It does not drop the Form of Universality. That Form is the Concept, and it is in the formless as the three that remain. The page does not print that. The page prints the three principles and the Pure and the Impure.
