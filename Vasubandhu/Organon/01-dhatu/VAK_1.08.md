**Close construe.** Those which are Impure are Bases of appropriation; they are also with conflict. Those same are suffering, origin, world, station of views, and becoming.

`AppropriationBaseMember` classifies a Dharma under a Base of appropriation; it does not identify an individual Dharma with an entire Base. The first clause is read as a class specification, as the Bhāṣya confirms. The conditioned Pure Path lies outside the restriction.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> Those which are Impure are the Bases of appropriation. They are also with conflict. They are suffering, origin, world, the station of views, and becoming.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Those conditioned dharmas that are with outflows are Bases of appropriation; they too are with conflict. These same dharmas are suffering, origin, the world, the station of views, and becoming.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The five Bases are divided. The Impure are the Bases of appropriation, and they are also the field of conflict. That same field is suffering, origin, world, the station of views, and becoming.

```text

                 not within this division
```

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya explains the three relations behind the name Base of appropriation: the Bases arise from afflictions, are governed by them, or give rise to them. It also explains that afflictions are called conflicts because they injure oneself and others.

Impure is the top-level division within the five-Base field. Once the division is made, the word can drop. The sheath is later. The first two names are the first two Truths of what persists. The station of views is not the station of prajñā. A Rational Dharma does not Persist.
