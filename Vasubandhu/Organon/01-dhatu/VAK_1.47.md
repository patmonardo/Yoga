# VAK_1.47 — The Ear, the Three, and Mind

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> Likewise the ear. Of the three, however, everything is of its own plane.
> Body-cognition is of a lower plane or its own. Mind is not fixed.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> The rule for the Eye Principle also applies to the Ear Principle. For the
> three Faculties beginning with the Nose, each Faculty, Condition, and
> Cognition Principle belongs to its own plane. The Body-Cognition Principle
> may be of a lower plane or its own; the Mind Principle is not fixed.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The ear takes the eye's rule. Not lower than the body. Sound not higher than the ear. Cognition not higher than the ear. Relative to the cognition, sound is open. Relative to the body, both are open.

The three that reach do not take that freedom. Nose, tongue, and body: organ, object, and cognition stay on their own plane. The exception is body-cognition. The body and the tangible stay. The cognition may drop. Own plane for one born in the desire realm or the first dhyāna. A lower plane for one born in the second or above.

Mind is the open one. Same plane as the body, or higher, or lower. A body of five planes does not fix the mind. Attainment and rebirth are where that is worked. Not here. The incidental discussion closes.
