# VAK_1.09 Bhāṣya — Faculties, Meanings, Projector Named

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> rūpaṃ pañcendriyāṇy arthāḥ pañcāvijñaptir eva ca /
>
> tadvijñānāśrayā rūpaprasādāś cakṣurādayaḥ // 1.09 //

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> Rūpa is the five faculties, the five *arthas*, and avijñapti also. The eye and the others are material clarities, supports of the corresponding Principles.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_1.09.md`](./VAK_1.09.md). Previous: 1.08. Next: 1.10 counts the five *arthas*; 1.11 defines avijñapti.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> It has been said that there are five aggregates beginning with form. Among them, form comprises the five faculties, the five *arthas*, and avijñapti. The five faculties are the faculties of eye, ear, nose, tongue, and body. The five *arthas* are the five respective *viṣayas* of those very faculties beginning with the eye, called visible form, sound, odor, taste, and the tangible. And there is avijñapti. This is the full extent of the aggregate of form.
>
> Now, with regard to those five *arthas* beginning with visible form that have been mentioned: the eye and the other faculties are material clarities serving as supports for the Principles of those *viṣayas*. The five material clarities that serve as supports for the Principles of visible form, sound, odor, taste, and the tangible are to be understood, respectively, as eye, ear, nose, tongue, and body.
>
> As the Blessed One has said: “The eye, monk, is the internal *āyatana*, a material clarity dependent upon the four great Elements,” and so forth. With regard to those faculties beginning with the eye that have been mentioned, the eye and the others are material clarities serving as supports for their corresponding Principles. The meaning is: supports of eye-principle and the rest. Understood in this way, the Prakaraṇa text is also followed: “What is the eye? A material clarity serving as the support of eye-principle,” and so forth.
>
> The five faculties have been explained. The five *arthas* are to be explained.

*Viṣaya* is not Object. Sphere is not used. *Artha* is kept as the verse-word.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
    → faculties as rūpa-prasāda supporting Principle

        → second tad: named from the faculty

```

## 5. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

1. Eleven members. Visible form is one *artha* inside the aggregate, not the aggregate.
2. *Arthāḥ* are the respective *viṣayāḥ*, *yathāsvam*. Not Object.
3. Both antecedents of *tad* are kept.
4. *Rūpaprasāda* is material clarity dependent on the four Elements.
5. *Upādāya* here is dependence of derived form, not 1.08 appropriation.
6. Scripture-word is *āyatana*, not Sphere.
7. Avijñapti is included and undefined. 1.11 defines it.
