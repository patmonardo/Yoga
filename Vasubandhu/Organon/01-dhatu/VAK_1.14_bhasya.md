# VAK_1.14 Bhāṣya — Ten Essences, Feeling, Saṃjñā

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> indriyārthās ta eveṣṭā daśāyatanadhātavaḥ /
>
> vedanānubhavaḥ saṃjñā nimittodgrahaṇātmikā // 1.14 //

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> These very faculties and meanings are accepted as the ten essences and the ten principles. Feeling is undergoing. *Saṃjñā* consists in taking up a mark.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> These very faculties and meanings are accepted as the ten essences and the ten principles.
>
> In the essence-arrangement, ten essences: the eye-essence, the form-essence, up to the body-essence and the tangible-essence.
>
> In the principle-arrangement, those very ten principles: the eye-principle, the form-principle, up to the body-principle and the tangible-principle.
>
> The form-aggregate has been stated, and its essence-arrangement.
>
> Feeling and the rest are to be stated.
>
> Feeling is undergoing. The feeling-aggregate is a threefold undergoing: pleasant, painful, and neither-painful-nor-pleasant. Divided, it is six groups of feeling: feeling born of eye-contact, up to feeling born of mind-contact.
>
> *Saṃjñā* consists in taking up a mark. The *saṃjñā*-aggregate is the taking up of a mark such as blue, yellow, long, short, woman, man, friend, enemy, pleasant, painful. Divided, it is six groups of *saṃjñā*, as with feeling.

## 5. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

1. *Te eva*: the faculties and meanings already stated. No new item.
2. Two arrangements of the same ten. Essence, then principle.
3. Feeling is a threefold undergoing. Six groups by contact.
4. *Saṃjñā* is taking up a mark. Six groups, on the feeling pattern.
5. The form-aggregate is closed before feeling is opened.

## 6. Organon Note

Not part of the conventional translation. Eye-essence, then eye-principle. Relation is not the English.
