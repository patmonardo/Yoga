# VAK_1.14 Bhāṣya — Same Factors, New Grade; Nāma Opens

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Those very faculties and *arthas* are accepted as the ten *āyatanas* and as the ten *dhātus*. The five faculties and five *arthas* already numbered in the form-aggregate are not new items. They occupy ten places in the *āyatana* arrangement and ten in the *dhātu* arrangement. Avijñapti is not among these ten.
>
> Feeling is undergoing: pleasant, painful, or neither. It is sixfold as born from eye-contact through mind-contact. *Saṃjñā* has the nature of taking up a mark — blue, yellow, long, short, woman, man, friend, enemy, pleasure, pain — and is sixfold by analogy with feeling.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

## 5. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

1. *Te eva*: no added sensible entities. Two arrangements, not one hybrid “āyatana-dhātu.”
2. The ten are faculties plus *arthas*. Avijñapti stays outside that ten.
3. *Anubhava* is hedonic undergoing, not mind in general.
4. *Nimitta* is a mark by which content is taken up, not a finished thing.
5. Sixfold division of *saṃjñā* follows *vedanā*; no temporal order of the two is stated.
