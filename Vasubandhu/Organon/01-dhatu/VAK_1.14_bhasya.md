# VAK_1.14 Bhāṣya

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> indriyārthās ta eveṣṭā daśāyatanadhātavaḥ /
>
> vedanānubhavaḥ saṃjñā nimittodgrahaṇātmikā // 1.14 //

> These very faculties and objects are accepted as the ten Relations and the ten Principles. Feeling is undergoing. Saṃjñā is the taking up of a mark.

## 2. Decisions

1. `te eva`: no new items. Two arrangements, not one hybrid.
2. The ten are faculties plus objects. Avijñapti stays outside.
3. Vedanā is anubhava: pleasant, painful, or neither. Sixfold by contact. Not citta.
4. Saṃjñā is mark-taking, sixfold on the same pattern. No order between the two is stated.
5. Relation is pure composite chained access to appearance.
6. Principle is the second placement. Prior Territory / Conceiving note is withdrawn.

## 3. Movement

```text
te eva       same factors, two arrangements
             avijñapti not in the ten
vedanā       undergoing, three tones, six contacts
saṃjñā       taking up a mark, six groups
```
