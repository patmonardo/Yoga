# VAK_1.14 Bhāṣya — Ten Essences, Feeling, Saṃjñā

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> indriyārthās ta eveṣṭā daśāyatanadhātavaḥ /
>
> vedanānubhavaḥ saṃjñā nimittodgrahaṇātmikā // 1.14 //

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> These very faculties and meanings are accepted as the ten essences and the ten principles. Feeling is undergoing. *Saṃjñā* consists in taking up a mark.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> These very faculties and meanings are accepted as the ten essences and the ten principles. The faculties and meanings as stated are accepted as ten essences and ten principles. *Avijñapti*, however, is not an essence and not a principle among these ten, because it is included in the dharma-essence and the dharma-principle.
>
> Feeling is undergoing. Undergoing has the nature of pleasant, painful, or neither. There are six groups of feeling, born of eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, and mind-contact.
>
> *Saṃjñā* consists in taking up a mark. The taking up of a mark such as blue, yellow, long, short, woman, man, friend, enemy, pleasant, painful. There are six groups of *saṃjñā*.

## 5. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

1. *Te eva*: no new items. Two placements of the five faculties and five meanings.
2. *Avijñapti* is outside the ten. It falls inside the dharma-essence and the dharma-principle.
3. Feeling is undergoing: pleasant, painful, or neither. Sixfold by contact.
4. *Saṃjñā* is taking up a mark. Sixfold on the same pattern.
5. No order between the two is stated.

## 6. Organon Note

Not part of the conventional translation.

Essence, then principle. Eye-essence, eye-principle. Relation is not the English. The dharma-essence and the dharma-principle are named here only as the place *avijñapti* falls. They are not developed.
