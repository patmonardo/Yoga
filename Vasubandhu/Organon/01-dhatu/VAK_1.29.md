**Feature chart checkpoints.** Visible spans [0, 4); resistant [4, 7); indeterminate [7, 12); the root spans [0, 12). Three answers to three questions. No claim that the Form Base is visible is generated by the verse.

**Close construe.** Here one is visible: Visible Form. Ten are resistant: the Form-bearing. Eight are indeterminate: those same, without Visible Form and Sound.

Each clause is a biconditional over the eighteen, so the counts are extensional: one, ten, and eight are checked against the finite valuation, not asserted by FOL arithmetic. `OnlyIndeterminate` answers the question "how many are indeterminate" as the Bhāṣya does: these eight are not declared wholesome or unwholesome. The ethical status of the remaining ten is not determined here. `Resistant` in the count is resistance by obstruction; the other two resistances are prose.

Three questions, three counts. The masculine adjectives agree with the understood Principle. Neuter *rūpam* names the one.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> One here is visible: Form. Ten are resistant. Eight Form-bearing ones are indeterminate: those same, excluding Form and Sound.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Among the eighteen Principles, one is visible: the Visible Form Principle. Ten included in the Form Base are resistant. Eight of those ten are indeterminate: those same ten excluding Visible Form and Sound.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

Visibility, resistance, and ethical status are three determinations. They do not collapse.

The one visible is the Visible Form Principle, not the Form Base. The ten resistant are the Form Base as Principles: five Faculties and five meanings. The eight indeterminate are those ten minus Visible Form and Sound.

The Cognition Principles are not in this count. The Mind Principle is not in this count.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya counts the ten Faculties and meanings included in the Form Base as resistant by obstruction. Its eight indeterminate Principles are those ten without Visible Form and Sound; indeterminate is glossed as neither wholesome nor unwholesome. The commentary distinguishes this obstruction-based resistance from condition-resistance and support-resistance.

The hinge is the three counts. Interpretation of the three resistances is in the Bhāṣya. The eight are not a denial of Form.
