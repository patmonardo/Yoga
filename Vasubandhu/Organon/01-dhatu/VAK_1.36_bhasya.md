# VAK_1.36 Bhāṣya — The Outer Four

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> chinatti chidyate caiva bāhyaṃ dhātucatuṣṭayam /
>
> dahyate tulayaty evaṃ vivādo dagdhṛtulyayoḥ // 1.36 //

> The outer tetrad of principles cuts and is cut. It is burned, and it weighs. Likewise. There is a dispute about the burner and the weigher.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Of the eighteen principles, which cuts, which is cut, which burns, which is burned, which weighs, which is weighed?
>
> The outer tetrad of principles cuts and is cut.
>
> The one called visible, smell, taste, and tangible, designated as axe, wood, and the like.
>
> What dharma is this cutting? The production, as divided, of a stream of aggregates that arises in connection.
>
> The body-organ and the others are not cut. When a limb is cut off without remainder, they are not made twofold by that. For organs do not become two, because the severed limb is without the organ. Nor do they cut, because of clarity, like the radiance of a jewel.
>
> As the outer tetrad cuts and is cut, so it is burned and it weighs. That same one is burned. That same one weighs. The organs do not, because of clarity, like the radiance of a jewel. Nor does sound, because it is cut off.
>
> There is a dispute about the burner and the weigher. Some say that same tetrad of principles is the burner and the weigher. Some say the principled fire alone is the burner, and heaviness alone is what is weighed.

## 4. Movement

The question is six. The verse answers with the outer four, then the burn and the weighing, then a dispute. Cutting is defined as a divided arising. The organ is refused twice: it is not cut, and it does not cut. Sound is refused for the burn and the weighing. The dispute is not settled. There is no school name.

## 5. Decisions

1. The outer four principles cut and are cut: visible, smell, taste, tangible.
2. Cutting is the divided production of a stream of aggregates that arises in connection.
3. The organs are not cut. A severed limb does not make them two.
4. The organs do not cut, burn, or weigh. Clarity, like jewel-radiance.
5. Sound is not burned or weighed. It is cut off.
6. Some say the four are the burner and the weigher. Some say the principled fire burns, and heaviness is weighed.

## 6. Organon Reading

The organ is not divided by the cut that divides the limb. The four do the work the names axe and wood are given for. The fire in the other account is the element, principled. The page does not decide the dispute. The page prints the four, the organ, and the two sides.
