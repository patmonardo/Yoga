## 3. Gloss translation

Fenced. This is what the Bhāṣya says, for the Pruden check. It is not the science.

> Which are those conditioned dharmas? They are the five aggregates beginning with form. The witness names form, feeling, formations, and cognition.
>
> They are called conditioned because they are made by conditions that have come together and act together. Nothing is produced by one condition. Future dharmas take the name by kind, as milk and fuel do.
>
> The same field is the courses: gone, going, about to go; or consumed by impermanence.
>
> The Prakaraṇa includes *kathāvastu* in the eighteen *dhātus*.
>
> The Bhāṣya says the exit from everything conditioned is nirvāṇa.
>
> The Vaibhāṣikas say, *kila*, that the word *vastu* expresses cause.
>
> These are alternative designations of conditioned dharmas.
