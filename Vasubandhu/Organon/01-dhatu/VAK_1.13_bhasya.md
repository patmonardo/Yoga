## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> What distinguishes the common names “earth” and the like from the earth-*dhātu* and the other *dhātus*? By worldly designation, color and configuration are called “earth.” For when people point out earth, they point out color and configuration. As with earth, so with water and fire: color and configuration alone are called by those worldly names.
>
> Wind, however, is the *dhātu* itself. The very wind-*dhātu* is what is called “wind” in the world. Yet wind is also designated in the same way: “a blue windstorm,” “a circular windstorm.”
>
> Why is this series, extending as far as avijñapti, called the aggregate of form? Because of being affected. The Blessed One said it is affected; therefore it is called the aggregate of appropriation consisting of form. Even touched by the hand, it is affected. “Affected” means afflicted. The affliction of form is the production of alteration. Others say: obstruction by form.
>
> Then the atom would not be form. A single atom does not exist separately; in an aggregate it is affected. Then past and future form would not qualify. That too has been affected, will be affected, and is of that kind — as with fuel.
>
> Then avijñapti would not qualify. Proposed: it is affected through manifestation, as a shadow moves with a tree. Rejected: it does not so modify, and it would cease when manifestation ceases. Proposed: because its elemental support is affected. Objection: then eye-principle would be form. The comparison is unequal: faculties are only conditions of arising. Shadow/jewel analogies are then rejected (not Vaibhāṣika; avijñapti can outlast those Elements). Others finally: cognition's supports are mixed (eye affected, manas not); avijñapti's are not. So the consequence is not parallel.

## 5. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

1. Water and fire follow earth's naming clause. Wind has its own *tu*.
2. *Tathāpi ca* adds visible names for wind; it does not flatten the four.
3. *Loka-saṃjñā* is pointing, not Chapter-3 cosmology.
4. The talk then leaves the verse and tests the unity of Form through avijñapti.
5. Analogies are rejected in sequence. The last reply is others' and left standing in-unit.
