# VAK 6.63 — Seven Names, Six Path-Continuities

## 1. Sanskrit Devanāgarī

प्रयोगाक्षसमापत्तिविमुक्त्युभयतः कृताः ।
पुद्गलाः सप्त षट् त्वेते द्वौ द्वौ मार्गत्रये यतः ॥ ६.६३ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit IAST

prayogākṣasamāpattivimuktyubhayataḥ kṛtāḥ |
pudgalāḥ sapta ṣaṭ tv ete dvau dvau mārgatraye yataḥ || 6.63 ||

The verse does not present seven serial stages. The Bhāṣya derives seven designations from several classificatory principles, then distinguishes seven **by name** from six **in actual non-duplicated classification** because the same two developmental continuities traverse each of the three Paths.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

**Padaccheda**

prayoga-akṣa-samāpatti-vimukti-ubhayataḥ | kṛtāḥ |
pudgalāḥ | sapta | ṣaṭ | tu | ete | dvau | dvau | mārga-traye | yataḥ

| Form | Analysis | Contextual force |
|---|---|---|
| **prayoga** | masculine compound member | application or mode of undertaking the Path |
| **akṣa** | masculine compound member | faculty; interpreted by the Bhāṣya as *indriya* |
| **samāpatti** | feminine compound member | meditative attainment |
| **vimukti** | feminine compound member | liberation |
| **ubhayataḥ** | indeclinable | from both; through attainment and liberation together |
| **kṛtāḥ** | masculine nominative plural past passive participle | constituted, established, or distinguished |
| **pudgalāḥ** | masculine nominative plural | persons classified through Path-relations |
| **sapta** | numeral | seven [by designation] |
| **ṣaṭ tu ete** | numeral with adversative particle and pronoun | but these are six [in actual distinctness] |
| **dvau dvau** | distributive dual | two in each case |
| **mārga-traye** | masculine locative singular compound | in the three Paths |
| **yataḥ** | causal relative/adverb | because |

The three Paths are Seeing, cultivation, and beyond-training. The Bhāṣya's local contrast **nāmataḥ / dravyataḥ** concerns designation versus non-duplicated actual classification; it does not posit substantial selves.

## 4. Grammar

The long first compound is governed by adverbial **-taḥ** and supplies the respects according to which **pudgalāḥ** are **kṛtāḥ**: by application, faculty, meditative attainment, liberation, or both attainment and liberation.

The second line first predicates **sapta**, then corrects or qualifies it with **ṣaṭ tu ete**. The causal clause **dvau dvau mārgatraye yataḥ** explains the difference: because there are two persons in each of three Paths, the non-duplicated structure is six.

The sentence therefore distinguishes two counts under different relations:

```text
seven
    count of valid technical designations

six
    count of paired Path-positions
    across three Path-stages
```

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

Seven persons are constituted according to application, faculty, meditative attainment, liberation, and both. But these are six, because there are two and two in the three Paths.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

Noble persons receive seven technical designations according to five principles: mode of application distinguishes the faith-follower and Dharma-follower; faculty distinguishes the faith-resolved and seeing-attained; realization of cessation-attainment gives the name body-witness; liberation gives wisdom-liberated; and attainment together with liberation gives doubly liberated. Yet these are seven by name and six in actual non-duplicated Path-position, because the same two developmental continuities appear successively as two persons in Seeing, two in cultivation, and two beyond training.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

A Path-person is not a fixed substance standing behind changing labels. One developmental continuum receives a new valid name when its mode of application, faculty, attainment, liberation, or Path-stage is redetermined. The name expresses an objective Path-relation without creating another entity.

The sevenfold list is therefore a structured crossing of longitudinal development and transverse attainment. Two continuities pass through the three Paths; cessation-attainment introduces an additional designation across that movement. Numerical plurality belongs to articulated determination, not necessarily to numerical multiplication of subjects.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**The seven noble-person names**

The Bhāṣya enumerates:

```text
1. śraddhānusārin
2. dharmānusārin
3. śraddhādhimukta
4. dṛṣṭiprāpta
5. kāyasākṣin
6. prajñāvimukta
7. ubhayatovimukta
```

This is not the “seven Śrāvakas” of 6.62, which arose from five condition-dependent Arhat-types plus two subdivisions of the unshakeable Arhat. The present seven are generated by another principle of division.

**prayoga: the two followers**

The Bhāṣya differentiates the two Seeing persons by how they previously applied themselves to meanings:

```text
dependence upon another
    → śraddhānusārin

following Dharma
    → dharmānusārin
```

Their names preserve the genetic mode by which application approached noble Seeing.

**akṣa / indriya: cultivation persons**

The Bhāṣya interprets **akṣa** through faculty:

```text
mild faculties
    + predominance of faith/resolution
    → śraddhādhimukta

sharp faculties
    + predominance of wisdom
    → dṛṣṭiprāpta
```

The distinction is not one of different Truths but of operative capacity within the cultivation Path.

**samāpatti: the body-witness**

The designation **kāyasākṣin** is generated by direct realization of the attainment of cessation (**nirodha-samāpatti-sākṣātkaraṇa**). It must not be generalized into a merely metaphorical “embodied knowing.”

Because this designation arises from an attainment-axis, it cross-cuts the paired developmental lines rather than forming a third independent line.

**vimukti and ubhaya**

```text
liberation
    → prajñāvimukta
      wisdom-liberated

samāpatti + liberation
    → ubhayatobhāgavimukta
      liberated in both respects
```

The latter is distinguished jointly through meditative attainment and liberation.

**Two persons across three Paths**

The Bhāṣya gives the longitudinal identity:

```text
DARŚANA-MĀRGA
śraddhānusārin        dharmānusārin
       ↓                     ↓

BHĀVANĀ-MĀRGA
śraddhādhimukta       dṛṣṭiprāpta
       ↓                     ↓

AŚAIKṢA-MĀRGA
samayavimukta         asamayavimukta
```

“Those very same two” become the corresponding two in each later Path. The changed names register real changes in Path-relation while preserving developmental continuity.

**Seven by name, six in actual distinctness**

The Bhāṣya states:

```text
nāmataḥ
    seven designations

dravyataḥ
    six non-duplicated Path-positions
```

Here **dravyataḥ** is classificatory. Reading it as seven or six permanent personal substances would directly miss the argument, which is about overlap among valid names.

**The larger combinatorial field**

The prose immediately demonstrates that a named type can be further differentiated by faculty, disposition, Path-position, degree of dispassion, existential basis, attainment, and liberation. Very large counts therefore arise through intersecting dimensions:

```text
pudgala determination
    = prayoga
    + indriya
    + gotra
    + mārga-position
    + vairāgya
    + āśraya
    + samāpatti
    + vimukti
```

These are systematic coordinates of a continuum, not newly invented selves.

## 8. Logical Determination

The naming functions are:

```text
classifyBy(Prayoga)
    → FaithFollower / DharmaFollower

classifyBy(Faculty)
    → FaithResolved / SeeingAttained

classifyBy(CessationAttainment)
    → BodyWitness

classifyBy(Liberation)
    → WisdomLiberated

classifyBy(AttainmentAndLiberation)
    → DoublyLiberated
```

Development preserves continuity under changed predicates:

```text
ContinuumA
    → FaithFollower during Seeing
    → FaithResolved during Cultivation
    → ConditionDependentLiberated beyond Training

ContinuumB
    → DharmaFollower during Seeing
    → SeeingAttained during Cultivation
    → OccasionIndependentLiberated beyond Training
```

Thus:

```text
ChangeOfTechnicalName
    does not entail
NewSubstantialPerson

SameDevelopmentalContinuum
    does not entail
UnchangedPathDetermination
```

The *kāyasākṣin* adds a cross-cutting predicate based on attainment, explaining how the nominal count can exceed the non-duplicated longitudinal count.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 6.63 gives exact form to the claim that the individuals are the Path “in appearances.” They are not accidental illustrations appended to an already completed universal. Each person-name is the Path itself under a determinate relation: how Meaning was followed, which faculty predominates, which attainment is realized, and how liberation is possessed.

In the Organon reconstruction, the two continuities are the longitudinal movement of the Path, while *kāyasākṣin* supplies a transverse determination. The resulting structure is not a simple wheel or ladder but a multidimensional morphology in which one continuum can truthfully bear different names under different relations.

This does not import a metaphysical subject beneath the Kośa's analysis. The Bhāṣya itself distinguishes nominal proliferation from actual non-duplicated person-types and then multiplies classifications combinatorially. The restrained conclusion is that *pudgala* here functions as a determinate Path-position within a system of relations.
