# VAK_6.44 Bhāṣya — The Diamond-Like Final Uninterrupted Path

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ā-bhavāgrāṣṭa-bhāga-kṣid arhattve pratipannakaḥ |
> navamasyāpy ānantarya-pathe vajropamaś ca saḥ || 6.44 ||

> The candidate for arhatship destroys the grades from the first through the eighth, ending at the summit of existence. Even the uninterrupted Path directed against the ninth grade is diamond-like.

The non-returner remains a candidate for arhatship even on the final
uninterrupted Path. The diamond image expresses the power of that
Path, not possession of the fruit.

## 3. Continuous Translation

The non-returner remains the subject. From detachment from the first
cultivation-abandonable grade of the first dhyāna through the
abandonment of eight grades at the summit of existence, that person
proceeds toward arhatship. Even on the uninterrupted Path undertaken
to abandon the ninth grade at the summit, the practitioner is still
a candidate for arhatship.

That uninterrupted Path is called diamond-like concentration because
it can break all latent afflictions. It does not now break them all:
those already broken do not need to be broken again. Yet it has the
capacity to break all, for it is the strongest of all uninterrupted
Paths.

A plurality of diamond-like concentrations is then described. On
the preliminary ground (*anāgamya*), eight are associated
with aspects of Anvaya-Knowing concerning suffering and origin and
take suffering and origin at the summit as cognitive Conditions.
Eight further configurations are associated with aspects of
Dharma-Knowing concerning cessation and Path. Four taking cessation
of the first dhyāna as cognitive Condition are associated with
Anvaya-Knowing aspects of cessation; four such configurations are
counted for each ground through cessation at the summit, yielding
thirty-two. Another four are associated with Anvaya-Knowing aspects
of Path and take its whole Anvaya-Knowing side as cognitive Condition.
Thus eight plus eight plus thirty-two plus four, differentiated by
Knowing, aspect, and cognitive Condition, yield fifty-two diamond-like
concentrations for that ground. The same pattern extends through the
fourth dhyāna.

For the Essences of infinite space, infinite Cognition, and nothingness,
the stated counts are twenty-eight, twenty-four, and twenty,
respectively. The reason given is that Dharma-Knowing is absent there,
as is Anvaya-Knowing that takes cessation on a lower ground as its
cognitive Condition. Anvaya-Knowing can nevertheless take a Path
counteracting a lower ground as its cognitive Condition, because
those Paths stand in a mutual causal relation.

Those who allow Anvaya-Knowing concerning Path to take each ground's
counteractive Path separately add twenty-eight variants, bringing
the count on the preliminary ground to eighty. Corresponding counts
extend through the fourth dhyāna; in the three formless spheres the
running witness's alternative counts are forty, thirty-two, and
twenty-four. Further distinctions of lineage and faculty yield still
more variants. These are counts of cognitive and meditative
configurations of the same final counteractive function, not separate
arhat fruits.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

The commentary specifies the full range of arhatship candidacy,
including the last uninterrupted Path. It derives “diamond-like”
from universal counteractive capacity, while distinguishing that
capacity from the one grade presently removed. It then expands the
Path into variants by cognitive Condition, Knowing aspect, meditative
ground, and doctrinal treatment of the Path's Conditions.

## 5. Translation and Interpretation

**Translation decisions.** The source's `pratipakṣakaḥ` is not
silently replaced in §2; the study's `pratipannakaḥ` follows the
explicit candidate gloss in the prose. `Ānantarya-patha` remains the
uninterrupted Path directed against the ninth grade. `Sarvānuśaya-
bheditva` expresses capacity to break all latent afflictions,
qualified by the fact that earlier ones are already broken.
`Ālambana` is a cognitive Condition, while the last grade is the
Path's present target. Numerical alternatives remain attributed to
those who describe or accept them. The local 40, 32, 24 sequence is
translated as transmitted; Sangpo's 20, 32, 48 sequence requires
edition-level collation.

**Organon determination.** The Path's counteractive capacity can be
universal while its present work is determinate. Candidate status,
final severance, and fruit possession are separate moments; the
arhat fruit is not assigned within this unit. This is project
synthesis.

## 6. Final Path and Its Variants

| Determination | Source ground | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Arhatship candidate | From first higher-grade abandonment through eighth summit grade | Still proceeding |
| Final uninterrupted Path | Directed against ninth summit grade | Still candidate |
| Diamond-like concentration | Strongest uninterrupted Path | Capable of breaking all anuśayas |
| Present breaking | Earlier grades already broken | Only final grade remains |
| Variant calculation | Knowing, aspect, Condition, ground, lineage, faculty | Multiple configurations of one final function |

The source reports fifty-two configurations on the preliminary ground
under one account, eighty under an alternative, and corresponding
lower counts for three formless spheres. The function remains the
final uninterrupted counteraction.
