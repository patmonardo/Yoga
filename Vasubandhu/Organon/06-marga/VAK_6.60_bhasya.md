# VAK_6.60 Bhāṣya — Fallen, Not Dead; Nine Pairs to Unshakeable

Vaibhāṣika frame from the Talk. Sautrāntika refusal of fruit-fall still stands on the prior page.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> mriyate na phalabhraṣṭas tadakāryaṃ karoti na |
> vimuktyānantaryapathā navākopye 'tisevanāt || 6.60 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> One fallen from the fruit does not die, nor does he perform what is incompatible
> with it. In [the transition to] the unshakeable [disposition], there are nine
> liberation-paths and uninterrupted paths because of deep habituation.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> If she falls from Arhat-fruit, is she born again? No. No one fallen from the fruit
> dies while still fallen. Otherwise the holy life would give no assurance.
>
> Even fallen, she does not do what that fruit forbids. Like a hero who stumbles.
>
> When faculties shift into the unshakeable *gotra*, the one able to pierce through
> takes nine uninterrupted Paths and nine liberation Paths — as when Arhatship itself
> is got. Why so many? The weak-faculty type has been used hard. Trainee Path and
> beyond-training Path made it firm. A little work will not turn it.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *atisevanā* = the old weak type worn in, not a slogan for more practice;
- nine + nine, not nine total;
- this half stays inside the school that allows fruit-fall.
