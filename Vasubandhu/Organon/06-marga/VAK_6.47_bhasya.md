# VAK_6.47 Bhāṣya — Local Support; Range Through the Summit

First half finishes 6.46. Second half answers: from where, by which ground, dispassion?

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> bhavāgrādhavimuktordhvajātavattvasamanvayaḥ |
> anāsraveṇa vairāgyam anāgāmyena sarvataḥ || 6.47 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> As with one liberated from half [the grades] of the summit of existence, or with
> one born above, there is no [renewed] possession. Through an uncontaminated [Path]
> based on *anāgamya*, dispassion is attained from every level.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.47.md`](./VAK_6.47.md). Witness first compound is crushed; Bhāṣya reads *ardha-prakāra-vimukta*. Next: 6.48.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit Witness

> katapayā punar bhūmyā kuto vairāgyaṃ bhavati /
> anāsraveṇa vairāgyam anāgāmyena sarvataḥ / ābhavāgrāt /
> atha yaḥ sāmantakaṃ niśritya … kim asyānantaryamārgavat sarve vimuktimārgāḥ sāmantakād bhavanti / nety āha /

**Witness.** First-half counterexamples already at 367|05–10 (with 6.46). New question 367|11–14. Voice: Vasubandhu. 6.48 at 367|15.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> By which ground, and from what, does dispassion come? By the uncontaminated Path
> based on *anāgamya*, from every side — that is, through the summit of existence.
>
> If one relies on a preliminary concentration to get dispassion from a lower ground,
> do all liberation Paths arise from that same preliminary, as the uninterrupted Paths
> do? No. That is 6.48.

(The half-liberated-at-the-summit and born-above cases stay with 6.46's verdict: losing a *prāpti* is not the kleśa walking back.)

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *sarvataḥ* = *ābhavāgrāt*;
- first line is argument, not a new fruit;
- *anāgamya* is preliminary, not weak.
