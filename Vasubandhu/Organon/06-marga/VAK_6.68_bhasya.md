# VAK_6.68 Bhāṣya — Ten Under Thirty-Seven

Same Path. Count the stuff, keep the names.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> śraddhā vīryaṃ smṛtiḥ prajñā samādhiḥ prītyupekṣaṇe |
> praśrabdhiśīlasaṃkalpāḥ prajñā hi smṛtyupasthitiḥ || 6.68 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> Confidence, energy, recollection, prajñā, collectedness, joy, equanimity, pliancy,
> ethical discipline, and resolve are [the ten]. For the establishment of recollection
> is prajñā.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> All the Bodhi-side factors are ten *dravyas*. Which ten? Confidence, energy,
> recollection, prajñā, collectedness, joy and equanimity, pliancy, discipline,
> resolve. How counted? The establishments of recollection are prajñā. Energy is
> what is called the right abandonments. The bases of power are collectedness.
>
> Those three groups have the nature of prajñā, energy, collectedness. Faculties and
> powers are taken by their names. Establishments, investigation-of-dharmas, right
> view: prajñā. Right abandonments, energy-limb, right effort: energy. Bases of
> power, collectedness-limb, right collectedness: collectedness. Recollection-limb
> and right recollection: recollection. What is left? Joy, pliancy, equanimity as
> limbs; right resolve; the ethical limbs. Ten.
>
> Vaibhāṣikas: eleven. Body-act and speech-act do not merge, so the ethical limbs
> are two *dravyas*.
