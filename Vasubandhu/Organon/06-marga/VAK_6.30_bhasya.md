# VAK_6.30 Bhāṣya — Prior Abandonment and the Fruit Approached

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> yāvat pañcaprakāraghnau dvitīye 'rvāṅ navakṣayāt |
> kāmād viraktāv ūrdhvaṃ vā tṛtīyapratipannakau || 6.30 ||

> The Faith-Follower and Dharma-Follower still proceed toward stream-entry
> if a mundane path had previously abandoned no more than five grades of
> desire-realm affliction. If it had abandoned six, seven, or eight grades,
> they proceed toward once-return. If all nine desire-realm grades have
> been abandoned, or detachment extends higher, they proceed toward
> non-return. Their fifteen-moment Path of Seeing remains the same;
> the prior degree of detachment determines the fruit approached.

The commentary maps prior mundane abandonment to the fruit each follower
approaches within the same Path of Seeing.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

“Up to five grades destroyed”: if a mundane path previously abandoned
as many as five grades among desire-realm afflictions to be abandoned by
cultivation, the two followers are still said to be proceeding toward
the first fruit.

“Toward the second, short of destruction of the ninth”: if six, seven,
or eight grades in total were previously abandoned, these
two are called practitioners proceeding toward the second fruit. The
second fruit is once-return.

“Detached from the desire-realm, or higher”: if the ninth grade has also
been abandoned, the two are free of attachment to the desire-realm.
If detachment extends still higher, as far as the Essence of Nothingness,
they are called practitioners proceeding toward the third fruit. The
third fruit is non-return.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

The Bhāṣya retains the two followers of 6.29 and distributes them across
three prospective fruits according to prior mundane abandonment. Up to
five grades keeps first-fruit candidacy; six through eight move to the
second; nine and any specified higher detachment move to the third.

## 5. Translation and Organon Study

**Translation decisions.** `Yāvat pañca` sets the first-fruit upper
bound. `Arvāk navakṣayāt` means short of the ninth grade's destruction;
the prose fills the interval with six, seven, or eight previously
abandoned grades. `Kāmād viraktau` marks complete desire-realm
detachment. `Ūrdhvaṃ vā` extends that condition up to the Essence of
Nothingness. The source's uneven kārikā reading is preserved in the
witness and the separate study reading is kept in the anchor.

**Organon determination.** A reflective Model can type faculty,
prior-abandonment count, higher detachment, and target fruit separately.
The same fifteen-moment Seeing course reaches different fruit-candidate
statuses according to the beginning state of the continuum. This is
project synthesis.

## 6. Prior Abandonment and Prospective Fruit

| Prior mundane abandonment | Candidate for |
|---|---|
| Zero through five desire-realm grades | Stream-entry |
| Six through eight desire-realm grades | Once-return |
| All nine desire-realm grades | Non-return |
| Complete desire-realm detachment extending higher, as far as the Essence of Nothingness | Non-return |

These are statuses of proceeding toward fruit during Seeing, not
assertions that the fruits are already attained.
