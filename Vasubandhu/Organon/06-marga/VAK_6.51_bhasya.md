# VAK_6.51 Bhāṣya — Stainless Path; Conditioned and Unconditioned Fruit

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> śrāmaṇyam amalo mārgaḥ saṃskṛtāsaṃskṛtaṃ phalam |
> ekānnavatis tāni muktimārgāḥ saha kṣayaiḥ || 6.51 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> The stainless Path is śrāmaṇya. Its fruit is conditioned and unconditioned. Those
> are eighty-nine: the liberation-paths together with the cessations.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Whose fruits are the four? Fruits of śrāmaṇya. What is that? The stainless Path —
> the uncontaminated Path. By it one is a śramaṇa: afflictions are quieted. An
> ordinary person is not a śramaṇa in the strict sense: the quieting is not complete.
>
> Fruit is conditioned and unconditioned. Eighty-nine: eight uninterrupted and eight
> liberation Paths for what seeing abandons; nine grounds times nine grades for what
> cultivation abandons — eighty-one more. Uninterrupted Path *is* śrāmaṇya.
> Liberation Paths are the conditioned fruits. Abandonments of those afflictions are
> the unconditioned fruits. That is how eighty-nine is reached.
