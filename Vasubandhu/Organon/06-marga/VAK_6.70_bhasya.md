# VAK_6.70 Bhāṣya — Seven Groups, Seven Stations; Predominance Not Exile

Same Path. The principal moves.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ādikarmika-nirvedhabhāgīyeṣu prabhāvitāḥ |
> bhāvane darśane caiva sapta vargā yathākramam || 6.70 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> The seven groups are especially developed, in corresponding sequence, in the
> beginner, in the factors conducive to penetration, in Cultivation, and in Seeing.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> In which stage are which of these especially developed?
>
> Beginner: establishments of recollection, to mark body and the rest. Heat: right
> abandonments, because energy is bound to a further distinction. Summits: bases of
> power, because she has entered wholesome roots that do not fall. Receptivity:
> faculties, because governing power is gained and there is no further fall. Highest
> mundane dharmas: powers, because they cannot be crushed by kleśa — or by mundane
> dharmas. Cultivation: awakening-limbs, because Bodhi is near. Seeing: Path-limbs,
> because going is especially swift there.
>
> Others keep the order but place awakening-limbs in Seeing, because dharma-reality
> is first seen there, and stretch the eightfold Path across Seeing and Cultivation.

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *yathākramam* is real order;
- seven before eight is number-order, not a proof of time-order;
- keep *apare* on the page.
