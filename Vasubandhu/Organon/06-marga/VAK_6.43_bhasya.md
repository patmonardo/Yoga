# VAK_6.43 Bhāṣya — Five Pure Abodes; Bodily Witness

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tatpāñcavidhyāt pañcaiva śuddhāvāsopapattayaḥ |
> nirodhalābhy anāgāmī kāyasākṣī punar mataḥ || 6.43 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> Because of its fivefold differentiation, there are exactly five rebirths in the
> Pure Abodes. A non-returner who possesses attainment of cessation is further
> regarded as a bodily witness.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.43.md`](./VAK_6.43.md). Next: 6.44, diamond-like Path.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Why exactly five Pure-Abode rebirths? The mixed cultivation of fourth dhyāna is
> fivefold, mild through strongest. First grade: three cittas — uncontaminated,
> contaminated, uncontaminated. Then six, nine, twelve, fifteen. The five Abodes are
> their results in order. Rebirth there is by the contaminated piece. Others say:
> five from predominance of faculties beginning with faith.
>
> A non-returner who has the cessation attainment is called bodily witness: a
> nirvāṇa-like dharma realized through the body. How, if mind is absent? First
> answer: no citta, support is the body. Better: on emerging, a never-before bodily
> peace with cognition — “how peaceful is cessation, how like nirvāṇa.” Peace is
> thus bodily realized. Direct realization = making present through attainment and
> knowledge.
>
> Not a nineteenth trainee class: trainee-count rides the three uncontaminated
> trainings and their fruits; cessation is neither trainee nor non-trainee fruit.
> Coarse types so far. Fine cut: thousands — faculty, ground, lineage, station,
> detachment. The arithmetic is a field, not another ladder.
