# VAK_6.63 Bhāṣya — Seven Names; Two Lines Through Three Paths

Not the seven Śrāvakas of 6.62. *Pudgala* here is a Path-cut, not a little self.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> prayogākṣasamāpattivimuktyubhayataḥ kṛtāḥ |
> pudgalāḥ sapta ṣaṭ tv ete dvau dvau mārgatraye yataḥ || 6.63 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> Seven persons are constituted according to application, faculty, meditative
> attainment, liberation, and both. But these are six, because there are two and two
> in the three Paths.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> All noble persons are seven: faith-follower, Dharma-follower, faith-resolved,
> seeing-attained, body-witness, wisdom-liberated, liberated both ways.
>
> By application: the two followers — one leaned on another, one followed Dharma.
> By faculty: faith-resolved and seeing-attained — mild vs sharp, faith-hold vs
> wisdom. By attainment: body-witness, because she directly realizes cessation-
> attainment. By liberation: wisdom-liberated. By attainment and liberation: both.
>
> By name, seven. In actual distinctness, six. Seeing: the two followers. Those same
> two on cultivation: faith-resolved and seeing-attained. Those same two beyond
> training: condition-held liberated and liberated without regard to occasion.
