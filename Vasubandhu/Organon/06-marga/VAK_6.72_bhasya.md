# VAK_6.72 Bhāṣya — Ground Subtracts; Path Does Not Leave

6.71 gave first dhyāna the full set. 6.72 walks the rest of the house.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dvitīye 'nyatra saṃkalpād dvayos tad-dvaya-varjitāḥ |
> dhyānāntare ca śīlāṅgais tābhyāṃ ca triṣv arūpiṣu || 6.72 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> In the second [dhyāna], except for resolve; in the two [higher dhyānas] they are
> deprived of that pair, and likewise in the intermediate dhyāna. In the three
> formless [attainments] they are deprived of the ethical limbs and those two.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.72.md`](./VAK_6.72.md). Next: 6.73.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Second dhyāna: thirty-six. Right resolve gone, because *vitarka* is gone.
> Third and fourth: thirty-five. Joy and resolve gone. Intermediate dhyāna: the same
> two gone.
>
> In the three formless: thirty-two. Those two gone, and the ethical limbs — right
> speech, right action, right livelihood — gone with them.

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *tad-dvaya* = *prīti* + *saṃkalpa*;
- *triṣv arūpiṣu* = the three below Bhavāgra;
- count the thirty-seven names, not the ten *dravyas*.
