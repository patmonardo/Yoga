# VAK_6.75 Bhāṣya — Partial Cut Is Real; “Liberated” Is Not Yet the Name

Give the trainee her abandonments. Do not give her the unqualified word.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> mārgaś ca dravyatas tu dve śraddhā śīlaṃ ca nirmalāḥ |
> noktā vimuktiḥ śaikṣāṅgaṃ baddhatvāt sā punar dvidhā || 6.75 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> And the Path [is Dharma]. But according to operative reality they are two,
> confidence and ethical discipline, and they are stainless. Liberation is not stated
> as a factor of the trainee because of being bound; that liberation, in turn, is
> twofold.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.75.md`](./VAK_6.75.md). Next: 6.76.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit Witness

> kasmāt samyagvimuktiḥ samyagjñānaṃ ca śaikṣasya nokte /
> śaikṣo hi kleśabandhanabaddhaḥ / na hi bandhanaikadeśān mukto mukta ity ucyate /
> vinā ca vimuktyā vimuktijñānaṃ kutaḥ /
> aśaikṣas tu sarvakleśabandhanair vimuktaḥ /
> keyaṃ vimuktir nāma / saṃskṛtā cāsaṃskṛtā ca /
> asaṃskṛtā kleśahānam / adhimuktas tu saṃskṛtā /

**Witness.** Pradhan 387|14–388|05. Voice: Vasubandhu. 6.76 takes *ceto-* / *prajñā-vimukti*.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Why are right liberation and right knowledge not said of the trainee? Because she
> is still bound by kleśa-bonds. One loosed from only a portion of bondage is not
> called liberated. Without liberation, from where knowledge of liberation?
>
> The one beyond training is loosed from all those bonds. So liberation and the
> immediate knowledge of it are said of her.
>
> What is this liberation? Conditioned and unconditioned. Unconditioned: abandonment
> of the afflictions. Conditioned: the Aśaikṣa's *adhimokṣa*.

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *baddhatvāt* is leftover kleśa-bond, not “homework remains”;
- trainee-cuts stay real;
- two liberations, not a mood and a vacuum.
