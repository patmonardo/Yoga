# VAK_6.52 Bhāṣya — Four Principal Fruits; Five Reasons

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> catuṣphalavyavasthā tu pañcakāraṇasaṃbhavāt |
> pūrvatyāgo 'nyamārgāptiḥ kṣayasaṃkalanaṃ phale || 6.52 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> But the establishment of four fruits is due to the occurrence of five reasons:
> relinquishment of the preceding [Path], attainment of another Path, and the
> gathering of cessations in the fruit.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.52.md`](./VAK_6.52.md). Reasons 4–5 open 6.53.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Then must the Buddha add a longer list? No. Though the fruits are many, the four
> are set where five reasons occur together in an abandonment-Path stage.
>
> Relinquish the previous Path; get a Path not had before — drop the candidate-Path,
> take the fruit-Path. Gather the abandonments: one attainment of the whole. (Then
> eight knowledges at once, and cultivation of the sixteen aspects beginning with
> impermanence.) These five occur at each principal fruit.
