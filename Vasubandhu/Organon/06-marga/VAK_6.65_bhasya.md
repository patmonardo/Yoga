# VAK_6.65 Bhāṣya — Completeness Beyond Training; Path in Four Names

He steps back from the person-shop. Mārga in summary.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> aśaikṣaparipūrṇatvaṃ dvābhyāṃ mārgaḥ samāsataḥ |
> viśeṣamuktyānantaryaprayogākhyaś caturvidhaḥ || 6.65 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> The completeness of one beyond training is through two. In summary, the Path is
> fourfold, bearing the names Further Distinction, Release, Immediacy, and
> Application.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Completeness beyond training is by two: faculty and attainment. If the fruit were
> missing she would not be beyond training at all. So fruit is not counted again as
> complete vs incomplete.
>
> Many Path-cuts have been said. How many is this Path in summary? Four.
> Application: that after which Immediacy arises at once. Immediacy: that by which
> she abandons the cover. Release: that which first arises free of the cover to be
> abandoned. Further Distinction: Path other than those three.
>
> Why still Path? This is the road to nirvāṇa; by it one goes there. Release and
> Further Distinction stay Path because they can be stronger of the same kind, can
> reach the next, or can enter nirvāṇa without remainder.
