# VAK_6.62 Bhāṣya — Relinquish the Weak Complex; Nine by Faculty

Closes *yataḥ* from 6.61. Seven Śrāvakas here ≠ seven Āryas of 6.63.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> saviśeṣaṃ phalaṃ tyaktvā phalam āpnoti vardhayan |
> dvau buddhau śrāvakāḥ sapta navaite navadhendriyāḥ || 6.62 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> One strengthening [the faculties], having relinquished the fruit together with its
> specific determination, attains [the] fruit. There are two Buddhas and seven
> Śrāvakas; these nine are [distinguished] through the ninefold faculties.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.62.md`](./VAK_6.62.md). Next: 6.63.

**Witness.** Pradhan 379|13–380|05. Voice: Vasubandhu. Seven Āryas start at 380|04 for 6.63.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Strengthening faculties, she drops the fruit together with the Path marked by weak
> faculties, and takes the fruit-and-Path of the sharp-faculty type. Non-returner
> fruit is not held on a formless ground. That is why the trainee has only six.
>
> These same six Arhats become nine by faculty-split. Seven Śrāvakas: the five from
> the fall-liable type, plus the unshakeable split in two — arrived by sharpening,
> or that *gotra* from the start. Two Buddhas: Pratyekabuddha and Buddha. Nine
> persons from the nine grades beginning mild-mild.
