# VAK_6.31 Bhāṣya — Sixteenth Moment; Standing in the Fruit

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ṣoḍaśe tu phalasthau tau yatra yaḥ pratipannakaḥ |
> śraddhādhimuktadṛṣṭyāptau mṛdutīkṣṇendriyau tadā || 6.31 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> But at the sixteenth [moment], those two are established in the fruit toward
> which each had been proceeding. Then the practitioners of gentle and sharp
> faculties are respectively the Faith-Resolved and the one Attained through View.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.31.md`](./VAK_6.31.md). Next: 6.32.

**Witness.** Pradhan 354|09–354|18. Voice: Vasubandhu. 6.32 at 354|18–19.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> When the sixteenth citta has arisen they are no longer called Faith-Follower and
> Dharma-Follower, nor persons proceeding toward a fruit. What then? They stand in
> the fruit. Whichever fruit each had been approaching, in that fruit each now
> stands: stream-entry, once-return, or non-return. Arhatship cannot be got at the
> outset: Seeing does not abandon what cultivation abandons, and prior detachment
> from the summit of existence is impossible.
>
> Then the gentle-faculty one who was a Faith-Follower is called śraddhādhimukta;
> the sharp-faculty one who was a Dharma-Follower is called dṛṣṭiprāpta — faith and
> prajñā predominating, so resolve and seeing mark them.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text

```
