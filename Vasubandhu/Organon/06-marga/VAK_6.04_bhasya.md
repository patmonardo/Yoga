# VAK_6.04 Bhāṣya — Conventional and Ultimate: What Cognition Survives

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> yatra bhinnena tadbuddhir anyāpohe dhiyā ca tat |
> ghaṭārthavat saṃvṛtisat paramārthasad anyathā || 6.04 ||

> Where, when it is divided, or when other [dharmas] are excluded by cognition,
> the cognition of that [fails] — that is conventionally real, like the object
> “pot.” The ultimately real is otherwise.

Hearing / reflection / cultivation: 6.05.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> If, when the thing is broken part by part, cognition of *that* no longer
> occurs — pot into shards — it is conventionally real. Or if, when other dharmas
> are excluded in thought, that cognition fails — water without visible form and
> the rest. Saying “the pot exists,” “water exists” is true under convention, not
> a lie.
>
> Ultimately real is otherwise: cognition of own-nature remains — visible form
> after atoms, after other associated dharmas are set aside; feeling likewise.
>
> Former teachers add: what is grasped in the same way by supramundane knowledge
> or by the mundane knowledge acquired after it is ultimate truth; what is
> grasped otherwise is conventional. Reported, not the verse's own test.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
two tests for saṃvṛti: break / exclude
paramārtha: own-nature cognition remains
convention still truth
```

## 5. Not Ultimate ≠ Unreal

Pot-test ≠ water-test. `Apoha` here is exclusion in analysis, not a later
philosophy of names. This Vaibhāṣika criterion ≠ Madhyamaka emptiness. Two
truths ≠ a replacement for the four.

## 6. Tests

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- negative “fails” supplied in prose;
- pot = breakage; water = cognitive exclusion;
- `anyathā` = persistence of svabhāva-buddhi.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Paramārtha in kāmadhātu still has a test: what remains when the configuration
is taken apart. Vidyā does not promote the pot. Appearance would call the pot
false or the atom God. The verse does neither. Project overlay from the prior
pass is not reused here.
