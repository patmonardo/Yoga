# VAK 6.73 — Limits of Ground and Verified Confidence

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
कामधातौ भवाग्रे च बोधिमार्गाङ्गवर्जिताः ।
त्रिसत्यदर्शने शीलधर्मावेत्यप्रसादयोः ॥ ६.७३ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
kāmadhātau bhavāgre ca bodhimārgāṅgavarjitāḥ |
trisatyadarśane śīladharmāvetyaprasādayoḥ || 6.73 ||
```

The first half completes the distribution of the thirty-seven factors by ground. The second opens a new question within the Bodhi-aligned Path: when does verified confidence arise, and which object does direct realization verify?

The second half is elliptical. The Bhāṣya supplies “acquisition” (**pratilambha**) and explains the dual as confidence concerning Dharma and concerning noble ethical discipline.

## 4. Grammar

In the first line, the understood subject remains the Bodhi-aligned dharmas. The compound **bodhimārgāṅgavarjitāḥ** removes two complete groups—the seven Bodhyaṅgas and eight Mārgāṅgas—from the thirty-seven.

The locatives **kāmadhātau** and **bhavāgre** specify the ground-attribution of those factors, not necessarily the practitioner’s realm of birth.

The second line can be construed:

```text
tri-satya-darśane
    at seeing the three Truths

śīla-dharma-avetyaprasādayoḥ
    [there is acquisition] of the two verified confidences
    concerning ethical discipline and Dharma
```

The fourth Truth and the other two confidences are supplied by the Bhāṣya and completed in 6.74.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> In the desire realm and at the summit of existence, they are deprived of the factors of awakening and factors of the Path. At the seeing of the three Truths, [there is acquisition] of the two verified confidences concerning ethical discipline and Dharma.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> In the desire realm and at the summit of existence, the awakening and Path factors are absent. At the seeing of the three Truths, [there is] acquisition of verified confidence in ethical discipline and Dharma.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The highest conditioned ground and the ordinary desire realm meet at one limit: neither is itself a ground of uncontaminated Path-operation. Extremes of sensuous involvement and conditioned formless refinement therefore lack the specifically noble awakening- and Path-functions, although other Bodhi-aligned cultivation remains possible.

Direct Seeing then transforms confidence. Prasāda is no longer anticipatory trust directed toward an unseen result; it becomes settled clarity grounded in comprehension of Truth. The Path generates its own verified orientation through realization.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Kāmadhātu / Bhavāgra` — twenty-two factors in each ground**

The count is:

```text
37 total Bodhi-aligned functions
  - 7 Bodhyaṅgas
  - 8 Mārgāṅgas
  = 22
```

The Bhāṣya’s reason is explicit:

```text
no anāsrava-mārga assigned to the ground
    → no Bodhyaṅga group
    → no Mārgāṅga group
```

The five remaining groups may be contaminated or uncontaminated in principle, as 6.71 established; here the ground itself lacks the uncontaminated Path-functions technically designated by the final two groups.

**`Kāmadhātu / bhūmi` — ground assignment and the practitioner's realm**

This statement must not be inferred:

```text
a being presently born in Kāmadhātu
    cannot generate the Noble Path
```

The distribution concerns the **bhūmi** to which a Path-dharma belongs. A practitioner with a desire-realm existential basis can generate an uncontaminated Path belonging to an eligible contemplative ground such as anāgamya or a dhyāna.

Thus:

```text
practitioner’s existential location
    ≠ necessarily
Path-dharma’s contemplative ground
```

This distinction prevents the existential Kāmadhātu from being treated as an absolute prohibition on awakening.

**`Bhavāgra` — the summit of conditioned existence**

Bhavāgra is the summit of conditioned existence, the attainment of neither perception nor non-perception:

```text
highest conditioned refinement
    ≠ uncontaminated liberating ground
```

Its subtlety does not convert it into the unconditioned. In the present system it supports twenty-two Bodhi-aligned functions but not the Bodhyaṅgas and Noble Mārgāṅgas.

**`Bodhipakṣya / lābha` — from distribution to acquisition**

Vasubandhu asks:

```text
For one proceeding among the Bodhi-aligned factors,
when is avetyaprasāda acquired?
```

The analysis changes from:

```text
where Path-functions can occur

to

what direct realization generates
in the practitioner
```

**`Avetyaprasāda` — verified confidence**

The compound joins:

```text
avetya
    having directly comprehended or ascertained

prasāda
    clarity, serene confidence, settled trust
```

“Verified confidence” preserves the genetic relation:

```text
direct realization
    → verified comprehension
    → settled confidence
```

This is not blind belief and not merely assent to a proposition.

**`Trisatya-darśana` — Seeing the first three Truths**

Direct realization of:

```text
duḥkha
    suffering

samudaya
    origin

nirodha
    cessation
```

produces verified confidence in:

```text
Dharma
    the teaching and realized truth now directly confirmed

āryakānta-śīla
    ethical disciplines dear to the noble ones
```

The ethical confidence is not verification of conventional morality in isolation. It concerns the uncontaminated ethical discipline belonging to noble Path-actuality.

**`Mārga-satya` — the fourth Truth and four confidences**

The Bhāṣya anticipates the continuation:

```text
seeing Mārga-satya
    → verified confidence in Buddha
    → verified confidence in Saṃgha
    → and also Dharma and noble ethical discipline
```

The word **api** carrying this inclusion belongs to the next verse’s construal and should not be inserted into the primary translation of 6.73.

**`Buddha / Saṃgha` — their dharmic constitution**

The Bhāṣya explains confidence in these objects through their dharmic constitution:

```text
Buddha
    constituted by aśaikṣa dharmas

Saṃgha
    constituted by śaikṣa and aśaikṣa dharmas
```

Verified confidence in Buddha and Saṃgha therefore remains connected to direct comprehension of Path-dharmas rather than becoming an externally appended devotional requirement.

## 8. Logical Determination

The ground rule is:

```text
GroundLacks(AnāsravaMārga)
    → excludes(BodhyaṅgaGroup)
    → excludes(MārgāṅgaGroup)
```

Applied to the two limits:

```text
Kāmadhātu supports 22 Bodhipakṣya functions
Bhavāgra supports 22 Bodhipakṣya functions
```

The attribution rule is:

```text
PersonOccursIn(x, Kāmadhātu)
    does not entail
EveryPathDharmaOf(x) belongsTo KāmadhātuGround
```

The epistemic-practical rule is:

```text
DirectlyRealizes(x, Truth)
    → Acquires(x, VerifiedConfidence)
```

More specifically:

```text
RealizationOf(FirstThreeTruths)
    → DharmaConfidence
    + NobleEthicalConfidence
```

Confidence here is therefore an effect of Seeing, not a substitute for it.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya counts twenty-two factors in each ground and explains the missing groups by the absence of an uncontaminated Path there. It supplies acquisition (`lābha`) for the compressed second line and names suffering, origin, and cessation as the three Truths. The Path-truth and the remaining confidences open 6.74, so they are left out of this verse translation.

This verse presents two forms of determination by field. First, a contemplative field determines which Path-functions can become actual within it. Second, the direct field of Truth determines which confidence becomes verified.

```text
bhūmi
    field permitting an operation

satya-darśana
    object-field directly realized

avetyaprasāda
    transformed capacity generated by that realization
```

Bhavāgra supplies the sharp warning: the furthest conditioned refinement is not liberation merely because it is highest. It remains a *bhava* and lacks the uncontaminated Path. Conversely, Kāmadhātu does not imprison the person within its own ground-determination; the practitioner can actualize a Path belonging to another eligible contemplative bhūmi.

For the Organon reconstruction, verified confidence is Truth returned into practical subjectivity. Seeing does not leave behind only a known proposition; it produces a stable orientation toward Dharma and noble conduct. The source-layer claim is the precise acquisition of *avetyaprasāda* at the direct realization of the Truths.
