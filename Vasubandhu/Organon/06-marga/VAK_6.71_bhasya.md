# VAK_6.71 Bhāṣya — Purity Cut; First Dhyāna Holds All Thirty-Seven

Two more axes. Same Path.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> anāsravāṇi bodhyaṅgamārgāṅgāni dvidhetare |
> sakalāḥ prathame dhyāne anāgamye prīti varjitāḥ || 6.71 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> The factors of awakening and factors of the Path are without outflows; the others
> are twofold. All are present in the first dhyāna; in the preliminary concentration
> they are without joy.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.71.md`](./VAK_6.71.md). Next: 6.72.

**Witness.** Pradhan 385|12–23. Voice: Vasubandhu. 6.72 continues the ground-map.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> How many of these are with outflows, how many without? Awakening-limbs and
> Path-limbs are without outflows: they are set on Cultivation and Seeing. Mundane
> right view and the rest do occur; they do not take the name Noble Path. The other
> five groups are both ways.
>
> In which ground, how many? All thirty-seven in the first dhyāna. In *anāgamya*
> they lack joy. Why? The neighboring attainments run by effort, and they stay
> wary of the lower ground.
