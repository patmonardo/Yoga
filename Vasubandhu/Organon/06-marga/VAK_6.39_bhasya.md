## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Three kinds of non-returner, each split three ways: nine who go to form. Which
> three? Intermediate-completer, after-rebirth completer, upward-streamer. First:
> quick, less quick, delayed — three comparisons. After-rebirth: soon after arising,
> with exertion, without. Upward-streamer: leaper and the rest. Or each of the three
> by speed of completion.
>
> Their difference: from difference of karma, affliction, and faculty. The three
> (and the nine) differ by karma to be felt at formation, at rebirth, or in a later
> existence; by mild, medium, strong active affliction; by strong, medium, mild
> faculty. First two triads: affliction and faculty as before. Last triad: later-
> result karma. So ninefold affliction-and-faculty: nine non-returners.
