# VAK 6.26 — The Ordered Moments of Uncontaminated Realization

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
कामदुःखे ततोऽत्रैव धर्मज्ञानं तथा पुनः ।
शेषे दुःखेऽन्वयक्षान्तिज्ञाने सत्यत्रये तथा ॥ ६.२६ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
kāmaduḥkhe tato 'traiva dharmajñānaṃ tathā punaḥ |
śeṣe duḥkhe 'nvayakṣāntijñāne satyatraye tathā || 6.26 ||
```

```text
kāma-duḥkhe | tataḥ | atra | eva | dharma-jñānam | tathā | punaḥ |
śeṣe | duḥkhe | anvaya-kṣānti-jñāne | satya-traye | tathā
```

The verse depends upon the first moment announced in 6.25, **duḥkhe dharmajñānakṣānti**. Its compressed **anvayakṣāntijñāne** is expanded by the Bhāṣya as **anvayajñānakṣānti** and **anvayajñāna**.

## 4. Grammar

The first clause continues directly from 6.25. **tataḥ** refers to Dharma-Knowledge-Acceptance concerning Desire Realm suffering; **atraiva** retains that same cognitive Condition; and **dharmajñānam [utpadyate]** supplies the next arising: “immediately thereafter, Dharma-Knowledge arises with regard to this same [suffering].”

In **śeṣe duḥkhe**, “remaining” means suffering belonging to the form and formless domains. The dual **anvayakṣāntijñāne** supplies two successive subjects of the understood verb “arise”: Anvaya-Knowledge-Acceptance and Anvaya-Knowledge.

**satyatraye tathā** extends the full pattern established for suffering to the other three Truths. It does not merely say that those Truths are similar in content; it carries over the ordered four-moment construction.

The ellipsis resolves as:

```text
for each Truth:
    Dharma-Knowledge-Acceptance arises;
    then Dharma-Knowledge;
    then Anvaya-Knowledge-Acceptance;
    then Anvaya-Knowledge.
```

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> After that, Dharma-Knowledge arises regarding this same Desire Realm
> suffering. Then Anvaya-Acceptance and Anvaya-Knowledge arise regarding
> the remaining suffering. So too for the other three Truths.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> For suffering in the desire realm comes Dharma-Knowledge; then, for the
> remaining suffering, Anvaya-Acceptance and Anvaya-Knowledge; and likewise
> for each of the other three Truths.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

The first uncontaminated breakthrough unfolds into an articulated order. Acceptance of suffering in the Desire Realm is followed immediately by Knowing of that same field. The realization then follows through into the corresponding suffering of the higher domains, first as Acceptance and then as Knowing.

This ordered relation is repeated for origin, cessation, and Path. Direct realization is therefore neither external inference from propositions nor an undifferentiated intuition without internal form. It is one continuous Path whose identity is sustained through determinate differences of Truth, domain, and cognitive function.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Dharma-jñāna` — the Dharma pair**

Acceptance then Knowing, with Desire Realm suffering as cognitive Condition for both. Named for the first knowing of the reality of the dharma (*dharmatattva*).

**`Anvaya-jñāna` — the Anvaya pair**

The same pair for the Truth in the higher domains, whose form- and Formless Realm fields together form one cognitive Condition (*samastālambana*).

**`Anvaya` — ordered following**

*Anugamana*: the higher-domain realization follows the Desire Realm realization of the same Truth. It carries one Truth across domains, not a new Truth.

**`Kṣānti` / `jñāna` — Acceptance and Knowing**

The invariant sequence in every pair, as flower to fruit (6.25). Their identification with uninterrupted and liberation paths belongs to 6.28.

**`Anāsrava` — uncontaminated throughout**

The whole sequence is uncontaminated; no later moment falls back into mundane cognition.

**`Duḥkha` — the four moments concerning suffering**

```text
1. duḥkhe dharmajñānakṣānti
2. duḥkhe dharmajñāna
3. duḥkhe anvayajñānakṣānti
4. duḥkhe anvayajñāna
```

The same four arise for origin, cessation and Path. For Path, the Desire Realm Condition is the Path counteracting Desire Realm suffering.

## 8. Logical Determination

The basic unit has two differentiations:

```text
domain:
    Desire Realm → Dharma pair
    higher domains collectively → Anvaya pair

function:
    Acceptance → Knowing
```

For one Truth:

```text
DharmaAcceptance
    → DharmaKnowing
    → AnvayaAcceptance
    → AnvayaKnowing
```

Applied to all Four Truths:

```text
4 Truths × 4 ordered moments = 16 uncontaminated moments
```

The Bhāṣya characterizes their temporal form as **samanantarotpattikrama**, a sequence of immediately successive arising. Each cognition is generated immediately after its predecessor; the series is not a simultaneous survey.

In Organon terms, explicitly as project synthesis, this is the **articulated copula of direct realization**:

```text
identity of one Path
    through
difference of Truth
+ difference of domain
+ difference of cognitive function
+ necessary succession
```

Meaning is direct here precisely because the articulation has become the self-developing order of uncontaminated cognition, not because articulation has disappeared.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The Bhāṣya specifies the combined higher-realm
Condition, universal uncontaminated status, and immediate succession. It also
enumerates the remaining Truths and explains `anvaya` as following in
accordance with the initial realization, rather than as a separate Truth.


VAK 6.25 crossed from the final mundane preparation into the first uncontaminated Acceptance. VAK 6.26 shows that this threshold is not a solitary event. It generates the ordered content of the Path of Seeing.

The distinction between Dharma and Anvaya is especially important. Realization first takes the Desire Realm form of a Truth, then follows that Truth through into its corresponding higher-domain field. Identity is maintained not by ignoring difference but by an ordered correspondence across it.

Likewise, Acceptance and Knowing must remain distinct while internally connected. The first leads immediately to the second for every Truth and domain-pair. At this verse, their exact later path-functions remain deliberately open; what is secure is their generated order.

This provides a fitting stopping-point for the first dive. Paramārtha Meaning has emerged neither as a detached item nor as a featureless intuition. It is Path: a rigorously ordered realization in which Truth, domain, cognitive function, purity-status, and succession determine one another.

The kārikā states the Dharma-Knowledge following the first Acceptance, the Anvaya pair for remaining suffering, and repetition across the other three Truths. Collective higher-domain support, universal uncontaminated status, the explanations of Dharma and Anvaya, the full enumeration, immediate succession, and the specific counteractive Path Condition are Bhāṣya determinations. “Articulated copula of direct realization” is the Organon's reconstruction.
