# VAK_6.53 Bhāṣya — Eight Knowledges; Mixed Fruit Held Stainless

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> jñānāṣṭakasya lābho 'tha ṣoḍaśākārabhāvanā |
> laukikāptaṃ tu miśratvād anāsravāptir dhṛteḥ phalam || 6.53 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> And there is acquisition of the eight knowledges and cultivation of the sixteen
> aspects. What was attained mundanely is nevertheless fruit because of mixture and
> because it is maintained by an uncontaminated attainment.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.53.md`](./VAK_6.53.md). Next: 6.54, brāhmaṇya / wheel.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Eight knowledges at once: the four Dharma-knowledges and four Anvaya-knowledges.
> Cultivation of the sixteen aspects beginning with impermanence. These five reasons
> occur at each principal fruit.
>
> If śrāmaṇya is the uncontaminated Path, how are the two fruits partly got by a
> mundane Path still fruits of śrāmaṇya? Not mundane abandonment alone. Seeing-Path
> abandonment is mixed in. One attainment of separation holds everything in that
> fruit. And an uncontaminated attainment of separation maintains that abandonment.
