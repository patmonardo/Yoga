# VAK_6.42 Bhāṣya — Fourth Dhyāna Mixed First; Streams, Not Moments

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ākīryate caturthaṃ prāk niṣpattiḥ kṣaṇamiśraṇāt |
> upapattivihārārthaṃ kleśabhīrutayāpi ca || 6.42 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> The fourth [dhyāna] is mixed first; completion is through the mixing of moments
> — for the sake of rebirth and abiding, and also because of fear of the afflictions.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.42.md`](./VAK_6.42.md). Next: 6.43.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Which dhyāna is mixed first? The fourth — a concentration that can do every work,
> first among pleasant courses. Arhat or non-returner enters a continuous uncontaminated
> fourth, then the same contaminated, then uncontaminated again. Preparation shortens
> that to two-and-two-and-two moments.
>
> Completion through mixing moments: Vaibhāṣikas say one contaminated moment between
> uncontaminated ones. They liken moments to uninterrupted and liberation Paths. After
> the fourth is mixed, the others are mixed by its power. First done in three continents
> of the desire-domain; later, after a fall, in the form-domain.
>
> But moment-mixing is impossible except for a Buddha. We take completion as entering
> the three streams at will. Why mix? Rebirth, present abiding, fear of affliction.
> Sharp-faculty non-returner: Pure Abodes and pleasant abiding here. Mild-faculty: also
> to keep savoring-samādhi distant and not fall. Sharp arhat: abiding. Mild arhat: also
> fear of affliction, not to fall.
