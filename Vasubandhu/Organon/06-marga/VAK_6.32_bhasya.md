# VAK_6.32 Bhāṣya — Fruit Is Not the Next Path

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> phale phalaviśiṣṭasya lābho mārgasya nāsty ataḥ |
> nāprayukto viśeṣāya phalasthaḥ pratipannakaḥ || 6.32 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> When [one is established] in the fruit, there is no attainment of the Path
> superior to that fruit. Therefore, a fruit-established person who is not applied
> toward further distinction is not one proceeding [toward another fruit].

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.32.md`](./VAK_6.32.md). Next: 6.33, ānupūrvika.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit Witness

> kiṃ punaḥ kāraṇaṃ prahīṇapañcaprakāro 'pi ṣoḍaśe citte srota-āpanna evocyate na sakṛdāgāmiphalapratipannakaḥ /
> yasmāt phale … phalaviśiṣṭo mārgo na labhyata ity eṣa niyamaḥ /

> yas tu tṛtīyadhyānavītarāgo 'dharāṃ bhūmiṃ niśritya niyāmam avakrāmati so 'vaśyaṃ phalaviśiṣṭaṃ mārgaṃ saṃmukhīkaroti /

**Witness.** Pradhan 354|18–355|03. Voice: Vasubandhu. 6.33 after `ānupūrvikaṃ tu vaktavyam`.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Why, even with five grades already gone, is one a stream-enterer at the sixteenth
> citta, not one proceeding toward once-return? Because when the fruit is being
> got, the Path distinguished beyond that fruit is not got. Fixed rule. Until one
> standing in a fruit applies toward further distinction for another fruit, that
> one is not called a candidate. Same at the other fruits.
>
> Exceptioned case: already detached from the third dhyāna, entering the fixed order
> on a lower ground, must bring the superior Path to presence — else, born higher,
> without the pleasure faculty. Thus far: persons entering with prior desire-domain
> detachment. The gradual case next.
