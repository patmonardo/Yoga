# VAK_6.78 Bhāṣya — Three Domain-Names; One Unconditioned Liberation

Not the eighteen. Not the three worlds. Termini of one End.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> asaṃskṛtaiva dhātvākhyā virāgo rāgasaṃkṣayaḥ |
> prahāṇadhātur anyeṣāṃ nirodhākhyas tu vastunaḥ || 6.78 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> The [liberation] bearing the dhātu-designations is exclusively unconditioned.
> Dispassion is the complete exhaustion of attachment; the abandonment-domain
> concerns the others, whereas what is called cessation concerns the basis.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.78.md`](./VAK_6.78.md). Next: 6.79.

**Witness.** Pradhan 389|12–20. Voice: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> What is the difference among these? One unconditioned liberation. Three names.
> Abandonment of attachment: domain of dispassion. Abandonment of the other
> afflictions: domain of abandonment. Cessation of the basis already freed from the
> afflictions: domain of cessation.

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *vastu* = kleśa-free basis, not a blank thing;
- three names, not three unconditioned stuffs;
- *dhātu* here is Domain of liberation, not the 18.
