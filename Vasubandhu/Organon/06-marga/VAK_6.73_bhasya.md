# VAK_6.73 Bhāṣya — Two Limits of Ground; Confidence After Seeing

Highest *bhava* is still *bhava*. Desire-ground is not a jail of the person.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāmadhātau bhavāgre ca bodhimārgāṅgavarjitāḥ |
> trisatyadarśane śīladharmāvetyaprasādayoḥ || 6.73 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> In the desire domain and at the summit of existence, they are deprived of the
> factors of awakening and factors of the Path. At the seeing of the three Truths,
> [there is acquisition] of the two verified confidences concerning ethical
> discipline and Dharma.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.73.md`](./VAK_6.73.md). Next: 6.74.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit Witness

**Witness.** Pradhan 386|10–18. Voice: Vasubandhu. Fourth Truth completed in 6.74.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> In desire-domain and at Bhavāgra: twenty-two. No uncontaminated Path there, so no
> awakening-limbs, no Path-limbs.
>
> For one moving among the Bodhi-side factors: when is verified confidence gained?
> At seeing the three Truths — suffering, origin, cessation — confidence in Dharma
> and in the discipline dear to the noble ones. At seeing the Path-truth: confidence
> in Buddha and Saṅgha. Those two are confidence in the beyond-training dharmas that
> make a Buddha, and in the training and beyond-training dharmas that make a Saṅgha.
> *Api*: Path-seeing also takes the first two confidences.

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- twenty-two is 37 minus 7 minus 8;
- *prasāda* here is after *abhisamaya*, not a prior mood;
- do not lock the person inside Kāmadhātu as ground.
