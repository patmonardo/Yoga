# VAK 6.31 — From Fruit-Candidate to Fruit-Established

## 1. Sanskrit Devanāgarī

षोडशे तु फलस्थौ तौ यत्र यः प्रतिपन्नकः ।
श्रद्धाधिमुक्तदृष्ट्याप्तौ मृदुतीक्ष्णेन्द्रियौ तदा ॥ ६.३१ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit IAST

ṣoḍaśe tu phalasthau tau yatra yaḥ pratipannakaḥ |
śraddhādhimuktadṛṣṭyāptau mṛdutīkṣṇendriyau tadā || 6.31 ||

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

**Padaccheda**

ṣoḍaśe | tu | phala-sthau | tau | yatra | yaḥ | pratipannakaḥ |
śraddhā-adhimukta-dṛṣṭi-āptau | mṛdu-tīkṣṇa-indriyau | tadā

| Form | Analysis | Contextual force |
|---|---|---|
| **ṣoḍaśe** | locative singular | at the sixteenth cognitive moment |
| **tu** | transitional or contrastive particle | but, however; marks the change from candidacy to attained fruit |
| **phala-sthau** | masculine nominative dual compound | the two established in a fruit |
| **tau** | masculine nominative dual demonstrative | those two: the former Faith- and Dharma-Followers |
| **yatra yaḥ pratipannakaḥ** | distributive relative construction | whichever fruit each had been proceeding toward |
| **śraddhā-adhimukta-dṛṣṭi-āptau** | masculine nominative dual coordinate compound | the Faith-Resolved and the one Attained through View |
| **mṛdu-tīkṣṇa-indriyau** | masculine nominative dual compound | the practitioners of gentle and sharp faculties, respectively |
| **tadā** | indeclinable | then, at the sixteenth moment |

The kārikā's compact **dṛṣṭyāpta** is explained in the Bhāṣya through the standard designation **dṛṣṭiprāpta**, “one who has attained through seeing or view.” **śraddhādhimukta** is retained in Sanskrit because no single English rendering captures its technical range without possible overstatement.

## 4. Grammar

The locative **ṣoḍaśe** supplies the decisive temporal-path condition: “at the sixteenth [moment].” The particle **tu** contrasts this moment with the first fifteen moments during which the two persons were still *pratipannakas*.

**tau phalasthau** is the main nominal statement: “those two are established in fruit.” The distributive expression **yatra yaḥ pratipannakaḥ** preserves the distinct trajectory of each practitioner: each becomes established in whichever fruit that practitioner had been approaching.

The second line renames the same two persons. The order of **mṛdutīkṣṇendriyau** distributes the two predicates respectively:

```text
mṛdu-indriya   → śraddhādhimukta
tīkṣṇa-indriya → dṛṣṭiprāpta
```

**tadā** links the new names to the same sixteenth-moment transition. The faculty remains; path-position and technical designation change.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

But at the sixteenth [moment], those two are established in the fruit toward which each had been proceeding. Then the practitioners of gentle and sharp faculties are respectively the Faith-Resolved and the one Attained through View.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

When the sixteenth cognitive moment arises, the Faith-Follower and Dharma-Follower cease to be candidates and become established in the particular fruit each had approached—stream-entry, once-return, or non-return. The gentle-faculty practitioner is then called *śraddhādhimukta*, Faith-Resolved, while the sharp-faculty practitioner is called *dṛṣṭiprāpta*, one who has attained through Seeing.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

At the sixteenth moment, the directed course becomes an attained position. The fruit that governed the preceding movement as its End is now actual in the practitioner. Yet this actualization preserves the determinations through which it arose: faculty, prior abandonment, and prospective fruit are not erased.

The same continuum therefore receives a new name according to its accomplished status. Following has become attainment. But the attained End is not absolute completion: because cultivation-removable afflictions remain, fruit-establishment becomes the new Beginning from which the Path of Cultivation proceeds.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**pratipannaka**

A **pratipannaka** is one who has entered upon and proceeds toward a fruit. During the fifteen moments of the Path of Seeing, the fruit is the determinate End being approached, not yet the position occupied.

**phalastha**

A **phalastha** is one established in a Noble fruit. At the sixteenth moment, candidacy ceases and each practitioner stands in the fruit already determined by the prior abandonment-state:

```text
0–5 grades previously abandoned → established in stream-entry
6–8 grades previously abandoned → established in once-return
9+ grades previously abandoned  → established in non-return
```

The sixteenth moment does not select a new result arbitrarily. It actualizes the trajectory determined in 6.29–6.30.

**śraddhādhimukta**

The gentle-faculty practitioner formerly called **śraddhānusārin** is now **śraddhādhimukta**. “Faith-Resolved” preserves the predominance of faith and decisive inclination without implying that every work of liberation has already been completed.

The Bhāṣya relates the name to the predominance of **śraddhā** and **adhimokṣa**, confidence and decisive resolve:

```text
gentle faculty
    → faith predominates
    → decisive resolve predominates
    → śraddhādhimukta
```

**dṛṣṭiprāpta**

The sharp-faculty practitioner formerly called **dharmānusārin** is now **dṛṣṭiprāpta**, one who has attained through Seeing or View. The Bhāṣya relates this designation to the predominance of Prajñā and *dṛṣṭi*.

```text
sharp faculty
    → Prajñā predominates
    → Seeing/View predominates
    → dṛṣṭiprāpta
```

The pair is not a contrast between irrational devotion and rational knowledge. It is a technical differentiation by predominating faculties within the attained Noble continuum.

**Why immediate arhatship is excluded**

The Bhāṣya states that arhatship cannot be attained immediately through this first completion of Seeing. It gives two reasons:

1. the Path of Seeing does not abandon the afflictions removable through cultivation;
2. prior mundane detachment cannot extend through **bhavāgra**, the summit of conditioned existence.

Seeing has a determinate scope: it abandons seeing-removable afflictions. Even extensive prior mundane detachment has a determinate upper limit. No admissible starting-state therefore makes immediate arhatship the fruit of this sixteenth moment.

**Path-functional names**

The same continuum passes through two designations:

```text
during first Seeing:
    Faith-Follower / Dharma-Follower

when established in fruit:
    Faith-Resolved / Attained through View
```

The names are not personal essences. They encode faculty, causal history, current path-stage, and attained result.

## 8. Logical Determination

The sixteenth moment performs two coordinated transitions:

```text
Path transition:
    Path of Seeing → Path of Cultivation

Person transition:
    fruit-candidate → fruit-established
```

The result preserves its genesis:

```text
faculty
+ prior abandonment-state
+ fifteen-moment Seeing
+ sixteenth liberation-path moment
= establishment in the corresponding fruit
```

The renaming function is state-dependent:

```text
FaithFollower + fruit establishment
    → FaithResolved

DharmaFollower + fruit establishment
    → AttainedThroughView
```

In Organon terms, explicitly as project synthesis, this is the conversion of **End-in-view** into **End-attained**:

```text
pratipannaka → movement determined by its End
phalastha    → the End actualized as present position
```

Because that fruit remains short of arhatship, the result immediately functions as a new Beginning. The completed course preserves itself by changing into the movement appropriate to what has now been attained.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 6.28 showed that Seeing ends when it has made the unseen Truth seen. VAK 6.31 shows the same completion from the side of the practitioner. One no longer proceeds toward the fruit; one stands in it.

This confirms that the End was real throughout the course. During the fifteen moments it selected and organized the movement as fruit-candidacy. At the sixteenth it becomes actuality and changes the practitioner's formal status. The End is not merely a subjective picture of a possible future; it is the rule according to which the course produces its result.

Yet the new position contains a further task. Seeing cannot do the work proper to Cultivation, and mundane preparation cannot already remove the summit of existence. The attained fruit therefore becomes a determinate Beginning for the next Path-course.

The kārikā states fruit-establishment at the sixteenth moment, preservation of the previously approached fruit, and the two new faculty-based names. The three possible fruits, exclusion of immediate arhatship, its two causal reasons, and the predominance of faith/resolve or Prajñā/view are Bhāṣya determinations. The End-in-view / End-attained / new-Beginning structure is Organon synthesis.
