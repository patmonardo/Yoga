# VAK_6.58 Bhāṣya — What Can Fall; What Seeing Holds

The Arhat Talk. Voices stay distinct. Follow the argument through.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> gotrāc caturṇāṃ pañcānāṃ phalād dhānir na pūrvakāt |
> śaikṣānāryāś ca ṣaḍgotrāḥ saṃcāro nāsti darśane || 6.58 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> For four there is loss from disposition, and for five loss from fruit, but not from
> what was first. Trainees and non-noble persons also have six dispositions. There
> is no transition during Seeing.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> From what do they fall — fruit or disposition? Four, from *cetanādharman* on: from
> an acquired *gotra*. The lowest type does not fall from her own type; there is
> nowhere lower. Five, from the first type on: from the fruit — that is the
> Vaibhāṣika count. But not from what was first. Whatever *gotra* was first, she
> does not fall from that: trainee and beyond-training Paths have made it firm.
> What she got later by sharpening faculties, from that she can fall. Whatever fruit
> was first, she does not fall from that. Hence: no fall from stream-entry.
>
> Why no fall from the first cuts? What Seeing abandons is *avastuka* in the school's
> sense. They run on a self-seat; they are rooted in the view of a person. That self
> is not there. So are they objectless? No. They take a real object — the aggregates
> — but they take it falsely: they overlay agent, feeler, controller, self onto form
> and the rest. Cultivation-afflictions — passion, hate, pride, ignorance — run on
> the thing as agreeable or not. Those have a basis in that contrast. A lapse of
> mindfulness can raise a cultivation-affliction, as a snake-notion on a rope. The
> self-view does not return that way after Seeing has cut it. So: no fall from what
> Seeing abandoned.
>
> Sautrāntikas: no fall from Arhatship either. Scripture: what noble wisdom has
> abandoned is abandoned. Vigilance-talk is for the trainee, not for the Arhat. Gain
> and honour may shake pleasant abiding in this life; from unshakeable liberation of
> mind the Blessed One declares no fall in any way. If someone says condition-held
> liberation still falls — examine what that name names. Is it Arhatship, or the
> dhyānas? A fundamental concentration, present when the occasion is present, is
> called condition-held liberation; sought again for pleasant abiding now. Arhatship
> itself stays with her; it is not that kind of hold.
>
> The dilemma: if the antidote that makes afflictions incapable of arising has arisen,
> how does she fall from Arhatship? If it has not arisen, how is she one whose
> influxes are exhausted? Vaibhāṣikas still teach fall from Arhatship.
>
> The six *gotras* already run in trainees and ordinary persons. Arhat types are
> those earlier types completed. Faculty-shift does not happen inside Seeing:
> the preparatory work it needs has no room there.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
fall from acquired gotra ≠ fall from first gotra
fall from later fruit ≠ fall from stream-entry
Seeing-cut does not return by inattention
Vaibhāṣika: five may lose Arhat-fruit
Sautrāntika: Arhatship holds; dhyāna-ease can shake
no indriya-shift inside Seeing
```

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *na pūrvakāt* = first *gotra* and first fruit;
- *avastuka* ≠ no object; it means false overlay on a real thing;
- *phalād dhāniḥ* is Vaibhāṣika on the page; Sautrāntika refuses it.
