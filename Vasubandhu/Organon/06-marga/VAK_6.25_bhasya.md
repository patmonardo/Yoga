# VAK_6.25 Bhāṣya — Uncontaminated Dharma-Acceptance

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> śrutacintāmayaṃ trīṇi karmāṇy ākṣipyate nṛṣu |
> laukikebhyo 'gradharmebhyo dharmakṣāntir anāsravā || 6.25 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> [The liberation-conducive root] is formed through hearing and reflection; it
> is the three actions and is projected among human beings. From the Highest
> Mundane Dharmas [arises] uncontaminated Dharma-Acceptance.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.25.md`](./VAK_6.25.md). Next: 6.26, dharmajñāna then anvaya.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Digression on the liberation-root closed. Sequence of direct realization resumes.
> Immediately after the Highest Mundane Dharmas, uncontaminated Dharma-Knowledge-
> Acceptance arises. Object? Desire-domain suffering. Hence: Dharma-Knowledge-
> Acceptance regarding suffering. Named toward the uncontaminated knowledge that
> follows; Acceptance toward that knowledge — flower and fruit on a tree.
>
> This same is called entry into the fixed order (`niyāmāvakrānti`): entering the
> settled determination of rightness. Rightness, in the sūtra, is Nirvāṇa.
> `Niyāma` is becoming one-sided, settled. Entering that is `avakramaṇa`. When this
> has arisen, one is called a Noble person. Future Acceptance is said to turn away
> ordinary-person status — that alone is its work while still future; like a lamp
> being born. Others: by the Highest Mundane Dharmas — no fault, they oppose that
> status even while of that level, like mounting an enemy’s shoulders to strike.
> Others: by both, from likeness to uninterrupted and liberation paths.
