# VAK_6.69 Bhāṣya — Take the Principal; Keep the Whole

6.68 counted. 6.69 says how the count is legal.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vīryaṃ samyakprahāṇākhyam ṛddhipādāḥ samādhayaḥ |
> pradhānagrahaṇaṃ sarve guṇāḥ prāyogikās tu te || 6.69 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> Energy is what is called the right abandonments, and the bases of spiritual power
> are samādhis. This is taking the principal factor; but all the qualities belonging
> to application are in them.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.69.md`](./VAK_6.69.md). Next: 6.70.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> What was said — those groups have the nature of prajñā, energy, collectedness —
> is said by taking the principal. But the establishments, right abandonments, and
> bases of power hold all the applied qualities.
>
> Why is energy the right abandonment? Through it body, speech, and mind are
> rightly exerted. Why is collectedness a base of power? Attainment of all qualities
> stands on it.
>
> Those who say collectedness itself is the power, and desire and the rest are its
> feet: for them the *dravyas* become thirteen, and the sūtra is opposed.
>
> Why are the faculties themselves called powers? Mild and intense: can be crushed,
> cannot be crushed. Order of the five: one who trusts starts energy for the fruit;
> energy started, recollection stands; recollection standing, the mind collects;
> collected, she knows as it is.
