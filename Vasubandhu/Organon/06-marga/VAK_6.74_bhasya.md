# VAK_6.74 Bhāṣya — Four Confidences; Two Operations; Knowledge First

Seeing first. Confidence after.

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> lābho mārgābhisamaye buddha-tat-saṃghayor api |
> dharmaḥ satya-trayaṃ bodhisattva-pratyekabuddhayoḥ || 6.74 ||

Literal (from the kārikā study):

> At direct realization of the Path there is also acquisition [of verified
> confidence] concerning the Buddha and his Saṃgha. Dharma is the triad of
> Truths—[and, according to the Bhāṣya, the Path also]. The Bodhisattva–Pratyekabuddha
> dual remains compressed in the transmitted verse.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_6.74.md`](./VAK_6.74.md). Next: 6.75.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit Witness

> dharmaḥ satyatrayam / mārgaś ca / caturṇām api satyānām abhisamayād dharmāvetyaprasādaprāptiḥ /
> nāmatas catvāra āvetyaprasādāḥ / śraddhādhiṣṭhānabhedāt /
> dravyatas tu dvau / śraddhā śīlaṃ ca /
> nirmalāḥ /
> yathābhūtasatyāny avabudhya saṃpratyayo 'vetyaprasādaḥ /

**Witness.** Pradhan 386|18–387|14. Voice: questioner + Vasubandhu. Dual compound not unpacked here.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> What Dharma? The three Truths — and the Path. So by seeing all four Truths,
> Dharma-confidence is gained. Four verified confidences by name, because the
> objects differ. By *dravya*, two: confidence and discipline. Buddha, Dharma,
> Saṃgha: *śraddhā*. Discipline dear to the noble ones: *śīla*. Are they with
> outflows or without? They are clean.
>
> What is verified confidence? Having understood the Truths as they are, settled
> assurance is *avetyaprasāda*.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
four objects ← two operations
Dharma = four Truths, not three only
know first → settle after
dual left unmarked
```

## 6. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- *api* carries Dharma and *śīla* into Path-seeing;
- *nirmala* = noble-only;
- do not invent a Bodhisattva curriculum from the leftover dual.
