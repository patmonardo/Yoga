# VAK_7.34 Bhāṣya — Buddha-Equality through Cause, Fruit, and Beneficence

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> saṃbhāradharmakāyābhyāṃ jagataś cārthacaryayā |
> samatā sarvabuddhānāṃ nāyurjātipramāṇataḥ || 7.34 ||

> All Buddhas are equal through accumulation and Dharma-body, and through
> activity for the welfare of the world; not with respect to lifespan, birth,
> or bodily measure.

Question: alike in every respect? No. Closes the eighteen. Shared qualities
with disciples begin at 7.35.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Are all Buddhas common in every mode? He says: no.
>
> Equality of all Buddhas is by three reasons: from fulfillment of the whole
> accumulation of merit and knowledge; from perfection of the Dharma-body;
> and by activity for the world's welfare. Difference is made by lifespan,
> birth, clan, and measure: longer or shorter life; kṣatriya or brāhmaṇa
> birth; clans such as Kāśyapa or Gautama; smaller or larger measure —
> according to the time.
>
> By attending to this threefold excellence the wise can produce intense love
> and reverence toward the Blessed Buddhas: excellence of cause, of fruit,
> and of beneficence.
>
> These, then, are called the eighteen distinctive qualities of the Buddhas.

## 5. Respect-Relative Equality

Not one numerically identical person. Not identical biographies. Bodily
*excellence* (marks, strength, adamantine bones) ≠ uniform *size*.
`Dharmakāya` here = perfected Dharma-constitution — no later trikāya imported.

Cause four: all accumulations practiced; long; uninterrupted; respectful.
Fruit four: knowledge, abandonment, effective power, material body.
Knowledge four: uninstructed; everywhere; in every respect; effortless —
effortless now does not cancel long preparation.
Abandonment four: all kleśas; definitive; with vāsanā; obstacles to samādhi
and attainment.
Beneficence: release from three bad destinies and saṃsāra-suffering / or
establishment in three vehicles and good destiny — compressed; destinies and
vehicles not listed in the sentence.

Confidence overcoming evil specifies *unfixed* maturation. Do not drop that.
Damaged prabhāva compounds: heads named, procedures not reconstructed.

## 6. The Praise Is Argument

Reflection on the three excellences → love and reverence. Buddhas as
unsurpassed puṇyakṣetra. Attributed doctrine, not a prediction printed as fact
about a present reader's karma.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- dual + third instrumental = three grounds;
- `jagataḥ` with arthacaryā;
- `na` denies equality only under āyus-jāti-pramāṇa;
- gotra added in prose.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Idea holds Concept: same completed jñāna-constitution, different Qualitative
Concepts of life-span, clan, measure. Formation → fruit → welfare is the
science, not two dialectics. Appearance would say “all Buddhas identical” or
“therefore none equal.” Singular remains Buddha Mind, not a merged biography.
