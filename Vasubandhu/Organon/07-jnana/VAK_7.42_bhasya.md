# VAK_7.42 Bhāṣya — Six Higher Knowledges as Direct Cognitive Realizations

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ṛddhiśrotramanaḥpūrvajanmacyutyudayakṣaye |
> jñātasākṣīkriyābhijñāḥ ṣaḍvidhā muktimārgadhīḥ || 7.42 ||

> The higher knowledges are sixfold direct cognitive realizations concerning
> extraordinary efficacy, hearing, mind, former birth, death and arising, and
> exhaustion. They are cognitions of the liberation path.

`Jñātasākṣīkriyā` compressed; prose `jñānasākṣātkriyā`. Category and level:
7.43.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.42.md`](./VAK_7.42.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> These, then, are qualities shared with śrāvakas — the higher knowledges and
> the rest — also with ordinary persons.
>
> What is this called abhijñā? Direct cognitive realization in the field of
> extraordinary efficacy is an abhijñā. Likewise the direct realizations of
> divine hearing, another's mental course, recollection of former abodes,
> death and arising, and influx-exhaustion. These are the six.
>
> Five of them are shared with ordinary persons. All of them have the nature
> of liberation-path discriminative knowing — like the fruits of the ascetic path.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
shared class opened (even pṛthagjana for five)
    six fields named
    definition: jñāna-sākṣātkriyā
    five shared; sixth not said shared here
    nature: vimuktimārga-prajñā
```

## 5. Access ≠ Liberation

Shared kind ≠ every ordinary person has them. Liberation-path nature ≠ the
bearer is liberated. Worldly paths also have path-functions relative to what
they accomplish. Direct ≠ causeless, simultaneous, or report-free in every
case — it marks making present vs mere report. Analogy to śrāmaṇya-phala does
not add the four fruits to the six.

No progressive ladder or “sixth governs the five” in this unit.

## 6. The Six

| | field | pṛthagjana here |
|---|---|---|
| ṛiddhi | extraordinary efficacy | yes |
| divyaśrotra | hearing | yes |
| cetaḥparyāya | another's mental course | yes |
| pūrvanivāsa | former abodes | yes |
| cyutyutpāda | death and reappearance | yes |
| āsravakṣaya | influx-exhaustion | no |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first compound = six fields, not one object;
- `jñātasākṣīkriyā` = sākṣātkriyā;
- `muktimārgadhīḥ` = vimuktimārga-prajñā, not a seventh abhijñā.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Vidyā: six fields made present. Technē: path-function and cultivation still
to be specified. Greater reach is a Qualitative Concept; it stays inside the
Idea and does not become wisdom. Appearance would sell spectacle as liberation.

without closing it.
