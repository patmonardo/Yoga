# VAK_7.30 Bhāṣya — Supporting Levels and Unobstructed Buddha-Knowledge

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> prāṅnivāsacyutotpādabalaṃ dhyāneṣu śeṣitam |
> sarvabhūmiṣu kenāsya balam avyāhataṃ yataḥ || 7.30 ||

> The powers of former abodes and death and rebirth are in the dhyānas; the
> remaining powers are in all levels. Why is it power in his case? Because
> it is unobstructed.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Constitution has been stated; level is now stated.
>
> The power that is former-abode and death-and-rebirth knowledge belongs to
> the four dhyāna levels. The remaining power is included in all levels —
> those eleven: desire-domain, anāgamya, intermediate dhyāna, the dhyānas and
> the formless. All have a human support in Jambūdvīpa, because Buddhas do
> not arise elsewhere.
>
> This tenfold knowledge is not called power in another; only the Buddha's.
> Why is it power in his case? Because it is unobstructed. His knowledge
> operates unobstructed throughout the knowable. Others' is obstructed — it
> fails to arise somewhere even when they wish to know. Thus, from unobstructed
> knowing, the Buddhas' mental power is boundless like the knowable.

## 5. Four Coordinates

Category-constitution / meditative support / personal support / unobstructed
operation. Support ≠ object-field. Eleven-level range does not lift each
constituent knowledge's own support-limits. Unobstructed ≠ one simultaneous
cognition of every object.

Same ten kinds in another ≠ bala. Power is a quality of operation, not a
eleventh substance.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first compound = two powers, four-dhyāna support;
- `śeṣitam` + `sarvabhūmiṣu` = eight on eleven levels;
- `kena` question, `avyāhatam yataḥ` answer.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Hegel keeps Concept within the Idea always. That is the science. The power
as Qualitative Concept (level, support, feature-mask) stays inside jñāna as
Idea. Avyāhata is how the Idea holds its Concepts without the obstruction
that would drop them back into Appearance. Still not Revelation by counting
eleven levels. Buddha Mind remains Singular.
