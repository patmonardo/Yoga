# VAK_7.37 Bhāṣya — Resolve-Knowledge and the Four Discriminating Knowledges

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tathaiva praṇidhijñānaṃ sarvālambaṃ tu tat tathā |
> dharmārthayor niruktau ca pratibhāne ca saṃvidaḥ || 7.37 ||

> Knowledge through resolve likewise has conventional Knowledge as its
> constitution, belongs to the fourth dhyāna and an immovable arhat, and
> has a human Basis. Unlike araṇā, it has all dharmas as Support. The four
> discriminating knowledges likewise share the immovable-arhat and human-
> Basis conditions; they concern dharma and meaning, linguistic expression,
> and ready articulation.

Two “likewise” — different inheritances. Definitions of the four: 7.38.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> “Just as araṇā is described, so too is Knowledge through resolve.” For
> this too is conventional Knowledge, in the final dhyāna, of an immovable
> one, and has a human Basis. “But it takes all as Support.” Knowledge
> through resolve has all dharmas as Support: that alone is the difference.
> Yet the formless attainments are not known directly by Knowledge through
> resolve. How, then? Through a distinction of completed conduct [the local
> compound is compressed]. And the farmer illustration belongs here—so say
> the Vaibhāṣikas.
>
> Knowledge preceded by resolve is Knowledge through resolve. For, having
> resolved, one enters the fourth dhyāna developed to its culminating limit:
> “May I know this”—and knows it as it is. “All [this] is the Condition of
> that concentration” [the local sentence is compressed].
>
> “Likewise, there are discriminating knowledges concerning dharma and
> meaning, linguistic expression, and ready articulation.” For there are
> four discriminating knowledges: dharma-discrimination, meaning-discrimination,
> linguistic-expression-discrimination, and ready-articulation-discrimination.
> These too are like araṇā. In what respect are they alike? In belonging to
> an immovable arhat and having a human Basis. Their differences of Support,
> meditative level, and constitution are stated separately.

## 5. Range Is Not Simultaneous Omniscience

“All” = eligible field. “This” = directed exercise. Not every object present
at once. Not uniform access. Wish alone ≠ the attainment. Farmer story not
invented here.
