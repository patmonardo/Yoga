# VAK_7.15 Bhāṣya — Meditative Level and Basis of Actualization

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dhyāneṣv anyamanojñānaṃ kāmarūpāśrayaṃ ca tat |
> kāmāśrayaṃ tu dharmākhyam anyat traidhātukāśrayam || 7.15 ||

> Knowledge of another's Consciousness belongs to the four dhyānas alone
> and is made present with a desire- or form-domain Basis. Dharma-knowledge
> is made present only with a desire-domain Basis; the other eight knowledge
> designations have possible Bases across the three domains.

This finishes 7.14's level account and adds `āśraya`. The Bhāṣya closes:
`kṛto bhūmyaśrayanirdeśaḥ` — level and Basis are distinct and now stated.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.15.md`](./VAK_7.15.md).

## 3. Continuous Translation

> “Knowledge of another's Consciousness [occurs] in the dhyānas”:
> Knowledge of another's Consciousness is in the four dhyānas only,
> nowhere else.
>
> “And it has a desire- or form-domain Basis”: that Knowledge of another's
> Consciousness is made present in the desire and form domains.
>
> “Dharma-knowledge, however, has a desire-domain Basis”: it has only a
> desire-domain Basis [the local verse reads `dharmāruyam`]. It is not
> made present in the form or formless domains.
>
> “The rest has a Basis across the three domains.” What is “the rest”?
> [The other eight knowledge designations,] excluding Knowledge of
> another's Consciousness and Dharma-knowledge [local compressed gloss
> `paracittajñānaṃ dharmajñānanirmuktam`]. The determination of levels
> and Bases is complete.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
paracitta level: four dhyānas only (not anāgamya, not dhyānāntara)
paracitta āśraya: kāma or rūpa — where *it* is made present
dharma-knowledge āśraya: kāma only
remainder āśraya: three domains, exclusions kept
```

## 5. Four Relations That Must Not Collapse

```text
bhūmi                 meditative level          (7.14–7.15)
āśraya                support of actualization  (this verse)
ālambana              object-support            (7.03, 7.11)
pratipakṣa            counteractive range       (7.09)
```

`Kāmarūpāśrayam` is not the minds known. An object-reading is
refused. `Saṃmukhīkriyate` = this knowledge is brought into presence.
Uncontaminated minds can still be objects (7.11) while support is only
kāma/rūpa.

Dharma-knowledge: six levels (7.14) and kāma-support only. No contradiction.
Three-domain counteraction under 7.09 conditions does not give three-domain
actualization.

`Āśraya` in the formless case is not a material body. Three-domain support
is a range, not three supports at once.

A truth-knowledge included under dharma-knowledge keeps desire-support.
The remainder's wide range does not override the specific rule.

## 6. Completing 7.14

Paracitta was the missing level. Four dhyānas only — narrower than
dharma-knowledge's six. Then a new axis: support. The source itself marks
the pair complete.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `dhyāneṣu` = four only, `nānyatra`;
- `tat` resumes paracitta for the support predicate;
- `dharmākhyam` + `eva` on kāma-support;
- `anyat` = remainder after those two exclusions.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Kośa Technē keeps three existential domains: kāma, rūpa, ārūpya. Our Vidyā
four-fold — dharma, ārūpya, rūpa, kāma — is not a fourth realm inserted into
this verse. The dyad we care about is dharma:kāma as visible enclosure.
The verse's own fact is the hook: `dharmajñāna` is actualized with kāma-support
only. Dharma does kāma — this realm is holding-to-be-true through and through —
as overlay, not as a gloss of `kāmāśrayam`.

Rūpa and ārūpya remain the stack Technē must still specify. They are not the
dyad. Paramārtha does kāmadhātu: the first uncontaminated knowledge-kind is
made present *here*. Flight to ūrdhva is not how dharma-knowledge occurs.

Appearance would mix support with object and call dharma-knowledge a higher-
realm faculty. The Idea keeps `āśraya` as where the holding is brought to
presence.
