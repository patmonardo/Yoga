# VAK_7.10 Bhāṣya — The Distribution of Knowledge-Aspects

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dharmajñānānvayajñānaṃ ṣoḍaśākāram anyathā |
> tathā ca sāṃvṛtaṃ svaiḥ svaiḥ satyākāraiś catuṣṭayam || 7.10 ||

> Dharma- and anvaya-knowledge each range over all sixteen truth-aspects.
> Conventional knowledge can take these same aspects and others besides.
> The four truth-knowledges—suffering, origination, cessation, and Path—each
> operate through the four aspects proper to its own truth.

The Bhāṣya postpones the sixteen names: `tān purastād upadekṣyāmaḥ`.
This unit distributes ranges only.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> Of these ten knowledges:
>
> Dharma-knowledge and anvaya-knowledge are sixteen-aspected. We shall
> teach those [aspects] later.
>
> Otherwise, and thus also, conventional [knowledge]: conventional knowledge
> is sixteen-aspected and otherwise-aspected, because it apprehends the own
> and common characteristics of all dharmas, and so forth.
>
> The group of four [operates] through their own respective truth-aspects.
> Suffering-, origination-, cessation-, and Path-knowledge proceed through
> their own respective truth-aspects; thus each is four-aspected.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
7.08's ākāra-ground now as ranges
    → dharma and anvaya: each the full sixteen
    → conventional: those sixteen AND other aspects
         ground: sva- / sāmānya-lakṣaṇa of dharmas, etc.
    → four truth-knowledges: four aspects each, own truth only
```

`Anyathā` construes with conventional knowledge, not as a restriction on
dharma/anvaya.

## 5. Three Range-Types

| Kind | Aspect-range |
|---|---|
| Dharma-knowledge | sixteen truth-aspects |
| Anvaya-knowledge | sixteen truth-aspects |
| Conventional knowledge | sixteen + other |
| Each truth-knowledge | the four of its own truth |

Shared sixteen does not identify dharma-knowledge with anvaya-knowledge.
Using a truth-aspect does not make conventional knowledge uncontaminated.
Kind-range does not mean one event takes all sixteen at once.

A cognition that is both dharma-knowledge and suffering-knowledge is held
to the four suffering-aspects by the more specific classification. The
broader dharma-range does not cancel that.

`Catuṣṭaya` here is the four truth-knowledges, not 7.05's four overlapping
categories of paracitta.

`Svalakṣaṇa` / `sāmānyalakṣaṇa` explain conventional *breadth*. They are
not a second closed list of aspects partitioned off from the sixteen.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- singular compound distributes sixteen to *each* of the two;
- `anyathā tathā ca` = sixteen and otherwise, of conventional knowledge;
- `pravartante` / `ekaikaṃ caturākāram` for the four;
- names of the sixteen deferred.

No change to the Organon literal line.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Yoga Vidyā first: Philosophical Cognition sees both sides and generates
the Principle. The sixteen are the Principle's aspects — one field, visible
on both sides. That is why dharma-knowledge and anvaya-knowledge share the
sixteen without becoming one knowledge. Inference would start after the
Principle. Here the aspect-field *is* the Principle as knowable.

Yoga Technē second: Kośa assigns who may use which slice. Conventional
knowledge can use the sixteen and still be conventional — flow around artha
under own- and common-characteristics as well. Each truth-knowledge is
bound to its four. Shared form, different status: 7.02's nature-cut and
7.09's efficacy-cut are not erased by aspect.

Sublation = nirodha stays parked. Cessation-knowledge has four aspects;
that is Technē distribution, not Aufhebung.

Appearance would dump the sixteen names into this verse. The Idea waits
where Vasubandhu waits.
