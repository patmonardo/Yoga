# VAK_7.14 Bhāṣya — Ethical Character and Levels of Knowledge

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tridhādyaṃ kuśalāny anyāni ādyaṃ sarvāsu bhūmiṣu |
> dharmākhyaṃ ṣaṭsu navasu tv anvayākhyaṃ tathaiva ṣaṭ || 7.14 ||

> Conventional knowledge may be wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate
> and occurs at every level; the other nine knowledges are wholesome.
> Dharma-knowledge belongs to six levels—the four dhyānas, preliminary
> concentration, and intermediate dhyāna—while anvaya-knowledge belongs
> to those six plus the first three formless levels. The remaining six
> knowledges span nine levels collectively, but six when included under
> dharma-knowledge and nine under anvaya-knowledge.

`Ādyam` = conventional knowledge by verse-placement (`ślokādau`), not by
every prose list order. Paracitta's level waits for 7.15; its wholesomeness
is already among the nine.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> Next we shall examine the difference among these knowledges as wholesome
> and so forth.
>
> “The first is threefold; the others are wholesome.” Conventional
> knowledge is “the first” because it stands at the start of the verse
> [local `bhavatvād`]. It is threefold: wholesome, unwholesome, and
> indeterminate. The other nine knowledges are wholesome only.
>
> “The first is in all levels”: from the desire domain [local
> `kāmadhātī`] up to the summit of existence.
>
> “That called Dharma is in six”: Dharma-knowledge in the four dhyānas,
> preliminary concentration, and intermediate dhyāna.
>
> “That called anvaya, however, is in nine”: anvaya-knowledge in those
> same six levels and the first three formless levels.
>
> “Likewise the six”: suffering-, origination-, cessation-, Path-,
> exhaustion-, and non-arising-knowledge are in those same nine levels
> when considered without differentiation. With differentiation, those
> included in Dharma-knowledge are in six; those included in
> anvaya-knowledge are in nine [local `punr`].

## 5. Two Axes, Not One

Wholesome ≠ uncontaminated. The nine include paracitta; 7.11 already had
its contaminated form. Do not translate `kuśala` as “pure” here.

`Bhūmi` = level of occurrence or affiliation, distinct from the
Condition-range of 7.03, the counteractive reach of 7.09, and the
embodied Basis discussed in 7.15.

```text
SixLevels  = four dhyānas + anāgamya + dhyānāntara
NineLevels = SixLevels + first three formless
```

Bhavāgra is in conventional knowledge's range. It is not a tenth level for
anvaya-knowledge. The summit Bases taken as Support at first arising in
7.05 are a different relation from this level-range.

Kind-range ≠ one act in every listed level. Threefold ethics × all levels
does not license every pair.

## 6. Subsumption

`Abhedena` / `bhedena` are how the six names are considered, not two rival
facts about one fully specified case. A dharma-subsumed suffering-knowledge
does not inherit the extra formless levels.

Overlaps stay: wholesome conventional knowledge can overlap another wholesome
name. Exclusive wholesomeness of the nine does not expel them from the
wholesome portion of conventional knowledge.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `ādyam` = saṃvṛtijñāna by verse-place;
- `tridhā` = kuśala / akuśala / avyākṛta;
- `eva` on the nine;
- final `ṣaṭ` = four truths + two terminal names;
- six/nine of those six governed by saṃgraha.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Paramārtha does kāmadhātu: conventional knowledge *occurs* from kāma through
bhavāgra, and the Idea's work is not a ticket out of that range. Rated XYZ
only as joke-label. The verse is Technē: ethics and level of the holding.

Vidyā first still: anvaya as Philosophical Cognition (both sides, Principle)
is not the same fact as “nine levels.” Nine levels are where the Technē-form
can sit. Dharma-knowledge staying in six is not a failure to see the higher
side; it is Path-affiliation.

Appearance would say the higher knowledges live upstairs and conventional
lives downstairs. The text puts conventional through the whole stack and
still calls it aparamārtha by nature (7.08). That tension is the entertainment.
