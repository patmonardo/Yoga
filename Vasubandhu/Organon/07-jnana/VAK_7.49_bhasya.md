# VAK_7.49 Bhāṣya — Constructed Manifestations and Their Producing Consciousnesses

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāmāptaṃ nirmitaṃ bāhyaṃ caturāyatanaṃ dvidhā |
> rūpāptaṃ dve tu nirmāṇacittaistāni caturdaśa || 7.49 ||

> A desire-domain construction is external and consists of four Essences; it is
> twofold. A form-domain construction consists of two [Essences]. [The
> constructions are produced] by construction-Consciousnesses; those

## 3. Continuous Translation

> To begin with, a desire-domain constructed manifestation is external and
> consists of four Essences. Desire-domain construction has the nature of the
> visible Form, taste, smell, and tangible Essences. It is twofold: connected
> with one's own body or with another's body. A form-domain construction,
> however, has two Essences: visible Form and the tangible, because smell and
> taste are absent there. It too is twofold in the same way. Thus construction
> has four forms in the desire realm and likewise in the form realm, eight
> in summary.
>
> How can one born in the form realm fail to become endowed with smell and
> taste when making a desire-domain construction? As with clothes and
> ornaments, there is no such endowment. Others say [in the form realm one
> produces only two Essences?]; the local Sanskrit of this alternative is
> damaged.
>
> Is the construction produced by the higher knowledge itself? No, it is
> said. By what then? By its results. “By construction-Consciousnesses;
> those [Consciousnesses] are fourteen.”

## 5. Product ≠ Producer

Sound not in the four. No state/event theory offered here for that omission.
`Dvidhā` is own-body / other-body — not real vs illusion. Producer-realm ≠
product-domain. Fourteen counts construction-Consciousnesses, not objects or
simultaneous acts.
Clothes-ornaments is technical non-endowment, not ordinary speech about
"having" a garment.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `kāmāpta` / `rūpāpta` = product domain;
- `dve` = two āyatanas, not a second `dvidhā`;
- `tāni` = the minds.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Capacity is not the immediate operator. Resulting construction-Consciousnesses are.
Concept of the product stays inside the Idea of the capacity; properties do
not leak backward onto the producer. Appearance would say the abhijñā itself
mints bodies. Technē waits for 7.50's lawful downward reach.
