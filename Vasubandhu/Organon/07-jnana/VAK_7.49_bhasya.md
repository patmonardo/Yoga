# VAK_7.49 Bhāṣya — Constructed Manifestations and Their Producing Minds

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāmāptaṃ nirmitaṃ bāhyaṃ caturāyatanaṃ dvidhā |
> rūpāptaṃ dve tu nirmāṇacittaistāni caturdaśa || 7.49 ||

> A desire-domain constructed manifestation is external, consists of four
> Essences, and is twofold. A form-domain one consists of two. [They are
> produced] by construction-minds; those minds are fourteen.

Dhyāna distribution of the fourteen: 7.50.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.49.md`](./VAK_7.49.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Desire-domain construction has the nature of the visible-form, taste, smell,
> and tangible fields. It is twofold: connected with one's own body or another
> body. Form-domain construction: two fields, visible form and tangible — smell
> and taste are absent there. That too is twofold in the same way. Thus four
> modes in the desire realm and likewise in the form realm — eight in summary.
>
> How does one born in the form realm, in a desire-domain construction, not
> become endowed with smell and taste? As with clothes and ornaments: no such
> endowment.
>
> Is construction produced by the higher knowledge itself? No. By what then?
> By its results. Those construction-minds are fourteen.

## 5. Product ≠ Producer

Sound not in the four. No state/event theory offered here for that omission.
`Dvidhā` is own-body / other-body — not real vs illusion. Producer-realm ≠
product-domain. Fourteen counts minds, not objects, not simultaneous acts.
Clothes-ornaments is technical non-endowment, not ordinary speech about
"having" a garment.

## 6. Constitutions

| product | fields | body |
|---|---|---|
| desire-domain | form, taste, smell, tangible | own or other |
| form-domain | form, tangible | own or other |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `kāmāpta` / `rūpāpta` = product domain;
- `dve` = two āyatanas, not a second `dvidhā`;
- `tāni` = the minds.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Capacity is not the immediate operator. Resulting construction-minds are.
Concept of the product stays inside the Idea of the capacity; properties do
not leak backward onto the producer. Appearance would say the abhijñā itself
mints bodies. Technē waits for 7.50's lawful downward reach.
