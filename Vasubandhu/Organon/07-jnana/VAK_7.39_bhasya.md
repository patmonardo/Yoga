# VAK_7.39 Bhāṣya — Support, Knowledge Constitution, and Level of the Pratisaṃvids

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vāṅmārgālambanā cāsau nava jñānāni sarvabhūḥ |
> daśa ṣaḍvārthasaṃvit sā sarvatrānye tu sāṃvṛtam || 7.39 ||

> The fourth discriminating Knowledge takes speech and the Path as Supports,
> comprises nine Knowledge categories, and occurs on every level.
> Meaning-discrimination comprises ten categories when its field is all
> dharmas, or six when its field is nirvāṇa; it too occurs on every level.
> The other two have conventional Knowledge as their nature.

`Asau` = pratibhāna. Dharma- and nirukti-levels: 7.40.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> “This [fourth discriminating Knowledge] has speech and the Path as
> Supports.” Speech and the Path are its Supports [the local `ālmvanam` is
> damaged]. “Nine Knowledges”: pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid has nine Knowledge
> categories as its constitution, excluding Cessation-Knowledge. “On every
> level”: it occurs from the desire domain through Bhavāgra because it can
> take either speech or the Path as Support.
>
> “Meaning-discrimination has ten, or six.” If all dharmas are meanings,
> artha-pratisaṃvid comprises ten Knowledge categories [the local prose
> reads `arthapratibhānasaṃvit`]. If nirvāṇa is the meaning, it comprises
> six: Dharma-Knowledge, anvaya-Knowledge, Cessation-Knowledge,
> Exhaustion-Knowledge, Non-arising-Knowledge, and conventional Knowledge.
> “It occurs everywhere”: this artha-pratisaṃvid occurs on every level.
>
> “The other two are conventional”: dharma- and nirukti-pratisaṃvid have
> conventional Knowledge as their constitution, because their Supports
> are name-collections and the related forms, and speech, respectively
> [the local `pratisavidau` is damaged].

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
pratibhāna: vāk | mārga as Supports; ten minus nirodha; all levels (either Support)
artha: scope of artha → 10 or 6; all levels
dharma / nirukti: conventional Knowledge (linguistic Supports); levels next
```

## 5. Three Axes

Support ≠ constitution ≠ level. All-level does not put every category on every
level. Speech or Path ≠ speech is the Path. Conventional Knowledge is the right
nature for names and speech, not a demotion.

Six ≠ ten. Two definitions of `artha`, two ranges — same pattern as 7.29.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `asau` = fourth;
- nine by explicit Cessation-Knowledge exclusion;
- `daśa ṣaḍ vā` = two meanings of artha;
- `anye` = dharma and nirukti.
