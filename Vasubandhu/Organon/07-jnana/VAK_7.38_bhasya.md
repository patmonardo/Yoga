# VAK_7.38 Bhāṣya — Discriminating Knowledge of Designation, Meaning, and Expression

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tisro nāmārthavāgjñānam avivartyaṃ yathākramam |
> caturthī yuktamuktābhilāpamārgavaśitvayoḥ || 7.38 ||

> The three are respectively unfailing knowledge of name, meaning, and speech.
> The fourth is [such knowledge] concerning fitting and free expression and
> mastery of the Path.

`Tīsro` / `nāmatha` unrestored. Fourth keeps `avivartyaṃ jñānam` — not
mastery instead of knowledge. Level and category: 7.39.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.38.md`](./VAK_7.38.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> nāmapadavyañjanakāyeṣv arthavākṣu tā avivartyajñānaṃ dharmārthaniruktipratisaṃvido yathākramam /
> avivartyaṃ jñānam iti vartate /
> yuktamuktābhilāpitāyāṃ … cāvivartyaṃ jñānaṃ pratibhānasaṃvit /

**Witness.** Pradhan 418|13–18. Lemma `yuktomukta`, prose `yuktamuta` and
`samādhivaśisaṃprakyāne` unrestored. 418|19–20 (7.39) confirms speech and
Path as the fourth's objects. Voice: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> In the bodies of names, phrases, and verbal units, in meaning, and in speech:
> that unfailing knowledge is dharma-, artha-, and nirukti-pratisaṃvid,
> respectively.
>
> “Unfailing knowledge” continues. Unfailing knowledge concerning fitting and
> free expression and [the compressed samādhi / Path-mastery phrase] is
> pratibhāna-pratisaṃvid.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
three fields in order: nāma-pada-vyañjana / artha / vāk
fourth: yukta-mukta-abhilāpa + mārga-vaśitva
common predicate: avivartya jñāna
category and level deferred
```

## 5. Dharma Here Is Not All Dharmas

First pratisaṃvid's field is the designation-collections. Not every dharma as
object. Name ≠ meaning ≠ speech. Ordered mapping ≠ required temporal pipeline
in every utterance. Fourth ≠ synthesis of the first three (source does not say
that). Fourth ≠ Path itself. Avivartya here ≠ 7.30's unobstructed Buddha-range.

## 6. Four Fields

| | field |
|---|---|
| dharma | nāma / pada / vyañjana-kāya |
| artha | meaning / matter meant |
| nirukti | speech |
| pratibhāna | fitting-free expression and Path-mastery |

`Artha` not locked by English yet. 7.39 will press what is taken as artha.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `yathākramam` assigns the three;
- `nāma` expanded to the three kāyas;
- `iti vartate` carries avivartya onto the fourth;
- dual locative = two fields for the fourth.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Knowledge vs Meaning is now on the table as two *fields*, not two English
synonyms. Jñāna remains Idea; nāma / artha / vāk are Qualitative Concepts
inside it. Pratibhāna is Technē of fitting speech under Path — still knowledge
*concerning* those, not the Path renamed. Appearance would print “eloquence.”
