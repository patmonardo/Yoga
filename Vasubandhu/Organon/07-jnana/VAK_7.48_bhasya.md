# VAK_7.48 Bhāṣya — Ṛiddhi as Samādhi and Three Modes of Movement

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ṛiddhiḥ samādhiḥ gamanaṃ nirmāṇaṃ ca gatistridhā |
> śāsturmanojavā anyeṣāṃ vāhinyapyādhimokṣikī || 7.48 ||

> Ṛiddhi is concentration; movement and construction [are accomplished through
> it]. Movement is threefold. The Teacher has mind-swift movement; others have
> body-conveying and also resolve-based movement.

Vaibhāṣika account. Construction details: 7.49.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.48.md`](./VAK_7.48.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> What is this ṛiddhi? By the Vaibhāṣika method: ṛiddhi is concentration —
> one accomplishes by this. What is accomplished through it? Movement and
> construction.
>
> Movement is threefold: body-conveying, resolve-based, and mind-swift. Mind-
> swift belongs to a Buddha alone, not another: even a very distant place is
> reached in the time of an arising of mind. Hence the Blessed One said the
> Buddha-domain is inconceivable. The other two movements are established for
> the Buddha without being stated.
>
> For śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas: body-conveying — carrying the body in
> sequence like a bird — and resolve-based: swift going by determining the
> distant as near.
>
> Construction is twofold: desire-domain and form-domain.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
means: samādhi
operations: gamana | nirmāṇa
gamana: three modes; manojavā Buddha-only; other two also his
nirmāṇa opened, not analyzed
```

## 5. Means ≠ Operation

Not every samādhi yields these. Not “teleportation” as one blob. No power-field
under the body. Resolve-based ≠ a spacetime theory. Mind-swift ≠ mind travels
while the body stays. Class-admission ≠ every member has it. Fivefold ordinary-
person share from 7.42 is not revoked by this three-class contrast.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- identity = means, not movement-as-samādhi;
- `tridhā` = movement only;
- `anyeṣām` does not strip the Buddha of the first two.
