# VAK_7.48 Bhāṣya — Ṛiddhi as Samādhi and Three Modes of Movement

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ṛddhiḥ samādhiḥ gamanaṃ nirmāṇaṃ ca gatistridhā |
> śāsturmanojavā anyeṣāṃ vāhinyapyādhimokṣikī || 7.48 ||

> According to the Vaibhāṣika account, ṛddhi is samādhi, through which going
> and construction are accomplished. Movement is threefold: the Teacher's is
> swift as mind; others' is body-conveying and resolve-based.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> What is this extraordinary efficacy? According to the Vaibhāṣika account,
> extraordinary efficacy is samādhi. The expression is to be construed as
> “one accomplishes by means of it.” What is accomplished by it? Going and
> construction. Of these, going is threefold: body-conveying, resolve-based,
> and mind-swift.
>
> “The Teacher's is mind-swift.” It is called mind-swift because [its speed
> is] like that of mind (the local speed gloss reads damaged `rava`). This
> movement belongs to the Buddha alone, not to another, since he reaches
> even a very distant place in the time of an
> arising of Consciousness. For this reason the Blessed One said, “The
> Buddha-domain of Buddhas is inconceivable” [quotation damaged in the
> local witness]. The other two movements are established for a Buddha
> without being stated [in the verse].
>
> “Others' is body-conveying and also resolve-based.” Śrāvakas and
> pratyekabuddhas have body-conveying movement, because the body is conveyed
> successively, like a bird. Resolve-based movement is swift going through
> resolving the distant to be near. Construction, in turn, is twofold:
> belonging to the desire domain and belonging to the form domain.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
nirmāṇa opened, not analyzed
```

## 5. Means ≠ Operation

Not every samādhi yields these. Not “teleportation” as one blob. No power-field
under the body. Resolve-based ≠ a spacetime theory. Mind-swift ≠ mind travels
while the body stays. Class-admission ≠ every member has it. Fivefold ordinary-
person share from 7.42 is not revoked by this three-class contrast.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- identity = means, not movement-as-samādhi;
- `tridhā` = movement only;
- `anyeṣām` does not strip the Buddha of the first two.
