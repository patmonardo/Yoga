# VAK_7.55 Bhāṣya — Divine Vision: Range, Acquisition, and the Intermediate Being

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

Quoted contrast (not the numbered verse):

> dūrastham āvṛtaṃ sūkṣmaṃ sarvataś ca na paśyati |
> māṃsacakṣur yato rūpam ato divyaṃ dṛgiṣyate ||

Numbered:

> dvitrisāhasrakāsaṃkhyadṛśo 'rhatkhaḍgadaiśikāḥ |
> anyad apy upapattyāptaṃ taddṛśyo nāntarībhavaḥ || 7.55 ||

> With exertion: arhat, solitary Buddha, and Teacher see the two-thousandfold,
> three-thousandfold, and innumerable world-systems. Other [capacities] too are
> acquired through birth. The intermediate being is not visible to that eye.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> How far does one see with the divine eye? According to the kind of eye one has.
> Without deliberate exertion, śrāvaka, pratyekabuddha, and Buddha see respectively
> the thousandfold, two-thousandfold, and three-thousandfold world-systems. With
> exertion: the śrāvaka also the two-thousandfold; the rhinoceros-like one the
> three-thousandfold; the Blessed One innumerable systems, as far as he wishes.
>
> Is only ṛiddhi birth-acquired? The four beginning with divine hearing also may
> be. But nothing acquired merely through birth receives the name abhijñā.
>
> The intermediate being is seen by the abhijñā-eye, not by the birth-acquired one.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
fleshly limits → why divine sight is posited
range depends on the eye possessed
without / with abhisaṃskāra (two tables)
birth can give the four after ṛiddhi; name abhijñā withheld
antarābhava: abhijñā-eye only
```

## 5. Name ≠ Range ≠ Status

Scales are cosmological, not meters. Without exertion ≠ no prior cultivation.
Birth-acquired capacity admitted; designation withheld (same cut as 7.46).
No birth-acquired āsravakṣaya. Restriction is this eye's object, not a theory
that no one in intermediate existence perceives.

## 6. Two Tables

| bearer | no exertion | with exertion |
|---|---|---|
| śrāvaka | 1 000-fold | 2 000-fold |
| pratyekabuddha | 2 000-fold | 3 000-fold |
| Buddha | 3 000-fold | innumerable, as wished |

| eye | abhijñā-name | sees antarābhava |
|---|---|---|
| birth-acquired | no | no |
| abhijñā | yes | yes |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first compound = with-exertion ranges;
- `anyat` = the four after ṛiddhi;
- `tad-` = birth-acquired divine eye.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Vidyā: same Qualitative Concept “eye,” different Ideas of provenance and object.
Technē of exertion widens range without minting the name. Appearance would
stamp every far-sight abhijñā. Ārya-path still does not confuse birth-gift with Path-knowledge.
