## 3. Continuous Translation

> How far does one see with the divine eye? That depends on the kind
> of eye one has. Without deliberate exertion, śrāvakas,
> pratyekabuddhas, and Buddhas see respectively thousandfold,
> two-thousandfold, and three-thousandfold world-systems.
>
> With deliberate exertion, however, Arhats, rhinoceros-like solitary
> Buddhas, and the Teacher see respectively two-thousandfold,
> three-thousandfold, and innumerable [world-systems]. With full
> exertion, even a śrāvaka sees a two-thousandfold world-system with
> the divine eye; a rhinoceros-like one sees a three-thousandfold
> system. The Blessed Buddha sees innumerable world-systems, as far
> as he wishes [the local `lokahātūn` is damaged].
>
> Is extraordinary efficacy alone acquired through birth, or are
> there others as well [the local question is compressed]? Others too
> are acquired through birth: the four [higher knowledges] beginning
> with divine hearing may also be acquired through birth. Yet nothing
> acquired through birth receives the designation *abhijñā*.
> As for a divine eye acquired through birth, the intermediate being
> is not visible to it. The intermediate being is seen only by the
> eye that is an *abhijñā*, not by the birth-acquired one.

## 5. Name ≠ Range ≠ Status

Scales are cosmological, not meters. Without exertion ≠ no prior cultivation.
Birth-acquired capacity admitted; designation withheld (same cut as 7.46).
No birth-acquired āsravakṣaya. Restriction is this eye's object, not a theory
that no one in intermediate existence perceives.

## 6. Two Tables

| bearer | no exertion | with exertion |
|---|---|---|
| śrāvaka | 1 000-fold | 2 000-fold |
| pratyekabuddha | 2 000-fold | 3 000-fold |
| Buddha | 3 000-fold | innumerable, as wished |

| eye | abhijñā-name | sees antarābhava |
|---|---|---|
| birth-acquired | no | no |
| abhijñā | yes | yes |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first compound = with-exertion ranges;
- `anyat` = the four after ṛiddhi;
- `tad-` = birth-acquired divine eye.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Vidyā: same Qualitative Concept “eye,” different Ideas of provenance and object.
Technē of exertion widens range without minting the name. Appearance would
stamp every far-sight abhijñā. Ārya-path still does not confuse birth-gift with Path-knowledge.
