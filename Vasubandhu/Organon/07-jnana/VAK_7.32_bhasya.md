# VAK_7.32 Bhāṣya — Knowledge-Grounded Fearlessness and the Mindfulness Triad

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vaiśāradyaṃ caturdhā tu yathādyadaśame bale |
> dvitīyasaptame caiva smṛtiprajñātmakaṃ trayam || 7.32 ||

> Fearlessness is fourfold, corresponding to the first and tenth powers, and
> likewise to the second and seventh. The triad has mindfulness and
> discriminative knowing as its nature.

Duals name power-pairs. Not “located in” those powers. Next of the eighteen:
four + three. Great compassion is 7.33.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.32.md`](./VAK_7.32.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> As the power of knowing possibility and impossibility, so the fearlessness
> “I am fully awakened.” As the influx-exhaustion power, so “my influxes are
> exhausted.” As the second power, so the obstructive factors declared to
> disciples. As the power of the course leading everywhere, so the path taught
> for leading out.
>
> Fearlessness is absence of fear. Through these he is without fear. Fearlessness
> effected by knowledge is fitting — not knowledge itself.
>
> The three establishments of mindfulness arise from difference in the assembly,
> as in the sūtra. They have mindfulness and clear comprehension as their nature.
>
> Even in a disciple there may be no delight or resentment toward those who
> listen, do not listen, or both. Why then are these unshared Buddha-qualities?
> From abandonment together with residual traces. Or: for one whose they are —
> his own disciples — the occasion for pleasure and the rest is especially
> strong; their non-arising there is established as a marvel of his, not another's.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
four declarations ↔ powers 1, 10, 2, 7
    → jñānakṛta, not jñānam eva
triad by assembly (listen / not / mixed)
    constitution: smṛti + saṃprajñāna
    why āveṇika: savāsana-prahāṇa / own-disciples occasion
```

## 5. Ground ≠ Result

Fearlessness ≠ a fifth knowledge. Correspondence ≠ identity. Contextual
absence of reactive delight ≠ absence of all wholesome joy. This triad ≠ the
four body/feeling/mind/dharma applications. `Athavā` keeps two answers.

## 6. Correspondences

| Declaration | Power |
|---|---|
| complete awakening | 1 possibility / impossibility |
| exhausted influxes | 10 āsravakṣaya |
| obstructive factors | 2 karmic maturation |
| path leading out | 7 sarvatragāminī pratipad |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- duals = those four powers in order;
- `nirbhayatā` glosses vaiśāradya;
- `trayam` = three smṛtyupasthānas;
- `prajñā` in the triad = saṃprajñāna.
