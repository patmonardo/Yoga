# VAK_7.51 Bhāṣya — Construction, Speech, and Sustained Manifestations

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> svabhūmikena nirmāṇaṃ bhāṣaṇaṃ tvadhareṇa ca |
> nirmātraiva sahāśāstuḥ adhiṣṭhāyānyavarttanāt || 7.51 ||

> Construction is by a mind of its own ground; speech, however, also by a lower
> one. For one other than the Teacher, [it speaks] only with the constructor.
> Having sustained it, another mind operates.

`Sahāśāstuḥ` = saha a-śāstuḥ. Persistence after death: 7.52.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.51.md`](./VAK_7.51.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> A construction of one ground is not produced by a construction-mind of another
> ground. `Ca` means also by a same-ground mind. A desire-domain or first-dhyāna
> constructed form speaks by a mind of its own ground; a higher-ground one, by
> a first-dhyāna mind — because above there is no initiator of manifest expression.
>
> For someone other than a Buddha the construction speaks with the constructor.
> When there are many, they speak simultaneously. When he speaks, they speak with
> him; when silent, all are silent. A Buddha's constructed forms speak before or
> after, as he wishes.
>
> [Objection:] When speech-mind is present, construction-mind is absent; from that
> absence, absence of the constructed? They sustain the construction with the wish
> that it remain, and set speech going with another mind.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
product-ground = construction-mind ground
speech: own-ground if speech lives there; else first dhyāna
non-Buddha: lockstep with constructor
Buddha: before or after as wished
sustain then other-mind speech (not two simultaneous minds)
```

## 5. Three Coordinates

Source dhyāna (7.50) ≠ immediate producing mind ≠ product. First-pass that
bound simultaneous speech to the Teacher is reversed. Sustaining here is not
said to be Buddha-only. Manifestations are not given their own continua.
Death-limit not yet asked.

## 6. Speech Grounds

| product | constructs | speaks |
|---|---|---|
| kāma / 1st dhyāna | same | same |
| 2nd–4th | same | first dhyāna |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `svabhūmikena` matches product;
- `ca` keeps same-ground speech;
- negative in `a-śāstuḥ`.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Produce / persist / speak are three operations. The Concept of the product is
not the Concept of the current mind. Appearance would collapse them into one
magic body that talks because it exists. Instruction (7.47) still outranks this
Technē.
