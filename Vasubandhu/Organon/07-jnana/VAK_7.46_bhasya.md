# VAK_7.46 Bhāṣya — Vidyā: Intrinsic Status and Designation through the Continuum

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> aśaikṣyantyā tadākhye dve tatsaṃtānamudbhavāt |
> iṣṭe śaikṣasya nokte tu vidye sāvidyasaṃtateḥ || 7.46 ||

> Of the three vidyās, the last—direct realization of influx-exhaustion—is
> beyond training in the strict sense. The other two bear that designation
> because they arise in a beyond-training continuum. They are admitted for a
> learner, but are not called vidyās for that learner, because the continuum is
> accompanied by ignorance.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> “Of these, in the strict sense, the last is beyond training”: only direct
> realization by Knowledge of influx-exhaustion is a beyond-training vidyā
> in that sense. “The other two are called that because they arise in that
> continuum”—the local verse wording is damaged. The other two are called
> beyond-training because they arise in the continuum of one beyond training.
> In themselves they are neither learner nor beyond-training.
>
> Are these two higher knowledges, then, not stated for a learner, since they
> are not called learner vidyās? “They are admitted for a learner, but are
> not called vidyās, because of a continuum accompanied by ignorance.”
> Establishment as vidyā does not fit a continuum accompanied by ignorance,
> because ignorance can overpower again.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
strict aśaikṣī = exhaustion only
other two: name from continuum, not from own nature
objection: then denied to the learner?
reply: capacity admitted; vidyā-name withheld (renewed avidyā possible)
```

## 5. Occurrence ≠ Designation

Not: the learner has no knowledge. Not: every aśaikṣa cognition is intrinsically
aśaikṣa. Not: the two become śaikṣa-by-nature when a learner has them. Ethical
classes from 7.45 stand. `Paramārthena` here = strict sense of the name — not
the project overlay. Renewed overpowering ≠ this memory later turns false.

- `iṣṭe` admits the capacities;
- `nokte` withholds the name.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Vidyā is not an event-trophy. The Idea holds the cognition; the continuum is
whether avidyā can still govern. Local correctness inside a learner is Concept
without the Idea's closure. Appearance would either deny the śraṇaka his two
abhijñās or hand him the vidyā-name. Ārya-path still not Arhat-obsession: the
cut is designation, not contempt for the learner.
