# VAK_7.43 Bhāṣya — Higher Knowledges: Constitution, Ground, and Acquisition

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> catasraḥ saṃvṛtijñānaṃ cetasi jñānapañcakam |
> kṣayābhijñā balaṃ yadvat pañca dhyānacatuṣṭaye || 7.43 ||

> Four are conventional knowledge; concerning mind, a group of five knowledges.
> Exhaustion-higher-knowledge is as the corresponding power. Five are in the
> four dhyānas.

Four = 1, 2, 4, 5 of 7.42 — not “the first four.” Object-range: 7.44.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Leaving aside other-mind and influx-exhaustion higher knowledges, four are
> conventional knowledge. Other-mind: five — dharma, anvaya, path, conventional,
> other-mind. Exhaustion-abhijñā is to be understood as that power was said:
> six or ten knowledges; its levels likewise.
>
> The remaining five are in the four dhyānas. Why not formless? Three first,
> because they take form as object. Other-mind also is not [generated there],
> because it is produced through a form-based route. Wishing to know another's
> mind, one takes up the sign of one's own body and mind — of such a body, such
> a mind — and so attends to others. Once the higher knowledge is accomplished,
> one knows without dependence on form.
>
> Recollection of former abodes: backward from the immediately ceased mind-
> cognition, through states, as far as the linking mind. Once accomplished,
> recollection may skip. Only what was previously experienced. Pure Abodes:
> experienced by hearing. One arrived from formless existence: produced in
> dependence on another continuum; others, on one's own.
>
> For ṛiddhi and the rest: attention to lightness, sound, and light is the
> application.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
constitution: 4 conventional; mind = 5 categories; kṣaya as 7.29/7.30
ground: five in four dhyānas (not ārūpya)
acquisition ≠ accomplished exercise (sign / sequence vs skip / form-free)
```

## 5. Genesis ≠ Runtime

Form as object (ṛiddhi, hearing, death-rebirth) blocks formless support.
`Rūpa` includes sound, not colour only. Sign-route for other-mind does not
make mature knowing an inference from the sign. Skipping stages does not open
a formless ground. Six/ten for kṣaya is the old semantic cut, not “depending
on the person” as a vague slogan.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `catasraḥ` by exclusion;
- `cetasi` = other-mind;
- `yadvat` = prior power-account;
- `pañca` = the five non-exhaustion.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

inside Idea: the sign is not the knowledge. Appearance would freeze the
