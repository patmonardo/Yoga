# VAK_7.43 Bhāṣya — Higher Knowledges: Constitution, Ground, and Acquisition

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> catasraḥ saṃvṛtijñānaṃ cetasi jñānapañcakam |
> kṣayābhijñā balaṃ yadvat pañca dhyānacatuṣṭaye || 7.43 ||

> Four higher knowledges—extraordinary efficacy, divine hearing, former-
> abode recollection, and death-and-reappearance—have conventional knowledge
> as their nature. Higher knowledge of another's Consciousness comprises five
> Knowledge categories: dharma, anvaya, Path, conventional, and Knowledge of
> another's Consciousness.
> Influx-exhaustion higher knowledge is as the corresponding power; the
> other five are supported on the four dhyānas.

Four = 1, 2, 4, 5 of 7.42 — not “the first four.” Object-range: 7.44.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.43.md`](./VAK_7.43.md).

## 3. Continuous Translation

> “Four are conventional Knowledge”: excluding the higher knowledges of
> another's Consciousness and influx-exhaustion. “For Consciousness, a group
> of five Knowledge kinds”: the higher knowledge of another's Consciousness
> comprises dharma-, anvaya-, Path-, conventional, and Knowledge of another's
> Consciousness. “The influx-exhaustion higher knowledge is as the power”:
> it should be understood as the influx-exhaustion Knowledge-power was
> explained—six or ten Knowledges. Its levels too are to be understood in
> the same way.
>
> “The remaining five are in the four dhyānas.” The prose then says “the
> five higher knowledges are on the fourth-dhyāna level” [local wording
> conflicts with the verse's four-dhyāna statement]. Why are they absent
> from the formless levels? Three are absent because they take form as
> Support. The higher knowledge of another's Consciousness is also absent,
> because it is produced through a form-based route. Recollection of former
> abodes [is absent] because it is accomplished by successive attention to
> changing states and death; it also takes such things as place and lineage
> as Supports.
>
> One wishing to know another's Consciousness takes up the sign of one's own
> body and Consciousness: “With what kind of body does what kind of
> Consciousness occur for me?” Attending likewise to others, one brings
> about Knowledge of their Consciousness. Once that higher knowledge is
> accomplished, one knows without dependence on form.
>
> One wishing to recollect former abodes takes up the sign of the immediately
> ceased mental Cognition and attends in reverse sequence to earlier states,
> as far as the linking Consciousness. From there [one reaches] a moment of
> intermediate existence and the death [moment]; the local syntax of this
> completion is uncertain. One also recollects another person's former
> abodes in this way. Once the capacity is accomplished, recollection can
> skip stages. Only what has previously been experienced is recollected.
> How are the Pure Abodes recollected? Because they were experienced through
> hearing. For one who has fallen from formless existence and been born
> here, [the capacity] is produced in dependence on another continuum; for
> others, in dependence on their own continuum. For extraordinary efficacy
> and the related higher knowledges, attention to lightness, sound, and
> light is the preparatory application.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
constitution: 4 conventional; mind = 5 categories; kṣaya as 7.29/7.30
ground: five in four dhyānas (not ārūpya)
acquisition ≠ accomplished exercise (sign / sequence vs skip / form-free)
```

## 5. Genesis ≠ Runtime

Form as Support (ṛiddhi, hearing, death-rebirth) excludes formless levels.
`Rūpa` includes sound, not colour only. Sign-route for other-mind does not
make mature knowing an inference from the sign. Skipping stages does not open
a formless ground. Six/ten for kṣaya is the old semantic cut, not “depending
on the person” as a vague slogan.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `catasraḥ` by exclusion;
- `cetasi` = other-mind;
- `yadvat` = prior power-account;
- `pañca` = the five non-exhaustion.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Vidyā names constitution and field. Technē names the route that makes the
capacity available. After that, exercise need not replay the route. Concept
inside Idea: the sign is not the knowledge. Appearance would freeze the
training-wheels as the power.
