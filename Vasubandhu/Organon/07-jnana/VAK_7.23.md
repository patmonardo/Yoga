# VAK_7.23 — Sevenfold Cultivation across Higher Path Functions

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
सप्तभूमिजयाभिज्ञाकोप्याप्त्याकीर्णभाविते ।
आनन्तर्यपथेषूर्ध्वं मुक्तिमार्गाष्टकेऽपि च ॥ ७.२३ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
saptabhūmijayābhijñākopyāptyākīrṇabhāvite |
ānantaryapatheṣūrdhvaṃ muktimārgāṣṭake 'pi ca || 7.23 ||
```

The Sanskrit follows the local kārikā witness. The running
Bhāṣya has irregular spacing and `ānanatarya-`; the study
retains the kārikā's `ānantarya-`. The supplied predicate
is explicitly `sapta jñānāni bhāvyante`, “seven knowledges
are cultivated.” The prose controls both the compressed
syntax and the changing membership of those seven.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text

ānantarya-patheṣu | ūrdhvam | mukti-mārga-aṣṭake | api | ca
```

The first line above displays compound members for analysis;
it is not a claim that each has an independent case ending
in the verse.

| Form | Morphology | Contextual sense |

| `akopyāpti` | compound member, `akopya + āpti` | attainment of immovability |

`Abhijñā + akopya` yields `abhijñākopya`; the initial `a`
of `akopya` must not disappear from the lexical analysis.
`Āpti + ākīrṇa` yields `āptyākīrṇa`.

## 4. Grammar

**Four settings governed by uninterrupted paths.** The
Bhāṣya expands the first line into detachment from seven
levels, the five higher knowledges, penetration of immovability,
and mixed cultivation of dhyāna. `Ānantaryapatheṣu` specifies
the path function within those settings. It is not a fifth
independent occasion appended to four unrestricted activities.

**The scope of “above.”** The commentary resolves `ūrdhvam`
as beyond detachment from the seven levels, in detachment
from Bhavāgra. It belongs with the ensuing eight liberation
paths. The first-pass construe “higher uninterrupted paths”
does not preserve this relation.

**The trainee qualification.** The prose includes `śaikṣasya`
in its compressed account of uninterrupted paths. This
requires care: attainment of immovability cannot simply be
made a trainee attainment by distributing that genitive
indiscriminately across the whole list. The following
immovability exception receives its own determination.
The study therefore keeps that case separate rather than
calling every practitioner in the verse a trainee.

**Close construe.** On the uninterrupted paths in detachment
from seven levels, in the higher knowledges, in attainment
of immovability, and in mixed cultivation, [seven knowledges
are cultivated]; and above [those seven levels], also in
the group of eight liberation paths.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> In the conquest of seven levels, the higher knowledges,
> attainment of immovability, and mixed cultivation, seven
> knowledges are cultivated on the uninterrupted paths;
> and above that, also in the eight liberation paths.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Seven knowledges are cultivated on the relevant uninterrupted
> paths for detachment from the four dhyānas and the first three
> formless levels, for the five higher knowledges, for attainment
> of immovability, and for mixed cultivation of dhyāna. Ordinarily
> these are dharma-, anvaya-, suffering-, origination-, cessation-,
> Path-, and conventional knowledge. At immovability, however,
> conventional knowledge is excluded because it is not a
> counteragent to Bhavāgra; a different seventh is specified
> in a textually doubtful reading.
>
> Beyond detachment from those seven levels, in the eight
> liberation paths of detachment from Bhavāgra, the six truth-
> related categories together with knowledge of another mind
> are cultivated. Conventional knowledge is again excluded
> because it is not a counteragent to Bhavāgra.

Source: Pradhan 408|08–408|19. The doubtful replacement-word
at 408|16 is treated below; the explicit later seven-item
list is secure. The prose also distinguishes the present
cognition from these cultivated sets.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Cultivation is determined by the work of a particular
> path function at a particular level. A sevenfold count
> can persist while its members change. Conventional
> knowledge's inclusion has a limit: it cannot supply
> counteraction to the summit of existence. The relevant
> knowledge field must therefore be specified anew as
> the undertaking changes.

This local counteraction argument is not a universal rule
that every cultivated category must itself be the presently
operative counteragent. The inclusion of knowledge of another
mind in the liberation-path range already requires that
actual counteraction and the wider cultivated field remain
distinct questions.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Saptabhūmi` — seven levels.** Four dhyānas and the first
three formless levels. These are levels from which detachment
is undertaken, not the seven support levels counted in VAK
7.21. Equal numbers do not establish equal inventories.

**`Jaya` — conquest.** Explicitly glossed as `vairāgya`,
detachment. Here conquest of a level means detachment with
respect to it, not simply entering or mastering its attainment.

**`Pañcābhijñā` — five higher knowledges.** The prose restricts
the verse's `abhijñā` to five. This study preserves the count
without importing the sixth higher knowledge into the rule.

**`Akopyāpti` / `akopyaprativedha` — attainment / penetration
of immovability.** A distinct attainment-setting with an
explicit substitution in its cultivated set. It is not a
synonym for detachment from any one of the seven levels.

**`Vyavakīrṇabhāvita dhyāna` — dhyāna cultivated in mixed form.**
The Bhāṣya's expansion of `ākīrṇabhāvita`. This passage
specifies its uninterrupted-path cultivation count; it
does not detail the sequence of the mixing procedure.

**`Bhavāgra` — summit of existence.** The highest formless
level, beyond the three included in the seven-level list.
Its detachment is the setting of the eight liberation paths
named in the latter half.

**`Bhavāgrāpratipakṣatva` — not being a counteragent to Bhavāgra.**
Resolve `bhavāgra + a-pratipakṣa-tva`. The negative `a-`
is essential. The research vocabulary's positive “capacity
to act as counteragent” reverses the stated reason.

## 8. Logical Determination

Let `B` retain VAK 7.22's six-category set:

```text
B = {DharmaKnowledge, AnvayaKnowledge, SufferingKnowledge,
     OriginationKnowledge, CessationKnowledge, PathKnowledge}
```

| Setting | Path function | Cultivated range |
|---|---|---|
| Detachment from seven levels | Uninterrupted | B plus conventional knowledge |
| Five higher knowledges | Uninterrupted | B plus conventional knowledge |
| Mixed cultivation of dhyāna | Uninterrupted | B plus conventional knowledge |
| Attainment of immovability | Uninterrupted | B plus a replacement seventh; reading requires review |
| Bhavāgra detachment | Eight liberation paths | B plus knowledge of another mind |

**Present cognition.** In the worldly case the prose names
conventional knowledge. In the supramundane case it names
one of four anvaya-knowledges or two dharma-knowledges. It
repeats that sixfold present-cognition range for the eight
Bhavāgra liberation paths.

The four anvaya determinations concern the four truths.
The two dharma determinations concern cessation and Path,
consistent with their three-domain counteractive efficacy
established in VAK 7.09. These are six truth-specific options,
not six simultaneous cognitions or six new inventory members.
The worldly/supramundane distinction is conditional; it does
not establish that every listed attainment admits both modes.

The eight liberation paths are a bounded set. The verse
does not extend its count to every liberation path against
Bhavāgra or to all higher uninterrupted paths. The continuation
must control the remaining cases.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**The doubtful seventh at immovability.** The running source
prints `tatra jayajñānaṃ saptamaṃ veditavyam`. The research
study promotes `jayajñāna` to “knowledge of conquest.” That
would introduce an unsupported category into an account
organized by the established ten knowledges. A possible
restoration is `kṣayajñāna`, knowledge of exhaustion, but
the local witness does not verify that restoration. The
official study records the uncertainty rather than treating
either a new category or an emendation as settled text.

The secure content survives this defect: conventional
knowledge is excluded at immovability; the count remains
seven; another seventh is intended. The exact replacement
requires a checked textual witness before final review.

**Counteraction has a specific scope.** The negative reason
explains conventional knowledge's exclusion in the two
Bhavāgra-related cases. It does not abolish the distinction
between what performs an uninterrupted path's work and what
is cultivated on a liberation path. A general eligibility
test imposed on every member would exceed this passage.

**Organon reconstruction — Yoga Vidyā and Yoga Technē.** Yoga
Vidyā determines the knowledge field in its internal relations:
the truth-specific options, the cultivated categories, and
the scope of their efficacy. Yoga Technē, its kriyā–karma
companion, determines the undertaking through its target
level and path function. Their conjunction makes the
cultivation count intelligible.

For our Yoga system, an operation must be specified by what
it is directed toward and the function it performs. Detachment,
higher knowing, mixed cultivation, and stabilization in
immovability cannot be collapsed into a generic “advanced
practice.” Nor can an unchanged count certify an unchanged
capacity. The determinate members and their relations to
the undertaking must be carried with the number.

The uncertain seventh also disciplines reconstruction:
an attractive term such as “knowledge of conquest” cannot
become a new Yoga Vidyā category merely because a damaged
source string suggests it. The conceptual account retains
an explicit unresolved position until textual review supplies
its content.

**Review status.** Provisional, Bhāṣya-controlled study.
The syntactic grouping, seven target levels, five higher
knowledges, negative counteraction reason, and eight-path
range are source-checked. The immovability replacement
and compressed trainee qualification remain flagged.
