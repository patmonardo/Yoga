# VAK_7.53 Bhāṣya — Construction-Consciousness Ethics and Five Sources of Ṛiddhi

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> avyākṛtaṃ bhāvanājaṃ trividhaṃ tūpapattijam |
> ṛddhirmantrauṣadhābhyāṃ ca karmajā ceti pañcadhā || 7.53 ||

> A construction-Consciousness resulting from cultivation is ethically
> indeterminate; one acquired through birth is threefold. Ṛddhi also arises
> through mantra, medicinal means, and karma: thus it is fivefold.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> Now, is every construction-Consciousness ethically indeterminate?
> The one born of cultivation is indeterminate: whatever is a result
> of cultivation is necessarily indeterminate. The birth-acquired
> one, however, is threefold. A construction-Consciousness acquired
> through birth can be wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate—among
> devas, nāgas, piśācas, and others. The constructions of one's own or
> another's body made by it are nine-Essence: [they concern] material
> Essences other than sound, [the constructed body being] inseparable
> from faculties [the compound's exact syntax remains uncertain].
> But a faculty itself is not constructed.
>
> Is extraordinary efficacy only of these two kinds, produced through
> cultivation and acquired through birth? These are two kinds; with
> efficacy through mantra and medicine and that produced by karma,
> the count is five. In summary, they describe five kinds of extraordinary
> efficacy: a result of cultivation, acquired through birth,
> mantra-produced, medicine-produced, and karma-produced, as with
> Māndhātṛ and beings in intermediate existence.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

## 5. Capacity ≠ Resulting Consciousness

7.45 called ṛiddhi-abhijñā wholesome. This verse calls the *cultivated
construction-Consciousness* indeterminate. Different cognitions (7.49). Three ethical
classes ≠ three of the five sources. Mantra and medicine counted separately —
no procedure given. No ethical table for mantra / medicine / karma here.
Birth-list and karma-list are not a denial that birth has karmic conditions.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first half = construction-Consciousness;
- `trividham` = three ethical possibilities;
- second half = five sources of ṛiddhi.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Spectacle still does not certify (7.47). Provenance is a determination, not a
sticker. Appearance would read one flying body as Path. Vidyā asks the source
before it names the ethical cell. Technē of mantra or medicine is not our work.
