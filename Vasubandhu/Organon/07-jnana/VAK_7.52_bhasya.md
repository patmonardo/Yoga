# VAK_7.52 Bhāṣya — Disputed Persistence after Death and Mastery of Construction

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> mṛtasyāpyastyadhiṣṭhānaṃ nāsthirasya apare tu na |
> ādāvekamanekena jitāyāṃ tu viparyayāt || 7.52 ||

> Sustaining determination remains even of one who has died, but not of an
> unstable thing; others, however, deny this. At first one [is constructed]
> through many; when mastered, the relation is reversed.

Ethical status of construction-minds and five sources of ṛiddhi: 7.53.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.52.md`](./VAK_7.52.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> kiṃ jīvita evādhiṣṭhānam anuvartate atha mṛtaś cāpi /
> āryamahākaśyapādhiṣṭhānena tad asthisaṃkalāvasthānāt /
> asthirasya tu bhāvasya nāsty adhiṣṭhānam /
> apare punar āhur nāsti mṛtasyādhiṣṭhānam /
> asthiśaṅkalāvasthānaṃ tu devatānubhāvād iti /
> ādita ekaṃ nirmitam anekena nirmāṇacittena nirmiṇoti /
> jitāyāṃ tv abhijñāyām ekena cittenānekaṃ nirmiṇoti yāvan nirmātum iṣṭaṃ bhavati /

**Witness.** Pradhan 428|01–12. Flesh-example `adhhiṣṭhānāt` unrestored. Voices:
first account; `apare`; Vasubandhu on counts.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Does the sustaining determination continue only while one lives, or also of
> one who has died? [First account:] even of the dead — from the standing of
> that skeletal framework by Ārya Mahākāśyapa's determination. But not of an
> unstable thing.
>
> Others say: there is no determination of the dead. The standing of the
> skeleton is from the efficacy of deities.
>
> At first one constructs one manifestation with many construction-minds. When
> the higher knowledge is mastered, with one mind one constructs many — as many
> as one wishes to construct.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
question: after death?
A: yes, if the thing is stable (bones cited)
B: no — deities hold the bones
separate question: how many minds / how many products
novice many→1; mastered 1→many as wished
```

## 5. Do Not Harmonize

Same remains, two causes. Bones are not called a constructed apparition.
Stable ≠ eternal. Reversal is production-count, not a new persistence theory
and not a lift of 7.50–51 ground-rules. Mastery ≠ āsravakṣaya. School name of
`apare` not supplied here.

## 6. Two Questions

| | first account | others |
|---|---|---|
| after death | adhiṣṭhāna continues (stable only) | denied |
| bones | his determination | devatānubhāva |

| | minds | products |
|---|---|---|
| beginning | many | one |
| mastered | one | many as wished |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `asthirasya` = unstable *thing*;
- `apare tu na` denies post-mortem adhiṣṭhāna;
- `jitāyām` = mastered abhijñā.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

A lasting result does not name its cause. Vidyā keeps the two accounts apart.
Technē of mastery is a changed ratio of operations to products — still under
instruction's rank. Appearance would either canonize the bones or call mastery
cheap magic.
