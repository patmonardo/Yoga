# VAK_7.35 Bhāṣya — Shared Qualities and the Distribution of Attainment

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> śiṣyasādhāraṇā anye dharmāḥ kecit pṛthagjanaiḥ |
> araṇāpraṇidhijñānapratisaṃvidguṇādayaḥ || 7.35 ||

> Other excellent qualities of Buddhas are shared with śrāvaka disciples,
> and some also with ordinary persons, as appropriate: araṇā, Knowledge
> through resolve, discriminating knowledges, and so forth.

Eighteen closed. These are still Buddha-qualities; sharing is the new
relation. `Kecit` and `yathāyogam` govern the ordinary-person clause.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.35.md`](./VAK_7.35.md).

## 3. Continuous Translation

> “Other qualities are shared with disciples.” But other excellent qualities
> of Buddhas are shared with śrāvakas. “Some with ordinary persons.” Which
> are they? As applicable: “qualities beginning with freedom from conflict,
> Knowledge through resolve, and discriminating knowledges.” These are
> freedom from conflict, Knowledge through resolve, discriminating knowledges,
> higher knowledges, dhyānas, formless attainments, immeasurables,
> liberations, Essences of mastery, totality-Essences, and so on [the
> catalogue's joins are irregular in the local witness].
>
> Among these, freedom from conflict is this: a certain arhat, knowing that
> beings' suffering arises from afflictions and knowing himself to be a
> distinguished recipient of offerings, wishes to avoid the arising in
> others of afflictions taking him as Support. He produces such Knowledge
> that [the local `bhena` is damaged] he does not occasion conflict in others
> in any way. Attachment taking him as Support does not arise in anyone, nor
> hatred or pride. This course does not bring anyone into conflict; hence
> “freedom from conflict.” As for this quality, it is—[the sentence
> continues with its Knowledge classification at 7.36].

## 5. Sharing ≠ Equivalence

Buddha-possession ≠ exclusive possession. Shared kind ≠ every disciple has
it. Shared kind ≠ every ordinary person has it. Shared kind ≠ same range or
mastery. `Pṛthagjana` is practitioner-status, not “anyone kind.” `Śiṣya` =
śrāvaka here.

`Ādi` opens the class; it does not dump every human capacity into the list.

## 6. Araṇā Introduced before Its Classification

An arhat, knowing suffering from afflictions and himself a distinguished
recipient of offerings, produces Knowledge so that attachment, hatred, and
pride do not arise in others with him as Support. This introductory
undertaking and the derivation from a non-conflict-provoking course belong
to 7.35; its Knowledge constitution and further limits belong to 7.36.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `anye dharmāḥ` = other *Buddha*-qualities;
- `śiṣya` = śrāvaka;
- `kecit` + `yathāyogam` restrict the pṛthagjana share;
- `guṇādayaḥ` expanded, not closed at three names.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Same Qualitative Concept-kind can be instantiated in different continua.
The Idea keeps those Concepts; it does not make the instantiations equal.
Āveṇika remains Singular-closure. Appearance would either seal three
ontologies or flatten all attainments. Technē starts here with distribution;
individual analyses supply the masks.
