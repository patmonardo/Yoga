# VAK_7.25 Bhāṣya — Exhaustion-Knowledge and the Nine-, Ten-, and Eightfold Ranges

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> navānāṃ tu kṣayajñāne akopyasya daśa bhāvanā |
> tatsaṃcare 'ntyamuktau ca proktaśeṣe 'ṣṭabhāvanā || 7.25 ||

> At Knowledge of exhaustion there is cultivation of nine; for one of immovable
> nature, ten. At the final liberation path, in transition to that state, there
> is cultivation of ten; in the remaining stated cases, eight.

Ninth Bhavāgra-liberation path = kṣayajñāna. Completes 7.23's eight vimukti
paths and 7.24's Bhavāgra-ānantarya six.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.25.md`](./VAK_7.25.md).

## 3. Continuous Translation

> “But at exhaustion-Knowledge, of nine”: the ninth liberation path in
> detachment from Bhavāgra is exhaustion-Knowledge. There, nine
> knowledges are cultivated, excluding non-arising-Knowledge.
>
> “For one of immovable nature, cultivation of ten”: for one who is of
> immovable nature, ten knowledges are cultivated, because
> non-arising-Knowledge is acquired.
>
> “And at the final liberation path in transition to that [state]”: for
> one who passes into immovability too, ten are cultivated at the final
> liberation path.
>
> “In the remainder of what has been stated, cultivation of eight.”
> What, then, remains? The ninth liberation path in detachment from
> desire; liberation paths in detachment from the seven levels, in the
> higher knowledges, and in dhyāna cultivated in mixed form;
> penetration of immovability and eight liberation paths [their exact
> syntactic attachment is uncertain]; and all preparatory and superior
> paths of one detached from desire. In all these, eight knowledges are
> cultivated: in future cultivation, exhaustion- and
> non-arising-Knowledge are excluded. Thus for the trainee [the scope
> of this closing qualification across the list is uncertain].
>
> For one beyond training, however, in preparatory, liberation, and
> superior paths connected with higher knowledges and the like, nine
> or ten knowledges are cultivated. But in the uninterrupted and
> liberation paths connected with higher knowledges and mixed
> cultivation, eight or nine [are cultivated]. In two higher-knowledge
> liberation paths, however, nothing is cultivated in the future
> because they are indeterminate [local `tvabhhijñā-`].
>
> For an ordinary person, in the final liberation paths of detachment
> from desire and the first three dhyānas, and in the dhyāna-level
> preparatory paths, three higher-knowledge liberation paths, and the
> production of qualities such as the immeasurables, conventional
> Knowledge is cultivated in the future, and so is Knowledge of
> another's Consciousness except in the aids to penetration. In those
> aids, Knowledge of another's Consciousness is not cultivated, because
> they form an entourage of the Path of Seeing.
>
> Elsewhere, when an unacquired path is gained, only conventional
> Knowledge [local `saṃvāṛtijñānam`] is cultivated in the future.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
Bhavāgra ninth vimukti = kṣaya → T minus anutpāda = 9
immovable at that occasion → T = 10 (anutpāda acquired)
final vimukti of transition into immovability → 10
proktaśeṣa, future cultivation → T minus kṣaya and anutpāda = 8
    compressed remainder list; śaikṣasyaivam has uncertain scope
aśaikṣa: 9/10 on listed preparation/liberation/superior paths;
          8/9 on listed uninterrupted/liberation paths;
          two indeterminate higher-knowledge liberations: no future cultivation
pṛthagjana: future conventional plus other-Consciousness Knowledge,
             except no latter at aids to penetration;
             elsewhere on first path acquisition: conventional only
```

## 5. Not a Scale

Eight, nine, ten are different conditions of the two terminal knowledges
(7.07: accomplished work vs non-repetition). Not “more knowledge.” Not ten
present events. Future cultivation ≠ guaranteed later arising.

Eightfold *includes* conventional and paracitta. Do not import 7.23–7.24
ānantarya exclusions into these vimukti / prayoga / viśeṣa rooms.

`Śaikṣasyaivam` cannot make immovability a trainee attainment. The attachment
of `akopyaprativedho 'ṣṭau vimuktimārgāḥ` is compressed; its relation to the
preceding tenfold final liberation path requires collation. Do not assign
the trainee eightfold rule indiscriminately to that final path.

Two higher-knowledge liberation paths: no future cultivation because they
are indeterminate. The local Sanskrit does not name them; Xuanzang names
celestial eye and ear. For the ordinary person, conventional Knowledge is
future-cultivated in the listed settings; Knowledge of another's Consciousness
is excepted at `nirvedhabhāgīya` because these aid the Path of Seeing.
Elsewhere on acquisition of a new path: future conventional Knowledge only.
Then 7.26 asks which levels of Knowledge are cultivated.

The beyond-training nine/ten and eight/nine clauses are retained as separate
source ranges; their overlap in path wording is not resolved into a table.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first locative named as ninth Bhavāgra-vimukti;
- ten = acquisition of anutpāda;
- `tat` = immovability;
- `proktaśeṣa` enumerated, not a dump-bin.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is Organon reconstruction. Completion, practitioner status,
and path function jointly determine the cultivated Knowledge range. Nine
at exhaustion-Knowledge excludes non-arising-Knowledge; ten marks its
acquisition for the immovable case. The remaining eightfold statement
cannot be detached from its list and later qualifications.
