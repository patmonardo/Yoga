# VAK_7.25 — Exhaustion-Knowledge and the Nine-, Ten-, and Eightfold Ranges

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
नवानां तु क्षयज्ञाने अकोप्यस्य दश भावना ।
तत्संचरेऽन्त्यमुक्तौ च प्रोक्तशेषेऽष्टभावना ॥ ७.२५ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
navānāṃ tu kṣayajñāne akopyasya daśa bhāvanā |
tatsaṃcare 'ntyamuktau ca proktaśeṣe 'ṣṭabhāvanā || 7.25 ||
```

The Sanskrit follows the local kārikā witness. The separate
`kṣayajñāne akopyasya` is retained. The Bhāṣya identifies
the first occasion precisely: the ninth liberation path
in detachment from Bhavāgra. This completes the contrast
with its eight liberation paths in VAK 7.23 and its
uninterrupted paths in VAK 7.24.

`Proktaśeṣe + aṣṭabhāvanā` yields `proktaśeṣe 'ṣṭabhāvanā`.
The numbers count knowledge categories in cultivation, not
separate simultaneous cognitive events.

## 4. Grammar

**Elliptical counts.** Supply `jñānānāṃ bhāvanā` with
`navānām`. The compressed `daśa bhāvanā` is resolved by
the prose as `daśānāṃ jñānānāṃ bhāvanā`, cultivation of
ten knowledges. The verse's surface numeral does not
require inventing a new construction in the prose.

**The qualification at exhaustion-Knowledge.** `Akopyasya`
specifies the practitioner's status. The tenfold case
qualifies the preceding ninefold rule: for an immovable
practitioner, Knowledge of non-arising is acquired too.
The verse does not describe an obligatory nine-then-ten
sequence undergone by every practitioner.

**Transition and its endpoint.** The Bhāṣya resolves `tat`
through `akopyatāṃ saṃcarati`, passes into immovability.
Carry the tenfold cultivation into `antyamuktau`: it is
at the final liberation path of that transition, not
at every preceding moment of the undertaking.

**A bounded remainder.** `Proktaśeṣa` refers to cases left
over from the preceding discussion. The commentary asks
what remains and enumerates it. This is not a universal
default for any path-event absent from the present verse.

**Close construe.** At exhaustion-Knowledge, cultivation is
of nine [knowledges]; for the immovable practitioner, of
ten. Also at the final liberation path in transition to
that state, [cultivation is of ten]. In the remainder of
the stated cases, there is cultivation of eight.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> At Knowledge of exhaustion, there is cultivation of nine
> knowledges; for the immovable practitioner, of ten. Also
> at the final liberation path in transition to that state,
> ten are cultivated. In the remaining stated cases, eight
> are cultivated.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation


> At Knowledge of exhaustion there is cultivation of nine; for one of immovable
> nature, ten. At the final liberation path, in transition to that state, there
> is cultivation of ten; in the remaining stated cases, eight.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> Completion has a determinate knowledge structure. The
> ninefold range includes Knowledge of exhaustion while
> excluding Knowledge of non-arising. The tenfold range
> includes that further determination through its acquisition.
> The eightfold range excludes both terminal knowledges.
> These counts express different attained conditions and
> path functions, rather than three interchangeable degrees
> of cognitive abundance.

The distinction follows the chapter's account of accomplished
work and non-repetition in VAK 7.07. Here the subject is
cultivation at specified occasions; it does not follow
that all ten categories become ten present cognitions or
that each cultivated item is guaranteed a later occurrence.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Kṣayajñāna` — Knowledge of exhaustion.** Here identified
with the ninth liberation path against Bhavāgra. “Exhaustion”
maintains the chapter's established terminology for completed
liberative work, rather than suggesting the destruction
of every conditioned phenomenon.

**`Anutpādajñānalābha` — acquisition of Knowledge of non-arising.**
The explicit reason for tenfold cultivation in the immovable
case. It is the acquisition of this knowledge-category,
not merely the occurrence of the word “non-arising” in
another description, that supplies the distinction.

**`Akopyadharma` — of immovable nature.** The prose's
description of the practitioner for whom the tenfold
range is established. The passage distinguishes this
case from a later transition into immovability.

**`Saṃcara` — passage / transition.** Its destination here
is immovability. The final liberation path of the transition
receives the tenfold count explicitly.

**`Anāgatabhāvanā` — future cultivation.** The prose specifies
this when stating the eightfold range. The exclusion of the
two terminal knowledges concerns that cultivated field.

**`Śaikṣa`, `aśaikṣa`, `pṛthagjana`.** Trainee, one beyond
training, and ordinary person. The closing prose makes
these statuses indispensable to the cultivation account.

**`Avyākṛta` — indeterminate.** The reason given for no
future cultivation in two higher-knowledge liberation
paths. “No future cultivation” is the restricted predicate;
it does not mean no present cognition or no attained ability.

## 8. Logical Determination

Let `T` be the established ten knowledge categories, `K`
exhaustion-Knowledge, and `N` Knowledge of non-arising.

| Occasion | Cultivated set | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Exhaustion-knowledge, before the immovable qualification | T minus N | 9 |
| Immovable case at exhaustion-Knowledge | T | 10 |
| Final liberation path in transition to immovability | T | 10 |
| Specified eightfold future-cultivation range | T minus K and N | 8 |

**The stated remainder.** The prose lists the ninth liberation
path of desire-detachment; liberation paths associated with
seven-level detachment, higher knowledges, and mixed dhyāna
cultivation; `akopyaprativedho 'ṣṭau vimuktimārgāḥ`; and all
preparatory and superior paths of the detached practitioner.
The immovability phrase is syntactically rough. Whether the
eight liberation paths attach to its penetration or form a
separate list member remains open; the preceding sentence
already assigns ten to the final liberation path in the
transition to immovability.

The following `śaikṣasyaivam`, “thus for the trainee,” cannot
make every item a trainee attainment. In particular, the
immovability undertaking calls for care against the separate
beyond-training qualification which follows. The compressed
list and its qualification remain visible without presenting
a universal eightfold rule.

**Beyond training.** The continuation gives nine or ten in
preparatory, liberation, and superior paths associated with
higher knowledges and related undertakings. It then specifies
eight or nine for uninterrupted/liberation paths associated
with higher knowledges and mixed cultivation. The compressed
wording overlaps in its mention of liberation paths; a final
event-by-event table requires closer textual resolution.
These clauses are recorded as qualifications, not merged
into the trainee's eightfold set.

For two higher-knowledge liberation paths, the source explicitly
says that nothing is cultivated in the future because they
are indeterminate. The Sanskrit does not name the two;
[Xuanzang's parallel](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E9%98%BF%E6%AF%98%E9%81%94%E7%A3%A8%E4%BF%B1%E8%88%8D%E8%AB%96/%E5%8D%B7026)
identifies the celestial eye and celestial ear liberation paths.

**Ordinary persons.** The prose includes future conventional Knowledge
and Knowledge of another's Consciousness at the final liberation
paths of detachment from desire and the first three dhyānas,
and in the specified dhyāna-based preparatory paths, three
higher-knowledge liberation paths, and production of qualities
such as the immeasurables. It excepts the aids to penetration
(`nirvedhabhāgīya`): Knowledge of another's Consciousness is not cultivated
there because they form an entourage of Seeing.

Elsewhere, on acquisition of a previously unacquired path,
only conventional Knowledge is cultivated in the future.
This closing restriction at 410|02 precedes the new question
about the levels of cultivated knowledge in VAK 7.26.

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Translation note.** The nine excludes Knowledge of non-arising; ten
includes it. Eight is the count assigned to the stated remainder, followed
by a trainee qualification whose exact scope remains open.

**The full continuation matters.** The former Bhāṣya excerpt
stopped at the trainee qualification. The restored clauses give
distinct counts for one beyond training, the indeterminate-path
exception, ordinary-person future cultivation, and the final
first-acquisition restriction. These prevent the eightfold
count from absorbing those separate accounts.

**Enumeration needs grammatical control.** The attachment of
`akopyaprativedho 'ṣṭau vimuktimārgāḥ` is uncertain. An
immovability-related eight-path group is plausible, but the
syntax and `śaikṣasyaivam` do not yet settle its scope.
The final liberation path in transition to immovability has
already been assigned tenfold cultivation.

**Eightfold cultivation includes conventional Knowledge.**
`T − {K,N}` includes both conventional Knowledge and Knowledge of
another's Consciousness. The exclusions in the preceding uninterrupted-
path studies therefore cannot be carried indiscriminately
into these liberation, preparation, and superior-path cases.

**Organon reconstruction — Yoga Vidyā and Yoga Technē.** Yoga
Vidyā articulates what completion and non-repetition determine
within the knowledge field. Yoga Technē, its kriyā–karma
companion, articulates the undertaking and the path function
through which a particular cultivation range is established.
The terminal moment must be understood through both relations.

For our Yoga system, eight, nine, and ten are meaningful
only when their members and conditions travel with the count.
Accomplished work, acquired Knowledge of non-arising, transition
to immovability, and remaining cultivation are distinct
determinations. A single scale of “more knowledge” would
erase the very relations that explain the counts.

The closing status distinctions also matter. A practice
description cannot be detached from whether its practitioner
is a trainee, beyond training, or an ordinary person. Our
reconstruction must retain those conditions and preserve
unresolved source attachments as unresolved, rather than
manufacturing a complete-looking table from uncertain prose.
