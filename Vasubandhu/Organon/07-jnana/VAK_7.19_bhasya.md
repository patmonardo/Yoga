# VAK_7.19 Bhāṣya — Progressive Possession of Knowledge

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ekajñānānvito rāgī prathame 'nāsravakṣaṇe |
> dvitīye tribhir ūrdhvas tu caturṣv ekaikavṛddhimān || 7.19 ||

> The practitioner still attached possesses one knowledge in the first
> uncontaminated moment; in the second, three. Subsequently, at four
> moments, he gains one more at each.

New question: who possesses how many. Not the 7.17–7.18 object-tables.
Cultivation is 7.20.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_7.19.md`](./VAK_7.19.md).

**Witness.** Pradhan 405|07–17. Joined `rāgīprathame` unrestored. Voice:
Vasubandhu. `Ānvita` here is possession, not anvayajñāna.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> This is to be stated: who is possessed of how many knowledges?
>
> The ordinary person, first, is possessed of one: conventional knowledge.
> The detached, however, of other-mind knowledge as well.
>
> One not detached from the desire domain, at the acceptance of
> dharma-knowledge concerning suffering, is possessed of conventional
> knowledge alone.
>
> At suffering-dharma-knowledge, of three: conventional, dharma, and
> suffering knowledge.
>
> After that, at four moments, an increase of one knowledge each:
> anvaya-knowledge increases at suffering-anvaya-knowledge; origination-,
> cessation-, and Path-knowledge increase at their dharma-knowledge moments.
> At Path-dharma-knowledge he is possessed of seven.
>
> The detached is to be known throughout as possessed of one more:
> other-mind knowledge.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
ordinary baseline: conventional
    + paracitta if already detached
Seeing, not previously detached:
    moment 1 kṣānti → still 1 (conventional); acceptance is not jñāna
    moment 2 suffering-dharma-knowledge → 3
    then +1 at four named moments → 7 at Path-dharma-knowledge
detached case: +paracitta at every stage (possession, not Seeing-occurrence)
```

## 5. Possession ≠ Present Act ≠ Cultivation

One present cognition at moment 2 instantiates two new categories (dharma
and suffering). Conventional remains possessed without being the present
Path-cognition. 7.01 still excludes uncontaminated acceptances from jñāna.

Paracitta possessed by the detached practitioner does not occur in Seeing
(7.06). Possession ≠ actualization.

Moment numbers 4, 6, 10, 14 are from the established sixteen-moment sequence,
not printed in this prose.

## 6. The Seven (Non-Detached, by Path-Dharma-Knowledge)

Conventional, dharma, anvaya, suffering, origination, cessation, Path.
Not kṣaya, not anutpāda, not (in this series) paracitta.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `rāgī` = kāmāvītarāga;
- first moment = duḥkhadharmajñānakṣānti;
- `tribhiḥ` named;
- four increases named;
- detached +1 is commentary contrast, not a second verse subject.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Taking as non-self is method. In 7.18 that method is a conventional act
with an own-complex limit. Our Dialectic uses the method; the Dialectic
itself is Ideal — Idea, not a Concept-triad running the sixteen moments.
Possession-sequence is Technē: the ārya path accumulating determinations.
Do not rewrite kṣānti as the Idea arriving. Uncontaminated entry ≠ knowledge
possessed. Sublation = nirodha stays parked.
