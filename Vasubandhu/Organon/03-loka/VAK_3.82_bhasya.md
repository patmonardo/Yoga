# VAK_3.82 Bhāṣya — Lifespans in the Six Hot Hells

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
kāmadevāyuṣā tulyā ahorātrā yathākramam /
saṃjīvādiṣu ṣaṭsv āyus tais teṣāṃ kāmadevavat // 3.82 //
```

> In the six hells beginning with Saṃjīva, day-and-nights correspond in order
> to the lifespans of the desire-realm devas; by those units, the hell-beings'
> lifespans follow the same numerical counts.

## Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Durgatā idānīṃ vaktavyāḥ. Tatra tāvat:

Kāmadevāyuṣā tulyā ahorātrā yathākramam /
Saṃjīvādiṣu ṣaṭsu.

Yāvat ṣaṇṇāṃ kāmāvacarāṇāṃ devanikāyānām āyur uktaṃ, tena tulyā
ahorātrāḥ ṣaṭsu narakeṣu yathākramaṃ veditavyāḥ:

Saṃjīve, Kālasūtre, Saṃghāte, Raurave, Mahāraurave, Tāpane ca.

Āyus tais teṣāṃ kāmadevavat.

Tair idānīṃ svair ahorātraiḥ teṣāṃ yathā ṣaṇṇāṃ kāmāvacarāṇāṃ devānām āyus
tathaiva yathākramaṃ veditavyam.

Kathaṃ kṛtvā?

Yad dhi Cāturmahārājakāyikānām āyuḥpramāṇaṃ, tat Saṃjīvane mahānarake ekaṃ
rātrindivam.

Tena yāvad dvādaśamāsakena saṃvatsareṇa tatratyāni pañca varṣaśatāny āyuḥ.

Yat Trāyastriṃśānām āyuḥpramāṇaṃ, tat Kālasūtre mahānarake ekaṃ rātrindivam.

Tena rātrindivena tasmin varṣasahasrāṇy āyuḥpramāṇam.

Evam anyeṣv api yathāyogaṃ yojyaṃ, yāvat Paranirmitavaśavartinām
āyuḥpramāṇaṃ tattulyenāhorātreṇa Tāpane ṣoḍaśa varṣasahasrāṇy
āyuḥpramāṇam.
```

Spacing and sandhi have been regularized. In the hell list, `Tāpane` is the
locative name of the sixth hell, Tāpana; the source's joined spelling is
preserved in sense, not treated as a different class.

## Continuous Conventional Translation

Now the unfortunate destinies are to be explained. First, in the six hells
beginning with Saṃjīva, day-and-night units correspond in order to the
lifespans of the six classes of desire-realm devas.

Thus, the lifespan of a Cāturmahārājika deva is one day-and-night in the
great hell Saṃjīva. By that day-and-night, with twelve months making a year,
the lifespan there is five hundred years. The lifespan of a Trāyastriṃśa
deva is one day-and-night in the great hell Kālasūtra; by that day-and-night,
the lifespan there is one thousand years. The same calculation applies
accordingly to the others: the lifespan of a Paranirmitavaśavartin is one
day-and-night in Tāpana, where the lifespan is sixteen thousand years.

## Natural Movement

The Bhāṣya pairs the first six hot hells, in order, with the six
desire-realm deva classes. It then explains `kāmadevavat` through examples:
the matched deva lifespan becomes one hell day-and-night, and the hell
lifespan, counted in years built from that unit, takes the same numerical
year-count as the corresponding deva lifespan.

## Ordered Correspondence

| Desire-realm deva | Corresponding hell | One hell day-and-night equals | Hell lifespan |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Cāturmahārājika | Saṃjīva | deva lifespan | 500 years |
| Trāyastriṃśa | Kālasūtra | deva lifespan | 1,000 years |
| Yāma | Saṃghāta | deva lifespan | 2,000 years |
| Tuṣita | Raurava | deva lifespan | 4,000 years |
| Nirmāṇarati | Mahāraurava | deva lifespan | 8,000 years |
| Paranirmitavaśavartin | Tāpana | deva lifespan | 16,000 years |

There are two related but distinct determinations:

1. A corresponding deva's complete lifespan sets the magnitude of one hell
   day-and-night.
2. The hell's lifespan has the same number of years as that deva class's
   lifespan, now counted within the hell calendar.

The temporal units are therefore not simply identical across the two realms;
the deva duration is used to define a hell day-and-night.

## Doctrinal Note

The Bhāṣya presents the six hells as having an ordered temporal structure
correlated with the six desire-realm deva classes. A deva lifespan is
compressed into one hell day-and-night; the same numerical lifespan count is
then assigned to the hell-years. Keep the day-unit conversion distinct from
the matching lifespan count.

## Organon Contact Point

Light contact: a temporal unit in one conditioned domain can define the
day-and-night scale of another. This is a project-level observation, not
