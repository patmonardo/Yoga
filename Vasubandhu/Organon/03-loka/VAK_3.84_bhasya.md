# VAK_3.84 Bhāṣya — The Sesame Comparison and Cold-Hell Lifespans

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> vāhād varṣaśatenaika-tiloddhāra-kṣayāyuṣaḥ /
> arbudād viṃśatiguṇa-prativṛddhyāyuṣaḥ pare // 3.84 //

> The lifespan in Arbuda is indicated by a vāha emptied one sesame seed every
> hundred years; each later cold-naraka lifespan is twenty times the preceding
> one.

The source uses the spelling `arvuda`; the normalized `arbuda` follows the
local kārikā study and is the form used below.

The twenty-khārī specification, sealed-container description, strict
comparative inequality, and cited examples of the recurrence are Bhāṣya
determinations supplied through a saying attributed to the Buddha.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Śītanarakeṣv āyuṣaḥ kiṃ pramāṇam?

Vāhād varṣaśatenaika-tiloddhāra-kṣayāyuṣaḥ /
Arbudād viṃśatiguṇa-prativṛddhyāyuṣaḥ pare.

Upamānamātreṇa teṣv āyur ākhyātaṃ Bhagavatā:

“Tadyathā, bhikṣavaḥ, iha syād viṃśatikhārīko Māgadhakas tilavāhaḥ
pūrṇas tilānāṃ cūḍikāvaddhaḥ.

Tataḥ kaścid eva varṣaśatasyātyayād ekaṃ tilam apanayate.

Kṣiprataraṃ, bhikṣavaḥ, sa viṃśatikhārīko Māgadhakas tilavāho 'nena
upakrameṇa parikṣayaṃ paryādānaṃ gacchet;

na tv evāham Arbudopapannānām āyuṣaḥ paryantaṃ vadāmi.

Yathā khalu, bhikṣavo, viṃśatir Arbudā evam eko Nirarbudaḥ;
vistareṇa yathā khalu, bhikṣavo, viṃśatiḥ Padmā evam eko Mahāpadmaḥ” iti.
```

Spacing and sandhi are regularized. The source's `cūḍikāvaddhaḥ` is rendered
as tied or sealed. The final `iti` introduces an abbreviated citation, whose
intervening sequence is not reproduced in this witness.

## 3. Continuous Translation
What is the measure of lifespan in the cold hells?

The Blessed One has explained their lifespan by comparison alone: “Suppose,
monks, there were here a Magadhan sesame-load with a capacity of twenty
khārīs, full of sesame seeds and tied shut. Someone removes one sesame seed
from it only after the passage of each hundred years. That twenty-khārī
Magadhan sesame-load would be depleted and come to an end more quickly by
this procedure. But I do not say that the lifespan of beings born in Arbuda
would thereby have reached its limit.

“Just as twenty Arbudas make one Nirarbuda … and, in the detailed sequence,
just as twenty Padmas make one Mahāpadma.”

The later cold-hell lifespans increase successively twentyfold.

## 4. Natural Movement

Vasubandhu asks how the cold-hell lifespans are measured. The citation gives
an immense but finite depletion process: remove one sesame seed from a
twenty-khārī store every hundred years. The store would run out before an
Arbuda lifespan ends, so it is an analogy for magnitude, not an equation
with Arbuda's duration. The verse then gives a separate, exact relation:
each later lifespan is twenty times the preceding one. The quotation
illustrates that succession with Arbuda/Nirarbuda and Padma/Mahāpadma.

## 5. Analogy and Succession

```text
analogy for Arbuda:
    finite sesame-load depletion
    < Arbuda lifespan

later cold hells:
    each successive lifespan = 20 × the preceding lifespan
```

The Bhāṣya calls the first account `upamānamātra`, “comparison alone,” and
the Buddha's quotation explicitly denies that sesame depletion marks the
Arbuda lifespan's endpoint. The twentyfold relation is distinct: it orders
the later stations by a stated multiplier.

## 6. Doctrinal Note

The passage separates an illustrative measure from an exact ratio. Arbuda's
duration is made imaginable through a process that itself has an endpoint,
but that endpoint falls short of Arbuda's. Beyond Arbuda, the source gives a
successive twentyfold lifespan relation. It does not require us to fill out
the ellipsis in the cited series here.

## 7. Interpretation

Light contact: an analogy can indicate magnitude without asserting numerical
identity, while a separate rule specifies exact relations within an ordered
series. This is a project-level observation, not Vasubandhu's technical
terminology.
