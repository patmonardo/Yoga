# VAK_3.27 — The Recursion Rule of the Members of Becoming

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> क्लेशात् क्लेशः क्रिया चैव ततो वस्तु ततः पुनः ।
>
> वस्तु क्लेशाश्च जायन्ते भवाङ्गानामयं नयः ॥ ३.२७ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> kleśāt kleśaḥ kriyā caiva tato vastu tataḥ punaḥ /
>
> vastukleśāś ca jāyante bhavāṅgānām ayaṃ nayaḥ // 3.27 //

The second line is construed as `tataḥ punaḥ vastu kleśāḥ ca jāyante`:
from Vastu in its support-function, further Vastu and afflictions arise
again.

The Bhāṣya identifies `kriyā` functionally with karma and expands the compact
verse into five permitted transition-types.

## 4. Grammar

The first half states two productions from affliction and the production from
action:

```text
kleśāt
    kleśaḥ [jāyate]
    kriyā ca eva [jāyate]

tataḥ [kriyātaḥ]
    vastu [jāyate]
```

> From affliction arise both affliction and activity; from that activity
> arises Vastu in its resultant-support function.

The second half closes the recurrence:

```text
tataḥ punaḥ [vastunaḥ]
    vastu
    kleśāḥ ca
    jāyante
```

> From that Vastu-support again arise further Vastu and afflictions.

The concluding nominal sentence identifies the rule:

```text
bhava-aṅgānām
    ayam nayaḥ
```

> This is the governing rule of the members of becoming.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> From affliction arise both affliction and activity; from that arises the
> Vastu in its support-function. From that again arise further Vastu and
> afflictions.
> This is the governing rule of the members of becoming.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Affliction can generate another affliction or karmic action; karmic action
> generates Vastu in its support-function; that Vastu develops into further
> Vastu and also supports renewed affliction. This
> recursive causal grammar governs the twelve members and reconnects their
> apparent beginning and end without requiring any additional member.

The five transition-types, their concrete member-pairs, and the response to
the boundary objection are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The list has become a generative law. Affliction can reproduce its own
> functional kind or pass into projective action; action produces an embodied
> support; the support develops immanently and becomes the condition of
> renewed affliction. The World System therefore regenerates the conditions
> of its own recurrence. Its apparent first and final members are boundaries
> of exposition, not absolute boundaries of the operating law.

The five transitions and their principal examples are:

```text
kleśa → kleśa
    tṛṣṇā → upādāna

kleśa → karma
    avidyā → saṃskāra
    upādāna → bhava

karma → vastu
    saṃskāra → vijñāna
    bhava → jāti

vastu → vastu
    vijñāna → nāmarūpa → ṣaḍāyatana → sparśa → vedanā
    jāti → jarāmaraṇa

vastu → kleśa
    vedanā → tṛṣṇā
```

## 8. Logical Determination

### The boundary objection

Read only as a finite list:

```text
avidyā
    has no cause explicitly enumerated before it

jarāmaraṇa
    has no result explicitly enumerated after it
```

This seems to entail either a first beginning and final end or the need to add
further members. But each added member would demand another ground:

```text
add a thirteenth member
    → it requires a fourteenth
    → indefinite supplementation
```

Vasubandhu rejects supplementation. The answer is not a longer list but a
higher-order rule governing the functional types already present.

### The conditional grammar

In hypothetical form:

```text
If a kleśa is operative,
then either renewed kleśa or karma may arise.

If karma becomes projectively efficacious,
then a corresponding vastu arises.

If a vastu has arisen,
then further vastu-development or renewed kleśa may arise.
```

Formally:

```text
K → K
K → A
A → V
V → V
V → K
```

where:

```text
K = kleśa
A = karmic activity
V = Vastu (Thing/basis in its support-function)
```

These rules explain every transition required for recurrence. The listed
endpoints are reconnectible because they belong to functional classes whose
production rules extend beyond the boundaries of the displayed sequence.

### Enumeration becomes principle

```text
3.20–3.24
    concrete twelve-stage data

3.25
    principle of stage-naming and View selection

3.26
    functional classes and inference from the middle

3.27
    generative relations among the classes
```

Vasubandhu has moved from components to classification to executable rule.
The `naya` explains more than the enumeration because it can generate the
relations of the enumeration and reconnect its apparent limits.

## 9. Interpretive Note

The Organon's project-level synthesis reads the Bhāṣya's transitions as a
recursive Ground–Condition pattern: affliction conditions karma, karma
conditions Vastu, and Vastu supports renewed affliction or further Vastu.
Where the Path can exhaust this recurrence is a further question, not settled
by this verse.
