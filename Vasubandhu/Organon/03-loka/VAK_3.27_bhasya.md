# VAK_3.27 Bhāṣya — The Recursion Rule of the Members of Becoming

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kleśāt kleśaḥ kriyā caiva tato vastu tataḥ punaḥ /
> vastukleśāś ca jāyante bhavāṅgānām ayaṃ nayaḥ // 3.27 //

> From affliction arise affliction and karmic action; from action, Vastu; from
> Vastu again arise Vastu and afflictions. This is the governing rule of the
> members of becoming.

The Bhāṣya presents this rule in answer to an objection about the apparent
endpoints of the twelve-member list. Ignorance need not be assigned an
additional thirteenth cause, nor aging-and-death an additional result: the
functional transitions among affliction, karma, and Vastu-support already
reconnect the sequence.

The five transition-types, their concrete member-pairs, and the response to
the boundary objection are Bhāṣya determinations.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation
An objection arises. If *samutpāda* consists of precisely twelve members,
then, because no cause of Ignorance is taught, Saṃsāra would have a
beginning; and because no result of Aging-and-Death is taught, it would have
an end. Alternatively, another member would have to be added. But that member
would in turn require another, producing an infinite regress.

No additional member is to be counted, because the Blessed One has shown the
following here:

> From affliction arise both affliction and activity; from that activity,
> Vastu in its support-function. From that Vastu again arise further Vastu
> and afflictions. This is the governing rule of the members of becoming.

Affliction arises from affliction: Appropriation arises from Craving. Karma
arises from affliction: Becoming arises from Appropriation, and Formations
from Ignorance. Vastu arises from karma: Discriminative Cognition arises from
Formations, and Birth from Becoming. Vastu arises from Vastu: Name-and-Form
arises from Discriminative Cognition, and so on through Feeling from Contact;
Aging-and-Death arises from Birth. Affliction arises from Vastu: Craving
arises from Feeling.

Because this rule of the members of becoming is established, it is thereby
made known that Ignorance too, being of the nature of affliction, arises from
Vastu or from affliction. And under the influence of Feeling, affliction
arises again from the Aging-and-Death Vastu. Nothing further therefore needs
to be added here. For scripture says: “Thus the arising of this whole great
Suffering Base occurs.” Otherwise, what explanatory force would that
statement possess?

## 4. Objection, Reply, and Scriptural Confirmation

The Bhāṣya stages a genuine boundary objection:

```text
first listed member has no listed cause
    → saṃsāra appears to begin

last listed member has no listed result
    → saṃsāra appears to end

add further members
    → each addition requires another
    → infinite regress
```

Vasubandhu rejects additional enumeration. The verse supplies a higher-order
rule connecting the functional classes already present. A final scriptural formula confirms that the rule explains the arising of
the complete Suffering Base, not merely an isolated twelve-member episode.

## 5. Five Licensed Transition-Types

The prose expands the compact verse into five kinds of causal transition:

```text
affliction → affliction
    tṛṣṇā → upādāna

affliction → karma
    avidyā → saṃskāra
    upādāna → bhava

karma → Vastu
    saṃskāra → vijñāna
    bhava → jāti

Vastu → Vastu
    vijñāna → nāmarūpa → ṣaḍāyatana → sparśa → vedanā
    jāti → jarāmaraṇa

Vastu → affliction
    vedanā → tṛṣṇā
```

The system includes both cross-class production and propagation within one
class. It is therefore more exact than the simplified cycle “affliction
produces karma, karma produces result, result produces affliction.”

## 6. Why No Thirteenth Member Is Needed

The apparent first and last positions belong only to the displayed
enumeration. Once their functional types are known, their unstated relations
are already determined:

```text
avidyā is an affliction
    → under the rule, it may arise from Vastu or prior affliction

jarāmaraṇa is a resultant Vastu
    → under the rule, it conditions renewed affliction
```

Adding another named member would not explain recurrence more adequately; it
would merely shift the same demand outward and generate regress. The `naya`
does the explanatory work that an indefinitely lengthened list cannot do.

## 7. Enumeration Becomes Generative Rule

The movement across the preceding verses is now complete:

```text
VAK 3.20–3.24
    enumerate and define twelve stages

VAK 3.25
    explains stage-naming and pedagogical selection

VAK 3.26
    groups members as affliction, karma, and Vastu/result

VAK 3.27
    states the permitted productions among those classes
```

`Naya` is therefore well rendered as a governing rule here. It
does not designate an additional cause. It specifies how members of given
functional kinds can generate members of the same or another kind.

## 8. The Recurrence of the Great Suffering Base

The cited formula concerns the arising of the “whole great Suffering Base.”
Its force depends upon recursion:

```text
resultant experience
    → renewed affliction
    → renewed karma
    → renewed Vastu-field
```

If aging-and-death simply terminated without further conditioning, or if
ignorance appeared without causal placement, the formula would not explain
the completeness and recurrence of the suffering-series. The rule supplies
both continuity and causal closure without positing an enduring operator.

This closure is not logical necessity in the sense that liberation is
impossible. VAK 3.19 has already stated that recurrence ends when its causes
are exhausted. The present rule describes how the wheel operates while its
conditions remain effective.

## 9. Interpretation

The passage moves from classified members to a generative causal grammar:

```text
typed member
    + permitted transition
    → next typed member
```

For the Organon, this is a more precise prototype than a merely circular
diagram. An Ocular representation would need both nodes and licensed
relations: affliction can produce affliction or karma; Vastu can produce
further Vastu or affliction. The type alone does not determine a unique next
state, but it constrains the possible transitions.

An outflow can now be described more exactly. A projection is not only a
label assigned to an appearance; through action it can generate resultant
Vastu, which can support further appearance, Feeling, and
appropriation. The output becomes a new condition of operation.

This Organon formulation remains a reconstruction. Vasubandhu's source-level
achievement is the recursive sufficiency of the twelve-member account: causal
continuity is explained through the rule of the members themselves, without
an additional member or an inner transmigrating agent.

Read with VAK 3.25–3.26, this closes the transcendental time schema: the
displayed beginning and end are not absolute limits, because the transition
rule reconnects prior cause, present Vastu/result, renewed cause, and future
result. This schema is the project's synthesis; the Bhāṣya's own claim is
the recursive sufficiency of its member-transition rule.
