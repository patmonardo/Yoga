# VAK_3.03

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> आरूप्यधातुरस्थानः उपपत्त्या चतुर्विधः ।
>
> निकायं जीवितं चात्र निश्रिता चित्तसन्ततिः ॥ ३.३ ॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> ārūpyadhāturasthānaḥ upapattyā caturvidhaḥ /
>
> nikāyaṃ jīvitaṃ cātra niśritā cittasantatiḥ // 3.3 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

```text
ārūpya-dhātuḥ + asthānaḥ → ārūpya-dhātur asthānaḥ
upapattyā                  → upapattyā
catur-vidhaḥ               → catur-vidhaḥ
nikāyam                    → nikāyam
jīvitam                    → jīvitam
ca + atra                  → cātra
niśritā                    → niśritā
citta-santatiḥ             → citta-santatiḥ
```

| Form | Morphology | Lexical force here |
|---|---|---|
| ārūpya-dhātuḥ | nominative masculine singular compound | formless-domain |
| asthānaḥ | nominative masculine singular adjective, `a-` + `sthāna` | without station |
| upapattyā | instrumental feminine singular of `upapatti` | according to rebirth / by differentiation of rebirth |
| catur-vidhaḥ | nominative masculine singular adjective | fourfold, of four kinds |
| nikāyam | accusative masculine singular | class; interpreted by the Bhāṣya as common class-character |
| jīvitam | accusative neuter singular | life; interpreted by the Bhāṣya as life-faculty |
| atra | locative adverb | here, in this formless-domain |
| niśritā | nominative feminine singular past participle of `ni-√śri` | resting upon, dependent upon |
| citta-santatiḥ | nominative feminine singular compound | continuity of consciousness |

The sandhi `ārūpyadhātuḥ + asthānaḥ → ārūpyadhātur asthānaḥ` joins the
subject and its predicate. `Cātra` resolves as `ca + atra`.

## 4. Grammar

The first half negates station while retaining differentiation:

```text
ārūpya-dhātuḥ
    asthānaḥ
    upapattyā catur-vidhaḥ
```

`Upapattyā` is instrumental and states the basis of the fourfoldness. The
formless-domain is not fourfold through four spatial stations; it is fourfold
according to modes of rebirth.

The second half gives a dependency structure:

```text
citta-santatiḥ        subject
    atra              domain-locator
    nikāyam jīvitam ca
                      objects of the dependency relation
    niśritā            predicate
```

The Bhāṣya specifies the compressed terms as `nikāyasabhāga` and
`jīvitendriya`: common class-character and life-faculty. That specification is
the Ābhidhārmika interpretation subsequently challenged in the commentary; it
must not be mistaken for an uncontested expansion of the verse.

## 5. Translation

### Close syntactic construe

> The formless-domain has no station; it is fourfold according to rebirth.
> Here the continuity of consciousness rests upon class and life.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> The formless-domain has no spatial station, yet it is fourfold according to
> rebirth. In this domain, the continuity of consciousness is said to depend
> upon the common class-character and the life-faculty.

“Spatial” reflects the Bhāṣya's explanation `adeśastha`, not a separate word
in the Kārikā. “Common class-character” and “life-faculty” render the
Bhāṣya's `nikāyasabhāga` and `jīvitendriya`. The phrase “is said to” preserves
the fact that the commentary subjects this support-model to criticism.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> At its most abstract limit, the World System relinquishes spatial station
> without relinquishing conditioned differentiation. The formless-domain is
> articulated through four modes of rebirth, while continuity of consciousness
> remains dependent. Form and location disappear; world-order, genesis, life,
> and continuity do not.

This is an Organon rendering. It does not translate `ārūpya` as Absolute or
identify the formless-domain with liberation.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

| Sanskrit | Controlled rendering | Determination in this unit |
|---|---|---|
| ārūpyadhātu | formless-domain | the domain without form-stations but still differentiated by conditioned rebirth |
| asthāna | without station | direct negation of the `sthāna` structure used for rūpadhātu |
| adeśastha | not spatially situated | Bhāṣya clarification of `asthāna`; also predicated there of other non-spatial dharmas |
| upapatti | rebirth | mode of conditioned arising that differentiates the formless-domain |
| nikāya | class | Kārikā term expanded by the Bhāṣya as `nikāyasabhāga` |
| nikāyasabhāga | common class-character | proposed non-material support in the Ābhidhārmika account |
| jīvita | life | Kārikā term expanded by the Bhāṣya as `jīvitendriya` |
| jīvitendriya | life-faculty | second proposed support of consciousness-continuity |
| citta-santati | continuity of consciousness | conditioned succession of `citta` |
| niśrita | dependent upon | specifies the relevant support relation |
| samāpattiviśeṣaja | arisen from a distinctive attainment | Ābhidhārmika explanation of the strength of the proposed supports |
| ākṣepahetu | projecting cause | Sautrāntika genetic explanation of a continuity arising with or without form |

The Bhāṣya names the four formless modes of rebirth:

```text
ākāśānantyāyatana
    sphere of the infinitude of space

vijñānānantyāyatana
    sphere of the infinitude of differentiated cognition

ākiṃcanyāyatana
    sphere of nothingness

naivasaṃjñānāsaṃjñāyatana
    sphere of neither perception nor non-perception
```

These names are Bhāṣya enumeration. Their order is not a spatial hierarchy
produced by higher and lower locations.

## 8. Logical Determination

The opening sequence now yields three progressively deeper determinations of
the World System:

```text
VAK 3.01 — kāmadhātu
    World differentiated through destinies and inhabited locations

VAK 3.02 — rūpadhātu
    World differentiated through stations and dhyāna-grounds

VAK 3.03 — ārūpyadhātu
    station negated
    differentiation retained through rebirth
    consciousness-continuity retained through dependence
```

The decisive inference is:

```text
spatial station is negated
    but World System persists

therefore
    World System ≠ merely spatial or physical container
```

The Bhāṣya then converts classification into a problem of ground:

```text
if consciousness-continuity no longer depends upon form,
what sustains its occurrence?
```

Two models emerge.

```text
Ābhidhārmika support-model
    citta-santati
        depends upon
            nikāyasabhāga ↔ jīvitendriya

Sautrāntika genetic model
    projecting cause not free from craving for form
        → continuity arises with form and depends upon form

    projecting cause free from craving for form
        → continuity arises without form
```

The Sautrāntika explanation replaces a questionable reciprocal substrate with
the causal orientation that generates the continuity. The explanatory movement
is from static support to genesis.

## 9. Interpretive Note

VAK 3.03 prevents the World System from being reduced to the physical world.
The negation of `sthāna` does not terminate `loka`. An ordered continuum of
conditioned existence remains, differentiated by rebirth and marked by life
and consciousness-continuity.

This makes ārūpyadhātu the strongest possible abstraction internal to the
World System—not the Absolute:

```text
formlessness
    still has upapatti
    still has conditioned continuity
    still has class-character and life-faculty in the Ābhidhārmika account
    still has a projecting cause in the Sautrāntika account

therefore
    formlessness remains World
```

The large Organon architecture can now be stated more rigorously:

```text
Tāmasic World System
    kāmadhātu
        stationed desire-world
    rūpadhātu
        stationed form-world
    ārūpyadhātu
        non-stationed conditioned continuity

Pāramārthika / Pure Principles
    is not the final formless state
    is not another rebirth-mode
    is not the highest location
    comprehends and sublates the complete World System
```

This is a project-level synthesis, not Vasubandhu's assertion. Its textual
discipline lies in recognizing exactly what the formless-domain fails to
overcome: conditioned arising and continuity. The pāramārthika can enclose the
entire World System precisely because it is not one determination within the
system's opposition of form and formlessness.

The most compact result is:

```text
Loka survives the negation of Location.

The World System is not fundamentally extension;
it is the determinate field of conditioned continuity.
```
