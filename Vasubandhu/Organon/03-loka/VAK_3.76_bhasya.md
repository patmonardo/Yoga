# VAK_3.76 Bhāṣya — Deva Body-Measures

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
pādavṛddhyā tanur yāvat sārdhakrośo divaukasām /
kāmināṃ rūpiṇāṃ tv ādau yojanārdhaṃ tataḥ param // 3.76 //
```

> The bodies of the desire-realm devas increase by quarter-krośas, up to one
> and a half krośas. But among the form-realm devas, the first measure is half
> a yojana; beyond that, [the measure increases].

## Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Pādavṛddhyā tanur yāvat sārdhakrośo divaukasām kāminām.

Pādaḥ krośasya caturtho bhāgaḥ.

Tanmātraṃ śarīraṃ Cāturmahārājakāyikānāṃ; dvau pādau Trāyastriṃśānām;
trayo Yāmānām; catvāras Tuṣitānām; pañca Nirmāṇaratīnām; adhyardhaḥ
krośaḥ Paranirmitavaśavartinām.

Rūpiṇāṃ tv ādau yojanārdham.

Rūpiṇāṃ devānāṃ prathame sthāne Brahmakāyikānām ardhayojanamātraṃ śarīram.

Tataḥ param.
```

The witness's word spacing and sandhi have been regularized. The prose
identifies `pāda` as one quarter of a krośa and names all six desire-realm
classes. `Tataḥ param`, “beyond that,” closes the verse's explanation while
pointing forward; the further form-realm measures belong to the next unit.

## Continuous Conventional Translation

Among the desire-realm devas, bodily measure increases by quarter-krośas, up
to one and a half krośas. A quarter is one fourth of a krośa. The body of a
Cāturmahārājakāyika is one such quarter; that of a Trāyastriṃśa is two
quarters; of a Yāma, three; of a Tuṣita, four; of a Nirmāṇarati, five; and
that of a Paranirmitavaśavartin is one and a half krośas.

But among the form-realm devas, at the first station—that of the
Brahmakāyikas—the body measures half a yojana. Beyond that, [the measure
increases].

## Natural Movement

The Bhāṣya first defines the increment and assigns it to the six desire-realm
deva classes in ascending order. It then changes domain: the first station
among the form-realm devas is that of the Brahmakāyikas, whose bodily measure
is half a yojana. The closing `tataḥ param` signals continuation without
giving the subsequent values here.

## Two Measurement Sequences

| Desire-realm class | Body measure |
|---|---:|
| Cāturmahārājakāyika | ¼ krośa |
| Trāyastriṃśa | ½ krośa |
| Yāma | ¾ krośa |
| Tuṣita | 1 krośa |
| Nirmāṇarati | 1¼ krośas |
| Paranirmitavaśavartin | 1½ krośas |

The measure rises by one quarter-krośa at each step. With the first form-realm
station, the Bhāṣya starts a distinct measure: half a yojana for the
Brahmakāyikas. It does not give a conversion between krośa and yojana here.

## Doctrinal Note

The passage specifies bodily magnitude by cosmological class and station.
Within the six desire-realm deva classes the increase is a fixed additive
step; at the transition to the form realm, the Bhāṣya names a new starting
measure and unit. The further progression is deferred, not supplied by
inference.

## Organon Contact Point

Light contact: the conditioned world-field differentiates embodied measures
by domain and station. This is a project-level observation, not Vasubandhu's
technical terminology.
