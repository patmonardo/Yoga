# VAK_3.77 Bhāṣya — The Form-Realm Measure Series

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
ardhārdhavṛddhir ūrdhvaṃ tu parīttābhebhya āśrayaḥ /
dviguṇadviguṇā hitvānabhrakebhyas triyojanam // 3.77 //
```

> Above, the bodily support increases by successive half-yojana steps; above
> the Parīttābhas it is doubled successively, with three yojanas subtracted at
> the Anabhraka level.

## Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Ardhārdhavṛddhiḥ.

Triṣu sthāneṣu Brahmapurohitānāṃ yojanam, Mahābrahmaṇo 'dhyardham,
Parīttābhānāṃ dve yojane.

Ūrdhvaṃ tu Parīttābhebhya āśrayaḥ; dviguṇadviguṇā hitvā 'nabhrakebhyaś
triyojanam.

Apramāṇābhānāṃ catvāri yojanāni; Ābhāsvarāṇām aṣṭau.

Evaṃ dviguṇavṛddhyā yāvac Chubhakṛtsnānāṃ catuḥṣaṣṭiḥ.

Anabhrakāṇāṃ tato yojanatrayeṇa hīnaṃ dviguṇaṃ pañcaviṃśatiyojanaśatam.

Tasmāt pareṇa punaḥ Puṇyaprasavānāṃ dviguṇaṃ dviguṇaṃ yāvad Akaniṣṭhānāṃ
ṣoḍaśayojanasahasrāṇi śarīram.
```

Spacing and sandhi have been regularized. The verse's compressed `hitvā`
clause is read with the prose: the expected doubled measure is reduced by
three yojanas for the Anabhrakas.

## Continuous Conventional Translation

The measure increases by successive half-yojana steps. At the next three
stations, the Brahmapurohitas measure one yojana, Mahābrahman one and a half,
and the Parīttābhas two yojanas.

Above the Parīttābhas, the bodily support is successively doubled. The
Apramāṇābhas measure four yojanas and the Ābhāsvaras eight; continuing in
this way by doubling, the measure reaches sixty-four yojanas among the
Śubhakṛtsnas.

For the Anabhrakas, the doubled value, reduced by three yojanas, is one
hundred twenty-five yojanas. Beyond them, doubling resumes, so that the body
of the Akaniṣṭhas measures sixteen thousand yojanas.

## Natural Movement

This passage completes the form-realm body-measure sequence begun in 3.76.
The first rule adds half a yojana at each of three stations above the
Brahmakāyikas. The rule then changes to successive doubling, explicitly
illustrated by the four- and eight-yojana measures and carried to sixty-four
at Śubhakṛtsna. The Anabhrakas receive an exceptional value of 125; from the
level beyond them, doubling resumes through the stated Akaniṣṭha endpoint.

## The Measures and Their Status

| Station or group | Measure | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Brahmakāyika | ½ yojana | Stated in 3.76 |
| Brahmapurohita | 1 yojana | Explicit here |
| Mahābrahman | 1½ yojanas | Explicit here |
| Parīttābha | 2 yojanas | Explicit here |
| Apramāṇābha | 4 yojanas | Explicit here |
| Ābhāsvara | 8 yojanas | Explicit here |
| Śubhakṛtsna | 64 yojanas | Explicit endpoint of doubling |
| Anabhraka | 125 yojanas | Doubled value less 3 yojanas |
| Akaniṣṭha | 16,000 yojanas | Explicit endpoint after doubling resumes |

The Bhāṣya names the intervening stations in its received cosmological
sequence, but gives no separate numerical line for every class between
Ābhāsvara and Śubhakṛtsna, or between Anabhraka and Akaniṣṭha. Any such
intermediate values are consequences of the stated doubling rule, not
additional numbers explicitly listed in this passage.

## Doctrinal Note

The `āśraya` here is the measurable bodily support at a form-realm station.
Its measure follows two rules in succession—addition by half-yojana steps,
then doubling—with a specified Anabhraka exception. The exception is not a
break in the exposition: the text states that doubling resumes beyond it.

## Organon Contact Point

Light contact: the measure-series is rule-governed but includes a locally
specified exception. This is a project-level observation, not Vasubandhu's
technical terminology.
