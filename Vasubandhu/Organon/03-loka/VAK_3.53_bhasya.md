# VAK_3.53 Bhāṣya — Jambūdvīpa and the Vajra Seat

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

```iast
lakṣatrayaṃ sahasrāṇi viṃśatir dve ca tatra tu /
jambūdvīpo dvisāhasras tripārśvaḥ śakaṭākṛtiḥ // 3.53 //
```

> The outer ocean is three hundred-thousand plus twenty-two thousand yojanas
> wide. In it, Jambūdvīpa has three sides of two thousand yojanas and is
> shaped like a cart.

The Bhāṣya locates the four continents on the four sides of Sumeru and adds a
decisive soteriological determination: at the center of Jambūdvīpa, upon the
golden earth, the Vajra Seat uniquely supports the Bodhisattva's diamond-like
samādhi.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Sa khalu vistāreṇa yojanānāṃ

lakṣatrayaṃ sahasrāṇi viṃśatir dve ca.

Tatra catvāro dvīpāś caturṣu Sumerupārśveṣu. Tatra tu

Jambūdvīpo dvisāhasras tripārśvaḥ śakaṭākṛtiḥ.

Sāhasrāṇi trīṇi pārśvāṇy asya; śakaṭasyevākṛtiḥ. Tasya ca madhye
kāñcanamayyāṃ pṛthivyāṃ vajrāsanam abhinirvṛttaṃ, yasmin niṣadya sarve
bodhisattvā vajropamaṃ samādhim utpādayanti. Na hi tam anya āśrayaḥ pradeśo
vā soḍhuṃ samarthaḥ.
```

The running prose reads `sāhasrāṇi trīṇi pārśvāṇy asya`, which appears
mechanically deficient: the Kārikā explicitly gives three sides as
`dvisāhasra`, two thousand each. The continuous Sanskrit preserves the prose
reading rather than silently inserting a missing word; the conventional
translation follows the clear metrical statement and marks the discrepancy
for critical review.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

That outer great ocean measures three hundred-thousand plus twenty-two
thousand yojanas in breadth. In it are four continents, situated on the four
sides of Sumeru.

Among them, Jambūdvīpa has three sides of two thousand yojanas and is shaped
like a cart. Its three sides have the stated measure, and its form is like
that of a cart.

At the center of Jambūdvīpa, upon the golden earth, the Vajra Seat comes into
formation. Seated upon it, all Bodhisattvas generate diamond-like samādhi. No
other support or place is capable of bearing that samādhi.

The short fourth side that completes the explanation of Jambūdvīpa's cart
shape belongs to VAK 3.54 and is not supplied in advance here.

## 4. Natural Movement and Voice

The commentary moves from a general field to a uniquely capable locus:

```text
outer great ocean
    → width of 322,000 yojanas

four sides of Sumeru
    → four continents

Jambūdvīpa
    → three measured sides
    → cart-like form

middle of Jambūdvīpa on golden earth
    → Vajra Seat
        → uniquely bears diamond-like samādhi
```

Vasubandhu speaks directly throughout. No objector or alternative school is
introduced in this unit.

The statement concerns all Bodhisattvas who generate this samādhi, not merely
one historical occupant of the seat. The uniqueness is expressed through
support-capacity: no other `āśraya` or `pradeśa` can bear the event.

## 5. Outer Ocean and Fourfold Placement

The opening completes the measure withheld at the end of VAK 3.52:

```text
3 lakṣas       = 300,000
20 thousands   =  20,000
2 thousands    =   2,000
                 -------
                  322,000 yojanas
```

`Vistāreṇa` specifies breadth. This is the transverse measure of the outer
ocean between Nimindhara and Cakravāḍa, not its shore-wise or longitudinal
extent.

The four continents are then correlated with the four sides of Sumeru. The
present sentence states the fourfold placement but does not explicitly name
all four directions or assign each continent to one. Those determinations
emerge through the continuing continent sequence and should not be silently
inserted into the literal translation.

The container-world is now no longer merely an enclosing ocean. It becomes a
field in which four differentiated regions can be situated relative to the
central mountain.

## 6. Jambūdvīpa's Measure and Form

The compound `dvisāhasras tripārśvaḥ` is distributive:

```text
three sides
    each measures 2,000 yojanas
```

It does not mean that all three sides together total two thousand. Nor does
it supply the fourth side's measure.

`Śakaṭākṛti`, cart-shaped or wagon-shaped, relates the measurements to an
overall morphology. The present unit announces that form, while VAK 3.54
explains it through the very short fourth side.

The damaged prose restatement must not be used to replace the clearer verse
with a 3,000-yojana reading. It likely repeats the three sides while omitting
or corrupting their two-thousand measure, but that remains an editorial
judgment requiring witness comparison.

Thus Jambūdvīpa is determined through several distinct features:

```text
container      outer ocean
relation       one of four continent-regions around Sumeru
side count     three sides presently measured
side length    2,000 yojanas each
shape          cart-like
```

## 7. The Vajra Seat as Unique Support

The Vajra Seat is a Bhāṣya addition, not wording found in the Kārikā. Its
address is nested:

```text
world-system
    → outer ocean
        → Jambūdvīpa
            → its middle
                → golden earth
                    → Vajra Seat
```

It `abhinirvṛtta`, comes into formation, upon the golden earth. The seat is
therefore a produced locus within the container-world, not an unconditioned
principle outside it.

Its singularity is functional:

```text
Vajra Seat
    → capable of bearing vajropama-samādhi

any other support or place
    → incapable of bearing it
```

`Vajropama-samādhi`, diamond-like concentration, is not merely a decorative
name for the location. It is the Path-operation whose required intensity or
weight explains the seat's unique support-capacity in this account.

The text says that Bodhisattvas sit there and generate the samādhi. It does
not say that geographical location alone produces realization independently
of the Bodhisattva's Path.

## 8. Geography and Path-Function

This passage gives a precise relation between `Loka` and `Mārga` without
collapsing them:

```text
produced physical locus
    provides a uniquely adequate support

Path-event
    is generated by the Bodhisattva seated there
```

The Vajra Seat is necessary as support within this doctrinal account, but it
is not sufficient as agent or practice. Conversely, the Path-event is not
presented as indifferent to every physical support.

Three axes must remain distinct:

| Axis | Determination |
|---|---|
| location | middle of Jambūdvīpa |
| material support | golden earth and Vajra Seat |
| path-function | bearing diamond-like samādhi |

This prevents both a merely symbolic reading of the place and a geographical
reduction of the contemplative event.

## 9. Philosophical and Organon Study

VAK 3.53 moves from spatial address to capacity:

```text
where a locus is
    does not yet determine
what that locus can support
```

The Vajra Seat unites both determinations. It has a precise address within the
world-system, but it is distinguished from every other place by an exclusive
operational capacity.

For the Organon Dataset, a locus record would therefore require more than
coordinates:

```text
containing domains
material substrate
formation status
supported operation
capacity constraint
agent who performs the operation
```

This is an Organon reconstruction, not Vasubandhu's formal terminology. It
offers a disciplined technical analogue: an operation may be logically
describable yet unrealizable unless its execution environment has the
required support capacity.

The analogy should remain restrained. The Bhāṣya makes a specific doctrinal
claim about Bodhisattvas, the Vajra Seat, and diamond-like samādhi. It does
not formulate a general theory that every place possesses an esoteric Path-
power or that the container-world itself is the agent of realization.

## 10. Review Status

This Study restores the complete VAK 3.53 unit at 161.08–161.15: the outer
ocean's 322,000-yojana breadth, the four continents on the four sides of
Sumeru, Jambūdvīpa's three 2,000-yojana sides and cart-like form, and the
Vajra Seat as the unique locus capable of bearing diamond-like samādhi.

The Sanskrit and translation remain provisional. The damaged prose
restatement of Jambūdvīpa's side measure, the precise morphology intended by
`śakaṭākṛti`, the technical stage denoted by `vajropama-samādhi`, and the
scope of `soḍhum` require comparison with critical Sanskrit, Tibetan,
Chinese, and established translations.

The Study stops before `sārdhatriyojanam tv ekam`, the opening of VAK 3.54.
It does not supply Jambūdvīpa's fourth side or begin the description of
Prāgvideha.
