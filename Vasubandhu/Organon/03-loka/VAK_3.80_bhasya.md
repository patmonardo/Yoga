# VAK_3.80 Bhāṣya — Two Calendars of the Devas

## Kārikā Anchor

```iast
dviguṇottaram ūrdhvānām ubhayaṃ rūpiṇāṃ punaḥ /
nāsty ahorātram āyus tu kalpaiḥ svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ // 3.80 //
```

> For the higher [desire-realm devas], both measures double successively. For
> the form-realm devas, in turn, there is no day-and-night, but lifespan is
> measured in kalpas commensurate with their own bodily support.

## Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Dviguṇottaram ūrdhvānām ubhayam.

Ūrdhvānāṃ devānām ubhayaṃ dviguṇottaram: ahorātraś cāyuś ca.
Kathaṃ kṛtvā?

Yan manuṣyāṇāṃ varṣaśataṃ tat Trāyastriṃśānāṃ devānām ekaṃ rātrindivam.
Tena rātrindivena divyaṃ varṣasahasram āyuḥ.

Evaṃ Yāmādīnāṃ yathākramam.

Mānuṣyakāṇi dve catvāry aṣṭau ṣoḍaśa varṣaśatāny ekaṃ rātrindivam.
Tena rātrindivena dve catvāry aṣṭau ṣoḍaśa divyāni varṣasahasrāṇy
āyuṣaḥ pramāṇam.

Yugandharād ūrdhvaṃ Sūryacandramasor abhāvāt.

Kathaṃ devānām ahorātravyavasthānam ālokakṛtyaṃ vā?

Puṣpāṇāṃ saṃkocavikāsāt, kumudabandhuvat, śakunīnāṃ kūjanākūjanāt,
middhāpagamāgamāc ca ālokakṛtyam; svayaṃprabhatvāt.

Uktam āyuḥ kāminām.

Rūpiṇāṃ punaḥ:

Nāsty ahorātram; āyus tu kalpaiḥ svāśrayasaṃmitaiḥ.

Yeṣāṃ rūpiṇām ardhayojanam āśrayaḥ, teṣām ardhaṃ kalpam āyuḥ;
yeṣāṃ yojanaṃ, teṣāṃ kalpam.

Evaṃ yasya yāvad yojanam āśrayas tasya tāvat kalpam āyuḥ; yāvad
Akaniṣṭhānāṃ ṣoḍaśakalpasahasrāṇy āyuḥpramāṇam.
```

The running source has `rātrindiva`/`rātrindivena` beside the kārikā's
`ahorātra`; both forms are retained. The phrase `kumudabandhuvat` is
transmitted in a compressed list of signs for day-and-night; I retain it
without forcing a more specific botanical or lunar interpretation.

## Continuous Conventional Translation

For the higher devas, both measures double successively: the day-and-night
measure and the lifespan. How so? One hundred human years make one
day-and-night for the Trāyastriṃśa devas. By that day-and-night, their
lifespan is one thousand divine years. The same rule applies in order to the
Yāmas and the higher classes: two, four, eight, and sixteen hundred human
years make one day-and-night, while their lifespans are two, four, eight,
and sixteen thousand divine years, respectively.

Above Yugandhara there are no sun and moon. How, then, are the devas' day and
night and illumination established? Day and night are indicated by flowers'
contraction and opening, by birds' alternating calls and silence, and by
sleepiness departing and returning. Illumination is due to the devas'
self-luminosity. This completes the account of the desire-realm lifespans.

For the form-realm devas, there is no day-and-night, but lifespan is measured
in kalpas commensurate with their own bodily support. Those whose support is
half a yojana live half a kalpa; those whose support is one yojana live one
kalpa. In this way, the number of kalpas in a lifespan matches the number of
yojanas in the bodily support; the Akaniṣṭhas' lifespan is sixteen thousand
kalpas.

## Natural Movement

The first half identifies `ubhayam`, “both,” as day-and-night and lifespan,
then illustrates the joint doubling at higher desire-realm stations. The
Bhāṣya pauses over how day and night can be distinguished above the region
where sun and moon are absent; recurrent natural and bodily signs mark the
alternation, while self-luminosity accounts for illumination. It then closes
the desire-realm discussion and turns to a different form of temporal
measure: form-realm lifespan in kalpas directly commensurate with bodily
support.

## The Two Deva Sequences

| Desire-realm class | Human years per divine day-night | Lifespan |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cāturmahārājika | 50 | 500 divine years |
| Trāyastriṃśa | 100 | 1,000 divine years |
| Yāma | 200 | 2,000 divine years |
| Tuṣita | 400 | 4,000 divine years |
| Nirmāṇarati | 800 | 8,000 divine years |
| Paranirmitavaśavartin | 1,600 | 16,000 divine years |

The Bhāṣya specifies the doubling for the higher classes, while the
Cāturmahārājika base comes from 3.79. In the form realm the correspondence
changes: no daily calendar is given, and lifespan in kalpas equals bodily
support measured in yojanas, from the half-unit examples through the stated
Akaniṣṭha endpoint.

## Doctrinal Note

The absence of `ahorātra` for form-realm devas is not an absence of lifespan
or duration. Rather, the basis of measurement changes: the desire-realm
sequence uses day, month, and year, while the form-realm sequence directly
matches lifespan in kalpas to bodily support in yojanas. Keep these two
temporal determinations distinct.

## Organon Contact Point

Light contact: the same broad category, deva lifespan, is measured through
different temporal organizations in different realms. This is a project-level
observation, not Vasubandhu's technical terminology.
