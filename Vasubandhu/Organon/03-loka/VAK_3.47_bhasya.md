# VAK_3.47 Bhāṣya — The Equal Breadth of Water and Gold

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

```iast
tiryak trīṇi sahasrāṇi sārdhaṃ śatacatuṣṭayam /
lakṣadvādaśakaṃ caiva jalakāñcanamaṇḍalam // 3.47 //
```

> The water-and-gold maṇḍala extends transversely twelve hundred-thousand,
> three thousand, and four hundred fifty yojanas.

The Bhāṣya makes one concise but decisive clarification: the water and golden
domains have the same horizontal extent.

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

```iast
Tiryak trīṇi sahasrāṇi sārdhaṃ śatacatuṣṭayam /
lakṣadvādaśakaṃ caiva jalakāñcanamaṇḍalam.

Samānaṃ hy etad ubhayaṃ vistārataḥ.
```

The running witness is mechanically clear in this brief unit. Punctuation
and word division have been regularized without altering its substance.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

The water-and-gold maṇḍala measures transversely twelve hundred-thousand,
three thousand, and four hundred fifty yojanas. For these two—the water
domain and the golden domain—are equal in breadth.

The next phrase in the running source states that the surrounding measure is
threefold. That phrase is the opening of VAK 3.48 and is therefore not folded
into this translation.

## 4. Natural Movement and Voice

The commentary performs a single exegetical operation:

```text
compound subject
    jala-kāñcana-maṇḍala

resolved as two domains
    water-maṇḍala + golden-earth maṇḍala

one shared measure
    equal in horizontal extent
```

Vasubandhu speaks directly. There is no objection, alternative school, or
reported disagreement in this unit. Its brevity should be preserved rather
than expanded into a cosmological argument not present in the text.

## 5. The Numerical Determination

The additive expression resolves as follows:

```text
12 lakṣas       = 1,200,000
3 thousands     =     3,000
4½ hundreds     =       450
                  ---------
                    1,203,450 yojanas
```

`Sārdhaṃ śatacatuṣṭayam` means four hundreds together with half a hundred,
not four hundred and a half yojanas. The total transverse measure is thus
1,203,450 yojanas.

The yojana unit is understood from the continuing cosmological measurement.
The Bhāṣya's `vistārataḥ`, “with respect to breadth,” confirms that `tiryak`
selects the horizontal rather than vertical axis.

## 6. Vertical Difference and Horizontal Equality

VAK 3.46 differentiated the two domains vertically:

| Domain | Vertical extent |
|---|---:|
| remaining water | 320,000 yojanas |
| golden earth | 800,000 yojanas |

VAK 3.47 now relates them horizontally:

| Domain | Transverse extent |
|---|---:|
| water-maṇḍala | 1,203,450 yojanas |
| golden-earth maṇḍala | 1,203,450 yojanas |

Their equality is dimension-specific. Water and gold remain different in
material determination, genesis, vertical position, depth, and function.
They are equal only `vistārataḥ`, with respect to breadth.

The singular compound `jalakāñcanamaṇḍalam` therefore does not introduce a
third substance. It gathers the vertically differentiated water and gold
supports under their common transverse measure.

## 7. Translation and Doctrinal Consequences

The Bhāṣya warrants a distributive reading:

```text
the water and gold domains
    each possess the stated breadth
    and are horizontally coextensive
```

“The water-and-gold maṇḍala” is acceptable if the compound is understood as
the combined support-system. A translation suggesting one homogeneous
water-gold material would erase the differentiation established in 3.46.

This verse completes the basic comparison of the two support layers along a
second spatial axis:

```text
3.46
    distinct vertical measures

3.47
    equal transverse measure
```

The threefold circumferential reckoning belongs grammatically and metrically
to VAK 3.48. Although the prose immediately explains it, importing that
measure here would obscure the actual verse boundary.

## 8. A Dimension-Indexed Relation

The proposition “water and gold are equal” is false without qualification.
The proposition established by the commentary is:

```text
water-maṇḍala = golden-earth maṇḍala
    under the relation: horizontal extent
```

At the same time:

```text
water-maṇḍala ≠ golden-earth maṇḍala
    under the relations:
        substance
        vertical depth
        position
        genesis and support-function
```

The Bhāṣya's short gloss thus prevents a syntactically available
misconstrual. `Ubhayam`, “both,” keeps the relata distinct; `samānam`,
“equal,” connects them; and `vistārataḥ` specifies the exact axis of
comparison.

## 9. Philosophical and Organon Study

This compact unit supplies a useful rule for the Organon: equality and
difference must be indexed to the feature under which entities are compared.
One configuration can sustain vertical difference and horizontal
coextension without contradiction.

An Ocular representation would therefore not store a bare assertion such as
“water equals gold.” It would preserve the relation and its dimension:

```text
left domain      water-maṇḍala
right domain     golden-earth maṇḍala
relation         equal extent
selected axis    transverse breadth
value            1,203,450 yojanas
```

This is an Organon reconstruction, not Vasubandhu's own formal terminology.
Its purpose is to preserve the semantic determination already explicit in
the Bhāṣya: comparison becomes true only through a specified respect.

The container-world has now moved from genesis and transformation to
coordinated measurement. It is becoming an articulated common field, but the
present verse does not yet establish its circumference, center, or mountain
system.

## 10. Review Status

This Study restores the complete compact VAK 3.47 unit at 158.16–158.18: the
transverse measure of the water-and-gold support-system and the Bhāṣya's
clarification that its two domains have equal breadth.

The Sanskrit is comparatively secure in the running witness. The numerical
construal, distributive force of `ubhayam`, and rendering of `vistāra` should
still be checked against critical Sanskrit, Tibetan, Chinese, and established
translations during the later learning pass.

The Study stops before `samantatas tu triguṇam`, the first pāda of VAK 3.48.
It does not include the circumferential calculation, Meru, or the surrounding
mountain system.
