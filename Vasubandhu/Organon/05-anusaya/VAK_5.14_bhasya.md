# VAK 5.14 Bhāṣya — Six With Uncontaminated Supports

## Kārikā Anchor

> mithyādṛgvimatī tābhyāṃ yuktā 'vidyā 'tha kevalā |
> nirodhamārgadṛggheyāḥ ṣaḍ anāsravagocarāḥ || 5.14 ||

> False view, doubt, and ignorance associated with those two or
> independent of them: six cases abandoned through seeing cessation
> and the path have uncontaminated cognitive supports.

This is a classification of the anuśaya's **support**, not a
statement that the affliction itself is uncontaminated.

## Continuous Conventional Translation

Of the ninety-eight anuśayas, how many take a support with outflows
and how many a support without outflows? The verse identifies
false view, doubt, and ignorance, whether associated with those
two or independent: six cases abandoned through seeing cessation
and seeing the path range over uncontaminated supports.

Three anuśayas abandoned through seeing cessation are named:
false view, doubt, and ignorance. The prose appears to include
ignorance associated with the first two and independent
ignorance within the ignorance category; its damaged wording
prevents a more exact syntactic claim here. The same three types
are abandoned through seeing the path. Thus six have
uncontaminated cognitive supports. The remainder are thereby
established as having supports with outflows. “Now, concerning
these further—” leads into the next unit.

## Argument and Doctrinal Determination

The count is three types in each of two truth-specific classes:

| Support | False view | Doubt | Ignorance | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cessation | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| Path | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| **Total** | **2** | **2** | **2** | **6** |

The six remain afflicted anuśayas. `Anāsravagocara` and
`anāsravālambana` qualify what they range over, not their own
afflictive status. Thus the text distinguishes the status of a
cognition from the status of its cognitive support.

The damaged ignorance sentence is important for the count.
Associated and independent forms are mentioned, but the prose
still says `trayo 'nuśayāḥ` for cessation and `eta eva trayaḥ`
for the path. It would be wrong to count the two ignorance
descriptions as two additional entries in each truth-specific
set without further source evidence.

## Translation and Organon Study

**Translation decisions.** `Sāsrava` and `anāsrava` are kept as
technical status terms, glossed here as “with outflows” and
“without outflows.” `Gocara` in the verse and `ālambana` in the
prose both concern cognitive range or support in this unit; that
local relation does not make them universal synonyms. The
corrupted punctuation near `āveṇikī` remains visible.

**Organon reconstruction.** The distinction between a defective
cognitive act and an uncontaminated support matters for the
Science of Citta: presenting a path or cessation as an object
does not itself guarantee adequate cognition of it. This is a
project inference from the source's relational classification,
not an additional doctrine in the Kārikā.
