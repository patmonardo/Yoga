# VAK_5.52

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

```sanskrit
तदन्ये भावनाहेयाः स्वतन्त्राश्च तथा मलाः ।
कामेऽशुभाः त्रयो द्विधा परेणाव्याकृतास्ततः ॥ ५.५२ ॥
```

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

```text
tad-anye bhāvanā-heyāḥ svatantrāś ca tathā malāḥ |
kāme 'śubhāḥ trayo dvidhā pareṇāvyākṛtās tataḥ || 5.52 ||
```

VAK 5.52 completes the abandonment classification of the ten
paryavasthānas, extends the same status to the six stains, and then determines
the ethical value of the upakleśas according to domain.

## 3. Padaccheda and Lexical Analysis

```text
tat-anye | bhāvanā-heyāḥ | sva-tantrāḥ | ca | tathā | malāḥ |
kāme | aśubhāḥ | trayaḥ | dvidhā | pareṇa | avyākṛtāḥ | tataḥ
```

| Form | Morphology | Contextual sense |
|---|---|---|
| `tat-anye` | nominative plural masculine | the others apart from those [five of VAK 5.51] |
| `bhāvanā-heyāḥ` | nominative plural masculine compound | to be abandoned through cultivation |
| `sva-tantrāḥ` | nominative plural masculine | independent in the stated classificatory sense |
| `ca tathā malāḥ` | coordinated nominative plural | and the stains likewise |
| `kāme` | locative singular | in the desire-domain |
| `aśubhāḥ` | nominative plural masculine | unwholesome |
| `trayaḥ` | nominative plural masculine | three |
| `dvidhā` | indeclinable adverb | twofold, here in ethical value |
| `pareṇa` | instrumental singular used adverbially | beyond it, above the desire-domain |
| `avyākṛtāḥ` | nominative plural masculine | ethically indeterminate |
| `tataḥ` | ablatival adverb | from there, therefore or beyond that point |

`Dvidhā` no longer refers to the two abandonment modes of VAK 5.51. The
Bhāṣya identifies a new axis: torpor, excitation, and sleepiness may each be
unwholesome or indeterminate within the desire-domain.

## 4. Grammar

**Remaining paryavasthānas**

```text
tat-anye
    bhāvanā-heyāḥ
    sva-tantrāḥ ca
```

The Bhāṣya resolves `tadanye` as envy, avarice, remorse, anger, and
concealment—the five remaining after VAK 5.51's twofold group. Both predicates
apply to all five.

**Extension to the stains**

```text
tathā
    malāḥ [bhāvanā-heyāḥ sva-tantrāḥ ca]
```

`Tathā` carries both properties forward. All six kleśa-malas are likewise
cultivation-abandoned and independent.

**Ethical value by domain**

```text
kāme
    [upakleśāḥ] aśubhāḥ
    trayaḥ dvidhā

pareṇa tataḥ
    [yathāsaṃbhavam] avyākṛtāḥ
```

The general desire-domain rule is qualified internally by the three. Above the
desire-domain, only upakleśas that occur there “as applicable” are called
indeterminate.

## 5. Translation

### Literal Translation

> The others are to be abandoned through cultivation and are independent; the
> stains are likewise. In the desire-domain they are unwholesome, although
> three are twofold; beyond it they are indeterminate.

### Bhāṣya-informed study translation

> Envy, avarice, remorse, anger, and concealment—the five envelopments other
> than those listed in VAK 5.51—are abandoned only through cultivation and are
> independently classified because of their intense mode of association. The
> six stains have these same two statuses. The upakleśas under discussion are
> generally unwholesome in the desire-domain, but torpor, excitation, and
> sleepiness may there be either unwholesome or indeterminate. Above the
> desire-domain, those upakleśas that occur there, as applicable, are
> indeterminate.

The exact five factors, ground of independence, three ethically twofold
factors, and higher-domain scope restriction are supplied by the Bhāṣya.

## 6. Philosophical Translation

> The same afflictive factor is intersected by several irreducible judgments.
> One judgment concerns how it is abandoned; another whether it has independent
> standing; another the ethical value it bears in a specified domain. These
> relations do not coincide. A factor can be cultivation-abandoned and
> independently classified while its ethical value still changes with its
> domain and mode of occurrence. Even the repeated word “twofold” changes its
> determination: in VAK 5.51 it named two Path-operations; here it names two
> ethical values. Meaning therefore follows the active classificatory relation,
> not the isolated lexical form.

The Bhāṣya supplies the doctrinal matrices; their representation as intersecting
axes is an analytical reconstruction.

## 7. Technical Vocabulary

**`Tad-anye` — the remaining five**

The Bhāṣya identifies them exactly:

```text
īrṣyā       envy
mātsarya    avarice
kaukṛtya    remorse
krodha      anger
mrakṣa      concealment
```

All five are exclusively cultivation-abandoned and are called independent.

**`Bhāvanā-heya` — to be abandoned through cultivation**

The sole abandonment mode of the remaining five paryavasthānas and all six
kleśa-malas. The Bhāṣya does not define this here through a general modern
theory of habit.

**`Svatantra` — independent**

Independent within the Abhidharma classification. The Bhāṣya grounds the term
in `adhimātra-saṃprayogitva`, an intense or strong mode of association, rather
than in metaphysical self-subsistence or arising without conditions.

**`Adhimātra-saṃprayogitva` — intense mode of association**

The explicit reason given for the independent status of the five. Its finer
doctrinal nuance remains provisional, but its relational role must be retained.

**The six stains under Path-status**

```text
māyā | śāṭhya | mada | pradāśa | upanāha | vihiṃsā
    → bhāvanā-heya
    → svatantra
```

`Tathā` gives them both predicates, not merely the first.

**`Akuśala` / `aśubha` — unwholesome**

The general ethical value of the stated upakleśas in the desire-domain, subject
to the three-factor exception.

**`Avyākṛta` — ethically indeterminate**

Neither wholesome nor unwholesome. It applies as one possible value to three
factors in the desire-domain and generally to applicable upakleśas above it.

**The ethically twofold three**

```text
styāna       torpor
auddhatya    excitation
middha       sleepiness
```

Each may be `akuśala` or `avyākṛta` within the desire-domain.

**`Yathāsaṃbhavam` — according to applicability**

The Bhāṣya's restriction on the higher-domain rule. It does not assert that
every named upakleśa occurs in every higher domain.

## 8. Logical Determination

The abandonment distribution is complete:

```text
For x in {Īrṣyā, Mātsarya, Kaukṛtya, Krodha, Mrakṣa}:
    AbandonedBy(x, Cultivation)
    AND Independent(x)

For x in SixKleśaMalas:
    AbandonedBy(x, Cultivation)
    AND Independent(x)
```

Ethical valuation is domain-indexed:

```text
OccursIn(x, DesireDomain)
AND Upakleśa(x)
    → GenerallyHasValue(x, Unwholesome)

x in {Styāna, Auddhatya, Middha}
AND OccursIn(x, DesireDomain)
    → HasValue(x, Unwholesome OR Indeterminate)

OccursIn(x, HigherDomain)
AND Upakleśa(x)
AND ApplicableThere(x)
    → HasValue(x, Indeterminate)
```

The two uses of `dvidhā` must be typed:

```text
Dvidhā(VAK_5.51, AbandonmentAxis)
    = {Seeing, Cultivation}

Dvidhā(VAK_5.52, EthicalAxis)
    = {Unwholesome, Indeterminate}
```

Therefore:

```text
SameLexeme(dvidhā)
    ⇏ SameDeterminationAcrossRelations
```

## 9. Interpretive Note

**Source determination.** VAK 5.52 first supplies the complement of VAK 5.51:
five paryavasthānas are cultivation-abandoned only. It then extends their
independent and cultivation-abandoned status to the six stains before moving
to ethical valuation.

**Independence is relationally grounded.** `Svatantra` should not be glossed as
“a factor that arises entirely on its own.” The Bhāṣya grounds it in strong
association. Whatever the finer technical interpretation, independence here
is compatible with relational determination.

**A new meaning of twofold.** The transition from VAK 5.51 is exact:

```text
there: two paths of abandonment
here: two ethical values within one domain
```

The repetition itself tests whether the analysis follows semantic function or
merely carries a prior gloss forward.

**Higher-domain caution.** Above the desire-domain, the occurring upakleśas are
indeterminate only `yathāsaṃbhavam`. A range assertion must first establish
that the factor can occur in the domain; ethical value cannot manufacture
occurrence.

**Organon interpretation.** The Dataset requires reified contextual valuation:

```text
EthicalValuation
    ofFactor
    inDomain
    hasValue
    underQualification
    supportedBy SourceSpan
```

Abandonment mode and independence belong in parallel assertions, not as values
derived from the same global type-label. This is project architecture built to
preserve the Bhāṣya's distinctions.
