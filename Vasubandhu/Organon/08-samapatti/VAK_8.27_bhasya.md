# VAK_8.27 Bhāṣya — Further Samādhi Restrictions and Present-Life Pleasant Abiding

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sāsravāḥ nṛṣu akopyasya saptasāmantavarjitāḥ |
> samādhibhāvanā dhyānaṃ śubham ādyaṃ sukhāya hi || 8.27 ||

> The further samādhis are contaminated; they arise among human beings, for the
> unshakeable arhat, excluding the seven access stages. The first cultivation of
> samādhi is wholesome first dhyāna, for pleasant abiding in the present life.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.27.md`](./VAK_8.27.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> ekāntena caite parasamādhayaḥ sāsravāḥ / āryamārgadveṣitvāt /
> manuṣyeṣv eva na deveṣu / nānyasyārhataḥ /
> sapta sāmantakāni hitvānyāsv ekādaśasu bhūmiṣu /
> kāmadhātv anāgamya dhyānāntara dhyānārūpyeṣu /
> kuśalaṃ prathamaṃ śuddhakam anāsravaṃ vā dṛṣṭadharmasukhavihārāya /
> tadādikatvād anyāny api jñeyāni /
> nāvaśyaṃ saṃparāyasukhavihārāya /

## 3. Continuous Translation

> These further samādhis are exclusively contaminated — opposition to the noble
> path. They arise among humans, not gods; of the unshakeable arhat, not another.
> Dropping seven access stages: on the other eleven — desire-domain, anāgamya,
> intermediate dhyāna, the dhyānas and ārūpyas.
>
> Four cultivations of samādhi are then stated. First: wholesome first dhyāna —
> pure or uncontaminated — for pleasant abiding in the present life. Because it
> is first, the others too are to be understood. Not necessarily pleasant abiding
> in a later existence.

## 5. Contaminated ≠ Conditioned

Opposition to the path ≠ the arhat now hates. Anāgamya stays in the eleven.
Human occurrence ≠ rebirth-level of the sitting. Present sukha-vihāra ≠ one
feeling faculty.

## 6. Two Subjects

| further triad | first cultivation |
|---|---|
| sāsrava only | śuddha or anāsrava |
| humans, akopya | not locked to that arhat |
| 11 levels | first dhyāna as start of series |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `sāsravāḥ` = ekāntena, with the path-opposition reason;
- seven excluded ≠ anāgamya excluded;
- `sukhāya` = dṛṣṭadharmasukhavihāra.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Knowing the uncontaminated sitting does not make the further act a gate. Vidyā:
object, act, bearer, ground, purpose stay five. Technē of pleasant abiding is
still work in this life — paramārtha in kāmadhātu, not flight. Appearance would
either promote the further triad to anāsrava or dismiss present abiding as
vibe. Neither.
