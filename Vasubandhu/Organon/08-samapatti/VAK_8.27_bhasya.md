# VAK_8.27 Bhāṣya — Further Samādhi Restrictions and Present-Life Pleasant Abiding

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> sāsravāḥ nṛṣu akopyasya saptasāmantavarjitāḥ |
> samādhibhāvanā dhyānaṃ śubham ādyaṃ sukhāya hi || 8.27 ||

> The further samādhis are contaminated; they arise among human beings, for the
> unshakeable arhat, excluding the seven access stages. The first cultivation of
> samādhi is wholesome first dhyāna, for pleasant abiding in the present life.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> And these further samādhis are invariably “contaminated,” because
> they are opposed to the noble Path; they are therefore not
> uncontaminated. Where do they arise? “Among humans”: among human
> beings alone, not among gods. In whom do they arise? “For the
> unshakeable”: not for another arhat. On how many grounds? “Excluding
> the seven access stages”: leaving aside seven access attainments,
> on the other eleven grounds—desire domain, *anāgamya*, intermediate
> dhyāna, the [four] dhyānas, and the [four] formless grounds.
>
> Again, four cultivations of samādhi are stated. “[There is] a
> cultivation of samādhi which, when practiced, cultivated, and made
> abundant, conduces to pleasant abiding in the present life,” and so
> forth. Here: “Cultivation of samādhi is wholesome first dhyāna,
> for happiness.” Wholesome first dhyāna, whether pure or
> uncontaminated, is cultivation of samādhi for pleasant abiding in
> the present life. Since the first is named, the others too should
> be understood. It is not necessarily for pleasant abiding in a
> subsequent life, since that is absent for those described by the
> compressed [non-declining, higher-reborn, finally liberated] clause.

## 5. Two Subjects

The first half completes the further-samādhi classification from
8.25–8.26. Contaminated mode, human occurrence, practitioner type,
and grounds are separate restrictions. The second half starts the
four-cultivation teaching; its restrictions do not inherit those of
the further triad.

## 6. The Eleven Grounds and First Cultivation

| determination | source statement |
|---|---|
| further-samādhi grounds | desire domain, *anāgamya*, intermediate dhyāna, four dhyānas, four formless grounds |
| excluded | the other seven access stages |
| first cultivation | wholesome first dhyāna, pure or uncontaminated |
| purpose | pleasant abiding in the present life |

The Bhāṣya extends the first-dhyāna statement by “the others too
should be understood.” The final clause denies a necessary
later-life counterpart; it does not negate the present-life purpose.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `sāsravāḥ` is strengthened by *ekāntena* and explained by `āryamārgadveṣitvāt`;
- `nṛṣu` excludes gods and `akopyasya` excludes other arhat types;
- `saptasāmantavarjitāḥ` excludes seven access stages while keeping *anāgamya*;
- `sukhāya` is specified as *dṛṣṭadharmasukhavihāra*.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is Organon reconstruction. A further samādhi's Support
may be an uncontaminated adept attainment without making the further
act itself uncontaminated. Mode, practitioner, ground, and purpose
should remain separate. The cultivation discussion changes question
from what a samādhi is to what cultivation conduces to.
