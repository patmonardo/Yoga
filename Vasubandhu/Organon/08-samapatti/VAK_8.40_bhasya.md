# VAK_8.40 Bhāṣya — Expository Method, Error, and Authority

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāśmīravaibhāṣikanītisiddhaḥ prāyo mayāyaṃ kathito 'bhidharmaḥ |
> yad durgṛhītaṃ tad ihāsmadāgaḥ saddharmanītau munayaḥ pramāṇam || 8.40 ||

> This Abhidharma has been expounded by me, established for the most
> part according to the method of the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣikas. Whatever has
> been badly grasped here is our fault; in the guidance of the true
> Dharma, the sages, the Buddhas themselves and the Buddha-sons,
> are the authority.

Local verse: `munayaḥ`. Running verse prints `manayaḥ`. Prose names Buddhas and
Buddha-sons. Official kārikā points next to a lament-coda (8.41).

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.40.md`](./VAK_8.40.md).

## 3. Continuous Translation

> Is the Abhidharma expounded here in this treatise precisely the
> Abhidharma taught [the construal of `śāstrābhidharma` remains
> uncertain]? “This Abhidharma has been related by
> me, established for the most part according to the Kāśmīra
> Vaibhāṣika method. Whatever is badly grasped here is our fault;
> in the guidance of the true Dharma, the sages are the authority”
> [the running verse reads `manayaḥ`, against the paired kārikā's
> `munayaḥ`].
>
> For this Abhidharma has been expounded by us for the most part as
> established according to the method and so forth of the Kāśmīra
> Vaibhāṣikas [the local `nītyādisiddha` is compressed, and
> `abidharma` is retained in the Sanskrit]. What we have badly
> grasped here is our own error. In the guidance of the true Dharma,
> however, the Buddhas themselves and the Buddha-sons are authoritative.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

The question tests the status of the Abhidharma expounded in the
treatise. The verse answers in Vasubandhu's first person: it names
the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika method as the predominant source of the
exposition, assigns any faulty grasp to the expositor, and places
authority for the true Dharma with the sages. The prose repeats the
qualified method claim, explains the fault as the author's error,
and specifies the sages as Buddhas and Buddha-sons.

## 5. Bhāṣya Explanation

`Prāyaḥ` and prose `prāyeṇa` limit the school-method attribution.
`Yad … tad` assigns responsibility conditionally; it does not list
particular errors. Prose `aparādhaḥ` explains verse `āgaḥ` as fault.
The local running verse's `manayaḥ` is not silently changed to the
paired kārikā's `munayaḥ`; the prose's Buddhas and Buddha-sons
support the latter sense without repairing the running letters.
The opening `śāstrābhidharma` question and prose `nītyādisiddha`
remain philologically uncertain.

## 6. Organon Determination

Represent method followed, actual exposition, authorial responsibility,
and authority concerning the true Dharma as distinct relations. The
verse does not transfer the named sages' authority to the Organon or
claim that every sentence of this treatise is identical with the
Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika position.

## 7. Comparative Synthesis

The passage offers a bounded methodological comparison: a systematic
work can state its predominant inherited method and own its errors.
That project analogy remains separate from the Bhāṣya's specific
authority attribution to Buddhas and Buddha-sons. The passage gives
no inventory of errors or general epistemological theory of *pramāṇa*.
