# VAK_8.40 Bhāṣya — Expository Method, Error, and Authority

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāśmīravaibhāṣikanītisiddhaḥ prāyo mayāyaṃ kathito 'bhidharmaḥ |
> yad durgṛhītaṃ tad ihāsmadāgaḥ saddharmanītau munayaḥ pramāṇam || 8.40 ||

> This Abhidharma, established chiefly according to the method of the Kāśmīra
> Vaibhāṣikas, has been expounded by me. Whatever has been badly grasped here is
> our fault; in the interpretation of the true Dharma, the sages are the authority.

Local verse: `munayaḥ`. Running verse prints `manayaḥ`. Prose names Buddhas and
Buddha-sons. Official kārikā points next to a lament-coda (8.41).

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.40.md`](./VAK_8.40.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> prāyeṇa hi kāśmīravaibhāṣikāṇāṃ nītyā … eṣo 'smābhir abhidharma ākhyātaḥ /
> yad atrāsmābhir durgṛhītaṃ so 'smākam aparādhaḥ /
> saddharmanītau tu punar buddhā eva pramāṇaṃ buddhaputrāś ca /

**Witness.** Pradhan 459|15–460|03. Voices: Vasubandhu, first person. Opening

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Is the Abhidharma spoken in this treatise that same Abhidharma taught as
> śāstra? This Abhidharma has been related by me *for the most part* as established
> according to the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika method. Whatever we have badly grasped here
> is our fault. In the guidance of the true Dharma, the Buddhas themselves are the
> measure — and the Buddha-sons.

## 5. Method ≠ Measure

`Prāyaḥ` is not a full confession of every dissent. No list of retractions here.
`Durgṛhīta` ≠ a bad manuscript. Organon does not inherit this `pramāṇa`.
