# VAK_8.40 Bhāṣya — Expository Method, Error, and Authority

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> kāśmīravaibhāṣikanītisiddhaḥ prāyo mayāyaṃ kathito 'bhidharmaḥ |
> yad durgṛhītaṃ tad ihāsmadāgaḥ saddharmanītau munayaḥ pramāṇam || 8.40 ||

> This Abhidharma has been expounded by me, established for the most
> part according to the method of the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣikas. Whatever has
> been badly grasped here is our fault; in the guidance of the true
> Dharma, the sages, the Buddhas themselves and the Buddha-sons,
> are the authority.

Local verse: `munayaḥ`. Running verse prints `manayaḥ`. Prose names Buddhas and
Buddha-sons. Official kārikā points next to a lament-coda (8.41).

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.40.md`](./VAK_8.40.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> yo 'yam iha śāstre 'bhidharma uktaḥ kim eṣa eva śāstrābhidharmo deśitaḥ /
> prāyeṇa hi kāśmīravaibhāṣikāṇāṃ nītyā … eṣo 'smābhir abhidharma ākhyātaḥ /
> yad atrāsmābhir durgṛhītaṃ so 'smākam aparādhaḥ /
> saddharmanītau tu punar buddhā eva pramāṇaṃ buddhaputrāś ca /

**Witness.** Pradhan 459|15–460|03. Voices: Vasubandhu, first person. Opening
question compressed — construe still a review point.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Is the Abhidharma spoken in this treatise that same Abhidharma taught as
> śāstra? This Abhidharma has been related by me *for the most part* as established
> according to the Kāśmīra Vaibhāṣika method. Whatever we have badly grasped here
> is our fault. In the guidance of the true Dharma, the Buddhas themselves are the
> measure — and the Buddha-sons.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

method named → error owned → measure placed above school and book

## 5. Method ≠ Measure

`Prāyaḥ` is not a full confession of every dissent. No list of retractions here.
`Durgṛhīta` ≠ a bad manuscript. Organon does not inherit this `pramāṇa`.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `prāyaḥ` kept;
- `āgaḥ` = aparādha;
- `munayaḥ` specified as Buddhas and Buddha-sons.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

The book names its school and does not become that school. Vidyā keeps method,
exposition, and measure three. Technē of this work is the same: check, own the
miss, do not Mara the saints or steal their authority for the Organon. Appearance
would call the treatise the Absolute. Yama still stands at the door: raise samādhi
to Pure Reason or be refuted. That is our line. This verse's measure is the
Buddhas. After Chapter 8: Marga hell, as planned.
