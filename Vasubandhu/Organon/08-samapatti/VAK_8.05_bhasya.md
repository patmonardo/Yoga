# VAK_8.05 Bhāṣya — Eight Fundamental Attainments and Their Admissible Modes

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> iti maulaṃ samāpattidravyam aṣṭavidhaṃ tridhā |
> sapta āsvādanavacchuddhānāsravāṇi aṣṭamaṃ dvidhā || 8.05 ||

> The four dhyānas and four formless attainments constitute
> the fundamental attainment inventory, which is thus eightfold.
> Seven of them are threefold: associated with savoring, pure,
> and without influx. The eighth, the summit of existence, is
> twofold: savoring-associated and pure.

Definitions of the three modes: 8.06. Do not pull them back into this file.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.05.md`](./VAK_8.05.md).

## 3. Continuous Translation

> [436|12–14] Thus the principal attainment inventory is eightfold: these
> are the eight principal attainments, namely the four dhyānas and the four
> formless attainments.
>
> [436|14–19] Of them, “seven are threefold”: the seven other than the
> summit of existence are threefold, being associated with savoring,
> pure, and without influx.
>
> [437|01–02] “The eighth is twofold”: the summit of existence is associated
> with savoring or pure. There is no [attainment there] without influx.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
iti gathers four + four = eight principals (not accesses)
seven × three modes
bhavāgra × two; anāsrava denied
śuddha ≠ anāsrava
```

## 5. Height ≠ Path-Status

`Dravya` here = the inventory of actual attainments, not a permanent substance.
Three modes are alternatives, not three tags on one sitting. Do not derive the
eighth's exclusion from 8.04's mild saṃjñā in this verse — the text does not
say "because." Highest conditioned ≠ liberated.

## 6. Admissible Modes

| | savoring | śuddha | anāsrava |
|---|---|---|---|
| first seven | yes | yes | yes |
| bhavāgra (8th) | yes | yes | no |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `maulam` = principal eight;
- `sapta` = all except bhavāgra;
- `aṣṭamam` = neither-perception-nor-non-perception.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Level and mode are two coordinates with a real exclusion. Appearance would climb
the ladder and call the top vidyā. Vidyā: the summit of existence is still
existence. Technē of anāsrava is not minted by altitude.
