# VAK_8.23 Bhāṣya — Intermediate Dhyāna and the Vitarka–Vicāra Classification

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tridhā aduḥkhāsukhaṃ tac ca mahābrahmaphalaṃ ca tat |
> savitarkavicāro 'dhaḥsamādhiḥ parato 'dvayaḥ || 8.23 ||

> That is threefold and neither painful nor pleasant, and it has Mahābrahmā as
> its result. Samādhi below it is accompanied by vitarka and vicāra; beyond it,
> samādhi is without the two.

Emptiness / wishlessness / signlessness: 8.24. Chapter continues.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.23.md`](./VAK_8.23.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> nātra sukhaṃ duḥkham ity aduḥkhāsukham upekṣendriyasaṃprayuktam /
> na prītisaṃprayuktaṃ sābhisaṃskāravāhitvāt / ata eva duḥkhā pratipat /

> nātra dvayam astīty advayaḥ / pareṇa … dvitīyadhyānasāmantakād yāvad bhavāgram /

**Witness.** Pradhan 448|11–449|07. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> That intermediate dhyāna is threefold: savoring-associated, pure, and
> uncontaminated. Neither pain nor pleasure means joined with the faculty of
> neutral feeling. Not joined with rapture — it proceeds with effort. Hence a
> difficult mode of progress. Cultivate that intermediate and one becomes
> Mahābrahmā.
>
> The sūtra collects all samādhis as three. Intermediate is without vitarka,
> vicāra alone [of the pair]. Below it: first dhyāna and anāgamya, both factors.
> “Without the two” = those two are not there. Beyond intermediate: from access
> of the second through the summit — neither factor.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

## 5. Vicāra Alone ≠ Only One Dharma

Difficult progress ≠ painful feeling. Advaya ≠ metaphysical nonduality. No
vitarka-without-vicāra fourth cell. Mahābrahmā is a cultivation-result, not
liberative jñāna, and not blindly assigned to the anāsrava mode.

## 6. The Pair

| range | vitarka | vicāra |
|---|---|---|
| anāgamya + 1st dhyāna | yes | yes |
| dhyānāntara | no | yes |
| 2nd-access through bhavāgra | no | no |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `tridhā` now = modes of intermediate, not kecit's first-access;
- `aduḥkhāsukham` = upekṣendriya;
- `advayaḥ` = no vitarka and no vicāra.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Selective negation with a middle that still *has* vicāra — content, not emptied
light. Vidyā: three questions stay three. Technē of effort explains missing
prīti without cancelling feeling. Appearance would call advaya the Absolute.
It is the absence of two named factors.
