# VAK_8.20 Bhāṣya — The Summit Exception and Attainment Object-Range

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> āryākiñcanyasāṃmukhyād bhavāgre tv āsravakṣayaḥ |
> satṛṣṇāḥ svabhavālambāḥ dhyānaṃ sadviṣayaṃ śubham || 8.20 ||

> At the summit of existence, however, the destruction of the outflows
> occurs through the direct presence of the noble nothingness. Those
> accompanied by craving take as their object the existence of their
> own level. Wholesome dhyāna has whatever exists as its object.

Reading: `sadviṣayam` (what exists), not `ṣaḍviṣayam` (six fields). Formless
object cuts: 8.21.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.20.md`](./VAK_8.20.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> utsargaṃ kṛtvāpavādaṃ karoti /
> bhavāgre tūpapannasyānāsravākiñcanyāyatanasaṃmukhībhāvād āsravakṣayo bhavati /
> svasyābhāvāt tasya cābhyāsāt /
> āsvādanāsaṃprayuktāḥ svabhūmikaṃ bhavam ālambante /
> nādharam … vītarāgatvān nottaraṃ tṛṣṇāparicchinnatvād bhūmīnām /
> nānāsravaṃ kuśalatvaprasaṅgāt /
> kuśalaṃ dhyānaṃ śuddhakam anāsravaṃ ca / tat sarvālambanaṃ yat kiñcid asti saṃskṛtam asaṃskṛtaṃ vā /

**Witness.** Pradhan 446|22–447|07. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Having given the general rule, he makes an exception. For one reborn at the
> summit, destruction of outflows comes from making present the uncontaminated
> Essence of nothingness — because that ground has no uncontaminated of its own,
> and because that [nothingness] has been cultivated.
>
> What is the object of these dhyānas and ārūpyas? Savoring-associated [items]
> take existence of their own ground — `bhava` here = contaminated entity. Not
> lower (dispassion); not higher (grounds delimited by craving); not uncontaminated
> (that would entail wholesomeness).
>
> Wholesome dhyāna — pure and uncontaminated — has all as object: whatever exists,
> conditioned or unconditioned.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
8.19 utsarga → this apavāda
āśraya → ālambana
craving bounds the field; śubha dhyāna does not
```

## 5. Range ≠ Omniscience

No native anāsrava-bhavāgra. `Sva-bhava` ≠ svabhāva, not “contemplates itself.”
Sarvālambana = class-range, not every fact at once. Do not extend this last
clause to wholesome ārūpya yet.

## 6. Two Fields

| mode | objects |
|---|---|
| satṛṣṇa | own-ground sāsrava only |
| śubha dhyāna | whatever exists, saṃskṛta or not |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first half = exception, not a new native summit mode;
- `sadviṣayam` = sarvālambana;
- śubha here includes śuddhaka and anāsrava dhyāna.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Highest seat ≠ widest Knowing. The crown is not empty light: at bhavāgra the
work is a *named* lower attainment made present. Vidyā: support, resource,
object. Technē of outflow-destruction has content. Craving is a horizon, not a
glow. Appearance would call sarvālambana a blank “all.” It is the field of what
*is*.
