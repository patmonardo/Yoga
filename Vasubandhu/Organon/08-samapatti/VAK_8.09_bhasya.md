# VAK_8.09 Bhāṣya — Eleven Distinct Dhyāna Factors

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dravyato daśa caikaṃ ca prasrabdhiḥ sukham ādyayoḥ |
> śraddhā prasādaḥ prītis tu saumanasyaṃ dvidhāgamāt || 8.09 ||

> The eighteen factors counted by name are, by substance, ten and
> one, that is, eleven distinct dharmas. In the first two dhyānas
> the pleasure is pliancy; inward clarity is confidence; rapture,
> however, is mental joy, as scripture states it in two ways.

Which factors fail in defiled dhyāna: 8.10.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.09.md`](./VAK_8.09.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> dravyata etāny ekādaśa bhavanti /
> prāthamadhyānikāni pañca / dvitīye 'dhyātmasaṃprasādo vardhate /
> tṛtīye upekṣāsmṛtisaṃprajñānasukhāni / caturthe 'duḥkhāsukhā vedanā /
> tṛtīye dhyāne sukhaṃ dravyāntaram / tad vedanāsukham /
> dhyānayos tu prasrabdhisukham ādyayoḥ /
> vitarkavicārakṣobhavirahāt praśāntavāhitā saṃtater adhyātmasaṃprasādaḥ /
> śraddhā prasādaḥ /
> tasmāt prītir eva saumanasyaṃ na sukham /
> tasmād vicāryam etat /

**Witness.** Pradhan 438|12–440|22 (long middle debate compressed). Voices:
Vasubandhu; `apare` (bodily pleasure); objectors. Count is eleven, not ten.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> By substance these are eleven. Five of the first dhyāna. In the second, inward
> clarity increases [the count]. In the third: equanimity, mindfulness, clear
> comprehension, and pleasure. In the fourth: neither-pain-nor-pleasure feeling.
>
> Why is third-dhyāna pleasure another substance? Because that is pleasure as
> feeling; in the first two, the pleasure is pliancy. What dharma is inward
> clarity? From absence of the disturbance of vitarka and vicāra, the continuum's
> peaceful flow — and, as the distinct factor, confidence. Rapture is
> pleasant-mindedness, not the [third's] pleasure — from two scriptural
> statements. The bodily-pleasure alternative is left to be examined.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
18 nāmataḥ → 11 dravyataḥ
5 + 1 + 4 + 1

prīti = saumanasya

```

## 5. Same Word ≠ Same Dharma

Pariśuddhi of fourth upekṣā/smṛti does not add two new substances. Newly
counted as aṅga ≠ wholly absent earlier. Do not promote the bodily-pleasure
account to the principal list.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `daśa caikam` = eleven;
- `ādyayoḥ` limits pliancy-sukha;
- `dvidhāgamāt` = two āgama passages, not two occurrences of prīti.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Marks are not identities. One name (`sukha`) can carry two dharmas; two names
(`prasāda` / `śraddhā`) can carry one. Vidyā does that discrimination before
Technē uses the limbs. Appearance would either freeze eighteen labels or smash
them into one glow. Knowing stays in the count: saṃprajñāna is a distinct
item. The debate is not closed by us.
