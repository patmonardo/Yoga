# VAK_8.13 Bhāṣya — First-Dhyāna Cognition in Higher Dhyāna Rebirth

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dvitīyādau tadādyāptam akliṣṭāvyākṛtaṃ ca tat || 8.13 ||

> In the second and higher dhyāna rebirths, the body-, eye-, and
> ear-Cognition, together with the Cognition that initiates manifest

## 3. Continuous Translation

> If the three groups of Cognition are not found in the second and higher
> dhyānas, and neither are initial and sustained examination (*vitarka* and
> *vicāra*), how do those beings see, and so on through touching? How do they
> bring forth nonmanifest action (*avijñapti*)? The question's word is retained here; the answer
> below speaks of manifest action (*vijñapti*).
>
> It is not that eye-Cognition and the others are absent for those reborn
> there. They simply do not belong to their own level. What, then? “The
> body-, eye-, and ear-Cognition, and what initiates manifest action: in the
> second and higher [dhyānas], this belongs to the first.” They make present
> first-dhyāna-level eye-, ear-, and body-Cognition, and the initiator of
> manifest action, in the second and higher dhyānas. As with
> construction-Consciousness, by this they see and so on, up to bringing
> forth manifest action.
>
> “And that is undefiled and ethically indeterminate.” The first-dhyāna-level
> Cognition they make present is to be understood as unobstructed and ethically
> indeterminate: it is not defiled because of freedom from passion, and not
> wholesome because it is inferior. Thus the work concerning dhyāna is
> concluded.

## 5. Level of Rebirth ≠ Level of Operation

The question concerns beings reborn at a higher dhyāna level. Its answer
distinguishes the level of their rebirth from the level of the Cognition made
present. Ethical indeterminacy here is distinct from *upekṣā* feeling and
from the uncontaminated mode. *Nirmāṇacitta* supplies a comparison, not a
general claim that seeing is construction.

- two reasons for the ethical cell.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Cognition's ground and the rebirth ground are distinct determinations. The
Organon reading distinguishes belonging to a ground from being made present
for a being reborn there. This is a project determination of the Bhāṣya's
reply, not a further claim about uninterrupted higher attainment.
