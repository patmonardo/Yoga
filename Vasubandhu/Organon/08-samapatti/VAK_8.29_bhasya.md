# VAK_8.29 Bhāṣya — Four Immeasurables: Objects, Counterforces, and Mental Natures

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> apramāṇāni catvāri vyāpādādivipakṣataḥ |
> maitry adveṣo 'pi karuṇā muditā sumanaskatā || 8.29 ||

> There are four immeasurables, because they oppose ill will and the rest:
> friendliness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity. Friendliness
> is non-hatred, and compassion is so too; sympathetic joy is gladness.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.29.md`](./VAK_8.29.md).

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Now the qualities that rest on samādhi. Four immeasurables: friendliness,
> compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity — because they take immeasurable
> beings as object. Why four? To abandon, in order: ill will, harmfulness,
> discontent, and predominance of sensual desire and ill will.
>
> If both impurity-contemplation and equanimity oppose sensual desire, what is the
> difference? Vaibhāṣikas: impurity against attachment to visible appearance,
> equanimity against sexual desire. This is fitting: impurity against sexual
> desire; equanimity against attachment to mother, father, child, and kin.
>
> Friendliness has the nature of non-hatred. Compassion likewise. Sympathetic joy
> has the nature of gladness.

## 5. Immeasurable ≠ Omniscience

Compassion is not gladness here. Family-rāga ≠ care. Opening list ≠ finished
liberation. Equanimity's nature waits.

## 6. Four Works

| | counters | nature here |
|---|---|---|
| maitrī | ill will | adveṣa |
| karuṇā | harmfulness | adveṣa |
| muditā | discontent | saumanasya |
| upekṣā | kāmarāga + vyāpāda predominance | 8.30 |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `api` carries adveṣa onto karuṇā;
- `sumanaskatā` binds muditā only;
- two impurity/equanimity accounts kept distinct.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

The House is not empty light. Beings are the object. Vidyā: scope, counter-work,
nature. Technē of friendliness is non-hatred made to cover without measure —
still content. We do not Mara the saints. Yama's line stands outside the verse:
raise samādhi to Pure Reason or be refuted. Appearance would call this vibe.
It is Practical Reason next to the Yoga path — how one stands toward living
beings in kāmadhātu.
