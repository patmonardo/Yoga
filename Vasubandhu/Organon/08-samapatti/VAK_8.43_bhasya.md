# VAK_8.43 Bhāṣya — The Liberation-Seeker Must Not Be Negligent

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> iti kaṇṭhagataprāṇaṃ viditvā śāsanaṃ muneḥ |
> balakālaṃ malānāṃ ca na pramādyaṃ mumukṣubhiḥ || 8.43 ||

> Having thus known the Sage's teaching to have its life-breath at the throat,
> and this to be a time of strength for the defilements, those desiring liberation
> must not be negligent.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Knowing thus that the Sage's teaching has its breath at the throat, and that
> this is the season of strength for the stains, those who desire liberation must
> not be heedless.
>
> Thus, in the Abhidharmakośabhāṣya, the eighth Kośasthāna, named Exposition of
> Attainments.
