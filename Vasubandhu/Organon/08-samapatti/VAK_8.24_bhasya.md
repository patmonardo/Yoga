# VAK_8.24 Bhāṣya — Three Samādhis and the Sixteen Truth-Aspects

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ānimittaḥ śamākāraiḥ śūnyatānātmaśūnyataḥ |
> pravartate 'praṇihitaḥ satyākārair ataḥ paraiḥ || 8.24 ||

> Signless [samādhi proceeds] through the aspects of peace; emptiness through
> non-self and emptiness. Non-directed through the other aspects of the truths.

`Śamākāraiḥ` provisional (running `samākāraiḥ`); sense fixed as nirodha-aspects.
Modes and vimokṣamukha: 8.25.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.24.md`](./VAK_8.24.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> nirodhasatyākāraiḥ saṃprayuktaḥ samādhir ānimittaś caturākāraḥ /
> nirvāṇaṃ hi daśanimittāpagatatvād animittam / tadālambanaḥ samādhir ānimittaḥ /
> pañcaviṣayastrīpuruṣatrisaṃskṛtalakṣaṇanimittāni daśa /
> anātmaśūnyatākārābhyāṃ saṃprayuktaḥ śūnyatāsamādhir dvyākāraḥ /
> paraiḥ śeṣaiḥ satyākāraiḥ … apraṇihito daśākāraḥ /
> anityaduḥkhataddhetubhya udvegāt mārgasya ca kolopamatayā … /
> śūnyatānātmatābhyāṃ tu nodvego nirvāṇasāmānyāt /

**Witness.** Pradhan 449|07–450|01. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Signless samādhi is joined with the four aspects of the truth of cessation.
> Nirvāṇa is signless — free from ten signs — and the samādhi that takes it as
> object is signless. The ten: five Essences, female and male, three marks of
> the conditioned.
>
> Emptiness samādhi is joined with two aspects: non-self and emptiness.
>
> Non-directed is joined with the remaining truth-aspects — ten. From recoil at
> impermanence, suffering, and their causes, and because the path is like a raft
> and must finally be given up. No such recoil at emptiness and non-self — they
> have commonality with nirvāṇa.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
new triad ≠ the vitarka/vicāra triad
16 ākāras partitioned 4 / 2 / 10
ten signs ≠ those ten remaining aspects
```

## 5. Not Blankness

Signless still has four aspects and an object. Raft ≠ throw the path away now.
Do not call every instance already a gate of liberation — that is 8.25.

## 6. Partition

| samādhi | aspects |
|---|---|
| ānimitta | four of nirodha |
| śūnyatā | anātman, śūnyatā |
| apraṇihita | the other ten |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- first instrumental = nirodha-aspects;
- `ataḥ paraiḥ` = the remainder of sixteen;
- name of ānimitta from the object.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Knowing stays full. Three relations to the same sixteen, not three emptied
lamps. Vidyā: aspect, object, rationale. Technē of the raft uses the path and
knows it is a path. Anātman here is two ākāras of emptiness-samādhi — not yet
the project's Dialectic of taking-as-nonself. Appearance would call signless the
shine with no content. The text gives ten signs *and* four aspects.
