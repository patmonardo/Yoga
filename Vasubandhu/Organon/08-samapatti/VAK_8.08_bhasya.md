# VAK_8.08 Bhāṣya — Third and Fourth Dhyāna Factors: Eighteen by Name

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tṛtīye pañca tūpekṣā smṛtiḥ prajñā sukhaṃ sthitiḥ |
> catvāry ante 'sukhāduḥkhopekṣāsmṛtisamādhayaḥ || 8.08 ||

> In the third dhyāna are five factors: equanimity, mindfulness,
> clear comprehension, ease, and stability, that is, samādhi. In
> the last are four: feeling that is neither pleasure nor pain,
> purified equanimity, purified mindfulness, and samādhi.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> [438|04–05] “In the third are five: equanimity, mindfulness, prajñā,
> ease, and stability.” In the third dhyāna there are five factors:
> equanimity, mindfulness, clear comprehension, ease, and samādhi.
>
> [438|06–07] For “stability” is a synonym of samādhi, as the sūtra says:
> “What is right samādhi? That which is the stability of Consciousness.”
>
> [438|08–10] “In the last are four: neither-pleasure-nor-pain,
> equanimity, mindfulness, and samādhi.” The fourth dhyāna is the last;
> in it are four factors: feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant,
> purified equanimity, purified mindfulness, and samādhi.
>
> [438|10–12] These make eighteen dhyāna factors, since the first and
> third each have five factors and the second and fourth each have four.
> This is so by name.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
3rd: five; verse prajñā glossed saṃprajñāna; sthiti = samādhi
4th: four; feeling remains (neutral); two factors purified
5+4+5+4 = 18 nāmataḥ
dravyataḥ postponed
```

## 5. Name-Count ≠ Kind-Count

Upekṣā ≠ the neutral feeling next to it. Neutral feeling ≠ no feeling.
Pariśuddhi ≠ anāsrava-mode. Not listing saṃprajñāna in the fourth ≠ no Knowing
there. Ante = fourth dhyāna, not bhavāgra.

## 6. Two Inventories

| 3rd | 4th |
|---|---|
| upekṣā | upekṣā-pariśuddhi |
| smṛti | smṛti-pariśuddhi |
| saṃprajñāna | — as named limb |
| sukha | aduḥkhāsukhā vedanā |
| samādhi (sthiti) | samādhi |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `prajñā` here = saṃprajñāna;
- `sthiti` = samādhi;
- fourth upekṣā/smṛti carry pariśuddhi.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

The fourth is not an emptied lamp. Feeling remains; mindfulness remains; they
are qualified. Vidyā: same name, different determination of the factor in the
whole. Technē of purification is not the mode-axis of 8.05. Appearance would
call eighteen names eighteen substances — that is 8.09's question, not this
verse's answer.
