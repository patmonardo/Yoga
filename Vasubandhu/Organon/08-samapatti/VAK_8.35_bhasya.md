# VAK_8.35 Bhāṣya — Eight Bases of Mastery

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> abhibhvāyatanāny aṣṭau dvayam ādyavimokṣavat |
> dve dvitīyavat anyāni punaḥ śubhavimokṣavat || 8.35 ||

> There are eight bases of mastery. A pair is like the first release; two are
> like the second; the others, in turn, are like the beautiful release.

Ten kṛtsnāyatanas: 8.36.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.35.md`](./VAK_8.35.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> adhyātmaṃ rūpasaṃjñī bahirdhā rūpāṇi paśyati parīttāni suvarṇāni durvarṇāni … abhibhūya jānāty abhibhūya paśyati … idaṃ prathamam /
> evam adhimātrāṇi / adhyātmam arūpasaṃjñy evam eva / ity etāni catvāri /
> … nīlapītalohitāvadātāni paśyatīty aṣṭau /
> yathā prathamo vimokṣa evaṃ dve … prathamadvitīye /
> yathā dvitīyo … tṛtīyacaturthe /
> yathā śubho vimokṣa evam anyāni catvāri /
> ayaṃ tu viśeṣaḥ tair vaimukhyamātram / ebhis tv ālambanābhibhavanaṃ yatheccham adhimokṣāt kleśānutpādāc ca /

**Witness.** Pradhan 457|01–12. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Internally perceiving form, one sees external forms — limited, attractive or
> unattractive — and knows and sees them as mastered. That is the first. Likewise
> the extensive. Without internal form-perception, the same pair. Those four.
> Again without internal form-perception: blue, yellow, red, white. Eight.
>
> First two like the first release; third and fourth like the second; remaining
> four like the beautiful. But this is the difference: by the releases, mere
> turning away; by these, mastery of the object — determination at will, and
> kleśa does not arise.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
enumerate 2+2+4
pattern after 1st / 2nd / beautiful release
then the viśeṣa: turning away vs command of the object
```

## 5. Like ≠ Same Class

Release ordinals ≠ dhyāna numbers. No internal form-perception ≠ no external
form. Command at will ≠ rearranging the world. Non-arising ≠ every kleśa gone
forever.

## 6. Eight

| | inner form-saṃjñā | outer |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | present | limited / extensive |
| 3–4 | absent | limited / extensive |
| 5–8 | absent | blue / yellow / red / white |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `dvayam` = bases 1–2;
- `dve` = 3–4;
- `anyāni` = four colors.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Turning away, then command of the field. Vidyā keeps the two functions. Technē:
know and see as mastered — still content, still form. Appearance would call this
empty shine painting colors. The text keeps blue, yellow, red, white. Yama does
not Mara the taxonomy. He still asks for Pure Reason. Āyatana here is the
contemplative sphere of an object, not yet Essence in the Organon sense.
