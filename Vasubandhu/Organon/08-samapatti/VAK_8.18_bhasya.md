# VAK_8.18 Bhāṣya — Pure-Type Successors and Preparation for Skipping Levels

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> dve trīṇi trīṇi caikaṃ ca hānabhāgādyanantaram |
> gatvāgamya dvidhā bhūmīr aṣṭau śliṣṭaikalaṅghitāḥ || 8.18 ||

> Immediately after the decline-conducive and subsequent [types]: two, three,
> three, and one. Having gone and returned through the eight grounds in two
> modes, contiguously and with one skipped …

Perfected skip-attainment: 8.19. Second half continues there.

**Witness.** Pradhan 445|11–446|06. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> Of these four, how many arise immediately after which?
>
> After decline-conducive: two — decline and stability. After stability: three,
> except penetration-conducive. After distinction: three, except decline-conducive.
> After penetration-conducive: that same one only.
>
> How does skip-attainment arise? “Having gone” = entering forward. “Having
> returned” = entering reverse. “In two ways” = contaminated and uncontaminated
> grounds. Contiguous = in sequence. One skipped = omitting one each time. Eight
> contaminated grounds mastered forward and reverse; and seven uncontaminated.
> Then one-skip in both directions, both series. This is the *preparation* for
> skip-attainment.

## 5. Two Graphs

Subtype-neighbors ≠ level-traversal. “Only one” after nirvedha = only one of the
four *pure* types; 8.17 still lets anāsrava arise from that type. Sāsrava ≠
kliṣṭa. Skip is not ignorance of the middle.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- numerals = those four sets;
- `dvidhā` = two *modes*, not two directions (directions already gatvā/āgamya);
- this unit ends at prayoga.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Content of transition, not empty hop. Vidyā keeps the directed neighbor-set.
Technē: command the middle, then omit it. Appearance would either freeze the
ladder or jump blind. Sahasrāra stays full: the skip is Knowing that knows the
step it does not take.
