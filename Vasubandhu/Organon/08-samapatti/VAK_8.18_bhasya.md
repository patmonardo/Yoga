## 3. Continuous Translation

> Of these four, how many arise immediately after each? “After the
> decline-conducive and the following types, two, three, three, and
> one [arise].” The decline-conducive is the decline-directed type.
> Immediately after it, two arise: decline-conducive and
> stability-conducive. After the stability-conducive, three arise,
> excluding the penetration-conducive. After the
> distinction-conducive, three arise, excluding the
> decline-conducive. After the penetration-conducive, that one
> alone arises among these four.
>
> How does attainment involving skipped levels arise? “Having gone
> and returned, in two ways, through eight levels, contiguously and
> with one skipped”; the quoted continuation says, “Skipping
> attainment goes to the third [item] of a different class.”
> “Having gone” means entering in forward order; “having returned”
> means entering in reverse order. “In two ways” means contaminated
> and uncontaminated grounds. “Eight” means the dhyāna and formless
> attainments. “Contiguously” means in sequence; “with one skipped”
> means passing over one at a time.
>
> Having mastered forward and reverse entry through eight
> contaminated grounds and seven uncontaminated ones, one then
> enters the contaminated third dhyāna after the contaminated
> first dhyāna; after that, the sphere of infinite space; after
> that, the sphere of nothingness. Having likewise mastered the
> reverse order, one enters the uncontaminated grounds with one
> skipped, in both forward and reverse order. This is the
> preparatory practice for skipping attainment.

## 5. Two Graphs

Subtype-neighbors ≠ level-traversal. “Only one” after nirvedha = only one of the
four *pure* types; 8.17 still lets anāsrava arise from that type. Sāsrava ≠
kliṣṭa. Skip is not ignorance of the middle.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- numerals = those four sets;
- `dvidhā` = two *modes*, not two directions (directions already gatvā/āgamya);
- this unit ends at prayoga.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

The two questions specify different relations. The first gives the
immediate successor sets among pure subtypes; the second prescribes
sequential mastery and then one-level skipping through attainment
grounds. The Organon reading keeps that ordered preparation distinct
from the completed skipping attainment described next.
