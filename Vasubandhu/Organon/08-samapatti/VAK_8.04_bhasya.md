# VAK_8.04 Bhāṣya — Formless Names: Preparatory Practice and Attenuated Perception

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ākāśānantya-vijñānānantyākiṃcanya-saṃjñakāḥ |
> tathāprayogān māndyāt tu naivasaṃjñānāsaṃjñakaḥ || 8.04 ||

> The first three are designated infinity of space, infinity of
> Cognition, and nothingness, according to the way they are
> applied; the fourth, however, is designated
> neither-perception-nor-non-perception because perception in
> it is attenuated.

Eight fundamental attainments by savoring, purity, influx-free: 8.05.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> [436|01–02] Are space and the rest alone the Supports of these states,
> beginning with the infinite-space sphere, so that they receive these names?
> No. How, then? First, the three [are called] infinity of space, infinity of
> Cognition, and nothingness “because of application in those ways.”
>
> [436|05–06] Attending respectively to “space is infinite,” “Cognition is
> infinite,” and “there is nothing,” practitioners apply themselves to these
> [states]. Hence these are their names.
>
> [436|07–09] The fourth, however, is called neither-perception-nor-non-
> perception because perception is mild. Its perception is not sharp, yet it
> is not the case that there is no perception at all.
>
> [436|09–11] Although they also apply themselves there in this way:
> “Perception is a disease; perception is a swelling; perception is a thorn;
> non-perception is delusion. This is peaceful, this is excellent: namely,
> the neither-perception-nor-non-perception sphere,” one must still explain
> why they apprehend it thus. That is just what has been stated: because
> perception is mild.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
name ≠ exclusive Support

```

## 5. Name ≠ Inventory

`Vijñāna` here is Cognition, not Chapter-7 jñāna and not omniscience.
“There is nothing” ≠ no dharmas (four Bases still). Fourth ≠ nirodha-
samāpatti. Preparation exists for the fourth too; it is not the naming ground.

## 6. Two Naming Grounds

| | why so called |
|---|---|
| space / Cognition / nothingness | corresponding application |
| neither-perception-nor-non-perception | saṃjñā attenuated |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- opening `eva` rejected;
- `tathāprayogāt` governs the three;
- `māndyāt` = mildness of saṃjñā.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is Organon reconstruction.

Unbounded words become determinations only inside the series. Vidyā: the name
records the mode of approach, or the state of a remaining factor. Technē of
attention is given as formula, not as a protocol we will write. Appearance would
read “nothing” as ontology. Leave project “knowledge” for vijñāna out of the
close translation.
