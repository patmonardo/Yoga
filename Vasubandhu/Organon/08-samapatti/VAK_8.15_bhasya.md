# VAK_8.15 Bhāṣya — Immediate Successors of Wholesome Attainments

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tṛtīyādyāvad ūrdhvādho 'nāsravānantaraṃ śubham |
> utpadyate tathā śuddhāt kliṣṭaṃ cāpi svabhūmikam || 8.15 ||

> Immediately after an uncontaminated attainment, a wholesome
> attainment, whether pure or uncontaminated, arises on the same
> level or upward or downward as far as the third, counting the
> starting level as the first. Likewise after a pure attainment, and
> in addition a defiled attainment belonging to its own level may
> arise.

Defiled predecessors and death-time: 8.16.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.15.md`](./VAK_8.15.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> katamasmāt samāpattidravyād anantaraṃ katy utpadyante /
> śubhagrahaṇena śuddham anāsravaṃ ca gṛhyate kuśalatvāt /
> … svabhūmike ca śuddhānāsrave utpadyete / ūrdhvādhobhūmike ca tṛtīyād yāvat /
> vyutkrāntakasamāpattāro hi tṛtīyāt pareṇa laṅghayituṃ notsahante /
> anvayajñānānantaraṃ cārūpyān samāpadyante na dharmajñānānantaram /
> svabhūmikaṃ kliṣṭam adhikaṃ śuddhakād anantaram utpadyate /
> śeṣaṃ yathaivānāsravāt / anāsravasya hi samanantaraṃ kliṣṭotpattir nāsti /

**Witness.** Pradhan 443|13–444|08. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> After which attainment-item do how many immediately arise?
>
> By saying “wholesome,” both pure and uncontaminated are taken — because
> uncontaminated is wholesome. After an uncontaminated item: same-level pure and
> uncontaminated, and those of grounds up or down as far as the third. Skip-
> attainment practitioners cannot leap beyond that third. They enter the formless
> immediately after anvayajñāna, not after dharmajñāna — lower support and object.
>
> Likewise after a pure: the same remainder as after uncontaminated, plus a
> defiled item of that same ground. Immediately after uncontaminated, no defiled
> arises.

## 5. May Follow ≠ Must Follow

Bhavāgra still has no anāsrava cell. Distance alone does not license every hop —
anvayajñāna for ārūpya. Later subtype cuts on śuddha wait at 8.17. Death-time
is another verse.

## 6. Asymmetry

| predecessor | wholesome range | immediate kliṣṭa |
|---|---|---|
| anāsrava | same ± two levels | none |
| śuddha | same range | same level only |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `tṛtīyād yāvat` = bounded skip, not “third and following”;
- `tathā` carries the wholesome rule;
- `svabhūmikam` binds only the extra defiled.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

A determination includes what may come next. Vidyā: anvayajñāna is the ūrdhva-
side Path-form of seeing both and generating Principle — not “inference” locked
as English. Technē of skip is two steps, not a flight. Appearance would let
anāsrava drop straight into stain. The text forbids that *next* moment.
