# VAK_8.15 Bhāṣya — Immediate Successors of Wholesome Attainments

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> tṛtīyādyāvad ūrdhvādho 'nāsravānantaraṃ śubham |
> utpadyate tathā śuddhāt kliṣṭaṃ cāpi svabhūmikam || 8.15 ||

> Immediately after an uncontaminated attainment, a wholesome
> attainment, whether pure or uncontaminated, arises on the same
> level or upward or downward as far as the third, counting the
> starting level as the first. Likewise after a pure attainment, and
> in addition a defiled attainment belonging to its own level may
> arise.

Defiled predecessors and death-time: 8.16.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.15.md`](./VAK_8.15.md).

## 3. Continuous Translation

> Immediately after which distinct attainment item do how many arise?
> After an uncontaminated first dhyāna, six arise: the pure and
> uncontaminated of its own ground, and those belonging to the second
> and third dhyāna grounds. After the sphere of nothingness, seven:
> the pure and uncontaminated of its own ground, and those of the
> spheres of infinite Cognition and infinite space; at the summit of
> existence, only the pure arises, because the uncontaminated is absent.
>
> After the second dhyāna, eight arise: the pure and uncontaminated
> of its own ground, and those belonging to the third, fourth, and
> first dhyāna grounds. After the sphere of infinite Cognition, nine:
> two of its own ground, four belonging to the spheres of infinite
> space and the fourth dhyāna, and three belonging to the sphere of
> nothingness and the summit of existence. In the same way, ten
> distinct items are to be worked out after the other dhyānas and
> formless attainments. Here is the summary:
>
> “After an uncontaminated [attainment], a wholesome [attainment]
> arises upward or downward as far as the third.” “Wholesome” includes
> pure and uncontaminated, because the uncontaminated is wholesome.
> Immediately after an uncontaminated attainment item, pure and
> uncontaminated items of its own ground arise, and those of higher
> or lower grounds as far as the third. Practitioners of skipping
> attainments cannot leap beyond the third. They enter formless
> attainments immediately after *anvayajñāna* (anvaya-knowledge),
> not immediately after *dharmajñāna* (Dharma-knowledge), because
> the latter has a lower Basis and Support.
> What was said of succession after an uncontaminated attainment is
> to be understood likewise here.
>
> “Likewise after a pure [attainment], and also a defiled one of its
> own ground.” Immediately after a pure attainment, a defiled item
> of its own ground arises in addition; the rest is just as after an
> uncontaminated attainment. For a defiled item does not arise
> immediately after an uncontaminated one.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
examples first: 6 / 7 / 8 / 9; remaining cases: 10
śubha = śuddha + anāsrava
third counting from the source ground limits the range
anāsrava: no immediate kliṣṭa
śuddha: an additional own-ground kliṣṭa
```

## 5. May Follow ≠ Must Follow

The summit contributes one pure item, because it lacks an uncontaminated
one. Distance alone does not license every transition: entry into the
formless attainments after uncontaminated Knowledge requires
*anvayajñāna*. The source gives the lower Basis and Support of
*dharmajñāna* as the reason it is excluded. Pure subtypes are treated
later, and death-time succession belongs to 8.16.

## 6. Asymmetry

| predecessor | wholesome range | immediate kliṣṭa |
|---|---|---|
| anāsrava | same ± two levels | none |
| śuddha | same range | same level only |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `tṛtīyād yāvat` = bounded skip, not “third and following”;
- `tathā` carries the wholesome rule;
- `svabhūmikam` binds only the extra defiled.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

An attainment is determined here by the immediate successors available
to it. The Organon reading records predecessor, successor, ground, mode,
and the Knowledge condition on formless entry as distinct relations.
The exclusion of a defiled successor after an uncontaminated item
concerns the next occurrence, not every later occurrence.
