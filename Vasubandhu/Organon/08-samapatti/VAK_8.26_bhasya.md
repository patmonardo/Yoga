# VAK_8.26 Bhāṣya — Objects and Aspects of the Further Samādhis

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ālambete aśaikṣaṃ dvau śūnyataś cāpy anityataḥ |
> ānimittānimittas tu śāntato 'saṃkhyayā kṣayam || 8.26 ||

> The two take an adept's [samādhi] as object, under emptiness and under
> impermanence. Signlessness-of-signlessness, however, [takes] cessation without
> analysis under the aspect of peace.

Mode and who produces them: 8.27.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.26.md`](./VAK_8.26.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> aśaikṣaṃ samādhiṃ dvāv aparasamādhī ālambete /
> śūnyatāśūnyatā aśaikṣaṃ śūnyatāsamādhim ālambate śūnyatākāreṇa /
> apraṇihitāpraṇihito 'py aśaikṣam apraṇihitam anityākāreṇa /
> na duḥkhato na hetvādito 'nāsravasyātallakṣaṇatvān na mārgākāraiḥ dūṣaṇīyatvāt /
> ānimittānimittas tu … aśaikṣasyānimittasyāpratisaṃkhyānirodham ālambate śāntākāreṇa /
> anāsravasya pratisaṃkhyānirodhābhāvāt /

**Witness.** Pradhan 450|12–20. Voices: Vasubandhu. Running `akṣaikṣaṃ` controlled by prose `aśaikṣa`.

## 3. Continuous Conventional Translation

> The two further samādhis take an adept's samādhi as object. Emptiness-of-
> emptiness: an adept's emptiness-samādhi, under emptiness. Non-directedness-of-
> non-directedness: an adept's non-directed, under impermanence. Not under
> suffering or origin-aspects — an uncontaminated item does not have those marks.
> Not under path-aspects — here the object is approached as something to be
> depreciated.
>
> Signlessness-of-signlessness takes the *non-analytical cessation* of an adept's
> signless samādhi, under peace. Not analytical cessation: an uncontaminated
> dharma has none of that. The other three cessation-aspects excluded: commonality
> with cessation-by-impermanence; ethically indeterminate; not disjunction.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

## 5. Absence ≠ Nirvāṇa

Impermanence of anāsrava ≠ it became sāsrava. Analytical cessation is
disjunction from *contaminants*. Object being aśaikṣa ≠ the further sitting is
already anāsrava (8.27).

## 6. Assignments

| further | object | aspect |
|---|---|---|
| śūnyatā-śūnyatā | adept emptiness-samādhi | emptiness |
| apraṇihita-apraṇihita | adept non-directed | impermanence |
| ānimitta-ānimitta | its apratisaṃkhyānirodha | peace |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `dvau` = first two further;
- `asaṃkhyayā kṣayam` = apratisaṃkhyānirodha;
- `tu` splits the object-construction.

## 8. Philosophical and Organon Study

This section is reconstruction.

Reflection is licensed only for what the object *is*. Vidyā: aspect must fit.
Technē of depreciation is still content — not a lamp staring at a lamp.
Appearance would treat the vanishing of signless samādhi as the Absolute. The
text names a different cessation.
