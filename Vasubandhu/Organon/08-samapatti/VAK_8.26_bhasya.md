# VAK_8.26 Bhāṣya — Supports and Aspects of the Further Samādhis

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> ālambete aśaikṣaṃ dvau śūnyataś cāpy anityataḥ |
> ānimittānimittas tu śāntato 'saṃkhyayā kṣayam || 8.26 ||

> The two [further samādhis] take an adept's samādhi as Support, under emptiness
> and under impermanence respectively; but signlessness-of-signlessness takes
> non-analytical cessation as Support, under the aspect of peace.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> Now, concerning these: “The two take [an adept's samādhi] as Support,
> under emptiness and also under impermanence.” The two further
> samādhis take an adept's samādhi as Support. Emptiness-of-emptiness
> takes an adept's emptiness samādhi as Support under the aspect of
> emptiness. Non-directedness-of-non-directedness likewise takes an
> adept's non-directed samādhi [as Support] under the aspect of
> impermanence. It does not take it under suffering or the cause
> aspects, because an uncontaminated [samādhi] does not have those
> characteristics; nor under the Path aspects, because [it is] to
> be depreciated [the referent of this reason is compressed].
>
> “But signlessness-of-signlessness takes cessation without analysis
> under the aspect of peace.” Signlessness-of-signlessness takes the
> non-analytical cessation of an adept's signless samādhi as Support,
> under the aspect of peace. It does not take analytical cessation,
> because an uncontaminated [samādhi] has no analytical cessation.
> It does not use the aspects of cessation, excellence, or escape:
> [respectively] because [this cessation] is shared with cessation
> through impermanence, because it is ethically indeterminate, and
> because it is not disjunction.

## 4. Movement of the Commentary

```text
first two further samādhis: adept samādhis as Supports
emptiness-of-emptiness: adept emptiness, emptiness aspect
non-directedness-of-non-directedness: adept non-directed, impermanence aspect
suffering and cause aspects excluded; Path aspects excluded separately
third: non-analytical cessation of adept signless samādhi as Support
peace aspect only; three other cessation aspects each excluded by reason
```

## 5. The Third Support Differs

The first two directly take corresponding adept samādhis as Supports.
The third takes the *apratisaṃkhyānirodha* of an adept's signless
samādhi, not that samādhi directly. The verse's `tu` marks this turn.
The further samādhis' own mode is determined in 8.27; taking an
uncontaminated adept samādhi as Support does not transfer its mode.

## 6. Support and Aspect Assignments

| further samādhi | Support | aspect |
|---|---|---|
| emptiness-of-emptiness | adept emptiness samādhi | emptiness |
| non-directedness-of-non-directedness | adept non-directed samādhi | impermanence |
| signlessness-of-signlessness | non-analytical cessation of adept signless samādhi | peace |

The second excludes suffering and cause aspects because its
uncontaminated Support lacks those marks, and Path aspects for the
separate depreciability reason. For the third, the prose excludes
analytical cessation as Support. It also excludes the aspects of
cessation, excellence, and escape, giving separate reasons: commonality
with cessation through impermanence, indeterminate ethical status,
and absence of disjunction. The final compound is damaged across
a witness line break, so the exact syntax remains open.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `dvau` identifies the first two further samādhis;
- `akṣaikṣaṃ` in the running verse is controlled by prose `aśaikṣaṃ` without changing the witness;
- `asaṃkhyayā kṣayam` is resolved as *apratisaṃkhyānirodha*;
- `tu` separates the third Support construction from the first two.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is Organon reconstruction. Support identity and admissible
aspect constrain each further samādhi. The third case prevents a
uniform self-observation rule for the doubled names. Depreciating
an adept samādhi under a selected aspect does not make its own mode
contaminated.
