# VAK_8.25 Bhāṣya — Modes, Gates of Liberation, and Three Further Samādhis

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> śuddhāmalāḥ nirmalās tu te vimokṣamukhatrayam |
> śūnyatāśūnyatādyākhyās trayo 'parasamādhayaḥ || 8.25 ||

> The three samādhis are of two kinds, worldly-pure and uncontaminated;
> but the uncontaminated ones are called the three gates of liberation.
> There are three further samādhis, named emptiness-of-emptiness and so forth.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> These three samādhis are of two kinds: “pure and stainless.” They are
> pure and uncontaminated, being worldly and supramundane respectively.
> The worldly [forms] occur on eleven grounds; the supramundane [forms]
> occur where the Path occurs.
>
> “The stainless ones, however, are the three gates of liberation.”
> These three samādhis, when uncontaminated, are called the three gates
> of liberation: the emptiness gate, the non-directed gate, and the
> signless gate [the witness's recital is compressed]. They are so
> called because they are doors of liberation.
>
> Further, there are said to be “three other samādhis, named
> emptiness-of-emptiness and so forth.” These are emptiness-of-emptiness,
> non-directedness-of-non-directedness, and signlessness-of-signlessness.
> Their names arise because they take emptiness and so forth as Supports.
> Their precise Supports are specified in the following unit.

## 5. Mode and Gate

The aspect structures from 8.24 can occur in worldly-pure and
uncontaminated modes. Only the uncontaminated forms receive the
technical gate designation here. The Bhāṣya explains *vimokṣamukha*
by *mokṣadvāra*, a door of liberation. The eleven-ground statement
belongs to the worldly forms; the supramundane forms occur where
the Path occurs.

## 6. The Further Three

| earlier samādhi name | further name supplied here |
|---|---|
| emptiness | emptiness-of-emptiness |
| non-directed | non-directedness-of-non-directedness |
| signless | signlessness-of-signlessness |

The compact naming explanation refers to their Supports, beginning
with emptiness. It does not yet give an identical direct-Support rule
for all three; 8.26 supplies the differentiated account.

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `śuddhāmalāḥ` gives worldly-pure and uncontaminated modes;
- `nirmalās tu te` restricts the liberation-gate designation to the uncontaminated forms;
- `ādi` expands to the other two doubled names;
- `śūnyatādyālambanatvāt` names a Support relation whose details follow.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is Organon reconstruction. Mode, ground, gate designation,
and naming by Support should remain distinct. “Further” introduces a
new group, without establishing a higher liberative rank or a uniform
self-observation relation.
