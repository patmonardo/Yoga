# VAK_8.25 Bhāṣya — Modes, Gates of Liberation, and Three Further Samādhis

## 1. Kārikā Anchor

> śuddhāmalāḥ nirmalās tu te vimokṣamukhatrayam |
> śūnyatāśūnyatādyākhyās trayo 'parasamādhayaḥ || 8.25 ||

> The three samādhis are of two kinds, worldly-pure and uncontaminated;
> but the uncontaminated ones are called the three gates of liberation.
> There are three further samādhis, named emptiness-of-emptiness and so forth.

Kārikā study: [`VAK_8.25.md`](./VAK_8.25.md).

## 2. Continuous Sanskrit

> ta ete trayaḥ samādhayo dvividhāḥ / śuddhakāś cānāsravāś ca /
> laukikā ekādaśasu bhūmiṣu / lokottarā yatra mārgaḥ /
> anāsravās tv ete trayaḥ samādhayas trīṇi vimokṣamukhāny ucyante /
> mokṣadvāratvāt /
> śūnyatāśūnyatā apraṇihitāpraṇihita ānimittānimittaś ca /
> śūnyatādyālambanatvāt tan nāma /

**Witness.** Pradhan 450|01–11. Voices: Vasubandhu.

## 3. Continuous Translation

> These three samādhis are twofold: worldly-pure and uncontaminated. Worldly: on
> eleven grounds. Supramundane: where the path is. The uncontaminated three are
> called the three gates of liberation — emptiness, non-directed, signless —
> because they are doors of liberation.
>
> Also named: emptiness-of-emptiness, non-directedness-of-non-directedness,
> signlessness-of-signlessness. The name is from taking emptiness and the rest as
> object. Their exact objects: next.

## 5. Name ≠ Gate

Worldly form of the same aspect-work is not the technical door. Further ≠ higher
grade by default. Do not copy one object-rule onto all three — 8.26 splits the
third.

## 6. Two Layers

| | worldly-pure | uncontaminated |
|---|---|---|
| śūnyatā / apraṇihita / ānimitta | eleven grounds | gates |
| further three | named here | objects next |

## 7. The Bhāṣya's Decisions for the Kārikā

- `śuddha` / `amala` = laukika / lokottara;
- `nirmalās tu te` restricts the gate name;
- `ādi` filled as the other two doubled names.

## 8. Interpretation

This section is reconstruction.

Form of Knowing is not yet the gate. Vidyā: mode, then the further object-
relation. Technē of the door is uncontaminated operation of those sixteen under
three headings — still content. Appearance would call the doubled names a
shine looking at a shine. Wait for 8.26: the third is not a copy.
