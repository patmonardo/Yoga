# Dharma-skandha

## A Working Prolegomenon to the System of Science

This essay introduces **Dharma-skandha** as a project-level philosophical determination inferred through the *Abhidharmakośa* Organon. It must not be confused with a proposed sixth Buddhist skandha. In the Kārikā and Bhāṣya, `dharmaskandha` can mean a body, division, or functional unit of teaching. The Organon asks what makes such a teaching-body possible as Science.

The governing thesis is:

> **Dharma-skandha is the scientific product in which Dharma comprehends itself as Skandha, Āyatana, and Dhātu.**

## 1. The Threefold Consideration of Dharma

The Kośa does not present skandha, āyatana, and dhātu as three unrelated inventories. The same dharmas are reorganized according to three distinct systematic functions.

```text
Dharma as Skandha
    = constituted and gathered multiplicity

Dharma as Āyatana
    = relational sphere of manifestation and cognition

Dharma as Dhātu
    = principial domain within the complete system
```

The movement can be provisionally expressed as:

```text
aggregation
    → mediation
    → systematic determination
```

This sequence is not imposed by attaching foreign labels to Buddhist lists. It must be inferred through the Kośa's explicit cross-mappings. The same vijñānaskandha, for example, is considered as mana-āyatana in the sphere-system and as six vijñāna-dhātus together with manodhātu in the domain-system.

The Concept of Dharma is therefore:

```text
<Skandha, Āyatana, Dhātu>
```

Each term preserves a necessary standpoint. Science is not any one member taken in isolation; it is their comprehended movement and coordination.

## 2. Dharma Is Essence and Reflection

For the Organon, **Dharma is Essence**. Essence is not an inert interior hidden behind appearance. It is Reflection: determination becoming what it is through relation, distinction, manifestation, and return into intelligible unity.

```text
immediate determination
    → relation to another determination
    → reflected identity
    → manifestation
    → comprehension
```

Dharma-skandha is therefore not a heap of propositions about dharmas. It is Dharma's reflective organization as Science.

## 3. Name, Speech, and the Essential Discursive “I Think”

The Kośa asks whether a Dharma-body belongs to rūpa as speech or to saṃskāra as name. Both classifications identify a real bearer of teaching, but neither alone states the truth of Dharma-skandha.

```text
Dharma-skandha as speech
    = audible manifestation
    = rūpa-side

Dharma-skandha as name
    = retained formative determination
    = saṃskāra-side
```

Speech without reflected determination is mere sound. Name without possible manifestation is an inert mark. Their unity is the **Essential Discursive “I Think”**: the act through which determinations can be gathered, related, named, asserted, and recognized as one Science.

```text
Name / Saṃskāra
    = reflection inward

Dharma-skandha
    = essential discursive determination

Speech / Rūpa
    = reflection outward
```

The “I Think” here is not an empirical inner monologue. It is the rational unity required for a manifold of determinations to become one communicable and recollectable body of Science.

## 4. The Dhātu and Indriya Systems

The Dhātu System and the Indriya System are reciprocal architectures.

```text
Dhātu System
    = articulation of principial domains

Indriya System
    = articulation of powers capable of disclosure and knowing

their correspondence
    = Sāṃkhya–Yoga as living cognition
```

An indriya is not adequately defined as a gross organ or an occult atom. Its truth is functional: it is a power supporting a determinate cognition, and alteration of that power alters the cognition.

A dhātu is not an inert region containing objects. It is a determinate domain within which a corresponding power and cognition can become actual.

```text
indriya
    ↔ viṣaya
    ↔ vijñāna
```

This correspondence is transcendental because it concerns the conditions under which determinate knowing is possible.

## 5. Sattva as Transparent Intelligibility

Sattva is not exhausted by serenity, pleasant emotion, pure food, or moral respectability. These may be empirical consequences of sattva, but they do not state its Concept.

> **Sattva is the power by which determination becomes luminous and available to true cognition.**

Pure sattva occurs when the cognitive power does not obscure its domain and the domain does not confront cognition as an alien finished object.

```text
indriya ↔ dhātu
        ↓
transparent correspondence
        ↓
Prajñā shines as Jñāna
```

Dharma-skandha is a product of sattva because the system becomes transparent to its own principle through it.

## 6. Rūpa-skandha and the Beginning of Actuality

Rūpa-skandha is the first actuality of the system, but `rūpa` must not be flattened into modern matter. The Kośa presents faculties, sensory domains, manifest action, non-disclosive action, and elemental functions through an inherited atomistic vocabulary. Its philosophical truth is a transactional morphology of sensible and moral powers.

The Organon therefore distinguishes:

```text
Prakṛti
    = productive Transactional Reality

Bhūtādi
    = Primal Matter within cosmogenesis

Tanmātra
    = pure sensible Form

Bhūta
    = Content, the unity of Primal Matter and Form
        ├── subtle Content
        └── gross Content
```

This is a Sāṃkhya–Yoga reconstruction, not a claim that the Kośa explicitly teaches the Sāṃkhya sequence. It provides the accepted cosmogenic architecture against which the Kośa's empirical determinations are interpreted and tested.

## 7. Viṣaya, Thing, and Object

The sensory `arthas` of VAK 1.09 are glossed by the Bhāṣya as `viṣayas`. They must not automatically be translated as finished objects.

```text
viṣaya
    = a priori domain-principle of possible sensible content

empirical content
    = variable appearance within that domain

vastu
    = Thing or real basis

Object
    = comprehended Truth of the Thing
```

Objectivity is an achievement of synthesis and comprehension. A sensory correlate does not become an Object merely by standing opposite a faculty.

## 8. Vijñapti and Avijñapti

Vijñapti and avijñapti articulate manifestation and consequential continuity.

```text
vijñapti
    = action disclosing itself to another

avijñapti
    = ethically determined continuity
      that does not disclose itself in that manner
```

Avijñapti is not an occult atom or a motionless residue. Its `anubandha` is explained as `pravāha`, a continuing stream. It may arise from manifest action or samādhi, remain while attention has moved elsewhere, and persist after the originating manifestation has ceased.

The shadow analogy initially expresses dependence:

```text
vijñapti : avijñapti
    ::
tree : shadow
```

But the analogy fails if treated as passive mirroring. An ordinary shadow ceases with the tree, whereas avijñapti can outlive the manifest action and its original support. Avijñapti is therefore stronger than shadowhood: it is inherited consequential continuity.

## 9. Rational–Empirical Moral Science

The Kośa is neither pure rational construction nor empirical enumeration.

```text
empirical power
    = encounter, comparison, causal testing, correction

rational power
    = domain-formation, valid distinction, systematic unity

Rational–Empirical Science
    = empirical differences comprehended through their proper principles
```

Its practical end does not make it less than Science. The system analyzes how actions arise, become manifest, persist without manifestation, enter living continuities, and produce further consequences.

Moral Agency is therefore:

> **the capacity to comprehend, assume, interrupt, and transform consequence.**

## 10. The Product of the Organon

Dharma-skandha is crystalline Dharma because its compression remains capable of complete inference.

```text
Kārikā
    = principle in compressed form

Bhāṣya
    = articulation, objection, and determination

Organon
    = reconstruction of the systematic movement

Dharma-skandha
    = the communicable scientific product
```

A genuine Dharma-skandha must be expandable into the discourse, examples, practices, and consequences implicit within it. It must also be compressible back into the principle from which those consequences were inferred.

The governing formula is:

```text
<Skandha, Āyatana, Dhātu>
    → Reflection
    → Essential Discursive “I Think”
    → Dharma-skandha
    → Practical Moral Science
```

The work of the Organon is to derive this product determination by determination, `pṛthak pṛthak`, without confusing inherited interpretation with textual fact or speculative reconstruction with literal translation.
