# Dharma Principle Dossier

## Purpose

This dossier gathers the Kośa's Dharmadhātu teaching in one source-first
sequence. It does not replace the chapter-by-chapter studies or turn Dharma
Principle into a generic name for a later realization. Each section records
what the relevant kārikā or Bhāṣya determines; the final synthesis is
explicitly marked as a project inquiry.

The controlled terms are:

```text
skandha     = Base
āyatana     = Essence
dhātu       = Principle
dharmadhātu = Dharma Principle
```

## The Question That Orders the Collection

[VAK 1.02](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02.md) defines Abhidharma first in its ultimate
sense:

```text
stainless prajñā with attendants
    = ultimate Abhidharma
```

The Bhāṣya further determines this as an outflow-free five-Base complex whose
operation is discrimination of Dharma. It then extends the name
conventionally to the prajñā and treatise directed toward attaining that
stainless discrimination.

Thus the focal point is not the impure field taken for its own sake. The
focal point is **stainless prajñā with its attendants**. The analysis of
impurity identifies what this Science discriminates and what no longer
sustains an outflow.

## Source Witnesses

### 1. The Pure / Impure Division

[VAK 1.04 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.04_bhasya.md) gives the summary
designation of all Dharmas:

```text
Impure
    = conditioned Dharma other than the Truth of the Path

Pure
    = conditioned Truth of the Path
      + unconditioned Dharma
```

Its decisive distinction is between an outflow's object-relation and its
persistence through a Dharma. An outflow can take the Path or Cessation as
its object without persisting through either.

[VAK 1.05 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.05_bhasya.md) states the Pure field
positively: the Truth of the Path, space, cessation through discrimination,
and cessation not through discrimination. Their shared criterion is that
outflows do not persist in them.

The Path is therefore the required hinge: it is conditioned and Pure. Purity
is neither mere withdrawal from conditioned activity nor a synonym for the
unconditioned.

### 2. The Constitution of Dharma Principle

[VAK 1.15](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.15.md) first names Dharma Essence and Dharma
Principle. They include:

```text
Feeling Base
Reflection Base
Formations Base
Form Controller (avijñapti)
three unconditioned Dharmas
```

[VAK 1.16 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.16_bhasya.md) confirms this
cross-classification at the chapter's census:

```text
Feeling, Reflection, Formations
+ avijñapti
+ unconditioned Dharma
    → Dharma Essence + Dharma Principle
```

This is a cross-classification, not a separate homogeneous Base. Form
Controller remains within Form Base while also entering Dharma Essence and
Dharma Principle; the unconditioned has no Base.

### 3. Scope: All Dharma, Not Only the Appropriated Field

[VAK 1.18 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.18_bhasya.md) gives the necessary scope
distinction:

```text
Bases of appropriation      → all Impure Dharmas
Bases                        → all conditioned Dharmas
Essences and Principles      → all Dharmas
```

The same passage states that Form Base, Mind Essence, and Dharma Principle
jointly include all Dharmas. Their inclusion is by own-nature, not by
another's nature. This distinguishes principial membership from occasional
or conventional gathering; it does not erase causal, supportive, or
object-relations.

### 4. The Outflow-Free Side of Dharma Principle

[VAK 1.31 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.31_bhasya.md) identifies Mind Principle,
Dharma Principle, and Mind-Cognition Principle as the only three Principles
admitting both outflow-bearing and outflow-free instances.

```text
Outflow-free instances
    = instances included in Path-truth
      + unconditioned instances
```

Unconditioned instances belong under Dharma Principle. Path-truth supplies
outflow-free conditioned Cognition and its relevant accompanying Dharmas
without making Cognition unconditioned.

### 5. Association, Object-Support, and Appropriation

[VAK 1.32 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.32_bhasya.md) keeps the associated and
unassociated portions of Dharma Principle distinct. Its classification by
*vitarka* and *vicāra* is an association classification, not an
outflow-status classification.

[VAK 1.34](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.34.md) adds a second distinction:

```text
associated mental-factor portion of Dharma Principle
    → object-taking

whole Dharma Principle
    → necessarily unappropriated
```

Object-taking and organic appropriation therefore have different extensions.
The whole Dharma Principle is never a Form support taken up into a living
continuum, although an associated portion can take an object-support.

### 6. The First Pure Moment and Substance-Status

[VAK 1.38 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.38_bhasya.md) makes two central
determinations:

```text
the Unconditioned is present in Dharma Principle
    → Dharma Principle alone bears substance-status in this local sense

at the first pure complex
    → citta is Mind Principle + Mind-Cognition Principle
    → co-arisen Dharmas are Dharma Principle
```

The first pure complex is receptivity to Dharma-knowledge concerning
suffering. Its special one-moment absence of homogeneous continuation does
not make it unconditioned; it marks a specified causal exception at the
beginning of pure occurrence.

### 7. Path-Status and Ascertainative View

[VAK 1.40 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.40_bhasya.md) assigns the final three
Principles—Mind, Dharma, and Mind-Cognition—across three path-statuses:

```text
latent afflictions and their complex
    → abandoned by seeing

remaining Impure Dharmas
    → abandoned through cultivation

Pure Dharmas
    → not to be abandoned
```

[VAK 1.41](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.41.md) then identifies only a portion of Dharma
Principle as view. This portion includes afflicted views, worldly right view,
and the Pure views of trainees and adepts. Science of Principles becomes view
only through ascertainment; discernment co-born with sensory Cognition does
not thereby count as view.

### 8. Dharma Principle as a Field for Mind-Cognition

[VAK 1.39](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.39.md) classifies Dharma Principle among the
outer field-side Principles and gives it a special functional status:

```text
every Dharma can become a field for Mind-Cognition
    → Dharma Principle is always functionally participating
```

This does not make Dharma Principle a single object or identify it with the
Mind Principle. It specifies its availability as the field of
Mind-Cognition.

## Study Sequence

Read the witnesses in this order:

```text
1.02  → what Abhidharma is
1.04–1.05
      → why Pure / Impure is the first discrimination
1.15–1.16
      → what enters Dharma Principle
1.18  → how its scope exceeds the appropriated field
1.31–1.34
      → its outflow-status, association, and appropriation determinations
1.38–1.41
      → first pure occurrence, path-status, and ascertainative view
```

This order prevents two reductions: treating Dharma Principle as merely the
Impure field, or treating it as an unarticulated absolute.

## Project Inquiry: Pure Māyā and the Entry into the Pure

This section is a project synthesis, not a claim that the Kośa itself uses
the phrase “Pure Māyā theory.”

The source supplies a determinate entry into the Pure:

```text
stainless prajñā with attendants
    → ultimate Abhidharma

Path-truth and the unconditioned
    → outflows do not persist through them

first pure complex
    → Dharma Principle includes the co-arisen Dharmas
```

The proposed Pure Māyā inquiry asks how the conditioned Pure Path can be
mastered as Appearance without becoming an appropriated field. Its initial
discipline is:

```text
do not deny Form or conditioned occurrence
do not mistake an object-relation for persistence of an outflow
do not reduce Dharma Principle to its Impure instances
do not turn the Pure into another appropriated support
```

On this inquiry, “Pure Skandhas” is best kept as a project shorthand for the
outflow-free five-Base complex described in the Bhāṣya to 1.02. It is not a
source-defined generic category, and it must not erase the separate
classifications of Base, Essence, and Principle.

The dossier's working conclusion is therefore:

> Abhidharma's detailed presentation of impurity is ordered toward the
> stainless discrimination in which the Pure is known as conditioned Path
> and unconditioned Dharma, while Dharma Principle comprehends the whole
> field without becoming identical with its appropriated instances.

## Project Inquiry: The Five Wisdom-Attendants

The Kośa does not name a doctrine of “Five Wisdoms” in these passages. It
does, however, define ultimate Abhidharma as Pure prajñā with an
outflow-free five-Base retinue. The following map is therefore a
Kośa-first derivation: each wisdom is proposed only where a Base's source
operation supplies a determinate Pure counterpart.

| Base | Source operation | Wisdom-attendant | Proposed Pure operation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Form Base | Form is affected; *avijñapti* is a wholesome or unwholesome Form-continuity | Dharma-Principle Wisdom | Form is known through its Dharma Essence and Dharma Principle entry, without being reduced to an appropriated display |
| Cognition Base | respective apprehension (*prativijñapti*) | Mirror-like Wisdom | Cognition reflects each Condition without possession, distortion, or a second object-world |
| Feeling Base | undergoing (*anubhava*) | Equality Wisdom | pleasant, painful, and neutral undergoing cease to govern latent attachment, aversion, and ignorance |
| Reflection Base | taking up marks (*nimittodgrahaṇa*) | Discriminating Wisdom | marks are differentiated precisely without being reified into independent determinations |
| Formations Base | active formation, with volition predominant because it has the nature of karma | All-accomplishing Wisdom | formation becomes effective Path-operation rather than outflow-driven karmic production |

### 1. Form Base and Dharma-Principle Wisdom

[VAK 1.11](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.11.md) defines *avijñapti* as wholesome or
unwholesome Form-continuity. [VAK 1.15](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.15.md) then gives
its double placement:

```text
avijñapti
    → Form Base
    → Dharma Essence
    → Dharma Principle
```

This is the reason Dharma-Principle Wisdom belongs with Form Base. It is not
a decorative pairing between a visible world and an abstract totality. Form
already has a non-disclosive continuity that crosses into Dharma Essence and
Dharma Principle. The proposed Pure operation does not deny Form; it
distinguishes Form's Dharma-Principle entry from its appropriated display.

### 2. Cognition Base and Mirror-like Wisdom

[VAK 1.16](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.16.md) defines Cognition as respective
apprehension:

```text
vijñāna
    = prativijñapti
    = apprehension with respect to each Condition
```

The same Cognition Base becomes Mind Essence and seven Principles: the six
Cognition Principles together with Mind Principle. Its wisdom-counterpart is
therefore mirror-like, not because it produces an image of a separate
world, but because it can receive determinations without seizing them as
possessions. Mirror-like Wisdom is the Pure activity of the Cognition Base.

### 3. Feeling Base and Equality Wisdom

[VAK 1.14](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.14.md) defines Feeling as undergoing:
pleasant, painful, or neither. [VAK 2.03 Bhāṣya](../Organon/02-indriya/VAK_2.03_bhasya.md)
states the afflicted articulation:

```text
pleasant feeling    → condition for latent attachment
painful feeling     → condition for latent aversion
neutral feeling     → condition for latent ignorance
```

Feeling is not identical with any affliction, and the same Indriya teaching
allows feeling to participate in purification. Equality Wisdom is thus not
the abolition of feeling or a neutralization of every tone. It is the Pure
operation in which undergoing no longer assigns graspable, rejectable, or
negligible rank to its own contents.

### 4. Reflection Base and Discriminating Wisdom

[VAK 1.14](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.14.md) defines Reflection through the taking up
of marks: color, extent, person, relation, and affective sign. This is the
site of articulated distinction. [VAK 1.41](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.41.md)
specifies that discernment becomes view only when it ascertains through
attentive examination.

Discriminating Wisdom is therefore not a multiplication of marks. It is the
Pure taking up of marks: exact differentiation without making the marked
Dharma into an independently fixed object. Its intimacy with Mirror-like
Wisdom is required. Reflection supplies determinate distinction; Cognition
receives that distinction without possession.

### 5. Formations Base and All-accomplishing Wisdom

[VAK 1.15 Bhāṣya](../Organon/01-dhatu/VAK_1.15_bhasya.md) identifies volition as
predominant in active formation because it has the nature of karma, while
preserving the wider Formations Base beyond volition alone. The same passage
requires the remaining mental and dissociated formations to be included in
full comprehension and abandonment.

All-accomplishing Wisdom is the proposed Pure counterpart of this active
formation. It does not promise effectiveness for an appropriating agent.
It names the Formations Base when its efficacy is Path-operation and no
longer the production of outflow-driven karmic continuity.

### The Required Unity

The five wisdom-attendants are not five experiences placed beside the
five Bases. They name the outflow-free operation of the five-Base retinue
that constitutes ultimate Abhidharma:

```text
Form                 → Dharma-Principle Wisdom
Cognition            → Mirror-like Wisdom
Feeling              → Equality Wisdom
Reflection           → Discriminating Wisdom
Formations           → All-accomplishing Wisdom
```

The order is not a temporal ladder. Form and Cognition provide the
Dharma-Principle and mirror-like poles; Feeling, Reflection, and Formations
specify how undergoing, distinction, and efficacy are purified within their
relation. The map remains a project derivation to be tested against the
Kośa's treatment of Path, Knowledge, and Samāpatti.
