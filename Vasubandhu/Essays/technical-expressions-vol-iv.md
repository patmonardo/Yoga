# Volume IV: Technical Expressions for Jñāna and Samāpatti

## Scope

Volume IV begins the Jñāna–Samāpatti movement of the Organon's Subjective
Wheel, `[[Anuśaya:Mārga] [Jñāna:Samāpatti]]`. [Volume III](technical-expressions-vol-iii.md)
develops Anuśaya–Mārga. [Volumes I](technical-expressions-vol-i.md) and
[II](technical-expressions-vol-ii.md) continue to govern the established
Base–Essence–Principle and Basis–Support–Condition distinctions.

This opening index comes from a scan of the existing Chapter 7 and 8 study
headings and selected technical-vocabulary sections. The local Sanskrit
witness was checked at the chapter openings: 7.01 `[391|02–391|15]` and
8.01 `[432|05–433|03]`. The later loci
below are leads for sequential review, not a completed source audit. Each
expression has three jobs: establish its Sanskrit force in the passage,
control its English in continuous translation, and state its Organon
determination separately. The third column names a question where the
reading has not yet established an answer.
