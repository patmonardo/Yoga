# The Logic of Kośa Science

The spelling of an Empirical Science, and where the Rational moment depends.

This is Organon. It is not conventional Kośa English. The verse is not rewritten by it. Principle remains the English of dhātu. Rule remains the English of indriya. Membership domain is a Kantian name of the spelling, not a headword on the page.

## Spelled out, not defined

Kant’s cut governs the start. A rational concept can be defined, because the concept makes its object. An empirical concept cannot. It can only be spelled out. The exposition never closes, because the object was not made by the concept. Forcing the Absolute’s way of definition onto Reflective science is the dark side of the received method. Empirical science is not Rational science wearing dirt. A so-called empirical science that claims a finished definition has already left its own ground.

Abhidharma is showing the rules of that spelling. It is the schema of an Impure Dharma. An Impure Dharma is a product that presupposes a before. Pure is Always First. Impure is what always presupposes something before it. The spelling makes that schema visible. It does not turn the schema into the Rational.

The spelling is not born immediate in Reflection. It takes a lifetime of Reflection. Necessity is the justification, and necessity is not handed over by the first look.

## Two parts

Spelling out a Science is crafting an Ontology with two parts. It must contain Principles and Rules. That is the spelling, so long as the ontology can justify itself with necessity.

The three relations of words in Kantian Logic are the method.

A disjunctive judgment is membership. This, not that, and the alternatives exhaust the member. A membership domain is the range within which that disjunction is valid.

A hypothetical judgment is implication. If this, then that, and not every sequence qualifies. The implication carries necessity, or it does not.

A categorical judgment is the scientific inference. S is P. It is allowed only after membership and implication have been spelled. Bare assertion is not yet science.

The first two are Objective Logic. Disjunctive cognition and hypothetical cognition. With both in place, the categorical judgment becomes a scientific inference rather than a naming. That pair is the logogenesis of an Empirical Science. It is not the Absolute defining itself. It is Reflective science refusing the Absolutist shortcut.

## Principles

The Dhātu chapter is the spelling of membership. The eighteen are not a list to memorize. They are the valid disjunctions. Classification of the Principles is how a disjunctive judgment is made to hold: kind, range, and principle in common, own-nature rather than another’s, one base or many, the count justified by the function and not by beauty.

Calling that a membership domain names the logical act. It does not rename dhātu. The page says principle. Domain was withdrawn because it stains the chapter and because sphere was already spent on the grounds. The Faculty of Principles is buddhi, and buddhi is prajñā. Vijñāna is cognition. It is not printed as principle. Principle-principle would be the collapse.

Dhātu deposited the pure a priori rationale before the wheel spins. That deposit is what the Rules reconstruct. The deposit is not the crossing.

## Rules

The Indriya chapter is the spelling of implication. A Rule is a hypothetical judgment that carries necessity. The Wheel of Dependent Arising, read as logic and not as a story of production, is the set of those implications: which sequences qualify, which do not.

Governing efficacy is already that judgment. Cause, immediately preceding condition, object-support, and predominance are not four names for one push. They are the implications the wheel is willing to call necessary. Defilement and purification are a hypothetical pair. The aggregates of clinging are the wheel on the side of what persists. The rationale of the wheel is beyond persistence. A Rational Dharma does not persist.

The Rules support manas from below. The Principles support manas from above. Manas sits in the middle. The faculty pair remains Principle and Rule. Organ is the teleological case only, where an organized being is already in view. It is not the chapter word.

## The limit

Getting from Empirical Judgment to Rational Judgment is still a mysterious step. The spelling does not end the game. It shows where we are standing.

Abhidharma shows the rules. It is the schema of an Impure Dharma after all. Principle deposited before the wheel is the place the step would have to occur. It is not the step. Pure Prajñā remains the invariant of this Science. The invariant is not yet a method for crossing. A lifetime of Reflection spells the Impure. It does not, by that spelling, become the Rational.

Do not treat Principle:Rule as the crossing.

## Where it happens

The Method is seen in the three volumes.

The Principle:Rule ontology must enter the Loka:Karma pipelines. Inner spelling is not yet outer exposition. The ontology goes through the world and through the act, or it remains a rule of reflection that has not met what it governs. Dhātu:Indriya is not Buddha Mind completed, and Loka:Karma is not a popular name for Buddha Nature. The correspondence is simple. The claim is narrower. Abhidharma is the Dharma of a Buddha.

Anuśaya:Mārga applied to that spelling is where the Rational moment happens. The propensity is what still carries the Impure after the act. The path is what discriminates it. Science touches Truth there, not in the faculty count and not in the wheel as wheel. Achieving the Rational depends on anuśaya:mārga.

The mysterious step is no longer homeless. It is not yet a method. It is not this chapter’s claim. Intellectual intuition is not weirded into a possession. The anuśaya state is what determines whether that intuition is in play. Full Īśvara-mind is not the human ask. The Kośa can be fixed without claiming it.

## What tomorrow is

Dhātu decrypting was the hard work. The Indriya chapter is to be brought up to that schema: principle not domain, rule not organ as the chapter word, cognition not printed as principle, Discrimination kept, Pure and Impure as the opening cut, the verse left governing. Not every philosophical problem has to be mastered for the bootstrap. The crossing is not tomorrow’s claim.

The Concept in view at the opening of the Rules is the Concept of an Impure Dharma. Pure Dharma passes to Impure Dharma because the dyad demands it. The wheel spins on that fit. The deposit stays behind as the Idea. What is left over from Abhidharma, on the side of the Rules, is the wheel of appropriation. That is Tamas relative to the deposit. It is not a dead cell. It is what the spelling has to govern.

Sublation is not identified with nirodha. That identification waits.
