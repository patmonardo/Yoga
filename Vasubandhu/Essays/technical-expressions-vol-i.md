# Volume I: Technical Expressions for Dhātu and Indriya

## Scope

Volume I follows the Objective Science from Dhātu to Indriya. The entries
distinguish a Sanskrit expression's local force, the English used in a
continuous translation, and the Organon determination. The latter is a
project reading, never a silent replacement for the source's wording.
Each passage can refine its entry. The [earlier combined Pre-Techne](technical-expressions-vol-i-ii.md)
preserves the extended arguments behind these controls.

The Organon reads `[[Dhātu:Indriya] [Loka:Karma]]` as the Objective Wheel.
Within Volume I, Skandha, Āyatana, and Dhātu articulate Base, Essence, and
Principle. The Essence:Form Engine names their systematic movement; these
names do not claim to be dictionary definitions of the Sanskrit terms.

Sāṃkhya is a **Rational Overlord** in the Organon reading of the Kośa:
its ordering of Manas, Buddhi, Ahaṃkāra, and the Faculties governs the
comparison through which Kośa determinations are tested and placed.
Local translations remain answerable to the Kośa's Sanskrit and Bhāṣya.

## Dhātu: Base, Essence, Principle

| Expression | Translation control | Organon determination |
|---|---|---|
| *dharma* | Retain **Dharma** in project compounds; in a passage translate its stated sense, whether a teaching, classifiable phenomenon, or principle. | One Dharma has the Defined Concept `<Base, Essence, Principle>`; Dharma Base, Dharma Essence, and Dharma Principle are its distinct projections. |
| *skandha* | **Aggregate** conventionally; **Base** when the Organon determination is explicitly in view. | Base is gathered multiplicity capable of systematic articulation through Essence and Principle, not a heap. |
| *rūpa-skandha / dharma-skandha* | Keep **Form aggregate** and the passage's Dharma classification distinct. | The Absolute Base spans Form and Dharma; Form and Law are determinations within Absolute Dharma. |
| *nāma-skandha / jñāna-skandha / vijñāna-skandha* | Keep source aggregate names distinct from the project's **ordinary-knowledge triad** and its special Cognition member. | The Jñāna-skandha and Vijñāna-skandha grouping is a project view of four non-Form aggregates, not a sixth aggregate or a source compound. |
| *āyatana* | Its conventional sense is passage-specific: sphere, basis, or domain; **Essence** marks the controlled Organon reading. | Essence is the Entry into an Essential Relation of an impure Dharma. Mind-Essence is Reflective Science at this level. |
| *dhātu* | Use the local conventional sense where needed, including **realm** in realm compounds; **Principle** is the controlled Organon rendering for the eighteenfold cognitive matrix. | Dhātu is Determinate Science and an operative cognitive Principle. It must not be rendered **Element**, which belongs to *bhūta*. |
| *bhūta / mahābhūta / bhautika* | **Element / Great Element / Element-dependent**. | Material support and its derivatives remain distinct from Dhātu as Principle. |
| *rūpa* | **Form**; specify aggregate, visible Form, or form realm according to the passage. | Form is not an automatic synonym for Matter. |
| *dharmāyatana / dharmadhātu* | State the source classification; **Dharma Essence / Dharma Principle** in the respective Organon systems. | Two determinations of Dharma within one Defined Concept, neither an Essence nested inside another nor a synonym for the other. |

## Cognition and its equipment

| Expression | Translation control | Organon determination |
|---|---|---|
| *vijñāna* | **Cognition**, more precisely **Discriminative Cognition**; never “consciousness.” | Cognition joins and governs Perception and Conception; its relation to Manas and Dharma-skandha must be determined without collapsing it into either. |
| *citta* | **Consciousness** in *cittāśraya* at 2.05; determine other occurrences from context. | Consciousness is wider than Mind. The Kośa concerns both Reason and Understanding in the Organon reading; Reason is higher than Understanding, and its Ideas are not functions of Understanding. This project ordering is distinct from any one Sanskrit occurrence. |
| Mind / *vijñāna* / *prajñā* as cognitive powers | Keep **Mind**, **Cognition**, and **discriminative comprehension** distinct in local translation. | Each can serve as a Basis of Consciousness without becoming identical to Consciousness or to the other powers. Reason remains higher than Understanding; the hierarchy is an Organon determination, not an added Sanskrit claim in 2.05–2.06. |
| *manas* | **Mind** according to its function in the passage. | Produced Cognitive Instrument, equipped with Mind Essence and Mind Principle. |
| *mana-āyatana* | State the source cross-classification; **Mind Essence** in the Organon reading. | The Essence determination of Manas, distinct from Manas itself and from Mind-Cognition. |
| *manodhātu* | State its source role as support; **Mind Principle** in the Organon reading. | The broader Principle of Manas and project Ordinary Logic; the Bhāṣya's immediately past Cognition is a local account of its support role. |
| *manovijñāna / manovijñānadhātu* | **Mind-Cognition / Mind-Cognition Principle** in the controlled system. | The latter is one of the six Cognition Principles and project Transcendental Logic, distinct from the broader *manodhātu*. |
| *caitta / caitasika* | **Mental factor** or **mental concomitant**, retaining the source's form. | The term can mark operations involving Mind without making Mind the whole of Consciousness. Under the Sāṃkhya ordering that governs the Organon comparison, such factors are also factors of Consciousness; they do not exhaust the roles of Buddhi, Ahaṃkāra, or Reason. Keep the Kośa's association claims explicit. |
| *vijñapti / avijñapti* | **Manifestation or disclosure / non-disclosing continuity** as the passage requires. | Keep this Dhātu dyad distinct from Cognition itself and from the Indriya acquisition dyad. |

## Indriya: Faculty and acquisition

| Expression | Translation control | Organon determination |
|---|---|---|
| *indriya* | **Faculty**: governing efficacy or power, as in 2.01. An anatomical organ requires its own source term. | Determine the Faculty by what it governs; Indriya develops the objective account's operative side. |
| *ādhipatya* in 2.01–2.03 | **Governing efficacy**: the shared predicate by which distinct faculties are named. In 2.02 the Bhāṣya distinguishes it from mere causal contribution through *adhikaṃ prabhutvam*, superior power. | Test which faculty governs which Meaning or process; a contributing Condition is not thereby a Faculty. |
| *svārtha / upalabdhi* in 2.02 | **One's own Meaning / apprehension**; *artha* is not translated “object.” | The Object Request seeks Meaning in apprehension before Object, the comprehended truth of Vastu, is disclosed. |
| *kāyāt strī-puruṣendriye* in 2.02 | **The female and male faculties are from the body-faculty**: the Bhāṣya identifies a named bodily portion, rather than two further substances. | Distinct governing functions can be specified within one bodily Faculty. |
| *prāpti / aprāpti* | **Acquisition / non-acquisition** in a continuum. | This Indriya dyad is distinct from *vijñapti:avijñapti*; acquisition, possession, and first attainment require separate tests. |
| *lābha* | **Obtaining** or **acquisition** where the passage uses it. | A cross-reference under acquisition, not an automatic synonym for *prāpti*. |
| *kuśala / akuśala / avyākṛta* | **Wholesome / unwholesome / ethically indeterminate**. | These predicates cannot be inferred from the influx classification alone. |
| *nikāyasthiti / saṃkleśa / vyavadāna* in 2.03 | **Class-persistence / defilement / purification**. The verse pairs them respectively with life, the five feelings, and those beginning with faith. | Keep the governed processes distinct: persistence is not creation, feeling is not affliction, and checking afflictions is not final abandonment. |
| *ājñāsyāmi / ājñā / ājñātāvin* in 2.04 | **“I shall know” / “knowledge” / “one who has known”** as three Faculty names. | The first two govern attainment of their respective successor Faculties; the third governs nirvāṇa. The names do not by themselves define three tenses of one act. |
| *uttarottara-saṃprāpti / nirvāṇa / ādi* in 2.04 | **Successive attainment / nirvāṇa / and so forth**. The Bhāṣya specifies parinirvāṇa for the third and takes *ādi* as another explanation: abandonment by seeing, abandonment by cultivation, and pleasant abiding in this life. | Keep the verse's nirvāṇa apart from the prose's parinirvāṇa; do not turn the alternative into a fourth Faculty or equate *saṃprāpti* here with every later use of acquisition. |
| *cittāśraya / tad-vikalpa* in 2.05 | **Basis of Consciousness / differentiation of that Basis**. The Bhāṣya identifies the Basis with the six faculties and their sixfold āyatana; *tad* resumes that Basis. | Here *āśraya* is Basis, not *ālambana* (Support), and *citta* is not the single *manas* faculty among the six. Although *vikalpa* can name a mental factor elsewhere, the Bhāṣya specifies female and male differentiation here. Mental factors can be understood as factors of Consciousness without reducing Reason to Understanding. |
| *saṃbhāra / yāvatā tāvat* in 2.05 | **Provision for purification / as far as that, so far**. The Bhāṣya assigns the five beginning with faith to provision and the three realization faculties to purification. | The correlative bounds the intended twenty-two-Faculty enumeration locally. A power can govern an act yet remain outside this enumeration; the five's provision role does not make them inert or erase their efficacy for purification. |
| *pravṛtti / nivṛtti / āśraya / tathā / vā* in 2.06 | **Engagement / withdrawal / Basis / likewise / or**. The Bhāṣya distributes fourteen and eight Faculties over the same four functional places. | *Vā* marks another opinion about number and order; *tathā* transfers the four places, not the same members. The *āśraya* role remains Basis in both courses. The passage does not flatten these powers into one level of cognition. |
| *duḥkhendriya / sukhendriya / kāyikī / caitasī* in 2.07 | **Pain-Faculty / Pleasure-Faculty / bodily / mental**. The Bhāṣya supplies bodily feeling in the elliptical pleasure clause; agreeable mental feeling is Pleasure-Faculty in the third dhyāna. | Keep tone, mode, level, and Faculty-name distinct. The same classification does not mean one bodily feeling becomes mental. |
| *aśātā / śātā / upaghātikā / anugrāhikā / pañcavijñānakāya* in 2.07 | **Disagreeable / agreeable / injurious / supportive / five Cognition-groups**. The first two are verse terms; the next two are commentary glosses, with *upaghātikā* a provisional repair of the running witness's *upadhātikā*. | Experienced tone does not establish ethical status. Absence of the five groups explains absence of bodily feeling, not absence of all Cognition or why mental pleasure is named pleasure rather than gladness. |
| *anyatra sā / saumanasya / daurmanasya / prīti* in 2.08 | **Elsewhere, that [agreeable mental feeling] / gladness / distress / rapture**. The Bhāṣya specifies the Desire-realm and first two dhyānas for gladness and locally identifies rapture with gladness. | Preserve the third-dhyāna exception; do not turn the local identification into a corpus-wide synonym rule. |
| *upekṣā / madhyā / ubhayī / avikalpanāt* in 2.08 | **Neutral feeling / middle / of both kinds / because of non-differentiation**. Neither agreeable nor disagreeable feeling is bodily and mental but counted as one Faculty. | Non-difference concerns remaining neutral, not identity of the modes in every respect. Keep the arising account distinct from the effect account. |
| *prāyeṇa vikalpanāt / viṣayavaśāt / svarasena* in 2.08 | **For the most part through differentiation / through the force of the Condition / in its own course**. | The qualified mental pleasure-and-pain claim is not universal causation. Bodily pleasure and pain remain possible for an arhat; neutral feeling is not thereby unconditioned or an attainment. |
| *nava trīṇi / dvidhā nava* in 2.09 | **The nine are designated as the three / nine admit either status**. Path-designation and outflow-status are separate questions concerning the same nine types. | The nine are not nine factors co-present in each Path-event. Their either-status does not make the three realization Faculties partly with outflows. |
| *amalaṃ trayam / rūpīṇi / duḥkhe* in 2.09 | **The stainless triad / Form Faculties / the two painful Faculties**. The Bhāṣya glosses stainless as without outflows, counts seven Form Faculties, and identifies the dual as pain and distress. | Preserve the 3–10–9 partition of twenty-two. Neither with-outflows status nor faith-group membership alone establishes ethical quality or the bearer's attainment. |
| *vipāka / savipāka / tat tv ekam* in 2.10 | **Maturation / producing maturation / that one alone [invariably]**. *Tat* resumes distress, not the twelve from which distress was excluded. | Keep being a result and producing a result independent. Distress alone invariably produces maturation; ten other types can produce it or not. |
| *jīvita / dvedhā dvādaśa / daśa dvidhā* in 2.10 | **Life / twelve twofold as maturation / ten twofold as producers**. The prose qualifies the life rule for concentration-projected life. | The partitions are 1 + 12 + 9 and 1 + 10 + 11, not one karmic ranking. Keep the reported lifespan alternatives and adopted explanation distinct; the formal model does not validate a practice. |
| *mano 'nyavittiśraddhādīni / aṣṭakaṃ kuśalam* in 2.11 | **Mind, other feelings, and those beginning with faith / the eight are wholesome**. The opening completes 2.10's ten; the eight begin the new ethical question. | Do not identify the carried ten with the wholesome eight. The latter are the five beginning with faith and the three realization Faculties. |
| *dvidhā daurmanasyam / anyā vittiḥ / anyad ekadhā* in 2.11 | **Distress twofold / other feeling / the remainder onefold**. Distress can be wholesome or unwholesome; Mind and four other feelings admit all three qualities; seven Form Faculties and life are exclusively indeterminate. | Preserve 8 + 1 + 5 + 8 = 22. Neutral feeling is not ethical indeterminacy; permitted qualities are alternatives across instances, not simultaneous qualities. |
| *kāmāpta / rūpāpta / ārūpyāpta* in 2.12 | **Desire / Form / Formless Realm-connected**. The Bhāṣya unfolds *āpta* as *pratisaṃyukta* and explicitly carries exclusions forward. | Possible connection ranges contain 19, 15, and 8 Faculty-types, not co-present factors. Keep type, instance, and bearer distinct. |
| *strīpumindriye / duḥkhe / sukhe / rūpi* in 2.12 | **Female and male Faculties / pain and distress / pleasure and gladness / what possesses Form**. The pairs are accusative dual exclusions, not locatives. | Remove both pleasant Faculties for the Formless range. The stainless triad is unconnected, not assigned to a higher conditioned realm; the sūtra's male name does not reinstate Male-Faculty. |
| *manovittitrayam / dviheyā durmanaskatā / nava bhāvanayā* in 2.13 | **Mind and three feelings / distress abandonable in two ways / nine by cultivation**. Pleasure, gladness, and neutral feeling join Mind in the threefold group; pain joins the seven Form Faculties and life in the nine. | Preserve 4 + 1 + 9 + 5 + 3 = 22. Threefold now concerns abandonment, not ethical quality. |
| *pañca tv aheyāny api / na trayam / nirdoṣa* in 2.13 | **The five also not to be abandoned / not the triad / faultless**. The five with outflows are cultivation-abandonable; without outflows they are not abandonable. The faultless realization triad is not fit to be abandoned. | Gerundive status is not completed action, ethical badness, or destruction of a Faculty. Not abandonable does not mean permanent or continuously operative. |
| *ādau / vipāka / labhyate / taiḥ* in 2.14 | **Initially / as maturation / is acquired / by them**. *Taiḥ* resumes the spontaneously born; the witness retains singular *labhyate* with dual *dve*. | Carry initial maturation scope through every count. Relinking presence does not imply acquisition as maturation; Mind and neutral feeling are afflicted there. |
| *avyañjana / ekavyañjana / ubhayavyañjana / uttara* in 2.14 | **Without sexual characteristic / with one / with both / higher**. The spontaneous cases yield six, seven, or eight Faculties; higher is specified as the Formless realm. | Characteristics condition the extra female/male Faculty-count, rather than themselves becoming Faculties. The Formless count of one concerns maturation, not the absence of Mind. |
| *tyāga / nirodhayati / uparamān nārūpye* in 2.15 | **Loss / brings to cessation / uncertain opening sequence**. The Bhāṣya's death question supports the Formless terminal reading; the opening's morphology remains unsettled. | Cessation at death is neither initial maturation in reverse nor path-abandonment. The causative does not establish deliberate action. |
| *sakṛnmaraṇa / kramamṛtyu / śubhe sarvatra pañca ca* across 2.15–2.16 | **Death all at once / gradual death / with wholesome consciousness, everywhere five in addition**. The continuation gives four for gradual death and adds the faith-group five in wholesome cases. | Keep numbered verse and cross-verse qualifications identifiable. The sensory five are not the faith-group five; final consciousness is not Mind-Faculty. |
| *navāptiḥ / antyaphalayoḥ / saptāṣṭanavabhir dvayoḥ* in 2.16 | **Attainment through nine / of the extreme fruits / through seven, eight, or nine for the middle two**. The extremes are stream-entry and arhatship, not the last two fruits. | Preserve route and Path-stage distinctions; equal counts need not identify equal compositions. |
| *ekādaśabhiḥ / ekasya saṃbhavāt* in 2.17's continuation of 2.16 | **Through eleven / because possible for one person**. One arhatship attainment uses one of three feelings; repeated attainments may range across all three. | Nine is not necessarily simultaneous, and eleven is across a history, not three feelings at once. Preserve the reported relapse argument without generalizing it. |
| *samanvāgata / yukta / anvita / avaśyam* in 2.17 | **Possessing / endowed / endowed / necessarily**. The Bhāṣya resolves any one of neutral feeling, life, or Mind as entailing possession of all three. | Endowment is not simultaneous occurrence, repeated attainment, or an exact total of three. Other Faculties are not fixed by this implication alone. |
| *apratilabdha / vihīna / aniyama* in 2.17 | **Not acquired / lost / no fixed rule**. Different histories can ground non-possession; ordinary-person and dispassion qualifications remain local. | Do not replace possession by current operation. Damaged realization-Faculty compounds remain provisional; the opening eleven-person clause concerns repeated attainment, not possession of eleven. |
| *sukhakāyābhyām / cakṣurādimān / saumanasyī* in 2.18 | **Through pleasure or body / eye-possessor and so forth / gladness-possessor**. Four distributes over two cases; eye, ear, nose, tongue each entail a five, while gladness entails a different five. | Necessary companionship includes the given Faculty, not an exclusive total. Equal counts do not mean identical sets or simultaneous occurrence. |
| *strīndriyādimān / aṣṭābhiḥ* across 2.18–2.19 | **Female-Faculty possessor and so forth / with eight**. Female, male, and distress each add to the pain-seven; any faith-group Faculty entails the five and the common triad instead. | The eight is a cross-verse completion. The second-dhyāna pleasure answer distinguishes possession from attainment without supplying a latent-state mechanism. |
| *ājñājñātendriyānvita / ājñāsyāmīndriyopeta* in 2.19 | **Possessing the knowledge-Faculty or the Faculty of one who has known / possessing “I shall know.”** The first two separately entail eleven; the third entails thirteen. | The elevens share ten but differ in the given Faculty. The damaged introductory gloss remains provisional; following explicit Faculty-names support the contrast. |
| *tair eva daśabhiḥ / trayodaśabhir anvitaḥ* in 2.19 | **With those same ten / endowed with thirteen**. The thirteen lists Mind, life, body, four feelings, the five beginning with faith, and the prospective Faculty. | Necessary possession is not an exclusive total, current operation, or ranking of realization. Do not restrict the entire list to one uninterrupted Path-moment. |
| *sarvālpaiḥ / niḥśubha / vit / bāla* in 2.20 | **With the fewest / one with severed wholesome roots / feeling / ordinary person**. The first minimum comprises five feelings, Mind, body, and life; vit is not an additional feeling. | A minimum is not every such person's exact total. Possession of five feelings is not their simultaneous manifestation. |
| *tathā / upekṣāyurmanaḥśubhaiḥ* in 2.20 | **Likewise / with neutral feeling, life, Mind, and the five beginning with faith**. Count and ordinary-person status restrict wholesome locally. | Likewise transfers the minimum eight, not severed roots. The realization Faculties remain wholesome but are not included in this local reference. |
| *bahubhiḥ / dviliṅga / samagrendriya* in 2.21 | **With the greatest number / possessing both sex Faculties / complete in Faculties**. The first nineteen retains all but the three stainless Faculties. | Both-sex status or absence of realization alone does not supply nineteen; the prose's completeness qualification matters. |
| *āryo rāgī / ājñātāvīndriyaṃ dvayoś cānyatarat* in 2.21 | **A noble trainee still with attachment / the Faculty of one who has known and either of the other two**. One sex Faculty and two stainless Faculties are excluded. | The two realization exclusions are conjunctive, with an alternative inside the second term. Equal maximum counts do not mean equal inventories or completed realization. |
| *paramāṇu / aparendriya / aśabda* in 2.22 | **Minimal Form-cluster / possessing another Faculty / soundless**. Desire-Realm 8/9/10 carries soundlessness throughout; another means body plus one of eye, ear, nose, tongue. | Sound adds one in the prose's qualified cases. Co-arising is not possession or simultaneous operation of a person's Faculty inventory. |
| *dravya / āyatana / jātidravya* in 2.22 | **Constituent / Essence / constituent-kind**. The objections distinguish individual constituents from Essence grouping, then count repeated Great-Element quartets by kind. | Eight is neither eight individual particles nor eight Essences. Preserve the disputed counting unit and the alternative seed account. |
| *cittaṃ caittāḥ sahāvaśyam / sarvaṃ saṃskṛtalakṣaṇaiḥ* in 2.23 | **Consciousness and mental factors necessarily together / everything with the marks of the conditioned**. The prose separates everything from the instrumental compound and carries necessarily together forward. | Reciprocal accompaniment is not all-to-all pairing. Consciousness remains distinct from Mind and Cognition; the unconditioned does not arise. |
| *prāptyā vā / pañcadhā / bhūmi* in 2.23 | **With acquisition, by distinction of cases / fivefold / ground as range of operation**. Acquisition accompanies only the sentient-designated arising dharmas; five counts mental-factor classes. | Acquisition does not replace marks or become optional. Great-ground kinds occur in every consciousness; arbitrary factor-tokens and all five classes do not. |
| *sarvacetasi / samagrā / kila* in 2.24 | **In every consciousness / all together / it is said**. The Bhāṣya resolves every consciousness-moment, with all ten great-ground kinds present together. | Ten coordinated kinds are not alternatives, ten successive operations, or an exclusive maximum. Kila reports the claim without itself proving rejection. |
| *mati / sparśa / smṛti* in 2.24 | **Discriminative comprehension / contact / mindfulness**. Contact arises from Faculty–Condition–Cognition conjunction; mindfulness is non-loss of the Cognitive Condition. | Keep these apart from reflection, attention, and concentration. Universal presence does not entail correctness, equal strength, or perfected practice; the reflection-gloss remains provisional. |
| *mūladvayam / kuśale sadā / amoha* in 2.25 | **The two roots / always in wholesome consciousness / non-delusion**. Non-greed and non-hatred count separately; non-delusion is discriminative comprehension, already in the universal class. | Ten wholesome kinds accompany the universal ten; neither ten nor twenty is an exclusive total. Non-delusion is not absent or an eleventh separately counted member. |
| *ābhoga / anābhoga / ekālambana* in 2.25 | **Application / non-application / having one Cognitive Condition**. Attention and equanimity raise an objection concerning co-occurrence and the shared Cognitive Condition. | Preserve the different-respects reply, renewed objection, and deferral. Do not report a demonstrated reconciliation or confuse wholesome equanimity with neutral feeling. |
| *ṣaṭ kleśamahābhūmikāḥ / akuśalamahābhūmikau* in 2.26 | **Six affliction great-ground factors / two unwholesome great-ground factors**. The six occur always and only in afflicted consciousness; the pair invariably in unwholesome consciousness. | Exclusive range is not an exclusive total. The narrower pair does not replace the six; afflicted is not synonymous with unwholesome. |
| *muṣitasmṛtitā / vikṣepa / styānacarita* in 2.26 | **Lost mindfulness / distraction / predominantly sluggish disposition**. Predominance does not break the co-presence of sluggishness and agitation. |
| *parīttakleśabhūmika / avidyāmātreṇa* in 2.27 | **Limited-affliction ground / only with ignorance**. | *bhāvanāheyena manobhūmikena / aniyata* in 2.27 | **Abandonable through cultivation and of the mental sphere / unfixed**. Unfixed is not ethically indeterminate; the examples do not exhaust the remaining factors. |
| *savitarkavicāratvāt / dvāviṃśati / kaukṛtyam adhikaṃ kvacit* in 2.28 | **Because accompanied by initial and sustained examination / twenty-two / regret additional in some cases**. Both counts are scoped to wholesome Desire-Realm consciousness; neither consciousness itself nor a deed is a counted factor. |
| *kaukṛtyālambana / phale hetūpacāra / ubhayādhiṣṭhāna* in 2.28 | **Having the badly done as Cognitive Condition / the cause's name applied to the effect / twofold basis**. | *āveṇika / dṛṣṭiyukta / prajñāviśeṣa* in 2.29 | **Isolated / view-associated / specialization of discriminative comprehension**. | *bhayādarśitva / viparyayeṇa / tatpūrvika* in 2.32 | **Failure to see danger / by reversal / preceded by that**. Not being governed by fear is not lack of caution toward blameworthy conduct. |
| *vitarkacārau / audāryasūkṣmate / bhūmitaḥ na kṣaṇataḥ* in 2.33 | **Initial and sustained examination / coarseness and subtlety / by level, not by moment**. The level/moment reply belongs to the separation account, not a withdrawal of all prior co-arising rules. |

The liquor analogy does not identify the factor with drinking; exhilaration remains an attributed alternative. |

| *cittaviprayukta-saṃskāra / prāptyaprāptī / samāpattī* in 2.35 | **Formation dissociated from consciousness / acquisition and non-acquisition / two attainments**. ## Condition, Thing, Meaning, Object

| Expression | Translation control | Organon determination |
|---|---|---|
| *ālambana* | **Cognitive Condition** or **support** where the local causal relation calls for it. | What Cognition takes as Condition is distinct from Object, the truth comprehended of a Thing. |
| *viṣaya* | **Condition** or **field** of a Faculty or Cognition according to the local relation; 1.09 relates it to the five *arthas*. | A determinate moment of the Seen and the Faculties' playground, not Object by default. |
| *artha* | **Meaning**; specify purpose or function only where context requires it. | Meaning is neither a cognitive Condition nor Object. |
| *vastu* | **Thing** or real basis. | The Thing is distinct from its Condition and from Object, its comprehended truth. |
| Object | Reserve this project term for the **comprehended truth of a Vastu**. | The Subject:Object relation belongs after the pre-Subject encounter with the Seed of the Subject; Object is disclosed by comprehension, not mere contact with a Condition. |

The Reflective relation places Mind-Essence as Seer and Dharma-Essence as Seen.
This is an Organon determination; each local *viṣaya* or *artha* still requires
its own grammatical and source-context analysis.

At 2.02 the Bhāṣya asks why an *artha* that contributes to apprehension
does not itself have *ādhipatya*. The answer appeals to the faculty's
superior efficacy. An Organon model may distinguish the sensory
Condition from the Meaning it presents, but the passage does not state
an identity or a separate Sanskrit ontology for those roles. It also
does not supply *hetu*, *phala*, *āśraya*, or *ālambana* as synonyms for
*artha*. Those relations require their own source and causal tests when
they occur.
