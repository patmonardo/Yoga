# Volume II: Technical Expressions for Loka and Karma

## Scope

Volume II follows the Objective Science from Loka to Karma. Each expression
has a source-bound translation control and a separately stated Organon
determination. A conventional rendering does not automatically assert the
project thesis. The [earlier combined Pre-Techne](technical-expressions-vol-i-ii.md)
preserves the longer argument; [Volume I](technical-expressions-vol-i.md)
establishes the Base–Essence–Principle and Condition–Object controls used here.

The Objective Wheel is `[[Dhātu:Indriya] [Loka:Karma]]`. Its second movement
asks how a determinate world and action become intelligible together. The
Organon relation `Citta:Caitta → Loka:Cetanā` guides that inquiry without
being inserted into a kārikā as a literal translation.

## Loka: world, being, and destination

| Expression | Translation control | Organon determination |
|---|---|---|
| *loka* | **World**; specify world of beings, receptacle-world, or their joined account as the passage does. | Loka is the world's determinate appearance in the Objective Wheel, not merely a place or a synonym for a realm. |
| *sattva-loka / bhājana-loka* in 4.01 | **World of beings / receptacle-world**. The Bhāṣya uses both to explain the *loka* whose differentiation is karma-born. | Distinguish beings and their environmental supports while tracing both to the actions of beings. |
| *loka-vaicitrya / karmaja* in 4.01 | **Differentiation of the world / born of action**. The verse predicates the latter of the former. | Meaning emerges through the determinate production of a differentiated world, not through an unqualified word “world.” |
| *dhātu* in realm compounds | **Realm** when the passage concerns a realm, including desire, form, or formless realm. | The realm can be read as Principle prior in explanatory order to the Cetanā of a being born there; this does not make “realm” the controlled rendering of every *dhātu*. |
| *bhava* | **Existence**, **becoming**, or a mode of continued existence according to context. | Bhava Chakra is the project name for the Loka–Karma movement, not a preset translation of each *bhava*. |
| *sattva* | **Living being** or **sentient being** as context requires. | Do not put project-level Being or pure *sattva* into a conventional translation by default. |
| *pudgala* | **Person** or **individual** in conventional discourse. | A person designation is distinct from the continuum and does not license a permanent-self inference. |
| *santāna* | **Continuum** or **stream**. | The continuing series is distinct from one cognition, from its person designation, and from a world-location. |
| *gati* | **Destination**, **course**, or **rebirth class** as classified by the text. | Keep the class of destination distinct from the rebirth event and the world it enters. |
| *upapatti* | **Rebirth** or **arising in a mode of existence**. | The event or mode of arising differs from *gati*, *bhava*, and *loka*. |
| *saṃsāra* | **Cyclic existence** or **conditioned wandering**. | A supporting cross-reference, not a substitute for Loka or a coequal system-expression in its place. |

## Karma: action and volition

| Expression | Translation control | Organon determination |
|---|---|---|
| *karma / karman* | **Action**; preserve the passage's distinction between volition and action produced after volition. | The objective world's practical determination. Action is neither fate nor every cause of a later result. |
| *cetanā* | **Volition** or **intending**, as the 4.01 division requires. | The operative side of `Loka:Cetanā`, through which laws of appearance become rules for action. |
| *tatkṛta / cetayitvā karma* in 4.01 | **What volition produces / action after having willed**. The Bhāṣya relates the verse's compressed *tatkṛta* to the cited action distinction. | Cetanā is already mental action; bodily and verbal action are generated from it rather than placed beside an unrelated mental cause. |
| *mānasa-karma / vāk-karma / kāya-karma* in 4.01 | **Mental / verbal / bodily action**. Volition is the first; the other two are produced after intending. | Preserve the direction from intending to its differentiated expressive acts. |
| *vipāka* | **Maturation** or **maturation result**. | Keep action, causal maturation, and ethical valuation distinct; a result is not automatically a reward. |
| *vijñapti / avijñapti* | **Manifestation or disclosure / non-disclosing continuity** where 4.02 and later action analyses require the distinction. | The continuity of action is not Cognition itself; the Dhātu dyad must remain distinct from Indriya's *prāpti:aprāpti*. |
| *saṃsthāna / gati / kṣaṇika / vyaya* in 4.02 | **Configuration / motion / momentary / perishing**. The verse identifies bodily manifestation with configuration and rejects the movement of one enduring thing from place to place. | Keep the form of an act, its temporal limit, and its continuing efficacy as separate problems. |
| *prāpti / aprāpti / lābha* | **Acquisition / non-acquisition / obtaining** according to the actual source form. | Do not collapse a current possession, its acquisition, and a first attainment into one event. |

## Formation and causal relations

| Expression | Translation control | Organon determination |
|---|---|---|
| *saṃskāra* | **Formation**, **conditioning**, or **conditioned formation** according to construction. | Distinguish the formation aggregate, causal operation, and dissociated conditioned factor. |
| *saṃskṛta / asaṃskṛta* | **Conditioned / unconditioned**. | Neither is equivalent to *saṃskāra* or to the influx distinction. |
| *saṃprayukta / viprayukta* | **Associated or conjoined / dissociated**. | A relation among factors; *viprayukta-saṃskāra* needs its own place, rather than being classified as a mental factor. |
| *hetu* | **Cause** or **causal ground**, with the named kind specified. | A restricted causal claim does not establish a universal cause. |
| *pratyaya* | **Condition** or **conditioning relation**. | Four conditions and six causes are different classifications; do not merge them into one list. |
| *kuśala / akuśala / avyākṛta* | **Wholesome / unwholesome / ethically indeterminate**. | Ethical status is distinct from whether an action is conditioned or bears an influx. |
| *āsrava / anāsrava* | **Influx / without influx** where this classification governs. | Do not identify influx with *kleśa*, moral badness, or conditioned being. |
| *kleśa* | **Affliction** or **defilement**, preserving its particular operation. | The afflictive ground of impure action opens the subsequent Anuśaya inquiry. |
| *prahāṇa / mārga* | **Abandonment / Path** with the stated operation and stage. | The source's Path action is distinct from the project's later Mārga-as-Concept determination. |
| *nirodha / nirvāṇa* | **Cessation / liberation or extinction**, respectively, as context determines. | Cessation, the unconditioned, and liberation cannot be inferred to be synonymous. |

## Organon determination of the second movement

The working relation `Dhātu:Indriya :: Loka:Karma` develops Principle and
Faculty toward world and action. Dhātu as Subjective Light means that its
Principle is operative, not an atomistic inventory. The Citta:Caitta
structure informs Loka:Cetanā; the terms retain their separate source
functions. A realm's priority to a being's Cetanā is structural, not a
claim about a first moment of the cosmos.

The proposed Opposition:Relation and Bhava Chakra articulation remains a
project inquiry. It should be argued in the Organon layer of a study, after
the source's grammar, conventional translation, and Bhāṣya determination.
Its aim is emergent Meaning in the System, not an extra lexical gloss on
*loka* or *karma*.
