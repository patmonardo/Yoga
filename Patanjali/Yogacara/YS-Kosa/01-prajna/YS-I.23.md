# YS I.23 — Or, Through Īśvara-praṇidhāna

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> ईश्वरप्रणिधानाद्वा ॥ १.२३॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> īśvarapraṇidhānād vā // 1.23 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Form | Construal | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *īśvara-praṇidhānāt* | ablative singular of *īśvara-praṇidhāna* | from or through dedication to Īśvara |
| *vā* | alternative particle | or; another means is admitted |

## 4. Grammar

The sūtra is elliptical: it names a means without stating its result. The
ablative states the means; *vā* opens an alternative to the preceding account
of graded intensity. I.24 takes up the character of Īśvara.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Or, through dedication to Īśvara.

*Praṇidhāna* is retained as dedication. The sūtra does not specify the
practice or its result further.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.20 names five means; I.21–22 grades the nearness of attainment according to
their intensity. I.23 opens another means with *vā*: dedication to Īśvara.
The short sūtra states the alternative without spelling out its full practice.

Prajñāpāda later defines *samāpatti* at I.41. It speaks of *samādhi* elsewhere,
while its technical definition comes at III.3 in Jñānapāda.
*Īśvara-praṇidhāna* returns in Kriyāpāda at II.1 and II.45, giving this
opening a practical continuation. The meaning and practice of OṂ belong to
I.27–28.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The Gītā's instruction to offer one's actions (9.27) can illuminate what
dedication does in practice, but does not define *īśvara-praṇidhāna* in this
sūtra. The Kośa's distinction between stainless *prajñā* and what supports
its attainment (VAK 1.02) helps us ask how practice serves knowing; it does
not identify the two paths. See the [Gītā verse](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C09/gita-9.27.md)
and [Kośa verse](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02.md).
