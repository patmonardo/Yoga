# YS I.2 — Yoga and Consciousness

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः ॥ १.२॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> yogaś cittavṛttinirodhaḥ // 1.2 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *yogaḥ* | nominative singular | yoga |
| *citta* | compound member | consciousness |
| *vṛtti* | compound member | determinate movements or turnings |
| *nirodhaḥ* | nominative singular; head of the compound | sublation in this Science; conventionally restraint or cessation |

## 4. Grammar

External sandhi gives *yogaḥ* → *yogaś* before *citta*. The sentence joins two nominatives: *yogaḥ* and *citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ*. The compound specifies the movements of consciousness and their *nirodha*. This grammatical construal leaves open the philosophical determination of that operation.

## 5. Translation

### Project translation

> Yoga is the Science of Consciousness.

### Grammatical gloss

> Yoga is the sublation of the movements of consciousness.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.1 announces the instruction; I.2 states its Science. *Citta* is consciousness, and its *vṛttis* are determinate movements through which a world can appear and be known. Their *nirodha* is sublation: the movement is comprehended and its claim to govern consciousness independently is overcome. This is the project determination of the sūtra's compact syntax. I.3–4 state the contrast through which its significance becomes visible.

## 7. Interpretive Note

Kośa VAK 2.23 and 2.34 analyze *citta* with its associated *caittas* through common support, object, and aspect. They help us inspect a movement without converting Yoga's five *vṛttis* into five Kośa factors. Kośa VAK 1.6 names two technical kinds of *nirodha*; neither defines the Yoga operation here. I.12 names Yoga's means of sublation. [Kośabhāṣya, VAK 2.23, 2.34, 1.6](../../../Vasubandhu/Sources/kosabhasya.txt).
