# YS I.39 — Meditation According to What Is Chosen

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> यथाभिमतध्यानाद्वा ॥ १.३९॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> yathābhimata-dhyānād vā // 1.39 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *yathā-abhimata* | compound | according to what is chosen or intended |
| *dhyānāt* | ablative singular | from or through meditation |
| *vā* | alternative particle | or |

## 4. Grammar

The ablative gives another means in the series. *Yathābhimata* qualifies
the meditation by reference to what is chosen. The sūtra leaves both the
object of that meditation and the precise result implicit in its context.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Or, through meditation according to what is chosen.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.39 closes the sequence of alternatives before I.40 describes mastery
and I.41 names *samāpatti*. Its openness permits a fitting meditation
without saying that any passing preference is sufficient. The earlier
alternatives supply examples of disciplined supports.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 6.09](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.09_bhasya.md)
selects different entry practices for practitioners dominated by attachment
or discursive thought. It shows that a choice of practice can answer a
specific condition. That diagnostic rule belongs to the Kośa; Yoga I.39
states no such taxonomy. The [I.41
report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-41-fichte-report.md) remains the
source for our later Organon account of *samāpatti*.
