# YS I.22 — Degrees Within Intensity

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> मृदुमध्याधिमात्रत्वात्ततोऽपि विशेषः ॥ १.२२॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> mṛdu-madhya-adhimātratvāt tato 'pi viśeṣaḥ // 1.22 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *mṛdu* | compound member | mild |
| *madhya* | compound member | middle, moderate |
| *adhimātra* | compound member | exceeding measure, very intense |
| *-tvāt* | ablative singular of the abstract suffix *-tva* | because of being so graded |
| *tataḥ api* | ablative adverb plus particle | even from that, further |
| *viśeṣaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | distinction, differentiation |

## 4. Grammar

The causal ablative *mṛdu-madhya-adhimātratvāt* gives the ground of *viśeṣaḥ*. *Tato 'pi* refers back to the nearness claimed in I.21 and says that even it admits further distinction. The sūtra itself does not name the distinguished cases; the Bhāṣya supplies them.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Because of mild, middle, and very intense degrees, even that admits further differentiation.

## 6. Logical Determination

The received Bhāṣya distinguishes mild-intense, middle-intense, and very-intense *saṃvega*. Attainment of samādhi and its fruit is near, nearer, or nearest accordingly; the last is also described as possessing a very strong means. I.22 therefore refines I.21's “near” rather than opening a separate topic.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa essay](../essays/01-prajna/degrees-of-intensity-according-to-kosa.md) compares the Kośa's recursive three-by-three grading, its graded cultivation of samādhi, and its account of faster penetration with sharper faculties. The matching scheme clarifies the logic of degree without making the two path taxonomies identical.
