# YS I.44 — Vicāra and the Subtle Field

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> एतयैव सविचारा निर्विचारा च सूक्ष्मविषया व्याख्याता ॥ १.४४॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> etayaiva savicārā nirvicārā ca sūkṣma-viṣayā vyākhyātā // 1.44 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *etayā eva* | instrumental singular feminine with emphasis | by this very account or distinction |
| *savicārā* | feminine nominative singular | samāpatti accompanied by *vicāra* |
| *nirvicārā* | feminine nominative singular | samāpatti without *vicāra* |
| *ca* | conjunction | and |
| *sūkṣma-viṣayā* | feminine nominative singular | having a subtle field or object |
| *vyākhyātā* | feminine nominative singular past participle | explained |

## 4. Grammar

The feminine singular forms qualify an understood *samāpattiḥ* in
each of the two cases. *Etayā eva* points back to the explanatory
movement of I.42–43. *Sūkṣma-viṣayā* states what distinguishes the
present field: its *viṣaya* is subtle. The same explanatory form is
applied to a field whose content is different.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> By this same account, *savicārā* and *nirvicārā* samāpatti, having
> subtle objects, are explained.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.42–43 distinguished a mixture and its clarification within
*vitarka*. I.44 preserves that explanatory distinction while
opposing *vicāra* to *vitarka* in the work performed. The field is
now subtle: relations and conditions through which an appearance
is determined become objects of investigation. The text names two
modes, with and without *vicāra*; I.45 will state how far subtle
objectivity extends.

## 7. Interpretive Note

In our Organon reading, *vicāra* opens **Essence Base** as Reflection
and the determinations of Reflection, opposed to *vitarka*'s Form
Base work. The [companion essay](../essays/01-prajna/vicara-reflection-and-the-mark-system-at-i-44.md)
develops that opposition. The mark system sought here
concerns how a mark refers beyond its immediate presentation, how
marks relate, and what governs their intelligibility. *Nirvicārā*
is the corresponding Principle determination of this reflective
work, still within the seeded sequence named at I.46. The
[Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 1.32](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.32_bhasya.md)
offers a basic comparison: it distinguishes levels at which the
mental and dharma domains are associated with *vitarka*, *vicāra*,
or neither. The [Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-44-fichte-report.md)
develops our wider Science of Reflection.
