# YS I.1 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> अथ योगानुशासनम्॥ १.१॥

> *atha yogānuśāsanam* — Now, the instruction of yoga.

## 2. Commentary

*Atha* begins an instruction. *Anuśāsana* is the ordered exposition through which Yoga can be learned as a Science. I.2 will identify its subject; I.3–4 will give the first relation to be explained. The opening does not claim that instruction and its achieved knowledge are the same act.

The Kośa begins its own instruction by distinguishing undefiled *prajñā* with its entourage from the teaching and preparatory wisdom that lead to it (VAK 1.2). The text is a means of access to discernment, while discernment is an attained operation. That is the useful comparison for I.1: a disciplined exposition can give form to a path without being the attainment it expounds. The two texts have different subjects; the comparison concerns the relation between instruction and knowing. [Kośabhāṣya, VAK 1.2](../../../Vasubandhu/Sources/kosabhasya.txt).

## 3. Project Determination

Our name **Prajñā-pāda** signals the inquiry into knowledge that this instruction will develop; the source chapter is *Samādhi-pāda*. We begin with Yoga as a teachable Science of Consciousness. Its exposition, practice, and achieved knowledge will acquire distinct roles as the sūtras proceed.

## 4. Review Status

Kośa-first revision. Read I.1 with I.2; the opening alone does not define yoga.
