# YS I.44 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> एतयैव सविचारा निर्विचारा च सूक्ष्मविषया व्याख्याता ॥ १.४४॥

> *etayaiva savicārā nirvicārā ca sūkṣma-viṣayā vyākhyātā* — By this same account, savicārā and nirvicārā samāpatti with subtle objects are explained.

## 2. Commentary

*Etayā eva* directs us back to I.42–43. There, the clear jewel first
held word, impure Objective Artha, and knowledge in mixture; purified
memory then allowed *artha* to shine without the inherited name
governing it. I.44 preserves that formal difference between a
seeded operation and its clarification. Yet *vicāra* is opposed to
*vitarka* in what it investigates: Reflection's own subtle
determinations rather than the presented Form. The sūtra names
*savicārā* and *nirvicārā* without supplying a new list of objects.

The change of *viṣaya* matters. A subtle object need not be a tiny
material thing. It can be a relation or condition through which a
presented determination has its form. The question is how Reflection
marks such a relation and how the mark remains answerable to what it
discloses. I.45 will test the limit of this subtle field at *aliṅga*;
that verse is needed before we claim to have mastered the whole mark
system.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 1.32](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.32_bhasya.md)
classifies *vitarka* and *vicāra* by association with cognition in
different domains and meditative levels. It shows that the two can
be distinguished in an operative cognitive field. Yoga I.44 gives
the present inquiry its own form: two samāpattis concerning subtle
*viṣaya*, explained by the previous pair.

## 3. Project Determination

Here **vicāra is Reflection**, and its work belongs to **Essence
Base**. *Savicārā* takes up determinations of Reflection while their
marks still mediate the subtle object. *Nirvicārā* seeks the Principle
of that mediation, so that Reflection can recognize what its marks
show and where their authority ends. This is the beginning of our
Central Mark System. The [companion essay](../essays/01-prajna/vicara-reflection-and-the-mark-system-at-i-44.md)
develops the Form : Essence opposition. I.45 will test the mark
system's limit; the
[Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-44-fichte-report.md)
develops this movement at greater length.
