# YS I.38 — Dream and Sleep Knowledge as Support

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> स्वप्ननिद्राज्ञानालम्बनं वा ॥ १.३८॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> svapna-nidrā-jñāna-ālambanaṃ vā // 1.38 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *svapna* | compound member | dream |
| *nidrā* | compound member | sleep |
| *jñāna* | compound member | knowledge or cognition |
| *ālambanam* | neuter nominative singular | taking as support |
| *vā* | alternative particle | or |

## 4. Grammar

The compound connects dream, sleep, knowledge, and support. It may be
construed as taking knowledge associated with dream and sleep as the
support; the terse wording leaves the exact division of its members open.
*Vā* continues the preceding series of alternatives. A subject such as
*cittam* from I.37 is understood.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Or, [consciousness] supported by knowledge of dream and sleep.

## 6. Logical Determination

The sūtra admits knowledge associated with dream and sleep as a possible
support for steadiness. It does not say that ordinary sleepiness is itself
the practice or that a dream's content is automatically true. The support
is *jñāna*, to be investigated as part of the path toward *samāpatti*.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad 4–5](../../../Sankara/Organon/sankara/derived/Mandukya/MK_C01.json)
distinguishes the dream state from deep sleep and examines their kinds of
knowing. It offers substantial material for the Organon Yoga study of this
support. The [Kośa at VAK 5.47](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/05-anusaya/VAK_5.47_bhasya.md)
classifies afflicted sleepiness as an envelopment. That separate analysis
warns against equating the support named here with mere dullness.
