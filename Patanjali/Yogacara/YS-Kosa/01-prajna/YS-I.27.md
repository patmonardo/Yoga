# YS I.27 — Praṇava as Designation

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तस्य वाचकः प्रणवः ॥ १.२७॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tasya vācakaḥ praṇavaḥ // 1.27 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *tasya* | genitive singular | of him, Īśvara of I.24–26 |
| *vācakaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | designation; that which expresses |
| *praṇavaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | praṇava, OṂ |

## 4. Grammar

*Praṇavaḥ* is the subject and *vācakaḥ* its predicate. *Tasya* gives the
one designated. The sūtra states the relation between expression and its
referent; it does not yet prescribe recitation.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Praṇava is his designation.

## 6. Logical Determination

After I.26 names Īśvara as teacher, I.27 gives a determinate expression by
which he can be addressed. I.28 will specify repetition and cultivation of
its meaning. Expression and meaning belong together, but this sūtra states
only the first relation.

## 7. Interpretive Note

Śaṅkara's [comment on Gītā 8.13](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C08/gita-8.13.md)
calls OṂ a designation of brahman and the Lord its meaning. This is a close
comparison for *vācakaḥ*. The [Kośa's Brahma-wheel
passage](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.54_bhasya.md) concerns the
Buddha's teaching made public and effective in other continuities; it does
not identify OṂ as the Buddha's designation. Our reading of the Buddha as
Īśvara therefore calls for inquiry into how a living teaching is expressed,
not an equation of OṂ and the Kośa's Dharma exposition.
