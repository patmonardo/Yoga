# YS I.41 — Samāpatti and the Clear Jewel

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> क्षीणवृत्तेरभिजातस्येव मणेर्ग्रहीतृग्रहणग्राह्येषु तत्स्थतदञ्जनता समापत्तिः ॥ १.४१॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> kṣīṇa-vṛtter abhijātasyeva maṇer grahītṛ-grahaṇa-grāhyeṣu
> tat-stha-tad-añjanatā samāpattiḥ // 1.41 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *kṣīṇa-vṛtteḥ* | genitive singular | of consciousness whose movements have been attenuated |
| *abhijātasya iva maṇeḥ* | genitive singular with comparison | like a clear, well-formed jewel |
| *grahītṛ-grahaṇa-grāhyeṣu* | locative plural | in the grasper, grasping, and grasped |
| *tat-stha-tad-añjanatā* | feminine nominative singular abstract compound | resting in that and taking its coloring |
| *samāpattiḥ* | feminine nominative singular | samāpatti; the attained coincidence or absorption |

## 4. Grammar

*Kṣīṇa-vṛtteḥ* and *maṇeḥ* stand in a genitive comparison: the
attenuated consciousness is likened to a clear jewel. The locative
plural gives the three possible stations of its resting. *Tat-stha-
tad-añjanatā* names the condition, and *samāpattiḥ* identifies it. The
sūtra does not say that the three stations have become identical.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> For consciousness whose movements have diminished, samāpatti is the
> condition of resting in and taking the coloring of the grasper,
> grasping, or grasped, like a clear jewel.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.40 established the range of mastery. I.41 now describes how
consciousness meets a determination within that range: it stays with
one of the three terms and shows that term clearly, as the jewel shows
the color of what it approaches. This is the opening determination of
the samāpatti sequence through I.51. The attenuation of *vṛtti* makes
the relation lucid; it need not erase every determinate movement of
consciousness.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
calls *samāpatti* wholesome one-pointedness and explains that through
one object-support with accompanying factors. This supplies a basic
analysis of the attained field. Yoga I.41 determines a further question:
how consciousness can rest upon and become lucid in relation to the
grasper, grasping, and grasped. The [paired Bhāṣya](YS-I.41_bhasya.md)
starts our Being–Essence–Concept reading; the existing [I.41 Fichte
report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-41-fichte-report.md) develops it
at greater length.
