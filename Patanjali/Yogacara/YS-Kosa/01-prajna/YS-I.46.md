# YS I.46 — Seeded Samādhi

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> ता एव सबीजः समाधिः ॥ १.४६॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tā eva sabījaḥ samādhiḥ // 1.46 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *tāḥ* | feminine nominative plural | those samāpattis |
| *eva* | emphasis | precisely those |
| *sa-bījaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | with seed |
| *samādhiḥ* | masculine nominative singular | samādhi |

## 4. Grammar

The plural *tāḥ* recalls the preceding samāpattis. The singular
predicate *sabījaḥ samādhiḥ* gathers them under one designation.
*Eva* makes the retrospective reference emphatic.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> These very samāpattis are samādhi with seed.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.42–45 developed two paired inquiries, *vitarka* and *vicāra*,
through the subtle limit at *aliṅga*. I.46 states their common
status: they remain seeded. Finding a Principle within Form or
Reflection does not erase the determinate field in which it was found.

## 7. Interpretive Note

For our reading, Form Base and Essence Base supply two distinct
*bīja* within the *saṃvṛti* process. Their corresponding Principle
discoveries remain real and seeded. The [Kośa at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
analyzes samāpatti as wholesome one-pointedness with one object-support;
Yoga I.46 classifies this preceding sequence as *sabīja samādhi*.
The [Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-46-fichte-report.md)
develops the wider project reading.
