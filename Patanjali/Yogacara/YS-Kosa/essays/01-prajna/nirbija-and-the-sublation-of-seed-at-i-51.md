# Nirbīja: The Seed Is Sublated

## A Samāpatti Essay on Yoga Sūtra I.51

I.46 gathered the earlier samāpattis as *sabīja*. I.50 gave the Prajñā born from their development a new *saṃskāra*, one capable of inhibiting others. I.51 now says *tasyāpi nirodhe*: **even that** formation undergoes *nirodha*. With the sublation of all such supports, the sūtra names *nirbīja samādhi*. Its close answers the seeded status diagnosed five verses earlier. [YS I.51](../../01-prajna/YS-I.51.md)

In this project, **nirodha is sublation**. The Prajñā-born formation has done necessary work; it no longer claims to be the ground of the Truth it helped reveal. The *api* matters: if even the formation made by truth-bearing knowing is exempted, a seed remains enthroned as final authority. The sūtra closes that dependence. The Form and Essence discoveries are comprehended from a central Principle rather than discarded with the supports through which they were reached.

This is our third Principle determination: after *nirvitarkā* found the Principle of Form and *nirvicārā* found the Principle of Reflection, *nirbīja* names the freedom of their relation from a determining seed. The three are not three interchangeable experiences. They have different work in the genesis of a Science. The [Kośa's samāpatti account](../../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md) analyzes an attainment through one object-support and accompanying factors. It gives a precise comparison for the conditions of attained knowing. Yoga's final *nirbīja*, however, is determined here by its own sequence and its own *nirodha*; no separate Kośa cessation attainment is silently inserted as its definition.

The movement from I.40 to I.51 can now be read as one arc: mastery opens the field; the jewel holds its relations; Form and Essence yield Principles; Prajñā bears their truth; its own formation is sublated. A fuller account of practice, OM, the two truths, and the Absolute belongs to the later Yogavidyā pass. The present Bhāṣya has established the transitions on which that account must answer.
