# Nirodha in the Science of Consciousness

## A Yoga–Kośa essay on Yoga Sūtras I.2 and I.12

**Yoga is the Science of Consciousness.** I.2 states that Science through *citta-vṛtti-nirodha*; in our reading, *nirodha* is the **sublation of the movements of consciousness**. I.12 returns to the term after I.5–11 have specified five kinds of movement and names *abhyāsa* and *vairāgya* as the two means of their sublation. The order matters. We cannot know what is sublated without first knowing how consciousness moves. [YS I.2](../../01-prajna/YS-I.02.md) · [YS I.12](../../01-prajna/YS-I.12.md)

The sūtra's grammatical compound can be glossed as “Yoga is the *nirodha* of the movements of consciousness.” The project translation states the Science that this operation opens. **Sublation** means that a movement is comprehended in the whole relation of knowing and loses its independent claim to rule that relation. The content it disclosed need not become false, and the capacity that produced it need not be erased. I.3 names own-form abiding for the seer; I.4 names the other condition, conformity to a movement's form. These are the first consequences that the Science must explain.

## The five movements make the question determinate

I.5 crosses two classifications: five kinds of *vṛtti* and afflicted or unafflicted condition. I.6 names the kinds. I.7–11 then gives each a criterion: warrant through perception, inference, or testimony; mistaken cognition established in an alien form; word-led construction without a corresponding *vastu*; sleep supported by an absence-presentation; and non-loss of an experienced object. This is an analysis of the instrument of knowing, not five identical obstacles to be extinguished. [YS I.5–12](../../../Sources/Yoga.txt) · [Five-vṛtti essay](five-vrittis-as-instrument-according-to-our-kosa.md)

A valid *pramāṇa* can remain valid while its claim to be complete knowledge is sublated. An error can be corrected by exposing the alien form and the rule that sustained it. A word-led *vikalpa* can lose its unsupported claim to a thing while retaining its power to articulate. Sleep can be known as an absence-supported movement; memory can retain access without making the past authoritative over the present. These are different determinate tasks within one Science. I.12 names their common means without assigning a separate technique to each kind.

## What the Kośa comparison contributes

The Kośa's *pratisaṃkhyānirodha* is disjunction attained through discriminative *prajñā*; *apratisaṃkhyānirodha* concerns non-arising through deficiency of conditions. These are technical unconditioned dharmas in the Kośa's argument. They cannot be identified respectively with Yoga's practice and dispassion, nor can either simply replace Yoga's *citta-vṛtti-nirodha*. Their value here is analytic: they require us to ask what discernment achieves and which conditions cease to support an occurrence. [Kośabhāṣya, VAK 1.6](../../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.06_bhasya.md)

The Kośa also examines *citta* and associated *caittas* as a determinate cognitive event, with shared support, object, and aspect. This helps us spell out the factors in a Yoga movement. It does not make Yoga's five *vṛttis* into five Kośa factors. The Science of Consciousness retains Yoga's question about the seer and the whole relation in which a movement appears. [Kośabhāṣya, VAK 2.23 and 2.34](../../../../Vasubandhu/Sources/kosabhasya.txt)

## Practice and dispassion together

The instrumental dual in I.12 joins *abhyāsa* and *vairāgya*. Practice sustains the investigation through which a movement is known; dispassion loosens its claim to final authority. Taken together, they make sublation a disciplined operation of consciousness rather than a momentary refusal of its contents. I.13–16 will determine what practice and dispassion are. Until then, I.12 gives their joint office, not a completed manual for every movement.

This returns us to the opening claim. Yoga's Science can retain valid knowing, correct error, examine construction, understand absence, and purify retention because *nirodha* concerns the **authority and place** of a movement within knowing. The Kośa sharpens the analysis of conditions and discrimination; the Yoga sequence determines the Science in which that analysis is used.
