# Vaśīkāra: The Range of Samāpatti

## A Samāpatti Essay on Yoga Sūtra I.40

The repeated *vā* of I.34–39 has offered several entrances to steadiness. I.40 closes that series by stating the reach of *vaśīkāra*: from *paramāṇu* to *parama-mahattva*, from the smallest to the greatest. The sūtra tests whether consciousness can hold a determination across changes of scale. I.41 will say what occurs when that capacity becomes the clear jewel of *samāpatti*. [YS I.40](../../01-prajna/YS-I.40.md)

Scale is already a philosophical problem. To attend to a minute feature is to distinguish it from its setting; to attend to a whole is to preserve its articulated parts. Neither end licenses a vague impression. Mastery means being able to move between them while keeping the determination answerable to what appears. Thus I.40 belongs to the samāpatti movement: it establishes the range within which the jewel can rest and take coloring.

The [Kośa's account of samāpatti](../../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md) calls it wholesome one-pointedness and asks what one-pointedness means with respect to an object-support. Its analysis of associated factors lets us ask what makes an attainment operable. Yoga's I.40 makes a different claim about *vaśīkāra* and its extremes. The Kośa does not convert those extremes into its own inventory.

In our Form–Essence–Principle inquiry, the smallest determination can be examined in its **Form**, the relations by which it is disclosed in **Essence**, and the **Principle** that makes those determinations one intelligible domain. The greatest whole must undergo the same examination. A whole without determinate members and a member without a governing relation both escape knowledge. I.40 therefore prepares a disciplined passage in both directions. It does not yet present the three stations of the jewel; I.41 does that explicitly.

This is the first condition for the later claim that samāpatti can find Principles. The capacity to see a Principle has to work on a determinate field. *Vaśīkāra* keeps that field available, however narrow or extensive its present articulation. The [I.41 essay](samapatti-and-the-three-bases-at-i-41.md) begins where this threshold ends.
