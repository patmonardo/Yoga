# YS I.34 — An Alternative Through Breath

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> प्रच्छर्दनविधारणाभ्यां वा प्राणस्य ॥ १.३४॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> pracchardana-vidhāraṇābhyāṃ vā prāṇasya // 1.34 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *pracchardana* | compound member | expulsion or exhalation |
| *vidhāraṇa* | compound member | holding or retention |
| *-ābhyām* | instrumental dual | through these two |
| *vā* | alternative particle | or |
| *prāṇasya* | genitive singular | of vital breath |

## 4. Grammar

The instrumental dual gives two means concerning *prāṇa*. *Vā* links this
short elliptical sūtra to the preceding ways of clarifying consciousness.
Its result is supplied by context, not stated again here.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Or, through exhalation and retention of the vital breath.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.33 named the cultivation of four relations as a means to
*citta-prasādanam*. I.34 offers another practice by way of breath. The
alternative does not cancel the four cultivations. This sūtra does not
give the detailed stages of prāṇāyāma that belong later in Kriyāpāda.

## 7. Interpretive Note

Śaṅkara's [Gītā 4.29 comment](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C04/gita-4.29.md)
distinguishes filling, emptying, and retention in its account of breath
practice. The [Kośa at VAK 6.12](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.12_bhasya.md)
calls recollection of in- and out-breath *prajñā* with wind as its object.
Both are illuminating comparisons, but neither supplies the missing
details of Yoga I.34 or makes it identical with its own method.
