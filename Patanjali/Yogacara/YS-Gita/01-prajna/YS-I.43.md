# YS I.43 — Nirvitarkā Samāpatti

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> स्मृतिपरिशुद्धौ स्वरूपशून्येवार्थमात्रनिर्भासा निर्वितर्का ॥ १.४३॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> smṛti-pariśuddhau svarūpa-śūnyevārtha-mātra-nirbhāsā nirvitarkā // 1.43 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *smṛti-pariśuddhau* | locative singular | when memory is purified |
| *svarūpa-śūnyā iva* | feminine nominative with comparison | as though empty of its own form |
| *artha-mātra-nirbhāsā* | feminine nominative singular | shining forth as *artha* alone |
| *nirvitarkā* | feminine nominative singular | without vitarka; qualifying the understood *samāpatti* |

## 4. Grammar

The locative gives the condition: the purification of memory. *Iva*
qualifies the apparent emptiness of own-form; it does not assert the
destruction of consciousness. *Samāpattiḥ* is understood from I.42,
and *nirvitarkā* names this mode.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> When memory is purified, samāpatti is as though empty of its own
> form and shines forth as *artha* alone: this is *nirvitarkā*.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.42's mixture of word, impure *artha*, and knowledge is now clarified. A
remembered name or form no longer governs what this *artha* may show. The
impure *artha* alone shines, while the knowing activity remains operative.
The *iva* leaves room for that activity without allowing it to
obscure *artha*. This clarity does not by itself convert that *artha*
into a pure object.

## 7. Interpretive Note

Our [companion essay](../essays/01-prajna/savitarka-nirvitarka-and-the-three-bases.md)
asks how *vijñāna* can make the Principle of the Form determination
accessible when the word-led mixture is purified. The Kośa's
[VAK 1.31](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.31_bhasya.md)
distinguishes cognition-domains with and without outflows. Thus
*vijñāna* names a possible cognitive access in our reconstruction;
the sūtra's own criterion is purified memory and the shining of
*artha-mātra*.
