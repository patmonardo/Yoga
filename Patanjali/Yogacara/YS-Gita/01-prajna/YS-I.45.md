# YS I.45 — The Limit of Subtle Objectivity

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> सूक्ष्मविषयत्वं चालिङ्गपर्यवसानम्॥ १.४५॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> sūkṣma-viṣayatvaṃ cāliṅga-paryavasānam // 1.45 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *sūkṣma-viṣayatvam* | neuter nominative singular | having a subtle field or object |
| *ca* | conjunction | and |
| *aliṅga-paryavasānam* | neuter nominative singular | terminating at the unmarked |

## 4. Grammar

*Aliṅga-paryavasānam* qualifies *sūkṣma-viṣayatvam*. The sūtra states
the terminus of the subtle field opened at I.44. It does not itself
enumerate the stages of that field.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> And subtle objectivity terminates at *aliṅga*, the unmarked.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.44 gave *vicāra* a subtle *viṣaya*. I.45 asks how far the subtle
inquiry can go. The terminus is *aliṅga*: the limit at which no
further manifest mark can serve as the next object. This is a limit
of the guṇa division, not the assertion that inquiry has reached a
blank nothing.

## 7. Interpretive Note

[Yoga II.19](../../Sources/Yoga.txt) names four *guṇa-parvans*:
*viśeṣa*, *aviśeṣa*, *liṅgamātra*, and *aliṅga*. This later verse
supplies the fourfold architecture needed to read I.45:

| Division | First-pass determination |
|---|---|
| *viśeṣa* | specified, manifest product |
| *aviśeṣa* | subtle, less specified generative condition |
| *liṅgamātra* | mark or determinative indication as such |
| *aliṅga* | unmarked root and limit of the marked series |

This is the division of the seen, not Reality itself. The local
[Sāṃkhya Kārikā 3](../../../Reference/Samkhya.txt) calls root-prakṛti
unproduced and the series beginning with *mahat* both productive and
produced; that supports treating the unmarked root as a different
station from its determinate effects. It does not by itself prove
every Yoga-to-Sāṃkhya identification.
Our Essence Base reading follows Reflection back through these
determinations; the [I.44 essay](../essays/01-prajna/vicara-reflection-and-the-mark-system-at-i-44.md)
opens the mark question, and the [Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-45-fichte-report.md)
develops its longer argument.
