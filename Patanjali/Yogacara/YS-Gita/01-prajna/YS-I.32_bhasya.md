# YS I.32 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> तत्प्रतिषेधार्थमेकतत्त्वाभ्यासः ॥ १.३२॥

> *tat-pratiṣedhārtham eka-tattva-abhyāsaḥ* — For counteracting those, sustained practice of one principle.

## 2. Commentary

The *tat* gathers the dispersions of I.30 and their accompaniments in
I.31. Their *pratiṣedha* calls for *abhyāsa*: practice repeated until
consciousness can remain with a determinate *tattva*. The sūtra does not
name that principle for us. Its point here is the positive exercise that
answers a scattered field.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
asks what one-pointedness is and answers with *ekālambanatā*, having one
object-support. It also calls *samāpatti* wholesome one-pointedness with
its attendant factors. This is a valuable comparison because it joins
stability to a complete mental condition. Yoga's phrase is *eka-tattva*,
however, and its own *samāpatti* will be stated at I.41. We should carry
the comparison forward without replacing that later description.

The [Māṇḍūkya study](../Yogavidya/Prajnapada/mandukya-om-artha-study.md)
gives an example already opened by I.27–28: sustained inquiry into OṂ's
meaning can hold together the designation and its determinate *artha*.
That is one possible application of I.32 within our reading, not a claim
that I.32 restricts *eka-tattva* to OṂ.

I.33 immediately broadens the practice into friendliness, compassion,
sympathetic joy, and equanimity toward living situations. These are not a
departure from the work of steadiness; their cultivation will show how
consciousness becomes clear in relation to others. From there the chapter
moves toward the explicit *samāpatti* sequence.

## 3. Project Determination

*Eka-tattva-abhyāsa* is sustained attention to a principle through the
dispersion that would break its intelligibility. Its role is preparatory:
it makes room for the relational work of I.33 and for the transparent
determinations of *samāpatti* at I.41.
