# YS I.32 — Practice of One Principle

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तत्प्रतिषेधार्थमेकतत्त्वाभ्यासः ॥ १.३२॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tat-pratiṣedhārtham eka-tattva-abhyāsaḥ // 1.32 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *tat* | compound member | those dispersions and obstacles of I.30–31 |
| *pratiṣedha-artham* | purpose construction | for counteracting them |
| *eka-tattva* | compound | one principle, one reality to be held steadily |
| *abhyāsaḥ* | nominative singular | sustained practice |

## 4. Grammar

*Abhyāsaḥ* names the prescribed practice; *eka-tattva* gives its
determinate focus. *Tat-pratiṣedhārtham* states its purpose. The sūtra does
not identify the one *tattva* with a particular object or give a complete
inventory of possible supports.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> For counteracting those, sustained practice of one principle.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.30–31 diagnosed dispersion and its accompaniments. I.32 answers with
continuity of practice around one principle. This is a response to the
obstacles, not yet the definition of *samāpatti*. The relational cultivation
at I.33 and the explicit account of *samāpatti* at I.41 remain ahead.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
defines *ekāgratā* as having one object-support and discusses *samāpatti*
as wholesome one-pointedness. This clarifies one way a practice resists
dispersion, but Yoga's *eka-tattva* is not simply the Kośa's *ekālambana*.
The [I.41 report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-41-fichte-report.md) shows
where this Yogic preparation will be taken up in the study of *samāpatti*.
