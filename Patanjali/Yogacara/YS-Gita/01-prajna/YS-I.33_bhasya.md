# YS I.33 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> मैत्रीकरुणामुदितोपेक्षाणां सुखदुःखपुण्यापुण्यविषयाणां भावनातश्चित्तप्रसादनम्॥ १.३३॥

> *maitrī-karuṇā-muditā-upekṣāṇāṃ sukha-duḥkha-puṇya-apuṇya-viṣayāṇāṃ bhāvanātaś citta-prasādanam* — Cultivating the four relations toward their four fields clarifies consciousness.

## 2. Commentary

The four relations are named in order: *maitrī*, *karuṇā*, *muditā*, and
*upekṣā*. The four fields follow in the same order: happiness, suffering,
virtue, and non-virtue. Friendliness meets another's happiness without
envy; compassion meets suffering without turning away; joy answers virtue
without resentment; equanimity meets non-virtue without surrendering the
clarity of judgment. Their cultivation brings *citta-prasādanam*, the
clearing and settling of consciousness.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.29](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.29_bhasya.md)
calls these four *apramāṇas* because they take immeasurable beings as
object-support. It makes them counterforces to ill will, harmfulness,
discontent, and possessive or hostile attachment. [VAK
8.30](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.30_bhasya.md) gives their
concrete modes: wishing beings happiness, recognizing their suffering,
rejoicing in their flourishing, and attending to beings without
appropriation. This is richer than treating the four as agreeable moods.

The texts do not make exactly the same allocation. Yoga explicitly orders
four relations against happiness, suffering, virtue, and non-virtue; the
Kośa describes beings as their object and analyzes the afflictions each
opposes. [VAK 8.31](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.31_bhasya.md)
adds that the immeasurables do not directly sever afflictions. Yoga I.33
claims *citta-prasādanam*, not final severance. That lets us use the Kośa
without overclaiming what the Yoga verse promises.

The placement matters for our path toward *samāpatti*. After the
dispersion of I.30–31 and the sustained principle of I.32, I.33 brings
clarity through relation to living beings. The jewel of I.41 will be
discussed in its own terms; the present verse supplies a practical
clarification of consciousness before that account.

## 3. Project Determination

The four cultivations are a Science of Consciousness in relation: they
correct distortions in how another's condition appears to us. In our
Buddha as Īśvara reading, the Kośa's immeasurables help articulate how a
teaching becomes active among beings. Yoga's specific result here remains
*citta-prasādanam*.
