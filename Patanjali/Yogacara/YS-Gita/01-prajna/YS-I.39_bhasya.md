# YS I.39 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> यथाभिमतध्यानाद्वा ॥ १.३९॥

> *yathābhimata-dhyānād vā* — Or, through meditation according to what is chosen.

## 2. Commentary

The final *vā* of this run keeps the series open. After the four
relations, breath, object-bearing activity, luminosity, the
dispassionate, and dream-and-sleep knowledge, I.39 admits a meditation
chosen for the practitioner. It names *dhyāna* but does not give a new
list of objects or authorize an inference that every fleeting preference
will steady consciousness.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 6.09](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.09_bhasya.md)
offers a useful comparison: when attachment predominates, it prescribes
the unattractive; when discursive thought predominates, recollection of
breathing. Practice is selected for what it addresses. That supports a
disciplined reading of *yathābhimata* without making the Kośa's diagnostic
scheme the grammar of Yoga I.39.

The next two Yoga sūtras move from these supports to the scope of mastery
and then to *samāpatti*. A chosen meditation matters because it can be
sustained and investigated. In our Organon reading, its object is not a
loose item detached from knowing: Principle Base, Essence Base, and Form
Base will be worked through together when I.41 brings grasper, grasping,
and grasped into a single rational process.

## 3. Project Determination

The series of *vā* alternatives permits multiple entrances to a stable
practice. Their unity lies in disciplined determination of consciousness,
which prepares the mastery of I.40 and the explicit *samāpatti* of I.41.
