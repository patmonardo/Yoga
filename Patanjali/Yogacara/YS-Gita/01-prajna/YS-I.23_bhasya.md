# YS I.23 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> ईश्वरप्रणिधानाद्वा ॥ १.२३॥

> *īśvarapraṇidhānād vā* — Or, through dedication to Īśvara.

## 2. Commentary

I.20 sets out confidence, energy, recollection, samādhi, and prajñā. I.21–22
then grade the nearness of attainment by intensity. The *vā* of I.23 opens
another way: dedication to Īśvara. The sūtra leaves its result unstated.

The ablative *-āt* says **through** this dedication. The sūtra does not yet
define Īśvara, specify a recitation, or describe how dedication works. Those
questions are taken up in the following sequence. We should not supply their
answers in advance or reduce *vā* to a decorative transition.

The [Gītā 9.27](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C09/gita-9.27.md) says: whatever one
does, eats, offers, gives, or practices as austerity, one should do as an
offering. That supplies a concrete comparison for dedication as a mode of
action. It is a comparison, not a claim that the Gītā's addressee and the
Īśvara of I.23 have already been identified.

The [Kośa 1.02](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02.md) names stainless
*prajñā* with its attendants as Abhidharma and also names the teaching that
serves its attainment. This distinction gives our Prajñāpāda reading a useful
question: how does dedication belong to the cultivation of knowing? The
Kośa verse does not answer the Yoga sūtra for us.

The same *īśvara-praṇidhāna* enters Kriyā Yoga at II.1 and recurs at II.45.
Prajñāpāda defines *samāpatti* at I.41; Jñānapāda defines *samādhi* at III.3.
Thus I.23 begins a thread of practice whose further determinations must be
read through Kriyāpāda and Jñānapāda.

## 3. Project Determination

Read I.23 within Prajñāpāda as an opening from graded intensity to dedication.
Kriyāpāda develops that practice; Jñānapāda gives the technical definition of
*samādhi*. This sequence is our reading of the three pādas, not a definition
contained in I.23 alone.
