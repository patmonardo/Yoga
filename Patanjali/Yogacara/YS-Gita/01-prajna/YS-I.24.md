# YS I.24 — The Unaffected Puruṣa

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> क्लेशकर्मविपाकाशयैरपरामृष्टः पुरुषविशेष ईश्वरः ॥ १.२४॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> kleśa-karma-vipāka-āśayair aparāmṛṣṭaḥ puruṣa-viśeṣa īśvaraḥ // 1.24 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *kleśa-karma-vipāka-āśayaiḥ* | instrumental plural | by afflictions, actions, their ripenings, and deposits |
| *aparāmṛṣṭaḥ* | nominative singular past participle with privative *a-* | untouched, unaffected |
| *puruṣa-viśeṣaḥ* | nominative singular | a distinctive puruṣa |
| *īśvaraḥ* | nominative singular | Īśvara, the one so characterized |

## 4. Grammar

The instrumental names what does not touch this *puruṣa-viśeṣa*. The compound
lists four related terms; the grammar alone does not identify them with one
another. *Aparāmṛṣṭaḥ* and *puruṣa-viśeṣaḥ* characterize *īśvaraḥ*.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Īśvara is a distinctive puruṣa untouched by afflictions, actions, their
> ripenings, and deposits.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.23 introduced dedication to Īśvara as a means. I.24 determines its referent
by freedom from a fourfold field of conditioning. It does not say that Īśvara
is a creator cause, nor does it yet describe the whole practice of dedication.

## 7. Interpretive Note

In [Gītā 9.29](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C09/gita-9.29.md), Śaṅkara reads the
Lord's equality toward beings together with a response to devotion that does
not arise from attachment. This helps articulate how dedication can meet a
responsive Īśvara without attributing a *kleśa* to him. The [Kośa Bhāṣya on
VAK 5.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/05-anusaya/VAK_5.01_bhasya.md) explains
how *anuśayas* condition the accumulation and efficacy of karma and how active
*kleśa* sustains the continuum. It clarifies the conditioning named here,
without defining Yoga's Īśvara or equating *āśaya* with *anuśaya*.
