# YS I.41 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> क्षीणवृत्तेरभिजातस्येव मणेर्ग्रहीतृग्रहणग्राह्येषु तत्स्थतदञ्जनता समापत्तिः ॥ १.४१॥

> *kṣīṇa-vṛtter abhijātasyeva maṇer grahītṛ-grahaṇa-grāhyeṣu tat-stha-tad-añjanatā samāpattiḥ* — Samāpatti is the clear jewel's resting in and taking the coloring of grasper, grasping, or grasped.

## 2. Commentary

After I.40 has established the reach of *vaśīkāra*, I.41 asks what
disciplined consciousness does within that reach. The jewel is clear
enough to show what it rests upon. Likewise, when *vṛtti* has diminished,
consciousness can stay with the grasper, the act of grasping, or the
grasped without losing the determination to another movement. Its
clarity is active: *tat-stha* is abiding there, and *tad-añjanatā* is
taking that term's coloring.

The three terms are moments of one cognitive relation. Yoga names all
three before it begins the differentiations of *savitarkā* and the later
samāpattis. Thus I.41 gives the architecture for the remaining sequence,
not merely a single experience to be left behind at I.42. Each later
determination must show what becomes clear and how the relation itself
changes.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
provides a basic analytic aid. It calls *samāpatti* wholesome
one-pointedness, asks what *ekāgratā* means, and answers with one
object-support. It also counts the accompanying factors in the full
attainment. This helps us inspect the working field of consciousness;
Yoga's jewel and its three stations supply the governing determination
of this sūtra.

## 3. Project Determination

Our Being–Essence–Concept machine begins here as an interpretation of
*samāpatti*. The grasper, grasping, and grasped are approached together
as Principle Base, Essence Base, and Form Base: distinct moments held in
one rational process. The clear jewel is the disciplined consciousness
in which their relation can appear without any one moment hiding the
others. The [companion essay](../essays/01-prajna/samapatti-and-the-three-bases-at-i-41.md)
works through the three Bases and poses *paramārtha* as their implicit
pure Principle and *saṃvṛti* as the Form : Essence field in which that
Principle becomes explicit. The
[Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-41-fichte-report.md)
develops the midpoint and the passage from factical to genetic knowing.
Here the Bhāṣya establishes the operation that the following sūtras
will specify.
