# YS I.42 — Savitarkā Samāpatti

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तत्र शब्दार्थज्ञानविकल्पैः सङ्कीर्णा सवितर्का समापत्तिः ॥ १.४२॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tatra śabdārthajñānavikalpaiḥ saṅkīrṇā savitarkā samāpattiḥ // 1.42 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *tatra* | locative adverb | there, within the samāpatti just introduced |
| *śabda-artha-jñāna-vikalpaiḥ* | instrumental plural | with distinctions involving word, *artha*, and knowledge |
| *saṅkīrṇā* | feminine nominative singular | intermixed |
| *sa-vitarkā* | feminine nominative singular | accompanied by vitarka |
| *samāpattiḥ* | feminine nominative singular | samāpatti |

## 4. Grammar

*Saṅkīrṇā* and *savitarkā* qualify *samāpattiḥ*. The instrumental
plural states what is intermixed in this attainment; it does not say
that word, *artha*, and knowledge are identical. *Tatra* ties the case
to I.41's clear jewel.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> There, samāpatti intermixed with distinctions of word, *artha*, and
> knowledge is *savitarkā*.

## 6. Logical Determination

This is the first specified mode of the samāpatti introduced at I.41.
The jewel now shows a determinate content, but the word that names it,
the impure *artha* named, and knowledge of it still arrive together in
mixture. In this *saṃvṛti* field, *artha* is Objective Artha and
implicates an object; it is
not a neutral dictionary substitute for “object.” The mixture is a
structure to be investigated, not by itself a falsehood.

## 7. Interpretive Note

In our [companion essay](../essays/01-prajna/savitarka-nirvitarka-and-the-three-bases.md),
*savitarkā* is the Dharma-skandha process of an impure Dharma: Form Base
presents; Essence Base relates, names, and discloses; Principle Base is
still implicit in their mixture. The [Kośa at VAK 1.32](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.32_bhasya.md)
classifies *vitarka* and *vicāra* by their association with different
cognition-domains. That classification helps analyze the operative field;
Yoga's mixture of word, impure *artha*, and knowledge sets the present
question.
