# YS I.24 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> क्लेशकर्मविपाकाशयैरपरामृष्टः पुरुषविशेष ईश्वरः ॥ १.२४॥

> *kleśa-karma-vipāka-āśayair aparāmṛṣṭaḥ puruṣa-viśeṣa īśvaraḥ* — Īśvara is a distinctive puruṣa untouched by afflictions, actions, their ripenings, and deposits.

## 2. Commentary

The *vā* of I.23 opened dedication as another way. I.24 asks what sort of
referent can sustain it. Its answer is positive as well as negative: Īśvara is
a *puruṣa-viśeṣa*, distinguished by being untouched by *kleśa*, *karma*,
*vipāka*, and *āśaya*. The four terms trace an intelligible field of
affliction, action, result, and retained conditioning. They should remain
distinct even as the sūtra gathers them under one instrumental ending.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 5.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/05-anusaya/VAK_5.01_bhasya.md)
says that karma accumulates under the power of *anuśayas* and cannot bring
forth existence without them. It then lists what an active *kleśa* does to
the continuum, including bending the stream of cognition away from the
wholesome. This gives substance to the field from which I.24 distinguishes
Īśvara. The Kośa's *anuśaya* and this sūtra's *āśaya* are not interchangeable
technical terms.

[Gītā 9.29](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C09/gita-9.29.md) says the Lord is equal
toward all beings, with none hated or preferred, while devotees dwell in him
and he in them. Śaṅkara compares this to fire: it warms those who approach,
without needing attachment or aversion to explain the difference. Read beside
I.23–24, the comparison makes room for grace and response without importing
an affliction into Īśvara. It is Śaṅkara's Gītā argument, not the lexical
meaning of *aparāmṛṣṭaḥ*.

The [Kośa's four immeasurables at VAK 8.29–30](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.29.md)
are a further, practical comparison. Friendliness, compassion, sympathetic
joy, and equanimity counter hostility and possessive partiality in relation
to beings. Their cultivation belongs to practitioners; I.24 speaks about
Īśvara. This distinction lets the comparison prepare our reading of I.33
without claiming that I.24 itself teaches the immeasurables.

## 3. Project Determination

Īśvara can be approached through dedication precisely as one untouched by
the conditioning that makes ordinary action fruit bearing. The sūtra gives a
relation for practice in Prajñāpāda; its determinations develop through the
following verses and the later Kriyāpāda.
