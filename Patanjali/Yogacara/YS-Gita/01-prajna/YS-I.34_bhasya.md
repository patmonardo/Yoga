# YS I.34 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> प्रच्छर्दनविधारणाभ्यां वा प्राणस्य ॥ १.३४॥

> *pracchardana-vidhāraṇābhyāṃ vā prāṇasya* — Or, through exhalation and retention of the vital breath.

## 2. Commentary

The *vā* opens another means after I.33's four cultivations. The sūtra
specifies two operations concerning *prāṇa*: *pracchardana*, letting it
out, and *vidhāraṇa*, holding it. Its brevity leaves the operative detail
unstated. In this place in Prajñāpāda, its apparent role is to aid the
clarity of consciousness named in I.33 and to answer the dispersion of
I.30–31.

The [Gītā 4.29](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C04/gita-4.29.md) describes
movements of *prāṇa* and *apāna*. Śaṅkara distinguishes filling,
exhalation, and retention in his explanation. That gives a nearby
vocabulary for reading I.34's two operations, while the Yoga sūtra itself
states only exhalation and retention.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya on VAK 6.12](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.12_bhasya.md)
determines breath recollection as *prajñā* directed to the wind-process,
supported by recollection. This is especially useful for our Prajñāpāda:
attention to breath can disclose an intelligible process, not merely
occupy the mind. Still, the Kośa describes *ānāpānasmṛti*, and I.34 says
*pracchardana-vidhāraṇa*. Their practices should be compared through their
objects and operations, without being declared identical.

I.34 is one of a series of options marked by *vā*. We can follow each in
turn without losing the main movement toward the later account of
*samāpatti*. The four relational cultivations of I.33 remain a positive
achievement of the chapter, not a stage displaced by breath practice.

## 3. Project Determination

Breath offers a concrete field for steadying consciousness and for the
discernment of a process. This is a provisional application of the sūtra;
its fuller practical architecture belongs to the later Kriyāpāda.

## 4. Review Status

First pass on I.34. Continue with the alternative of I.35.
