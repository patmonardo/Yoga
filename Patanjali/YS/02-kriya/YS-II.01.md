# YS II.1 — Kriyāyoga

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तपःस्वाध्यायेश्वरप्रणिधानानि क्रियायोगः ॥ २.१॥

*Source:* `Patanjali/Sources/Yoga.txt`, second chapter. Its received heading is *Sādhana-pāda*. Our study names the chapter **Kriyā-pāda**, taking the opening sūtra's *kriyāyogaḥ* as the governing problem. The received heading does not govern our interpretation.

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tapaḥsvādhyāyeśvarapraṇidhānāni kriyāyogaḥ // 2.1 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

The transmitted continuous form is *tapaḥsvādhyāyeśvarapraṇidhānāni*. Its three members are *tapas*, *svādhyāya*, and *īśvara-praṇidhāna*.

## 4. Grammar

The final plural *-āni* gathers the members; *kriyā-yogaḥ* is singular and names their unity as a practice.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Discipline, self-study, and dedication to Īśvara are yoga of action.

Here “self-study” is provisional: *svādhyāya* can include study or recitation of authoritative teaching. Its force in this chapter needs to be determined with further textual and commentary evidence.

## 6. Logical Determination

The sūtra gives a threefold specification of *kriyāyoga*. It does not yet state its purpose; II.2 supplies that purpose as the cultivation of samādhi and the attenuation of the kleśas. Thus II.1 should be read with II.2 before the three terms are expanded into a larger system.

Our first-pass study will test how this practical unity serves the two modes opened in I.17–18: *saṃprajñāta* is named in I.17, while I.18 calls the other mode *anyaḥ* and describes it through cessation-practice and residual dispositions. Calling the latter *asaṃprajñāta* is an interpretive designation, not a word in the cited I.18 sūtra. We must not let one mode erase the other.

## 7. Interpretive Note

### Questions for our Bhāṣya

- What makes the three practices one *yoga* rather than a list of exercises?
- How do *tapas* and *svādhyāya* determine the meaning of *īśvara-praṇidhāna*, and conversely?
- How does the practical operation of II.1 answer the account of consciousness and its cessation in I.2?
- How does this operation prepare or transform each of the two modes of samādhi without reducing one to the other?

These are questions for our commentary, not claims supplied by the sūtra alone.
