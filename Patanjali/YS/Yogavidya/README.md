# Yogavidya — Science of Absolute Knowing

This is the editorial home for the **second layer** of the Yoga Sūtra project: the author's Form : Essence : Principle : System, developed through Yoga Vidyā and the Fichte–Hegel studies. Its intended audience is friends while the work develops.

The [Kośa-focused commentary](../README.md) is the first complete-commentary project. It establishes the textual and comparative ground. Yogavidya can take up the author's interpretations of sublation, *saṃvṛti : paramārtha*, samāpatti, the Bhavacakra and Dharmacakra, and the later transformation of memory and *saṃskāra*. Each such claim should retain its source anchors and be identified as part of this second layer rather than attributed to Patañjali or Vasubandhu.

The existing Fichte and Hegel reports remain in their current locations. This directory records the distinct editorial destination; it does not claim that those reports or the Loka : Karma upgrades have been completed here.
