# Yogavidya — Science of Absolute Knowing

This is the Yogavidya component of **Organon Yoga** and the editorial home
for its second layer: the author's Form : Essence : Principle : System,
developed through Yoga Vidyā and the Fichte–Hegel studies. Its intended
audience is friends while the work develops.

The [Kośa-focused commentary](../README.md) is the first complete-commentary project. It establishes the textual and comparative ground. Yogavidya can take up the author's interpretations of sublation, *saṃvṛti : paramārtha*, samāpatti, the Bhavacakra and Dharmacakra, and the later transformation of memory and *saṃskāra*. Each such claim should retain its source anchors and be identified as part of this second layer rather than attributed to Patañjali or Vasubandhu.

The existing Fichte and Hegel reports remain in their current locations. This directory records the distinct editorial destination; it does not claim that those reports or the Loka : Karma upgrades have been completed here.

The [Māṇḍūkya study of OṂ and its *artha*](Prajnapada/mandukya-om-artha-study.md)
begins the second-layer inquiry prompted by Yoga Sūtra I.27–29. It reads the
Upaniṣad and Śaṅkara directly before developing the Organon comparison.

The [I.40 essay on mastery and the Kośa's *samāpatti*
analysis](Prajnapada/ys-i-40-range-and-samapatti.md) begins the companion
essay sequence for I.40–51. These essays develop the key Yogavidya topics
for a later Vārttika while the paired sūtra studies remain concise.

The [Organon Sāṃkhya report area](../../../Docs/Samkhya/README.md)
holds the separate Sāṃkhya source work needed before expanding earlier
Yoga studies, including the question of *aiśvarya* and the immeasurables.
