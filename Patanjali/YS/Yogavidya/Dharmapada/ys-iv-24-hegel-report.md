# Citta as the Mode of the Absolute

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.24 with Hegel and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`tad asaṃkhyeya-vāsanābhiś citram api parārthaṃ saṃhatya-kāritvāt`

### Simple translation

> Although Citta is variegated by countless Vāsanās, it works for a higher
> Artha because its activity is combinative.

The Sūtra moves through four determinations:

- `asaṃkhyeya-vāsanābhiḥ`: countless formative tendencies;
- `citram api`: an indefinitely variegated Citta;
- `saṃhatya-kāritvāt`: activity through combination or articulated
  cooperation;
- `parārtham`: activity for an Artha beyond the isolated composite.

```text
countless Vāsanās
        ↓
variegated Citta
        ↓ acts through combination
complete self-manifesting movement
        ↓
higher Artha
```

Hegel names this combinative manifestation the **Mode of the Absolute**.

## Why IV.24 follows the Attribute

IV.23 showed that the Seer-coloring and seen-coloring are each Attributes:
the complete content of Citta expressed under distinct forms.

But Attribute still leaves a problem. If each form merely returns into the
same Absolute identity, the distinction between Appearance and Reflection
disappears without being explained.

IV.24 asks how the distinction itself belongs to the Principle.

```text
IV.23 — Attribute
the whole content under each determinate form
                        ↓
IV.24 — Mode
the living movement through which the Absolute
externalizes itself and returns into itself
```

The countless Vāsanās and their combinations are no longer merely material
contained in Citta. They are modes of its actual manifestation.

## From A–U–M as structure to A–U–M as movement

IV.23 gave the structural triad:

```text
A                         U
Appearance                Reflection / thinking
objective Attribute       reflective Attribute
             \           /
                  M
Knowing their complete relation
```

IV.24 sets this triad in motion:

```text
A
the Absolute externalizes itself as variegated Appearance
        ↓
U
Reflection recognizes externality as reflective shine
        ↓
M
the movement manifests the Absolute as Actuality
```

M is not a stationary connector. It is the self-returning movement through
which A and U become one act of Knowing.

## Vāsanā as modal determination

A Vāsanā is not merely a private memory stored inside a psychological
container. It is a formative tendency through which the one field repeatedly
assumes a determinate mode.

```text
Citi-Śakti
the power of Knowing
        ↓ modalized through Vāsanā
determinate Citta-pattern
        ↓
a characteristic way of appearing and reflecting
```

Countless Vāsanās make Citta `citra`, variegated. Yet every variation is a
mode within the one combinative activity rather than a separate Citta-world.

This preserves both multiplicity and Principle.

## Hegel's Absolute Mode

Hegel begins with two extremes implicit in Attribute:

```text
simple absolute identity        determinate negation
the Absolute                    external Reflection
                \              /
                   Attribute
         both Absolute and determinateness
```

If negation is taken merely as external, the Absolute seems to lose itself
in contingency and change. Its modes appear to be a manifold lacking
totality.

This is Citta viewed only from the side of innumerable Vāsanās:

```text
formation after formation
change after change
no visible unifying Artha
```

But Mode is not mere externality. It is externality **posited as
externality**.

## Reflective shine as reflective shine

An unmastered Appearance claims independent reality. A Mode knows its own
externality as Appearance.

```text
mere externality
“this determination exists independently”

externality posited as externality
“this determination is the outward form
of a self-manifesting relation”
```

Hegel calls this reflective shine posited as reflective shine. The
appearance does not vanish. Its status becomes explicit.

This is the modal equivalent of Prajñā-Saṃskāra: the determination retains
not only its content but knowledge of its own genesis and formal status.

## The Absolute becomes itself through Mode

Hegel makes the paradoxical claim that the Absolute is first posited as
absolute identity in its Mode.

The Absolute is not fully itself as an unmoved identity behind Appearance.
It becomes explicit as self-identity only by passing through its external
determination and returning into itself.

```text
abstract identity
        ↓ externalizes itself
Mode / determinate Appearance
        ↓ recognizes externality as its own shine
self-referring negativity
        ↓
actual absolute identity
```

The Mode does not make the Absolute into something other than itself. It
makes the Absolute what it already is—explicitly and actually.

## `Saṃhatya-kāritva`: the Absolute acts through combination

The Sūtra says Citta works through combination. Hegel shows why combination
cannot be external assembly.

```text
not:

independent parts + external organizer = Citta


but:

the whole differentiates itself into moments
whose relation is its own self-manifestation
```

Each moment has meaning through the movement of the whole:

```text
identity → Attribute → Mode → Actuality
```

The combination is therefore genetic. Its terms arise through the same
movement that relates them.

## The exposition is the Absolute's own act

The exposition can initially look like an external philosopher collecting
finite determinations and reducing them to an Absolute presupposed in
advance.

```text
external Reflection
finds modes outside the Absolute
        ↓
returns them to undifferentiated identity
```

Hegel reverses this appearance. The determinateness from which Reflection
begins was already found within the Absolute. Even the first apparently
unmoved identity is a determination produced by reflective movement.

```text
Absolute
        ↓ determines itself
Reflection and externality
        ↓ return through their own movement
Absolute self-exposition
```

The Scientist is not manufacturing the connection. The content is exposing
its own relation.

## Transparent externality

Hegel gives one of the strongest definitions of Mode:

> a transparent externality that points to itself.

```text
external manifestation
        ↓ becomes transparent
points through itself to the Principle
        ↓
its outwardness is equally inwardness
```

Transparency does not erase the mode. A transparent form remains a form;
what disappears is its claim to hide or replace its Principle.

This is how a Vāsanā-pattern can be mastered without being treated as an
evil entity. Its operation becomes transparent within the Science of Citta.

## `Parārtha` as the Artha of manifestation

The conventional phrase “for another” can suggest an external Puruṣa using
Citta as an instrument. Hegel gives a more immanent reading.

The composite works for a higher Artha because no isolated mode contains the
purpose of the complete exposition.

```text
isolated Citta-mode
does not ground its own significance
                    ↓
complete self-manifesting movement
discloses why the mode exists
                    ↓
parārtha
```

The “other” is not another empirical mind. It is the complete Artha in which
the mode's outward determination and inward Principle belong together.

## Content is the exposition itself

Hegel asks what content the Absolute manifests. His answer is radical:

> Its content is this manifestation itself.

There is no hidden metaphysical packet behind the process that must be
transferred into Citta.

```text
not:

hidden content inside the Absolute
       ↓ transmitted outward
external Appearance


but:

the Absolute's differentiation and return
is itself the complete content
```

This is why Citta is the Science of Knowing. Its articulated process is not
a veil concealing the real content; once comprehended, the articulation is
the content's actuality.

## Absolute manifestation is Actuality

Hegel concludes:

> The Absolute is expression, not of an inner and not over against another,
> but absolute manifestation of itself for itself. Thus it is Actuality.

```text
Inner                           Outer
Principle                       manifestation
          \                    /
           no external opposition
                    ↓
       self-manifestation for itself
                    ↓
                Actuality
```

IV.24 therefore completes the exposition of the Absolute and opens
Actuality proper.

## Actuality is higher than mere Being

Being is immediate and passes into another. Concrete existence has proceeded
from ground and conditions but has not yet posited the unity of Reflection
and immediacy.

Actuality is their explicit unity:

```text
immediacy / Appearance
            +
immanent Reflection / Principle
            ↓
Actuality
```

The actual manifests itself. It is itself only through its outward
self-differentiation.

Thus `citra` is no obstacle to Actuality. Variegation is the required field
of self-manifestation.

## Fichte's IV.24: two forms of the `should`

Fichte approaches the same Sūtra through the relation of ordinary and
transcendental Knowing.

In ordinary Knowing, a hypothetical `should` joins terms while silently
assuming its premise:

```text
ordinary `should`
given this tacit premise, that result should follow
```

In transcendental Knowing, the `should` grounds its own premise genetically:

```text
transcendental `should`
the Science derives why the premise and result belong together
```

The two extremes—ordinary actual Knowing and transcendental Knowing—proceed
from a middle premise that both joins and distinguishes them.

## Hegel and Fichte compared

| Fichte | Hegel |
|---|---|
| ordinary Knowing operates from tacit premises | modes initially appear as external determinations |
| transcendental Knowing grounds the premise genetically | externality becomes reflective shine as reflective shine |
| the middle premise unites and separates the extremes | Attribute mediates absolute identity and determinate negation |
| terms reside a priori in insight into their connection | modes are generated by the Absolute's own reflective movement |
| the arbitrary `should` must become categorical | external exposition must reveal itself as self-exposition |
| the Science must justify its procedure | the content is the movement of exposition itself |

Fichte gives the logical form of the combination. Hegel gives the ontological
movement through which the combination is Actuality.

## Their mutual correction

Fichte prevents Hegelian Mode from being treated as an elegant description
of manifestation. The Science must demonstrate genetically why each term and
connection follows.

Hegel prevents Fichte's categorical insight from remaining an inward
justification of premises. The grounded connection must exist as transparent
externality—as actual manifestation.

```text
Fichte
the connection must justify itself genetically
                    +
Hegel
the justified connection must manifest itself objectively
                    ↓
Citta as self-grounding, self-manifesting combination
```

## A–U–M as Absolute Mode

The completed movement can now be stated:

```text
A — Appearance
the variegated external mode
        ↓
U — Reflection / thinking
externality is known as reflective shine
        ↓
M — Knowing
the self-returning movement is Actuality
```

A and U remain distinguishable. M is not a third static term but their
living, self-comprehending combination.

This is the Principle required by the Science of Citta.

## The Fichtean Five-Fold Synthesis

The triad A–U–M must not conceal the complete articulation of M. Hegel
completes the relation through the same five-fold synthesis found in
Fichte:

```text
1. A                     Appearance

2. U                     Reflection / thinking

3. A → U                 Appearance sublates itself into Reflection

4. U → A                 Reflection sublates itself into Appearance

5. M                     Knowing: the Concept containing both terms
                         and both directions of transition
```

The fifth is not an additional object after the first four. It is the
Concept to which both determinations and their reciprocal sublations are
immanent.

Hegel's elementary model is Becoming:

```text
Being                         Nothing
  |                              |
  └──► passing into Nothing      └──► passing into Being
          ceasing-to-be                 coming-to-be
                  \              /
                       Becoming
```

Being and Nothing each sublate the other, but neither is discarded as
foreign to the result. Both, together with the two opposed movements, are
inherent moments of the Concept of Becoming.

The same structure governs IV.24:

```text
Appearance                     Reflection
     |                             |
     └──► becomes transparent      └──► externalizes itself
          to its Principle              as Appearance
                   \              /
                    Absolute Mode
              the Concept of their
              reciprocal movement
```

Thus M is not merely the final return of Appearance into Reflection. It is
the complete double movement:

```text
A → U     outwardness becomes inwardly comprehended
U → A     inwardness becomes objective manifestation
```

Each side sublates the other while both remain inherent to their Concept.
This is why Hegel can say that outwardness is equally inwardness and that
the content of the Absolute is its exposition itself.

`Saṃhatya-kāritva` is therefore five-fold rather than merely aggregative.
Citta combines the two forms, both directions of their transformation, and
the Knowing that holds the entire reciprocal process as one Artha.

## Provisional result

IV.24 says:

> Although innumerable Vāsanās articulate Citta into manifold modes, their
> combinative activity exists for the higher Artha disclosed by the complete
> movement of manifestation.

Hegel shows that Mode is not a foreign accident attached to an unmoved
Absolute. It is the Absolute's own externality, posited as externality and
therefore transparent to its Principle. The content of the Absolute is this
self-exposition itself.

Fichte shows that the combination becomes scientific only when its middle
premise is genetically grounded and the hypothetical `should` becomes
categorical.

```text
IV.23
A and U are complete Attributes of one content
                    ↓
IV.24
M is their self-returning modal movement
                    ↓
Actuality
the Absolute manifests itself for itself as Citta
```

IV.25 can therefore expose a determinate self-formation as merely contingent
Actuality rather than Ātman.
