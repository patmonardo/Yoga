# Eka Citta as Essential Identity

## Yoga Sutra IV.5 with Hegel

`pravrtti-bhede prayojakam cittam ekam anekesam`

> In the differentiation of activities, one Citta directs the many.

The Kantian formulation remains our plain key:

> The Eka Citta must be able to accompany the many Cittas.

Hegel's Varttika prevents this unity from becoming an empty `I think` placed
above the manifold. Essential identity is not abstract sameness. It produces
itself through negativity and preserves itself in difference.

```text
abstract identity     excludes difference

essential identity    differentiates itself and is itself in that difference
```

Eka Citta is therefore not a master Citta externally directing subordinate
Cittas. Nor is it a Puruṣa observing them from elsewhere. It is the immanent
self-identity of reflective activity through its differentiated `pravrttis`.

The many are necessary. Without them the one would be an empty assertion;
without the one they would be an aggregate with no cognition of their
togetherness.

Hegel adds to Kant:

> The one Citta does not merely have to accompany the many. It produces and
> maintains its identity through the act of differentiating itself as many.

This does not make the empirical I the creator of reality. The empirical I is
one constructed determination. Eka Citta names the deeper identity of
reflection in which identity and difference are moments of one movement.

The fifth Citta-Dharma is thus synthetic identity: Citta is one only by being
one through its own many determinate activities.
