# Citta Cannot Be Both Sides of the Relation at Once

## Yoga Sūtra IV.20 with Hegel

## The Sūtra

`eka-samaye cobhayānavadhāraṇam`

> Nor can both be determined by Citta in the same moment.

The two sides are supplied by IV.19: Citta as present seeing and the object
seen, including Citta itself when a prior cognition becomes an object of
reflection.

```text
present seeing  ---------->  Thing seen

reflective seeing  ------->  prior seeing now seen
```

One immediate Citta-act cannot be completely present as the seeing and at the
same time completely determined as the seen. Their unity requires mediation.

## The truth of Appearance is Essential Relation

IV.19 dissolved the opposition between the World of Appearance and the
World-in-itself. Citta and Puruṣa could no longer remain two self-sufficient
worlds, yet neither could be reduced to the other.

Hegel states the result:

> The truth of Appearance is Essential Relation.

Each side of an Essential Relation has a real self-subsistence, but has that
self-subsistence only through reference to the other:

```text
seeing                                 seen
self-subsistent as one act             self-subsistent as content
          \                           /
           \                         /
            each is only through the other
```

IV.20 expresses the incompleteness of this first unity. Both sides belong to
one relation, but they cannot collapse into one immediate determination.

## Why simultaneity fails

To know the Thing, Citta occupies the position of seeing. To know that act of
seeing, Citta must make it determinate content. But the reflective act that
does this is a new relational position.

```text
act 1    Citta determines the Thing
act 2    Citta determines act 1
```

Act 2 can include act 1 as content, but it cannot be act 1 in the same
respect. If the distinction is erased, agent, act, and object lose the
determinations through which cognition is intelligible.

The limit is logical, not merely a defect of mental processing speed. Even
an infinitely fast Citta would still require the distinction between seeing
and what is seen.

## Vyāsa's compressed argument

Vyāsa rejects the idea that one momentary Citta could simultaneously
determine its own form and the form of another. A single undifferentiated
moment would have to be:

- the cognitive agent;
- the cognitive activity;
- the external object known;
- itself as object known.

The required functions would be asserted but not mediated. The moment would
stand on both sides of its own relation without differentiating those sides.

Thus `ubhayānavadhāraṇam` is not simple failure. It is the recognition that
relation has an irreducible internal difference.

## Each side is a totality

Hegel's Essential Relation is stronger than a relation between two partial
fragments. Each side is a totality from its own standpoint:

```text
Citta as seeing       contains its object as seen
Thing as seen         contains its relation to cognition as Appearance
```

Yet each totality has an opposite or beyond. Citta does not create the whole
objective content; the Thing does not illuminate its own Appearance in
Citta. Each is internally fractured by its reference to the other.

That fracture is not a weakness added from outside. It is what makes each
side a determinate totality rather than an empty identity.

## Relation is not yet Substance

At IV.20 the identity of the two sides remains only their reference. Their
self-subsistence still appears to reside separately within each side.

```text
identity of relation        seeing and seen belong together

self-subsistence of sides   each still appears to stand on its own
```

Hegel says that we do not yet have Substance. The unity has not yet realized
itself as the ground of the sides' own self-subsistence.

This explains the temporal form of the Sūtra. Citta moves from one side to
the other because the unity of the whole relation is not immediately present
as one self-grounding actuality.

## Fichte's hiatus

Fichte reaches the same boundary by asking how knowing could observe its own
complete self-genesis. Knowing cannot leave itself, stand outside itself,
and then watch itself as both generating subject and generated object.

```text
immanent knowing
       |
       | cannot exit itself
       v
no external standpoint containing both termini at once
```

The reconstruction of genesis therefore contains a necessary `hiatus`.
Pure Reason cannot appear as one more item within the series whose unity it
makes intelligible.

This is not an irrational hole. It is the formal limit that prevents the
World of Appearance from claiming to be Absolute Actuality.

## Aspectual Essential Relation

Our Spectral Theory expresses the distinction without multiplying Absolutes:

```text
Light under invariant Aspect       Puruṣa
Light under changing Aspect        Citta
their differentiated unity         Essential Relation
```

One Citta-act cannot collapse both Aspects into an immediate identity. If it
did, neither invariance nor transformation would remain determinate.

The two Aspects are not independent substances. They are the differentiated
forms through which Non-Appearing Light becomes intelligible within
Appearance.

## The practical limit

The same distinction holds in action. One cannot be wholly absorbed in a
living response and simultaneously possess a complete theoretical account of
oneself performing it.

```text
living Karuṇā                 reflection upon my Karuṇā
immediate practical act      cognition of that act
```

Reflection can educate action, and action can supply reflection with real
content. But their truth is mediated across moments rather than compressed
into one self-watching performance.

This is why an analytic inventory of Citta-factors cannot replace the living
whole it analyzes.

## The Organic Oculus cannot see itself all at once

IV.20 gives the Organic Oculus a constitutive limit. It cannot simultaneously
generate an Ocular dataset, inhabit its determinate seeing, and possess a
complete external account of that generation in one immediate act.

```text
Ocular operation
      ↓ produces a determinate field
Absolute Reflection
      ↓ reconstructs that operation
renewed Ocular operation
```

Self-reflection is therefore organic and sequential. The Oculus regenerates
its field through its reflection; it does not freeze itself into a total
object while remaining the living act reflected upon.

## Hegel-Vārttika

> Citta cannot determine both itself and the Thing in one moment because the
> truth of seeing and seen is Essential Relation. Each side is self-subsistent
> only through the other, yet their unity is not an immediate identity. The
> seeing becomes seen only through a new reflective act, and the whole
> relation must therefore realize itself through mediation.

IV.20 establishes the formal limit of Citta within Essential Relation:

> **One immediate cognition cannot occupy and comprehend both relational
> positions at once. Their unity belongs to the mediated whole, not to either
> side taken alone.**

IV.21 will test whether another Citta can supply that whole. The resulting
regress will force the relation of Whole and Parts to disclose its own
contradiction.
