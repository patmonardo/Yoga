# Infinite Knowledge and the Little That Remains Knowable

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.31 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`tadā sarvāvaraṇa-malāpetasya jñānasyānantyāj jñeyam alpam`

### Simple translation

> Then, because Knowledge is infinite when every covering impurity has
> fallen away, what remains to be known is little.

The terms establish a proportion:

- `tadā`: then, following the cessation of Kleśa and Karma;
- `sarva-āvaraṇa-mala-apetasya`: for that from which every covering and
  impurity has departed;
- `jñānasya ānantyāt`: because of the infinitude of Knowledge;
- `jñeyam alpam`: the knowable is little.

```text
coverings and impurities fall away
                    ↓
Knowledge discloses its infinitude
                    ↓
the independently knowable becomes little
```

The Sūtra does not say that the universe contains almost no information. It
says that once the Principle of Knowing is infinite, very little remains
standing over against it as an independently unknown object.

## The second moment of a Triple Siddhi

Fichte ends the corresponding movement by announcing three primary
modifications of primordial Light. Yoga IV.30–32 can be read as a Triple
Siddhi:

```text
IV.30    immanent certainty
         Kleśa and Karma cease
         the Vajropama moment

IV.31    infinite Knowledge
         the finite knowable is reduced to its proper status

IV.32    completed purpose
         the sequence of Guṇa-transformation ends
```

These are not three supernatural trophies accumulated by a saintly
personality. They are three necessary determinations of the Science of
Knowing.

IV.31 is the second Siddhi: Knowing comprehends the gap through which a
finite object appears.

## Why IV.30 is Vajropama

In IV.29, Fichte defined certainty as unshakable persistence in one
qualitative form. In IV.30, this certainty became immediately living and
self-projecting. No Kleśa or Karma can break its continuity.

This is structurally the Vajropama Samādhi:

```text
changeable contents and residual formations
                       ↓ cannot fracture
unchangeable qualitative certainty
                       ↓
Kleśa-Karma ceases
```

The comparison is systematic rather than philological: Fichte does not use
the Buddhist term. But the diamond-like function is exact. Knowing has
become incapable of being displaced from its Principle by a determinate
appearance.

## Knowledge is infinite

`Jñānasya ānantya` is encyclopedic omniscience in the sense of
`Samyag-Jñāna`: Knowledge of the Principles through which every determinate
knowable and science receives its systematic place. It is not `Omnividyā`,
the itemwise storage of infinitely many propositions in Manas.

Infinite Knowledge means:

> No determinate Artha lies outside the form through which Knowing can
> comprehend its genesis and relation.

```text
not Omnividyā:

an infinite database of separately collected facts


but Samyag-Jñāna:

the Principle capable of generating and comprehending
the form of every determinate knowable
```

The Encyclopedia is therefore not one actual science or a sum of sciences.
It is the actual Principle of a Science: the systematic form that generates,
orders, and connects the determinate sciences as one whole.

This is the culmination of `sarvārtha` from IV.23. Citta could bear every
Artha because it received the dual coloring of Seer and seen. IV.31 shows
that the Principle of this complete relation is unlimited.

## Why the knowable becomes little

As long as Knowing encounters each Thing as an independently existing
unknown, the field of the knowable appears boundless.

```text
finite Knowing
      versus
indefinitely many foreign Things
```

Once the law of Appearance is comprehended, the relation changes:

```text
infinite Principle of Knowing
              ↓ generates the form of
finite determinate appearances
```

The empirical details remain numerous. What becomes `little` is their claim
to constitute an independent realm standing outside the Science of Knowing.

The remaining finite object is a specification, not an alien absolute.

## Covering and impurity

An `āvaraṇa` covers the Principle by making its product appear independent.
A `mala` is the residual formation through which that appearance is
appropriated and repeatedly sustained.

```text
āvaraṇa
the genesis of appearance is concealed

mala
the concealed result is retained as a governing formation
```

When both fall away, the object need not disappear. It becomes transparent
as appearance: a determinate image whose relation to Knowing is understood.

## Fichte's Bhāṣya: Living Knowing intuits itself

Fichte begins:

> Living Knowing intuits itself unconditionally as it inwardly is because it
> really projects itself.

Knowing is absolutely from itself and must therefore intuit itself as such.
But it must also intuit itself as not existing outside intuition.

```text
Living Knowing
      ↓ projects itself
intuition of Knowing
      ↓
Knowing sees that its appearance
does not exist outside this intuition
```

This is the infinite side: Knowing is not limited by a foreign object that
would supply the ground of its intuition.

## The absolute gap

Paradoxically, self-projection produces a gap. Essence and its intuition are
distinguished:

```text
unintuitable Principle
          |
          | absolute gap
          ↓
Concept or image of the Principle
```

Fichte calls this projection through a gap the pure rational expression of
the true relation of things. The Concept is separated from Essence and is
not Essence itself; it is its image.

The gap is no longer the opening in which Kleśa rules. It has been
comprehended as a necessary difference within self-projection.

```text
unmastered gap of IV.27          mastered gap of IV.31

Saṃskāra supplies an            Knowing necessarily distinguishes
alien determination             Essence from its Concept or image
```

This is the second Siddhi: difference is retained without becoming alien
independence.

## Concept is not Essence

The Science of Knowing must preserve this discrimination:

```text
Essence                         Concept

the living, immanent           its projected image
Principle                       within intuition

not objectively present        objectively intelligible
```

To identify the Concept immediately with Essence would make the image into
the Absolute. To separate them absolutely would make Knowledge impossible.

The gap is therefore an Essential Relation. It distinguishes while remaining
the self-projection of one living Knowing.

This is why the finite `jñeya` remains. The knowable is the determinate image,
not the living Principle exhausted as an object.

## Apparent independence of the knowable

Within intuition, the process of projection appears to arise from itself.
The finite knowable therefore seems independent:

```text
Concept / intuition
appears to determine itself
and to stand on its own
```

But the Scientist of Knowing comprehends the genesis of this appearance.
The apparent independence is not intrinsically true. It is the appearance of
a higher, absolutely unintuitable `principle-providing`.

```text
finite knowable
appears independently self-producing
                   ↓ comprehended genetically
image of the higher unintuitable Principle
```

This is the precise meaning of “the knowable is little.” The finite image
retains its determination, but loses its claim to independent infinity.

## Buddhi-eva and the clean slate

`Buddhi-eva` does not erase the image. It removes the belief that the image
is supported by an independently existing cognitive substrate or foreign
Thing-in-itself.

```text
Manas
encounters a seemingly independent finite object

                    ↓ contemplated by

Buddhi
knows the object as a determinate image
within Knowing's self-projection
```

The slate is clean because every cognition begins from its intelligible
Principle rather than from accumulated appropriation. Its determinate
content can then be written without being mistaken for the slate itself.

## Dharma as finite determination

Citi-Śakti is infinite as the living power of Knowing. Dharma is the
determinate holding through which this power gives a particular Artha its
truth and stability.

```text
Citi-Śakti                     Dharma

infinite Knowing               finite determination held true
          \                    /
           \ self-projection /
               through Concept
```

Dharma is not outside Citi-Śakti. Nor does a single Dharma exhaust it. The
Cloud of Dharma signifies that unlimited Knowing can produce determinate
holdings without becoming confined by any one of them.

## Yoga and Fichte

| Yoga IV.31 | Fichte |
|---|---|
| every covering and impurity falls away | the Scientist sees through the apparent independence of intuition |
| Knowledge is infinite | living Knowing projects and intuits itself from itself |
| the knowable becomes little | the finite Concept is only the image of the higher Principle |
| the object remains as determination | the Concept is distinguished from Essence without becoming alien |
| knowing is unobstructed | the absolute gap is comprehended as necessary self-projection |
| the second Siddhi follows Vajropama | difference persists without fracturing certainty |

## Provisional result

IV.31 says:

> When every covering of the Principle and every residue of false
> appropriation has fallen away, Knowledge shows itself as infinite; the
> finite object remains only as its determinate image.

Fichte explains why an absolute gap nevertheless arises. Living Knowing
must project itself, and the projection distinguishes the unintuitable
Principle from its Concept. The Concept is not Essence but its image.

The gap is no longer a breach through which contingency or Kleśa can rule.
It is the rationally comprehended difference required for finite
intelligibility.

```text
First Siddhi — IV.30
certainty is unshakable; Kleśa-Karma ceases
                    ↓
Second Siddhi — IV.31
Knowledge is infinite; the finite knowable
is comprehended as its determinate image
```

IV.32 can now complete the Triple Siddhi by showing that the Guṇas have
fulfilled their Artha and their sequence of transformation ends.
