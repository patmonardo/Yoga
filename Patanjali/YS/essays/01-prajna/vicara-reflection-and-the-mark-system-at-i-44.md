# Vicāra: Reflection and the Central Mark System

## A Bhāṣya essay on Yoga Sūtra I.44

I.44 says that *savicārā* and *nirvicārā* samāpatti, whose *viṣaya*
is subtle, are explained **by this same account**. The phrase
*etayā eva* carries forward the distinction between a seeded process
and its clarification at I.42–43. It does not make *vicāra* a second
round of *vitarka* performed on smaller objects. The subtle field
changes the work of knowing. [YS I.44](../../01-prajna/YS-I.44.md)

### The opposition

*Vitarka* works in **Form Base**, where word, impure Objective Artha,
and knowledge are present together. Its question is whether the
presented determination can be held and distinguished without the
inherited name ruling its appearance. *Nirvitarkā* finds a Principle
in that Form work as *artha* shines through purified memory.
[I.42–43 essay](savitarka-nirvitarka-and-the-three-bases.md)

*Vicāra* works in **Essence Base**. Reflection no longer takes the
formed result as its primary field. It asks how that result is
mediated: what relation, condition, or mark lets it appear as this
determination? Its subtle *viṣaya* is not merely less visible Form.
It is the reflective determination through which Form becomes
intelligible. Thus Vitarka and Vicāra are opposed in their direction
of inquiry:

| Vitarka: Form Base | Vicāra: Essence Base |
|---|---|
| Holds the presented determination. | Returns into the relation that determines its presentation. |
| Tests word, impure *artha*, and knowledge in their mixture. | Tests the marks and conditions through which those moments are related. |
| In *nirvitarkā*, finds the Principle of the Form determination. | In *nirvicārā*, finds the Principle of Reflection. |

The opposition is productive. Reflection needs a determination to
reflect; Form becomes scientifically knowable when its mediation is
shown. Neither side is a detachable half of the Dharma.

### Why the subtle object matters

The word *sūkṣma* signals a change in what counts as an object of
samāpatti. If we look only for a physically minute thing, we remain
within the Form question. Vicāra instead makes a relation itself
available for examination. A mark may indicate a formed result while
carrying a rule for recognizing how that result is determined. The
mark therefore has two demands: it must answer to what it marks, and
its own authority must be examined. This is the opening of our
**Central Mark System**.

*Savicārā* holds those subtle determinations while Reflection still
works through its marks. *Nirvicārā* is the Principle found as
Reflection comprehends what those marks disclose and what they cannot
produce on their own. The prefix *nir-* does not require the
annihilation of reflection. It marks its sublation as an independent
claim to be the source of what it reflects.

I.45 will set the limit of subtle objectivity at *aliṅga*. We should
not pretend I.44 already specifies that limit. But its shift to
subtle *viṣaya* is enough to establish the distinct labor of Vicāra:
the science of reflective marks, rather than a finer inventory of
formed things.

### What the Kośa contributes

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 1.32](../../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.32_bhasya.md)
distinguishes *vitarka* and *vicāra* as associated factors and
identifies levels on which cognition may occur with both, with
*vicāra* alone, or with neither. That separation matters here: the
two do not form one undifferentiated act of “thinking.” The Kośa
classifies their occurrence; Yoga I.44 lets us ask what the
reflective movement determines. The project-level Form : Essence
opposition is our architectural use of the comparison, developed
further in the [Fichte report](../../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-44-fichte-report.md).

The first essay's [jewel](samapatti-and-the-three-bases-at-i-41.md)
can now be read more precisely. Its clarity does not flatten the
Form and Essence sides. It lets the Form determination be seen,
then lets Reflection disclose the marks through which that form
has its place. The two *bīja* remain operative in the seeded
samāpattis. Science finds a Principle in each; it does not replace
their distinct work with a general assertion of unity.
