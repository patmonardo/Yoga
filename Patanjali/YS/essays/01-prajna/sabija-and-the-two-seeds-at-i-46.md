# Sabīja: What the Samāpattis Still Carry

## A Samāpatti Essay on Yoga Sūtra I.46

*Tā eva*: **these very** samāpattis are *sabīja samādhi*. I.46 gathers the preceding modes instead of introducing another object of concentration. *Savitarkā* and *nirvitarkā* worked through the presented determination; *savicārā* and *nirvicārā* worked through subtle reflective determination. Reaching *aliṅga* did not remove the support on which this work proceeds. [YS I.46](../../01-prajna/YS-I.46.md)

In our account, **Form and Essence are the two seeds** of the *saṃvṛti* field. Form gives the presented determination; Essence gives its reflective mediation. They are not two little objects stored in consciousness. A seed is a determinate basis from which the present act of knowing can develop. *Nirvitarkā* finds the Principle of Form work; *nirvicārā* finds the Principle of Reflection. Each discovery remains situated in the seeded inquiry through which it was made. [I.42–43 essay](savitarka-nirvitarka-and-the-three-bases.md) · [I.44 essay](vicara-reflection-and-the-mark-system-at-i-44.md)

The [Kośa's samāpatti analysis](../../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md) asks after one object-support and the factors that accompany an attainment. It helps make intelligible why an attained act has conditions and a determinate field. Yoga's *sabīja* here gives the specific status of its own preceding samāpattis; the two-seed Form–Essence account is our reconstruction of their philosophical work.

This status guards the value of the earlier modes. The intermixture of word, impure Objective Artha, and knowledge is workable material for discriminating a Dharma. Reflection then investigates the conditions of that presentation. A Principle can be found through each without pretending that the entire Science is already finished. The term *sabīja* identifies what still has to be reconstructed: how these partial Principles belong to one Concept, and how that Concept can be known without an independently ruling seed.

I.47 begins the transition by specifying *vaiśāradya* in *nirvicārā*. Seeded practice becomes proficient enough for the inner field to clear. I.51 will return to *bīja* after the Prajñā born of that clarity has itself formed a *saṃskāra*.
