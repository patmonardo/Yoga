# YS I.51 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> तस्यापि निरोधे सर्वनिरोधान्निर्बीजः समाधिः ॥ १.५१॥

> *tasyāpi nirodhe sarva-nirodhān nirbījaḥ samādhiḥ* — Upon the nirodha even of that, because of the nirodha of all, there is seedless samādhi.

## 2. Commentary

*Tasya api* reaches back to I.50's prajñā-born *saṃskāra*.
Even the formation that inhibits other formations has a limit.
The sūtra says *nirbījaḥ samādhiḥ* when nirodha applies to it
as well. The contrast with I.46 is exact: *tā eva sabījaḥ* gathered
the preceding samāpattis as seeded; I.51 closes their dependence
on seed.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
accounts for a wholesome attainment through one object-support
and accompanying factors. Yoga's final sūtra poses the limit of
that supportive configuration in its own terms. We should not
make the Kośa's particular cessation attainments the definition
of this Yoga ending.

## 3. Project Determination

Here *nirodha* is sublation of the seed's claim to final authority.
The prajñā born in the seeded work has done its work; its
*saṃskāra* need not govern the truth it helped disclose. This
is the third Principle determination, *nirbīja*, after those
found in *nirvitarkā* and *nirvicārā*. Their content is not
discarded: the Science comprehends the two discoveries from
the central Principle.

## 4. Review Status

First pass through the close of Prajñāpāda. Review I.40–51 as
one movement before the later Yogavidyā synthesis.
