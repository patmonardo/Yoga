# YS I.50 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> तज्जः संस्कारोऽन्यसंस्कारप्रतिबन्धी ॥ १.५०॥

> *taj-jaḥ saṃskāro 'nya-saṃskāra-pratibandhī* — The saṃskāra born from that prajñā inhibits other saṃskāras.

## 2. Commentary

I.48–49 determined a prajñā bearing truth with a distinctive
*artha*. I.50 says its occurrence forms a *saṃskāra*. That
formation is *pratibandhī*: it inhibits other saṃskāras. Truth
thus has an effect on the conditions under which consciousness
will know again.

The [Kośa VAK 1.02 Bhāṣya](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02_bhasya.md)
distinguishes cultivated prajñā from stainless prajñā and treats
cultivation as directed toward attainment. It helps frame the
question of how knowing changes a path. Yoga I.50 names the
specific change: a prajñā-born formation checks the others.

## 3. Project Determination

The Saṃskāra Base is not a store of completed knowledge. The
Science finds Principles within its formations and leaves a new
capacity for knowing. I.50 gives that capacity a causal role, while
I.51 will sublate even this privileged formation. The Principle
found does not depend eternally on its seed.
