# YS I.26 — Teacher Beyond the Limit of Time

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> स पूर्वेषामपि गुरुः कालेनानवच्छेदात्॥ १.२६॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> sa pūrveṣām api guruḥ kālena anavacchedāt // 1.26 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *saḥ* | masculine nominative singular | he, the Īśvara of I.24–25 |
| *pūrveṣām api* | genitive plural with particle | even of those before, the earlier teachers |
| *guruḥ* | nominative singular | teacher |
| *kālena* | instrumental singular | by time |
| *anavacchedāt* | ablative singular | because of non-limitation |

## 4. Grammar

The final ablative gives the reason for the predication: he is teacher even
of the earlier ones because he is not delimited by time. The sūtra does not
name those earlier teachers or describe a historical chain of transmission.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> He is teacher even of the earlier teachers, because he is not bounded by
> time.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.25 determined unsurpassed knowledge in Īśvara; I.26 determines its teaching
relation. *Guruḥ* turns the claim of knowing toward transmission. The reason
given is *kālena anavacchedāt*, not a date of first instruction.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Gītā 4.1–2](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C04/gita-4.01.md) gives a concrete
teaching lineage, while Śaṅkara says that the succession can be interrupted
over time. The [Kośa at VAK 6.54](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.54_bhasya.md)
calls the Buddha *brahma* through unsurpassed *brāhmaṇya* and calls the
Brahma-wheel his because he set it in motion. In our reading the Buddha may
be named Īśvara as teacher; this is a project identification, not a name
supplied by I.26 or the Kośa passage. Its relation to the sūtra's explicit
non-limitation by time still requires interpretation.
