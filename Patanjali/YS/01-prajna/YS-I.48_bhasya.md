# YS I.48 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> ऋतम्भरा तत्र प्रज्ञा ॥ १.४८॥

> *ṛtambharā tatra prajñā* — There, prajñā bears truth.

## 2. Commentary

The *tatra* binds I.48 to I.47. In the clarity gained through
proficiency in *nirvicārā*, there is *ṛtambharā prajñā*. The phrase
says more than that a correct thought occurs: prajñā bears truth
within the achieved clarity. I.49 must still determine its distinct
*viṣaya* and *artha*.

The [Kośa's VAK 1.02](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02_bhasya.md)
names stainless prajñā with its attendants as ultimate Abhidharma.
That gives us a comparison for prajñā as an attained complex of
knowing, while Yoga I.48 gives its own concise predicate,
*ṛtambharā*.

## 3. Project Determination

Here the Science of Principles becomes explicit. The Principles
found through the Form and Essence seeds remain true; prajñā must
comprehend their relation as one determinate Concept. The next
sūtra will test this by distinguishing its *artha* from testimony
and inference.
