# YS I.27 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> तस्य वाचकः प्रणवः ॥ १.२७॥

> *tasya vācakaḥ praṇavaḥ* — Praṇava is his designation.

## 2. Commentary

I.26 named Īśvara *guruḥ*; I.27 names *praṇava* as his *vācaka*. The
teacher is not confined to an earlier empirical appearance. A designation
gives the present practitioner a way to address that teaching relation. The
sūtra itself says neither how OṂ is voiced nor what response follows. I.28
will join repetition to contemplation of its meaning.

In [Gītā 8.13](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C08/gita-8.13.md), the verse joins
uttering OṂ to remembering the Lord. Śaṅkara calls OṂ the Lord's designation
and the Lord its meaning. His comment thus gives a precise comparison for
the relation between *vācaka* and what is expressed. Its setting is departure
at death; that setting does not determine the use of *praṇava* in I.27.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 6.54](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.54_bhasya.md)
says the Buddha set the Brahma-wheel in motion and discusses the Dharma
exposition moving effectively into another continuum. This helps articulate
your distinction: the Buddha's earlier embodied teaching has extensions in
new practitioners. To become such extensions now is a matter of taking up
and realizing the teaching. The Kośa passage supplies this account of
transmission; it supplies no claim that its expression is OṂ.

## 3. Project Determination

On our Buddha as Īśvara reading, *praṇava* raises a live question about the
relation of a designation to realized teaching across generations. The
sūtra's fixed expression must be studied with I.28's practice of meaning,
without treating a sound alone as the whole of that inheritance.
