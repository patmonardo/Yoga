# YS I.47 — Clarity in Nirvicārā

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> निर्विचारवैशारद्येऽध्यात्मप्रसादः ॥ १.४७॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> nirvicāra-vaiśāradye 'dhyātma-prasādaḥ // 1.47 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *nirvicāra-vaiśāradye* | locative singular | upon proficiency or maturity in nirvicārā |
| *adhyātma-prasādaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | inner clarity or lucidity |

## 4. Grammar

The locative states the condition under which *adhyātma-prasādaḥ*
occurs. The sūtra gives no new object-list or separate technique.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Upon proficiency in *nirvicārā*, there is inner clarity.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.46 gathered the preceding attainments as seeded. I.47 does not
cancel that classification. It specifies what mature *nirvicārā*
does within it: Reflection becomes lucid enough for the inner field
to be clear. I.48 names the prajñā that arises there.

## 7. Interpretive Note

Our Essence Base reading treats *vaiśāradya* as reliable proficiency
in finding the Principle through reflective determinations. The
[Kośa VAK 1.02 Bhāṣya](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02_bhasya.md)
distinguishes cultivated prajñā from stainless prajñā; it offers a
basic comparison for the difference between a practiced clarity and
the full account of truth still to be developed. The
[Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-47-fichte-report.md)
expands the Science of Knowing reading.
