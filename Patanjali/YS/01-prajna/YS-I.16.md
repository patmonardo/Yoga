# YS I.16 — Higher Vairāgya

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तत्परं पुरुषख्यातेर्गुणवैतृष्ण्यम्॥ १.१६॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tat paraṃ puruṣakhyāter guṇavaitṛṣṇyam // 1.16 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *tat* | demonstrative, neuter nominative singular | that dispassion |
| *param* | neuter nominative singular | higher, supreme |
| *puruṣa-khyāteḥ* | ablative singular | from discernment of *puruṣa* |
| *guṇa-vaitṛṣṇyam* | neuter nominative singular | absence of thirst for the *guṇas* |

## 4. Grammar

*Tat* refers back to *vairāgyam* in I.15; *param* marks its higher grade. The ablative *puruṣakhyāteḥ* supplies the ground or cause of *guṇavaitṛṣṇyam*. The latter is the predicate description of that higher dispassion. The genitive and ablative singular endings of *khyāti* coincide in form; the causal relation here favors the ablative reading.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> That higher dispassion is freedom from thirst for the *guṇas* through discernment of *puruṣa*.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.15 described mastery concerning encountered and taught objects. I.16 shifts the scope to the *guṇas* themselves and gives *puruṣakhyāti* as the ground of freedom from thirst. The received Bhāṣya says that practice of seeing *puruṣa* nourishes Buddhi through purity and discrimination, making it dispassionate toward the *guṇas* in their manifest and unmanifest forms. It calls the higher grade “the very culmination of knowledge” (*jñānasyaiva parā kāṣṭhā vairāgyam*). This is a wisdom claim made explicitly by the commentator, not a literal occurrence of *jñāna* in the sūtra.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa companion essay](../essays/higher-vairagya-according-to-kosa.md) compares this higher grade with Kośa accounts of subtler attachment, supramundane dispassion, discriminative cessation, and *virāgadhātu*. The Kośa has no *puruṣakhyāti* in its account, so the comparison identifies a shared problem of residual attachment without equating the paths or their ultimate objects.
