# YS I.50 — The Prajñā-Born Saṃskāra

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तज्जः संस्कारोऽन्यसंस्कारप्रतिबन्धी ॥ १.५०॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> taj-jaḥ saṃskāro 'nya-saṃskāra-pratibandhī // 1.50 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *taj-jaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | born from that prajñā |
| *saṃskāraḥ* | masculine nominative singular | formation or acquired disposition |
| *anya-saṃskāra-pratibandhī* | masculine nominative singular | inhibiting other formations |

## 4. Grammar

*Saṃskāraḥ* is the subject, qualified by *taj-jaḥ* and
*anya-saṃskāra-pratibandhī*. The demonstrative reaches back to
the prajñā just determined in I.48–49.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> The saṃskāra born from that prajñā inhibits other saṃskāras.

## 6. Logical Determination

The attained knowing has a consequence in the ongoing life of
consciousness: it forms a *saṃskāra* that checks contrary formations.
The sūtra does not leave prajñā as an isolated insight. I.51 then
asks what happens even to this truth-born formation.

## 7. Interpretive Note

This is the place to test our thesis of extracting Principles from
the Saṃskāra Base: prajñā changes the formation of future cognition.
The [Kośa VAK 1.02 Bhāṣya](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02_bhasya.md)
describes cultivated prajñā directed toward stainless prajñā, a
comparison for the role of practice, not a replacement for Yoga's
specific inhibitory *saṃskāra*. See the
[Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-50-fichte-report.md).
