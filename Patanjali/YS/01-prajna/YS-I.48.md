# YS I.48 — Truth-Bearing Prajñā

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> ऋतम्भरा तत्र प्रज्ञा ॥ १.४८॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> ṛtambharā tatra prajñā // 1.48 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *ṛtambharā* | feminine nominative singular | bearing truth or right order |
| *tatra* | locative adverb | there, in the clarity just described |
| *prajñā* | feminine nominative singular | discriminating wisdom; our Science of Principles |

## 4. Grammar

*Ṛtambharā* qualifies *prajñā*. *Tatra* refers back to I.47's
*adhyātma-prasāda*. The sūtra predicates a kind of prajñā there.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> There, prajñā bears truth.

## 6. Logical Determination

The clarity resulting from mature *nirvicārā* now receives its
positive determination: *ṛtambharā prajñā*. The text does not
yet say how this prajñā differs from other warrants; I.49 does.

## 7. Interpretive Note

For our Science of Principles, truth-bearing prajñā must comprehend
the real discoveries made through Form and Essence rather than
discard them. The [Kośa VAK 1.02 Bhāṣya](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02_bhasya.md)
calls ultimate Abhidharma stainless prajñā with attendants; that
gives a substantial comparison, not an identity asserted by this
Yoga sūtra. See the [Fichte report](../../../Docs/Prajnapada/ys-i-48-fichte-report.md)
for the wider project development.
