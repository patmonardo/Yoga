# YS I.36 — Sorrowless Luminosity

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> विशोका वा ज्योतिष्मती ॥ १.३६॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> viśokā vā jyotiṣmatī // 1.36 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *viśokā* | feminine nominative singular | free from grief or sorrow |
| *vā* | alternative particle | or |
| *jyotiṣmatī* | feminine nominative singular | luminous, possessing light |

## 4. Grammar

The two feminine adjectives invite an understood feminine subject, most
naturally the *pravṛttiḥ* named in I.35. *Vā* marks another alternative
within the series. The sūtra does not explicitly identify the light with
a bodily light, an external object, or the final *samāpatti*.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Or, [an activity] free from sorrow and luminous.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.35 spoke of an object-bearing activity that secures mental steadiness;
I.36 qualifies another possible activity as sorrowless and luminous. Its
brevity leaves the exact object and operation open. It belongs to the
preparation for the explicit *samāpatti* account at I.41.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa at VAK 5.59](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/05-anusaya/VAK_5.59_bhasya.md)
names *ālokasaṃjñā*, a conception of light, as a countercondition to
mental sinking. Śaṅkara's [Gītā 13.17
comment](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C13/gita-13.17.md) treats the “light of
lights” as illumination by consciousness. These sources open different
ways of thinking about luminosity; neither defines Yoga's *jyotiṣmatī*.
