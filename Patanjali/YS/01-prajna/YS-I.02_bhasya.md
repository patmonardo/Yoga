# YS I.2 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः ॥ १.२॥

> *yogaś cittavṛttinirodhaḥ* — Yoga is the Science of Consciousness.

## 2. Commentary

Yoga is the Science of Consciousness. *Citta* is consciousness; its *vṛttis* are determinate movements. *Nirodha* is their sublation. Yoga must know these movements in order to determine how they are sublated.

The definition sets a task for instruction: know the movements of consciousness and determine their sublation. I.5–11 distinguish the movements; I.12 names practice and dispassion as means.

## 3. Project Determination

Prajñā-pāda develops the metaphysics of consciousness. The Kośa's discrimination of dharmas demands exact distinctions among consciousness, its movements, their contradictions, and their sublation. The Kośa's two determinations of *nirodha* will sharpen this account as the sūtras proceed.

The fuller argument is in [Why Yoga Is the Science of Consciousness](../../Essays/Yoga-Science-of-Consciousness.md).
