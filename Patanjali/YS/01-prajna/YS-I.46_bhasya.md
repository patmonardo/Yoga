# YS I.46 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> ता एव सबीजः समाधिः ॥ १.४६॥

> *tā eva sabījaḥ samādhiḥ* — These very samāpattis are samādhi with seed.

## 2. Commentary

*Tā eva* points back over the samāpatti sequence. The word *eva*
prevents us from treating I.46 as another subtle object or another
exercise. It names the status of what has been achieved: even at
I.45's *aliṅga* limit, these attainments have seed.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
shows how an attainment is one-pointed on an object-support with
accompanying factors. That analysis makes a seed intelligible as a
determinate support and operative formation. Yoga's *tā eva* gives
the exact scope here: the preceding samāpattis, not all possible
states, receive the *sabīja* designation.

## 3. Project Determination

Form and Essence are two *bīja* of this *saṃvṛti* work. *Nirvitarkā*
and *nirvicārā* find Principles through their respective seeded
processes; they are not yet *nirbīja*. The later movement must show
how the complete relation of the Principles is known and how the
seeded formation is sublated.
