# YS I.40 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> परमाणु परममहत्त्वान्तोऽस्य वशीकारः ॥ १.४०॥

> *paramāṇu parama-mahattvānto 'sya vaśīkāraḥ* — Mastery extends from the smallest atom to the greatest magnitude.

## 2. Commentary

The alternatives marked by *vā* have ended. I.40 now states
*vaśīkāra*: a range of mastery extending from *paramāṇu* to
*parama-mahattva*. The sūtra names both limits. It does not require us
to treat mastery as physical control over every tiny and vast thing; in
this sequence it concerns what disciplined consciousness can hold.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 8.01](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/08-samapatti/VAK_8.01_bhasya.md)
provides a useful account of the meditative side. *Samāpatti* is
wholesome one-pointedness; *ekāgratā* is explained through one
object-support, and the attainment with its accompaniments involves the
five aggregates. Yoga I.40 measures mastery by its range, while the Kośa
analyzes the constitution of an attainment. The comparison helps us ask
how complete attention can remain determinate across changing scale.

I.41 takes the next step: consciousness, like a clear jewel, rests upon
the grasper, grasping, or grasped. Thus I.40 establishes availability of
the field before I.41 describes *samāpatti* within it. The
[companion essay](../Yogavidya/Prajnapada/ys-i-40-range-and-samapatti.md)
works through this threshold with the Kośa and the existing Fichte report.

## 3. Project Determination

In Organon Yoga, *vaśīkāra* is the capacity to sustain exact attention
from a minimal determination to an articulated whole. It prepares the
one rational process in which Principle Base, Essence Base, and Form Base
can be grasped together at I.41. This is our reconstruction of the
transition, not a lexical claim about *paramāṇu*.
