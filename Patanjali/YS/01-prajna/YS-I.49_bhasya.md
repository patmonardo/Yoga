# YS I.49 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> श्रुतानुमानप्रज्ञाभ्यामन्यविषया विशेषार्थत्वात्॥ १.४९॥

> *śrutānumāna-prajñābhyām anya-viṣayā viśeṣārthatvāt* — Its field differs from hearing- and inference-born prajñā because its artha is specifically determinate.

## 2. Commentary

The subject carried forward is I.48's *ṛtambharā prajñā*. Its
*viṣaya* differs from those of the prajñās grounded in hearing and
inference. The difference rests on *viśeṣārthatva*: a distinctive
*artha*. This supplies a claim about what is known, not merely the
intensity with which a familiar thought is felt.

The [Kośa at VAK 1.02](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02_bhasya.md)
distinguishes prajñā cultivated toward ultimate Abhidharma from
stainless prajñā with its retinue. That comparison helps pose the
path question. Yoga's own I.49 tests its truth-bearing prajñā by
the distinctness of its field and *artha*.

## 3. Project Determination

The Science of Principles cannot be a renamed inference. Its
determinate Concept must disclose the Principle from which the
Form and Essence discoveries belong together. That is the issue
of this *viśeṣa-artha*. We leave the full Objective : Subjective
Artha reconstruction for the later Yogavidyā pass.
