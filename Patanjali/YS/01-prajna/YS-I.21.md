# YS I.21 — Intensity and Nearness

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तीव्रसंवेगानामासन्नः ॥ १.२१॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tīvra-saṃvegānām āsannaḥ // 1.21 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *tīvra-* | compound member | intense, sharp |
| *saṃvegānām* | genitive plural | of those with urgency or ardent impetus |
| *āsannaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | near, close at hand |

## 4. Grammar

The genitive plural identifies the practitioners for whom the understood attainment is near. *Āsannaḥ* is masculine because the Bhāṣya supplies *samādhi-lābhaḥ*, attainment of samādhi, and also its fruit. The sūtra itself leaves that subject implicit.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> For those whose impetus is intense, [attainment] is near.

## 6. Logical Determination

The Bhāṣya places the statement within a graded account of practitioners' means and urgency, then says both acquisition of samādhi and its fruit are near for those with intense *saṃvega*. The claim is relational: intensity alters proximity to attainment. I.22 will further distinguish degrees within that relation.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa essay](../essays/01-prajna/samvega-and-nearness-according-to-kosa.md) finds the same expression *tīvra-saṃvega* in a path context. The terms coincide closely, though the texts calculate nearness through different path structures.
