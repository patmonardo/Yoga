# YS I.47 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> निर्विचारवैशारद्येऽध्यात्मप्रसादः ॥ १.४७॥

> *nirvicāra-vaiśāradye 'dhyātma-prasādaḥ* — Upon proficiency in nirvicārā, there is inner clarity.

## 2. Commentary

I.47 turns from the scope of seeded samādhi to a result of practiced
*nirvicārā*. *Vaiśāradya* is skill or maturity, not a single accidental
glimpse. *Prasāda* names the resulting clarity of the inner field.
The sūtra leaves that clarity open for I.48 to determine as *prajñā*.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya at VAK 1.02](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/01-dhatu/VAK_1.02_bhasya.md)
places cultivated prajñā on a path toward stainless prajñā. That
helps us ask how repeated reflective practice can make insight
reliable. Yoga I.47 supplies its own condition: proficiency in
*nirvicārā*.

## 3. Project Determination

Essence Base Reflection becomes clear in its own operation. Its
Principle is no longer a fleeting discovery hidden by the marks
through which it was found. This clarity prepares the truth-bearing
Prajñā of I.48 without making I.47 the chapter's final attainment.
