# YS I.3 — The Seer in Its Own Form

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> तदा द्रष्टुः स्वरूपेऽवस्थानम्॥ १.३॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> tadā draṣṭuḥ svarūpe 'vasthānam // 1.3 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *tadā* | indeclinable | then; refers to the condition stated in I.2 |
| *draṣṭuḥ* | genitive singular of *draṣṭṛ* | of the seer |
| *svarūpe* | locative singular | in its own form |
| *avasthānam* | neuter verbal noun | abiding, standing in place |

## 4. Grammar

The sūtra is a nominal statement. *Draṣṭuḥ* supplies whose abiding is at issue; *svarūpe* gives the locus of that abiding. The elided initial *a-* of *avasthānam* follows final *-e* in *svarūpe*: *svarūpe 'vasthānam*.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Then there is the seer's abiding in its own form.

## 6. Logical Determination

*Tadā* makes I.3 depend on I.2. With the sublation of the movements of consciousness, the seer abides in its own form. The result is expressed as abiding, not as the production of a new seer.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The seer and consciousness must remain distinct in this study. I.3 names the seer's own form; it does not identify that form with a particular *vṛtti* or state what becomes of every operation of consciousness. I.4 will supply the contrasting condition.
