# YS I.19 — Attainment Conditioned by Becoming

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> भवप्रत्ययो विदेहप्रकृतिलयानाम्॥ १.१९॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> bhava-pratyayo videha-prakṛti-layānām // 1.19 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *bhava-pratyayaḥ* | masculine nominative singular | having becoming or a mode of existence as its condition |
| *videha-* | compound member | the bodiless |
| *prakṛti-layānām* | genitive plural | those absorbed in Prakṛti |

## 4. Grammar

*Bhava-pratyayaḥ* qualifies the understood *anyaḥ samādhiḥ* of I.18. The genitive plural *videha-prakṛti-layānām* specifies whose attainment is in view. The sūtra contrasts this conditioned route with the means-based route of I.20; it does not itself describe the two groups' fate.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> For the bodiless and those absorbed in Prakṛti, [the other samādhi] has becoming as its condition.

## 6. Logical Determination

The received Bhāṣya says the bodiless gods live by consciousness employing only their own *saṃskāras* and experience something **like** *kaivalya* until those impressions ripen. The Prakṛti-absorbed similarly experience a seeming *kaivalya* while consciousness remains operative and later returns. The qualification *iva* and the possibility of return are decisive: an appearance of release is not final release.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The [Kośa essay](../essays/bhava-pratyaya-and-kosa-attainment.md) compares attained absorption, rebirth-result, and the danger of mistaking a refined condition for liberation. It does not identify the Yoga *videhas* with the Kośa's non-percipient beings.
