# YS I.49 — Prajñā and Its Distinct Artha

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> श्रुतानुमानप्रज्ञाभ्यामन्यविषया विशेषार्थत्वात्॥ १.४९॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> śrutānumāna-prajñābhyām anya-viṣayā viśeṣārthatvāt // 1.49 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Force |
|---|---|---|
| *śruta-anumāna-prajñābhyām* | ablative dual | from wisdom through hearing and through inference |
| *anya-viṣayā* | feminine nominative singular | having a different field or object |
| *viśeṣa-arthatvāt* | ablative singular of an abstract compound | because its *artha* is specific or distinct |

## 4. Grammar

The understood subject is the *prajñā* of I.48. The ablative dual
marks the two knowings from which it differs. The final ablative
gives the reason for its distinct *viṣaya*.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Its field differs from the prajñās of hearing and inference,
> because its *artha* is specifically determinate.

## 6. Logical Determination

I.48 named truth-bearing prajñā; I.49 gives a criterion of its
distinctness. Received teaching and inference have genuine uses,
but the *viṣaya* of this prajñā is different because its *artha*
is distinctively specified. The sūtra does not erase testimony or
inference; it asks what this attained knowing can disclose that
they cannot establish by themselves.
