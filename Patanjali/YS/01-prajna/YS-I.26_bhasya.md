# YS I.26 — Our Bhāṣya

## 1. Sūtra Anchor

> स पूर्वेषामपि गुरुः कालेनानवच्छेदात्॥ १.२६॥

> *sa pūrveṣām api guruḥ kālena anavacchedāt* — He is teacher even of the earlier teachers, because he is not bounded by time.

## 2. Commentary

The *saḥ* returns to Īśvara. After the unsurpassed seed of omniscience in
I.25, I.26 says what that knowing means for others: he is *guruḥ*, even for
those before us. The sūtra grounds this in non-limitation by time. It does
not yet say how instruction is given; I.27–28 turn to *praṇava* and its
practice.

In [Gītā 4.1–2](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C04/gita-4.01.md), the Lord says he
taught yoga to Vivasvat, who taught Manu, who taught Ikṣvāku. Śaṅkara treats
this as a lineage of knowledge and says in his comment on
[4.2](../../../Sankara/BG/BG-C04/gita-4.02.md) that the succession was broken
over a long time. This makes the distinction useful: a teaching can be
available at its source even when its historical transmission fails. The
Gītā gives a lineage; I.26 gives non-limitation by time as the reason for
Īśvara's standing as teacher.

The [Kośa Bhāṣya on VAK 6.54](../../../Vasubandhu/VAK/06-marga/VAK_6.54_bhasya.md)
says explicitly that the Blessed One is called *brahma* through unsurpassed
*brāhmaṇya*, and that the Brahma-wheel is his because he set it in motion.
It therefore presents the Buddha as a teacher whose realization becomes a
public path. In our Yoga–Kośa reading, we can invoke the Buddha as Īśvara in
this teaching function. That identification is our determination; the Kośa
does not use the word *īśvara* in this passage, and the verse does not by
itself establish the sūtra's stronger claim about time.

## 3. Project Determination

Īśvara names a genus of realized teaching, not a single creator cause. The
Buddha can be invoked here as Īśvara: one whose knowing is authoritative and
whose wheel brings a path into shared practice. Whether and how his teaching
is *kālena anavacchinna* must be developed from the relation between the
living principle and its historical transmission.
