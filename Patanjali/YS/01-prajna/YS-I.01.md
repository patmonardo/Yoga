# YS I.1 — Prajñā-pāda

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> अथ योगानुशासनम्॥ १.१॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> atha yogānuśāsanam // 1.1 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Initial force |
|---|---|---|
| *atha* | indeclinable | now |
| *yoga* | compound member | yoga |
| *anuśāsanam* | neuter singular noun | instruction, teaching |

## 4. Grammar

This is a nominal sūtra without an expressed verb. *Yogānuśāsanam* names the instruction; *atha* opens it.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Now, the instruction of yoga.

## 6. Logical Determination

Yoga is given as a subject of instruction. I.2 defines it.

## 7. Interpretive Note

We call Chapter I **Prajñā-pāda**. Yoga and Kośa together require a communicable Science of Principles.
