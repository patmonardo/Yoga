# YS I.1 — The Instruction Begins

## 1. Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

> अथ योगानुशासनम्॥ १.१॥

## 2. Sanskrit (IAST)

> atha yogānuśāsanam // 1.1 //

## 3. Lexical Analysis

| Pada | Form | Initial force |
|---|---|---|
| *atha* | indeclinable | now |
| *yoga* | compound member | yoga |
| *anuśāsanam* | neuter singular noun | instruction, teaching |

## 4. Grammar

This is a nominal sūtra without an expressed verb. *Yogānuśāsanam* names the instruction; *atha* opens it.

## 5. Translation

### Literal translation

> Now, the instruction of yoga.

## 6. Logical Determination

*Atha* opens a teachable inquiry; *anuśāsanam* gives Yoga the form of ordered instruction. The instruction is the beginning of a Science, not a claim that its result has already been attained. I.2 states its subject as the Science of Consciousness; I.3–4 then show the first distinction that this Science must explain.

## 7. Interpretive Note

The source calls this chapter *Samādhi-pāda*; **Prajñā-pāda** is our project name for its study of knowing. Kośa VAK 1.2 distinguishes attained *prajñā* from the teaching that leads to it. The comparison helps keep instruction and achieved knowing distinct while we follow Yoga's own opening. [Kośabhāṣya, VAK 1.2](../../../Vasubandhu/Sources/kosabhasya.txt).
