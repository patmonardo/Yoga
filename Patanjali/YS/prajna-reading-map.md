# Prajñāpāda — Chapter I Reading Map

This is a table of contents and term index for the **first-pass** Yoga
Kośa commentary on all 51 sūtras of Chapter I. Each numbered entry
has a [sūtra study](01-prajna/YS-I.01.md) and our paired
[Bhāṣya](01-prajna/YS-I.01_bhasya.md). The map helps readers move through the
chapter; it does not settle the later essay and Yogavidyā readings.

## FEP in the samāpatti sequence

This is a **reading map**, not a word-for-word translation of the
sūtras. A single Dharma can be studied as Base (*skandha*), Essence
(*āyatana*), and Principle (*dhātu*) in our Yoga Kośa architecture.
The [I.41 essay](essays/01-prajna/samapatti-and-the-three-bases-at-i-41.md)
introduces that relation through the jewel.

| Work | Sūtras | Seed and discovery |
|---|---|---|
| **Form Base**: determinations of Being | [I.42–43](01-prajna/YS-I.42.md) | *Savitarkā* holds word, impure Objective Artha, and knowledge in mixture; *nirvitarkā* finds a Principle through clarified Form. |
| **Essence Base**: Reflection and its marks | [I.44–45](01-prajna/YS-I.44.md) | *Savicārā* investigates subtle relations; *nirvicārā* finds the Principle of Reflection; *aliṅga* tests its limit. |
| **Principle Base**: prajñā and its formation | [I.47–51](01-prajna/YS-I.47.md) | Truth-bearing Prajñā discloses a distinct *artha*, forms a *saṃskāra*, and culminates in *nirbīja* when that formation is sublated. |

[I.40](01-prajna/YS-I.40.md) is the threshold, [I.41](01-prajna/YS-I.41.md) the jewel's
architecture, and [I.46](01-prajna/YS-I.46.md) the boundary naming the earlier
samāpattis *sabīja*. The two seeded discoveries are real; the central
Principle must comprehend them as one Determinate Concept. The
[Vicāra essay](essays/01-prajna/vicara-reflection-and-the-mark-system-at-i-44.md)
develops the Form : Essence opposition. A fuller Yogavidyā pass can
test the three-Principle account without replacing this reading map.
