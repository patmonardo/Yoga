# Yoga Sūtra Commentary — Kośa-Focused Edition

This is the working home of a **complete, Kośa-focused commentary on Patañjali's Yoga Sūtra**. The intended finished work is a coherent commentary that readers can follow verse by verse. A public or sale edition is a possible later outcome, not the status of the present drafts.

The present pass takes the Yoga sūtra and its received Bhāṣya as the text to be explained. It uses Vasubandhu's Kośa and Kośabhāṣya to sharpen distinctions about cognition, mental factors, object-support, conditions, path, and cessation. A Kośa comparison must be tied to a passage and must say where Yoga and Kośa differ. The Kośa's categories do not silently become Patañjali's words.

## Two editorial layers

| Layer | Working name | Material and audience |
|---|---|---|
| First | **Yoga Sūtra Commentary: Kośa Review** | Sanskrit, grammar, received Yoga Bhāṣya, Kośa comparison, and a readable verse-by-verse commentary. This is the first complete-commentary project and the possible public edition. |
| Second | **Yogavidya: Science of Absolute Knowing** | The author's Form : Essence : Principle : System architecture, Fichte–Hegel work, and the larger Yoga Vidyā synthesis. This is a distinct work for friends while it develops. |

The fourfold Form : Essence : Principle : System is **the author's system**, not a doctrine of the Kośa or the Yoga Sūtra. The Dhātu : Indriya relation has received the sustained upgrade in that system; Loka : Karma and the remaining Kośa material have not yet undergone the same work. Do not describe those future upgrades as already achieved in either edition.

## Working form of the first layer

1. **Sūtra study:** exact Sanskrit, morphology, grammar, a conventional translation, and the immediate determination of the verse.
2. **Paired commentary:** a compact explanation in sequence, including the received Yoga Bhāṣya and a source-grounded Kośa comparison where it helps.
3. **Essays:** fuller Kośa-focused arguments, objections, and source boundaries that can later be edited into the continuous commentary.

The study and paired commentary files proceed one sūtra at a time. The [tests](tests/README.md) check file pairing, source anchors, and heading order; they cannot certify an attribution or a philosophical conclusion.

## Editorial status

The I.1–13 studies are working drafts. Some earlier paired commentaries and essays still contain second-layer claims under headings such as “Project Determination.” Those passages need editorial separation before this first layer is a complete Kośa-focused manuscript. The current heading template records the draft state; it is not the final publication structure. In particular, the I.12–13 comparisons keep Kośa's technical *nirodha* and *sthiti* distinct from Yoga's own usage.

Existing Fichte and Hegel reports remain source material for [Yogavidya](Yogavidya/README.md). They do not determine the first-layer translation or the Kośa comparison. The first layer may identify a later connection without developing it there.
