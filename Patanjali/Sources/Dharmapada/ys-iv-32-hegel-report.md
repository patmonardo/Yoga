# The Guṇas Fulfill Their Transformative Artha

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.32 with Hegel and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`tataḥ kṛtārthānāṃ pariṇāma-krama-samāptir guṇānām`

### Simple translation

> Then, because the Guṇas have accomplished their Artha, the sequence of
> their transformations comes to completion.

The Sūtra does not condemn the Guṇas or declare their work unreal:

- `kṛtārthānām`: their Artha has been accomplished;
- `pariṇāma`: determinate transformation;
- `krama`: the ordered genetic sequence;
- `samāptiḥ`: completion or consummation;
- `guṇānām`: of the Guṇas.

```text
the Guṇas articulate determinate actuality
                    ↓
their complete sequence discloses its Artha
                    ↓
the purpose of conditioned transformation is fulfilled
                    ↓
the sequence comes to completion
```

Hegel names this completion the return of Relative Necessity into
**Absolute Actuality**.

## The third actualization of Dharma-Megha

IV.32 completes the three immanent actualizations or Siddhis of
Dharma-Megha:

```text
Dharma-Megha — IV.29
Knowing as content-filled Real Actuality
        |
        ├── IV.30  freedom from afflicted conditioning
        |
        ├── IV.31  encyclopedic Samyag-Jñāna
        |
        └── IV.32  fulfillment of transformative Artha
```

The third is not the disappearance of Nature after two prior rewards. It is
Real Necessity comprehending the limitation and completed purpose of its own
determinate content.

## The textual boundary

The Hegelian material for IV.32 is the remainder of B.3, beginning:

> But this necessity is at the same time relative.

It continues through:

- the contingent presupposition of Real Necessity;
- the limited character of its determinate content;
- the contingency contained within its form;
- the incomplete return of Actuality and Possibility;
- the unity of Necessity and Contingency called Absolute Actuality.

It ends immediately before IV.33 and “C. Absolute Necessity.”

Thus IV.32 completes Relative Necessity but does not yet perform the movement
of Absolute Necessity.

## Why Real Necessity remains relative

IV.31 established:

> Under the complete given conditions, nothing else can follow.

This is genuine Necessity. Yet the formulation contains a qualification:

```text
under these given conditions
```

The result follows necessarily, but the determinate conditions from which it
begins are presupposed.

```text
given conditions C
        ↓ necessarily
result R

but:

why C rather than another determinate set of conditions?
```

Real Necessity grounds the result within the conditions. It has not yet
grounded the complete existence and determination of the conditions
themselves.

That is why it is relative.

## The contingent beginning

Hegel says that Relative Necessity takes its start from the contingent.

Real Actuality initially appears as a manifold of concretely existing
circumstances. This manifold becomes the Real Possibility of a determinate
fact. Once complete, those conditions necessarily produce the fact.

```text
contingent actual manifold
        ↓ functions as
Real Possibility / complete conditions
        ↓ produces
necessary determinate fact
```

The necessity of the result does not retroactively erase the contingent
immediacy from which the conditions began.

The complete movement must still comprehend why contingency itself belongs
to Necessity.

## The Guṇas as determinate real conditions

The Guṇas provide the qualitative conditions through which a determinate
actuality becomes possible:

```text
Sattva
luminosity, manifestation, intelligibility

Rajas
movement, opposition, and transformation

Tamas
stability, resistance, and determinate subsistence
```

Their combinations generate the manifold conditions of Nature and Citta.

```text
Guṇa-combination
        ↓
Real Possibility of a determinate fact
        ↓ complete conditions
necessary Actuality
```

But any determinate combination is limited. Its necessity therefore remains
relative to its qualitative constitution.

## Limitation makes necessity contingent

Hegel states that any limited actuality can be necessary in one respect and
contingent in another.

```text
within its conditions
the fact cannot be otherwise

with respect to the determination of those conditions
the fact remains contingent
```

This is not a defect that invalidates determinate science. It states the
proper validity of its knowledge.

```text
Physics may demonstrate a necessary result
under specified physical conditions.

That does not make those determinate conditions
the unconditioned Principle of every science.
```

Prajñā knows both the necessity and the limit of the determination.

## A determinate science becomes `kṛtārtha`

A determinate science accomplishes its Artha when it:

1. identifies its actual domain and conditions;
2. derives what is really possible within them;
3. demonstrates the necessary result;
4. recognizes the limitation of its own presupposed content;
5. yields its place within the systematic whole.

```text
not:

a science is complete when it declares its local laws absolute


but:

a science is complete when it demonstrates its necessity
and comprehends its own principled limit
```

This is `kṛtārtha`: the determinate science has fulfilled its purpose.

## Encyclopedia as the Principle of the sciences

The Encyclopedia is not another determinate science competing with the
others. It is the actual Principle through which their domains, limits, and
transitions become one system.

```text
determinate science A
necessary within content A
          \
determinate science B       Encyclopedic Principle
necessary within content B ─► orders their conceptual places
          /
determinate science C
necessary within content C
```

IV.32 supplies the movement required by this Principle. Each science's
conditioned sequence reaches completion, recognizes its limited Artha, and
returns into the whole without losing its determinate truth.

Encyclopedic Samyag-Jñāna therefore does not flatten the sciences into one
abstract doctrine. It comprehends why each science is necessary, limited,
and systematically connected.

## The sequence does not simply stop in time

`Pariṇāma-krama-samāpti` does not require empirical events to vanish. Bodies,
thoughts, histories, and natural processes may continue appearing.

What comes to completion is the demand that another transformation supply
the still-missing Artha of the sequence.

```text
before completion
each transformation points to another
as though its Principle were still absent

after completion
the whole sequence is comprehended
within its accomplished Artha
```

Nature ceases to be metaphysically productive of an unfinished purpose. Its
appearing activity can continue as the manifestation of an already
comprehended Principle.

## Relative Necessity contains Contingency

Hegel now makes the result explicit:

> Real Necessity contains Contingency.

This is true in two ways.

### In the content

The necessary actuality has a limited determinate content and is therefore
contingent with respect to other contents.

### In the form

Real Possibility initially appears as the mutual otherness of Actuality and
Possibility:

```text
Actuality returns from being the other of Possibility
but has not yet returned from itself into itself
```

Necessity has mastered the otherness of its moments but has not yet generated
its own contingent presupposition from itself.

That final self-return belongs to Absolute Necessity.

## A–U–M at the limit of Relative Necessity

```text
A — Appearance
the necessary determinate actuality

U — Reflection / thinking
recognition of its contingent conditions and limited content

M — Knowing
the unity of Necessity and Contingency
as Absolute Actuality
```

A is genuinely necessary within its conditions. U does not cancel that
truth; it reflects upon the conditions' contingent beginning. M holds both
determinations as one complete modal actuality.

## The five-fold synthesis completes itself

The Relative Necessity movement can now be summarized:

```text
1. Real Actuality
   content-filled manifestation

2. Real Possibility
   the complete manifold of actual conditions

3. Actuality → Possibility
   actual circumstances become conditions of another

4. Possibility → Actuality
   complete conditions become the necessary fact

5. Real Necessity
   the identity of possibility and actuality
```

IV.32 reflects the fifth term into its own limitation:

```text
Real Necessity
        ↓ discovers within itself
Contingency of determinate content
        ↓
unity of Necessity and Contingency
        ↓
Absolute Actuality
```

This is not an externally added sixth component. It is the fifth term
comprehending the whole content and limit of its synthesis.

## Why this is fulfillment rather than collapse

Relative Necessity does not fail because it discovers Contingency within
itself. Its purpose was to demonstrate how a determinate Actuality follows
necessarily from its real conditions.

It has accomplished that Artha.

The discovery of its limit is itself the final truth of the sequence:

```text
determinate necessity proved
        +
its contingent presupposition exposed
        ↓
complete truth of Relative Necessity
```

A science that knows its limit is more complete, not less true.

## Absolute Actuality

At the end of IV.32, Hegel names the implicit unity of Necessity and
Contingency **Absolute Actuality**.

```text
Necessity
the determinate result cannot be otherwise

Contingency
the determinate content began from immediate presupposition

Absolute Actuality
their unity is present as the completed result
```

This unity is present only `in itself`. IV.33 must make it explicit as
Absolute Necessity.

Thus IV.32 is the threshold, not the final completion of the Dharmapāda.

## The third Siddhi: fulfilled transformative Artha

The saintly language can now be translated exactly:

```text
the Guṇas have generated the complete manifold
of qualitative conditions
                    ↓
the necessary actuality has been demonstrated
                    ↓
the contingency of the determinate content is comprehended
                    ↓
no additional Guṇa-transformation is required
to disclose the Artha of the sequence
```

This is mastery of transformation through knowledge of its purpose.

It is not personal control over every natural event. It is encyclopedic
knowledge of why determinate sciences and processes occupy their exact place
within the whole.

## Fichte's IV.32: reconstruction of the Being of Knowing

Fichte derives two subordinate terms from living certainty:

```text
living principle-providing
self-construction or reconstruction
                 +
projected persisting intuition
the Being of Knowing
```

The terms that formerly stood as hypothetical are reconstructed from a
deeper insight into their relation.

Yet Fichte preserves one remaining difficulty:

> Why is living certainty necessarily principle-providing?

Given that presupposition, the subordinate terms follow necessarily. But the
highest premise has not yet justified itself absolutely.

This is Fichte's exact analogue of Relative Necessity.

## Hegel and Fichte compared

| Fichte | Hegel |
|---|---|
| certainty reconstructs self-construction and the Being of Knowing | Real conditions produce determinate necessary Actuality |
| the hypothetical terms receive a genetic ground | Real Possibility and Real Necessity are identical |
| the derivation presupposes certainty as principle-providing | Relative Necessity presupposes contingent determinate conditions |
| the highest presupposition remains to be justified | the limited content remains contingent |
| the relative derivation has completed its task | the Guṇas have accomplished their Artha |
| the next movement must ground the Principle absolutely | the unity of Necessity and Contingency requires Absolute Necessity |

Both systems reach the same boundary:

```text
the complete conditioned derivation is valid
                    ↓
its own highest presupposition remains
                    ↓
the sequence must return into an unconditioned Principle
```

## Their mutual correction

Fichte ensures that the completion of Guṇa-transformation remains a living
reconstruction rather than a static classification of conditions.

Hegel ensures that Fichte's remaining presupposition is understood modally:
the problem is the Contingency still contained in Relative Necessity.

```text
Fichte
reconstruct the genesis of the completed result
                    +
Hegel
determine the necessity and contingency of its content
                    ↓
fulfilled Artha without false finality
```

## The complete Dharma-Megha articulation

The four Sūtras now form one actual whole:

```text
IV.29 — Dharma-Megha
Real Actuality preserves itself through manifold content
                    ↓
IV.30 — freedom from afflicted conditioning
the real circle of conditions sublates externality
                    ↓
IV.31 — encyclopedic Samyag-Jñāna
Real Possibility is identical with Necessity
                    ↓
IV.32 — fulfilled transformative Artha
Relative Necessity comprehends its contingent limit
and yields Absolute Actuality
```

These are not a cause followed by three rewards. They are one Dharma-Megha
articulating its own Real Actuality, Real Possibility, Real Necessity, and
completed limit.

## Provisional result

IV.32 says:

> The Guṇas' ordered transformations come to completion because they have
> produced and disclosed the complete Artha available through determinate
> conditions.

Hegel shows that Real Necessity remains relative. Its result is genuinely
necessary, but its limited content began from contingently given conditions.
When this Contingency is comprehended as internal to Necessity, their unity
is Absolute Actuality.

Fichte reaches the same limit: the Being of Knowing has been reconstructed
from certainty, but certainty's principle-providing character remains the
unjustified highest presupposition.

```text
determinate sequence valid and complete
                    ↓
its contingent presupposition exposed
                    ↓
the Guṇas are kṛtārtha
                    ↓
Relative Necessity ends in Absolute Actuality
```

IV.33 can now begin Absolute Necessity: the complete sequence must no longer
presuppose its ground but show that its Law constructs itself through every
moment.
