# Freedom from the Kleśa-Karma Circuit

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.30 with Hegel and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`tataḥ kleśa-karma-nivṛttiḥ`

### Simple translation

> From that, the Kleśa-Karma circuit ceases.

`Tataḥ`, “from that,” does not introduce an external consequence added to
Dharma-Megha. It unfolds Dharma-Megha's first immanent actualization:

```text
Dharma-Megha
Knowing actual throughout its complete field
                   ↓ articulates itself as
freedom from afflicted conditioning
                   ↓
Kleśa-Karma-Nivṛtti
```

This is the first of three realized determinations or Siddhis of
Dharma-Megha.

## Not consequences, but immanent actualizations

The sequence IV.30–32 should not be read as:

```text
Dharma-Megha occurs
        ↓ afterward produces
three detachable rewards
```

Rather:

```text
Dharma-Megha is actual only through
        |
        ├── freedom from afflicted conditioning    IV.30
        ├── infinitude of Knowledge                IV.31
        └── fulfillment of transformative Artha   IV.32
```

The three Siddhis are how Dharma-Megha exists. They are not possessions of
a saintly personality.

## The textual boundary

The Hegelian material for IV.30 is B.2, the complete development of Real
Possibility.

It begins with Real Possibility as the content-filled in-itself of Real
Actuality. It unfolds the actual manifold of circumstances and conditions,
then shows this circle of conditions sublating its external form and becoming
the fact itself.

The section ends when the distinction between:

```text
actuality as the possibility of another
and
possibility as an alien actuality
```

has disappeared. Real Necessity can then emerge at IV.31.

## Kleśa and Karma as one circuit

Kleśa and Karma are distinguishable but inseparable moments:

```text
Kleśa
the contradictory formative determination
held as immediately true
        ↓ becomes objective through
Karma
the actual production and reproduction
of its conditions
```

Karma produces Saṃskāras, and Saṃskāras generate further Pratyayas through
which the Kleśa again becomes active:

```text
Kleśa → Karma → Saṃskāra → Pratyaya
   ↑                              |
   └──────────────────────────────┘
```

The word `circuit` is important. IV.30 does not merely remove two items from
a list. It ends a mode of conditioned self-reproduction.

## Hegel's Real Possibility

Formal possibility asked only whether a proposed content contradicted itself
in the abstract.

Real Possibility asks a harder question:

> Are the actual circumstances and conditions required for this fact
> present?

```text
formal possibility
“Can I think this without an immediate contradiction?”

real possibility
“Does the actual manifold contain the complete conditions
through which this fact can exist?”
```

This is the correct level for Karma. Karma is not merely a thinkable causal
story; it is the real organization of circumstances through which a fact is
produced.

## The manifold of conditions

Hegel defines Real Possibility as an immediately existent manifold of
circumstances referring to a fact.

```text
condition 1 ─┐
condition 2 ─┼──► the really possible fact
condition 3 ─┤
     ...    ─┘
```

The conditions are already actual. But their actuality is dispersed. Each
exists immediately while functioning as the in-itself of another actuality
that has not yet appeared as the completed fact.

This is exactly the structure of Saṃskāra:

```text
past actuality
      ↓ retained as a condition
in-itself of a future Pratyaya or act
```

A Saṃskāra is therefore neither merely past nor merely possible. It is an
actual formation functioning as the Real Possibility of another actuality.

## How Kleśa acquires real power

A Kleśa is not powerful merely because its proposition is confused. It
becomes powerful when an actual manifold supports and reproduces it.

```text
Kleśa as abstract error
        +
supporting bodily, cognitive, social, and karmic conditions
        ↓
Real Possibility of afflicted action
        ↓
Karma becomes actual
```

This is why prayer or intellectual correction alone may fail to stop a
hellish construction. If the real conditions remain intact, the fact remains
really possible and will repeatedly become actual.

The Immeasurables matter because they cultivate a different actual manifold
of dispositions, responses, and relations.

## Conditions are dispersed Actuality

Hegel calls Real Possibility a dispersed Actuality. The conditions are
actual, but they are not yet reflected into themselves as the one fact they
make possible.

```text
dispersed conditions
many actualities serving as the in-itself of another
                    ↓
complete circle
one fact in which their relation becomes explicit
```

The difference between condition and fact is therefore not a difference
between unreality and reality. It is a difference in the organization of
actual content.

This is Dharma as constructive form.

## Contradiction in the real conditions

Because Real Possibility contains a manifold, it contains distinction,
opposition, and contradiction.

The contradiction is not introduced merely by an external comparison:

```text
the actual conditions themselves
have the determination to cease standing separately
and to founder into their ground
```

The manifold is unstable as dispersed conditions. Its truth is the complete
fact for which it forms the Real Possibility.

Thus the contradiction of Kleśa is not resolved solely by arguing against
it. Its actual conditions must pass into a new total organization.

## When all conditions are present, the fact is there

Hegel states the principle categorically:

> Whenever all the conditions of a fact are completely present, the fact is
> actually there.

```text
complete conditions = the content of the fact

there is no additional magical step
between complete Real Possibility and Actuality
```

This explains both the danger of bad Karma and the power of Good Karma.

```text
complete afflicted conditions   → afflicted actuality

complete non-afflicted conditions → liberating actuality
```

One cannot continuously construct all the conditions of hell and expect a
prayer to insert an unrelated heavenly result. Nor is heaven granted by an
external Being after the conditions are complete. The fact is the completed
organization of its real conditions.

## The twofold self-sublation

Real Possibility contains both Actuality and Possibility. Its self-sublation
therefore has two inseparable directions.

### First movement: Actuality becomes in-itself

The conditions initially appear as immediately subsisting actualities.
Through their self-sublation they become moments of another and acquire
their truth as its in-itself.

```text
independent actual condition
        ↓ loses isolated subsistence
moment of the complete fact
```

### Second movement: in-itself becomes Actuality

The conditions were also the possibility of another. When their dispersed
form is sublated, that in-itself passes into the actuality of the fact.

```text
possibility of the fact
        ↓ conditions complete themselves
actual fact
```

These are the two reciprocal transitions of the five-fold synthesis.

## The five-fold synthesis of Real Possibility

```text
1. Real Actuality
   Dharma-Megha as content-filled manifestation

2. Real Possibility
   the actual manifold of conditions

3. Actuality → Possibility
   each condition becomes a moment or in-itself of another

4. Possibility → Actuality
   the complete circle of conditions becomes the fact

5. Real Necessity
   the self-rejoining identity of both movements
   to be made explicit in IV.31
```

IV.30 is primarily the double middle movement. The Kleśa-Karma circuit ends
when conditions and fact no longer confront one another as externally
separate terms.

## What exactly ceases?

Hegel says that what disappears is:

```text
Actuality determined merely as the possibility of another

and

Possibility determined as an actuality alien to its own fact
```

Applied to Yoga:

```text
Karma ceases to be an external chain pushing Citta

Saṃskāra ceases to be an alien past dictating a foreign future

Kleśa ceases to be an independent Principle of action
```

Their content is comprehended and reorganized within the complete actuality
of Dharma-Megha.

`Nivṛtti` is the cessation of their independent governing form.

## This is not the cessation of Kriyā

Action as such does not stop. Hegel's Real Actuality is explicitly active:
it announces itself through what it produces.

The distinction is:

```text
Kleśa-Karma
action reproducing an uncomprehended contradiction
                    ceases

Kriyā-Jñāna
Knowing constructing actuality from its comprehended Principle
                    remains
```

Dharma-Megha does not make the Yogi inert. It frees constructive activity
from the old circuit of afflicted conditions.

## The first Siddhi of Dharma-Megha

We can now name IV.30 precisely:

> Freedom from afflicted conditioning.

```text
not:
personal immunity awarded after Samādhi

but:
the real conditions of afflicted reproduction
are comprehended and self-sublated within Knowing
```

The Siddhi is the actuality of a changed causal field.

This is why it has practical force. The Yogi no longer merely resists one
afflicted result after another; the conditions producing the results have
lost their external, self-reproducing organization.

## Fichte's IV.30: projection without a gap

Fichte says that Certainty or Light is immediately living Principle. Its
living is immanent self-projection, not transmission across a gap.

```text
not:

Light ── gap ──► foreign image


but:

Light's living = its self-projection and intuition
```

All Light is immediately self-creating. Even the Science of Knowing cannot
avoid this necessity.

This gives the inward form of the Hegelian self-sublation of conditions.
The fact does not receive its Principle from an alien cause; the complete
movement is the living Principle projecting itself.

## Hegel and Fichte compared

| Fichte | Hegel |
|---|---|
| Light is immediately self-projecting | Real Possibility is the actual totality of conditions |
| projection does not cross an external gap | conditions and fact cease to stand externally apart |
| the Principle's living is its production | complete conditions are the actual fact |
| no alien determination grounds Knowing | the actuality serving merely as possibility of another disappears |
| Kleśa-Karma loses its inward cognitive opening | Kleśa-Karma loses its external conditional circuit |
| Kriyā remains as Light's self-expression | Real Actuality remains productive manifestation |

Fichte gives the absence of an inward gap. Hegel gives the completion of the
objective conditions. Together they explain `nivṛtti` without suppressing
activity.

## Their mutual correction

Fichte prevents the Hegelian circle of conditions from becoming causal
determinism. The conditions form one actuality because the living Principle
projects itself through them.

Hegel prevents Fichtean self-projection from remaining an inward act of
Light. The projection is real only when the complete objective conditions
are present and reorganized.

```text
Fichte
no gap between Principle and projection
                    +
Hegel
no external separation between conditions and fact
                    ↓
Kleśa-Karma-Nivṛtti
freedom from alien inward and outward determination
```

## The Immeasurables as real conditions

The Immeasurables are not merely beautiful mental states contemplated in
private. Practiced repeatedly, they construct Real Possibility:

```text
Maitrī       conditions non-hostile relation
Karuṇā       conditions response to suffering without aversion
Muditā       conditions joy without competitive appropriation
Upekṣā       conditions stability without indifference or compulsion
```

Together they alter the actual manifold from which Karma arises.

This is how Practical Reason participates in Dharma-Megha. It does not
replace OṂ-Artha; it constructs conditions adequate to its realization.

## Provisional result

IV.30 says:

> Dharma-Megha exists as freedom from the Kleśa-Karma circuit: the complete
> conditions of afflicted production lose their external organization and
> are comprehended within a self-determining Actuality.

Hegel explains `Karma` as Real Possibility—the actual manifold of conditions
through which a fact exists. When the conditions are complete, they are the
fact. Their scattered and externally conditioning form sublates itself.

Fichte supplies the corresponding inward result: Light projects itself
without a gap and therefore receives no alien Principle.

```text
Dharma-Megha
Real Actuality
        ↓ articulates itself as
complete Real Possibility
        ↓ conditions self-sublate
freedom from afflicted conditioning
        ↓
Kleśa-Karma-Nivṛtti
```

IV.31 can now state the identity achieved by this double movement: Real
Possibility is Real Necessity, Knowledge is infinite, and the independently
knowable is little.
