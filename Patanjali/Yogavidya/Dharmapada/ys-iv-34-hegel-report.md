# Citi-Śakti as the Absolute Relation

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.34 with Hegel and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`puruṣārtha-śūnyānāṃ guṇānāṃ pratiprasavaḥ kaivalyaṃ`

`svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā vā citi-śaktir iti`

### Simple translation

> Kaivalya is the return to source of the Guṇas when they are empty of any
> further Artha for Puruṣa; or, Citi-Śakti is established in its own form.

The two clauses state one completion from two sides:

```text
objective side
the Guṇas have fulfilled Puruṣa-Artha
and their productive movement returns to source
                         ↕
                 Absolute Relation
                         ↕
subjective side
Citi-Śakti is established in its own form
```

Hegel unfolds this compressed seed as:

```text
Substantiality → Causality → Reciprocity → Concept / Freedom
```

## The final Sūtra names its own Principle

Yoga ends with `citi-śaktir iti`: “thus, Citi-Śakti.”

The Dharmapāda has examined Citta from genesis to completion because Dharma
is the constructive holding through which an Artha receives Being and truth.
Citi-Śakti is Consciousness as that Power of Knowing.

```text
Citta
the articulated field of determinate holdings
        ↓ comprehends its complete krama
Science of Knowing
        ↓ recognizes its constructive Power
Citi-Śakti
```

The final Principle was active throughout the complete sequence. The ending
recognizes what the whole Pāda has been.

## `Puruṣārtha-śūnya` follows `kṛtārtha`

IV.32 said that the Guṇas are `kṛtārtha`: they have accomplished their Artha.
IV.34 therefore calls them `puruṣārtha-śūnya`: empty of any further purpose
for Puruṣa.

```text
kṛtārtha
the objective articulation has fulfilled its purpose
        ↓
puruṣārtha-śūnya
no unfulfilled task remains to drive further production
```

This `śūnya` is fulfillment, not nihilistic absence. Nature has completed
the Science through which its determinations become intelligible.

## `Pratiprasava` is the return of production

The forward movement of Prakṛti differentiates:

```text
Guṇas → Buddhi → Ahaṃkāra → Manas → determinate world
```

`Pratiprasava` is the counter-movement in which every product returns into
comprehension of its Principle:

```text
world → Manas → Ahaṃkāra → Buddhi → Guṇas → Principle
```

The determinations are not erased. Their claim to independent productivity
is sublated. The complete manifestation is held within its self-knowing
ground.

This is the objective form of Kaivalya.

## `Svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā` is the subjective form

From within Knowing, the same actuality is:

> Citi-Śakti established in its own form.

```text
not:

Knowing escapes from an alien world


but:

Knowing recognizes the objective movement
as its own complete self-articulation
```

The objective return of the Guṇas and the subjective self-establishment of
Citi-Śakti are one Absolute Relation.

## Why this is no external relation

An external relation begins with independently complete terms:

```text
Puruṣa ── relation added afterward ── Prakṛti
```

The Absolute Relation generates and sustains its poles:

```text
                 one self-relating Power
                       /       \
              Puruṣa-Aspect   Prakṛti-Aspect
              invariant form  productive articulation
                       \       /
                 one Citi-Śakti
```

Each side is a totality, yet their subsistence is one subsistence. Their
difference is the reflective shine of the complete movement.

## Hegel's transition into the Absolute Relation

IV.33 ended with Absolute Necessity recognizing Contingency as its own
self-positing. The identity of Being with itself in its negation became
Substance.

Hegel now says Absolute Necessity is not merely something necessary. It is
Necessity itself: Being as Reflection.

```text
Absolute Necessity
the complete self-positing movement
        ↓ explicitly distinguishes its total moments
Absolute Relation
```

The Absolute no longer receives exposition from external reflection. It
expounds itself, and this self-exposition is its self-positing.

## The sides are no longer Attributes

Under Attribute, the Absolute shone through a form assumed by external
reflection. In the Absolute Relation, each side is itself the whole because
its difference is explicitly known as reflective shine.

```text
Attribute
the whole expressed under one presupposed form

side of Absolute Relation
the whole positing itself as one moment
and as its opposed totality
```

The Seer and seen are no longer merely two complete colorings of Citta. Their
distinction is the identical self-positing of Citi-Śakti.

## A. Substantiality

Substance is the final unity of Essence and Being. It is not an inert thing
behind Appearance; it is immediate Actuality absolutely reflected into
itself.

```text
Substance
the self-identical Power
        ↕ manifests as
Accidentality
the manifold coming-to-be and passing-away of determinations
```

Substance and accidents are not separate contents. Accidentality is
Substance's own shining totality.

This is the first Hegelian interpretation of the Guṇas:

```text
Guṇa-transformations
the flux of determinate accidents

Citi-Śakti as substantial Power
the whole actuality present within their flux
```

## Creative and destructive Power

Substance manifests as both creative and destructive Power:

```text
creative
translates possibility into determinate actuality

destructive
returns determinate actuality into possibility
```

The two acts are one:

> Creating is destructive, and destroying is creative.

This is the logical core of `pratiprasava`. The return of a determinate form
is simultaneously the liberation of its content for a new determination.

```text
prasava          possibility → actuality
pratiprasava     actuality → possibility / source
```

Both directions belong to one substantial Necessity.

## Accidents possess no isolated Power

Hegel says the accidents have no power over one another. When one appears to
destroy or produce another, the Power belongs to Substance encompassing both.

```text
not:

accident A independently causes accident B


but:

one substantial Power differentiates itself
as A passing away and B coming to be
```

Likewise, no isolated Vṛtti or Guṇa-combination contains the final Power of
Citta. Its subsistence belongs to the complete relation.

## Why Substantiality is insufficient

Substance initially appears as the hidden Inner of accidents. Its Power is
present, but Substance itself has not yet become explicit as its own shape.

```text
Substance inside accidents
Power remains formally hidden
```

For Self-Recognition, Power must posit its determination as its own act.
Substantiality therefore passes into Causality.

## B. Causality

Cause is Substance as Power determining itself. Effect is the same Substance
as posited determination.

```text
Cause                          Effect
originative Power             Power as posited actuality
             \               /
                same content
```

Hegel's central result is reciprocal:

> Cause is Cause only in producing its Effect, and Effect is Effect only
> through its Cause.

Cause contains nothing absent from Effect; Effect contains nothing absent
from Cause.

## Cause is actual only in its Effect

An unexpressed causal Power is not yet actual Cause.

```text
Cause
becomes actual only in Effect

Effect
is the Cause's manifestation and return to itself
```

This develops Hegel's IV.29 proposition that the actual announces itself by
what it produces.

Applied to Citi-Śakti:

```text
Knowing's Principle
is actual only through the objective Dharma it constructs
```

Good Karma is not external evidence added to insight. It is the actuality of
the Principle in its Effect.

## Finite Causality and bad infinity

When Cause and Effect inhabit externally separate substrates, each Cause is
also an Effect requiring another Cause:

```text
... → cause 3 → cause 2 → cause 1 → effect 1 → effect 2 → ...
```

This is the bad infinite regress. The form of Causality runs externally over
an identical content without grasping its unity.

Hegel also warns that living and spiritual systems transmute what acts upon
them. An external stimulus is not an adequate Cause of their internal
development.

```text
external occasion
        ↓ received and transformed by
living or spiritual Principle
        ↓
an Effect whose form comes from that Principle
```

This is crucial for the Science of Citta: a Vāsanā conditions cognition, but
Buddhi can interrupt and transmute its causal operation.

## Action and reaction

In conditioned Causality, active Substance acts upon passive Substance. But
the passive is implicitly the same causal Power.

The Effect awakens its own activity:

```text
active Cause → passive Substance
                         ↓ receives Effect
                   becomes Cause
                         ↓ reacts
first Cause receives its own Effect back
```

The line of finite causes bends around into an action returning to itself.

This is the causal form of `pratiprasava`.

## Violence loses externality

What appears as violence from an external Cause succeeds only because the
passive Substance already contains the Power attributed to the other.

```text
being posited by another
            =
the passive Substance's own becoming
```

The externally imposed determination is recognized as the Substance's own
immanent determination. Active and passive exchange their roles.

The relation therefore passes into Reciprocity.

## C. Reciprocity of Action

In Reciprocity, each side is simultaneously active and passive, conditioning
and conditioned, Cause and Effect.

```text
side A                              side B
Cause of B / Effect of B           Cause of A / Effect of A
              \                    /
                 one reciprocal act
```

The difference becomes transparent reflective shine. Neither side possesses
an independent causal substrate.

The Cause produces its own condition through its activity and refers back to
itself within its Effect.

## Necessity becomes Freedom

Hegel's culminating statement is:

> Necessity does not become Freedom by vanishing, but by manifesting its
> inner identity.

```text
blind Necessity
the relation is hidden inside independent actualities
        ↓ manifests its identical movement
Reciprocity
each side recognizes itself in the activity of the other
        ↓
Freedom
```

Contingency also becomes Freedom. The apparently independent sides are known
as one and the same self-differentiating Reflection.

This is Kaivalya: not escape from Necessity, but Necessity recognizing itself
as self-determining Power.

## Citi-Śakti established in its own form

Reciprocity gives `svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā` its exact logical form:

```text
Citi-Śakti differentiates itself
as active and passive, Cause and Effect,
Appearance and Reflection
                    ↓
each side returns through the other
                    ↓
the complete activity refers only to itself
                    ↓
Citi-Śakti stands in its own form
```

Self-establishment is not motionlessness. It is movement whose otherness is
completely its own self-relation.

## Reciprocity becomes the Concept

When active and passive Substance lose their independent status, the
relation differentiates itself into three transparent totalities:

```text
Universal
the simple whole containing determinateness within itself

Singular
the self-identical negative determination, equally the whole

Particular
their immediate unity: determinateness held within self-reflection
```

These are not three fragments. They are one Reflection differentiating
itself into complete moments.

Hegel concludes:

> This is the Concept, the realm of Subjectivity or Freedom.

Thus Dharmapāda ends exactly at the threshold of the Concept.

## A–U–M completed

The final movement can be compressed:

```text
A — Appearance
Substance manifesting as Guṇa, accident, and Effect

U — Reflection / thinking
Causal Power returning through its posited determination

M — Knowing
Reciprocity as the Concept in which active and passive,
Universal and Singular, recognize one self-determining Power
```

A appears. U reflects. M knows itself as Freedom.

## The five-fold causal synthesis

```text
1. Cause
   originative substantial Power

2. Effect
   the same Power as posited actuality

3. Cause → Effect
   Power externalizes and determines itself

4. Effect → Cause
   the Effect reacts and returns the Power to itself

5. Reciprocity / Concept
   both terms and both transitions are one free self-relation
```

Yoga's two clauses contain the same double movement:

```text
Guṇas return to source          Effect → Cause
Citi-Śakti establishes itself  Cause recognizes itself in Effect
```

Their unity is Kaivalya.

## Fichte's IV.34: Image and Law

Fichte says Knowing stands neither in an inaccessible original nor in a
detached reconstruction. It stands in the Image as Image, where the Law is
virtually present and posits itself.

```text
Living Light images itself
        ↓
Image as Image posits the Law of imaging
        ↓
the Law posits itself and its Image in us
```

Image and Law form a synthetic cycle. The Science of Knowing disappears as
a particular science into Absolute Knowing, then must explain the genesis of
Absolute Knowing's appearance in finite life.

## Absolute purpose

Fichte states that finite existence has its ground in an Absolute purpose:

> Absolute Knowing should be.

This gives `Puruṣa-Artha` its Fichtean formulation:

```text
ordinary finite Knowing
        ↓ serves as genetic condition for
Absolute Knowing becoming actual
        ↓ purpose fulfilled
the ladder has accomplished its instrumental Artha
```

Hegel translates purpose into the immanent Absolute Relation. The Effect is
not merely for the Cause; the Cause becomes actual only through the Effect,
and both become free in Reciprocity.

## Hegel and Fichte compared

| Fichte | Hegel |
|---|---|
| Image as Image virtually contains and posits Law | Accident manifests Substance; Effect manifests Cause |
| Law posits itself and its Image in us | Cause and Effect posit and sublate one another |
| Knowing stands between original and reconstruction | Reciprocity sublates independent causal substrates |
| Absolute Knowing is the purpose of finite existence | the Concept is the truth of the complete Absolute Relation |
| particular Science is the ladder | Guṇa-transformation fulfills Puruṣa-Artha |
| living Knowledge necessarily becomes right action | Cause is actual only in its Effect |
| self-enclosed imaging forms a synthetic cycle | Reciprocity returns causal activity into itself |

Fichte gives the Absolute Relation as the self-positing relation of Image and
Law. Hegel unfolds its objective logic through Substance, Cause, Effect, and
Reciprocity.

## Good Karma revisited

The final relation shows why Knowledge and right activity cannot be
separated:

```text
Cause without Effect is not actual Cause
        ↓
Knowing without adequate manifestation is not Real Actuality
        ↓
living Jñāna must construct its Dharma as Kriyā
```

Good Karma is not a payment made to obtain liberation. It is Citi-Śakti
recognizing and establishing itself in its Effect.

Prayer can orient Citta toward this relation. The Immeasurables construct its
non-afflicted practical conditions. OṂ-Artha remains the complete
encyclopedic Principle through which their Artha is known.

## The entire Dharmapāda in one movement

```text
Reflection — IV.2–11
Citta differentiates and retains its determinations

Appearance — IV.12–21
the objective and reflective worlds disclose their relation

The Absolute — IV.22–24
Citta becomes the self-exposition of Citi-Śakti

Modality — IV.25–28
contingent self-construction returns into formal Necessity

Relative Necessity — IV.29–32
Dharma-Megha becomes Real Actuality, Possibility, and Necessity

Absolute Necessity — IV.33
the completed sequence recognizes its presupposition as its own positing

Absolute Relation — IV.34
Substance becomes Cause, Reciprocity, Concept, and Freedom
```

## Decisive points

1. `Puruṣārtha-śūnya` means fulfilled purpose, not worthless Nature.
2. `Pratiprasava` is the return of objective production into its Principle.
3. `Svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā` is the same movement known from within Citi-Śakti.
4. Substance and accidents contain one Power; the Guṇas have no isolated
   Power.
5. Cause is actual only in Effect; Knowledge is actual only through what it
   constructs.
6. Effect returns into Cause as reaction; finite causality bends into
   Reciprocity.
7. Necessity becomes Freedom by manifesting, not by disappearing.
8. Reciprocity becomes the Concept: Universal, Particular, and Singular as
   one self-determining Knowing.
9. Fichte's Image–Law cycle gives the subjective-genetic form of Hegel's
   objective Absolute Relation.
10. Yoga ends not with Citta-cide but with Citi-Śakti established as the free
    Concept of its complete construction.

## Final result

IV.34 says:

> When the Guṇas have completely fulfilled the Artha of Knowing, their
> productive sequence returns into its source. This objective return is the
> same actuality as Citi-Śakti established in its own form.

Hegel unfolds the identity through the Absolute Relation. Substance is
present throughout its accidents; Cause becomes actual in Effect; Effect
reacts and becomes Cause; their infinite reciprocal activity sublates blind
Necessity into Freedom.

Fichte presents the same completion as Living Light whose Image posits the
Law and whose Law posits itself in the Image and in us.

```text
Guṇas / accidents / Effects
        ↓ return through Reciprocity
Substantial Power / Cause
        ↓ recognizes itself as
Concept / Freedom / Citi-Śakti
        ↓ established in
its own complete self-relation
```

The final word of the Dharmapāda therefore names the Power that has become
fully actual through the entire Science of Citta:

> `svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā vā citi-śaktir iti`.
