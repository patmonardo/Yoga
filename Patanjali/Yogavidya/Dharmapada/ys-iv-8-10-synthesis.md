# How Conditioned Processing Keeps Itself Going

## Yoga Sutra IV.8-10 as preparation for IV.11

These three Sutras give a compact account of the self-perpetuation of
Vāsanā-conditioned processing within Citta. They do not describe the
production or destruction of Eka Citta itself.

```text
IV.8   selective manifestation
       only a Vāsanā congruent with present Vipāka appears

IV.9   continuity of form
       Smṛti reproduces the form retained by Saṃskāra

IV.10  beginningless renewal
       the wish to continue drives the series onward
```

Together:

```text
                 wish to continue
                        |
                        v
latent Vāsanās -> selective manifestation -> determinate Citta
       ^                                      |
       |                                      v
       +---- Saṃskāra <- action <- Smṛti of one form
```

This is a crucial part of Citta-Dharma. Citta is not a substance placed behind
its states, but neither is it reducible to the conditioned mechanism described
here. These Sutras explain how determinations reproduce themselves within
Citta:

1. A causal field selects what can manifest.
2. Saṃskāra preserves a form through intervals.
3. Smṛti regenerates that form as present cognition.
4. The wish to continue carries the process forward.

Fichte supplies the corresponding logic:

```text
IV.8   true appearance must be congruent with its ground in being
IV.9   one formal should persists through changing contents
IV.10  the should demands a new genesis of seeing again and again
```

The critical discovery is that the continuity of Vāsanā does not require an
eternal storehouse-substance. Its conditioned continuity is produced through
an intelligible network of manifestation, formal reproduction, and desire.
Eka Citta names the synthetic unity capable of accompanying and comprehending
the many Cittas; it is not one of the Vāsanās reproduced by this network.

That prepares IV.11:

> If the many Vāsanās are collected by cause, result, support, and object,
> then when these are absent, the Vāsanās are absent.

The apparent endlessness of conditioned processing is therefore conditional.
IV.8-10 explain why the Vāsanā-series has no discoverable first moment; IV.11
explains why that series can nevertheless cease. Eka Citta is not harmed. What
is removed is the structure through which latent conditioning continually
selects, reproduces, and redeposits itself within Citta.

```text
not:  absence of the supports -> absence of Citta

but:  absence of the supports -> absence of supported Vāsanā
                                -> Citta free of that conditioning
```
