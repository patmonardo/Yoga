# The Construction of Selfhood Ceases

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.25 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`viśeṣa-darśina ātmabhāva-bhāvanā-vinivṛttiḥ`

### Simple translation

> For one who sees the distinction, the construction of “I am this” ceases.

The terms say more precisely:

- `viśeṣa-darśinaḥ`: for the one seeing the distinction;
- `ātma-bhāva`: the being, condition, or appearance attributed to oneself;
- `bhāvanā`: formation, cultivation, or continued construction;
- `vinivṛttiḥ`: complete cessation or turning away.

The Sūtra does not say that Ātman ceases. It says that the activity which
constructs a determinate formation as Ātman ceases.

```text
not:

Ātman ──► nonexistence


but:

constructed Citta ──► “I am this construction”
                              ↓
                    discriminating vision
                              ↓
                 that identification ceases
```

## Why this follows IV.24

IV.24 established that Citta is variegated by innumerable Vāsanās and works
through combination for a higher Artha. Because the composite is not its own
Principle, it cannot legitimately declare itself to be the Self.

```text
IV.24
Citta is a constructed and purposive combination
                         ↓
IV.25
the construction cannot be Ātman
                         ↓
the production of “I am this” ceases
```

The result is discrimination, not destruction. Citta remains capable of
operation, but its assembled formations are no longer mistaken for their
pāramārthika Principle.

## What exactly ceases?

`Ātma-bhāva-bhāvanā` is a productive process:

```text
Vāsanā and Saṃskāra
        ↓
Manas constructs a determinate unity
        ↓
Ahaṃkāra appropriates the unity
        ↓
“this is me” / “I am this”
```

The final attribution is what ceases. The sensible field does not vanish;
Manas need not stop functioning; Buddhi is not killed; and the Inconceivable
One does not enter into a process of becoming or cessation.

This gives the categorical distinction:

```text
Ahaṃkāra                         Buddhi

vyāvahārika appropriation       pāramārthika Principle
“I am this determination”       knows the determination as determination
```

The error is not the ordinary use of the word `I`. It is the elevation of a
conditioned formation into the Principle of Knowing.

## `Viśeṣa-darśin`: seeing the distinction

The distinction is not merely between two objects called Puruṣa and
Prakṛti. If they were simply placed side by side, their relation would remain
uncomprehended.

The discriminating Seer understands their different functions within one
actual relation:

```text
Puruṣa-Aspect                   Prakṛti-Aspect
invariant knowing              determinate appearing
              \               /
               \             /
                Citta relation
                      ↓
          comprehended by Buddhi
```

Puruṣa is the invariant Seer-Aspect, a Spectral Tattva. Prakṛti supplies the
changing inner and outer objective determinations. Buddhi knows their
relation. Ahaṃkāra turns a determinate configuration of that relation into
“my essential being.”

`Viśeṣa-darśana` ends this last confusion.

## Fichte's Bhāṣya: genesis negates itself

Fichte begins with the genesis already accomplished. It is an absolute,
self-enclosed genesis; it is not an endless genesis of another genesis.

Its remarkable structure is:

> Genesis negates itself inwardly within knowing.

To transcendental reflection, the process appears as genesis. In immediate
knowing, however, only its stable result appears:

```text
transcendental construction       immediate knowing

genesis is comprehended           result persists as intuition
as genesis                        and appears as Being
          \                       /
           \                     /
            genesis negates itself
                   in its result
```

This explains how Manas can move too quickly to be observed. Its genesis
disappears into the stable Thing it produces. What was constructed appears
simply to be.

The same concealment produces `ātma-bhāva`:

```text
activity constructing selfhood
               ↓ disappears into its result
“I am this” appears as an immediate fact
```

Discriminating vision restores knowledge of the genesis and thereby ends
the illusion that its result is self-grounded Being.

## Facticity and genesis coincide

Fichte's next statement is especially strong:

> Knowing's immediate facticity is absolute genesis, and absolute genesis
> is present as a fact without a further ground.

At this point, facticity and genesis coincide. The fact of knowing is not an
inert product behind which another hidden production must always be sought.
Its actuality is the completed self-negating genesis itself.

```text
genesis ── completes and negates itself ──► factical Being of Knowing

therefore:

the fact is the accomplished genesis
```

This is why IV.25 must not become Citta-cide. The purpose is not to make the
fact of knowing disappear. It is to see that a conditioned determination
within knowing cannot appropriate this fact and call itself the absolute
Self.

## Two different geneses

Fichte warns that the whole context must remain present. Earlier he had
proved knowing as a `from`; now he again proves genesis factically. The two
arguments can sound identical, but they concern different levels:

```text
earlier                           now

genesis of knowing's             genesis of absolute knowing
absolute self-genesis            in its fundamental construction
```

The distinction cannot be learned by attaching different labels to two
isolated propositions. It becomes visible only through their place in the
complete movement.

This is also the discipline of `viśeṣa-darśana`. Discrimination is not the
mechanical separation of terms. It is knowing the genetic law that gives
each determination its exact place.

## The Science of Knowing is an art

Fichte consequently says that the Science of Knowing is not a lesson to be
memorized but an art.

Its distinctions are generated through the living context:

```text
not:

memorize “Puruṣa is not Prakṛti”


but:

reconstruct the relation until the distinct Principle
of each moment becomes visible from within the whole
```

This is why the cessation in IV.25 cannot be produced by repeating “I am not
Citta.” Such repetition could itself become another Saṃskāra appropriated by
Ahaṃkāra. The relation must become transparent to Buddhi.

## Three levels of Saṃskāra

The sequence now permits a useful discrimination:

```text
1. ordinary Saṃskāra
   a past activity of Manas retained as a conditioning formation

2. ātma-bhāva Saṃskāra
   Ahaṃkāra appropriates the formation as “I” or “mine”

3. Prajñā-Saṃskāra
   Buddhi retains insight into the Principle of the formation
```

Prajñā-Saṃskāra does not replace one empirical personality with a supposedly
more spiritual personality. It is the established Being of Knowing through
which the formation is known without self-appropriation.

Thus `vinivṛtti` has a positive content:

> The construction of determinate selfhood ceases because Prajñā has made
> its genesis transparent.

## A–U–M

The same structure can provisionally illuminate our A–U–M model:

```text
A                 U
objective pole    knowing pole
       \          /
        \        /
       M: their combining ground
```

The error of `ātma-bhāva-bhāvanā` is to identify Ātman with A, U, M, or their
assembled result. IV.25 does not destroy the construction. It ends its claim
to be the Inconceivable One.

The exact Māṇḍūkya allocation remains provisional. What is already secure is
the logical result: the Principle that comprehends a construction cannot be
reduced to one of the constructed determinations.

## Yoga and Fichte

| Yoga IV.25 | Fichte |
|---|---|
| the discriminating Seer sees the distinction | distinctions become intelligible through their complete genetic context |
| `ātma-bhāva` is a constructed self-condition | genesis disappears into its stable result as apparently immediate Being |
| construction of “I am this” ceases | insight restores the genesis concealed in the fact |
| neither knowing nor Ātman is annihilated | immediate facticity is the accomplished genesis of knowing |
| discrimination must become actual | the Science of Knowing is an art, not memorized doctrine |

Fichte therefore gives `vinivṛtti` a precise meaning:

> What ceases is the false immediacy of the constructed self, because its
> genesis has become intelligible.

## Provisional result

IV.25 says:

> When Buddhi sees the exact distinction between the constructed cognitive
> formation and its Principle, the production of “I am this formation”
> ceases.

Fichte shows why the error is so powerful. Genesis negates itself in its
result, so the constructed product appears as immediate Being. The same
movement makes Ahaṃkāra's formation appear to be an immediately existing
Self.

Genetic insight does not abolish the actuality of knowing. It restores the
process concealed within that actuality and prevents a determinate product
from claiming pāramārthika status.

Actuality has therefore advanced:

```text
IV.22    Buddhi knows itself

IV.23    Citta bears every Artha

IV.24    its composite activity works for a higher Artha

IV.25    the composite ceases to construct itself as Ātman
```

IV.26 can now describe the positive direction of Citta after this false
self-construction has ceased.
