# Nirvitarka Samāpatti: Artha Alone Shining

## A report on Yoga Sūtra I.43 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`smṛti-pariśuddhau svarūpa-śūnyeva-artha-mātra-nirbhāsā nirvitarkā`

### Relatively necessary ordinary translation

> When memory has been purified, Samāpatti is as though empty of its own
> form and only Artha shines forth. This is Nirvitarka Samāpatti.

The phrase “as though” is decisive. Citta is not destroyed, and Knowing does
not disappear. What disappears is the independent claim of the
representative form through which Artha was previously recognized.

```text
Savitarka
Word — remembered determination — Knowledge
                 intermixed
                      ↓ purification
Nirvitarka
the representative becomes transparent
                      ↓
               Artha alone shines
```

Fichte gives the genetic meaning:

> The image has no self-sufficiency apart from what it images, and what is
> imaged does not appear apart from its image. Their truth is neither term
> separately, but the living, self-identical relation through which each is
> posited by the other.

Nirvitarka is not thoughtlessness. It is the cognition in which the
factically inherited form of thought no longer obscures its living
Principle.

## Why I.43 follows I.42

I.42 showed Word, Artha, and Knowledge occurring in a genuine but
intermixed Samāpatti.

```text
Śabda presents Artha
Smṛti recognizes and stabilizes the presentation
Jñāna knows the recognized Artha
```

Because these operations occur together, the Scientist can mistake the
recognized form for Artha itself. I.43 purifies that mixture.

The movement is not:

```text
thought exists
      ↓
thought is killed
      ↓
an object remains by itself
```

It is:

```text
the inherited representative determines the appearance of Artha
      ↓
the representative is recognized as representative
      ↓
its independent validity is surrendered
      ↓
Artha shines through the now-transparent act of Knowing
```

## `Smṛti-pariśuddhi`: purification of memory

Memory supplies the already established forms by which a present
determination is named, classified, and recognized.

This work is necessary. Without retention, every cognition would vanish
without becoming available to Science. But memory also introduces a danger:
the retained form may replace fresh insight.

```text
Artha appears
      ↓
memory supplies its name and familiar determination
      ↓
the familiar determination is taken as the Artha itself
```

Purification does not erase memory. It removes memory's claim to be the
origin and measure of the present Artha.

```text
unpurified memory
“I know this because it matches what I already possess.”

purified memory
“The retained form serves the present construction
and becomes transparent to what now manifests.”
```

Smṛti is purified when it preserves the path of Knowing without dictating
the result in advance.

## `Svarūpa-śūnya iva`: as though empty of its own form

The Sūtra does not simply say that Citta is empty of its own form. It says
`iva`: *as though* empty.

This guards the exact philosophical meaning:

- Citta remains the luminous capacity in which Artha manifests.
- The determinate cognitive form remains operative.
- But neither presents itself as an independently existing object between
  Knowing and Artha.

The form functions perfectly by ceasing to obstruct what it presents.

The transparent jewel has not perished. Its own color simply does not
compete with the coloring of Artha.

```text
not absence of Citta

but absence of Citta's opaque self-presentation
within the accomplished Samāpatti
```

This is disciplined self-effacement, not Citta-cide.

## `Artha-mātra-nirbhāsa`: only Artha shines

`Artha-mātra` means Artha alone—not a verbal label plus a remembered image
plus a separate cognition inspecting them.

Yet “Artha alone” cannot mean an unknowable object existing outside all
Knowing. Such an object could never shine forth.

The phrase means:

```text
the whole cognitive construction remains active
        ↓
but only its Artha claims attention and validity
        ↓
Knowing is wholly present as transparent manifestation
```

Nirbhāsa is shining or manifestation. Artha is not merely left over after
subtraction. It positively manifests through purified Knowing.

## Fichte's new question: how is Light observed?

In I.42 Fichte distinguished the living Light from its objectification. In
I.43 he turns from what is attributed to Light to the inner form of the
observing itself.

When Light is observed, it is no longer immediately lived. It is present
through a representative or image:

```text
living Light
      ↓ represented
image of Light
      ↓ treated as an object
objectified Light
```

The representative makes scientific consideration possible, but it also
alienates and “kills” the Light when its image is mistaken for the original
Life.

This is the Fichtean form of the problem of memory. Smṛti retains an image
of what was livingly seen. Nirvitarka purifies that image so that it points
through itself instead of claiming primordial status.

## Neither the Light nor its image is the highest Principle

Fichte now refuses to locate the true Oneness in either term.

It is not merely in the Light taken as an object, because objectified Light
is already a projection within observation.

It is not in the image, because an image without what it images is nothing.

```text
something imaged                 image
primordial source                representative
         \                         /
          \                       /
             living relation
           in and between both
```

The Principle rests in the Oneness that lives through their distinction.
This Oneness is enacted in Thinking, not placed before Thinking as one more
object.

## Image and imaged are conceptually inseparable

The relation is stronger than an empirical association.

```text
image → necessarily an image of something
something imaged → manifest as imaged through an image
```

We can begin the construction from either side:

```text
something imaged → image
image → something imaged
```

The sequence changes, but the essential content of the thought does not.
The same relation is fulfilled in both directions.

This is Fichte's conceptual manifestness: Oneness only with disjunction,
and disjunction only within Oneness.

## The content remains one through the reversal

The image and imaged appear to exchange the roles of antecedent and
consequent:

```text
A posits B
B posits A
```

But their truth cannot be obtained by joining two independent propositions
with an unexplained “and.” The terms acquire their determination only
through their positions within the reciprocal relation.

Once the merely external order is removed, one content remains:

```text
the pure, enduring through-one-another
```

This is the Principle of Nirvitarka Samāpatti. The cognitive image becomes
empty of independent self-form because its entire Being consists in
manifesting Artha; Artha shines because its manifestation lives through the
image.

## The primordial Concept

Fichte calls this living unity the primordial Concept.

It is more original than objectified Light because the apparent Light and
its representative first arise as distinguishable terms within this
Concept.

```text
primordial Concept
organic through-one-another
       /              \
imaged Light          image of Light
```

The Concept is not an abstract generality imposed afterward. It has its own
self-identical content and grounds its appearance through its own essential
Being.

Its disjunction conditions its Life and appearance, but does not divide its
content.

This provides a stronger meaning for `artha-mātra`. Artha alone is not a
bare object. It is the self-identical content that remains one throughout
the living differentiation of image and imaged.

## From external reciprocity to organic Oneness

At the preceding level, the relation could look like external reciprocal
influence:

```text
Light qualifies Concept
Concept qualifies the appearance of Light
```

Now the Concept grounds both terms through its own Being:

```text
one self-subsistent content
      ↓ differentiates itself
image ↔ imaged
      ↓
appearance announces inner Being
```

The terms are constructed organically only through one another. Their
relation is not added after they exist; it constitutes what they are.

Nirvitarka therefore discloses more than a cognition purified of words. It
discloses the inner organization of Artha.

## Why this is not an attack on Śabda

The disappearance of Word from the foreground does not make language
worthless. Śabda was required to construct the distinction and guide
attention to Artha.

But the successful Word fulfills itself by becoming transparent:

```text
Śabda awakens construction
      ↓
construction becomes living insight
      ↓
Artha shines without dependence on repetition of Śabda
```

A Scientist of Knowing must therefore be able both to state the exact Word
and to release it. Remaining with the Word produces verbal discipleship;
discarding it prematurely produces indeterminacy.

Nirvitarka is the fulfilled use of the Word.

## Why this is not an attack on Vikalpa

Vikalpa made the moments distinguishable in I.42. Nirvitarka does not return
to an undifferentiated state before distinction.

The distinctions have been comprehended so completely that they no longer
fragment the content:

```text
before distinction
obscure unity

Savitarka
explicit distinction in mixture

Nirvitarka
distinction transparent within genetic unity
```

This is achieved determination, not regression into immediacy.

## Pure and impure Unconditioned

The sequence now becomes precise:

```text
pure Unconditioned
living Light in its immediate self-sufficiency

Savitarka
Light present through the mixture of
Word, Artha, memory, and Knowledge

Nirvitarka
the determinate presentation purified until
only the self-identical Artha shines
```

Nirvitarka remains a determinate Samāpatti and therefore is not simply the
Unconditioned without appearance. It is the impure Unconditioned becoming
transparent to the Pure within it.

## The Science of Citta at I.43

I.43 shows what it means for Citta to know its own operation.

Citta first recognizes that what it takes as the object is inseparable from
its mode of presentation. It then refuses to absolutize that presentation.
Finally it sees the living Principle that generates both presentation and
presented Artha.

```text
Citta remembers an Artha
        ↓
Citta discriminates memory from present manifestation
        ↓
memory becomes transparent
        ↓
Citta appears as though without its own form
        ↓
Artha shines as the self-identical content
of the entire cognitive act
```

This is why the Sūtra belongs naturally to the Science of Knowing. It
specifies the purification by which cognition ceases to conceal its own
Principle.

## A–U–M in Nirvitarka

Using our working specification:

```text
A — Appearance
the image or representative form in which Artha appears

U — Reflection / Thinking
the reversible movement from image to imaged
and from imaged to image

M — Knowing / Concept
the self-identical through-one-another
that comprehends both directions as one content
```

In Savitarka, these moments were explicitly present but mixed. In
Nirvitarka, M comprehends their reciprocity, and therefore A and U no longer
claim independent validity.

Artha alone shines because the complete A–U–M construction has become
transparent—not because Appearance and Reflection have been annihilated.

## The basic practice for Scientists of Knowing

I.43 suggests a strict procedure:

1. Construct the Artha through the exact Word.
2. Notice the remembered forms that arise with it.
3. Distinguish present insight from mere recognition.
4. Treat every image explicitly as an image *of* something.
5. Treat the imaged Artha as manifest only through its image.
6. Reverse the direction of construction and test whether the content
   remains self-identical.
7. Hold the reciprocal movement until its organic Principle shines.
8. Allow the Word and representative form to recede without losing the
   precise Artha they helped construct.

The test is not blankness. The test is whether Artha has become more exact,
more luminous, and less dependent on inherited verbal association.

## Compact synthesis

Yoga gives the purification:

```text
memory purified
      ↓
Citta as though empty of its own form
      ↓
Artha alone shining
      ↓
Nirvitarka Samāpatti
```

Fichte gives its genetic Principle:

```text
the observed Light appears through an image
      ↓
neither image nor imaged is self-sufficient
      ↓
each exists only through the other
      ↓
their order can reverse while their content remains one
      ↓
the primordial Concept is disclosed as
the pure enduring through-one-another
```

The apparent emptiness of Citta is therefore its highest transparency at
this stage. Its cognitive form no longer announces itself as a second thing
beside Artha. Knowing has mastered its representative machinery so fully
that the machinery manifests only its content.

I.43 is the hard achievement promised by I.42: not the abandonment of Word,
memory, distinction, or Citta, but their purification into the living
Concept through which Artha alone can shine.
