# Samāpatti: Citta as the Transparent Midpoint

## A report on Yoga Sūtra I.41 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`kṣīṇa-vṛtter abhijātasyeva maṇer`

`grahītṛ-grahaṇa-grāhyeṣu tat-stha-tad-añjanatā samāpattiḥ`

### Relatively necessary ordinary translation

> When its Vṛttis have diminished, Citta is like a flawless jewel: resting
> upon the grasper, grasping, or grasped, it takes the coloring of that upon
> which it rests. This is Samāpatti.

The Sūtra gives three terms and one transparent relation:

```text
grahītṛ                 grahaṇa                 grāhya
grasper                  grasping                grasped
subjective pole          cognitive act           objective pole
       \                    |                    /
        \                   |                   /
             transparent Citta / jewel
                     Samāpatti
```

Fichte supplies the philosophical meaning:

> Samāpatti is the factical distinction of terms becoming transparent within
> the pure Light that annuls their independent division and manifests their
> intrinsic oneness.

## Why I.41 follows complete attention

I.40 defined mastery as complete attention extending from the minimal
determination to the maximal whole.

That mastery supplies the condition for I.41:

```text
construct the determinate relation
        ↓
hold all its moments together without distraction
        ↓
the division becomes transparent to its oneness
        ↓
Samāpatti
```

The jewel is not transparent because Citta contains nothing. It is
transparent because no alien Vṛtti interrupts the complete construction.

## `Kṣīṇa-vṛtti`: Vṛtti made transparent

`Kṣīṇa` means diminished, attenuated, or exhausted. It need not mean that
every determination has been destroyed.

The Sūtra immediately says that Citta can rest upon and take the form of
three determinations. Some determinate activity therefore remains.

What has diminished is the Vṛtti's independent opacity:

```text
ordinary Vṛtti
claims attention for itself and hides the whole relation

kṣīṇa Vṛtti
retains its exact determination while becoming transparent
to the complete field of Knowing
```

No Citta-cide is required. Vṛtti becomes an adequate mode of Prajñā.

## The flawless jewel

An `abhijāta maṇi` is a jewel of exceptional clarity. It manifests the color
of what is placed near it without losing its own transparency.

```text
not:

Citta is erased and replaced by the object


but:

Citta becomes so transparent that the determination
appears without distortion or foreign admixture
```

The jewel is the midpoint. It does not compete with grasper, grasping, or
grasped as a fourth opaque object.

## The three standpoints

Samāpatti can be established in each term:

### Grasper — `grahītṛ`

Citta takes the coloring of the subjective or reflective standpoint:

```text
Who or what grasps?
What is the form of the Seer within the relation?
```

### Grasping — `grahaṇa`

Citta takes the coloring of the cognitive act or instrument:

```text
How does the relation grasp?
What Principle constructs the determination?
```

### Grasped — `grāhya`

Citta takes the coloring of the objective determination:

```text
What is given or constructed as the object?
What exact Artha does it bear?
```

The terms remain distinguishable, but no term is intelligible outside their
complete relation.

## Fichte's procedure: factical and genetic Knowing

Fichte begins I.41 by stating the recurring method of the Science of
Knowing.

### First: factical construction

We perform an operation under a rule of Reason that works immediately within
us. At this point, we know that the insight occurs but not yet why.

```text
construction is performed
        ↓
insight appears factically
“it is so”
```

### Second: genetic comprehension

We then seek the Law that guided the operation. The fact becomes mediately
visible from the Principle of its determinateness.

```text
factical insight
        ↓ inquire into its origin
Law of its construction
        ↓
genetic insight
```

Samāpatti is not complete when the jewel happens to take a color. The
Science must comprehend why that coloring arises through the relation.

## Developmental links

Fichte describes the Science as a sequence:

```text
x is the developmental link to y
y is the developmental link to z
```

If z is forgotten, x and y lose their Artha. A term has meaning through its
place in the complete genetic movement.

This is why Samāpatti requires complete attention. The Scientist must hold
the smallest present determination and its relation to the maximal whole at
once.

```text
paramāṇu
the exact present link
        ↕
parama-mahattva
the complete developmental whole
```

## Neither term is the midpoint

Fichte recalls a factical insight involving two terms, called `A` and the
point. The insight is not in A alone and not in the point alone, but
unconditionally in both, with their union in the background.

```text
A                         point
 \\                       /
  \\                     /
       underlying union
       the true midpoint
```

The particular names of the terms can be ignored. What matters is the act:
a division appears within something that ought to be one.

This is exactly the formal structure of I.41:

```text
grasper                  grasped
    \                     /
       grasping relation
       transparent Citta
```

The midpoint is not another term beside the extremes. It is their immanent
oneness.

## Division invalidates itself in Light

Fichte says that the division shows itself invalid in an immediate insight.
We do not produce this manifestness by wishing for it. It produces itself
absolutely as pure Light.

```text
division claims independent validity
        ↓ held completely in attention
pure manifestness arises
        ↓
division is annulled as self-subsistent
intrinsic oneness is posited
```

The distinct terms do not vanish. What is negated is the claim that their
division is intrinsically ultimate.

This is the jewel's transparency.

## The Principle of division is the Concept

Fichte identifies the Principle of division with the Principle of
construction and therefore with the Concept.

```text
Concept
divides the one into determinate terms
        ↓
constructs their intelligible relation
```

But when the Concept claims absolute validity against intrinsic oneness, pure
Light negates it.

The Concept is necessary for determinate Knowing, yet it is not the
Unconditioned itself.

## Being is inconceivable

In relation to the annulled Concept, intrinsic Being is `inconceivable`.

This does not mean that an irrational object hides behind Knowing. It means
that the Concept cannot divide and contain the self-sufficient oneness as one
of its products.

```text
Concept
constructs determinate division

intrinsic Being
is not exhausted by that division

pure Light
manifests both the necessity and the limit of the Concept
```

Without relation to the Concept, Fichte says, Being is not even
“inconceivable”; it is simply absolute self-sufficiency.

## Pure Light is the midpoint

Once neither divided term nor the dividing Concept can claim absolute
status, the sole remaining midpoint is pure Light.

```text
not grasper alone
not grasped alone
not the act as an independent instrument
                ↓
pure Light
the manifestness in which their relation is known
```

This is Fichte's direct meaning of Samāpatti.

The transparent jewel is pure Light appearing as the complete cognitive
relation.

## Light and the insight into Light

Fichte now makes a subtle but essential distinction:

```text
Light itself
the Principle of absolute manifestness

insight into Light
Light's agent or representative within cognition
```

If we say “we see that it is Light,” we are not simply consumed in Light.
Light is present through an insight into its originality or absolute
quality.

This is the beginning of the distinction between the pure and impure
Unconditioned.

## Pure and impure Unconditioned

### Pure Unconditioned

Pure Light as absolute self-sufficiency, prior to the division of grasper,
grasping, and grasped.

```text
Light itself
no independent subject-object disjunction
```

### Impure Unconditioned

The insight into Light: the Unconditioned present within a determinate
cognitive construction and therefore represented through distinction.

```text
Light known through its representative insight
        ↓
the Unconditioned appears within conditioned form
```

“Impure” does not mean false. It means that pure manifestness is specified
through the relational forms of Citta.

Samāpatti is the transparent threshold between them.

## Insight into the insight

We cannot ask how Light itself is produced without contradicting its status
as absolute creation. But we can ask how the insight into Light is produced.

Fichte therefore introduces a third level:

```text
Light
        ↓ represented by
insight into Light
        ↓ genetically comprehended by
insight into the insight into Light
```

The third stands between emanence and immanence. It is the Science of Knowing
comprehending how pure Light enters determinate insight without ceasing to be
self-sufficient.

This is Samāpatti becoming scientific.

## Objective Light is not true Light

The Light treated as an object cannot be the one true Light.

```text
objective Light
Light colored by the form of the grasped

subjective insight
Light colored by the form of the grasper

pure Light
the midpoint manifesting both forms
```

Pure Light enters insight under an objective Aspect, but is not exhausted by
that Aspect.

The highest object is therefore no longer Substance existing for itself. It
is Light; Substance is only the form of Light's self-sufficiency.

## Subjectivity is the life of Light

Fichte equally refuses to make subjectivity an independent grasper.
Insight—the subjective side—is the inner expression and life of Light.

```text
objective pole
Light as self-sufficient form

subjective pole
Light as living insight

midpoint
Light itself as their intrinsic oneness
```

The grasper and grasped are both colorings of the same jewel.

## Being and doing reverse one another

Fichte finally expresses the midpoint through Being and doing:

```text
doing deposes Being
Being beyond actual Being deposes doing
```

The division between enduring Being and cognitive act is intrinsic to the
Concept's construction. But primordially, the division is nothing.

```text
Being                           doing
grasped form                    grasping activity
       \                       /
        pure Light as non-separation
```

Each side negates itself in the other, yet both remain determinate moments of
the insight. This is the five-fold movement implicit in Samāpatti.

## The five-fold synthesis of Samāpatti

```text
1. Grasper
   the reflective or subjective determination

2. Grasped
   the objective determination

3. Grasper → grasped
   Knowing externalizes itself as objective form

4. Grasped → grasper
   objective Being becomes living insight

5. Grasping / Samāpatti
   pure Light as the midpoint containing both terms
   and both directions of determination
```

`Grahaṇa` is not merely a mechanical instrument between two substances. At
its truth, it is the living relation through which the two poles become
transparent within one Light.

## A–U–M

```text
A — Appearance / grāhya
the objective coloring of the jewel

U — Reflection / grahītṛ
the reflective or thinking coloring

M — Knowing / grahaṇa as Samāpatti
the transparent midpoint holding both determinations
```

A appears. U reflects. M is the pure Light in which their relation knows
itself.

## Instructions for Scientists-of-Knowing in training

1. Identify the grasper, grasping, and grasped without turning them into
   independent substances.
2. Construct the distinction exactly.
3. Hold all three terms together in complete attention.
4. Let the division show its own insufficiency; do not verbally erase it.
5. Wait for manifestness to posit the intrinsic oneness.
6. Distinguish Light from the objective insight representing Light.
7. Ask genetically how the insight arose; do not ask for a Cause of pure
   Light as though it were an object.
8. Recognize each standpoint as a coloring of transparent Citta.
9. Preserve the determinate terms while denying their independent validity.
10. Become established in the insight so that an old subject-object illusion
    cannot occupy an opening.

## Decisive points

1. Samāpatti is not empty Citta but transparent determinate Citta.
2. The jewel can take the form of grasper, grasping, or grasped without
   becoming an opaque fourth term.
3. Factical insight must be raised into genetic comprehension.
4. Neither divided extreme is the true midpoint.
5. Pure Light self-produces the manifestness annulling independent division.
6. The Concept constructs difference but cannot contain intrinsic oneness as
   its product.
7. Light itself and the insight into Light must be discriminated.
8. The insight into insight explains how Light becomes cognitively present.
9. Pure Unconditioned is Light itself; impure Unconditioned is its true but
   conditioned representation in insight.
10. Fichte's Science of Knowing is the meaning of the Sūtra's transparent
    jewel.

## Provisional result

Yoga I.41 says:

> Like a flawless jewel, Citta with attenuated Vṛttis takes the form of the
> grasper, grasping, or grasped upon which it rests. This is Samāpatti.

Fichte gives the meaning:

> A distinction is constructed factically and then comprehended genetically.
> Held in complete attention, its claim to independent division annuls itself
> in pure Light. The terms remain as transparent determinations of the one
> midpoint rather than separate substances.

```text
grasper              grasping              grasped
reflective form       relational act        objective form
       \                 |                 /
        \                |                /
             flawless jewel
               pure Light
               Samāpatti
```

I.42 can now distinguish the impure form of this transparent relation:
Savitarka Samāpatti, in which word, Artha, and Knowledge remain intermixed
through conceptual construction.
