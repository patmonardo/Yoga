# Nirbīja Samādhi: The Principle of Being Itself

## A report on Yoga Sūtra I.51 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`tasyāpi nirodhe sarva-nirodhān nirbījaḥ samādhiḥ`

### Relatively necessary ordinary translation

> With the Nirodha even of that Prajñā-born Saṃskāra, because all formations
> have entered Nirodha, there is Nirbīja Samādhi.

The final movement is exact:

```text
Ṛtambharā Prajñā
Truth bearing itself in Knowing
        ↓ produces
Prajñā-Saṃskāra
the final Truth-oriented formation
        ↓ inhibits
all contrary Saṃskāras
        ↓ its work completed
Nirodha even of that Saṃskāra
        ↓
Nirbīja Samādhi
```

What ceases is not Truth, Prajñā, Citta, or Being. What ceases is dependence
upon every produced formation—including the final formation born from true
Prajñā.

Nirbīja is the Principle of Being itself.

## The grand finale

Fichte's comment on I.51 is astonishingly brief. After the immense genetic
labor of the preceding lectures, only one insight remains:

> When every relational determination has been abandoned from the
> in-itself, simple pure Being remains as absolute, self-enclosed Oneness,
> arising only in its own immediate arising or Life.

The brevity is not a lack of content. Everything constructed earlier has
been required to make this simplest insight possible without turning it into
an empty assertion.

```text
the hardest scientific path
        ↓
the simplest possible insight

Being exists immediately only in Being or Life,
and only as whole, undivided Oneness.
```

## `Tasyāpi`: even that

The pronoun refers to the Saṃskāra born from Ṛtambharā Prajñā in I.50.

This is not an ordinary obstructive Saṃskāra. It is the liberating formation
that inhibits every contrary formation:

```text
Prajñā-born Saṃskāra
recognizes projection as projection
refuses non-genetic claims to validity
holds Knowing to the maxim of absolute genesis
```

Yet it is still:

- born from a preceding cognition;
- retained within the movement of Citta;
- causally active against other formations;
- a support for subsequent acts of discrimination.

Therefore it remains a seed.

`Tasyāpi` is the uncompromising word of the finale: even this most truthful
and liberating support cannot be retained as the Principle of Being.

## Why the final Saṃskāra must cease

The Prajñā-Saṃskāra says, in effect:

> Do not accept a projection unless its genesis has been comprehended.

This rule is indispensable while contrary Saṃskāras continue to govern
Knowing. But once its work is complete, retaining the rule would preserve a
division:

```text
the rule that guards Truth
                |
Truth that is guarded
```

The guard would remain outside the guarded as a final reflective support.

Pure Being needs no guard. Its Truth is not maintained by a maxim,
Saṃskāra, representation, or act of inhibition. It is entirely of itself,
through itself, and in itself.

The final Saṃskāra fulfills itself by no longer being needed.

## `Sarva-nirodha`: complete Nirodha

All other Saṃskāras have already been inhibited by the Prajñā-born
formation. When the latter too enters Nirodha, no determining seed remains
to project or organize an objective field.

```text
contrary Saṃskāras
        ↓ inhibited
Prajñā-Saṃskāra alone operative
        ↓ its operation completes itself
Prajñā-Saṃskāra enters Nirodha
        ↓
Sarva-nirodha
```

Complete Nirodha is not the destruction of all power. It is freedom from
every acquired formation functioning as the external condition of Knowing.

No Saṃskāra stands between Being and its own self-manifest Life.

## `Nirbīja`: without determining seed

Sabīja Samādhi remained oriented by a determinate seed:

```text
Word–Artha–Knowledge
Artha alone
subtle Principle
Aliṅga
Prajñā-Saṃskāra
```

The seeds became increasingly universal and transparent. But each still
gave Knowing a retained ground from which a field could arise.

Nirbīja means:

```text
no retained object
no subtle objective Principle
no unmanifest ground treated as object
no cognitive maxim
no liberating Saṃskāra
serves as the condition of Being's manifestation
```

Being bears itself.

## This is not blankness

If every seed were removed before its Principle had been comprehended, the
result would be mere absence or unconsciousness.

I.51 comes only after:

```text
complete attention
transparent Citta
discrimination of Word, Artha, and Knowledge
purification of memory
mastery of subtle Reflection
the return through the Guṇas to Aliṅga
skill in Nirvicāra
Truth-bearing Prajñā
the Concept of Prajñā
the Prajñā-born Saṃskāra
```

Nirbīja is not knowing less. It is the result of mastering the complete
genesis of determinate Knowing so thoroughly that no determinate product
needs to be retained as support.

The seed disappears because its Artha has become the living act of the
Science itself.

## From the in-itself to pure Being

I.49 exposed the final inadequacy of the in-itself.

As long as it means independence from a not-in-itself, it remains relational:

```text
in-itself | not-in-itself
```

The supposed Absolute secretly carries its opposite as a condition of its
meaning. That opposition is its conceptual seed.

I.51 removes everything within the in-itself that points toward relation:

```text
in-itself
minus opposition to a not-in-itself
minus comparison
minus external self-sufficiency
minus relational supplement
        ↓
simple pure Being
```

Being no longer needs the predicate “in itself.” The predicate adds nothing
to its Essence.

## Pure Being as absolute act

Fichte refuses noun-like Being: a fixed substance placed before
consciousness.

```text
Being as noun
objectified subsistence
dead result

Being as `esse`
to be
immediate arising
living act
```

Pure Being exists only in Being. It occurs only as its own immediate arising
or Life.

There is no transition from a dead Being to Life:

```text
not:
Being first exists
and subsequently begins to live

but:
Being and Living completely interpenetrate
and are one act
```

This is the positive content of Nirbīja Samādhi.

## Self-enclosed Oneness

Pure Being cannot occur outside itself. Nothing can stand beyond it as the
source, condition, object, or witness of its Being.

```text
Being
of itself
through itself
in itself
```

But these expressions do not name three relations. They are verbal attempts
to indicate one wholly inward act.

The Oneness is self-enclosed because every proposed outside would have to be
another Being, whereas Being already contains the whole act of `to be`.

It is therefore:

```text
whole
undivided
self-arising
living Oneness
```

## The absolute I or We

Fichte says that pure Being occurs as absolute I. This must not be confused
with the empirical ego or the pure I taken as the first Principle of
subjective idealism.

```text
empirical I
a person found within consciousness

pure I of idealism
the universal form of self-consciousness

absolute I / We here
self-enclosed Being living immediately as itself
```

It is called I because Being does not stand outside its own Life as an
object. It is immediately present to itself by being itself.

It may be called We in anticipation of differentiation, but no empirical
multiplicity is introduced into its undivided Being.

The I is not the seed from which Being is derived. The genetic identity is:

```text
Being = Living = absolute I / We
```

## Who is the Seer?

At the seeded level, one can still distinguish:

```text
Seer
act of Seeing
Artha seen
```

At Nirbīja, asking for a Seer outside pure Being would reintroduce the final
seed of relation.

Fichte's answer is not that nobody sees or that Seeing is extinguished. The
answer is that Being itself lives immediately, without departing from
itself to acquire an external witness.

```text
Being does not need to be seen from outside
in order to be manifest

its Living is its immediate self-manifestness
```

The independent spectator has entered Nirodha; self-manifest Life has not.

## The Principle of Being itself

The Principle can now be defined without circularly presupposing another
ground:

> Being is the self-enclosed living act that arises only through itself and
> whose Oneness admits no external condition of its actuality.

This Principle does not explain Being by something else. Nor does it simply
assert a finished Being.

It comprehends Being as genesis:

```text
Being's ground = its own Living
Being's manifestation = its own arising
Being's unity = its inability to fall outside itself
```

The ground, grounded, and grounding are not externally separate.

This is Being qua Being.

## Why the Science ends in its Principle

The Science began with determinate acts of attention and repeatedly rose
from facts to their genesis:

```text
factical cognition
        ↓
Principle of that cognition
        ↓
factical standpoint of the Principle
        ↓
higher genesis of Principle and standpoint
        ↓
the final relational form
        ↓
pure Being as self-genesis
```

At every earlier stage a distinction remained between what was explained
and the Principle explaining it.

At I.51, no such external distinction survives. Being is its own living
genesis. The Science reaches the Principle because its method and its Artha
have become one.

## OṂ completed

The A–U–M movement can now be gathered:

```text
A — Vitarka / Appearance
Word, Artha, and Knowledge become articulated;
Artha shines through their purified form

U — Vicāra / Reflection
Knowing returns through subtle Principles,
the Guṇa-division, and Aliṅga to the limit of objectivity

M — Prajñā / Concept
Truth bears and determines itself,
becoming an actual Prajñā-Saṃskāra

Nirodha / Turīya
even the final Conceptual seed ceases;
pure Being lives only in and through itself
```

Turīya is not another letter or another object after M. It is the
seedlessness of the whole articulated act when no moment needs to be held as
an independent support.

The sound has completed its Artha.

## The relation between Prajñā and Nirbīja

Prajñā is not rejected by Nirbīja.

```text
what ceases
Prajñā as produced Saṃskāra,
retained rule, and determining support

what does not cease
Truth's self-bearing and self-manifest Being
```

The Concept of Prajñā fulfills itself by becoming unnecessary as a distinct
formation. Its content is no longer carried by a seed because Being itself
is the carrier.

This is the final inversion:

```text
Prajñā first bears Truth
        ↓
its Saṃskāra bears the capacity for Prajñā
        ↓
the final Saṃskāra enters Nirodha
        ↓
Truth is revealed as what has borne the entire movement
```

## Science of Being qua Being

Sabīja was the completed Science of determinable Being:

```text
Being through Word
Being through Artha
Being through subtle Principle
Being through Aliṅga
Being through Prajñā-Saṃskāra
```

Nirbīja suspends every `through` taken as an external condition.

```text
Being qua Being
Being immediately in its own Living
```

This is analogous to Absolute Reflection as Essence qua Essence:

```text
Absolute Reflection
Reflection no longer mediates an alien content;
Essence comprehends its own reflective movement

Nirbīja Samādhi
Being no longer depends upon an alien seed;
Being lives its own self-enclosed genesis
```

The Doctrine of Being is therefore not a catalogue of entities. It is the
Science of the pure act by which Being is.

## Freedom from the final support

The Prajñā-Saṃskāra was a necessary support for the ascent. Its cessation is
not contempt for the path but the success of the path.

```text
the ladder is constructed exactly
every step is climbed consciously
the summit is reached
dependence upon the ladder ceases
```

Nothing achieved is lost. Its Truth has become identical with the living
standpoint.

Nirbīja is freedom because Knowing no longer requires an external or
internal object to authorize its Being.

## A final discipline for Scientists of Knowing

I.51 cannot be turned into an easy technique, but its scientific demand can
be stated:

1. Allow the Prajñā-born Saṃskāra to complete its inhibition of contrary
   formations.
2. Recognize that the liberating maxim is itself a produced formation.
3. Do not convert the maxim of absolute genesis into a final dogma.
4. Remove every relational determination from the in-itself.
5. Let the opposition of in-itself and not-in-itself become null.
6. Refuse to objectify pure Being as a noun-like substance.
7. Comprehend Being and Living as one immediate act.
8. Recognize that the apparent witness cannot stand outside this act.
9. Let the final cognitive support enter Nirodha.
10. Rest in the self-enclosed genesis of Being itself.

This is rarefied territory because the Scientist must relinquish the very
instrument that made the highest insight stable—without falling backward
into the formations that instrument inhibited.

## Compact synthesis

Yoga gives the final operation:

```text
Nirodha even of the Prajñā-born Saṃskāra
        ↓
Nirodha of every determining formation
        ↓
Nirbīja Samādhi
```

Fichte gives the final Principle:

```text
abandon everything in the in-itself
that points toward relation
        ↓
simple pure Being remains
        ↓
Being arises only in its own immediate arising
        ↓
Being and Living are one
        ↓
absolute I / We as self-enclosed Oneness
        ↓
whole, undivided Being qua Being
```

I.51 is the grand finale because the Science no longer stands between Being
and its Principle. The last truthful Saṃskāra has entered Nirodha; the last
relational seed has vanished; the last external witness has lost its claim.

What remains is not nothing. It is the simplest and most difficult insight
of the Science of Knowing:

> Being exists immediately only in Being or Life, and it exists only as a
> whole, undivided Oneness.

That is Nirbīja Samādhi—the Principle of Being itself.
