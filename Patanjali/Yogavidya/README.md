# Yogavidya — Science of Absolute Knowing

Yogavidya is the master-level course work of **Organon Yoga**. It develops
the author's Form : Essence : Principle : System through Yoga Vidyā and the
Fichte–Hegel studies. Its intended audience is friends while the work develops.

The [Kośa-focused Yoga Sūtra commentary](../YS/README.md) establishes textual and comparative ground for part of this course work. Yogavidya can take up the author's interpretations of sublation, *saṃvṛti : paramārtha*, samāpatti, the Bhavacakra and Dharmacakra, and the later transformation of memory and *saṃskāra*. Each such claim should retain its source anchors and be identified as the author's determination rather than attributed to Patañjali or Vasubandhu.

The Fichte and Hegel reports in this directory are developing course material. Their presence does not imply that the Loka : Karma upgrades have been completed.

The [Māṇḍūkya study of OṂ and its *artha*](Prajnapada/mandukya-om-artha-study.md)
begins an inquiry prompted by Yoga Sūtra I.27–29. It reads the
Upaniṣad and Śaṅkara directly before developing the Organon comparison.

The [I.40 essay on mastery and the Kośa's *samāpatti*
analysis](Prajnapada/ys-i-40-range-and-samapatti.md) begins the companion
essay sequence for I.40–51. These essays develop the key Yogavidya topics
for a later Vārttika while the paired sūtra studies remain concise.

The [Organon Sāṃkhya report area](../../Docs/Samkhya/README.md)
holds the separate Sāṃkhya source work needed before expanding earlier
Yoga studies, including the question of *aiśvarya* and the immeasurables.
