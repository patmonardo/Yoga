# Eka Citta Is Not Harmed

## Fichte and Hegel on Yoga Sutra IV.11

`hetu-phala-asraya-alambana-sangrhitatvad esam abhave tad-abhava`

> Because the Vāsanās are held together by cause, result, support, and object,
> when these are absent, the Vāsanās are absent.

## Plain meaning

IV.11 states a specification of conditioned knowledge processing:

```text
INPUTS AND SUPPORTS
    Hetu        causal formation
    Phala       consequential reinforcement
    Āśraya      locus of conditioned operation
    Ālambana    presented object
          |
          | Saṃgraha: collection into one operative complex
          v
    Vāsanā-conditioned cognition
```

If the four are absent, the corresponding conditioned operation is absent.
The Sutra does not include Eka Citta among the items negated.

## Fichte-Bhasya

Fichte identifies the fourfold complex with derivative knowing: cognition
explains itself through an origin, a result, a bearer, and an opposed object.
This reconstruction contradicts itself when it claims merely to seek an
insight that it already enacts.

The contradiction is resolved when light and genesis are recognized as one.
The supporting We and opposed object cease to stand as independent terms.
Their derivative construction is absent, but pure light remains.

> Fichte shows why the conditioned processor can fall away without the loss
> of knowing.

## Hegel-Varttika

Hegel shows that the four conditions are not external accessories attached to
a separately existing Vāsanā. They are its complete conditioned content. The
Vāsanā comes forth when they recollect themselves into one determinate fact;
without them, there is no such fact.

Ground does not survive underneath as a hidden substrate. It disappears as a
separate mediator in the fact it grounds. Likewise, when the conditions are
absent, no latent Vāsanā-substance remains waiting behind them.

> Hegel shows why the absence is determinate rather than annihilative.

## Combined determination

```text
Fichte                        Hegel

remove the independence      remove the apparent separation
of subject and object        of ground and conditions
          \                    /
           \                  /
            v                v
       conditioned Vāsanā-processing is absent
                         |
                         v
       Eka Citta remains as the unity of knowing
```

Eka Citta is isomorphic to the pāramārthika standpoint because it is not one
of the conditioned Cittas or one of their supporting factors. It is the unity
within which their construction, manifestation, continuity, and absence are
known.

This can be stated in the vocabulary developed through IV.2-11:

```text
Asmitā      supplies the repeatable form:          I am
Vāsanā      supplies the conditioned specification: I am thus
Eka Citta   supplies the synthetic unity:           I think these together
IV.11       removes the conditions of:               I am thus
```

It does not remove the `I think`.

## Completion of Reflection

The ten retained Sutras of Reflection now form one account of Citta-Dharma:

1. Nature fills out determinate forms.
2. External occasions do not supply nature's power.
3. Cittas are constructed through I-am-ness.
4. Eka Citta is one through their differentiated activity.
5. Dhyana generates a Citta without karmic deposit.
6. Its action does not produce colored maturation.
7. Congruent Vāsanās selectively manifest.
8. Smrti and Samskara preserve one form through intervals.
9. The wish to continue makes the Vāsanā-series beginningless.
10. The series is nevertheless absent when its four supports are absent.

The final result is not the death of Citta:

> Reflection completes itself when Citta knows the conditions of its own
> conditioned processing and can let that processing be absent without
> mistaking its absence for the absence of knowing.

That is the threshold of Appearance. Eka Citta does not vanish. Freed from
the Vāsanā-complex, it can let essence appear without compulsively specifying
appearance through inherited conditioning.
