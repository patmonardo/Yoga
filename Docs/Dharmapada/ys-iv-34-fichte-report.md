# Citi-Śakti and the Seed of the Absolute Relation

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.34 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`puruṣārtha-śūnyānāṃ guṇānāṃ pratiprasavaḥ kaivalyaṃ`

`svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā vā citi-śaktir iti`

### Simple translation

> Kaivalya is the return to source of the Guṇas when they are empty of any
> further Artha for Puruṣa; or, Citi-Śakti is established in its own form.

The two clauses give the same completion from opposite sides:

```text
objective side
the Guṇas have no further Puruṣa-Artha
and return to their source
                  ↕
        one Absolute Relation
                  ↕
subjective side
Citi-Śakti stands established
in its own form
```

This is the Seed of the Absolute Relation.

## The terms

- `puruṣa-artha-śūnyānām`: of those empty of further purpose or Artha for
  Puruṣa;
- `guṇānām`: of the Guṇas;
- `pratiprasavaḥ`: counter-production, return to origin, or reversal of
  productive emergence;
- `kaivalyam`: Kaivalya, complete independence or self-standing;
- `svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā`: establishment in one's own form;
- `vā`: or—another complete formulation of the same actuality;
- `citi-śaktiḥ`: the Power of Consciousness as Knowing;
- `iti`: thus—the closing word of the Yoga Sūtra.

Yoga ends by naming the Principle whose complete Science it has presented:

> Citi-Śakti.

## `Śūnya` here means accomplished purpose

The Guṇas are not empty because manifestation has always been worthless.
IV.32 already said they are `kṛtārtha`: their Artha has been accomplished.

```text
before fulfillment
the Guṇas bear Puruṣa-Artha
by articulating the complete field of experience and discrimination

after fulfillment
no further Puruṣa-Artha remains to be produced
```

Their emptiness is the completion of their positive function.

```text
kṛtārtha
Artha accomplished
       ↓
puruṣārtha-śūnya
empty of any further task
```

This is not nihilism. Nothing is missing because the purpose has been
fulfilled.

## `Pratiprasava`: production returns to source

Prakṛti's ordinary movement is productive:

```text
Guṇas → Buddhi → Ahaṃkāra → Manas → sensible world
```

`Pratiprasava` reverses the explanatory direction:

```text
sensible world → Manas → Ahaṃkāra → Buddhi → Guṇas
                                                ↓
                                      productive sequence rests
```

The products are returned into comprehension of their Principles. Nature no
longer has to produce another determination in order to disclose her Artha.

Thus the Guṇas become nonproductive without becoming unintelligible or
absolutely nonexistent.

## Kaivalya is not abandonment by Nature

The objective formulation can sound as though Puruṣa simply walks away from
Prakṛti. But the second clause prevents that external interpretation.

Kaivalya is also:

> Citi-Śakti established in its own form.

```text
not:

Puruṣa here       abandoned Nature there


but:

the complete objective production returns to its Principle
because Knowing has become self-established
```

Puruṣa remains a Spectral Tattva, the invariant Seer-Aspect. Prakṛti remains
the articulated objective Aspect. Their completed Artha is held within the
self-establishment of Citi-Śakti.

## Why this is the Seed of the Absolute Relation

An ordinary relation presupposes two independently constituted terms and
then connects them:

```text
term A ── external relation ── term B
```

The Absolute Relation produces and comprehends its terms through the
relation itself:

```text
              one living Principle
                       |
              Absolute Relation
                 /           \
                /             \
      Puruṣa-Aspect         Prakṛti-Aspect
      self-established      fulfilled production
```

Neither side is intelligible outside the complete movement. The return of
the Guṇas and the self-establishment of Citi-Śakti are not two independent
events. Each is the truth of the other.

IV.34 is a seed because it compresses this entire relation into two clauses.
The full Doctrine of the Absolute Relation can unfold what Yoga presents
here in Sūtra form.

## The whole Dharmapāda is Citi-Śakti

The complete fourth Pāda can now be read retrospectively:

```text
Reflection — IV.2–11
Citta's genesis, plurality, Vāsanā, and Saṃskāra

Appearance — IV.12–21
the laws through which Things and Cittas appear

Actuality — IV.22–34
Buddhi knows itself and Citi-Śakti becomes self-established
```

This is why Dharmapāda is Citi-Śakti. Dharma is the holding through which an
Artha receives Being and truth. Citi-Śakti is Consciousness as the power of
such Knowing.

The Pāda begins from determinate Citta-production and ends with the Power
that has been holding the entire sequence together.

## Fichte's Bhāṣya: the remaining illusion

At the end of IV.33, Fichte discovered that the supposedly primordial Law
was still reconstructed by the Scientist. Knowing appeared divided into an
original and its copy, but both were reconstructions, one of which concealed
its status.

The remaining task is to show:

> The Law itself posits itself in us.

```text
not:

the Scientist reconstructs the Absolute Law


but:

the Absolute Law constructs its own appearance
within the Scientist of Knowing
```

Only then does arbitrariness fall away and Absolute Necessity become actual.

## Image as Image

Fichte's crucial premise is `the image as image`.

An image intrinsically refers beyond its isolated existence:

```text
image       refers to content
copy        refers to an original
reconstruction refers to a prior construction
```

This reference is not externally added to an otherwise complete object. It
belongs to what an image is.

Therefore, the higher Law is already virtually present within the Image as
its constitutive relation.

```text
Image as Image
      ↓ intrinsically points through itself
Law virtually present in its structure
      ↓
the Law posits itself ideally in us
```

Fichte concludes that the Law itself constructs or posits itself within the
Scientist. The Image is the place of the Law's self-manifestation.

## Knowing stands between original and reconstruction

Fichte now corrects the earlier duality. Knowing stands neither in the
reconstruction alone nor in an inaccessible original Thing-in-itself.

It stands wholly between them:

```text
original                    reconstruction
Thing-in-itself             representation
          \                 /
           \               /
              Image as Image
          the Law arises within it
```

This `between` is not a third object inserted between two others. It is the
living relation through which original, Image, and Law become determinate.

That is the Fichtean Seed of the Absolute Relation.

## Light inhabits its living

Fichte says:

> The Light lives what it is in its very self; it inhabits its living.

Light is not first a substance and later an image-maker. It is living,
self-enclosed imaging.

```text
Light
does not possess an imaging activity

Light's living
is the immanent act of imaging itself as Image
```

The Image then posits a Law of its imaging. Image and Law posit one another
through their inner essence without an external supplement.

This is `svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā`: the Power of Knowing does not leave itself to
produce its Image. It inhabits and sustains its own living form.

## The synthetic cycle

Fichte says that the terms form a synthetic cycle into which nothing else
can enter:

```text
Light lives as imaging
        ↓
Image posits itself as Image
        ↓
Image posits the Law of imaging
        ↓
Law explains and produces the Image
        ↓
Light lives as imaging
```

The cycle is closed but not dead. It is the self-comprehending activity of
Knowing.

This is the completed relation of Dharma:

```text
Citi-Śakti holds Artha as true
Artha manifests the Law of its holding
the Law establishes Citi-Śakti in its own form
```

## The Science of Knowing disappears as a particular science

At this height, Fichte says that the Science of Knowing as a particular kind
of knowing has disappeared. What has been derived is one pure Knowing in its
absolute disjunction.

```text
before
a particular scientist studies Absolute Knowing

now
the Science is Absolute Knowing knowing itself
```

“We” are Absolute Knowing only insofar as the Science itself is Absolute
Knowing. The empirical personality does not acquire ownership of the
Absolute.

This corresponds to `puruṣārtha-śūnya`: no private purpose remains for which
the Science must continue producing distinctions.

## Why the particular Science must reappear

Fichte does not remain in an undifferentiated Absolute. We were not always
living this one insight; we ascended to it through a determinate sequence.
That ascent must itself be genetically explained.

The question is not the origin of Absolute Knowing in itself, because it has
no origin. It is:

> How does Absolute Knowing actually exist and appear in us as the
> particular Science of Knowing?

```text
Absolute Knowing in itself
originless
          ↓ must explain
its actual appearance in finite life
          ↓
Science of Knowing in specie
```

This is why the Absolute Relation is a seed rather than a terminal blank.
Its self-establishment contains the genesis of its finite appearance.

## Ordinary Knowing and Absolute Knowing

Fichte suggests that ordinary knowing may be the primordial condition for
the appearance of Absolute Knowing.

The two ends clarify each other:

```text
ordinary Knowing              Absolute Knowing
finite Citta                  self-grounding Citi-Śakti
              \              /
               \            /
      genesis of Absolute Knowing's
           existence in finite life
```

Ordinary cognition is not garbage to be discarded after liberation. It is a
necessary moment in the genetic appearance of the Science.

This is another reason Yoga cannot be Citta-cide.

## Absolute purpose

Fichte states the outcome of his doctrine with extraordinary force:

> Simple existence does not have its ground in itself, but in an Absolute
> purpose: that Absolute Knowing should be.

Everything is posited and determined through this purpose and reaches its
true destination only through its attainment.

```text
all finite existence
      ↓ receives ground and destination from
Absolute purpose
      ↓
Absolute Knowing should become actual
```

This corresponds directly to `Puruṣa-Artha`. The objective sequence exists
for the realization of Knowing. When Absolute Knowing is actual, the Guṇas
are empty of further purpose because the purpose has been fulfilled.

## The ladder and OṂ-Artha

Fichte distinguishes Absolute Knowing from the particular Science used to
reach it. The latter has instrumental value. Once one has arrived, one no
longer worries about the ladder.

```text
Science of Knowing in specie
the reconstructable path or ladder
                    ↓
Absolute Knowing
the intrinsic Artha of the path
```

The ladder is not false. Its complete sequence is indispensable. But its
purpose is not endless commentary upon itself; it is the actuality of
OṂ-Artha, the complete intelligibility of All This.

## Good Karma and constructive Dharma

The constructive character of Dharma makes Karma genuinely important.
Action is not an external aftereffect added to Knowing. It embodies a way of
holding Artha as true and deposits that form within actuality.

```text
insight into Dharma
        ↓ determines
Kriyā
        ↓ constructs and reinforces
the actuality in which further Knowing occurs
```

Bad Karma objectifies an uncomprehended contradiction. Good Karma gives
objective existence to a determination coherent with the living Law.

Fichte therefore insists that right conduct arises on its own from genuine,
living Knowledge. When Light has dawned inwardly, it necessarily shines
outwardly.

```text
clear insight
      ↓ without external compulsion
right action with love and pleasure
      ↓
the act is self-sufficient and rewards itself
```

This is stronger than prudential calculation or obedience to a hated Law.
Good Karma is the outward self-expression of Knowing's inward clarity.

## Kriyā-Jñāna

IV.34 therefore joins theoretical and practical Reason:

```text
Jñāna
Citi-Śakti knows and establishes its own form
                    ↓
Kriyā
the living Law constructs its adequate actuality
```

Kriyā without Jñāna reproduces hidden Kleśa. Jñāna without Kriyā would fail
to express its living constructive power. In their Middle Way, Good Karma is
Knowing becoming objective without becoming alienated from its Principle.

## Yoga and Fichte

| Yoga IV.34 | Fichte |
|---|---|
| the Guṇas are empty of further Puruṣa-Artha | finite existence is grounded in the purpose that Absolute Knowing should be |
| the Guṇas return to their source | reconstruction returns into the Law that produces it |
| Citi-Śakti is established in its own form | Light lives what it is and inhabits its living |
| Kaivalya has objective and subjective formulations | Knowing stands in the living relation of Image and Law |
| the Absolute Relation produces its own terms | Image and Law posit one another through their inner essence |
| the Science culminates in Absolute Knowing | particular science disappears into one pure Knowing |
| ordinary knowing remains genetically necessary | Absolute Knowing must explain its appearance in finite life |
| knowledge necessarily becomes right action | inward Light shines outwardly as self-sufficient conduct |

## The Seed of the Absolute Relation

The final Sūtra can now be compressed into one diagram:

```text
                       CITI-ŚAKTI
                 living Power of Knowing
                            |
                 establishes its own form
                            |
                   ABSOLUTE RELATION
                    /               \
                   /                 \
          Puruṣa-Artha              Guṇas
          purpose fulfilled         production completed
                   \                 /
                    \               /
                      PRATIPRASAVA
              objective return into Principle
                            |
                         KAIVALYA
```

Fichte supplies the inner mechanism:

```text
Light lives as self-enclosed imaging
              ↓
Image as Image virtually contains and posits Law
              ↓
Law posits itself and its Image within us
              ↓
Absolute Knowing becomes actual in finite life
```

The Yoga Sūtra gives the seed; the Science of Knowing unfolds its genetic
structure.

## Final result

YS IV.34 says:

> When the objective articulation of Nature has completely fulfilled the
> Artha of Knowing, its productive sequence returns into its Principle. This
> same actuality, stated from within, is Citi-Śakti established in its own
> form.

Fichte shows why this is an Absolute Relation rather than an external union.
Knowing stands neither in an inaccessible original nor in a detached
representation. It stands in the living Image as Image, where the Law is
virtually present and posits itself.

The Law constructs the Scientist, the Image, and their relation. Absolute
Knowing then explains its own appearance through ordinary finite Knowing.
Its truth necessarily becomes constructive action.

The whole Dharmapāda is therefore the Science of Citi-Śakti:

```text
Citta's determinations
        ↓ become transparent through
the Science of Knowing
        ↓ fulfills
Puruṣa-Artha
        ↓ completes
the Guṇas' productive sequence
        ↓ establishes
Citi-Śakti in its own form
```

The text closes not with the death of Citta, but with the Power of Knowing
comprehending the complete construction through which it became actual:

> `svarūpa-pratiṣṭhā vā citi-śaktir iti`.
