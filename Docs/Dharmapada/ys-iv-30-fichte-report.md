# Kleśa and Karma Cease

## A report on Yoga Sūtra IV.30 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`tataḥ kleśa-karma-nivṛttiḥ`

### Simple translation

> From that, Kleśa and Karma cease.

- `tataḥ`: from Dharma-Megha Samādhi;
- `kleśa`: the formative contradictions that afflict knowing;
- `karma`: action generated and governed by those formations;
- `nivṛttiḥ`: cessation or turning back from operation.

This is a necessary consequence, not a new command:

```text
Dharma-Megha Samādhi
          ↓
Kleśa and Karma cease
```

The Yogi is not instructed to begin another campaign against affliction.
The conditions allowing afflicted action to reproduce itself no longer
govern Knowing.

## Why Kleśa and Karma belong together

Kleśa is the formative contradiction; Karma is that contradiction enacted
and reproduced objectively.

```text
Kleśa
a conditioned determination is held as self-grounding truth
                     ↓
Ahaṃkāra appropriates and acts from it
                     ↓
Karma
the contradiction becomes objective and generates consequences
```

Karma produces Saṃskāras supporting further afflicted Pratyayas:

```text
Kleśa → Karma → Saṃskāra → Pratyaya → renewed Kleśa
   ↑                                      |
   └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

IV.30 breaks this circuit at its Principle. When no determination can be
appropriated outside continuous Viveka, Kleśa cannot ground action.

## `Nivṛtti` is not the end of activity

The Sūtra does not say that every action stops. Bodies move, Manas determines
objects, speech occurs, and the Science of Knowing continues.

What ceases is `kleśa-karma`: action whose Principle is an uncomprehended
afflictive formation.

```text
activity as such                    Kleśa-Karma

determinate expression             action governed by contradiction
of Knowing                         and self-appropriation

does not cease                     ceases
```

This matters for Kriyā-Jñāna. Liberation is not incapacity. It is the
cessation of action driven by a concealed and contradictory ground.

## The consequence of relative necessity

IV.29 gave the conditional structure:

```text
continuous Viveka
        +
no investment in its result
        ↓
Dharma-Megha Samādhi
```

IV.30 states what follows:

```text
Dharma-Megha
the complete field is saturated by its intelligible Principle
        ↓
no opening remains for an afflictive formation
to claim independent authority
        ↓
Kleśa-Karma has no operative ground
```

Necessity now determines what can no longer occur as a governing Principle.

## Fichte's Bhāṣya: Certainty is living Principle

Fichte begins from the result of IV.29:

> Certainty, or Light, is an immediately living Principle.

Its qualitative oneness cannot be described further. It can only be carried
out.

```text
not:

an observer possesses a definition of certainty


but:

certainty lives and performs its own qualitative oneness
```

This is why Fichte calls it the eternal immediate `I`. This `I` is not the
empirical personality. It is the immanent form in which living Knowing is
immediately present to itself.

## Description and immediacy

When Fichte says “certainty is a living Principle,” he has begun describing
what is intrinsically self-enclosed. Description objectifies the Principle
and therefore appears to contradict its immediacy.

But `principle-providing` is certainty's primordial effect within us:

```text
pure immanent Certainty
          ↓ its primordial effect
provides and projects a Principle
          ↓
becomes describable within the Science of Knowing
```

Expression is not imposed from outside. Certainty describes itself through
the Scientist of Knowing because the Scientist's living activity is Light.

## Immanent self-projection without a gap

Fichte identifies `principle-providing` with immanent self-projection. This
does not occur across an interval separating subject and object:

```text
not:

Light ── gap ──► image of Light


but:

Light's living = making itself projection and intuition
```

Fichte describes this as an inward and essential transformation. All Light
is immediately self-creating and therefore immediately intuiting.

The absence of a gap is the decisive connection with IV.30.

## The opening required by Kleśa

A Kleśa governs only when a separation opens between Knowing and the
Principle of its determination:

```text
Knowing          gap          determination
   |                           appears independent
   └──── concealed genesis ────────────┘
```

Within that gap, Ahaṃkāra appropriates the determination, and action proceeds
as though the conditioned formation were self-grounding.

But in pure immanent self-projection:

```text
Knowing = living Principle = projection of itself
```

No alien determination can acquire an independent ground. The Kleśa-Karma
circuit loses its Principle and ceases.

## The Science cannot escape this fate

Fichte says that even the Science of Knowing, in all its living activity,
cannot avoid the fate of self-projection.

```text
wherever Knowing lives
        ↓ necessarily
it provides and projects a Principle
        ↓
it becomes intuition of itself
```

The Science does not manufacture this necessity by choosing the right
method. Its method works because it is already a manifestation of Light's
necessary life. The factical scientist is displaced as the ground of the
Science.

## `Buddhi-eva` as slate cleaner

Seen from Buddhi alone, every cognition is known as an expression of Knowing.
No additional substrate is required, and no Pratyaya can claim to be the
independent Self.

```text
ordinary cognition
“I, this constructed subject, possess and cause the cognition”

                    ↓ Buddhi-eva

Science of Knowing
the cognition is Light's determinate self-projection
```

This cleans the slate by removing the hidden owner that converts
determination into attachment and action into Karma. It does not erase the
intelligible content.

## Dharma-Megha and Citi-Śakti

Dharma-Megha is the actuality in which the complete field is saturated by
the self-grounding form of certainty. Citi-Śakti holds its determinations as
true without becoming displaced into them.

```text
Citi-Śakti
the living power of Knowing
        ↓ self-projects
Dharma
determinate Artha held in truth
        ↓ without appropriation
Kriyā
expression no longer governed by Kleśa-Karma
```

The saintly state is therefore a consequence of the Science of Knowing. Its
purity is the practical actuality of a theoretically comprehended Principle.

## Yoga and Fichte

| Yoga IV.30 | Fichte |
|---|---|
| Kleśa and Karma cease from Dharma-Megha | pure certainty is immediately living Principle |
| the afflictive circuit loses its ground | Light projects itself immanently rather than receiving alien determination |
| cessation follows necessarily | all Light is necessarily self-creating and intuiting |
| activity itself need not end | the Science continues as Light's living activity |
| no constructed self owns the result | the factical scientist is not the ground of certainty |
| pure Kriyā becomes possible | expression proceeds from the self-grounding Principle |

## Provisional result

IV.30 says:

> From Dharma-Megha, the complete circuit of afflictive formation and
> consequence ceases.

This is not annihilation of Citta or termination of all activity. Kleśa
ceases to determine action; action therefore ceases to reproduce Kleśa
through Karma and Saṃskāra.

Fichte supplies the metaphysical ground. Certainty is living Light, and
living Light is immediately self-projecting. Its Principle is not received
through a gap from elsewhere. No alien determination can therefore establish
itself as the independent ground of Knowing.

```text
IV.29
Knowing relinquishes ownership of its highest insight
                    ↓
IV.30
Light acts from its immanent Principle;
Kleśa-Karma loses every ground of recurrence
```

IV.31 can now disclose the positive infinitude of Knowledge once its
coverings and impurities have fallen away.
