1. **Being-Bound**: The state of being confined or limited by certain conditions or determinations.

2. **Being-Determined**: A state of being defined or shaped by specific attributes or conditions.

3. **Being-Determined-for-Itself**: The process of self-determination, where a being defines or constitutes itself independently.

4. **Being-Determined-in-and-for-Itself**: A synthesis of being determined both independently (in itself) and in relation to others or the whole (for itself).

5. **Being-Determined-in-Itself**: Being defined or characterized by its intrinsic nature or essence.

6. **Being-for-Another**: Existing or being perceived in relation to another entity or concept.

7. **Being-for-Itself**: The state of being self-referential or existing independently for its own sake.

8. **Being-for-One**: Existing or being considered for a singular entity or purpose.

9. **Being-for-Other**: Existing in relation to or for the sake of something else.

10. **Being-in-and-for-Itself**: A state that encompasses both independent existence and relational existence.

11. **Being-in-It**: Existing within a certain context or framework.

12. **Being-in-Itself**: Existence as understood in its essential or inherent nature.

13. **Being-in-the-Something**: Existing within a specific entity or concept.

14. **Being-Mediated**: The state of being influenced or conditioned by intermediary factors or processes.

15. **Being-Negated**: The process of being denied, contradicted, or nullified.

16. **Being-Other**: The state of being different or distinct from something else.

17. **Being-Outside-Itself**: Existing or extending beyond its own limits or nature.

18. **Being-Outside-One-Another**: The state of multiple entities existing independently and externally to each other.

19. **Being-Posited**: Being established, asserted, or put forward in a certain way.

20. **Being-Reflected-Into-Itself**: The process of self-reflection or turning back upon itself.

21. **Being-Reflected-Within-Itself**: The state of internal self-reflection or self-examination.

22. **Being-That-Is-Posited**: An entity or concept that has been established or defined in a certain way.

23. **Being-The-Other-of-Each-Other**: The mutual relationship where entities or concepts define themselves in opposition to each other.

24. **Being-Turned-Back-Into-Itself**: The process of returning or reverting to its own nature or essence.

25. **Being-Within-Itself**: The state of containing or encompassing within its own nature or essence.
