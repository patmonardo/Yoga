# The Prajñā-Born Saṃskāra: Truth Inhibiting Contrary Formation

## A report on Yoga Sūtra I.50 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`taj-jaḥ saṃskāro 'nya-saṃskāra-pratibandhī`

### Relatively necessary ordinary translation

> The Saṃskāra born from that Prajñā inhibits the other Saṃskāras.

`Taj-jaḥ` means “born from that”: the cause is the Ṛtambharā Prajñā defined
in I.47–49.

The sequence is exact:

```text
Being of Prajñā
Nirvicāra skill and inner clarity
        ↓
Essence of Prajñā
Truth bearing itself in Knowing
        ↓
Concept of Prajñā
Truth manifesting its own distinctive Artha
        ↓
actualization of Prajñā in Citta
Prajñā-born Saṃskāra
        ↓
inhibition of contrary Saṃskāras
```

The examination of Saṃskāra follows the Concept of Prajñā because the
Concept must become actual in the history and functioning of Knowing.

## Vasubandhu is justified

Abhidharma can classify Prajñā within Saṃskāra without thereby reducing
Prajñā's Principle to an empirical mental residue.

The distinction is:

```text
Prajñā in itself
the unseeded, self-validating Principle of Truth-bearing Knowing

Prajñā actualized in conditioned Citta
a produced determination that persists,
orients future cognition, and opposes contrary formations
```

The second is a Saṃskāra because it has been accomplished, deposited, and
made causally effective within the stream of Knowing.

Prajñā is therefore not a static component lodged inside Buddhi. It becomes
a Saṃskāra when its insight is established as a durable power of cognition.

This is the Being of Knowing as acquired actuality.

## Saṃskāra is not merely memory

The Prajñā-born Saṃskāra certainly includes retention, but it is more than a
remembered proposition.

```text
memory alone
“I recall that immediate consciousness is insufficient.”

Prajñā-Saṃskāra
Knowing spontaneously refuses to grant independent validity
to a discontinuous projection of consciousness
```

The insight has become operative form. It determines what cognition accepts,
rejects, attends to, and reconstructs.

Thus Saṃskāra is the formed capacity produced by a previous act of Knowing.

## `Pratibandhī`: inhibition rather than erasure

The Sūtra does not say that the Prajñā-born Saṃskāra instantly destroys
every other formation. It says that it inhibits, obstructs, or counteracts
them.

Fichte supplies a precise model:

```text
factical projection remains unavoidable
        ↓
but its claim to validity is recognized
        ↓
the Scientist ignores and abstains from its result
        ↓
the projection no longer governs the judgment of Truth
```

The contrary formation may still arise. What changes is its power.

```text
before Prajñā-Saṃskāra
projection → belief → further construction

after Prajñā-Saṃskāra
projection → discrimination → inhibition
```

This is cognitive mastery, not psychic mutilation.

## The contrary Saṃskāras

In Fichte's analysis, the principal contrary formation is:

```text
proiectio per hiatum irrationalem
projection through an irrational gap
```

Consciousness projects a finished object or fact without exhibiting its
genesis:

```text
projective act
        || unexplained gap ||
projected `is`
```

The result appears independent because the constructive activity has
vanished into the gap.

Repeated throughout ordinary cognition, this becomes a powerful Saṃskāra:
the tendency to accept the categorical `is`, the object, or immediate
consciousness as self-validating.

The Prajñā-born Saṃskāra inhibits precisely this habit.

## The maxim of absolute genesis

Fichte identifies the governing rule of the Science of Knowing:

> Nothing that has not been genetically realized may be admitted as
> intrinsically valid.

This is identical with refusing projection through an irrational gap.

```text
ordinary maxim
“It appears immediately, therefore it is.”

maxim of absolute genesis
“Show the Principle through which its content,
standpoint, and manifestation arise.”
```

When merely stated, the maxim remains a rule. When born from Ṛtambharā
Prajñā and stabilized in cognition, it becomes Saṃskāra.

That is the Fichtean meaning of `taj-jaḥ saṃskāraḥ`.

## Why this is a practical power

I.47–49 might appear entirely theoretical: clarity, Truth in itself, and the
distinctive Artha of genetic Knowing.

I.50 shows their practical actuality.

Prajñā changes what Citta can do:

```text
it interrupts inherited cognitive momentum
it prevents facticity from becoming authority
it protects the distinction between Appearance and Principle
it sustains attention through genetic reconstruction
it makes renewed insight increasingly available
```

Thus the Concept of Prajñā becomes an effective causal power within the
stream of cognition.

## Fichte's “basic proposition”

Fichte labels the lecture attached to I.50 as containing the basic
proposition. He says the doctrine of Reason and Truth consists in a single
insight.

After the in-itself has been exposed as relational, pure Being remains.
Fichte reconstructs it as:

```text
Being of itself
Being in itself
Being through itself
```

But the `self` must no longer be taken as one term opposed to another. It is
pure inwardness, without the relational supplement of a not-in-itself.

Being is thereby constructed as pure act.

## Being and Living interpenetrate

Pure Being is not noun-like substance. A completely objectified Being would
be dead.

Fichte's basic proposition is:

```text
Being is Living
Living is Being

each completely interpenetrates the other
```

Only the infinitive “to be”—the living `esse`—expresses the act adequately.

```text
dead Being
a fixed object presented before consciousness

living Being
the self-enclosed act of Being itself
```

The substantial form must be surrendered in the fact and Truth of insight,
not merely denied verbally.

This is the Truth borne by Prajñā and impressed as its Saṃskāra.

## The One Being admits no external multiplicity

If Being is entirely of itself, through itself, and in itself, nothing can
stand outside it.

```text
not:
one Being beside many other Beings

but:
Being whose inner act is self-enclosed Oneness
```

Multiplicity does not belong to pure Being as an independently valid
division. It belongs to the phenomenology that must later be deduced as
Appearance.

Prajñā-Saṃskāra preserves this insight against the contrary tendency to
project a second self-subsistent term outside the One.

## “We are” the One Being

Fichte's next movement is deliberately startling:

> We live immediately in the act of Living; therefore we are the one
> undivided Being itself.

This “We” is not the empirical collection of persons and not a cosmic ego
standing over against the world.

```text
not empirical we
not objectified absolute We

but immediate living itself
before the objectifying repetition of consciousness
```

When spoken, the We is inevitably objectified. But the Prajñā-born maxim
already knows that this projection adds nothing to the living content.

The `We` is Being's self-enclosed Living, anticipated as capable of later
differentiation without losing its unity.

## The genetic I

Fichte does not appeal to empirical awareness of personal life. The I is
generated through pure Being:

```text
self-enclosed Being
        =
self-enclosed Living
        =
self-enclosed I / We
```

Conversely, the truly self-enclosed I is Being.

The direction is reversible because the content is one. The empirical fact
that we happen to see this is irrelevant to its Truth and can be abstracted.

This is not the absolute I of subjective idealism. It is the genetic I
emerging from the construction of one living Being.

## The first disjunction is annulled

Previously, Being still stood objectively before the Scientist:

```text
we contemplate Being
        ↓
Being appears separated from the contemplative We
```

The maxim told us not to grant this separation validity, but factically the
division remained.

Now the insight that Being itself is the living We annuls even this factical
separation:

```text
we cannot go out toward Being
because we are the living act of Being
```

The maxim of non-objectification has received its proof, Law, and immediate
realization.

This insight is the positive content whose Saṃskāra can inhibit objectifying
habits.

## Repetition without real division

Objectifying consciousness can still recur. It may repeat or double the
self-enclosed I:

```text
living We
        ↓ reflection
objectified image of the We
```

But the repetition introduces no new content and no genuine division in
Being. The We remains one, self-enclosed, and unalterable.

The source of this empty doubling belongs to the later phenomenology of
Appearance. For the doctrine of Truth, it is enough to know that repetition
adds nothing to Being.

Thus the other Saṃskāra can arise without regaining authority.

## Doctrine of Truth and phenomenology

Fichte divides the Science of Knowing into two parts:

```text
1. Doctrine of Reason and Truth
the single insight into self-enclosed living Being

2. Doctrine of Appearance and illusion
the genetic derivation of objectification,
doubling, multiplicity, and error from Truth
```

The second is true only because it is grounded in the first.

I.50 occupies their hinge. Prajñā bears the single insight of Truth, while
its Saṃskāra becomes the stable power required to descend into Appearance
without confusing Appearance with Being itself.

## Prajñā as Saṃskāra: the Being of Knowing

We can now state why Prajñā belongs to Saṃskāra-skandha in its conditioned
actuality.

```text
Prajñā as Principle
Truth's self-comprehension

Prajñā as Saṃskāra
the established Being of that Knowing within a cognitive stream
```

The Saṃskāra holds the achieved insight available across successive acts.
It is the formed Sattva of Knowing: the clarity and stability through which
the Principle continues to govern cognition.

Without this formation, the insight would occur and disappear. With it,
Prajñā becomes a power of continued scientific life.

## Why the Prajñā-Saṃskāra is still a seed

The Saṃskāra is born from unseeded Truth, but as a produced formation it is
itself a seed for subsequent cognition.

```text
Ṛtambharā Prajñā
unseeded in its source of validity
        ↓ produces
Prajñā-Saṃskāra
a conditioned seed of future Truth-oriented cognition
```

This explains the necessity of I.51. The Prajñā-Saṃskāra is uniquely
liberating because it inhibits contrary seeds, but it is still something
produced, retained, and causally effective.

Its special work must be completed before it too can undergo Nirodha.

## The mechanism of inhibition

The Prajñā-born Saṃskāra does not oppose every content indiscriminately. It
targets the form of non-genesis.

```text
contrary Saṃskāra arises:
“This object simply is.”

Prajñā-Saṃskāra responds:
“Its immediate projection is unavoidable,
but its independent validity has not been generated.”

result:
the appearance remains;
the illusion is inhibited.
```

This is much more efficient than intellectually destroying each projected
object one at a time.

Fichte says that once the origin of non-genesis is known, one need not fight
the illusion. One allows it to operate peacefully while abstaining from its
results.

That is an exact account of `pratibandha`.

## From intellectual struggle to established skill

Before the maxim became explicit, the Science had to proceed laboriously:

```text
divide an illusion
destroy one part intellectually
allow another part temporarily to bear the projection
return later and destroy that bearer
```

The path still reaches Truth, but with great difficulty.

Once the Prajñā-born Saṃskāra is established:

```text
recognize projection as projection
ignore its unsupported result
enter genetic insight directly
```

The Scientist no longer exhausts energy trying to eliminate an unavoidable
feature of consciousness. The contrary Saṃskāra is prevented from maturing
into belief.

## A–U–M and the Saṃskāra of Prajñā

The full movement can be read through A–U–M:

```text
A — Appearance
objectifying consciousness projects a categorical `is`

U — Reflection
Nirvicāra traces the projection to its subtle Principle
and exposes the irrational gap

M — Knowing
Ṛtambharā Prajñā comprehends self-enclosed living Being
as the Principle of Appearance

Saṃskāra
M becomes an established power that governs future A and U
```

The Concept has entered actuality. M is no longer only an insight; it has
become a durable determinant of the cognitive process.

## A practice for Scientists of Knowing

I.50 suggests a concrete discipline:

1. Notice every categorical `is` projected by consciousness.
2. Do not try to suppress the unavoidable appearance.
3. Ask whether the object's genesis has been exhibited.
4. Identify the irrational gap between projection and projected result.
5. Abstain from granting the result independent validity.
6. Return to the insight that Being and Living interpenetrate.
7. Recognize the contemplated Being as the living act in which Knowing
   already participates.
8. Repeat this discrimination until it becomes spontaneous.
9. Observe contrary formations lose their ability to govern judgment.
10. Preserve the Prajñā-Saṃskāra until its inhibiting work is complete.

The mark of success is not the disappearance of Appearance. It is freedom
from being deceived by Appearance's discontinuous projection.

## Compact synthesis

Yoga gives the causal actuality of Prajñā:

```text
Ṛtambharā Prajñā
        ↓ gives birth to
Prajñā-Saṃskāra
        ↓ inhibits
other Saṃskāras
```

Fichte gives its Principle and operation:

```text
pure Being is reconstructed as living act
        ↓
Being, Living, and the genetic We interpenetrate
        ↓
the separation between Knowing and Being is annulled
        ↓
the maxim of absolute genesis becomes established
        ↓
projection through an irrational gap is recognized
without needing to be factically eliminated
        ↓
Knowing abstains from its unsupported results
```

I.50 justifies Vasubandhu's placement of Prajñā among the Saṃskāras while
preserving the transcendental distinction. Prajñā in itself is the
self-bearing Principle of Truth. Prajñā as Saṃskāra is that Principle made
actual as an acquired and causally effective power of Citta.

This Saṃskāra is the Being of Knowing established in cognitive history. It
blocks the formations that mistake projection for genesis. Yet precisely
because it has become a produced power, it remains a final seed. I.51 must
therefore complete its work by bringing even this liberating Saṃskāra to
Nirodha.
