# Vicāra Samāpatti: Reflection within the Subtle Principle

## A report on Yoga Sūtra I.44 and Fichte

## The Sūtra

`etayaiva savicārā nirvicārā ca sūkṣma-viṣayā vyākhyātā`

### Relatively necessary ordinary translation

> By this same distinction, Savicāra and Nirvicāra Samāpatti, whose objects
> are subtle, are explained.

“By this same distinction” refers back to the movement from Savitarka to
Nirvitarka:

```text
mixed determination                 purified determination
Savitarka                           Nirvitarka
Word–Artha–Knowledge                Artha alone shining

             the same movement at a subtler level

Savicāra                            Nirvicāra
reflective determination            reflection transparent
of the subtle object                to its subtle Principle
```

I.44 is brief because it repeats an already established form. But its new
field—the subtle object—allows Fichte to expand the repetition into the
inner Science of Reflection.

## Vicāra is the U-Principle

In our A–U–M specification:

```text
A — Appearance
the determinate content presented

U — Reflection / Thinking
the return into the conditions and Principle
of that presentation

M — Knowing / Concept
the comprehension of Appearance and Reflection
as moments of one self-articulating act
```

Vitarka begins from an articulated appearance: Word, Artha, and Knowledge.
Vicāra moves within the subtle grounds by which such an appearance is
possible.

```text
Vitarka asks:
What exactly is presented and meant here?

Vicāra asks:
Through what subtle Principle does this presentation arise,
differentiate itself, and remain one?
```

Vicāra is therefore not merely “thinking harder.” It is Thinking reflected
into the genesis of its own determinations.

## What makes an object subtle?

A gross object appears as a finished determination. A subtle object is not
simply a physically tiny thing. It is a condition, ground, relation, or
Principle that does not ordinarily appear as an independent object.

```text
gross object
the formed result that appears

subtle object
the generative determination through which it appears
```

Fichte's text makes the distinction especially exact. The empirical result
is not scientifically constructed in all its accidental detail. Science
constructs the Principle from which the field of possible appearances
becomes intelligible.

The proper objects of Vicāra are therefore not merely hidden things. They
are Principles.

## Why the same distinction applies

Savitarka and Nirvitarka distinguished two conditions of cognition:

```text
the representative form claims attention
                     ↕
the representative becomes transparent to Artha
```

Savicāra and Nirvicāra repeat the same purification within Reflection:

```text
the subtle Principle is still considered through
the reflective form that objectifies it
                     ↕
Reflection comprehends its own limit and becomes
transparent to the living Principle it cannot replace
```

Thus the two pairs are genuinely the “same thing,” but they are not
duplicates. The second pair internalizes the first movement.

## Savicāra: the Principle under reflective consideration

In Savicāra, Thinking has moved beyond the immediately given object. It
considers the Principle, relation, or subtle ground from which the object
arises.

But the Principle is still present *as considered*:

```text
living Principle
        ↓ reflection
representation of the Principle
        ↓
Principle made into a subtle object
```

This is real progress. The Scientist is no longer confined to factical
appearance. Yet Reflection still distinguishes itself from its object and
may confuse its representation of the Principle with the Principle's own
Life.

Savicāra is therefore subtle genetic inquiry that has not yet made its own
reflective form fully transparent.

## Nirvicāra: Reflection comprehends its limit

Nirvicāra does not abolish Reflection. It completes Reflection by allowing
it to comprehend exactly what belongs to the Concept and what cannot be
generated by reflective objectification.

```text
Reflection follows the Principle to its root
        ↓
it reconstructs every constructible distinction
        ↓
it discovers the boundary of construction
        ↓
it negates its own claim to be the living source
        ↓
the subtle Principle shines through Reflection
```

This is why Vicāra is King: properly completed, Reflection does not merely
accumulate more subtle objects. It discovers its own Principle and its own
limit.

## Fichte's focal point: truth seen for oneself

Fichte begins by insisting that the achieved insight is not his report or
the doctrine of another philosopher. It must be seen directly and retained
as self-insight.

The insight is:

```text
an absolutely produced division is negated as independently valid
        ↓
a self-subsistent ground is manifested in the background
        ↓
the ground cannot be described as one more divided term
```

This truth does not become true when someone recognizes it. Nor is it a
hypothesis accepted because it conveniently explains phenomena. It is
unconditionally manifest in the fulfilled construction.

That categorical difference between truth and error is the basis of
Nirvicāra. Reflection is purified by the Principle; the Principle is not
validated by the empirical success of an external hypothesis.

## Science legislates for appearance

Once a Principle is genuinely manifest, phenomena are judged through it.

```text
derivable from the Principle
        → genuine possible appearance

not derivable from the Principle
        → error, deception, or phantasm
```

This does not mean that a philosopher may dismiss inconvenient facts at
will. The claimed Principle and every inference must themselves be sound.
It means that Science does not receive its ultimate Law from an accidental
collection of appearances.

Vicāra seeks the subtle Law of appearance. Nirvicāra sees that Law clearly
enough for the Law to determine what can truly count as its appearance.

## Light, Concept, and Being

Fichte first presents three inseparable moments:

```text
L — Light
living self-manifestation

T — Thinking / Concept
the dividing and representative form

B — Being
the self-subsistent term posited when Concept is negated
```

The Light is one. The Concept that divides what is one in itself is one.
The Being posited through the Concept's negation is one.

At first it appears that Light generates the division:

```text
             Light
             /   \
        Being   Thinking
```

But Fichte's Vicāra does not stop at this first subtle schema. It reflects
upon the act that sees and states the schema.

## The negation of the I

The formal Concept appears to be freely constructed by the I. When
self-producing manifestness invalidates that Concept's independent claim,
the I as its supposed Principle is invalidated with it.

```text
I construct the Concept
        ↓
Truth manifests itself with necessity
        ↓
my construction is grasped and displaced by the insight
        ↓
the I is no longer the source of validity
```

This is not the destruction of the living Scientist. It is the destruction
of the claim that personal construction produces Truth.

The Scientist survives by merging with the Principle rather than standing
outside it as proprietor.

## Inner and external Life of Light

Fichte then makes a decisive subtle distinction.

Light has two modes of existence for the inquiry:

```text
inner Life
Light lives immediately through itself,
whether or not anyone reflects upon it

external existence
Light appears as an object within our reflective insight,
through the procedure of the Science of Knowing
```

The second can be reconstructed. We know the abstractions and reflections
through which Light became an object for us.

The first cannot be objectified without ceasing to be immediate Life. It is
not irrational; it is the exact limit discovered by fully accomplished
Reflection.

## The great reversal

Initially, the division into Being and Thinking seemed to belong to Light
itself. But we have now seen and articulated that division. Therefore the
division as division belongs to insight into Light.

```text
first appearance
Light itself splits into B and T

deeper Vicāra
our insight represents Light through B and T

what remains
the inward Life of Light in pure Identity,
from itself, through itself, without the represented split
```

This is the movement from Savicāra to Nirvicāra. Reflection first discovers
the subtle division and then recognizes the division as its own necessary
form of access—not as an incision inside absolute Life.

## Fichte pursues death to reach Life

Every predicate assigned to what lies beyond completed insight is obtained
through negating the conditions of insight. Even “absolute substance,” if
taken as a determinate object, is only a negative construction.

Fichte therefore refuses to begin with a dead Being and decorate it with
Life afterward.

```text
dead metaphysical procedure
fixed Being → attributes → apparent Life

Science of Knowing
follow every objectifying Concept to its dissolution
                         ↓
discover the living Light presupposed by the whole movement
```

His striking formula is that reality, according to its form, is the
“graveyard of the Concept” that tries to find itself in Light.

This is not Concept-cide. The Concept fulfills its scientific office by
determining exactly where its objectification ends and living Reality
begins.

## Pure Identity is not an inert One

When every division belonging to the reflective Concept has been returned
to that Concept, Light remains one and self-identical.

But this Identity is not an empty thing sitting beyond Science. It is
manifest as the living Reality through which Reflection occurs and toward
which completed Reflection points.

```text
pure living Identity
        ↓ appears in Concept
B — T and every further differentiable determination
        ↓ comprehended by Vicāra
Concept recognizes the divisions as its own forms
        ↓
Nirvicāra: Life shines beyond objectification
```

I.43 raised the differentiated identity of Savitarka into Nirvitarka. I.44
now performs the same elevation upon Reflection itself.

## What the Concept can completely know

Fichte gives the Science an exact range:

> Insight can have insight into itself; the Concept can conceive itself.

Everything belonging to the form of Knowing can therefore be reconstructed:

```text
the Concept's differentiations
their possible combinations
their relation to Being
their status as appearances of Light
the boundary at which Concept ceases to be self-sufficient
```

The Concept's completed self-conceiving is also its conception of its own
limit.

Beyond that limit is not another subtle object waiting to be conceptualized.
It is the living Light to which insight points and in which it participates.

This is Nirvicāra: not incomplete thinking, but Thinking completed all the
way to the lucid comprehension of its boundary.

## Why Vicāra is King

Vicāra governs the crucial middle movement of the Science:

```text
Appearance is given
        ↓
Vicāra returns from result to Principle
        ↓
it distinguishes the Principle from its representation
        ↓
it reconstructs the necessary forms of appearance
        ↓
it discovers and conceives its own limit
        ↓
Knowing opens onto living Light
```

Without Vicāra, one remains with either empirical appearance or an asserted
Absolute. Through Vicāra, Appearance is genetically reconstructed and the
Absolute is approached without being converted into a dead object.

Vicāra is King because it carries the Scientist from the first Identity to
the self-comprehension of Identity.

## The U movement

The letter U names the reflective passage between A and M:

```text
A
the Principle appears in a determinate division

U
Reflection moves through the division,
reverses its directions, and returns it to its ground

M
Knowing comprehends division and Identity
as the self-articulation of one Principle
```

U is therefore not merely a second item after A. It raises A:

```text
A preserved
the determinate Appearance remains articulable

A negated
its independent and immediate validity is surrendered

A elevated
its Artha is known through the Principle of its genesis
```

This is exactly how Nirvitarka raises Savitarka, and how Nirvicāra raises
Savicāra.

## A practice for Scientists of Knowing

I.44 supplies a demanding but clear procedure:

1. Begin with a determinate appearance whose Artha has been clarified.
2. Ask for the subtle Principle that makes this appearance possible.
3. Construct the distinctions implied by that Principle.
4. Distinguish the living Principle from its representation in Reflection.
5. Trace every claimed division back to the act of insight that posits it.
6. Determine which features belong to Reality and which to the Concept's
   form of access.
7. Reverse the construction to test whether the Principle genuinely
   generates the possible appearance.
8. Conceive the exact limit of conception.
9. Let Reflection become transparent to the living Light it cannot replace.

The result is not vague mysticism. It is maximal conceptual precision joined
to freedom from the illusion that a Conceptual image is living Reality
itself.

## Compact synthesis

Yoga gives the formal extension:

```text
Savitarka → Nirvitarka
          same distinction
Savicāra  → Nirvicāra

now directed toward subtle objects
```

Fichte gives its genetic development:

```text
Reflection discovers a Principle behind appearance
        ↓
the Principle is first objectified within Reflection
        ↓
Reflection examines its own act of objectification
        ↓
Being–Thinking and further divisions are recognized
as forms of insight into Light
        ↓
the Concept completely conceives itself and its limit
        ↓
living Light remains as pure self-identical Reality
```

I.44 is therefore much more than “the same Samāpatti applied to subtler
objects.” It is that exact repetition raised into the Science of the subtle
Principle itself.

Savitarka and Nirvitarka give the A movement from articulated Appearance to
transparent Artha. Savicāra and Nirvicāra give the U movement in which
Reflection returns that Artha to its Principle, comprehends its own
objectifying activity, and finally becomes transparent to living Light.

Vicāra is King because this reflective return is the labor through which
the first Identity becomes self-knowing.
